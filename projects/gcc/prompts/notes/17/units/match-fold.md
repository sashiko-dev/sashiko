# match-fold

This unit provides GCC's `match.pd` pattern database (`gcc/match.pd`), the headers and runtime helpers for GENERIC and GIMPLE pattern matching and constant folding (`generic-match*`, `gimple-match*`, `fold-const.h`), and target-parameterized polynomial integer types (`poly-int-types.h`). It extracts GIMPLE into `gimple_match_op` descriptors, dispatches depth-bounded simplifications and conditional `IFN_COND_*` rewrites, and materializes results into statement sequences.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `convert_conditional_op` and `try_conditional_simplification` must share the `IFN_COND_*` layout: `ops[0]` is `cond`, `ops[1..num_ops]` are unconditional operands, `ops[num_ops + 1]` is `else_value`, and when `cond.len` is non-null (`num_cond_ops == 4`), `convert_conditional_op` upgrades the IFN via `get_len_internal_fn` and places `len` and `bias` in the last two slots; `else_value` must satisfy `types_compatible_p (orig_op->type, TREE_TYPE (else_value))` or be populated via `targetm.preferred_else_value`. [F2, F36, F37]
- In `maybe_resimplify_conditional_op`, a conditional `res_op` with non-null `else_value` that simplifies to a gimple value (`gimple_simplified_result_is_gimple_val`) must be wrapped with `else_value` into `IFN_VCOND_MASK_LEN` (vector with `len`), `VEC_COND_EXPR` (vector without `len`), or `COND_EXPR` (scalar); if `else_value` is null, `res_op->cond.cond` may be cleared only if `gimple_simplified_result_is_gimple_val (res_op)` holds or the operation cannot trap (`generic_expr_could_trap_p` for `COND_EXPR`, `operation_could_trap_p` otherwise). [F22, F45]
- `gimple_resimplify1`..`gimple_resimplify4` must check `if (depth > 10)` before calling `gimple_simplify` and balance `++depth` with `--depth` on all return paths; in `gimple_resimplify1`..`gimple_resimplify3`, `constant_for_folding` constant folding must run before the `depth > 10` check so all-constant expressions still fold at max depth. [F6, F7]
- In `gimple_match_op`, only `ops[0..num_ops - 1]` are initialized; code reading potentially unused slots (`build_call_internal`, `maybe_resimplify_conditional_op`, `maybe_push_res_to_seq`) must bound indices by `num_ops` or use `op_or_null (i)`. [F16]
- For single-RHS reference codes (`REALPART_EXPR`, `IMAGPART_EXPR`, `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR`, `BIT_FIELD_REF`), `gimple_extract` must require `TREE_CODE (op0) == SSA_NAME || is_gimple_min_invariant (op0)`, `gimple_simplify (gimple *, ...)` must not call `res_op->resimplify` when `res_op->reverse` is true, and `maybe_build_generic_op` must wrap `res_op->ops` back into a GENERIC tree (setting `REF_REVERSE_STORAGE_ORDER (val) = res_op->reverse` on `BIT_FIELD_REF`) before `gimple_build_assign`. [F21, F25, F47]
- In `build_call_internal`, a `direct_internal_fn_p` call unsupported by `direct_internal_fn_supported_p` may be built only for `IFN_CLZ`, `IFN_CTZ`, `IFN_CLRSB`, `IFN_FFS`, `IFN_POPCOUNT`, `IFN_PARITY`, `IFN_BSWAP`, or `IFN_BITREVERSE` on a `BITINT_TYPE_P` `ops[0]` with precision `> MAX_FIXED_MODE_SIZE` before `PROP_gimple_lbitint`. [F48]
- In `match_cond_with_binary_phi`, the 2-argument `gphi` predecessors must form a triangle or diamond where the branch block has `EDGE_COUNT (succs) == 2`, every intermediate block has `EDGE_COUNT (preds) == 1` and `EDGE_COUNT (succs) == 1` (with the middle block's single pred verified as the other PHI pred block in a triangle), and `edge_for_pred_0` must be set to the `gcond` outgoing edge reaching `pred_b0` before testing `EDGE_TRUE_VALUE`. [F5, F32, F50]
- In `types_match`, both GENERIC and GIMPLE helpers must extract `TREE_TYPE` when an argument is not `TYPE_P`, and GENERIC must compare `TYPE_MAIN_VARIANT (t1) == TYPE_MAIN_VARIANT (t2)` whereas GIMPLE must compare `types_compatible_p (t1, t2)`. [F13]
- In `gcc/match.pd`, patterns on general `INTEGRAL_TYPE_P` or `BITINT_TYPE` operands must verify target precision `<= MAX_FIXED_MODE_SIZE` (or `< HOST_BITS_PER_WIDE_INT`) before calling `build_nonstandard_integer_type`; when narrowing `PARITY`, `CTZ`, or `POPCOUNT` on a widened `BITINT_TYPE` `@0` with `TYPE_PRECISION (type0) > MAX_FIXED_MODE_SIZE`, keep `BITINT_TYPE` and emit `CFN_PARITY`/`CFN_CTZ`/`CFN_POPCOUNT`. [F58, F79]
- In `gcc/match.pd`, simplifying `(minus real_zerop@0 @1)` to `(negate @1)` must not use `fold_real_zero_addition_p`; it requires `!tree_expr_maybe_nan_p (@1)` and `(!HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS (type) || tree_expr_nonzero_p (@1) || (!flag_rounding_math && real_negzerop (@0)))`. [F95]
- In `gcc/match.pd`, under `flag_trapping_math`: `(cmp @0 REAL_CST@1)` against `+-Inf` with `HONOR_NANS (@0)` must not fold ordered relations (`>`, `<=`, `==`) to constants, `eq`, or `>`/`<`, and must rewrite `!= +Inf` to `unle`/`unge`; eliding `float` in `(convert (float @0))` or `(cmp (float@2 @0) REAL_CST@1)` when the float mode cannot exactly represent `@0`'s type or range requires `!flag_trapping_math`; sinking `(cmp (cond @0 @1 @2) @3)` into `cond` requires `!FLOAT_TYPE_P (TREE_TYPE (@3)) || !operation_could_trap_p (cmp, true, false, @3)`. [F81, F97, F98]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: folding `(cnd (ne @0 uniform_integer_cst_p@1) (plus@2 @0 uniform_integer_cst_p@3) uniform_integer_cst_p@4)` when `cst1 + cst2 == cst3` must perform the addition in `unsigned_type_for (type)` and convert back if `wi::add` sets `ovf` and `!TYPE_OVERFLOW_WRAPS (TREE_TYPE (cst1))`; folding `(plus:c @0 (minmax:cs@3 (minus @1 @0) @2))` to `(minmax @1 (plus @2 @0))` for `TYPE_UNSIGNED (type)` requires `overflow_free_p` on both `MINUS_EXPR (vr1, vr0)` and `PLUS_EXPR (vr0, vr2)` via `gimple_match_range_of_expr`. [F100, F102]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: simplifying `(X {+,-} M*N) / N` to `(X / N) {+,-} M` under `vr0.nonpositive_p () && vr4.nonpositive_p ()` requires `!vr0.contains_p (wi::min_value (TYPE_PRECISION (type), SIGNED)) || !vr2.contains_p (wi::minus_one (TYPE_PRECISION (type)))`; integer division (`trunc_div`, `ceil_div`, `floor_div`, `round_div`, `exact_div`) and modulo (`ceil_mod`, `floor_mod`, `round_mod`, `trunc_mod`) rules must check `!integer_zerop` on the divisor before folding. [F66, F103]
- In `gcc/match.pd`, canonicalizing `(NT) ((UT) X > NT_MAX ? (-X) >> (PREC - 1) : X)` to `min`/`max` requires `!TREE_SIDE_EFFECTS (@0)`, `TYPE_OVERFLOW_UNDEFINED (TREE_TYPE (@3))`, and `!TYPE_OVERFLOW_SANITIZED (TREE_TYPE (@3))` on `(negate@3 ...)`; narrowing integral binary ops across widening conversions (`(convert (op (convert@3 @1) (convert@4 @2)))` or `(bit_and (op:s (convert@2 @0) (convert@3 @1)) INTEGER_CST@4)`) requires `type_has_mode_precision_p` on both inner operand types and `type`. [F86, F106]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: simplifying `cond @0 -1 0` to a negation in integral `type` must omit `negate` when `TYPE_PRECISION (type) == 1` and convert multi-bit `BOOLEAN_TYPE` via `build_nonstandard_integer_type (TYPE_PRECISION (type), TYPE_UNSIGNED (type))` before negating; in two-value `(cond (eqne@4 SSA_NAME@0 INTEGER_CST@1) INTEGER_CST@2 INTEGER_CST@3)`, `type1` must select `TREE_TYPE (@0)` over `type` when `TYPE_PRECISION (TREE_TYPE (@0)) > TYPE_PRECISION (type)` or when their precisions are equal and `!TYPE_UNSIGNED (type)`. [F80, F104]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: lowering a single-element insertion in `(vec_perm @0 @1 VECTOR_CST@2)` to `bit_insert` requires `TYPE_MODE (TREE_TYPE (op0)) != BLKmode`; rewriting alternating vector add/sub `(vec_perm (plusminus @0 @1) (minusplus @2 @3) VECTOR_CST@4)` via `view_convert` to a wider-element `negate` requires `!FLOAT_WORDS_BIG_ENDIAN`, matching IEEE sign-bit offsets, and `targetm.can_change_mode_class (TYPE_MODE (ntype), TYPE_MODE (type), ALL_REGS)`; `(reduc (op @0 VECTOR_CST@1))` across all `IFN_REDUC_*` (including `IFN_REDUC_FMAX`/`IFN_REDUC_FMIN`) must use `(reduc!:type @1)`. [F87, F88, F107]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: `pointer_diff` on `pointer_plus` operands must reinterpret the offset as signed via `(view_convert:stype @1)` (`stype = signed_type_for (TREE_TYPE (@1))`) before converting to `type`; rules rewriting constructs subject to `-Wconversion` or UBSan (unsigned narrowing casts to `bit_and`, `REFERENCE_TYPE` null/alignment checks, `SANITIZE_SHIFT_EXPONENT`, `SANITIZE_BUILTIN` on `CLZ`/`CTZ`) must be gated on `GIMPLE` or `PROP_ssa` when sanitizing. [F69, F82]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: `(match with_possible_nonzero_bits ...)` and `(match with_known_nonzero_bits ...)` must only match `with_possible_nonzero_bits_1` and `with_known_nonzero_bits_1` (never self-recursively); constant-free `(match ...)` predicates consumed by scalar comparison/shift rewrites (such as `ior_range_test_0`) must guard on `INTEGRAL_TYPE_P (TREE_TYPE (@0))`; multi-constant bit-manipulation recognizers (such as `IFN_POPCOUNT`) must constrain every captured `INTEGER_CST@N` in `(if (...))`. [F54, F59, F72]

## API Contracts

- In static initializers or manifestly-constant-evaluated contexts, callers must use `fold_init`, `fold_build1_initializer_loc`, `fold_build2_initializer_loc`, `fold_build_call_array_initializer_loc`, or `fold_binary_initializer_loc` so `folding_initializer` is set and trap flags are cleared; optimizations valid in static initializers but forbidden in C++ `constexpr` must check `!folding_cxx_constexpr` in addition to `folding_initializer`, and C++ `constexpr` callers must set `folding_cxx_constexpr` separately. [F11, F12]
- `bitwise_inverted_equal_p` (GENERIC) and `gimple_bitwise_inverted_equal_p` (GIMPLE) must initialize `wascmp = false`, pass `HONOR_NANS (op10)` to `invert_tree_comparison`, match `BIT_XOR_EXPR` against `EQ_EXPR` on GIMPLE only when `INTEGRAL_TYPE_P (type) && TYPE_PRECISION (type) == 1`, and set `wascmp = true` on all comparison-inversion matches; `match.pd` callers needing all bits flipped must check `(!wascmp || element_precision (type) == 1)`. [F29, F34, F49, F77]
- `find_different_opnum` requires `arg0_op.code == arg1_op.code` and `arg0_op.num_ops == arg1_op.num_ops`, and on commutative matches must return the differing index in `arg0_op` (`first` or `first + 1`) while storing `arg0_op`'s operand in `*new_arg0` and `arg1_op`'s in `*new_arg1`. [F3]
- In `factor_operation_ok`, `opnum < 0` allows `args` to be null (treating `all_ssa_names` as true and skipping type checks) while still requiring equal `locs[0..num_args-1]` for `IFN_UBSAN_*` and `IFN_ASAN_CHECK`; when `!all_ssa_names_p (args, num_args)`, it must reject factoring if `opnum` is a constant-required operand (`BIT_FIELD_REF` ops 1/2, `BIT_INSERT_EXPR` op 2, `VEC_PERM_EXPR` op 2, or `fn_arg_must_be_const_p`), an integer divisor (`!divconstok`), or a `POINTER_PLUS_EXPR` offset (`!ptrplusconstok`). [F31, F43]
- `maybe_push_res_to_seq` must return `NULL_TREE` without appending to `seq` if `res_op->cond.cond` is non-null, any `SSA_NAME` operand has `SSA_NAME_OCCURS_IN_ABNORMAL_PHI`, or the target builtin/internal function lacks `ECF_CONST`. [F23]
- `gimple_match_ctx` must return `SSA_NAME_DEF_STMT (op)` only when `op` is an `SSA_NAME` whose defining statement has a non-null `gimple_bb` (returning `NULL` otherwise), and `gimple_match_range_of_expr` must reject ranges where `r.undefined_p ()` is true. [F26]
- In the conversion overload `directly_supported_p (code_helper code, tree otype, tree itype, optab_subtype query_type)`, the optab or `associated_internal_fn` lookup must be keyed on `itype`, and `convert_optab_handler` must be queried with `(TYPE_MODE (otype), TYPE_MODE (itype))` in that order. [F39]
- `expr_no_side_effects_p` in `gcc/generic-match-head.cc` must check both `!TREE_SIDE_EFFECTS (t)` and `!generic_expr_could_trap_p (t)`, whereas in `gcc/gimple-match-head.cc` it asserts `is_gimple_val (t)`; when comparing optional tree operands that may be `NULL_TREE`, callers must use `safe_operand_equal_p` rather than `operand_equal_p`. [F20, F52]
- In `gcc/match.pd`, patterns that iterate over all vector lanes or size containers (`auto_sbitmap`, `vec_perm_builder`) to `TYPE_VECTOR_SUBPARTS` or `VECTOR_CST_NELTS` must check `.is_constant ()` before calling `.to_constant ()`; in `gcc/poly-int-types.h`, `vector_element_size` and `vector_unroll_factor` require exact constant-multiple arguments (`exact_div (...).to_constant ()`), and byte-alignment macros (`bits_to_bytes_round_down`, `round_up_to_byte_boundary`, etc.) require non-constant `poly_int` bit quantities to be multiples of `BITS_PER_UNIT`. [F61, F84, F91]

## Error Handling

- Before extracting a non-internal `GIMPLE_CALL` in `gimple_extract`, the call must have a non-null `gimple_call_lhs`, 1 to 7 arguments, and a non-null `gimple_call_fn` that valueizes to an `ADDR_EXPR` of a `FUNCTION_DECL` with `DECL_BUILT_IN_CLASS (decl) == BUILT_IN_NORMAL` and `gimple_builtin_call_types_compatible_p`; in `match_cond_with_binary_phi`, the last statement of `edge_for_pred_0->src` must be obtained with `safe_dyn_cast <gcond *>` and checked for non-null. [F15, F18]

## External Contracts

- In `gcc/match.pd`, `(view_convert (convert@0 @1))` must not strip the inner conversion `@0` when `BITINT_TYPE_P (TREE_TYPE (@0))` and `targetm.c.bitint_type_info (TYPE_PRECISION (TREE_TYPE (@0)), &info)` reports `info.extended` is true. [F90]

## Known Pitfalls

- `gcc/gimple-match.h` guards `directly_supported_p` declarations (with `optab_default` default arguments) under `#ifdef GCC_OPTABS_TREE_H`, which is not yet defined when `gimple-match-exports.cc` and `gimple-match-head.cc` include `gimple-match.h`; `gcc/fold-const.h` guards `fold_vec_perm` (and `vec_perm_indices` declarations) with `#if GCC_VEC_PERN_INDICES_H` (spelled `PERN`), requiring `vec-perm-indices.h` before `fold-const.h` in the match preamble files. [F9, F10]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: an outer `(if (<cond>) ...)` applies `<cond>` only until its matching `)`, as `parser::parse_if` pops `active_ifs` on `)` and continues parsing at top level without warning; any `#define` inside a `(with { ... })` block is expanded by `libcpp` across subsequent patterns unless `#undef`'d; `(match zero_one_valued_p ...)` rules for `(bit_and:c@0 @1 integer_onep)` and `(convert@0 @1)` must not match `zero_one_valued_p` recursively on `@1`. [F53, F60, F63]
- In `gcc/match.pd` C expressions: referencing `@N` outside `TREE_TYPE`, `TREE_CODE`, `TREE_REAL_CST`, or a `define_predicates` call (or after an inner `)` inside one) marks `@N` as escaping in `capture_info::walk_c_expr`, rejecting GENERIC operands with `TREE_SIDE_EFFECTS`; when moving an operand into/out of a conditional `cond` arm, rules must require `(GIMPLE || !TREE_SIDE_EFFECTS (...))` or `expr_no_side_effects_p (...)`; dynamically computed comparison codes from `combine_comparisons` or `invert_tree_comparison` must use `(with rescmpcode (tcc_comparison) { ... })` rather than an outer `(for ...)` loop. [F62, F68, F105]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: bitmask checks on `INTEGER_CST` operands of general integral types must use `wi::mask (..., TYPE_PRECISION (type))` and `wi::to_wide` rather than `tree_to_uhwi`/`HOST_WIDE_INT_1U`; comparing two `INTEGER_CST` operands (such as shift counts) whose types may differ in `TYPE_PRECISION` must use `wi::to_widest` rather than passing `wi::to_wide` to `wi::leu_p`; operands matched only by `real_zerop`/`real_onep`/`real_minus_onep` may be `VECTOR_CST` or `COMPLEX_CST` and must not be passed to `TREE_REAL_CST` without checking `TREE_CODE == REAL_CST` or using `real_negzerop`. [F65, F70, F71]
- In `gcc/match.pd`: introducing a new `negate` on a signed operand (e.g. `(A ^ -cmp) + cmp -> cmp ? -A : A`) must negate in `unsigned_type_for (type)` and convert back; `(convert (abs (convert@1 @0)))` may fold directly to `(abs @0)` only when `!ANY_INTEGRAL_TYPE_P (TREE_TYPE (@0))`, and integral narrowing must use `(convert (absu:utype @0))`; `unsigned_type_for (type)` rewrites across `nop_convert` must require `ANY_INTEGRAL_TYPE_P` with `&&` on both `type` and `t = TREE_TYPE (@0)` and check `TYPE_UNSIGNED (t) || !TYPE_OVERFLOW_SANITIZED (t)`. [F73, F76, F101]
- When narrowing `(FTYPE) N CMP CST` to an integer comparison in `gcc/match.pd`, `real_to_integer` does not report overflow for `SIGNED` values in `[INT_MAX + 1, UINT_MAX]`, so when `isign == SIGNED && !overflow_p`, the result must be round-tripped with `real_from_integer` and verified with `real_identical`. [F75]
