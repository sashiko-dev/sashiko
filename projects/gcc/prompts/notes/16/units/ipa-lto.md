# ipa-lto

The `ipa-lto` unit implements GCC's symbol table and callgraph infrastructure (`symtab*`, `cgraph*`), interprocedural optimization passes (`ipa-cp`, `ipa-devirt`, `ipa-fnsummary`, `ipa-inline*`, `ipa-icf*`, `ipa-free-lang-data`), profile representation and branch/value profiling (`profile*`, `predict`, `value-prof`, `auto-profile`), and Link-Time Optimization streaming and orchestration (`lto-streamer*`, `lto-section*`, `lto-opts`, `lto-wrapper`, `lto-ltrans-cache`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Once `cgraph_node::get_edge` allocates `call_site_hash` (>100 edges), it queries `call_site_hash` exclusively; every edge insertion, removal, or `call_stmt` update must update `call_site_hash`, and speculative call sequences sharing a `call_stmt` must keep their slot pointing to `first_speculative_call_target` via `update_call_stmt_hash_for_removing_direct_edge` before removing a direct edge. [F9, F10]
- In `cgraph_edge::set_call_stmt`, `old_stmt` must be captured from `direct->call_stmt` before the loop overwrites speculative direct edges with `new_stmt` so `iterate_reference` can match `ref->stmt == old_stmt`. In `symtab_node::create_reference`, `IPA_REF_ALIAS` entries must be inserted at index 0 of `referred_node->ref_list.referring` with subsequent `referred_index` fields incremented, and if `safe_grow` relocates `ref_list.references`, every `ref2->referred_ref_list ()->referring[ref2->referred_index]` pointer must be updated. [F3, F250, F280]
- In `symtab_node::register_symbol`, `insert_to_assembler_name_hash` must run last after setting `decl->decl_with_vis.symtab_node` and clearing `ref_list` (computing `DECL_ASSEMBLER_NAME` can recursively create symbols), and assembler-name hash operations must guard against `DECL_ASSEMBLER_NAME` returning `NULL`. `symbol_table::change_decl_assembler_name` must also update direct `transparent_alias` nodes and their `TREE_CHAIN (DECL_ASSEMBLER_NAME)` to `ultimate_transparent_alias_target`. [F283, F284, F285]
- An analyzed unexpanded thunk (`node->thunk == true`) has one `callees` edge with `call_stmt == NULL` and no GIMPLE body; `cgraph_update_edges_for_call_stmt` (requiring `!orig->thunk`) and `create_edge_including_clones` must skip clones with `node->thunk` set. In `expand_thunk` (`symtab-thunks.cc`), result-adjusting thunks (`!this_adjusting && (fixed_offset || virtual_offset)`) with `POINTER_TYPE` `restmp` must guard `thunk_adjust` with a `NE_EXPR` null check. [F61, F62, F311]
- When creating clones (`create_clone`, `create_virtual_clone`, `create_version_clone_with_body`, `set_new_clone_decl_and_node_flags`), per-body flags (e.g., `has_omp_variant_constructs`) must be copied, `DECL_RTL` cleared, and `DECL_VIRTUAL_P`, `DECL_STATIC_CONSTRUCTOR`/`DESTRUCTOR`, `DECL_SET_IS_OPERATOR_NEW`/`DELETE`, and `DECL_IS_REPLACEABLE_OPERATOR` cleared on `new_decl`. [F24, F32, F60]
- Clone creation may copy `x_section` only when `!implicit_section`, and `cgraph_node::make_local` must clear `section` and `comdat_group`. `symtab_node::set_section` and `resolve_alias` must keep a symbol and its aliases in the same section and COMDAT group; `ipa_comdats`/`propagate_comdat_group` must not localize symbols with an explicit `get_section ()`, across different `symtab_type`s, or separately from their thunks (mapped via `function_symbol ()`). [F50, F51, F307]
- Functions with `DECL_STATIC_CONSTRUCTOR` or `DECL_STATIC_DESTRUCTOR` must be rejected by `cgraph_node_cannot_be_local_p_1`, `only_called_directly_or_aliased_p`, `can_remove_if_no_direct_calls_and_refs_p`, and `ipa_comdats`. In `set_const_flag_1` and `set_nothrow_flag_1` (under `flag_non_call_exceptions`), `TREE_READONLY` and `TREE_NOTHROW`/`can_throw_external = false` require `node->binds_to_current_def_p ()` (downgrading non-binding or `virtual_offset_p` thunk callers to `set_pure_flag`). [F46, F47, F48]
- In `cgraph_edge::redirect_call_stmt_to_callee`, expanding a speculative direct edge via `gimple_ic` must not clear `indirect->speculative` or set `indirect->count` to the BB count until `num_speculative_call_targets` reaches 0; if a call becomes noreturn and loses an `SSA_NAME` LHS, that `SSA_NAME` must be bound to a new temp var, given a `gimple_build_nop ()` def stmt, and registered via `set_ssa_default_def`. [F41, F44]
- In `ipa-cp.cc`, `ipa_argagg_value` lists must be strictly ordered by ascending `(index, unit_offset)` without duplicates; shifting aggregate offsets across ancestor jump functions or lattices (`merge_aggregate_lattices`, `push_adjusted_values`, `push_agg_values_from_plats`) must skip entries whose adjusted offset is negative; and `merge_agg_lats_step` must cap `dest_plats->aggs_count` at `param_ipa_max_agg_items`. [F65, F77, F116]
- In `ipa-cp.cc`, if `!ipcp_versionable_function_p (cs->caller)` and `src_lat` is not a single constant (`contains_variable || values_count > 1`), scalar, context, and aggregate lattices must not propagate from `src_lat`. Scalar constant lattices and `known_csts` must never hold `TREE_BINFO` nodes. In `ipcp_bits_lattice`, `m_value` must have unknown bits cleared (`(m_value & m_mask) == 0`) whenever `m_lattice_val == IPA_BITS_CONSTANT`; `propagate_bits_across_jump_function` must use `op_type` precision/sign, and `meet_with_1` must apply `m_value &= ~m_mask` after all `m_mask` updates including `cap_mask`. [F101, F108, F120, F123]
- In `ipa-devirt.cc`, `get_odr_type` pushes only `polymorphic_type_binfo_p` bases into `val->bases` and maintains `val->id > base_id` by zeroing `odr_types[val->id]` and appending `val` at a new `id` while `val->derived_types` is empty; therefore any loop over `odr_types` must guard against `NULL` entries, and loops matching `TYPE_BINFO` bases against `val->bases` must increment a separate index `j` only for polymorphic bases. [F70, F71, F93]
- `possible_polymorphic_call_targets` canonicalizes its key (`TYPE_MAIN_VARIANT (otr_type)`, prevailing `get_odr_type (..., true)->type` for `context.outer_type` and `speculative_outer_type` under `in_lto_p`, cleared unused speculation fields, and `key.n_odr_types = odr_types.length ()`), and its cache (`polymorphic_call_target_hash` and `cached_polymorphic_call_targets`) must be flushed via `free_polymorphic_call_targets_hash` on ODR graph updates, removal of cached virtual functions (`!thunk_expansion`) or anonymous-namespace vtables, or `final_warning_records` init. [F79, F88, F118]
- In `ipa-fnsummary.cc`, index 0 of `size_time_table` and non-empty `call_size_time_table` must hold the unconditional `true` predicate entry, and `analyze_function_body` must first run `find_necessary_statements` to skip statements without `STMT_NECESSARY` (clearing `GIMPLE_COND`s guarding `BUILT_IN_UNREACHABLE`/`TRAP`). In `inline_call`, `ipa_merge_fn_summary_after_inlining (e)` must precede `ipa_propagate_indirect_call_infos (curr, new_edges)`, and disabled `flag_strict_aliasing` or `flag_assume_sane_operators_new_delete` in `callee->decl` must be propagated to `DECL_FUNCTION_SPECIFIC_OPTIMIZATION (to->decl)`. [F126, F169, F173, F174]
- In `ipa-icf`, `congruence_class::members` must be populated via `sem_item_optimizer::add_item_to_class` so `item->index_in_class` matches its vector index; `sem_function::get_hash`, `equals_wpa`, and `equals_private` must all check `DECL_STATIC_CHAIN`; and `func_checker::compare_gimple_call` must verify `gimple_call_must_tail_p`, `types_compatible_p` on `gimple_call_fntype` and variadic/unprototyped/asm operands, and `ipa_jump_functions_equivalent_p` on non-internal call edges. `compare_operand` on `OP_MEMORY` must check `safe_for_total_scalarization_p` when `compare_ao_refs == 0`. [F127, F177, F181, F183, F185, F186]
- In `ipa-free-lang-data.cc`, `free_lang_data_in_cgraph` must run `assign_assembler_name_if_needed` on all `fld->decls`, then `free_lang_data_in_decl` on all decls, and only then `free_lang_data_in_type` on `fld->types` (and `free_lang_data` must not reset `lang_hooks.types_compatible_p`). `free_lang_data_in_decl` must not strip `DECL_CONTEXT` via `fld_decl_context` on `FIELD_DECL`, `DECL_VIRTUAL_P` functions/vars, or `DECL_CXX_DESTRUCTOR_P` functions, and `FUNCTION_TYPE` `TYPE_ARG_TYPES` must have `const`/`volatile` stripped via `build_qualified_type` before `fld_simplified_type`. [F160, F161, F164, F166]
- `lto_prepare_function_for_streaming` and `input_function` must assign `gimple_uid`s to non-virtual PHIs and BB statements in identical order before removing debug statements or numbering virtual PHIs (`fixup_call_stmt_edges_1` indexes `stmts[lto_stmt_uid - 1]`). `output_struct_function_base` and `input_struct_function_base` must pack and unpack `struct function` flags in exact lockstep. During `lto_read_tree_1`, `DECL_INITIAL` of a non-function/non-TU decl must be read via `lto_input_tree_1` (not `stream_read_tree`/`lto_input_tree`) so `dref_queue` is not flushed mid-SCC. [F191, F234, F246]
- `profile_count` and `profile_probability` scaling and addition must use overflow-checked `safe_scale_64bit`/`slow_safe_scale_64bit` and clamp `m_val` to `max_count` (`(1ULL << 61) - 2`) or `max_probability` (`1U << 27`) so values never reach `uninitialized_count`/`uninitialized_probability`. In `predict.cc`, `expr_expected_value_1` on an `SSA_NAME` must insert `{NULL_TREE, PRED_UNCONDITIONAL, -1}` into `ssa_expected_value` before recursing and re-fetch the slot after recursion before writing. [F261, F263, F293, F298]
- In `profile.cc`, `get_exec_counts`, `read_profile_edge_counts`, and `instrument_edges` must traverse BBs/edges in identical order and advance their counter index only when `!EDGE_INFO (e)->ignore && !EDGE_INFO (e)->on_tree`; in `branch_prob`, every outgoing edge of a BB ending with a `gimple_call_must_tail_p` call (checked under `cfun->has_musttail` via `gsi_last_nondebug_bb`, not `gsi_last_bb`) must have `EDGE_INFO (e)->ignore = 1`. [F253, F320, F321]

## Synchronization
- In `lto_output`, calling `output_offload_tables` must be guarded by `lto_get_out_decl_state ()->output_offload_tables_p` so offload tables are emitted into only a single partition rather than duplicated across parallel LTRANS partitions. [F247]

## Object Lifetimes
- When freeing a `cgraph_node` or `cgraph_edge` with `m_summary_id != -1`, `symbol_table::release_symbol` and `free_edge` must push `m_summary_id` onto `cgraph_released_summary_ids` or `edge_released_summary_ids` before `ggc_free`. `cgraph_node::ipa_transforms_to_apply` (`GTY((skip))` heap vec) must be freed via `.release ()` in `release_body` and `remove` (and deep-copied via `.copy ()` when cloning). [F12, F17, F18]
- Every pass-list execution in `cgraphunit.cc` and `ifunc_ref_map` usage in `symtab_node::check_ifunc_callee_symtab_nodes` (`symtab.cc`, where `ifunc_ref_map` must be a raw `bitmap` pointer, not file-scope `auto_bitmap`) must be bracketed by `bitmap_obstack_initialize (NULL)` and `bitmap_obstack_release (NULL)`. [F16, F273]
- `symtab->m_thunks`, `m_clones`, and `ipa_fn_summaries` must be destroyed with `ggc_delete` (not `ggc_free` or `delete`) so their destructors release internal containers (and before PCH output, front ends must queue thunks via `thunk_info::register_early` rather than `get_create`); similarly, `odr_name_hasher::remove` and `lto_delete_in_decl_state` must free their `GTY((skip))` / `streams[i]` containers before `ggc_free`. When `ipcp_lattice::add_value` transitions a lattice to bottom, it may return `val->sources` to `ipcp_sources_pool` but must never free the `ipcp_value` objects themselves because other `ipcp_value_source::val` pointers in the SCC may reference them. [F82, F85, F144, F210, F282, F306]

## API Contracts
- `symbol_table::compile` must call `symtab_node::check_ifunc_callee_symtab_nodes ()` before `ipa_passes ()` (marking IFUNC resolvers and their transitive callees with `called_by_ifunc_resolver = true`, and `binds_to_current_def_p` must return `false` when `ifunc_resolver` is set). When `cgraph_edge::redirect_call_stmt_to_callee` rewrites a call with `ipa_param_adjustments::modify_call`, it must move the statement in its EH landing pad without running EH CFG cleanup. [F43, F54, F308]
- In `ipa-cp.cc`, `ipa_get_parm_lattices` must only be called when `!info->ipcp_orig_node` (clones use `known_csts`/`known_contexts` or `ipcp_orig_node`); `ipa_get_jf_arith_result` must evaluate non-`NOP_EXPR` operations in `op_type` and pass results through `ipacp_value_safe_for_type`; and `values_equal_for_ipcp_p` (with non-null args) must be used to compare IPA-CP constant trees. [F68, F121, F124]
- In `ipa-devirt.cc`, `get_odr_type` must normalize `type` to `TYPE_MAIN_VARIANT` and (when `!in_lto_p && !TYPE_STRUCTURAL_EQUALITY_P`) `TYPE_CANONICAL` before `hash_odr_name`; `warn_odr` and `add_type_duplicate` must call `lto_location_cache::current_cache->apply_location_cache ()` before reading `DECL_SOURCE_LOCATION`; and callers of `possible_polymorphic_call_targets` must never `.release ()` the returned `vec<cgraph_node *>` or retain it across symbol removals. [F84, F87, F103]
- Binary operations and comparisons on `profile_count` require `compatible_p` (use `.ipa ()` across functions and check `cs->count.compatible_p (desc->count)` in `adjust_clone_incoming_counts`), and cross-function `apply_scale (num, den)` requires normalizing via `profile_count::adjust_for_ipa_scaling (&num, &den)` first. In `branch_prob` (`profile.cc`), all `gcov_write_*` calls (including `output_location`) must be guarded by `if (output_to_file)`. [F112, F294, F296, F322]

## Known Pitfalls
- Loops mutating speculative call targets (`set_call_stmt`, `make_direct`, `set_call_stmt_including_clones`) must advance `next_speculative_call_target ()` before mutating the current edge; `cgraph_edge::make_direct` must match `callee` against `direct->speculative_call_target_ref ()->referred` via `semantically_equivalent_p` (not `direct->callee`). Indexed vector loops that remove elements (`cgraph_edge::rebuild_references`, `handle_alias_pairs`, `merge_and_complain`, `symtab_node::remove_stmt_references`, `resolve_alias`) must not increment the index on iterations that remove the current element. [F2, F5, F42, F190, F252]
- In `ipa-cp.cc`, when `call_passes_through_thunk (cs)` is true (chasing `get_alias_target ()` first), constants, contexts, ranges, bits, and aggregates must not be propagated to parameter 0 (`this`). In `ipa-devirt.cc`, `TYPE_NAME (t)` must be checked for `TREE_CODE == TYPE_DECL` before `DECL_SOURCE_LOCATION`/`decl_function_context`; `BINFO_VTABLE` must unwrap `POINTER_PLUS_EXPR` (`TREE_OPERAND (TREE_OPERAND (vtable, 0), 0)`) before `varpool_node::get`; and `odr_types_equivalent_p` must guard `TYPE_PRECISION` with `!VECTOR_TYPE_P (t1)`. [F97, F102, F105, F114]
- In `ipa-free-lang-data.cc`, `find_decls_types_r` must explicitly push both `ce->index` and `ce->value` of `CONSTRUCTOR_ELTS` (and `TREE_PURPOSE` of `GIMPLE_ASM` operands), and must not push `TYPE_MAX_VALUE_RAW` or `TYPE_BINFO` for record/union types. In LTO streaming, `DFS_write_tree_body` and `hash_tree` must guard `DECL_ASSEMBLER_NAME` with `DECL_ASSEMBLER_NAME_SET_P`, `DECL_VALUE_EXPR` with `(VAR_P || PARM_DECL) && DECL_HAS_VALUE_EXPR_P`, and `DECL_DEBUG_EXPR` with `VAR_P && DECL_HAS_DEBUG_EXPR_P`; and `lto_write_options` must check `x_flag_pie` before `x_flag_pic` and mask `x_flag_cf_protection & ~CF_SET`. [F155, F182, F187, F221, F223, F242, F243]
- `profile_count` and `profile_probability` relational operators (`<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`) return `false` when either operand is `!initialized_p ()`, so `!(a < b)` is not equivalent to `a >= b`. In `gimple_value_profile_transformations`, after a transform returns `true`, `stmt`, `bb`, and `gsi` must be refreshed; and in `gimple_ic`, `gimple_call_fn (icall_stmt)` must be wrapped in `unshare_expr`, `stmt_ends_bb_p` calls must split the fallthru edge instead of `split_block`, and EH landing pads/edges/PHI args must be copied to `dcall_bb`. [F258, F290, F295, F302]
