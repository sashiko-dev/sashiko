# rtl-optimization

This unit implements core Register Transfer Language (RTL) representation and analysis (`rtl.h`, `rtlanal.cc`), algebraic simplification (`simplify-rtx.cc`), instruction recognition and constraint matching (`recog.cc`), `.md`/`.rtl` parsing (`read-rtl.cc`, `read-rtl-function.cc`), and RTL optimization passes including if-conversion (`ifcvt.cc`), IRA regional loop-tree/allocno building (`ira-build.cc`), redundant extension elimination (`ree.cc`), hard-register copy propagation (`regcprop.cc`), store-forwarding avoidance, block reordering/partitioning, and stack-adjustment combining.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `ifcvt.cc`, `bb_ok_for_noce_convert_multiple_sets` must bound `then_bb` to `param_max_rtl_if_conversion_insns` active `SET`s and verify `REG_P (dest)`, `!contains_mem_rtx_p (src)`, `can_conditionally_move_p (GET_MODE (dest))`, and `noce_can_force_operand (src)`. `init_noce_multiple_sets_info` and `noce_convert_multiple_sets_1` must increment `count` only after `active_insn_p (insn)`. Final `info->temporary` to `info->target` moves must iterate `insn_info` in reverse (`FOR_EACH_VEC_ELT_REVERSE`) and record `REGNO (info->target)` in `set_targets`. [F1, F2, F10, F49]
- In `noce_convert_multiple_sets_1`, if any insn in `seq2` satisfies `modified_in_p` for `cc_cmp` or `rev_cc_cmp`, `seq2` must be discarded; if `seq1` is chosen and modifies `cc_cmp` or `rev_cc_cmp`, both must be cleared to `NULL_RTX`. When `reg_overlap_mentioned_p (target, cond)` holds, `temp` must be a new pseudo on `!second_try` or when `count < *last_needs_comparison` (updated to `count` when `seq1` is chosen or `seq2` sets `read_comparison`). Before emitting `seq`, reject if any insn satisfies `JUMP_P`, `CALL_P`, or `recog_memoized (insn) == -1`. [F55, F56, F58]
- In `ifcvt.cc`, `noce_get_condition`, `noce_operand_ok`, `noce_process_if_block`, and `check_cond_move_block` must reject `side_effects_p`; `noce_operand_ok` rejects non-`MEM`s with `may_trap_p`, and `noce_try_cmove_arith`/`noce_try_sign_mask` reject speculated operands with `may_trap_or_fault_p`. `noce_process_if_block` rejects `!set_b && MEM_P (orig_x)` and `BLKmode` destinations/conditions, and `noce_try_move`/`minmax`/`abs` reject `HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS (if_info->x)` (`move`/`minmax` also reject `HONOR_NANS`). [F34, F44, F46, F52, F53]
- When hoisting instructions above `test_bb`'s jump (`noce_process_if_block` moving `insn_b`, `dead_or_predicable` moving `head..end`), `REG_EQUAL` notes on moved insns—and in `dead_or_predicable`, all `REG_EQUAL`/`REG_EQUIV` notes for `merge_set` registers—must be removed. When `dead_or_predicable` reverses the jump (`reversep && other_bb != new_dest`), it must swap branch/fallthru probabilities and call `update_br_prob_note (test_bb)`. If-conversion passes must update `max_regno = max_reg_num ()` when new pseudos are allocated and call `fixup_partitions ()` at exit. [F11, F35, F36, F37]
- In `ifcvt.cc`, when `targetm.small_register_classes_for_mode_p` holds for a hard register's mode, `noce_process_if_block` must not use that hard reg directly as `if_info->x` or keep `insn_b` when `SET_SRC (set_b)` is such a hard reg, and `check_cond_move_block` must reject such destinations. `noce_emit_cmove` `SUBREG` promotion requires `!reload_completed` and matching inner modes, `SUBREG_BYTE`, `SUBREG_PROMOTED_VAR_P`, and signedness. `bbs_ok_for_cmove_arith` requires non-`MEM` `SET_DEST` in `bb_b` to satisfy `REG_P || paradoxical_subreg_p`, and `bb_valid_for_noce_process_p` must unwrap `SUBREG_P (dest)` before checking `REG_P` and overlap with `cond`. [F41, F42, F43, F51]
- In `ira-build.cc`, `finish_loop_tree_nodes` must iterate up to `last_basic_block_before_change` (not `last_basic_block_for_fn (cfun)`), and `ira_flattening` / `rebuild_regno_allocno_maps` must size maps to `max_reg_num ()` (not `max_regno_before_emit`). Appending via `safe_push` to `allocno_vec`, `ira_object_id_map_vec`, `pref_vec`, or `copy_vec` requires updating the global raw pointer (`ira_allocnos`, `ira_object_id_map`, `ira_prefs`, `ira_copies`) and `*_num` from `.address ()` and `.length ()`. Functions accessing loop-node maps must check `node->bb == NULL`, and `mark_loops_for_removal` must never mark the root node (`parent == NULL`). [F3, F8, F9, F19, F27, F39]
- In `create_insn_allocnos`, `outer` must be passed as `x` only for generic `'e'`/`'E'` operands and as `NULL` for `SET`, `CLOBBER`, `MEM`, or auto-inc/dec; every pseudo reference with `outer != NULL && GET_CODE (outer) == SUBREG` must widen `ALLOCNO_WMODE (a)` if `partial_subreg_p (ALLOCNO_WMODE (a), GET_MODE (outer))`, even if `a` already existed. Auto-inc/modify expressions must recurse on `XEXP (x, 0)` twice (`output_p = true` and `false`). When `!ira_conflicts_p`, `ira_build` must add `ira_need_caller_save_regs (a)` to conflicts when `ALLOCNO_CALLS_CROSSED_NUM (a) != 0`. [F28, F40, F45, F47]
- In `recog.cc`, `validate_change_1` must only take its no-op `rtx_equal_p` shortcut when `!in_group`; when `in_group` is true, it must record the change and `old_code = INSN_CODE (object)` so `cancel_changes` restores `INSN_CODE`. `validate_change_1`, `cancel_changes`, and `swap_change` may access `INSN_CODE (object)` only when `object && !MEM_P (object)`, and `confirm_change_group` calls `df_insn_rescan` only when `INSN_P (object)`. `cancel_changes` and `undo_recog_changes` iterate backwards down to `num`, whereas `~undo_recog_changes` iterates forward from `num_changes - s_num_changes` to `num_changes - m_old_num_changes`. [F62, F95, F130]
- In `validate_replace_rtx_1` and `insn_propagation::apply_to_pattern_1`, when traversing a `PARALLEL`, if element `i > 0` is a `SET` with `ASM_OPERANDS` `SET_SRC`, recursion must skip `SET_SRC`. `verify_changes` and `insn_propagation::apply_to_rvalue_1` must reject replacing a `register_asm_p` hard register in an inline `asm` (and `copyprop_hardreg_forward_1` must skip `is_asm` operands where `REGNO (op) == ORIGINAL_REGNO (op)`). `insn_invalid_p` must reject instructions where a `REG_INC` note register overlaps any stored register (`check_invalid_inc_dec`). [F97, F114, F134]
- `insn_propagation::apply_to_rvalue_1` must reject substituting `from` inside `RTX_AUTOINC` when `reg_overlap_mentioned_p (XEXP (x, 0), from)` holds (and `replace_oldest_value_addr` must never replace inside `RTX_AUTOINC`); when replacing a hard register `x` in a different mode than `from`, it requires `REG_NREGS (x) == REG_NREGS (from)`, `REG_CAN_CHANGE_MODE_P`, `CONSTANT_P (to)` if `paradoxical_subreg_p (GET_MODE (x), GET_MODE (from))`, and no `SUBREG` in `newval`. `general_operand` must reject hard-reg `SUBREG`s failing `REG_CAN_CHANGE_MODE_P` and paradoxical float `SUBREG`s when `!lra_in_progress`. [F115, F117, F133]
- In `regcprop.cc`, `kill_value_regno` must kill `[regno, regno + nregs)` and scan `j` in `[max(0, regno - vd->max_value_regs), regno)`, killing all `n = hard_regno_nregs (j, vd->e[j].mode)` registers of `j` whenever `j + n > regno`. On a `CALL_INSN` with a `SET` in `CALL_INSN_FUNCTION_USAGE`, `copyprop_hardreg_forward_1` must skip `[set_regno, set_regno + set_nregs)` in ABI clobbers while still running `kill_clobbered_values`. `copy_value` must ignore copies to fixed/global/stack/frame registers or overlapping ranges, and when `partial_subreg_p (vd->e[sr].mode, GET_MODE (src))` holds, require `REG_CAN_CHANGE_MODE_P` and record `vd->e[sr].mode`. [F64, F65, F126]
- In `regcprop.cc`, `maybe_mode_change` returns `stack_pointer_rtx` for `STACK_POINTER_REGNUM` only when `orig_mode == new_mode && new_mode == GET_MODE (stack_pointer_rtx)` (callers must not mutate `ORIGINAL_REGNO`, `REG_ATTRS`, or `REG_POINTER` on `stack_pointer_rtx`), and requires `mode_change_ok` for both `regno` and `copy_regno`. `find_oldest_value_reg` must check `REG_CAN_CHANGE_MODE_P`, and `copyprop_hardreg_forward_1` must skip register move replacements that increase `targetm.register_move_cost`. `cprop_hardreg_bb` must not inherit `value_data` across `EDGE_ABNORMAL_CALL | EDGE_EH` edges and must clear inherited `debug_insn_changes` pointers without calling `free_debug_insn_changes`. [F89, F101, F108, F125, F137]
- In `ree.cc`, `add_removable_extension` must reject candidates whose source `reg` is in `fixed_regs`, is an uninitialized `ZERO_EXTEND` (`!bitmap_bit_p (init_regs, REGNO (reg))`), is a `VECTOR_MODE_P` extension with another non-debug use, or is a multi-reg extension with `reg_overlap_mentioned_p (dest, reg)`. `get_sub_rtx` must reject any `PARALLEL` with more than one `REG_P`-destination `SET`. On `WORD_REGISTER_OPERATIONS` targets, `combine_reaching_defs` must reject `copy_needed` extensions of sub-word definitions if any `!DEBUG_INSN_P` reaching use is a `paradoxical_subreg_p`. [F99, F122, F123, F124]
- In `rtlanal.cc`, `refers_to_regno_p` and `reg_overlap_mentioned_p` must compute a `SUBREG`'s exclusive upper bound as `regno + subreg_nregs (x)` for hard regs and `regno + 1` for pseudos. Writing to a `SUBREG` preserves part of `SUBREG_REG` (`read_modify_subreg_p`) iff the inner mode size exceeds both the outer mode size and `REGMODE_NATURAL_SIZE`. `strip_address_mutations` strips a lowpart `SUBREG` when `!OBJECT_P (SUBREG_REG (*loc)) || CONSTANT_P (SUBREG_REG (*loc))`, `valid_base_or_index_term_p` unwraps `(plus X const_scalar_int)` to `X`, and `decompose_normal_address` prefers the `PLUS` operand matching `targetm.addr_space.address_mode (info->as)` as base before `baseness`. [F141, F181, F182, F189, F202]
- In `nonzero_bits1` and `num_sign_bit_copies1`, `nonzero_bits1` must never call `num_sign_bit_copies`, and any binary arithmetic code recursing on both operands must be in `nonzero_bits_binary_arith_p` / `num_sign_bit_copies_binary_arith_p`. `word_register_operation_p` must return `false` for `CONST_INT`, `ROTATE`, `ROTATERT`, `SIGN_EXTRACT`, and `ZERO_EXTRACT`. `POINTERS_EXTEND_UNSIGNED` applies to `REG_POINTER (x)` (and `ptr_mode`-to-`Pmode` extensions in `simplify_unary_operation_1`) only when `target_default_pointer_address_modes_p () && !targetm.have_ptr_extend ()`. `truncated_to_mode` requires `num_sign_bit_copies (x, GET_MODE (x)) >= num_sign_bit_copies_in_rep[GET_MODE (x)][mode] + 1`. [F175, F184, F198, F199, F285]
- In `simplify-rtx.cc`, any simplification that drops or collapses an operand must verify `!side_effects_p` on the eliminated RTX. `simplify_associative_operation` must abort when `++assoc_count >= max_assoc_count` and keep recursive calls on the same `simplify_context`. `simplify_subreg` folds a hard-reg `SUBREG` only when `simplify_subreg_regno` returns a `HARD_REGISTER_NUM_P` (copying `ORIGINAL_REGNO` only at `subreg_lowpart_offset`), and collapses nested `SUBREG`s only when `ordered_p (outersize, innermostsize)` and `final_offset` is valid. `simplify_immed_subreg` on `FLOAT_MODE_P (outermode)` must re-encode the decoded RTX with `native_encode_rtx` and verify all bytes match. [F260, F277, F278, F279, F283]
- In `simplify-rtx.cc`, `simplify_const_vector_subreg` rejects paradoxical subregs, stepped `CONST_VECTOR`s when inner modes differ, and non-multiple variable-length `outermode`s, and clamps `out_npatterns * nelts_per_pattern` to `const_nunits` for constant-length `outermode`. `native_encode_rtx` and `native_decode_vector_rtx` must use `vector_element_size (GET_MODE_PRECISION (mode), GET_MODE_NUNITS (mode))` (packing sub-byte `MODE_VECTOR_BOOL` elements). In `simplify_const_binary_operation`, `builder.new_binary_operation` may pass `step_ok_p = true` only when both operands are stepped with `PLUS`/`MINUS` or one is stepped and `distributes_over_addition_p` holds; float folding must honor `flag_trapping_math` and `flag_rounding_math`. Folding `0 / x` in `UDIV`/`DIV` requires `!cfun->can_throw_non_call_exceptions`. [F262, F267, F270, F275, F280, F288, F289]

## API Contracts

- While `undo_recog_changes::is_active ()` is true, code must never call `validate_change_1` (`validate_change`, `validate_unshare_change`, `validate_change_xveclen`), `confirm_change_group`, or `cancel_changes`, and any nested `undo_recog_changes (num)` must have `num <= num_changes - s_num_changes`. In `copyprop_hardreg_forward_1`, if a tentative `validate_change` fails before the operand loop reading `recog_data` and `which_op_alt ()`, `extract_constrain_insn` and `preprocess_constraints` must be re-run. `insn_propagation::apply_to_note` must save and restore `INSN_CODE (insn)`, and temporary changes to `volatile_ok` must use `temporary_volatile_ok`. [F68, F69, F107, F131, F132]
- In `rtl.h` and `rtlanal.cc`, changing a `REG`'s mode in place must use `PUT_MODE` (not `PUT_MODE_RAW`) to update `REG_NREGS`. The 4-argument `refers_to_regno_p` takes a half-open range `[regno, endregno)` (`endregno = regnum + 1` for one register). `subreg_get_info` does not check `REG_CAN_CHANGE_MODE_P` or `HARD_REGISTER_NUM_P`, so callers folding a hard-reg `SUBREG` must call `simplify_subreg_regno` (after `validate_subreg` if synthesizing a subreg). `note_pattern_stores` strips outer `SUBREG`s of pseudos but passes `SUBREG`s of hard registers directly to the callback. [F140, F186, F188, F190, F204]
- Removing `REG_EQUAL` or `REG_EQUIV` notes must use `remove_note` or `remove_reg_equal_equiv_notes` (`no_rescan = false`) so `df_notes_rescan` updates `DF_REG_EQ_USE_CHAIN`. Speculative code motion must check `may_trap_or_fault_p (x)` rather than `may_trap_p (x)` or `MEM_NOTRAP_P (x)`. `rtx_properties::try_to_add_dest` on `STACK_POINTER_REGNUM` must also record an `rtx_obj_reference` for `MEM_REGNO` (`BLKmode`), `try_to_add_insn` on a `CALL_INSN` does not include ABI call clobbers (query `insn_callee_abi`), and `vec_rtx_properties_base` must never be copied by value. [F158, F159, F170, F171, F194, F201]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `ifcvt.cc`, when `noce_process_if_block` replaces `orig_x` with a temporary pseudo `x`, the `!else_bb && set_b` retry path must reset `b = orig_x` (not `x`), and when `else_bb` is NULL, `if_info->insn_b` is either NULL or in `test_bb`. Callers of `cond_exec_get_condition` (`noce_convert_multiple_sets_1`, `cond_exec_process_insns`) must `copy_rtx` the returned condition before passing it to expanders or multiple insns; `XEXP (SET_SRC (pc_set (jump)), 2)` must be checked for `GET_CODE (...) == LABEL_REF` before `label_ref_label`; and `set_used_flags` must be called on operands and insns before `unshare_all_rtl_in_chain`. [F7, F22, F23, F26, F57]
- In `rtlanal.cc`, `auto_inc_p` is false for `stack_pointer_rtx` (no `REG_INC` notes are attached), so `reg_set_p` must scan `MEM` addresses for `RTX_AUTOINC`. Frame/arg pointer checks in `rtx_unstable_p`, `rtx_varies_p`, and `nonzero_address_p` must test pointer identity (`frame_pointer_rtx`, `hard_frame_pointer_rtx`, `arg_pointer_rtx` with `fixed_regs[ARG_POINTER_REGNUM]`), not `REGNO`, and `nonzero_bits1` must only use `REGNO_POINTER_ALIGN` on stack/frame/arg pointers. In `nonzero_bits1` and `num_sign_bit_copies1`, high bits of an unpromoted paradoxical `SUBREG` under `WORD_REGISTER_OPERATIONS` are preserved only when `MEM_P (SUBREG_REG (x))` holds. [F176, F185, F191, F193]
- In `rtlanal.cc`, `may_trap_p_1` must check `HONOR_NANS`/`HONOR_SNANS` on `XEXP (x, 0)` and `XEXP (x, 1)` as well as `x` for comparisons, must not treat `PARALLEL`, `NEG`, `ABS`, `SUBREG`, or `VEC_*` as trapping solely due to `FLOAT_MODE_P && flag_trapping_math`, and must treat variable-length `CONST_VECTOR` divisors as trapping unless `CONST_VECTOR_DUPLICATE_P` holds with no `const0_rtx` elements. `set_noop_p` must verify `!side_effects_p` on both operands before `rtx_equal_p`; `vec_duplicate_p` must check `!VECTOR_MODE_P (GET_MODE (XEXP (x, 0)))`; `replace_rtx` must fold `CONST_SCALAR_INT_P` substitutions into `SUBREG_REG` or `ZERO_EXTEND` using the original inner mode before overwriting; and `same_vector_encodings_p` must check `CONST_VECTOR_NPATTERNS` and `CONST_VECTOR_NELTS_PER_PATTERN` on variable-length vectors. [F178, F179, F180, F183, F196, F203, F205]
