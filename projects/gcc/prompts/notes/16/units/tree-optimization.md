# tree-optimization

This unit implements GCC's GIMPLE SSA middle-end optimization and diagnostic passes, including the Ranger value-range framework and range operators, the tree alias oracle, scalar evolution (SCEV), loop optimizations, and scalar/control-flow passes.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `gswitch` label 0 is the default label (no `CASE_LOW`); non-default labels are in `[1, gimple_switch_num_labels)`. `simplify_gimple_switch_label_vec`, `clean_up_after_unswitching`, `find_unswitching_predicates_for_bb`, `gimple_outgoing_range::switch_edge_range`/`calc_switch_ranges`, and `find_case_label_range` must start non-default label loops at 1, and `clean_up_after_unswitching` must never remove `default_e`. `switch_edge_range` returns `false` if label 1's `CASE_LOW` and the switch index differ in `TYPE_PRECISION`, while `find_unswitching_predicates_for_bb` and `find_case_label_range` must convert label bounds/ranges when types differ. [F1, F83, F126, F348, F349, F383, F385, F448, F498]
- Statements hoisted or with guards relaxed across conditionals (`ifcombine_replace_cond`, `move_computations_worker`, `phiopt`'s `move_stmt`/`value_replacement`/`minmax_replacement`/`cond_removal_in_builtin_zero_pattern`) or modified in `backprop::prepare_change` must call `reset_flow_sensitive_info` on defined SSA names; `ifcombine_replace_cond`, `move_computations_worker`, `final_value_replacement_loop`, and `split_loop` must also rewrite `TYPE_OVERFLOW_UNDEFINED` integer arithmetic via `ifcombine_rewrite_to_defined_overflow` or `rewrite_to_defined_overflow`. [F57, F117, F213, F317, F378, F420]
- In `irange`, `VR_VARYING` is defined by precision bounds `[wi::min_value, wi::max_value]` with `m_bitmask.unknown_p ()` (not `TYPE_MIN_VALUE`/`TYPE_MAX_VALUE`), and `irange_bitmask` requires `wi::bit_and (m_mask, m_value) == 0` (`update_known_bitmask` must clear `value &= ~mask`). Because `irange::maybe_resize` only reallocates when `m_resizable` and caps at `HARD_MAX_RANGES`, multi-range writers (`operator=`, `union_append`, `union_`, `intersect`) must clamp to `m_max_ranges` and collapse excess upper bounds into the last pair. [F202, F455, F501, F502]
- For `frange`, `MODE_COMPOSITE_P` modes must convert zero/denormal values to `DFmode` before `real_nextafter` in `frange_nextafter`/`frange_arithmetic`, widen `frange_arithmetic` by 1/2/3 extra ulps (`PLUS`/`MINUS`, `MULT`, `RDIV`), skip `frange_nextafter` narrowing in `build_lt`/`build_gt`, and return `false` from `frange::internal_singleton_p` if `m_min` is infinite or exact in `DFmode`. Under `flag_trapping_math && MODE_HAS_INFINITIES`, finite operations overflowing to singleton `+/-INF` in `range_operator::fold_range` must be widened to include `real_min/max_representable`. [F152, F168, F169, F170, F500]
- In `pointer_plus_operator::fold_range` and `fold_using_range::range_of_address` (`&MEM_REF[ssa, off]`), folding to non-null requires `!TYPE_OVERFLOW_WRAPS`; if the offset can be negative (`wi::sign_mask (rh_ub)` or `!known_gt (off, 0)`) or only the base is non-zero in `range_of_address`, `flag_delete_null_pointer_checks` must also hold. In `operator_addr_expr::op1_range`, a non-zero LHS constrains `op1` to non-zero only when `TYPE_OVERFLOW_UNDEFINED (type)` holds. [F128, F158, F159]
- Under `HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS`, `record_equality` (`tree-ssa-dom.cc`) and `associate_equivalences_with_edges` (`tree-ssa-uncprop.cc`) may only record an equality with a non-zero `REAL_CST`, and `record_edge_info` must set `can_infer_simple_equiv = false` when `DECIMAL_FLOAT_MODE_P` or `HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS (op) && real_maybe_zerop (op)`. Under `HONOR_NANS`, `valid_lattice_transition` (`tree-ssa-ccp.cc`) must not transition a NaN constant to a non-NaN constant, and `minmax_replacement` (`tree-ssa-phiopt.cc`) must emit `COND_EXPR` instead of `MIN_EXPR`/`MAX_EXPR` when `HONOR_NANS || HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS`. [F46, F66, F285, F445, F491]
- In `gimple_ranger::range_of_expr`, debug statements (`is_gimple_debug`) must query `m_cache.range_of_expr` (`RFD_NONE`) rather than `range_of_stmt` or `range_on_entry`. In `ranger_cache` and `gimple_ranger`, `infer_oracle ()` ranges must not be applied across `EDGE_EH | EDGE_ABNORMAL` edges or when `has_abnormal_call_or_eh_pred_edge_p (start_bb)` holds; `set_global_range` must call `m_temporal->set_timestamp (name)`, and `register_inferred_value` must call `gori_ssa ()->set_range_invariant (name, false)` when `!gori ().has_edge_range_p (name)`. [F123, F124, F125]
- `path_range_query` stores `m_path` in reverse execution order (`exit_bb ()` at `0`, `entry_bb ()` at `length () - 1`). `ssa_range_in_phi` and `maybe_register_phi_relation` must not use `m_cache` or record equivalences when `ssa_defined_in_bb (arg, bb)` holds, and `compute_ranges_in_block` must clear cached ranges of exit dependencies defined in `bb`, call `killing_def` on redefinitions, and reset the path oracle (`reset_path ()`) across `relations_may_be_invalidated (e)` backedges. [F79, F119, F120]
- In `tree-ssa-loop-im.cc`, `can_sm_ref_p` must reject `tree_could_throw_p` references and require `ref_always_accessed_p (loop, ref, true)` when `tree_could_trap_p` or `TREE_READONLY (base)` holds; `execute_sm` must enable `multi_threaded_model_p` when `maybe_mt` holds and `bb_in_transaction` or `ref_can_have_store_data_races && !always_stored`; and WAW store disambiguation in `sm_seq_push_down`/`ref_indep_loop_p` must pass `tbaa_p = false` to `refs_independent_p`. [F319, F320, F322]
- Every `.cc` file must `#include "config.h"` before `"system.h"`, and C++ standard headers (`<algorithm>`, `<string>`) must be requested via `#define INCLUDE_ALGORITHM` or `#define INCLUDE_STRING` before `"system.h"` to avoid `safe-ctype.h` macro poisoning. In `tree-ssa-ifcombine.cc`, `#include "rtl.h"` must precede `"tm_p.h"` (for ARM `RTX_CODE` guards), and `"backend.h"`/`"target.h"` must precede `#ifndef` target macro fallbacks in `tree-ssa-ifcombine.cc`, `tree-ssa-loop-prefetch.cc`, and `tree-ssa-math-opts.cc`. [F17, F18, F92, F138, F237, F356, F357, F407, F408, F462]

## Object Lifetimes

- File-scoped and pass-level non-RAII state must be explicitly cleaned up before `execute` returns: bitmaps/vectors in `pass_dominator::execute`, `pass_dse::execute` (including `free_data_ref` on every entry in `dse_stmt_to_dr_map`), `pass_forwprop::execute`, `tree_dce_init`/`tree_dce_done`, `tree_optimize_tail_calls_1` (`destroy_live_vars`, `delete live_vars`, `BITMAP_FREE (tailr_arg_needs_copy)`), `pass_uncprop::execute` (freeing and nulling `e->aux`), and `pass_waccess`/`pass_wrestrict::execute` (`m_ptr_qry.flush_cache ()`, `disable_ranger`, `m_ptr_qry.rvals = NULL`, `m_clobbers.empty ()`, `m_bb_uids_set = NULL`). [F20, F22, F139, F140, F245, F468, F470]
- Non-RAII vectors and structs require explicit member release: `edge_info::~edge_info` must `.release ()` its `vec` members and `e->aux` must be freed via `free_dom_edge_info`; `scc_copy_prop::propagate` and `discover_iteration_bound_by_body_walk` must call `.release ()` on each inner `vec` element; `args_loc_t` must deep-copy `locvec` in copy/assign and `.release ()` it in `operator=`/`~args_loc_t`; `delete_var_map`, `ssa_conflicts_delete`, `free_lim_aux_data`, and `memref_free` (before freeing `mem_ref_obstack`) must release embedded vectors/obstacks; and `delete_coalesce_list` requires `cost_one_list == NULL` and `num_sorted == 0`. [F19, F24, F94, F95, F243, F244, F298, F299, F359]
- In Ranger and liveness, `block_range_cache` placement-news `ssa_block_ranges` in `m_range_allocator` (never `delete` elements of `m_ssa_ranges`); `phi_analyzer::process_phi` transfers `m_current` to `new phi_group` and must allocate a fresh `BITMAP_ALLOC (&m_bitmaps)` for `m_current` (while `~phi_analyzer` deletes all `m_phi_groups` elements); `ssa_lazy_cache::~ssa_lazy_cache` only releases `m_bitmaps` when `m_ob == &m_bitmaps` (otherwise `BITMAP_FREE (active_p)`); and `delete_tree_live_info`/`calculate_live_ranges` must pair `free (live->livein/liveout)` with `bitmap_obstack_release`. [F26, F97, F98, F99, F102]
- Jump-threading paths (`allocate_thread_path`) placement-new a `vec<jump_thread_edge *>` onto `m_obstack` with a heap buffer, so every discarded or completed path must have `path->release ()` or `cancel_thread` called (and `e->aux` cleared in `fwd_jt_path_registry`). `use_optype_d` nodes (`tree-ssa-operands.cc`) must never be freed individually (delink via `delink_imm_use` and chain onto `free_uses`), and in `pass_forwprop::execute`, `simple_dce_from_worklist` must run before `to_remove_defs` (which stores `SSA_NAME_VERSION` indices, not `gimple *`). [F53, F412, F464, F466]

## API Contracts

- When a compacted view is active on `_var_map` (`tree-ssa-live.cc`), `var_to_partition` and `version_to_var` must translate `partition_find` through `map->partition_to_view` (`NO_PARTITION` if unselected), while `partition_to_var` must translate a view index through `map->view_to_partition` before `partition_find`. `partition_view_fini` takes ownership of and frees its `selected` bitmap, and the `vec<bitmap_head>` from `compute_live_vars` must be freed via `destroy_live_vars`. [F4, F25, F27]
- `gimple_range_ssa_p` requires a non-null `SSA_NAME` that is neither `SSA_NAME_IS_VIRTUAL_OPERAND` nor `SSA_NAME_OCCURS_IN_ABNORMAL_PHI` and satisfies `value_range::supports_type_p`. `gimple_range_type` must null-check `gimple_call_fntype (s)` on LHS-less calls before reading `TREE_TYPE`. `range_op_handler` methods (`fold_range`, `op1_range`, `op2_range`) must check `!undefined_p ()` before calling `vrange::type ()` on any input range. [F107, F108, F200]
- `vrange_storage::set_vrange` and `irange_storage::set_irange` require `fits_p (r)` before overwriting in place; `irange_storage::get_irange` must check `r.m_max_ranges >= m_num_ranges` before bulk-copying pairs (otherwise rebuilding via `r.union_`); and `frange_storage::get_frange` must reconstruct via the `frange (type, m_min, m_max, m_kind)` constructor (mapping `VR_NAN` to `set_undefined ()` if `!HONOR_NANS`). [F456, F457, F507]
- Callers of `get_continuation_for_phi` must pass a non-null `bitmap *visited` and call `BITMAP_FREE (*visited)` afterward (and pass `TR_DISAMBIGUATE` to `maybe_skip_until` on backedges where `phi_bb` dominates `arg1`'s def block); callers passing a non-null `&visited` to `walk_aliased_vdefs` must likewise call `BITMAP_FREE (visited)`. Both `walk_non_aliased_vuses` and `maybe_skip_until` must check `(int)limit <= 0` and decrement `limit` before every `stmt_may_clobber_ref_p_1` call. [F233, F239, F240, F262]
- In `tree-ssa-dse.cc`, `get_byte_range` returns `(start, size)` as a 0-based byte offset and byte count (after `setup_live_bytes_from_ref` verifies `const_size / BITS_PER_UNIT <= param_dse_max_object_size`); `clear_live_bytes_for_ref` passes `(start, size)` to `bitmap_clear_range` (count), whereas `live_bytes_read` must pass `(start, start + size - 1)` to `bitmap_bit_in_range_p` (inclusive end bit). [F5, F6, F12]

## Error Handling

- Speculative folding must pair `fold_defer_overflow_warnings ()` with `fold_undefer_overflow_warnings` (or `fold_undefer_and_ignore_overflow_warnings`) on every return path in `combine_cond_expr_cond` (`tree-ssa-ifcombine.cc`), `evaluate_stmt` (`tree-ssa-ccp.cc`), `compare_values_warnv` (`tree-vrp.cc`), and niter analysis (`expand_simple_operations`, `number_of_iterations_exit_assumptions`, `loop_niter_by_eval`, `estimate_numbers_of_iterations`, `loop_exits_before_overflow`). [F30, F248, F370, F483]
- Early exits inside pass helpers must not skip local resource or CFG-marker cleanup: `analyze_scalar_evolution`/`instantiate_scev`/`resolve_mixers` (`delete global_cache`), `simplify_peeled_chrec` (`free_affine_expand_cache`), `tree_estimate_loop_size` (`free (body)`), `tree_ssa_iv_optimize_loop` (`goto finish`), `loop_prefetch_arrays` (`goto fail` for `release_mem_refs`), `do_split_loop_on_cond`/`tree_unswitch_single_loop` (`free_original_copy_tables`), `cond_if_else_store_replacement` (`free_data_refs`/`free_dependence_relations`), `execute_cse_reciprocals_1` (`goto out` for `free_bb`), `find_tail_calls` (`BITMAP_FREE (local_live_vars)`), `independent_of_stmt_p` (`break` to clear `bb->aux`), `handle_return_addr_local_phi_arg` (`locmap.remove ((gimple*)-1)`), and `pass_waccess::check_pointer_uses` (`delete phi_map`). [F96, F141, F190, F191, F303, F306, F362, F366, F409, F410, F469, F471]

## Known Pitfalls

- Tree expressions duplicated or gimplified into the IR must be unshared via `unshare_expr` (or `unshare_aff_combination`) before insertion or `force_gimple_operand`: in `forward_propagate_addr_expr_1`, `pass_forwprop::execute`/`optimize_vector_load` (which must also set `DECL_NOT_GIMPLE_REG_P` on split `auto_var_p` stores and skip `BIT_FIELD_REF`/`TARGET_MEM_REF` complex loads and `DECL_HARD_REGISTER` vector loads), `increment_start_addr`, `create_mem_ref`, `final_value_replacement_loop`, `ccp_folder::fold_stmt`, `remove_dead_stmt`, `gather_mem_refs_stmt` (also preserving `TREE_THIS_VOLATILE`), `rewrite_use_nonlinear_expr`, `create_iv`, `phiprop_insert_phi`, and `cond_store_replacement`. [F39, F40, F41, F52, F69, F195, F197, F250, F308, F314, F367, F416, F417]
- Reallocating a `vec` or `hash_map` during recursion invalidates references and cached pointers: in `range_def_chain::register_dependency`, re-index `m_def_chain[v]` after `get_def_chain (dep)`/`get_imports (dep)`; in `stmt_semi_invariant_p_1` (`tree-ssa-loop-split.cc`), call `stmt_stat.put (stmt, true)` rather than writing through a pre-recursion `bool &invar` reference; and in `split_loop`, re-fetch any `gphi *` via `SSA_NAME_DEF_STMT (phi_result)` after `loop_version`. [F82, F364, F379]
- Because `ao_ref_base` strips an inner `MEM_REF [&decl]` on `DECL_P` bases, `tree-ssa-alias.cc` helpers (`refs_may_alias_p_1`, `ao_compare::compare_ao_refs`, `ao_ref_base_alias_set`, `ao_ref_base_alias_ptr_type`) must walk `handled_component_p` from `ref->ref` to inspect `MR_DEPENDENCE_CLIQUE`, view conversions, or base alias sets; `nonoverlapping_refs_since_match_p`/`ncr_type_uid` must read `COMPONENT_REF` container types from `DECL_CONTEXT`/`DECL_FIELD_CONTEXT` of `TREE_OPERAND (ref, 1)` (for Fortran common blocks); and `indirect_ref_may_alias_decl_p` must skip the negative-offset check on `TARGET_MEM_REF` bases. [F246, F254, F258]
- Multi-bit boolean types (e.g. Ada) represent `true` with ranges like `[1, MAX]` or `~[0]`, and 1-bit signed types cannot represent `+1`: `range_is_either_true_or_false` and `get_bool_state` must test `!contains_p (zero)` rather than `[1, 1]`, `build_lt`/`build_gt` must use `wi::add/sub (val, -1, SIGNED, &ov)` for signed bounds, and `uaddc_cast`/`match_uaddc_usubc` and `factor_out_conditional_operation` must exclude 1-bit signed or multi-bit `BOOLEAN_TYPE` casts. [F127, F203, F204, F425, F437]
- In `operator_not_equal::fold_range` for `frange`, do not call `frelop_early_resolve` when `trio.op1_op2 () == VREL_EQ` and `maybe_isnan (op1, op2)` is true (or `x != x` folds to false on NaN); and in `frelop_early_resolve`, resolving to `range_true` requires `!maybe_isnan (op1, op2)`. In `operator_cast::op1_range`, guard `[min_val, lim - 1]` on `lim != min_val`, and in `operator_cast::fold_pair`, check `inside_domain_p` before building the outer range. [F154, F155, F205, F206]
