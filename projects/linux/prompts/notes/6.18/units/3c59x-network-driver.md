# 3C59X NETWORK DRIVER

This component is the Linux Ethernet driver for 3Com Fast EtherLink and EtherLink XL adapters (3c59x, 3c900, 3c905, and CardBus/EISA variants) across Vortex, Boomerang, Cyclone, and Tornado ASIC generations, supporting PIO/single-buffer DMA on Vortex and descriptor ring bus-master DMA on Boomerang and later chips.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `vortex_probe1` and `vortex_remove_one`, `vp->rx_ring` and `vp->tx_ring` share one coherent DMA allocation at `vp->rx_ring` (`vp->rx_ring_dma`). `vp->tx_ring` is `(struct boom_tx_desc *)(vp->rx_ring + RX_RING_SIZE)` and `vp->tx_ring_dma` adds `sizeof(struct boom_rx_desc) * RX_RING_SIZE`. Because `vp->rx_ring_dma` and `vp->tx_ring_dma` are byte-addressed `dma_addr_t` values, descriptor DMA address math must scale ring indices by `sizeof(struct boom_rx_desc)` or `sizeof(struct boom_tx_desc)`. [F81, F82]
- In `struct boom_tx_desc` (`DO_ZEROCOPY`), `frag[0]` stores the `dma_map_single()` `skb->data` mapping and `frag[1..nr_frags]` store `skb_shinfo(skb)->frags[0..nr_frags-1]` via `skb_frag_dma_map()`. `LAST_FRAG` (`0x80000000`) is set on the final fragment's `length` and must be masked off via `& 0xFFF` before DMA unmapping. [F57]
- DMA addresses from `dma_map_single()` and `skb_frag_dma_map()` must be checked with `dma_mapping_error()` in CPU byte order before `cpu_to_le32()` conversion into `boom_rx_desc` or `boom_tx_desc`, and descriptor `.addr`/`.length` fields must be converted back with `le32_to_cpu()` before DMA sync/unmap. [F59]
- `TX_RING_SIZE` and `RX_RING_SIZE` must remain powers of two, and `vp->cur_rx`, `vp->cur_tx`, and `vp->dirty_tx` must remain `unsigned int` counters that increment without in-place `%=` so that `(vp->cur_tx - 1) % TX_RING_SIZE`, `vp->cur_tx - vp->dirty_tx`, and `% RX_RING_SIZE` wrap across `0` and `UINT_MAX`. [F56]
- `vortex_set_duplex()` overwrites the entire `Wn3_MAC_Ctrl` register in Window 3 using `vp->large_frames` for bit `0x40`; any code changing oversized-frame support on pre-Cyclone chips (`set_8021q_mode()`) or `dev->mtu` must update `vp->large_frames` in lockstep with `Wn3_MAC_Ctrl`. [F43]
- `vp->status_enable` and `vp->intr_enable` shadow `SetStatusEnb` and `SetIntrEnb` and are rewritten to `EL3_CMD` by `vortex_error()` on `IntReq`. When `work_done` is exhausted in `_vortex_interrupt()` or `_boomerang_interrupt()`, pending status bits are saved in `vp->deferred`, masked via `SetStatusEnb | (~vp->deferred & vp->status_enable)`, and re-triggered when `vortex_timer()` issues `FakeIntr` (`IntReq`), which merges `vp->deferred` and restores `vp->status_enable` in `vortex_error()`. [F48, F70]
- Hardware statistics registers in Window 6 and Window 4 offset 12 (`BadSSD`) are clear-on-read and assert `StatsFull` on overflow; `vortex_up()` and `update_stats()` must read all of them, including `window_read8(vp, 6, 7)` (`Rx packets`), even though software counts `dev->stats.rx_packets`. [F47]
- Every iteration of `while ((rx_status = ioread16(ioaddr + RxStatus)) > 0)` in `vortex_rx()` must issue `RxDiscard` to `EL3_CMD` and wait for `CmdInProgress` in `EL3_STATUS` to clear (via the bounded poll before `continue` or `issue_and_wait(dev, RxDiscard)` on error/drop paths). [F69]
- Per-adapter module parameter arrays (`options`, `full_duplex`, `hw_checksums`, `flow_ctrl`, `enable_wol`, `use_mmio`) have `MAX_UNITS` (8) elements and use `-1` (not `0`) as the unconfigured sentinel alongside their `global_*` counterparts. Every index by `card_idx` must check `card_idx < MAX_UNITS` before falling back to `global_*` or defaults, and check `< 0` or `>= 0` rather than truthiness. [F32, F50]
- Full bus-master TX/RX (`vp->full_bus_master_tx`, `vp->full_bus_master_rx`) and `boomrang_netdev_ops` are enabled in `vortex_probe1()` only when EEPROM word 16 (`vp->capabilities`) has `CapBusMaster` set; `NETIF_F_IP_CSUM | NETIF_F_SG` may only be advertised when `vp->full_bus_master_tx` is enabled. [F101]
- `vp->cb_fn_base` is mapped only in `vortex_probe1()` when `pdev` is non-NULL and `vci->drv_flags` has `HAS_CB_FNS` set; all accesses in `vortex_up()`, `_boomerang_interrupt()`, and `vortex_remove_one()` must check `if (vp->cb_fn_base)`. When non-NULL, `iowrite32(0x8000, vp->cb_fn_base + 4)` must be written in `vortex_up()` and after acknowledging interrupts in `_boomerang_interrupt()`. [F25, F99]
- `media_tbl[]` is indexed by `enum xcvr_types` (`0`..`XCVR_Default = 10`) via `dev->if_port` and `media_tbl[].next`, so `"undefined"` entries at sparse indices `2` and `7` must remain. The media-selection loops in `vortex_up()` and `vortex_timer()` rely on `media_tbl[XCVR_Default].mask == 0xFF` and `(vp->available_media & 0xff) != 0` to terminate. [F55, F71]
- `vortex_info_tbl[]` is indexed by `enum vortex_chips` values stored in `vortex_pci_tbl[].driver_data` and `vortex_eisa_ids[].driver_data`, so all three must be updated in lockstep. Similarly, `VORTEX_NUM_STATS`, the array count of `ethtool_stats_keys` (using `char[ETH_GSTRING_LEN]`), and `data[]` writes in `vortex_get_ethtool_stats` must match. [F39, F85]

## Synchronization

- Lock hierarchy is `rtnl_lock > {vp->lock, vp->mii_lock} > vp->window_lock`. Neither `vp->lock` nor `vp->mii_lock` may be acquired while holding `vp->window_lock` or each other. Broken by: calling `mdio_read()`/`mdio_write()` while holding `vp->lock`. [F13]
- Because `vortex_boomerang_interrupt()` acquires `vp->lock` in hardirq context, all acquisitions of `vp->lock` in process, timer, or softirq context (`boomerang_start_xmit`, `vortex_timer`, `vortex_get_stats`, `vortex_get_ethtool_stats`) must use `spin_lock_irqsave()` or `spin_lock_irq()`. Conversely, `mdio_read()` and `mdio_write()` use `spin_lock_bh(&vp->mii_lock)`, so they and MII helpers (`vortex_check_media()`, `generic_mii_ioctl()`) must never be called from hardirq context or with IRQs disabled. [F18, F109]
- All accesses to banked register offsets `0x00`-`0x0d` must hold `vp->window_lock` and select the window via `window_set(vp, window)` (or `window_read*`/`window_write*`). In `vortex_probe1()`, `vp->window = -1` and `spin_lock_init()` on `vp->lock`, `vp->mii_lock`, and `vp->window_lock` must precede any windowed I/O, MDIO access, or `register_netdev()`. [F21, F88]
- `_vortex_interrupt()` holds `vp->window_lock` with window 7 selected across its work loop so `vortex_rx()` and `DMADone` handling can access `Wn7_*` directly; it must release `vp->window_lock` before calling `vortex_error()` and re-acquire it and call `window_set(vp, 7)` afterward. [F89]
- `_boomerang_interrupt()` must set `vp->handling_irq = 1` on entry and `0` before returning, and `boomerang_start_xmit()` must check `vp->handling_irq` and return `NETDEV_TX_BUSY` before modifying TX ring state or acquiring `vp->lock`. [F16]
- In `boomerang_start_xmit()`, `issue_and_wait(dev, DownStall)`, linking `prev_entry->next`, updating `DownListPtr`, incrementing `vp->cur_tx`, updating BQL/queue state, and `DownUnstall` must all execute under `spin_lock_irqsave(&vp->lock, flags)`. [F17]
- While the device is running with interrupts enabled, callers of `update_stats()` (`vortex_get_stats()`, `vortex_get_ethtool_stats()`) must hold `vp->lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave()` to serialize against `StatsFull` handling in `vortex_error()`. [F19]
- In `vortex_open()` and `vortex_up()`, all RX ring descriptors and mapped skbs in `vp->rx_ring[]`/`vp->rx_skbuff[]`, ring indices, `UpListPtr`/`DownListPtr`, and `vp->timer` must be initialized before enabling hardware interrupts via `SetIntrEnb` and calling `netif_start_queue()`. [F22]

## Object Lifetimes

- `vortex_open` acquires `dev->irq` and populates `vp->rx_skbuff[0..RX_RING_SIZE-1]` before `vortex_up(dev)`. `vortex_close` must call `vortex_down(dev, 1)` (stopping DMA/IRQs and deleting `vp->timer` via `timer_delete_sync`) and `free_irq(dev->irq, dev)` before unmapping and freeing `vp->rx_skbuff[]` and `vp->tx_skbuff[]`. Whenever a `vp->tx_skbuff[entry]` slot is unmapped and freed in `_boomerang_interrupt` or `vortex_close`, it must immediately be set to `NULL`. [F4, F6]
- In `vortex_remove_one` and `vortex_eisa_remove`, `unregister_netdev(dev)` and the `TotalReset` write to `vp->ioaddr + EL3_CMD` must complete before unmapping `vp->ioaddr`, releasing I/O regions, and calling `free_netdev(dev)`. In `vortex_remove_one`, after `unregister_netdev(dev)`, `pdev` must be woken to `PCI_D0` (`pci_set_power_state` and `pci_restore_state`) before issuing `TotalReset`. [F3, F103]

## API Contracts

- Because 3c59x supports PCI, EISA, and Compaq devices (`vp->gendev` is NULL for Compaq), all DMA API calls must pass `vp->gendev` rather than `&VORTEX_PCI(vp)->dev`. Shared code must check `VORTEX_PCI(vp)` or `VORTEX_EISA(vp)` before dereferencing `struct pci_dev`/`eisa_device` or calling `acpi_set_WOL(dev)`. [F23, F86]
- `window_write8/16/32(vp, value, window, addr)` take `value` before `window` and `addr`, whereas `window_read8/16/32(vp, window, addr)` take `(vp, window, addr)`. [F33]
- `vortex_suspend()` calls `netif_device_detach(ndev)` before `vortex_down(ndev, 1)`, and `vortex_resume()` calls `vortex_up(ndev)` before `netif_device_attach(ndev)`; `vortex_close()` and `vortex_get_stats()` must check `netif_device_present(dev)` before touching hardware. [F102]
- In `vortex_ioctl()`, if `VORTEX_PCI(vp)` is non-NULL and its `current_state != PCI_D0`, it must be powered up to `PCI_D0` before `generic_mii_ioctl()` and restored to its prior `state` afterwards. [F52]
- When `vortex_error()` resets the adapter on `HostError`, it must call `vortex_down(dev, 0)` with `final_down == 0` so `vortex_down()` does not call `pci_save_state()` or `acpi_set_WOL()` (`PCI_D3hot`) before `issue_and_wait(dev, TotalReset | 0xff)` and `vortex_up(dev)`. [F90]
- On the `rx_copybreak` path in `boomerang_rx()`, where `vp->rx_skbuff[entry]` remains mapped for device reuse, CPU reads of `vp->rx_skbuff[entry]->data` must be bracketed by `dma_sync_single_for_cpu()` and `dma_sync_single_for_device()` with `DMA_FROM_DEVICE`. [F113]
- In `boomerang_rx`, `skb->ip_summed = CHECKSUM_UNNECESSARY` may be set only when `rx_status & 0xee000000` equals `IPChksumValid | TCPChksumValid` or `IPChksumValid | UDPChksumValid` (ensuring no `IPChksumErr`/`TCPChksumErr`/`UDPChksumErr` bits are set). [F121]
- In Vortex single-buffer bus-master TX (`vortex_start_xmit` with `vp->bus_master`), `Wn7_MasterLen`, `dma_map_single()`, and `dma_unmap_single()` in `_vortex_interrupt()` must use `(skb->len + 3) & ~3`, while BQL accounting uses `skb->len`. Vortex 32-bit PIO FIFO transfers (`iowrite32_rep`/`ioread32_rep`) must use `(len + 3) >> 2` dwords, and `vortex_rx()` must allocate at least `pkt_len + 5` bytes (with `skb_reserve(skb, 2)`). [F37, F76]
- `SetRxThreshold` and `SetTxThreshold` take thresholds in 32-bit dwords (`bytes >> 2`), whereas Boomerang's `TxFreeThreshold` register takes 256-byte units (`bytes >> 8`). [F35]
- `vortex_eisa_init()` returns `> 0` when EISA/Compaq support is registered and `0` when neither is active; `vortex_init()` must check `pci_rc == 0` and `eisa_rc > 0`. `mdio_read()` returns `0xffff` (not a negative errno) when no PHY responds; PHY probing in `vortex_probe1()` must reject both `0` and `0xffff`. [F27, F28]

## Error Handling

- `vortex_init_one` acquires `pci_enable_device`, `pci_request_regions`, `pci_iomap`, and `vortex_probe1`, and on failure must roll them back in reverse order: `pci_iounmap`, `pci_release_regions`, `pci_disable_device`. [F2]
- In `boomerang_start_xmit()`, if `skb_frag_dma_map()` fails at fragment `i`, rollback must unmap `frag[i]..frag[1]` with `dma_unmap_page()` and `frag[0]` with `dma_unmap_single()`. [F30]
- In `boomerang_rx()`, every iteration of `while ((rx_status = le32_to_cpu(vp->rx_ring[entry].status)) & RxDComplete)` not breaking on `rx_work_limit`—including `newskb` or `dma_map_single()` failure—must jump to `clear_complete` to zero `vp->rx_ring[entry].status`, issue `UpUnstall`, and increment `vp->cur_rx`. In the non-copybreak branch, `vp->rx_skbuff[entry]` and `vp->rx_ring[entry].addr` must only be replaced (and old `dma` unmapped) after both `netdev_alloc_skb_ip_align()` and `dma_map_single()` succeed; on mapping error, free `newskb` and keep `vp->rx_skbuff[entry]`. [F67, F68]

## External Contracts

- In `update_stats()`, the 16-bit byte counters in Window 6 registers 10 and 12 and 8-bit TX packet counter in register 6 must be combined with upper bits from Window 4 register 13 (`(up & 0x0f) << 16` for `rx_bytes`, `(up & 0xf0) << 12` for `tx_bytes`) and Window 6 register 9 (`(window_read8(vp, 6, 9) & 0x30) << 4` for `tx_packets`). [F79]
- Whenever `RxReset` is issued to `EL3_CMD` (in `vortex_up` and `HostError` recovery in `vortex_error`), `set_rx_mode(dev)` and `set_8021q_mode(dev, 1)` must be called before `RxEnable` so Cyclone/Tornado ASICs restore `Wn3_MaxPktSize` and `Wn7_VlanEtherType`. In `vortex_up()`, `RxReset` must include bit `0x04` (`RxReset|0x04`) to exclude the PHY from reset. [F92, F119]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `vortex_rx()`, `ioread16(ioaddr + RxStatus)` must be stored in `short rx_status` before testing `rx_status > 0` so bit 15 (`0x8000`, incomplete frame) sign-extends as negative. Loop counter `i` in `boomerang_start_xmit()` and countdown variables in `vortex_probe1()`, `_vortex_interrupt()`, `_boomerang_interrupt()`, `vortex_rx()`, `boomerang_rx()`, and `mdio_*()` must remain signed `int` because termination tests `< 0` or `>= 0`. [F26, F58]
- When handling `TxComplete` in `vortex_error()` or draining `TxStatus` in `vortex_start_xmit()`, the driver must write `0` to `TxStatus` to pop the status stack and issue `TxEnable`. In `vortex_error()`, `tx_status & 0x30` (jabber/underrun) must be checked before `(tx_status & 0x08) && (vp->drv_flags & MAX_COLLISION_RESET)` so jabber/underrun leaves `reset_mask == 0` (full `TxReset`) instead of `0x0108`. [F65, F104]
- In `acpi_set_WOL()`, when `vp->enable_wol` is set, `SetRxFilter|RxStation|RxMulticast|RxBroadcast` and `RxEnable` must be issued before `pci_enable_wake()` and `PCI_D3hot`. [F105]
- In `vortex_probe1()`, MII PHY scanning (`phy` `0..31`) must remap `phy == 0` to `phyx = 24` and `1..24` to `phy - 1` so PHY 24 is probed first, and PCI devices with `IS_VORTEX` must have `PCI_LATENCY_TIMER` raised to `>= 248` before `pci_save_state(pdev)`. [F72, F100]
