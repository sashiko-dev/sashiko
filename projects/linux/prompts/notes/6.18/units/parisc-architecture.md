# PARISC ARCHITECTURE

This unit provides core platform bus adapters, IOMMU DMA engines, interrupt controllers, firmware configuration interfaces, and peripheral drivers for the PA-RISC architecture, including the SBA and CCIO IOMMUs (`sba_iommu.c`, `ccio-dma.c`), PCI/GSC/EISA/HP-PB bridges (`lba_pci.c`, `dino.c`, `gsc.c`, `asp.c`, `lasi.c`, `wax.c`, `eisa.c`, `hppb.c`, `superio.c`), I/O SAPIC (`iosapic.c`), PDC Stable Storage (`pdc_stable.c`), LED/LCD and soft-power drivers (`led.c`, `power.c`), 8250 serial glue (`8250_parisc.c`), and the STI framebuffer (`stifb.c`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Any non-NULL `dev.platform_data` on a `parisc_device` or PCI bridge must point to `struct pci_hba_data` or a struct embedding it at offset 0 (`dino_device`, `lba_device`) for `parisc_walk_tree()` and `GET_IOC()`. In `struct pci_hba_data`, `lmmio_space`, `elmmio_space`, and `gmmio_space` must remain contiguous because `dino_bridge_init()` indexes `&dino_dev->hba.lmmio_space` as a `DINO_MAX_LMMIO_RESOURCES`-element array. [F114, F118]
- In `sba_iommu.c` and `ccio-dma.c`, `ioc->pdir_size` is the byte size of `ioc->pdir_base` (`sizeof(u64)` per page) while `ioc->res_size` is the byte size of `ioc->res_map` (`ioc->res_size << 3` entries). In `sba_map_single`, `sba_unmap_page`, `ccio_map_single`, `ccio_unmap_page`, and `iommu_coalesce_chunks`, add the sub-page `offset`/`dma_offset` to byte length before ceiling-aligning to `IOVP_SIZE`. [F109, F122]
- `sba_search_bitmap()` and `sba_free_range()` access `ioc->res_map` as MSB-0 `unsigned long` words (`RESMAP_MASK(n)`), prohibiting `<linux/bitmap.h>` helpers and mappings `> BITS_PER_LONG` pages. Sub-word searches must step `bitshiftcnt` by `o = 1 << get_order(bits_wanted << PAGE_SHIFT)` for `IOC_PCOM` purge alignment, and reaching `res_end` must wrap `res_hint` to `res_map` and `res_bitshift = 0` for the `sba_alloc_range()` retry. [F60, F97, F120]
- `ccio_alloc_range()` and `ccio_free_range()` access `ioc->res_map` as `u8`, `u16`, `u32`, or `u64` words; `CCIO_FIND_FREE_MAPPING` must mask `ioa->res_hint` with `~((size >> 3) - 1)` for natural alignment, and `CCIO_FREE_MAPPINGS` asserts `BUG_ON((*res_ptr & mask) != mask)`, requiring unmap sizes to round to the same power-of-two bucket as allocation. [F83, F121]
- Because `ccio_io_pdir_entry()` and `sba_io_pdir_entry()` run `lci` on `sg_virt(startsg)`, `iommu_coalesce_chunks()` must only coalesce segments when `prev_end == sg_start` and page-aligned. Its inner `while (--nents > 0)` loop advances `startsg++` and zeroes `sg_dma_address`/`sg_dma_len` before testing coalescability, and tags chunk heads with `PIDE_FLAG` (`0x80000000UL`), which `iommu_fill_pdir()` strips (`~PIDE_FLAG`) before indexing `pdir_base`. [F79, F93, F161]
- `dino_mask_irq` and `dino_unmask_irq` must keep `dino_dev->imr` in lockstep with `DINO_IMR`. In `dino_isr()`, mask the initial `DINO_IRR0` read with `DINO_IRR_MASK` (`0x5ff`), and mask the post-loop `DINO_ILR` level re-check with `dino_dev->imr` bounded by `--ilr_loop > 0`. [F61, F98]
- In `lba_rd_cfg` and `lba_wr_cfg`, `LBA_CFG_SETUP`, `LBA_CFG_MASTER_ABORT_CHECK`, and `LBA_CFG_RESTORE` must save/restore `LBA_ERROR_CONFIG`, `LBA_STAT_CTL`, and `LBA_ARB_MASK` and only write `CLEAR_ERRLOG` when `(error_status & LBA_FATAL_ERROR) == 0`. Writes in `elroy_cfg_write()` (`LBA_SKIP_PROBE(d)`), `mercury_cfg_write()`, and `lba_pat_out*()`/`lba_astro_out*()` (`hw_rev < 3`) must flush by reading `LBA_PCI_CFG_ADDR` or `LBA_FUNC_ID`. [F65, F164]
- In `dino_fixup_bus()` and `lba_fixup_bus()`, `PCI_CLASS_BRIDGE_PCI` devices must call `pcibios_init_bridge(dev)` and `continue` before IRQ assignment, and `dino_card_setup()` must use `list_for_each_entry_safe()` when unlinking devices. [F101]
- In `iosapic_register`, `isi->isi_num_vectors` is `IOSAPIC_IRDT_MAX_ENTRY(isi->isi_version) + 1`. In `iosapic_xlate_pin()` and `irt_find_irqline()`, 1-based `PCI_INTERRUPT_PIN` must check `intr_pin == 0` and use `intr_pin - 1`; for non-root buses, swizzle `intr_pin` once via `pci_swizzle_interrupt_pin()` while `p` walks `while (p->parent->parent) p = p->parent` before reading `PCI_SLOT(p->self->devfn)`. [F84, F85, F100]
- In `pdc_stable.c`, `pdcspath_entries` must remain `NULL`-terminated, and `struct pdc_module_path` must keep `struct hardware_path path` at offset 0. Only `ktype_pdcspath` attributes may use `PATHS_ATTR` (which downcasts via `to_pdcspath_entry()`), whereas `stable_kobj` attributes must use `PDCS_ATTR` and reference `pdcspath_entry_primary`. [F74, F75, F116]
- In `stifb.c`, every callback reprogramming NGLE MMIO control registers (`stifb_setcolreg`, `stifb_blank`, `stifb_copyarea`, `stifb_fillrect`, `stifb_init_display`) must finish with `SETUP_FB(fb)`. In `power_init()`, `kthread_run(kpowerswd, ...)` must run whenever `!running_on_qemu || soft_power_reg`, and `kpowerswd()` only calls `process_shutdown()` when the active-low power-switch bit (`gsc_readl(soft_power_reg) & 0x1` or `__getDIAG(25) & 0x80000000`) is `0`. [F68, F91, F92]

## Synchronization

- In `pdc_stable.c`, `pdcspath_fetch` and `pdcspath_store` require `write_lock(&entry->rw_lock)`. Any code clearing `entry->ready = 0` on a registered entry (`entry->ready == 2`) to update `entry->dev` must hold `write_lock(&entry->rw_lock)` across `pdcspath_store(entry)` so `ready` is restored to 2 before unlocking, and must drop `entry->rw_lock` before sleeping sysfs/kobject calls (`sysfs_create_link`, `kobject_init_and_add`, `kobject_uevent`). [F2, F17, F63, F156]
- In `sba_iommu.c` and `ccio-dma.c`, hold `spin_lock_irqsave(&ioc->res_lock, flags)` across all PDIR bitmap allocations/frees (`sba_alloc_range`, `sba_free_range`, `ccio_alloc_range`, `ccio_free_range`), `iommu_coalesce_chunks`, and `iommu_fill_pdir`. [F24]
- In `dino.c`, hold `spin_lock_irqsave(&d->dinosaur_pen, flags)` across writing `DINO_PCI_ADDR` and accessing `DINO_CONFIG_DATA` or `DINO_IO_DATA`. In `dino_cfg_write()`, while holding `dinosaur_pen`, write `v & 0xffffff00` to `DINO_PCI_ADDR` and dummy-read `DINO_CONFIG_DATA` before writing target `v` and `DINO_CONFIG_DATA`. [F20, F128]
- EISA 8259 PIC mask (`0x21`, `0xa1`) and shadow (`master_mask`, `slave_mask`) updates require `spin_lock_irqsave(&eisa_irq_lock, flags)`, and `eisa_irq()` must drop `eisa_irq_lock` across `generic_handle_irq(irq)`. In `parisc_panic_event()`, use `pdc_soft_power_button_panic()` (which uses `spin_trylock_irqsave(&pdc_lock, flags)`) instead of `pdc_soft_power_button()`. [F151, F154]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `pdc_stable_init()`, error rollback and `pdc_stable_exit()` must unwind in reverse: `pdcs_unregister_pathentries()`, `kset_unregister(paths_kset)`, `kobject_put(stable_kobj)`. In `pdcs_register_pathentries()`, `rwlock_init(&entry->rw_lock)` must precede `pdcspath_fetch` and `kobject_init_and_add`; if `kobject_init_and_add(&entry->kobj, ...)` fails, call `kobject_put(&entry->kobj)` immediately without setting `entry->ready = 2`. [F1, F3, F19]
- In `ccio_free()` and `sba_free()`, call `ccio_unmap_page()` / `sba_unmap_page()` before `free_pages()`. In `ccio_probe()`, `iounmap(ioc->ioc_regs)` before `kfree(ioc)` on post-`ioremap()` errors. In `dino_probe()` and `lba_driver_probe()`, call `pci_free_resource_list(&resources)` if `pci_create_root_bus()` returns `NULL`. [F4, F5, F13]
- In `iosapic_fixup_irq` and `iosapic_serial_irq`, check `if (vi->irte) goto out;` on `vi = isi->isi_vector + isi_line` before allocating `vi->txn_irq` so shared lines reuse `vector_info`; otherwise populate `irte`, `txn_irq`, `txn_addr`, `txn_data`, `eoi_addr`, and `eoi_data` before calling `cpu_claim_irq`. [F23, F67]
- In `eisa_probe()`, unwind `request_resource()`, `request_irq()`, and `ioremap()` in reverse via `error_iounmap`, `error_free_irq`, and `error_release`. In `parse_slot_config()`, only `kfree(board)` when `id_string_used == 0`. In `hppa_led_generic_probe()`, failure at `i` must `led_classdev_unregister()` indices `i - 1 .. 0`, and `platform_led_remove()` must unregister all `NUM_LEDS_PER_BOARD` LEDs. [F9, F10, F11]
- In `power_init()`, initialize `power_task = NULL`, check `IS_ERR(power_task)` after `kthread_run()`, and call `pdc_soft_power_button(0)` on failure; `power_exit()` must stop `power_task`, unregister `parisc_panic_block`, and call `pdc_soft_power_button(0)`. In `stifb_init_fb()`, `framebuffer_alloc()` failure returns `-ENOMEM` directly before `out_err0`, while later errors jump to `out_err0`..`out_err4` returning `-ENXIO`; set `dev_set_drvdata(sti->dev, info)` only after `register_framebuffer()` succeeds and clear it in `stifb_cleanup()`. [F8, F14, F51, F56]

## API Contracts

- `gsc_alloc_irq()` and `gsc_claim_irq()` return `< 0` on failure without populating `struct gsc_irq *i`; `gsc_claim_irq(i, irq)` takes an unshifted GSC index (`ASP_GSC_IRQ`, `WAX_GSC_IRQ`) and adds `CPU_IRQ_BASE`. `gsc_assign_irq()` and `gsc_find_local_irq()` return `NO_IRQ` (`-1`) on failure, which callers (`gsc_asic_assign_irq`, `dino_assign_irq`) must check before storing. [F33, F34, F35]
- `iosapic_serial_irq()` returns `0` on failure (callers like `serial_init_chip()` must check `!dev->irq`), whereas `iosapic_fixup_irq()` returns `-1`. For NS87560 SuperIO, `iosapic_fixup_irq()` calls `superio_fixup_irq()` on all functions, programs the IRT only for `SUPERIO_USB_FN`, and relies on `superio_init()` (`PCI_FIXUP_FINAL`) to move `usb_pdev->irq` to `lio_pdev->irq` and set `suckyio_irq_enabled` before `superio_probe()`. [F36, F133]
- `ccio_free_range(ioc, iova, pages_mapped)` takes a page count (`size >> IOVP_SHIFT`), unlike `sba_free_range` and `ccio_mark_invalid` which take bytes. `ccio_unmap_sg()` and `sba_unmap_sg()` must loop on `while (nents && sg_dma_len(sglist))`. `sba_dma_supported` must accept 64-bit masks `>= ioc->ibase - 1 + (ioc->pdir_size / sizeof(u64) * IOVP_SIZE)`, and `ccio_ops`/`sba_ops` must wire `dma_common_get_sgtable`, `dma_common_alloc_pages`, and `dma_common_free_pages`. [F42, F95, F167, F168]
- `lba_set_iregs(lba, ibase, imask)` requires `ibase` with bits 0..20 cleared and an unshifted `imask`. `elroy_cfg_read`/`write` and `mercury_cfg_read`/`write` must return `-EINVAL` if `pos > 255` or `devfn > 255`. `pcibios_allocate_bridge_resources()` must claim bridge windows with `pci_claim_bridge_resource()` and zero `r->start`, `r->end`, and `r->flags` on failure. [F43, F127, F175]

## Error Handling

- `GET_IOC(dev)`, `ccio_get_iommu(dev)`, `iosapic_register()`, and `entry->dev` in `struct pdcspath_entry` (even when `entry->ready >= 1`) can all be `NULL` and must be checked before dereferencing. Check `ioremap()` for `NULL` in `lba_driver_probe`, `ccio_probe`, `eisa_probe`, `serial_init_chip`, and `stifb_init_fb`, and in `serial_init_chip()` `iounmap(uart.port.membase)` if `serial8250_register_8250_port()` returns `< 0`. [F37, F39, F46, F53, F55]
- In `eisa_probe()`, only call `eisa_root_register()` when `eisa_enumerator()` returns `result >= 0`, and set `result = -ENOMEM` before `goto error_iounmap` if `eisa_root_register()` fails. In `early_led_init()`, zero `chassis_info.actcnt`/`maxcnt` before `pdc_chassis_info()` and reset `lcd_info.model = DISPLAY_MODEL_NONE` on error. In `kpowerswd()`, check `if (soft_power_reg)` before `gsc_readl(soft_power_reg)`. [F49, F50, F52]

## External Contracts

- Every sysfs `.store` handler in `pdc_stable.c` must check `capable(CAP_SYS_ADMIN)` (`-EACCES`), and string handlers (`pdcspath_hwpath_write`, `pdcspath_layer_write`, `pdcs_auto_write`) must clamp `count` to `sizeof(in) - 1` and use `strscpy(in, buf, count + 1)`. `pdcs_osdep1_write` and `pdcs_osdep2_write` must check `pdcs_osid == OS_ID_LINUX` (`-EPERM`), bound `count` (`-EMSGSIZE`), zero-pad a 4-byte-aligned buffer `in`, and pass `sizeof(in)` to `pdc_stable_write()`. [F70, F123, F170, F171]
- `pdcspath_hwpath_write` must pre-fill `hwpath` with `0xff`, guard `likely(i)` in its reverse loop from `i = 5` to avoid underflowing `unsigned short i`, and verify `hwpath_to_device(&hwpath)` is non-NULL (`-EINVAL`) before locking or calling `pdcspath_store`. `pdcspath_layer_write` must zero-initialize `layers[6]`. In `eisa_eeprom.c`, reject `FMODE_WRITE` (`-EINVAL`) in `open` and clamp `llseek`/`read` (and `es->config_data_offset` in `eisa_enumerator`) to `HPEE_MAX_LENGTH`. [F71, F72, F73, F76, F172, F173]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `iommu_coalesce_chunks`, check `max_seg_boundary = dma_get_seg_boundary(dev) + 1` for non-zero before clamping `max_seg_size` (`0xffffffff + 1` overflows to `0`). In `iommu_fill_pdir()`, save `sg_dma_len(startsg)` to `dma_len` and zero it before `sg_dma_len(dma_sg) += startsg->length` because `dma_sg` aliases `startsg` when uncoalesced. In `sba_mark_invalid()`, loop `while (byte_cnt > IOVP_SIZE)` because the final PDIR entry is cleared after the `if` block. [F80, F94, F96]
- On PA8700 (`version <= 0x202` without cap `0x08L`), `sba_alloc_pdir()` must avoid `(phys & 0x00160000UL) == 0x00060000UL` and sets `piranha_bad_128k = 1` on 2MB PDIRs so `sba_common_init()` reserves `+1408K..+1536K`. On non-PAT systems with `CL_KEYBD`, `sba_hw_init()` must call `pdc_io_reset_devices()` before `sba_ioc_init()`. In `dino_probe()`, Cujo 2.0 (`hversion_rev == 1`) requires `ccio_cujo20_fixup()`, and `ccio_map_single()`/`ccio_alloc()` must set `HINT_SAFE_DMA` for `DMA_BIDIRECTIONAL` or sub-`L1_CACHE_BYTES` alignments. [F136, F138, F139, F140]
- In `iosapic_wr_irt_entry()`, flush each `iosapic_write()` with `readl(isp->addr + IOSAPIC_REG_WINDOW)`, and call `iosapic_eoi()` in `iosapic_unmask_irq()`. Hold `spin_lock_irqsave(&iosapic_lock, flags)` in `iosapic_mask_irq()` and `iosapic_set_affinity_irq()`, and pass `&dummy_d0` to `iosapic_set_irt_data(vi, &dummy_d0, &d1)` in `iosapic_set_affinity_irq` to preserve `IOSAPIC_IRDT_ENABLE` in `d0`. Check `if (cnt >= irt_num_entry)` after `irt_cell` loops, and widen `hpa` with `((hpa) | 0xffffffff00000000ULL)` (not `F_EXTEND(hpa)`) in 32-bit `COMPARE_IRTE_ADDR`. [F66, F105, F106, F132, F165]
- Zero `OFFSET_IMR` and read `OFFSET_IRR` before `request_irq()` and `OFFSET_IAR` in `lasi_init_chip()`/`wax_init_chip()`. Read `DINO_IRR0` after writing `DINO_IAR0` in `dino_common_init()`, and in `dino_unmask_irq()` check `DINO_ILR` after setting `DINO_IMR` to issue `gsc_writel(txn_data, txn_addr)` if asserted. Set `LBA_FLAG_SKIP_PROBE` in `lba_driver_probe()` only after `pci_scan_child_bus()`, and `break` in the `else` branch of `case PAT_LMMIO:` in `lba_pat_resources()`. [F102, F131, F134, F135]
- `pci_dev_is_behind_card_dino()` runs on all buses via `pci_fixup_tulip()` and must verify `dino_dev->hba.dev->id` before reading `dino_device`-specific fields. In `stifb.c`, convert between `stifb_info` and `fb_info` via `fb->info`/`info->par` (never cast), and use `SETUP_HW(fb)`, `GET_FIFO_SLOTS()`, and double `REG_40` writes in `SETUP_HCRX()`. Access HPAs like `soft_power_reg` via `gsc_read*()`/`gsc_write*()`, and follow LCD writes with `udelay(lcd_info.min_cmd_delay)`. [F113, F115, F142, F147, F150]

## Other

- On big-endian PA-RISC: decode EISA EEPROM fields with `get_16`/`get_24`/`get_32`; byte-swap `gsc_readw`/`l` and `gsc_writew`/`l` in `eisa_in16`/`32` and `eisa_out16`/`32`; access `SBA_PDIR_VALID_BIT` at `((u8 *)pdir_ptr)[7]` in `sba_mark_invalid()`/`sba_check_pdir()` because `sba_io_pdir_entry()` stores `cpu_to_le64(pa)`; and pre-swap `vi->eoi_data = cpu_to_le32(vi->txn_data)` for `__raw_writel()` in `iosapic_eoi()`. Keep `__res` pinned to `asm("r28")` in `__getDIAG(dr)`. [F77, F152, F155, F163, F166]
