# ETHERNET PHY LIBRARY

The Ethernet PHY library (`phylib`), MDIO bus subsystem, SFP cage infrastructure, and vendor PHY/MDIO drivers manage MDIO controller transfers (Clause 22/45), bus multiplexing, firmware/DT/ACPI device registration, software/fixed PHY emulation, SFP module state machines, and PHY features including auto-negotiation, LEDs, hwmon, Wake-on-LAN, cable diagnostics, and PTP hardware timestamping.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `mdio_bus_match()` must check `!(mdiodrv->mdiodrv.flags & MDIO_DEVICE_IS_PHY) == !(mdio->flags & MDIO_DEVICE_FLAG_PHY)` before DT/`bus_match`, and callers of `fwnode_mdio_find_device()` or `mdiobus_find_device()` must check `mdiodev->flags & MDIO_DEVICE_FLAG_PHY` before `to_phy_device()`. Every `phy_driver` entry must also appear in its sentinel-terminated `MODULE_DEVICE_TABLE(mdio, ...)` array. [F449, F464, F618]
- In `drivers/net/phy/marvell.c`, `m88e1111_config_aneg()`, `m88e1510_config_aneg()`, `marvell_suspend()`, and `marvell_resume()` must restore `MII_MARVELL_COPPER_PAGE` via `marvell_set_page()` on all exits (without overwriting `err`), whereas `marvell_read_status()` stays on `MII_MARVELL_FIBER_PAGE` if fiber link is up. When `phydev->interface == PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_SGMII`, do not touch `MII_MARVELL_FIBER_PAGE`. [F355, F384, F430]
- In `ksz9131_mdix_update()`, `ksz9477_mdix_update()`, and `lan87xx_config_aneg()`, never overwrite `phydev->mdix_ctrl`: KSZ helpers copy `phydev->mdix_ctrl` to `phydev->mdix` when `!= ETH_TP_MDI_AUTO`, while `lan87xx_config_aneg()` uses a local variable to force `ETH_TP_MDI` in `SPECIAL_CTRL_STS` (with `SPECIAL_CTRL_STS_OVRRD_AMDIX_`) and `phydev->mdix` when `autoneg == AUTONEG_DISABLE`. [F403, F562, F563, F629]
- Volatile PHY settings cleared by reset must run in `.config_init` (and after SW reset in `dp83822_phy_reset()`/`dp83869_phy_reset()`): `aqr_gen1_config_init()` must call `aqr_wait_reset_complete()` before restoring `priv->leds_active_low`/`leds_active_high` or MDI/downshift; `mv88q2xxx_config_init()` must call `mv88q2xxx_enable_temp_sense()`; `en8811h_config_init()` must call `en8811h_restart_mcu()` when `priv->mcu_needs_restart` is true. [F67, F68, F137, F239, F244]

## Synchronization
- Locked MDIO accessors (`mdiobus_read*`/`write*`/`modify*`) acquire sleeping `bus->mdio_lock` and must never run in atomic context. Unlocked accessors (`__mdiobus_*`, `__phy_*`) and `.read_page`/`.write_page`/`.read_mmd`/`.write_mmd` callbacks (`bcm7xxx_28nm_ephy_*_mmd`, `ip101g_*_page`, `rtl821x_*_page`) run with `mdio_lock` held and must never call locking `phy_*()` helpers. Nested bus accesses must use `mdiobus_*_nested()`. [F206, F325, F362, F481, F482, F533]
- Every `phy_save_page()` or `phy_select_page()` call (`air_en8811h.c`, `icplus.c`, `realtek_main.c`) must initialize `ret = 0`, use unlocked `__phy_*()` helpers while the page is selected, and exit through `phy_restore_page(phydev, oldpage, ret)` even when `oldpage < 0` so `mdio_lock` is released and earlier errors are preserved. [F15, F49, F61, F220, F550]
- Multi-step indirect register accesses must hold `phy_lock_mdio_bus(phydev)` across selector and data phases: `__bcm_phy_*_exp`/`_rdb`, `bcm54140_base_read_rdb()`/`write_rdb()`, and `lanphy_*_page_reg()`. In `_bcm_phy_cable_test_start()` and `_bcm_phy_cable_test_get_status()`, when `is_rdb` is true, hold `phy_lock_mdio_bus()` across `__bcm_phy_enable_legacy_access()`, expansion accesses, and `ret = __bcm_phy_enable_rdb_access(phydev) ? : ret;`. [F24, F40, F161, F205, F486]
- In `mdio-i2c.c`, `smbus_byte_mii_read_default_c22()`, `smbus_byte_mii_write_default_c22()`, and `i2c_transfer_rollball()` must hold `i2c_lock_bus(i2c, I2C_LOCK_SEGMENT)` across the full sequence; `i2c_transfer_rollball()` must save `main_err` from `__i2c_transfer_err()`, restore `saved_page` via `__i2c_rollball_set_page()`, unlock, and return `main_err ? : ret`. [F20, F42, F169]
- In `mdio-mux.c`, `mdio_mux_read*`/`write*` must hold `pb->mii_bus->mdio_lock` (`MDIO_MUTEX_MUX`) across `pb->switch_fn()` and parent transfer, updating `pb->current_child = cb->bus_number` only when `switch_fn()` returns `0`. In `mdio-realtek-rtl9300.c`, all child buses share `SMI_ACCESS_PHY_CTRL_0..3` and must hold `priv->lock` (via `((struct rtl9300_mdio_chan *)bus->priv)->priv`) across register accesses and `rtl9300_mdio_wait_ready()`. [F53, F58, F131, F168]
- Network timestamp hooks (`bcm_ptp_txtstamp`/`rxtstamp`, `dp83640_txtstamp`/`rxtstamp`, `mchp_rds_ptp_txtstamp`/`rxtstamp`, `lan8814_txtstamp`/`rxtstamp`) run in atomic packet context and must never perform sleeping MDIO I/O or lock mutexes. Queue walks and `__skb_unlink()` on `tx_queue`/`rx_queue` must hold `spin_lock_irqsave(&queue.lock, flags)` (in `decode_rxts()`, acquire `dp83640->rx_lock` before `rx_queue.lock`) and unlock before `skb_complete_tx_timestamp()`, `kfree_skb()`, or `netif_rx()`. [F28, F199, F319, F369, F490]
- In `dp83640.c`, `dp83640_clock_get()` and `dp83640_clock_get_bus()` return `clock` with `clock->clock_lock` held (requiring `dp83640_clock_put()` on all exits); `clock->clock_lock` must never be held while acquiring `phyter_clocks_lock`, and `ext_read()`/`ext_write()`/`tdr_write()`/`enable_broadcast()` require `clock->extreg_lock`. In `micrel.c`, LAN8814/LAN8842 `LAN8814_PAGE_COMMON_REGS` accesses require `shared->shared_lock` outside `mdio_lock`, and `mchp_rds_ptp_ltc_adjtime()`/`lan8841_ptp_adjtime()` must unlock `ptp_lock` before `gettime64`/`settime64`. [F188, F197, F200, F367, F368]
- In `sfp.c` and `sfp-bus.c`, the lock hierarchy is `rtnl_lock` -> `sfp->sm_mutex` -> `sfp->st_mutex`: `sfp_bus` endpoint registration and `phylib_stubs` require `rtnl_lock`; `__sfp_sm_event()` requires `rtnl_lock` and `sfp->sm_mutex`; `sfp_get_state()`/`sfp_set_state()` and `sfp->state*` updates require `sfp->st_mutex`. Never call `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` on `sfp->timeout` or `sfp->poll` under `rtnl_lock` or `sfp->sm_mutex`. `sfp_bus_release()` enters with `sfp_mutex` locked via `kref_put_mutex()` and must unlock it. [F527, F532, F650, F651, F655, F656]

## Object Lifetimes
- Because `phy_device_release()` and `mdio_device_release()` call `fwnode_handle_put(dev->fwnode)`, any error path after `fwnode_handle_get()` + `device_set_node()` where `phy_device_register()` or `mdio_device_register()` fails must call `device_set_node(..., NULL)` before `phy_device_free()` or `mdio_device_free()`. In `of_mdiobus_find_phy()`, `device_set_node(dev, of_fwnode_handle(child))` transfers `child` ownership to `dev`, so do not call `of_node_put(child)` on match. [F1, F350]
- In `fwnode_mdiobus_register_phy()`, assign `phy->psec` and non-NULL `phy->mii_ts` only after `phy_device_register()` and all fallible steps that jump to `unregister_phy` (since `phy_device_remove(phy)` frees `phy->mii_ts` and `phy->psec`), unwinding `phy_device_remove(phy)`, `phy_device_free(phy)`, and `unregister_mii_timestamper(mii_ts)` in reverse order. [F2, F44, F138, F185]
- `of_mdio_find_device()`, `of_phy_find_device()`, `mdio_find_bus()`, and `of_mdio_find_bus()` increment the embedded `struct device` refcount; callers (`of_phy_connect()`, `of_phy_deregister_fixed_link()`, `xgmiitorgmii_probe()`, etc.) must drop it via `put_device()`. Early exits from `for_each_available_child_of_node()` in `of_mdio.c`/`mdio-mux.c`, `of_get_child_by_name()` in `dp83822_of_init_leds()`/`mv88q2xxx_leds_probe()`, or `of_parse_phandle_with_fixed_args()` in `fwnode_find_mii_timestamper()` must call `of_node_put()`. [F3, F4, F101, F150, F195, F462, F521]
- `__mdiobus_register()` increments `dev_fwnode(&bus->dev)` only when `bus->state == MDIOBUS_ALLOCATED`, and `mdiobus_release()` drops it only when `bus->state == MDIOBUS_RELEASED`. Both `__mdiobus_register()` rollback and `mdiobus_unregister()` must call `mdiodev->device_remove()` then `mdiodev->device_free()` on all `bus->mdio_map[]` entries before asserting `bus->reset_gpiod` and calling `device_del(&bus->dev)`. Never `kfree(bus->priv)` separately, and never use `devm_*` in `mdiobus_register_gpiod()`/`mdiobus_register_reset()`. [F346, F348, F349, F351, F364, F365, F446, F456, F459]
- In `mdio_mux_init()`, call `get_device(&parent_bus->dev)` when using a caller-supplied `mux_bus`, and link `cb` onto `pb->children` only after `of_mdiobus_register(cb->mii_bus, ...)` succeeds (else `mdiobus_free(cb->mii_bus)`). `mdio_mux_uninit()` must unregister child buses before the parent `mii_bus` or mux clocks/controls. Non-devres `mii_bus` drivers must call `mdiobus_unregister(bus)` before disabling clocks/regulators and calling `mdiobus_free(bus)` (or `free_mdio_bitbang(bus)` for `alloc_mdio_bitbang()`). [F6, F7, F10, F18, F22, F142, F536]
- In `sfp-bus.c` and `sfp.c`: `sfp_bus_find_fwnode()` must `fwnode_handle_put(ref.fwnode)` on all paths and returns a kref-counted `sfp_bus` (dropped via `sfp_bus_put()`); `sfp_sm_main()` must detach `sfp->mod_phy` (`sfp_sm_phy_detach()`) before `sfp_i2c_mdiobus_destroy()`; `SFP_E_REMOVE` in `sfp_sm_module()` must unconditionally call `sfp_sm_mod_remove()` so `sfp_hwmon_remove()` cancels `hwmon_probe` and frees `hwmon_dev`/`hwmon_name`; and `devm_add_action_or_reset(sfp->dev, sfp_cleanup, sfp)` must precede `devm_request_threaded_irq()`. [F511, F512, F513, F515, F516, F517, F518, F519, F640]
- `.txtstamp` callbacks (`bcm_ptp_txtstamp`, `dp83640_txtstamp`, `mchp_rds_ptp_txtstamp`, `lan8814_txtstamp`) unconditionally consume `skb` (queueing with `SKBTX_IN_PROGRESS` or calling `kfree_skb(skb)`), whereas `dp83640_rxtstamp()` consumes `skb` if and only if it returns `true`. In `bcm_ptp_match_tstamp()`, matched one-step SKBs with `BCM_SKB_CB(ts_skb)->discard` set must be freed with `kfree_skb()` rather than passed to `skb_complete_tx_timestamp()`. [F176, F192, F357, F358]

## API Contracts
- `phy_read*()` and `mdiobus_read*()` return `0..0xffff` as `int` or a negative errno (store in `int` and check `< 0` before narrowing to `u16`, while 32-bit MMIO/regmap read callbacks must mask with `0xffff`). `*modify*_changed()` helpers (`__phy_modify_changed`, `phy_modify_changed`, `phy_modify_mmd_changed`, `__mdiobus_modify_changed`, `lanphy_modify_page_reg`, `aqr_set_mdix`, `bcm_setup_lre_master_slave`, `bcm_config_lre_advert`) return `< 0` on error, `0` if unchanged, and `> 0` if modified—never test `if (ret)` as an error. [F55, F125, F374, F540, F541]
- In `phy_modify*()`, `mdiobus_modify*()`, `aqr_phy_led_active_low_set()`, `__bcm_phy_modify_exp()`/`_rdb()`, and `air_buckpbus_reg_modify()`, `new = (old & ~mask) | set` does not mask `set` by `mask`: callers must pass a pre-shifted, clamped bitmask aligned with `mask`. Conversely, `phy_set_clr_bits()` and `__phy_set_clr_bits()` in `bcm7xxx.c` take `(dev, reg, set_mask, clr_mask)` with `set_mask` third and `clr_mask` fourth. [F36, F223, F445]
- Callers of `fwnode_mdiobus_register_phy()` and `fwnode_mdiobus_phy_device_register()` must validate `addr < PHY_MAX_ADDR` (`32`), and `__mdiobus_read`/`write`/`c45_read`/`c45_write` must reject `addr >= PHY_MAX_ADDR` with `-ENXIO` before indexing `bus->stats[addr]` or `bus->mdio_map[addr]`. [F123, F412, F413]
- Return conventions: `bcm_ptp_probe()` and `mchp_rds_ptp_probe()` return `NULL` when PTP is unsupported/disabled and `ERR_PTR` on failure (check `IS_ERR()` on probe and non-NULL before `bcm_ptp_stop()` or `mchp_rds_ptp_handle_interrupt()`); `sfp_bus_find_fwnode()` returns `NULL` when optional `"sfp"` is absent and `ERR_PTR` on error; `fixed_phy_register()` and `sfp_alloc()` return `ERR_PTR` (never `NULL`), whereas `sfp_register_socket()` returns `NULL` on failure. [F48, F218, F376, F537, F538]
- In `phy_driver::config_intr` (`aqr_config_intr`, `bcm_phy_config_intr`, `bcm54140_config_intr`, `xway_gphy_config_intr`), enabling interrupts must clear pending status bits before unmasking, and disabling must mask before clearing—except Vitesse `vsc82xx_config_intr()`, which must read `MII_VSC8244_ISTAT` before writing `0` to `MII_VSC8244_IMASK`. In `handle_interrupt`, any negative read error must call `phy_error(phydev)` and return `IRQ_NONE`. [F135, F136, F340, F341, F628]

## Error Handling
- When polling PHY registers with `read_poll_timeout` (`aqr_wait_reset_complete`, `phy_read_poll_timeout`, `phy_read_mmd_poll_timeout`, `lan87xx_read_status`), `val` must be a signed `int` checked for `val < 0` both in the stop condition and after polling, because negative MDIO errors are non-zero and have high bits set. In `aqr_gen1_read_status()`, ignore `-ETIMEDOUT` on `MDIO_PHYXS_VEND_IF_STATUS` so `TYPE_OFF` sets `phydev->link = false`. [F37, F100, F608]
- In `__of_mdiobus_parse_phys()`, `__of_mdiobus_register()`, `__acpi_mdiobus_register()`, and `mdiobus_scan_bus_c22()`/`c45()`, `-ENODEV` from child PHY registration/scanning indicates an absent device and must not abort bus registration. In `mdio_mux_init()`, child registration errors other than `-EPROBE_DEFER` or `-ENOMEM` free the failed child and continue scanning. [F46, F54, F375]

## Known Pitfalls
- When assembling 32/48-bit timestamps or counters from 16-bit MDIO reads (`dp83640`, `dp83td510`, `dp83tg720`, `micrel`, `microchip_rds_ptp`), store words in unsigned/64-bit types and shift in 16-bit steps (`<<= 16`) so `int` bit 15 does not sign-extend and `sec * 1000000000ULL` does not overflow. When writing 48-bit seconds, `*_SEC_MID` takes `upper_16_bits(sec)` and `*_SEC_HI` takes `upper_32_bits(sec)`. In `bcm_ptp_adjfine()` and `ptp_dp83640_adjfine()`, negate negative `scaled_ppm` before shifting into `u64` and use `div_u64(..., 15625)`. [F115, F278, F279, F280, F283, F437, F438, F439]
- Bitmap and parameter traps: `linkmode_*_bit()`, `phy_disable_eee_mode()`, and `phy_remove_link_mode()` take a bit index (`ETHTOOL_LINK_MODE_*_BIT`), whereas `phy_set_bits()`/`phy_clear_bits()`/`phy_modify()` take shifted bitmasks; `DECLARE_PHY_INTERFACE_MASK` bitmaps must only use `phy_interface_*` helpers, never `linkmode_*`; `mdiobb_read_c45()`/`write_c45()` take `(bus, phy, devad, reg)` despite swapped header parameter names; and `mdio_mux_gpio_switch_fn()` must copy `desired_child` into a `DECLARE_BITMAP` before `gpiod_multi_set_value_cansleep()`. [F134, F557, F558, F621]
- Loop cursor traps: after `skb_queue_walk_safe()` (`mchp_rds_ptp_match_*`, `lan8814_match_*`), `list_for_each_entry_safe()` (`mchp_rds_ptp_match_rx_skb`), or `skb_queue_walk()` with `__skb_unlink()` (`bcm_ptp_match_tstamp`, `decode_rxts`), never test the loop cursor directly after the loop—use a separate matched pointer or boolean flag and `break` immediately after `__skb_unlink()`. In `rx_timestamp_work()` (`dp83640.c`), when an unexpired SKB is pushed back via `skb_queue_head()`, the loop must `break` (not `continue`). [F103, F269, F273, F432, F433]
- String, EEPROM, and jiffies bounds: validate firmware strings in `aqr_fw_boot()` with `aqr_fw_validate_get()` + `strscpy()` before `%s`; in `sfp.c`, bound non-NUL-terminated `sfp_eeprom_id` strings with `sfp_strlen()` or `%.*s`, and pass `sizeof(...) - 1` to `sfp_check()` on `id.base`/`id.ext` to exclude `cc_base`/`cc_ext`; in `marvell_hwmon_name()` and `aqr_hwmon_probe()`, copy only `isalnum()` chars into `hwmon_name`. Store jiffies deadlines as `unsigned long` and compare with `time_after()`/`time_before()`/`time_is_before_jiffies()`. [F112, F126, F133, F281, F453, F581, F620]
