# CHAR and MISC DRIVERS

This unit implements core Linux character and miscellaneous device infrastructure (`misc_register`/`misc_deregister` in `drivers/char/misc.c` and memory pseudo-devices `/dev/mem`, `/dev/null`, `/dev/zero`, `/dev/full`, `/dev/port` in `drivers/char/mem.c`), alongside line printer (`lp`), CMOS/NVRAM (`nvram`), printk TTY (`ttyprintk`), hangcheck timer, and hardware drivers for SPARC ADI, PowerPC BSR and PS3 flash, SGI UV MMTimer, NetWinder flash/button/thermometer, Atari DSP56k, IBM ThinkPad Mwave ACP, Applicom Profibus cards, Xilinx HWICAP, NatSemi GPIO, and Analog Devices digital potentiometers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `drivers/char/xilinx_hwicap/`, ICAP transfers only 32-bit words (`u32`): `hwicap_write()` masks lengths with `~3`, buffers 1..3 trailing bytes in `write_buffer[4]` (flushed zero-padded in `hwicap_release()`), and after prepending `write_buffer_in_use` bytes to `kbuf` and calling `set_configuration()`, subtracts `write_buffer_in_use` from `len` and `left` and resets `write_buffer_in_use = 0` before `written += len`. In `hwicap_read()`, `read_buffer_in_use` is decremented and remaining bytes `memmove()`d to index 0 only after `copy_to_user()` succeeds. [F65, F66, F83]
- In `buffer_icap_set_configuration()`, each BRAM flush at `buffer_count == XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS - 1` must reset `buffer_count = 0` and `dirty = false`, while `buffer_icap_get_configuration()` starts with `buffer_count = XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS`. In `fifo_icap_get_configuration()`, each inner pass must clamp `read_fifo_occupancy` to `words_to_read` before subtracting and draining into `*data++`, with both loops bounded by `retries > XHI_MAX_RETRIES`. [F100, F101]
- In `drivers/char/ps3flash.c`, `ps3flash_fetch()` must invalidate `priv->tag = -1` when `start_sector != priv->tag` after `ps3flash_writeback()` and before `ps3flash_read_write_sectors()`, setting `priv->tag = start_sector` only after the read succeeds. In `ps3flash_write()`, partial writes (`n != dev->bounce_size`) must call `ps3flash_fetch()` and full writes with `sector != priv->tag` must call `ps3flash_writeback()`, updating `priv->tag` and `priv->dirty` only after copying into `dev->bounce_buf` succeeds. [F58, F59]
- In `ps3flash_read()` and `ps3flash_write()`, each bounce-buffer transfer is clamped to `dev->bounce_size - offset`; `dev->bounce_buf`, `userbuf`, and `kernelbuf` are byte-addressed (`+ offset`, `+= n`), whereas `sector` and `priv->tag` are in `dev->blk_size` sectors (`*pos / dev->bounce_size * priv->chunk_sectors`), advancing `sector` by `priv->chunk_sectors` and resetting `offset = 0` after the first `do-while` pass. [F88, F105, F128]
- In `drivers/char/bsr.c`, `bsr_add_node()` must verify DT properties `"ibm,lock-stride"` and `"ibm,#lock-bytes"` are non-NULL with equal byte lengths, and clamp `bsr_len` to `4096` if `4096 < bsr_len < PAGE_SIZE`. Both `bsr_mmap()` and `uv_mmtimer_mmap()` must set `pgprot_noncached(vma->vm_page_prot)`, and `bsr_mmap()` must use `remap_4k_pfn(..., dev->bsr_addr >> 12, ...)` when `dev->bsr_len < PAGE_SIZE && size == PAGE_SIZE`. [F85, F86, F164]
- In `drivers/char/nvram.c`, `nvram_open_cnt` and `nvram_open_mode` must be updated together under `nvram_state_lock`, and `nvram_misc_release()` must clear `NVRAM_WRITE` only when `file->f_mode & FMODE_WRITE` is set on the closing file. On `CONFIG_X86`, the PC CMOS checksum covers NVRAM offsets `PC_CKS_RANGE_START` (2) through `PC_CKS_RANGE_END` (31) inclusive and is stored big-endian at `PC_CKS_LOC` (32..33). [F64, F87]
- In `drivers/char/lp.c`, `lp_open()`, `lp_write()`, and `lp_release()` must update `lp_table[minor].current_mode` whenever negotiating parport modes (`lp_negotiate()`/`parport_negotiate()`), resetting `current_mode = IEEE1284_MODE_COMPAT` before `lp_wait_ready()` on an incomplete write. In `lp_check_status()`, `if ((status & LP_PERRORP) && !(LP_F(minor) & LP_CAREFUL))` must precede `LP_POUTPA` and `!(status & LP_PSELECD)`. [F68, F107]
- In `drivers/char/nwflash.c`, `flash_write()` must reject `p < 64 * 1024` unless `gbWriteEnable && gbWriteBase64Enable`, clamp `count` to `gbFlashSize - p`, and decrement the 64 KiB block count `temp` when `((int)(p + count) & 0xFFFF) == 0` so a block-aligned end does not erase the next block; `write_block()` must clamp `count` to `0x10000 - (p & 0xFFFF)`. [F89, F117]
- In `drivers/char/mwave/`, IPC indices in `mwave_ioctl()` are 0-based (`0..15`, bounds-checked against `ARRAY_SIZE(pDrvData->IPCs)` with `array_index_nospec()`), whereas `DspInterrupt()` iterates 1-based `usPCNum` (`1..16`) and indexes `IPCs[usPCNum - 1]`. In `3780i.h`, any bitfield struct accessed via `MKWORD(var)` must have `sizeof(var) >= sizeof(unsigned short)`. [F74, F93]
- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `devlist[]` is sparsely indexed by minor, so `memory_open()` must check `minor < ARRAY_SIZE(devlist)`, `dev->fops`, and `dev->fops->open`, and `read_mem()`/`write_mem()` must verify `(phys_addr_t)*ppos == *ppos` to detect 32-bit truncation. In `drivers/char/ttyprintk.c`, `tpk_buffer` and `tpk_curr` require `tpkp->spinlock`, and `tpk_buffer` must be at least `TPK_STR_SIZE + 2` bytes because line wrap writes `'\\'` at `TPK_STR_SIZE` and `'\0'` at `TPK_STR_SIZE + 1`. [F42, F73, F91, F109]

## Synchronization

- In `drivers/char/misc.c`, `misc_mtx` protects `misc_list` and `misc_minors_ida` across `misc_register()`, `misc_deregister()`, `misc_open()`, and `misc_seq_ops`. When `misc_open()` drops `misc_mtx` for `request_module()`, it must relock and rescan `misc_list` from the head before `fops_get()`. Because `misc_open()` calls `file->f_op->open()` under `misc_mtx`, a miscdevice `.open` must never call `misc_register()`/`misc_deregister()` or take locks nesting outside `misc_mtx`. [F18, F19]
- Every `CMOS_READ()`/`CMOS_WRITE()` call (including `__nvram_*` in `drivers/char/nvram.c` and `smapi_init()` in `mwave/smapi.c`) must hold `rtc_lock` with IRQs disabled; `pc_nvram_read()`, `pc_nvram_write()`, and `pc_nvram_initialize()` must hold `rtc_lock` continuously across checksum verification, byte loop, and checksum update, while user copies, `GFP_KERNEL` allocations, and `nvram_mutex` must stay outside `rtc_lock`. [F21, F154, F155]
- In `drivers/char/lp.c`, process-context parport claims/releases must use `lp_claim_parport_or_block()` and `lp_release_parport()` under lock order `lp_mutex` -> `lp_table[minor].port_mutex` -> `lp_claim_parport_or_block()`. In `lp_error()`, polled ports (`irq == PARPORT_IRQ_NONE`) must re-claim via `lp_claim_parport_or_block()` after sleeping. `lp_console_write()` runs under the console lock on `PARPORT_MODE_SAFEININT` ports and must use non-blocking `parport_claim()`. [F14, F25, F159, F160]
- In `drivers/char/applicom.c`, `apbs[i].mutex` (taken in hardirq `ac_interrupt()`) must be acquired with `spin_lock_irqsave()` in `ac_write()` and `ac_read()`, and `ac_write()` must recheck `DATA_FROM_PC_READY == 0` under the lock after `schedule()`. In `xilinx_hwicap.c`, `hwicap_open()` orders `hwicap_mutex` -> `drvdata->sem`, and `drvdata->sem` protects `is_open`, `read_buffer*`, `write_buffer*`, and `drvdata->config` calls. [F26, F32]
- In `drivers/char/mwave/3780i.c`, `dsp_lock` (`spin_lock_irqsave()`, nesting inside `mwave_mutex`) protects multi-port DSP register accesses and must be dropped before `put_user()`, `get_user()`, or `PaceMsaAccess()`; `dsp3780I_GetIPCSource()` must mask `DSP_HBridgeControl.EnableDspInt = false` under `dsp_lock` while reading and clearing `DSP_Interrupt`. In `ds1620.c` and `nwflash.c`, `nw_gpio_modify_*()` and `nw_cpld_modify()` require `raw_spin_lock_irqsave(&nw_gpio_lock, ...)` and must unlock before `msleep()`. [F34, F69, F156]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `drivers/char/bsr.c`, `of_find_compatible_node(bn, ...)` consumes `bn`'s refcount, so on `bsr_add_node(bn)` failure `bsr_create_devs()` must call `of_node_put(bn)` and `bsr_init()` must set `np = NULL`. In `bsr_add_node()`, `cur` is added to `bsr_devs` only after `cdev_add()` and `device_create()` succeed; if `device_create()` fails, `cdev_del(&cur->bsr_cdev)` and `kfree(cur)` must precede `bsr_cleanup_devs()`. [F6, F7]
- In `xilinx_hwicap.c`, `drvdata` and `base_address` use `devm_*` (never manually freed/unmapped), while `hwicap_setup()` must initialize `drvdata` and `drvdata->sem` before `cdev_add()`, jumping to `failed:` to reset `probed_devices[id] = 0` under `icap_sem` on error. `hwicap_drv_remove()` must call `device_destroy()` and `cdev_del()` before clearing `probed_devices[]`, and `hwicap_release()` must clear `drvdata->is_open = 0` under `drvdata->sem` on all exit paths. [F8, F9, F33, F134]
- In `drivers/char/mwave/`, `mwave_init()` must set `sLine = -1` and initialize `IPCs[i].ipc_wait_queue` before `tp3780I_EnableDSP()` and `misc_register()`, setting stage flags only after each step succeeds so `mwave_exit()` unwinds in reverse order (serial and miscdevice before `tp3780I_DisableDSP()` and `tp3780I_ReleaseResources()`). `tp3780I_EnableDSP()` must immediately `free_irq(usUartIrq)` after its test claim, and `exit_cleanup` must power down the DSP if `bDSPPoweredUp` and free `usDspIrq` (clearing `bInterruptClaimed`) if `bInterruptAllocated`. [F11, F12, F35]
- In `drivers/char/applicom.c`, `ac_register_board()` must initialize `FlagSleepSend` and `mutex` before `request_irq()`, and teardown must call `misc_deregister()` and `free_irq()` before `iounmap(RamIO)`. In `applicom_init()`'s `pci_get_class()` loop, early `return` paths must call `pci_dev_put(dev)`, `continue` paths must not, and post-loop `out:` must iterate `apbs[]` and never pass the `NULL` `dev` cursor to `pci_disable_device()`/`pci_dev_put()`. [F27, F94, F95]
- In `lp_open()`, any error after setting `LP_BUSY_BIT_POS` must clear `LP_BUSY` before unlocking `lp_mutex`. In `ps3flash_probe()`/`remove()`, `ps3_os_area_flash_register()`, `misc_register()`, `ps3stor_setup()`, drvdata, and `ps3flash_dev` must unwind in reverse order and clear pointers to `NULL`. In `read_mem()`/`write_mem()`, every `xlate_dev_mem_ptr()` must pair with `unxlate_dev_mem_ptr()` on the same iteration (`read_mem()` bounces via `copy_from_kernel_nofault()`). `ad_dpot_probe()` must copy `*bdata` by value. [F10, F13, F126, F152]

## API Contracts

- In `drivers/char/misc.c`, `misc_init()` uses `__register_chrdev(MISC_MAJOR, 0, MINORMASK + 1, ...)`. `misc_minor_alloc()` reserves both static (`< MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR`) and dynamic (`MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR` = 255, allocated in `[256, MINORMASK]`) minors in `misc_minors_ida`, and `misc_register()` rejects `misc->minor > MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR` with `-EINVAL`. On registration failure and in `misc_deregister()`, `misc_minor_free()` must be called and dynamic minors reset to `MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR`. [F1, F38, F78, F165]
- A `struct miscdevice` passed to `misc_register()` must stay alive until `misc_deregister()` completes; `misc_open()` sets `file->private_data` to the `struct miscdevice` pointer before `replace_fops()` and `file->f_op->open()`, and calls `request_module()` only when `minor < MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR`. [F3, F167]
- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `open_port()`/`open_mem()` must check `capable(CAP_SYS_RAWIO)` and `security_locked_down(LOCKDOWN_DEV_MEM)`, setting `filp->f_mapping = iomem_get_mapping()` for `DEVMEM_MINOR`. Nonblocking `devlist[]` entries must set `FMODE_NOWAIT`, and `read_iter_zero()` must return `written ? written : -EAGAIN` instead of `cond_resched()` when `need_resched() && (iocb->ki_flags & IOCB_NOWAIT)`. [F153, F168, F170]
- In `xilinx_hwicap/`, `get_configuration()`, `set_configuration()`, and `buffer_icap_device_read()`/`write()` take `size`/`offset`/`count` in 32-bit words (`offset + count <= XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS`), whereas `buffer_icap_set_size()` takes bytes (`count << 2`). In `mwave/3780i.c`, `dsp3780I_ReadDStore()`/`WriteDStore()` take `uCount` in 16-bit words and `ReadIStore()`/`WriteIStore()` take 32-bit words. [F43, F84, F122]
- In `ad525x_dpot.h`, `DPOT_CONF(features, wipers, max_pos, uid)` packs `uid` (6 bits, `<= 63`), `max_pos` (4 bits, `<= 15`, log2 of position count since `data->max_pos = 1 << DPOT_MAX_POS(devid)`), and `wipers` (8 bits); callers of `dpot_read*()` must check `< 0` before bit ops. In `applicom.c`, `ac_register_board()` returns a 1-based board number (`0` on failure) and `st_ram_io.num_card` is 1-based, so indexing `apbs[]` requires subtracting 1, `array_index_nospec(..., MAX_BOARD)`, and checking `RamIO != NULL`. [F46, F54, F55, F79, F92]

## Error Handling

- In `read_mem()`, `err` (initialized to `-EFAULT`) must not be overwritten by `copy_from_kernel_nofault()` (which returns `0` on success into `probe`), and `write_mem()`, `write_port()`, `read_zero()`, and `read_iter_zero()` must return the positive byte count transferred so far on a mid-transfer fault rather than `-EFAULT`. [F36, F41]
- In `hwicap_setup()`, `devm_platform_ioremap_resource()` must be checked with `IS_ERR()`, and `drvdata->config->get_status()` returns a raw status bitmask (not an errno) that must be tested against `XHI_SR_DALIGN_MASK`. In `ps3flash_read_write_sectors()`, non-zero `u64` LV1 status from `ps3stor_read_write_sectors()` must become `-EIO`, and `ps3flash_read()`/`write()` must guard `userbuf` and `kernelbuf` for `NULL`. In `nwflash.c`, `flash_write()` retries `RetryBlock` only when `write_block()` returns `0` (verify mismatch), aborting on `< 0`. [F37, F44, F48, F49, F57]
- To avoid stack infoleaks, `ac_read()` must `memset(&st_loc, 0, sizeof(st_loc))` before `do_ac_read()`, and `tp3780I_QueryAbilities()` must `memset(pAbilities, 0, sizeof(*pAbilities))`. In `nvram_misc_ioctl()`/`open()`, `arch_nvram_ops.initialize`, `set_checksum`, and `ppc_md.nvram_sync` must be checked for `!= NULL`. [F47, F51, F52, F123]

## External Contracts

- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `memory_lseek()` must reject `(unsigned long long)offset >= -MAX_ERRNO` with `-EOVERFLOW` and call `force_successful_syscall_return()`, with `FOP_UNSIGNED_OFFSET` set on `mem_fops` and `adi_fops`. In `lp.c`, `lp_set_timeout()` rejects `< 0` with `-EINVAL`, clamps `tv_usec > 999999` to `999999`, and clamps `tv_sec >= MAX_SEC_IN_JIFFIES - 1` to `MAX_JIFFY_OFFSET`; `lp_set_timeout64()` shifts `karg[1] >> 32` when `IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_SPARC64) && !in_compat_syscall()`. [F81, F82, F171]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `mmap_mem()` must check both `offset >> PAGE_SHIFT != vma->vm_pgoff` and `offset + (phys_addr_t)size - 1 < offset`, and `get_unmapped_area_zero()` must pass `NULL` (not `file`) to `shmem_get_unmapped_area()` for `MAP_SHARED`. [F75, F172]
- In `xilinx_hwicap/`, `hwicap_write()` must cast `u32 *kbuf` to `char *` before adding `write_buffer_in_use`; `buffer_icap.c` must write `XHI_SIZE_REG_OFFSET` and `XHI_BRAM_OFFSET_REG_OFFSET` with `out_be32()` before `XHI_RNC_REG_OFFSET`; and `fifo_icap_get_configuration()` must cap transactions to `XHI_MAX_READ_TRANSACTION_WORDS` (`0xFFF`) and write `XHI_SZ_OFFSET` before `XHI_CR_OFFSET`. [F120, F135, F136]
- In `applicom.c`, all `RamIO` accesses must use `readb()`/`writeb()` followed by a dummy `readb(RamIO + VERS)`, and `boardname` must be sized `(SERIAL_NUMBER - TYPE_CARD) + 1` with NUL at index `SERIAL_NUMBER - TYPE_CARD`. In `nwflash.c`, flash commands in `get_flash_id()`, `erase_block()`, and `write_block()` must call `kick_open()` first, write `0xFF` on exit to restore read mode, and poll `jiffies` with `time_before()`/`time_after_eq()`. [F118, F131, F137, F143]
- In `adi.c`, `adi_init()` must check `adi_capable()`, `adi_write()` must execute `membar #Sync` after `set_mcd_tag()`, and `ASI_MCD_REAL` inline asm using `%%g1` in `.fixup` must clobber `"g1"` and `"memory"`. `MMTIMER_GETOFFSET` in `uv_mmtimer_ioctl()` must return `0` when `uv_get_min_hub_revision_id() == 1`. [F148, F149, F150]
