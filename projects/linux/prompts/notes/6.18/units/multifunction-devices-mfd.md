# MULTIFUNCTION DEVICES (MFD)

Shared C headers in `include/linux/mfd/` (from `sun4i-gpadc.h` through `wm8400.h`, including `syscon/` and `wcd934x/`) define interfaces between MFD core drivers and child subsystem drivers, including register offsets, bitmasks, IRQ/regulator IDs, shared device contexts, USB wire layouts, and inline accessors.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Regulator ID enums must stay in exact positional lockstep with their driver descriptor arrays: `enum tps65218_regulator_id` with `regulators[]` (`tps->strobes` indexing); `enum tps65217_regulator_id` with `regulators[]` and `TPS65217_NUM_REGULATOR`; `enum lm363x_regulator_id` with `lm363x_regulator_desc[]`; `TPS65090_REGULATOR_*` (`TPS65090_REGULATOR_MAX`) with `tps65090_regulator_desc[]` and `tps65090_matches[]`; and `WM8400_LDO1`..`WM8400_DCDC2` (0..5) with `struct wm8400::regulators[6]` (used via `container_of`) and `regulators[]`. [F38, F39, F40, F41, F44]
- In `include/linux/mfd/twl.h`, `enum twl_module_ids`, `enum twl4030_module_ids`, and `enum twl6030_module_ids` (both chip-specific enums start at `TWL_MODULE_LAST`) index directly into `twl4030_map[]` and `twl6030_map[]` in `drivers/mfd/twl-core.c`. Enum changes must update those arrays at matching indices, and code shared between TWL4030 and TWL6030 must guard module IDs `>= TWL_MODULE_LAST` with `twl_class_is_4030()` or `twl_class_is_6030()`. [F42, F69]
- In `struct twl6040`, `power_count`, `pll`, `sysclk_rate`, `mclk_rate`, and regcache state must be updated in lockstep under `twl6040->mutex`: `twl6040_power()` syncs regcache and selects `LPPLL` (19.2 MHz) on `0 -> 1`, and marks regcache dirty/cache-only while zeroing `sysclk_rate` (and `mclk_rate` for `HPPLL`) on `1 -> 0`. [F51]
- Each `struct ucb1x00_dev` is linked into `struct ucb1x00.devs` via `dev_node` and `struct ucb1x00_driver.devs` via `drv_node`; traverse `ucb->devs` only with `dev_node`, `drv->devs` only with `drv_node`, and use `list_for_each_safe` whenever calling `ucb1x00_remove_dev()`. [F71]

## Synchronization

- In `struct ti_tscadc_dev`, `reg_lock` protects `reg_se_cache`, `adc_waiting`, `adc_in_use`, and `REG_SE` across `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()`, `am335x_tsc_se_set_once()`, `am335x_tsc_se_adc_done()`, and `am335x_tsc_se_clr()`; child drivers must use these helpers rather than touching `REG_SE` or `reg_se_cache` directly. All `reg_lock` acquisitions must disable local IRQs (`spin_lock_irq`/`spin_lock_irqsave`) because hardirq handler `titsc_irq()` calls `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()`. [F11, F113]
- `am335x_tsc_se_set_once()` must be called only from sleepable process context with interrupts enabled (it uses `spin_lock_irq(&tscadc->reg_lock)` and sleeps via `schedule()` on `reg_se_wait` in `am335x_tscadc_need_adc()`), and must be paired on every exit path with `am335x_tsc_se_adc_done()` because `adc_in_use = true` suppresses `REG_SE` writes in `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()` until cleared. [F12, F112]
- In `struct ucb1x00`, `io_lock`, `irq_lock` (`raw_spinlock_t`), and `adc_mutex` are private to `drivers/mfd/ucb1x00-core.c` (`io_lock` and `irq_lock` nest outside `ucb->mcp->lock`) and must not be acquired directly by sub-drivers. `io_lock` protects shadow registers `io_dir`/`io_out` and `UCB_IO_DIR`/`UCB_IO_DATA` writes (which must use `ucb1x00_io_set_dir()`, `ucb1x00_io_write()`, or `ucb->gpio` callbacks so `ucb1x00_resume()` preserves pin state); `irq_lock` protects `irq_mask`, `irq_ris_enbl`, `irq_fal_enbl`, `irq_wake`, and `UCB_IE_RIS`/`UCB_IE_FAL`. [F15, F48, F116]
- `ucb1x00_adc_enable()` locks `ucb->adc_mutex` and enables the SIB clock and ADC hardware, and `ucb1x00_adc_disable()` disables them and unlocks `adc_mutex`; callers of `ucb1x00_adc_read()` must bracket conversion sequences with `ucb1x00_adc_enable()` and `ucb1x00_adc_disable()` in process context. [F4]
- In `struct twl6040`, `twl6040->ready` and the `irq_ready` threaded handler (`twl6040_readyint_handler`) must be initialized and registered before child MFD devices or thermal IRQs can invoke `twl6040_power(twl6040, 1)`. Because `twl6040->mutex` nests outside the regmap lock and is held while `twl6040_power_up_automatic()` waits on `twl6040->ready`, `twl6040_readyint_handler()` must never acquire `twl6040->mutex` or call `twl6040_power()`/`twl6040_set_pll()`. [F18, F119]
- In `struct tc3589x`, `tc3589x->lock` serializes read-modify-write updates only inside `tc3589x_set_bits()`, and in `struct tps65218`, `tps->tps_lock` serializes password-protected writes (`TPS65218_REG_PASSWORD`) only inside `tps65218_set_bits()` and `tps65218_clear_bits()`; direct `tc3589x_reg_read()`/`tc3589x_reg_write()` and `tps65218_reg_write()` callers do not take these locks internally. [F19, F20]
- In `struct tps65217`, `irq_lock` is the `irq_chip` bus lock protecting `tps->irq_mask` updates and syncing `irq_mask` to `TPS65217_REG_INT` on unlock; never modify `tps->irq_mask` or `TPS65217_REG_INT` mask bits outside `tps->irq_lock`. [F21]
- All Viperboard sub-drivers alias `vb->buf` in `struct vprbrd` to USB message structs (`vprbrd_i2c_write_msg`, `vprbrd_i2c_read_msg`, `vprbrd_i2c_addr_msg`, `vprbrd_i2c_status`) in place; `vb->lock` must be held across populating `vb->buf`, running the USB transfer(s), and reading response fields from `vb->buf`. [F49]
- All syscon lookup/registration functions in `include/linux/mfd/syscon.h`, inline I2C/regmap helpers in `include/linux/mfd/twl.h` (`twl_i2c_read_*`, `twl_i2c_write_*`) and `include/linux/mfd/tps65090.h` (`tps65090_read`, `tps65090_write`, `tps65090_set_bits`, `tps65090_clr_bits`), and all `tps65010_*`/`tps65013_*` helpers in `include/linux/mfd/tps65010.h` except `tps65010_set_vbus_draw()` sleep on mutexes, I2C transfers, or `GFP_KERNEL` allocations and must only be called from sleepable process context. [F118, F120, F121]

## Object Lifetimes

- `device_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_compatible()`, and `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle*()` return a borrowed `struct regmap *` owned by `syscon_list` and neither they nor `of_syscon_register_regmap()` consume the caller's `struct device_node *np` reference. Broken by: calling `regmap_exit()` on a syscon regmap or omitting `of_node_put(np)`. [F1]
- `struct ucb1x00` embeds `struct device dev`; once `device_initialize(&ucb->dev)` is called, `ucb` must be freed only via `put_device()`/`device_unregister()` (`ucb1x00_release`), never direct `kfree(ucb)` or `devm_kzalloc`. Likewise, `struct ucb1x00_dev` passed to `struct ucb1x00_driver::add` and `::remove` is owned and freed by `ucb1x00_add_dev()`/`ucb1x00_remove_dev()`; sub-drivers use `dev->priv` and must never `kfree(dev)`. [F5, F97]
- `twl6040_power()` and `twl4030_audio_enable_resource()`/`twl4030_audio_disable_resource()` (`id < TWL4030_AUDIO_RES_MAX`) reference-count resources under their device mutexes and return `-EPERM` if disabled when the count is 0; callers must pair every successful enable with a disable and must not call disable if enable failed. [F6, F109]
- `wm8400_register_regulator()` writes `initdata->driver_data = wm8400` and stores `initdata` directly as `wm8400->regulators[reg].dev.platform_data` without copying; callers must pass a writable, non-stack `struct regulator_init_data *` and a `reg` index in `0..5` (`WM8400_LDO1`..`WM8400_DCDC2`). [F10]

## API Contracts

- Sub-device callers of `tps65090_write()`, `tps65090_read()`, `tps65090_set_bits()`, and `tps65090_clr_bits()` (`include/linux/mfd/tps65090.h`) and of all `tps6586x_*()` helpers (`include/linux/mfd/tps6586x.h`) must pass the MFD parent `pdev->dev.parent`, never the child `&pdev->dev`, because these helpers downcast `dev` / `dev_get_drvdata(dev)` directly to the parent `struct tps65090 *` or `struct i2c_client` / `struct tps6586x *`. [F85, F86]
- `ti_tscadc_dev_get()` dereferences `p->dev.platform_data` as `struct ti_tscadc_dev **` because `mfd_add_devices()` `kmemdup`s `cell->platform_data`; MFD cells for `struct ti_tscadc_dev` must set `cell->platform_data = &tscadc` and `cell->pdata_size = sizeof(tscadc)`. [F50]
- `classdev_to_ucb1x00(cd)` uses `container_of(cd, struct ucb1x00, dev)` and may only be called on the `ucb1x00_class` `&ucb->dev`, never on `mcp->attached_device` or a child `platform_device`'s `&pdev->dev`. [F84]
- Callers of `ucb1x00_reg_read()`, `ucb1x00_reg_write()`, `ucb1x00_io_read()`, `ucb1x00_io_write()`, and `ucb1x00_io_set_dir()` must hold an active SIB clock reference from `ucb1x00_enable()` (paired with `ucb1x00_disable()`) or `ucb1x00_adc_enable()`. `ucb1x00_io_set_dir(ucb, in, out)` and `ucb1x00_io_write(ucb, set, clear)` take `UCB_IO_0`..`UCB_IO_9` bitmasks (not pin indices), where `in` overrides `out` and `clear` overrides `set`. [F13, F35]
- Use `syscon_node_to_regmap()` instead of `device_node_to_regmap()` when a DT node has a custom regmap driver or managed clocks/resets, because `device_node_to_regmap()` passes `check_res = false` and skips clock/reset handling. Every declaration under `#ifdef CONFIG_MFD_SYSCON` in `include/linux/mfd/syscon.h` must have a matching `#else` stub returning `NULL` for `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_optional()`, `ERR_PTR(-ENOTSUPP)` for other regmap lookups, and `-EOPNOTSUPP` for `of_syscon_register_regmap()`. [F45, F88]
- In `include/linux/mfd/syscon/atmel-smc.h`, `atmel_hsmc_get_reg_layout()` returns `NULL` for legacy SMC, a `const struct atmel_hsmc_reg_layout *` for HSMC, or `ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)` on an unmatched node. HSMC controllers must use `ATMEL_HSMC_*`, `atmel_hsmc_cs_conf_apply()`, and `atmel_hsmc_cs_conf_get()` with a non-NULL layout (reading/writing `conf->timings` at stride `0x14`), whereas legacy SMC uses `atmel_smc_cs_conf_apply()`/`atmel_smc_cs_conf_get()` (stride `0x10`, ignoring `timings`). [F24, F52, F90]
- `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_timing()`, `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_setup()`, `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_pulse()`, and `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_cycle()` update only single bitfields of `struct atmel_smc_cs_conf`, so callers must initialize the struct with `atmel_smc_cs_conf_init()` or `atmel_*smc_cs_conf_get()` first and pass a valid `ATMEL_HSMC_TIMINGS_*_SHIFT` or `ATMEL_SMC_*_SHIFT` constant (`set_cycle` only accepts `ATMEL_SMC_NWE_SHIFT` or `ATMEL_SMC_NRD_SHIFT`). [F25, F77]
- `tc3589x_set_bits(tc3589x, reg, mask, val)` computes `ret &= ~mask; ret |= val;` without masking `val` by `mask`, so callers must ensure `val` has no bits set outside `mask`. [F32]
- `tps65010_set_gpio_out_value()` and `tps65010_set_led()` require 1-based indices (`GPIO1..GPIO4` = `1..4`, `LED1..LED2` = `1..2`), whereas `struct tps65010_board.outmask` and `gpio_chip` callbacks use 0-based offsets (`0..3` for GPIOs, `4..5` for LEDs). [F61]

## Error Handling

- `device_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_compatible()`, `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle()`, and `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_args()` return `ERR_PTR()` on failure and never `NULL` (check `IS_ERR()`), whereas `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_optional()` returns `NULL` when the property is absent or `!CONFIG_MFD_SYSCON` and `ERR_PTR()` on other errors (check both `IS_ERR()` and `NULL`). [F22, F73]
- `twl_i2c_read_u16()` converts between `__le16` and host `u16` (like `twl_i2c_write_u16()`), and unconditionally overwrites `*val = le16_to_cpu(value)` with uninitialized stack memory when `twl_i2c_read()` fails; callers must pass host-endian `u16` and check the return status before reading `*val`. [F26, F55]
- `twl6040_reg_read()`, `twl6040_get_vibralr_status()`, `tc3589x_reg_read()`, `twl4030_audio_enable_resource()`, `twl4030_audio_disable_resource()`, `twl_get_hfclk_rate()`, and `twl6040_get_pll()` return non-negative values on success (including `0` for `TWL6040_SYSCLK_SEL_LPPLL` or 8-bit register values) or negative error codes on failure; callers must store the return value in a signed `int` and check `< 0`. [F27, F28]

## External Contracts

- In `include/linux/mfd/viperboard.h`, `__packed` wire structs `struct vprbrd_i2c_write_hdr` (`sizeof == 9`), `struct vprbrd_i2c_write_msg`, `struct vprbrd_i2c_read_hdr`, and `struct vprbrd_i2c_addr_msg` place `u16` fields (`addr`, `spi`, `tf1`, `tf2`, `len`) at odd byte offsets and must be encoded with `cpu_to_le16()` without reordering fields or shrinking `vb->buf` below `VPRBRD_I2C_MSG_LEN` (512). [F54, F83]

## Known Pitfalls

- `tps65090_set_bits()` and `tps65090_clr_bits()` take a 0-based bit number (`uint8_t bit_num`) and compute `BIT(bit_num)` internally, unlike `tps6586x_set_bits()`, `twl6040_set_bits()`, `tps65217_set_bits()`, `tps65218_set_bits()`, and `tc3589x_set_bits()` which take pre-shifted bitmasks. [F30]
- `tps6586x_update(dev, reg, val, mask)` takes `val` in the 3rd parameter and `mask` in the 4th parameter, opposite to `regmap_update_bits()`, `tc3589x_set_bits()`, `tps65217_set_bits()`, and `tps65218_set_bits()`. [F31]
- In `include/linux/mfd/ti_am335x_tscadc.h`, `REG_STEPCONFIG(n)` and `REG_STEPDELAY(n)` take a 0-based step index `0 <= n < TOTAL_STEPS`, whereas `REG_SE` (`reg_se_cache`) reserves bit 0 for `CHARGE_STEP` and enables step `n` at `BIT(n + 1)`. [F60]
- `ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF(x)` in `include/linux/mfd/syscon/atmel-smc.h` computes `(((x) - 1) << 16)` without masking; callers must clamp `x` to `[ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF_MIN, ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF_MAX]` (`1..16`) to avoid underflow on `0` or spilling into `ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDFMODE_OPTIMIZED` (`BIT(20)`). [F59]
- Both `SUN4I_GPADC_CTRL1_ADC_CHAN_SELECT(x)` (`GENMASK(2, 0) & (x)`) and `SUN6I_GPADC_CTRL1_ADC_CHAN_SELECT(x)` (`GENMASK(3, 0) & BIT(x)`) take a 0-based channel index `x` (never `BIT(x)`), and must be matched to sun4i vs sun6i hardware. [F66]
- In `include/linux/mfd/tps6594.h`, register addresses encode the page index in bits `[15:8]` (`TPS6594_REG_TO_PAGE(reg)` = `(reg) >> 8`), and all `TPS6594_REG_BUCKX_*`, `TPS6594_REG_LDOX_*`, `TPS6594_REG_GPIOX_*`, and `TPS6594_BIT_*` macros require 0-based instance indices (`buck_inst` 0..4, `ldo_inst` 0..3, `gpio_inst` 0..10) despite 1-based hardware rail/pin names. [F78, F106]
- In `include/linux/mfd/tps65219.h`, `TPS65214`, `TPS65215`, and `TPS65219` reuse the same register offsets and bit positions for different LDOs (e.g., `0x05` is `TPS65219_REG_LDO3_VOUT`, `TPS65215_REG_LDO2_VOUT`, and `TPS65214_REG_LDO1_VOUT`), so sub-drivers must branch on `enum pmic_id` before indexing LDO registers or IRQs. [F110]
