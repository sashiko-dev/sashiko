# USB TYPEC PORT CONTROLLER DRIVERS

This component implements the USB Type-C Port Manager (TCPM) state machine and USB Power Delivery (PD) protocol stack (`tcpm.c`) along with the standard USB Type-C Port Controller Interface (TCPCI) hardware driver (`tcpci.c`). TCPM manages Type-C connection states, role swaps, PDO/APDO and PPS negotiation, VDMs, alternate modes, and cable plug (SOP') discovery via `struct tcpc_dev`.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- When `"pd-disable"` is set (`!port->pd_supported`), `tcpm_fw_get_caps()` returns without allocating `port->pds`/`port->pd_list` or populating `port->src_pdo`/`port->snk_pdo`; callers must guard `port->pds` and check `port->pd_supported` before reading PDO arrays. [F27]
- Every `struct usb_power_delivery_capabilities_desc` (`tcpm_register_source_caps()`, `tcpm_register_sink_caps()`, `tcpm_fw_get_caps()`) must have trailing `pdo[]` slots zeroed because `tcpm_pd_set()` and `usb_power_delivery_register_capabilities()` scan `pdo[]` up to `PDO_MAX_OBJECTS` until a `0` entry. [F95]
- USB PD RDO indices are 1-based while `port->src_pdo` and `port->source_caps` are 0-based: `tcpm_pd_check_request()` must reject `!index || index > port->nr_src_pdo` before indexing `port->src_pdo[index - 1]`, and `tcpm_pd_build_request()` / `tcpm_pd_build_pps_request()` must pass `src_pdo_index + 1` to RDO macros. [F57]
- SOP (`TCPC_TX_SOP`) and SOP' (`TCPC_TX_SOP_PRIME`) must maintain separate message IDs (`message_id`/`rx_msgid` vs `message_id_prime`/`rx_msgid_prime`), revisions (`negotiated_rev` vs `negotiated_rev_prime`), and mode tables (`mode_data`/`partner_altmode` vs `mode_data_prime`/`plug_prime_altmode`). Updating `port->negotiated_rev` or downgrading the partner's SVDM version on SOP `CMD_DISCOVER_IDENT` must clamp `port->negotiated_rev_prime` or the cable's SVDM version down to match if higher. [F46, F47]
- Before scheduling SOP `DISCOVER_IDENTITY` under an explicit contract in `SRC_READY` or `SNK_READY`, `run_state_machine()` must call `tcpm_set_initial_svdm_version(port)`. In `DR_SWAP_CHANGE_DR`, `tcpm_unregister_altmodes(port)` must be called unconditionally before toggling `port->data_role`. [F40, F42]
- In `vdm_run_state_machine()`, `port->vdo_data[0]` must be cleared to `0` once a VDM is transmitted in `VDM_STATE_SEND_MESSAGE`, when `CMD_DISCOVER_IDENT` gets `-EAGAIN` from `tcpm_ams_start()`, or before `VDM_STATE_ERR_SEND` queues a fallback SOP `CMD_DISCOVER_SVID`. In `vdm_state_machine_work()`, the loop must stop when `port->vdm_state` is `VDM_STATE_SEND_MESSAGE` or `VDM_STATE_BUSY`. [F41, F81]
- Neither `PORT_RESET` nor `tcpm_reset_port()` may clear `port->pd_events`, so asynchronous events like `TCPM_VBUS_EVENT` are preserved. During `PORT_RESET`, `tcpm_set_cc()` must set `TYPEC_CC_OPEN` when `port->self_powered` is true, and default role termination (`TYPEC_CC_RD` or `tcpm_rp_cc(port)`) only when false. [F37, F106]
- `_tcpm_pd_hard_reset()` must return early when `port->state` is `TOGGLING`, `ERROR_RECOVERY`, `PORT_RESET`, or `PORT_RESET_WAIT_OFF`. For `SNK_WAIT_CAPABILITIES`, `run_state_machine()` must pass `upcoming_state` (using `SNK_WAIT_CAPABILITIES_TIMEOUT` only when `!port->self_powered`) to `tcpm_set_state()`, and `hard_reset_state()` must return `SNK_READY` for both states. [F71, F72]
- In `tcpm_pd_ctrl_request()`, `PD_CTRL_REJECT`, `PD_CTRL_WAIT`, or `PD_CTRL_NOT_SUPP` in `GET_STATUS_SEND` or in `SRC_READY`/`SNK_READY` with `port->vdm_state > VDM_STATE_READY` must be handled without falling through to soft reset. [F73]
- `port->rx_msgid` and `port->rx_msgid_prime` must be initialized to `-1` on port reset, startup, and soft reset, and `tcpm_pd_rx_handler()` must exempt `PD_CTRL_SOFT_RESET` on `TCPC_TX_SOP` from duplicate `msgid` dropping. In `tcpm_send_queued_message()`, `port->queued_message` must be snapshotted and reset to `PD_MSG_NONE` before transmitting. [F76, F82]
- Setting `port->vbus_present = true` (in `_tcpm_pd_vbus_on()` or `tcpm_init()`) must clear `port->vbus_vsafe0v = false`. In `run_state_machine()`, `port->potential_contaminant` must be evaluated against `port->enter_state` before `port->enter_state = port->state` overwrites it. [F52, F74]
- When `op_mw < port->operating_snk_mw` in `tcpm_pd_build_pps_request()`, `op_ma` and `out_mv` must use ceiling division and round up to `RDO_PROG_CURR_MA_STEP` / `RDO_PROG_VOLT_MV_STEP`, whereas `tcpm_pps_set_op_curr()` and `tcpm_pps_set_out_volt()` must round down. [F86]
- Wire `struct pd_message` payloads are `__le32`: `tcpm_pd_data_request()` and `tcpm_handle_vdm_request()` must convert words with `le32_to_cpu()`, and TX paths must use `cpu_to_le32()`. In `svdm_consume_svids()`, each VDO unpacks two 16-bit SVIDs and must stop if `!svid` or `pmdata->nsvids >= SVID_DISCOVERY_MAX` before each write. [F60, F62]
- In `tcpci_irq()`, `TCPC_ALERT_RX_STATUS` must be excluded from the initial `TCPC_ALERT` clear and cleared only after reading `TCPC_RX_BYTE_CNT`, `TCPC_RX_HDR`, and `TCPC_RX_DATA`; `payload_cnt` is `cnt - (1 + sizeof(msg.header))` only when `cnt > 3` (else `0`), clamped to `sizeof(msg.payload)`. In `tcpci_set_vbus()`, `!source` and `!sink` disable writes must precede `source` and `sink` enable writes. [F54, F66, F68]

## Synchronization
- In role-swap and PPS operations (`tcpm_dr_set`, `tcpm_pr_set`, `tcpm_vconn_set`, `tcpm_pps_set_op_curr`, `tcpm_pps_set_out_volt`, `tcpm_pps_activate`), acquire `port->swap_lock` before `port->lock`, and release `port->lock` before waiting on `port->swap_complete` or `port->pps_complete` while keeping `port->swap_lock` held. [F12]
- In `tcpm_pd_transmit()`, call `reinit_completion(&port->tx_complete)` and `port->tcpc->pd_transmit()` under `port->lock`, unlock `port->lock` across `wait_for_completion_timeout(&port->tx_complete, ...)`, and re-acquire `port->lock` before inspecting `port->tx_status` or returning. [F13]
- In `tcpm_handle_vdm_request()`, set `port->vdm_sm_running = true` before dropping `port->lock` to call `typec_altmode_*` or `typec_cable_altmode_vdm()`, and re-lock `port->lock` before calling `tcpm_queue_vdm()` or returning. Altmode callbacks (`tcpm_altmode_ops`, `tcpm_cable_ops`) must never take `port->lock` synchronously; they queue `struct altmode_vdm_event` via `tcpm_queue_vdm_unlocked()`, and `tcpm_queue_vdm_work()` verifies `port->state` under `port->lock`. [F15, F16]
- Event notifications (`tcpm_cc_change()`, `tcpm_vbus_change()`, `tcpm_pd_hard_reset()`, etc.) must update `port->pd_events` under `port->pd_event_lock`, and `tcpm_pd_event_handler()` must snapshot and clear `port->pd_events` under `port->pd_event_lock` (inside `port->lock`) and drop `port->pd_event_lock` before running handlers. [F19]
- In `tcpm_set_state()`, immediate transitions (`delay_ms == 0`) must not call `mod_tcpm_delayed_work(port, 0)` when `port->state_machine_running` is true. [F20]
- All accesses to `port->logbuffer`, `logbuffer_head`, and `logbuffer_tail` in `_tcpm_log()`, `tcpm_debug_show()`, and `tcpm_debugfs_exit()` require `port->logbuffer_lock`, and `tcpm_unregister_port()` must call `tcpm_debugfs_exit()` only after `kthread_destroy_worker()` and `tcpm_reset_port()` finish. [F21]

## Object Lifetimes
- `tcpm_register_port()` borrows `tcpc->fwnode`, so callers like `tcpci_register_port()` that acquire `tcpc.fwnode` via `device_get_named_child_node()` must call `fwnode_handle_put(tcpci->tcpc.fwnode)` on `tcpm_register_port()` failure and after `tcpm_unregister_port()`. In `tcpm_fw_get_caps()`, every exit from the `port->pd_count` loop must pass through `put_caps:` (putting `caps` when `caps != fwnode`) and `put_capabilities:`. [F1, F2]
- Whenever `port->partner_source_caps` is unregistered via `usb_power_delivery_unregister_capabilities()` (in `tcpm_register_source_caps()`, `tcpm_reset_port()`, `SOFT_RESET`, `SOFT_RESET_SEND` for SOP, and `PR_SWAP_SNK_SRC_SINK_OFF`), it must immediately be set to `NULL`, and callers must not `kfree()` `sink_cap` or `source_cap` after unregistering. [F6, F102]
- In `tcpm_reset_port()`, unregister and null children before parents: `tcpm_unregister_altmodes()` before `tcpm_typec_disconnect()` (`plug_prime` before `cable`, `partner_pd` detach before `partner`), and `partner_sink_caps`/`partner_source_caps` before `partner_pd`. In `tcpm_typec_disconnect()`, `port->plug_prime` and `port->cable` must be unregistered and nulled outside `if (port->connected)`. [F7, F51]
- `tcpm_pd_receive()` and `tcpm_queue_vdm_unlocked()` transfer ownership of heap-allocated `struct pd_rx_event` and `struct altmode_vdm_event` (plus `event->data`) to `port->wq` on `kthread_queue_work()` success (freeing them on failure), and `tcpm_pd_rx_handler()` / `tcpm_queue_vdm_work()` must `kfree()` them on every exit path. [F8]
- In `tcpm_unregister_port()`, set `port->registered = false` and complete `kthread_destroy_worker(port->wq)` before calling `hrtimer_cancel()` on all port hrtimers, and every hrtimer handler must check `port->registered` before queueing work. [F17]
- In `tcpci_probe()`, mask `TCPC_ALERT_MASK` to 0 before `tcpci_register_port()` and `devm_request_threaded_irq()`, and unmask to `chip->tcpci->alert_mask` only after requesting the IRQ; in `tcpci_remove()`, always call `tcpci_unregister_port()` even if masking fails. `tcpci_suspend()` and `tcpci_resume()` must branch identically on `device_may_wakeup(dev)` to pair `enable_irq_wake()`/`disable_irq_wake()` and `TCPC_ALERT_MASK` save/restore. [F18, F114]
- `port->partner_desc.identity`, `port->cable_desc.identity`, and `port->psy_desc.name` must point to `struct tcpm_port`-embedded or `devm`-managed memory, never stack buffers. [F98]

## API Contracts
- `_tcpm_log` locks `port->logbuffer_lock` and allocates with `GFP_KERNEL`, so `tcpm_log`, `tcpm_log_force`, `tcpm_pd_transmit_complete`, and `tcpm_tcpc_reset` require sleepable context, whereas `tcpm_pd_receive` must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and avoid `tcpm_log`. [F120]
- In `tcpm_pd_transmit()` and `tcpc_dev.pd_transmit` callbacks (`tcpci_pd_transmit()`), `msg` is `NULL` for `TCPC_TX_HARD_RESET` and `TCPC_TX_BIST_MODE_2`, so check `msg` before dereferencing. In `tcpm_queue_vdm()` and `tcpm_queue_vdm_unlocked()`, `cnt` excludes `header`, so callers whose count includes the header (`tcpm_altmode_vdm`, `tcpm_cable_altmode_vdm`, `tcpm_handle_vdm_request`) must pass `count - 1`. [F28, F92]
- `tcpm_port` uses mV, mA, and mW (`tcpm_set_current_limit(port, max_ma, mv)` takes mA then mV), whereas `power_supply` properties and `"op-sink-microwatt"` use uV, uA, and uW (factor of 1000). [F34]
- When `tcpm_pd_set()` switches `port->selected_pd`, it must update `port_source_caps`/`port_sink_caps`, call `typec_port_set_usb_power_delivery(p, NULL)` before assigning `port->selected_pd = pd`, and then re-link `port->selected_pd`. In `tcpm_set_roles()`, a role mismatch with `TYPEC_PORT_DFP` or `TYPEC_PORT_UFP` returns `-ENOTSUPP`, and `usb_role` must be `USB_ROLE_NONE` when `TYPEC_SINK` on DFP-only or `TYPEC_SOURCE` on UFP-only. [F39, F136]
- `tcpci_register_port()` must not touch `dev` driver data or assume `dev_get_drvdata(dev)` is `struct tcpci_chip *`, and must only wire optional `tcpci->tcpc` callbacks (`is_vbus_vsafe0v`, `enable_auto_vbus_discharge`, `set_auto_vbus_discharge_threshold`, `check_contaminant`, `set_orientation`) when enabled in `tcpci->data`. `tcpci_data.set_vbus` returns `< 0` on error, `> 0` to bypass standard `TCPC_COMMAND` writes, and `0` to proceed. [F23, F96, F107]
- In `tcpci_set_auto_vbus_discharge_threshold()`, `requested_vbus_voltage_mv == 0` writes `0` directly; for `TYPEC_PWR_MODE_PD` (non-FRS), compute threshold from `apdo_min_voltage_mv * VPPS_SHUTDOWN_MIN_PERCENT / 100 - VSINKPD_MIN_IR_DROP_MV` when `pps_active`, or from `requested_vbus_voltage_mv` with `VSRC_NEW_MIN_PERCENT`, `VSRC_VALID_MIN_MV`, and `VSINKDISCONNECT_PD_MIN_PERCENT` when false, dividing by `TCPC_VBUS_SINK_DISCONNECT_THRESH_LSB_MV` (25 mV). [F35, F83]

## Error Handling
- In `tcpm_src_attach()`, enable VCONN, VBUS, roles/mux, and PD RX in order, and unwind in exact reverse order via `out_disable_mux:`, `out_disable_vbus:`, and `out_disable_vconn:`. [F5]
- Because `tcpm_typec_connect()` sets `port->connected = true` even if `typec_register_partner()` fails (leaving `port->partner == NULL`), and `svdm_consume_identity_sop_prime()` stores `ERR_PTR` returns directly into `port->cable` and `port->plug_prime`, always check `port->partner` even when `port->connected` holds, check `port->cable` / `port->plug_prime` with `IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`, and convert `IS_ERR(altmode)` to `NULL` in `tcpm_register_partner_altmodes()` / `tcpm_register_plug_altmodes()`. [F24, F25, F26]
- In `tcpm_pd_svdm()`, initialize `svdm_version` unconditionally on `TCPC_TX_SOP_PRIME` before checking `port->cable`, and drop `CMDT_INIT` SVDMs received on `TCPC_TX_SOP_PRIME`. In `tcpm_pd_ext_msg_request()`, require `PD_EXT_HDR_CHUNKED` and `data_size <= PD_EXT_MAX_CHUNK_DATA` (26). [F29, F61]
- Clamp fwnode property counts with `min(ret, PDO_MAX_OBJECTS)` for PDOs and `min(ret, VDO_MAX_OBJECTS)` for VDOs, and require count `4` for `"pd-revision"`. Regmap helpers in `tcpci.c` (`tcpci_check_std_output_cap()`, `tcpci_get_vbus()`) that return negative errnos alongside `0`/`1` must return `int`, not `bool`. [F22, F64]

## External Contracts
- In `tcpci_pd_transmit()`, scale `pd_header_cnt(header)` by 4 for byte length `cnt`, set `TCPC_TX_BYTE_CNT` to `cnt + 2`, and when `TX_BUF_BYTE_x_hidden` is set, write `[cnt + 2, msg->header, msg->payload]` in one `regmap_raw_write()` to `TCPC_TX_BYTE_CNT`. [F93]
- `tcpm_fw_get_caps()` must call `fw_devlink_purge_absent_suppliers(fwnode)` before parsing properties, and non-empty `"sink-vdos"` in `tcpm_fw_get_snk_vdos()` requires `"sink-vdos-v1"`. When `port->negotiated_rev < PD_REV30`, `tcpm_pd_send_source_caps()` and `tcpm_pd_send_sink_caps()` must filter PDOs through `tcpm_forge_legacy_pdo()`. [F133, F134]

## Known Pitfalls
- After `tcpm_pd_transmit()` or its wrappers in `run_state_machine()`, use `tcpm_set_state_cond()` rather than `tcpm_set_state()` whenever a concurrent event while `port->lock` was dropped could have changed `port->state`. [F14]
- In `tcpm_handle_vdm_request()`, dispatch between `typec_cable_altmode_vdm()` and `typec_altmode_vdm()`/`typec_altmode_notify()` using `rx_sop_type == TCPC_TX_SOP_PRIME`, not `response_tx_sop_type`. In `tcpm_pd_set()`, copy `source_desc.pdo` to `src_pdo` and `sink_desc.pdo` to `snk_pdo`, assigning the 0-based loop counter `i` directly to `nr_src_pdo`/`nr_snk_pdo` without adding 1. [F58, F69, F70]
- Guard `port->delayed_runtime` subtraction with `ktime_after(port->delayed_runtime, ktime_get())` and `port->max_wait - jiffies` with `time_is_after_jiffies(port->max_wait)` before passing unsigned delays. `_tcpm_log()` must use `do_div(ts_nsec, 1000000000)` on `u64 ts_nsec`. [F85, F90]
- `tcpci_set_cc()` must check `TCPC_POWER_STATUS_VCONN_PRES` and `TCPC_TCPC_CTRL_ORIENTATION` and keep the VCONN-sourcing CC pin at `TCPC_ROLE_CTRL_CC_OPEN`; `tcpci_set_polarity()` must read `TCPC_CC_STATUS` via `tcpci_get_cc()` when `TCPC_ROLE_CTRL_DRP` is set, clear DRP, and set the inactive CC pin to `TCPC_ROLE_CTRL_CC_OPEN`. [F43, F44]
- `tcpci_init()` must clear `TCPC_FAULT_STATUS_ALL_REG_RST_TO_DEFAULT` in `TCPC_FAULT_STATUS` and write `0xffff` to `TCPC_ALERT`. In `tcpci_irq()`, latch `irq_ret = status & tcpci->alert_mask` before `process_status:` and gate `TCPC_ALERT_EXTENDED_STATUS` handling on `tcpci->data->vbus_vsafe0v`. [F67, F108, F109]
