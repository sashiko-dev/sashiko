# COMMON INTERNET FILE SYSTEM CLIENT (CIFS and SMB3)

This unit implements the Linux SMB1/CIFS and SMB2/3 client (`fs/smb/client`), including VFS and netfs integration, TCP/multichannel connection and session lifecycle management, `cifsd` socket demultiplexing, authentication, SMB3 LZ77 compression, DFS referral walking and caching, directory caching, and reparse point handling.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct cifs_ses`, `ses->chans[0]` is the primary channel and `1..ses->chan_count - 1` are secondary channels (loops in `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()`, `__cifs_put_smb_ses()`, and `cifs_disable_secondary_channels()` must start at 1). Session list traversals in `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()`, `smb2_check_message()`, `smb2_is_valid_lease_break()`, `smb2_is_valid_oplock_break()`, and `cifs_is_network_name_deleted()` must use `pserver->smb_ses_list` (`pserver = SERVER_IS_CHAN(server) ? server->primary_server : server`), while per-channel helpers take `server`. [F104, F130, F456, F457]
- In `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()`, `ses->ses_status` is set to `SES_NEED_RECON` and `tcon_list`/`tcon_ipc` entries are marked `need_reconnect = true` and `status = TID_NEED_RECON` only when `mark_smb_session` is true or `CIFS_ALL_CHANS_NEED_RECONNECT(ses)` becomes true; when `__cifs_reconnect()` has `mark_smb_session` true, it must call `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()` first to mark all channels `CifsNeedReconnect`. In `cifs_setup_session()`, when binding a secondary channel (`!CIFS_ALL_CHANS_NEED_RECONNECT(ses)`), `ses->ses_status`, `ses->iface_last_update`, `ses->capabilities`, and `ses->auth_key` must not be modified or freed. [F69, F70, F75]
- In `smb2_compound_op()`, slot 0 of `vars->rsp_iov` and `resp_buftype` is for `SMB2_CREATE` and slot `num_cmds + 1` for `SMB2_CLOSE` (command `i` is at `i + 1`). `SMB2_OP_OPEN_QUERY` (at `cmds[0]`) and `SMB2_OP_MKDIR` do not emit separate PDUs in `rqst[]` or increment `num_rqst`. After adding 2 to `num_cmds` for `Open`/`Close` cleanup, `num_cmds` must be decremented by 2 before `replay_again` reinitializes `flags`, `oplock`, `num_rqst`, `server`, `vars`, and `resp_buftype`. [F408, F418, F446]
- Whenever an open, reopen, or oplock break sets an inode's oplock (`cifs_new_fileinfo()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, `cifs_oplock_break()`), a read-only oplock must be reset to 0 if `cifs_has_mand_locks(cinode)` is true. Unbuffered writes in `cifs_file_write_iter()` (`O_DIRECT`) or `cifs_strict_writev()` (`!CIFS_CACHE_WRITE(cinode)`) must call `cifs_zap_mapping(inode)` and reset `cinode->oplock = 0` if `CIFS_CACHE_READ(cinode)` is true. When `_cifsFileInfo_put()` empties `cifsi->openFileList`, it must call `cifs_set_oplock_level(cifsi, 0)` and set `CIFS_INO_INVALID_MAPPING` if `CIFS_MOUNT_STRICT_IO`. [F253, F254, F255]
- Under `cinode->open_file_lock`, `cifs_new_fileinfo()` must insert `FMODE_READ` `cifsFileInfo` entries at the head of `cinode->openFileList` (`list_add`) and write-only entries at the tail (`list_add_tail`), because `find_readable_file()` stops scanning at the first entry lacking `FMODE_READ`. [F205]

## Synchronization

- `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` must be held whenever modifying `server->srv_count`, `ses->ses_count` (including `cifs_smb_ses_inc_refcount()`), `tcon->tc_count`, `cifs_tcp_ses_list`, `server->smb_ses_list`, or `ses->tcon_list`. `match_server()` requires `server->srv_lock`, `match_session()` requires `ses->ses_lock` and `ses->chan_lock`, `match_tcon()` requires `tcon->tc_lock`, and all `cifs_chan_*` / `cifs_ses_get_chan_index()` helpers in `sess.c` require `ses->chan_lock`. Spinlock nesting order is `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` -> `srv_lock` -> `ses_lock` -> `iface_lock` -> `chan_lock` -> `tc_lock`. [F32, F33, F364, F365]
- `ses->session_mutex` ranks outer to `server->srv_lock`, `ses->ses_lock`, and `ses->chan_lock` (drop `chan_lock` before taking `session_mutex`). `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()` must never acquire `server->srv_lock` while holding `ses->ses_lock` or `ses->chan_lock`, and instead pins secondary channel `TCP_Server_Info` instances (`nserver->srv_count++`) onto a local list under `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` and `chan_lock` before updating `tcpStatus` under `srv_lock`. [F22, F163]
- All reads, replacements, and invocations of `mid_q_entry::callback` must be serialized by `mid->mid_lock` (via `mid_execute_callback()`). When draining `server->pending_mid_q` in `cifs_abort_connection()`, `is_smb_response()`, or `clean_demultiplex_info()`, entries must be moved to a local list under `mid_queue_lock` with `kref_get(&mid->refcount)` and `mid->deleted_from_q = true`, running `mid_execute_callback()` and `release_mid()` after dropping `mid_queue_lock`. [F21, F119]
- In `cifs_negotiate_protocol()`, `cifs_setup_session()`, and `cifs_tree_connect()`, post-RPC state updates must re-acquire `srv_lock`, `ses_lock`, or `tc_lock` and only transition status if it is still `CifsInNegotiate`, `SES_IN_SETUP`, or `TID_IN_TCON`. In `cifs_reconnect_tcon()`, after acquiring `ses->session_mutex`, `server->tcpStatus` (under `srv_lock`) and `cifs_chan_needs_reconnect()` / `ses->ses_status` (under `ses_lock` and `chan_lock`) must be revalidated before `cifs_negotiate_protocol()`. [F36, F37]
- `cifs_server_lock(server)` (`server->_srv_mutex`) must be held continuously across `allocate_mid()`, `cifs_sign_smb()`, `smb_send()`, and `sequence_number` rollback in `SendReceive()`, `SendReceiveBlockingLock()`, and `send_nt_cancel()` (decrementing `sequence_number` by 2 on `smb_send()` failure), across `sess_establish_session()`, across `smb311_update_preauth_hash()`, and when calling `reconn_set_ipaddr_from_hostname()` (which must drop `server->srv_lock` across `dns_resolve_name()`). [F30, F76, F166, F380, F415, F496]
- In `dfs_cache.c`, `cache_refresh_path()` returns a valid `cache_entry *` with `htable_rw_lock` held for reading and releases it on all `IS_ERR(ce)` paths (callers must `up_read(&htable_rw_lock)` iff `!IS_ERR(ce)`). `htable_rw_lock` must be dropped before `get_dfs_referral()`, and `lookup_cache_entry()` / `cache_entry_expired()` re-checked after `down_write(&htable_rw_lock)`. All reads/writes of `ce->tgthint` must use `READ_ONCE()`/`WRITE_ONCE()` because `dfs_cache_noreq_update_tgthint()` updates it under `down_read(&htable_rw_lock)`. [F15, F34, F35]
- `_cifsFileInfo_put()` and `cifsFileInfo_put()` must be called without holding `tcon->open_file_lock`, `cinode->open_file_lock`, or `cfile->file_info_lock` (and with `wait_oplock_handler = false` from `cifs_oplock_break()` or `cifs_close_deferred_file()`). Functions closing or reopening handles from `openFileList` (`cifs_close_deferred_file*()`, `cifs_reopen_persistent_handles()`, `cifs_get_writable_file()`) must pin entries with `cifsFileInfo_get()` or move them to a local list under `open_file_lock` before dropping the lock. [F189, F222, F283]
- In `smb2_is_valid_lease_break()` and `smb2_is_valid_oplock_break()`, locks nest as `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` -> `tcon->open_file_lock` -> `cfile->file_info_lock`. `cfile->file_info_lock` must be dropped before `cifs_queue_oplock_break(cfile)` (called under `tcon->open_file_lock` after setting `CIFS_INODE_PENDING_OPLOCK_BREAK` and `cfile->oplock_*`), and all spinlocks must be dropped before `smb2_queue_pending_open_break()`. [F215, F377]
- In `cifs_writev()` and `cifs_strict_readv()`, the netfs/VFS I/O lock (`netfs_start_io_*`, acquiring `inode->i_rwsem`) must be acquired before `cinode->lock_sem`, which ranks above `cfile->fh_mutex` (so `cifs_reopen_file()` must release `fh_mutex` before `filemap_write_and_wait()` or `cifs_relock_file()`). Write-locking `lock_sem` must use `cifs_down_write(&cinode->lock_sem)`, and `cifs_relock_file()` must use `down_read_nested(&cinode->lock_sem, SINGLE_DEPTH_NESTING)`. `cinode->lock_sem` must be held across `cifs_find_lock_conflict()`, `cifs_push_posix_locks()`, and `cifs_push_mandatory_locks()` / `smb2_push_mandatory_locks()`. [F209, F217, F318, F319, F325, F493]

## Object Lifetimes

- `cifs_get_smb_ses()` requires an active `server->srv_count` reference (and `cifs_get_tcon()` requires an active `ses->ses_count` reference): each drops the caller's reference when returning an existing object, transfers it into `ses->server` or `tcon->ses` when creating a new one, and leaves it with the caller on `ERR_PTR` return. Consequently, `cifs_mount_put_conns()` must release only the highest-level non-NULL pointer (`mnt_ctx->tcon`, else `mnt_ctx->ses`, else `mnt_ctx->server`) and clear all three to `NULL` before `free_xid()`. [F1, F2, F3]
- `cifs_put_tcon()` must return immediately when `tcon->ipc` is true (as `ses->tcon_ipc` is freed in `__cifs_put_smb_ses()`), and for non-IPC tcons must call `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` on both `tcon->query_interfaces` and `tcon->dfs_cache_work` before draining `tcon->dfs_ses_list` or freeing `tcon`. During DFS mount, `set_root_smb_session()` pins `mnt_ctx->ses` into `ref_walk_ses(rw)`, and `ref_walk_set_tcon()` moves each `ref->ses` to `tcon->dfs_ses_list` and sets `ref->ses = NULL` under `tcon->tc_lock` before queuing `tcon->dfs_cache_work`. [F7, F13, F24, F25]
- `cifs_setup_async_request()` uses `alloc_mid()` (unlinked) and cleans up on error with `release_mid(mid)`, whereas `cifs_setup_request()` uses `allocate_mid()` (linked on `pending_mid_q`) and must clean up with `delete_mid(mid)`. In `SendReceive()` and `SendReceiveBlockingLock()`, if `wait_for_response()` returns non-zero and `midQ->callback` is still non-NULL under `midQ->mid_lock`, the caller must set `midQ->callback = release_mid` and return without calling `delete_mid(midQ)`. [F11, F12]
- In `handle_mid()` (when `!mid->multiRsp`) and `cifs_check_trans2()` (for the first large Transaction2 response), ownership of `server->bigbuf` or `server->smallbuf` is transferred to `mid->resp_buf` by setting `server->bigbuf = NULL` or `server->smallbuf = NULL` (and `mid->large_buf = true`), so `cifs_demultiplex_thread()` must never dereference `bufs[i]` after calling `mid_execute_callback(mids[i])`. [F9, F149, F362]
- When `cfile->deferred` is queued for the first time in `cifs_close()`, the caller's `cifsFileInfo` reference transfers to the work without calling `_cifsFileInfo_put()`, whereas `mod_delayed_work()` must call `_cifsFileInfo_put()` (after `cifsFileInfo_get(cfile)` if `mod_delayed_work()` returned `false`). `cifs_add_deferred_close()` (under `cinode->deferred_lock`) takes ownership of `@dclose`, and any path cancelling `cfile->deferred` must call `cifs_del_deferred_close(cfile)` under `cinode->deferred_lock`. [F191, F192, F256]
- In `cifs_do_create()`, `cifs_nt_open()`, `cifs_query_reparse_point()`, `cifs_create_reparse_inode()`, and `smb2_create_reparse_inode()`, if a server open/create succeeds and a subsequent step fails, the handle must be closed (`server->ops->close()` / `CIFSSMBClose()`), any newly created reparse object deleted (`CIFSSMBDelFile()` preserving `rc`, or `smb2_unlink()`), and any allocated `newinode` released via `iput()`. [F18, F19, F58, F198, F506]
- When transferring a reparse response buffer out of `rsp_iov[i + 1]` in `smb2_compound_op()` or `data.reparse.io` in `smb2_query_reparse_point()`, the source `kvec` must be zeroed and its buftype set to `CIFS_NO_BUFFER`. Similarly, `smb311_posix_info_to_fattr()`, `cifs_open_info_to_fattr()`, `cifs_fattr_to_inode()`, `smb2_parse_native_symlink()`, and `alloc_cache_entry()` (`refs[0].path_name`) must NULL out source pointers when transferring buffer ownership. [F14, F195, F350, F361]

## API Contracts

- `smb2_compound_op()` unconditionally drops the caller's `cfile` reference via `cifsFileInfo_put(cfile)`, so any retry or fallback call must re-fetch `cfile`. Non-NULL `out_iov` and `out_buftype` arrays passed to `smb2_compound_op()` must have at least `num_cmds + 2` elements and be freed with `free_rsp_buf()`/`free_rsp_iov()` before returning or reusing. [F349, F409]
- In `cifs_readv_callback()` and `cifs_writev_callback()` (both SMB1 and SMB2), `credits.value` must be cleared to 0 and returned via `add_credits()`. Before terminating the subrequest, `NETFS_SREQ_NEED_RETRY` must be set alongside `-EAGAIN` on `MID_REQUEST_SUBMITTED` or `MID_RETRY_NEEDED`, `NETFS_SREQ_MADE_PROGRESS` set when `got_bytes > 0` or `written > 0`, and on reads `NETFS_SREQ_HIT_EOF` set (with `result = 0`) when `result == -ENODATA` or `start + trans == ictx->remote_i_size`. In `cifs_async_writev()`, `wdata` must not be accessed once `cifs_call_async()` returns 0. [F42, F89, F179, F180, F181]
- Non-standard return conventions: `decode_negTokenInit()` and `cifs_convert_address()` return `1` on success and `0` on failure; `check_smb2_hdr()` and `smb2_check_message()` return `0` on valid PDU and `1` on error; `target_share_matches_server()` and `match_target_ip()` return `0` on evaluation success and store the boolean match in `*target_match`/`*result`; `cifs_filldir()` returns `1` when the dir buffer is full (which `cifs_readdir()` must reset to `0`); `cifs_get_dfs_tcon_super()` returns `ERR_PTR` (never `NULL`), must be called without `tcon->tc_lock`, and requires `cifs_put_tcp_super(sb)`. [F44, F48, F51, F202, F227, F328, F382, F395]
- Callers of `dns_resolve_name()`, `dns_resolve_unc()`, `resolve_name()`, `cifs_convert_address()`, and `match_target_ip()` (with `extract_unc_hostname()`) must pass a `struct sockaddr_storage`-sized buffer (never a 16-byte `struct sockaddr`) and bound all hostname string operations by explicit length (`memchr`, `strncasecmp`, `%.*s`) because hostname slices inside UNC/share strings are not NUL-terminated. [F154, F309, F310]

## Error Handling

- When `cifs_get_tcp_session()`, `cifs_get_smb_ses()`, `cifs_get_tcon()`, `cifs_sanitize_prepath()`, or `smb3_fs_context_fullpath()` returns `IS_ERR()`, callers storing the result in `mnt_ctx->server/ses/tcon`, `chan->server` (in `cifs_ses_add_channel()`, which must also call `cifs_chan_clear_need_reconnect()` before decrementing `ses->chan_count`), `ctx->prepath`, `cifs_sb->prepath`, or `ctx->source` must reset the field to `NULL` before cleanup. Similarly, `dfs_referral_walk()` must reset `*rw = NULL` on `IS_ERR(*rw)`, and callers of `parse_dfs_referrals()` must initialize `*target_nodes = NULL`. [F56, F57, F187, F225, F233, F357, F393]

## External Contracts

- Wire offsets and lengths: SMB1 offsets (`ParameterOffset`, `DataOffset`, `cifs_get_reparse_point_buffer()`) are relative to `&pSMB->hdr.Protocol` (4 bytes past `pSMB`), whereas SMB2 offsets are relative to the SMB2 header start `(u8 *)io`. `smb2_get_data_area_len()` must zero `*off` and `*len` on entry, return `NULL` on error responses (`StructureSize == SMB2_ERROR_STRUCTURE_SIZE2_LE`) unless `Status == STATUS_MORE_PROCESSING_REQUIRED`, and reset both to 0 if out of range; `smb2_calc_size()` and `get_neg_ctxt_len()` must test overlap with `offset + 1 < len`. [F63, F383, F429, F442, F479]

## Known Pitfalls

- UTF-16 length and NUL conventions: `UniStrnlen()` and `cifs_strtoUTF16()` use 2-byte code-unit (`wchar_t`/`__le16`) counts, whereas `cifs_strndup_from_utf16()` and `cifs_strndup_to_utf16()` use byte lengths. Wire symlink buffers (`cifs_sfu_type()`, `parse_reparse_nfs()`, `smb2_parse_native_symlink()`) are not NUL-terminated and must be checked for even non-zero byte length and `UniStrnlen(..., len / 2) == len / 2` before `cifs_strndup_from_utf16()`. `cifs_strndup_to_utf16()` and `cifs_from_utf16()` include a trailing NUL in their returned byte length, which must be subtracted in `mknod_nfs()` (`- 2`), `SMB2_OP_RENAME`/`HARDLINK` `in_iov[i].iov_len` (`- 2`), and `cifs_filldir()` (`- nls_nullsize(nlt)`). [F275, F304, F391, F471, F473, F477, F480, F485]
- Multi-bit flag and mask checks: `cifs_enable_signing()` must test `((global_secflags & CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN) == CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN)` because `CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN` includes `CIFSSEC_MAY_SIGN`; `cifs_get_disposition()` and `cifs_do_create()` must test `(O_CREAT | O_EXCL)` and `(O_CREAT | O_TRUNC)` before single-bit `O_CREAT`; `cifs_open()` (when matching deferred-close `cfile`) and `cifs_reopen_file()` (before `cifs_posix_open()`) must mask off `O_CREAT | O_EXCL | O_TRUNC`; and `smb2_set_file_info()` must pass `cfile = NULL` whenever any timestamp in `FILE_BASIC_INFO` is non-zero. [F109, F276, F277, F278, F410]
