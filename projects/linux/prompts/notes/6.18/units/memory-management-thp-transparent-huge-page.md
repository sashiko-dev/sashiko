# MEMORY MANAGEMENT - THP (TRANSPARENT HUGE PAGE)

This unit implements Transparent Huge Page (THP) and multi-size THP (mTHP) management in the Linux kernel, covering PMD/PUD page faults, copy-on-write, zapping, protection changes, moving, and migration entries, as well as splitting large folios/PMDs/PUDs and collapsing PTE-mapped pages or pagecache folios via `khugepaged` or `MADV_COLLAPSE`.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Anonymous folios cannot be allocated at or split to order 1 (`THP_ORDERS_ALL_ANON` excludes `BIT(0) | BIT(1)`, `uniform_split_supported()` and `non_uniform_split_supported()` reject `new_order == 1` for anon folios, and `deferred_split_folio()` ignores `folio_order(folio) <= 1`) because `folio->_deferred_list` resides in subpage 2. [F88]
- In `__folio_split()`, when splitting a file-backed or shmem folio, sub-folios with `new_folio->index >= end` (where `end` is `DIV_ROUND_UP(i_size_read(mapping->host), PAGE_SIZE)`, adjusted by `shmem_fallocend()` for shmem) must not be stored into `mapping->i_pages`; instead remove them via `__filemap_remove_folio()`, uncharge them, and drop all cache references via `folio_put_refs(new_folio, nr_pages)` rather than `folio_put()`. [F1, F97]
- Transitions between present huge PMDs and swap/migration PMDs in `set_pmd_migration_entry()`, `remove_migration_pmd()`, and `change_huge_pmd()` must preserve soft-dirty (`pmd_swp_mksoft_dirty`), uffd-wp (`pmd_swp_mkuffd_wp` / `pmd_swp_clear_uffd_wp`), and `PageAnonExclusive` (`make_readable_exclusive_migration_entry`). In `move_huge_pmd()`, move the deposited pgtable if `pmd_move_must_withdraw()`, mark present/migration PMDs soft-dirty via `move_soft_dirty_pmd()`, and clear uffd-wp on present and swap PMDs when `vma_has_uffd_without_event_remap(vma)`. [F70, F71]
- In `__split_huge_zero_page_pmd()`, capture `old_pmd` from `pmdp_huge_clear_flush()` and propagate `pmd_uffd_wp(old_pmd)` via `pte_mkuffd_wp()` to every populated zero-page PTE before `pmd_populate()`. [F72]
- When `huge_zero_folio` is installed in `get_huge_zero_folio()` or freed in `shrink_huge_zero_folio_scan()`, update `huge_zero_pfn` in lockstep to `folio_pfn(zero_folio)` or `~0UL` via `WRITE_ONCE()`, because `is_huge_zero_pfn()` identifies huge zero PMDs by comparing against `huge_zero_pfn`. [F65]
- In `__thp_vma_allowable_orders()`, check `vma_is_dax(vma)` before `vm_flags & VM_NO_KHUGEPAGED` (which only rejects when `!in_pf && !smaps`); check `!in_pf && shmem_file(vma->vm_file)` before non-anonymous global flag checks and mask `shmem_allowable_huge_orders()` with `orders`; and restrict non-anonymous, non-special (`!vma_is_special_huge(vma)`) file VMAs to `THP_ORDERS_ALL_FILE_DEFAULT` (`1..MAX_PAGECACHE_ORDER`), reserving `THP_ORDERS_ALL_SPECIAL` for DAX/PFNMAP. [F103, F104, F153]
- In `hpage_collapse_scan_file()`, increment `swap` by `1 << xas_get_order(&xas)` and `present` by `folio_nr_pages(folio)` (not `1`) because they are compared against base-page thresholds (`khugepaged_max_ptes_swap` and `HPAGE_PMD_NR - khugepaged_max_ptes_none`). [F157]
- `struct mthp_stat` only allocates `stats` up to `PMD_ORDER`, so `mod_mthp_stat()` must guard with `if (order <= 0 || order > PMD_ORDER) return;` and `sum_mthp_stat()` must only be used for `1 <= order <= PMD_ORDER`. [F91]

## Synchronization

- In `__folio_split()`, `folio_ref_freeze(folio, 1 + extra_pins)` and `list_del_init(&folio->_deferred_list)` must run under `ds_queue->split_queue_lock` with IRQs disabled. Any removal from `ds_queue->split_queue` (`__folio_split()`, `__folio_unqueue_deferred_split()`, `deferred_split_scan()`) must under `split_queue_lock` decrement `split_queue_len`, call `list_del_init()`, clear `PG_partially_mapped` via atomic `folio_clear_partially_mapped()`, and decrement `MTHP_STAT_NR_ANON_PARTIALLY_MAPPED`. [F33, F35, F66]
- In `__folio_split()` for file/shmem folios, call `xas_split_alloc()` and `i_size_read(mapping->host)` before `local_irq_disable()` and `xas_lock(&xas)`. [F40]
- In `__split_folio_to_order()`, initialize each sub-folio's `flags`, `mapping`, `index`, `private`, and `swap` fields and execute `smp_wmb()` before `clear_compound_head(new_head)` clears `PageTail` while refcount is frozen; never pass `new_folio` to `folio_*` helpers before `clear_compound_head()` (and `prep_compound_page()` for `new_order > 0`). [F26, F123]
- In `__split_huge_pmd_locked()`, `__split_huge_zero_page_pmd()`, and `zap_huge_pmd()`, call `pgtable_trans_huge_withdraw()` (or `zap_deposited_table()`) only after invalidating or clearing the PMD via `pmdp_invalidate()` (only when `!is_pmd_migration_entry(*pmd)`), `pmdp_huge_clear_flush()`, or `pmdp_huge_get_and_clear_full()`. Execute `smp_wmb()` after populating PTEs before `pmd_populate(mm, pmd, pgtable)`. [F30, F38, F134]
- Before publishing a huge folio, mark it uptodate after copying/zeroing: call `__folio_mark_uptodate(folio)` before `set_pmd_at()` in `map_anon_folio_pmd()` and `collapse_huge_page()`, and call `folio_mark_uptodate(new_folio)` and `folio_ref_add(new_folio, HPAGE_PMD_NR - 1)` before `xas_store(&xas, new_folio)` in `collapse_file()`. In `collapse_huge_page()`, call `tlb_remove_table_sync_one()` after `pmdp_collapse_flush()` before `__collapse_huge_page_isolate()`. [F28, F29, F146]
- Whenever `collapse_huge_page()` or `madvise_collapse()` drops and re-acquires `mmap_lock`, call `hugepage_vma_revalidate()`. In `collapse_huge_page()`, after taking `mmap_write_lock(mm)` and revalidating the VMA, call `vma_start_write(vma)` before `check_pmd_still_valid()` and page table changes. [F36, F79]
- In `collapse_pte_mapped_thp()`, `ptl` nests inside `pml` (`pmd_lock`) when `ptl != pml`; if `userfaultfd_armed(vma)` on a private VMA, acquire `pml` before `ptl` and hold `ptl` until `pmdp_collapse_flush()` completes. [F39]
- In `move_pages_huge_pmd()`, drop caller-held `src_ptl` before `mmu_notifier_invalidate_range_start()`, `folio_lock(src_folio)`, and `anon_vma_lock_write(src_anon_vma)`, then re-lock via `double_pt_lock(src_ptl, dst_ptl)` and recheck `pmd_same()`. Update `folio_move_anon_rmap()` and `src_folio->index` only after `pmdp_huge_clear_flush()` and `!folio_maybe_dma_pinned(src_folio)` succeed (restoring `src_pmdval` via `set_pmd_at()` if pinned). [F3, F144]
- In `do_huge_pmd_wp_page()`, if `folio_trylock(folio)` fails under `vmf->ptl`, call `folio_get(folio)` before dropping `vmf->ptl` for `folio_lock(folio)`, and recheck `pmd_same(*vmf->pmd, orig_pmd)` and `PageAnonExclusive(page)` after relocking. In `do_huge_pmd_numa_page()`, if `migrate_misplaced_folio()` fails after dropping `vmf->ptl`, relock `vmf->ptl` and return immediately without restoring the PMD or calling `task_numa_fault()` if `!pmd_same(pmdp_get(vmf->pmd), vmf->orig_pmd)`. [F41, F42]
- In `change_huge_pmd()` and `change_huge_pud()`, invalidate entries in place via `pmdp_invalidate_ad()` / `pudp_invalidate()` rather than clearing to zero with `pmdp_huge_get_and_clear()` / `pudp_huge_get_and_clear()`. [F37]
- In `__discard_anon_folio_pmd_locked()`, after `pmdp_huge_clear_flush()`, place `smp_mb()` before reading `folio_ref_count(folio)` / `folio_mapcount(folio)` and `smp_rmb()` before `folio_test_dirty(folio)`. In `collapse_file()` for `!is_shmem`, place `smp_mb()` after `filemap_nr_thps_inc(mapping)` before `inode_is_open_for_write()`, and after `filemap_nr_thps_dec(mapping)` on `SCAN_COPY_MC` rollback. [F43, F44]
- In `hpage_collapse_scan_file()`, under `rcu_read_lock()`, acquire a ref with `folio_try_get()` and verify with `xas_reload()` before inspecting folio state/refcounts, releasing via `folio_put()` on all paths. [F131]
- In `khugepaged_scan_mm_slot()`, advance `khugepaged_scan.mm_slot` under `khugepaged_mm_lock` before `collect_mm_slot(slot)`. In `__khugepaged_exit(mm)`, if `slot == khugepaged_scan.mm_slot`, do not free `slot`; take and drop `mmap_write_lock(mm)` to wait for `khugepaged_scan_mm_slot()` to finish. [F31, F109]
- In `get_huge_zero_folio()`, call `folio_clear_large_rmappable(zero_folio)` before `cmpxchg()` into `huge_zero_folio`, and initialize `huge_zero_pfn` and `huge_zero_refcount` before `preempt_enable()`. [F25]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `deferred_split_scan()`, a folio retained on the unlocked local `list` must keep its ref held in `prev` via `swap(folio, prev)` until replaced or until after `list_splice_tail()`; if `split_folio(folio)` succeeds (`did_split`), `folio` is already unlinked and must not be passed to `list_del_init()` or `swap(folio, prev)`. Call `__folio_unqueue_deferred_split()` only when `folio_ref_count(folio) == 0` and before changing/clearing `folio->memcg_data`. [F7, F8, F108, F163]
- In `__folio_split()`, keep the original head `@folio` refcount-frozen until all tail sub-folios (`folio_next(folio)` to `end_folio`) are unfrozen and stored into `mapping->i_pages` (or removed if beyond EOF). In `__split_unmapped_folio()`, pass both `old_order` and `split_order` to `folio_split_memcg_refs()`, `split_page_owner()`, and `pgalloc_tag_split()` before `__split_folio_to_order()`. [F2, F6]
- In `do_huge_pmd_anonymous_page()`, `__do_huge_pmd_anonymous_page()`, `insert_pmd()`, and `copy_huge_pmd()`, a `pgtable_t` from `pte_alloc_one()` must either be deposited via `pgtable_trans_huge_deposit()` (with `mm_inc_nr_ptes(mm)`) or freed via `pte_free()` on every abort path. [F11]
- When `!IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_PERSISTENT_HUGE_ZERO_FOLIO)`, `mm_get_huge_zero_folio()` takes at most one `huge_zero_refcount` per `mm_struct` gated by `MMF_HUGE_ZERO_FOLIO`; never call `folio_put(huge_zero_folio)` on unmap, and only `shrink_huge_zero_folio_scan()` may drop `huge_zero_refcount` from 1 to 0. [F13]
- In `thpsize_create()`, after `kobject_init_and_add()` succeeds, clean up on error via `kobject_put(&thpsize->kobj)` (not `kfree(thpsize)` directly). In `start_stop_khugepaged()`, reset `khugepaged_thread = NULL` under `khugepaged_mutex` both when `kthread_run()` returns `IS_ERR()` and after `kthread_stop()`. [F14, F24]

## API Contracts

- `__pmd_trans_huge_lock()` and `__pud_trans_huge_lock()` return a locked `spinlock_t *` on a huge/swap PMD or huge PUD, and unlock the PTL and return `NULL` otherwise. `move_pages_huge_pmd()` is entered with `src_ptl` held and must return with `src_ptl` unlocked on all paths. [F9, F10]
- `collapse_huge_page()` is entered with `mmap_read_lock(mm)` held and always returns with `mmap_lock` unlocked (`hpage_collapse_scan_pmd()` must set `*mmap_locked = false`). `madvise_collapse()` is entered with `mmap_read_lock(mm)` held and must always return with it held, setting `*lock_dropped = true` if dropped and pinning `mm` across drops with `mmgrab(mm)` / `mmdrop(mm)`. [F15, F23]
- `__folio_split()` requires `new_order < folio_order(folio)` and `page_folio(split_at) == folio && page_folio(lock_at) == folio`; for file folios, it requires `folio->mapping != NULL`, `new_order >= mapping_min_folio_order(folio->mapping)`, and `filemap_release_folio()`. `split_at` selects the sub-folio split to `new_order`, while `lock_at` selects the post-split folio left locked. On non-uniform split, `-ENOMEM` from `xas_try_split()` may leave `@folio` partially split. [F48, F87, F116, F159]
- `thp_vma_allowable_orders()` takes/returns an order bitmask (`BIT(order)`), while `thp_vma_allowable_order()` and `thp_vma_suitable_order()` take an integer `order`. Callers of `highest_order()` / `next_order()` must check `orders != 0` before shifting by `order`. [F62, F119]
- `split_huge_pmd_locked()` and `unmap_huge_pmd_locked()` require `address` aligned to `HPAGE_PMD_SIZE`, whereas `__split_huge_pmd()` masks `address` with `HPAGE_PMD_MASK`. [F64]
- `vmf_insert_folio_pmd()` / `vmf_insert_folio_pud()` require `folio_order(folio)` to be `PMD_ORDER` / `PUD_ORDER`; `insert_pmd()` / `insert_pud()` must check `fop.is_folio` and map refcounted folios via `folio_mk_pmd()` / `folio_mk_pud()` without `pmd_mkspecial()` / `pud_mkspecial()`. [F52, F99, F151]
- In `collapse_file()`, call `mapping_set_update(&xas, mapping)` before mutating `mapping->i_pages`, and on synchronous collapse (`cc && !cc->is_khugepaged`) override `SCAN_SUCCEED` to `SCAN_PTE_MAPPED_HUGEPAGE`. [F50, F156]

## Error Handling

- In `collapse_huge_page()`, after `_pmd = pmdp_collapse_flush()`, any failure in `__collapse_huge_page_isolate()` or `__collapse_huge_page_copy()` must restore `*pmd` via `pmd_populate(mm, pmd, pmd_pgtable(_pmd))` before releasing isolated pages, while success must set `folio = NULL`. [F16]
- In `__collapse_huge_page_isolate()`, large folios are deduplicated onto `compound_pagelist`; `release_pte_pages()` and `__collapse_huge_page_copy_succeeded()` must skip `folio_test_large(folio)` in the PTE loop and release them once from `compound_pagelist`. `release_pte_pages(pte, _pte, compound_pagelist)` takes `_pte` as an exclusive upper bound and must filter out `pte_none()` and `is_zero_pfn()` before `pfn_folio()`. [F17, F55, F95]
- In `collapse_file()`, if `alloc_charge_folio()` fails (`new_folio == NULL`), jump to `out:` (not `rollback:`). After `shmem_charge(mapping->host, nr_none)` succeeds, any failure must clear `XA_RETRY_ENTRY` slots to `NULL`, subtract `nr_none` from `mapping->nrpages` under `xas_lock_irq(&xas)`, and call `shmem_uncharge()`. Isolated folios on `pagelist` hold `2 + folio_nr_pages(folio)` refs: drop all via `folio_put_refs()` on success, or unlock, `folio_putback_lru()`, and call `folio_put()` once at `rollback:`. [F18, F19, F57]
- In `collapse_pte_mapped_thp()`, `SCAN_PMD_NULL` and `SCAN_PMD_NONE` after locking the folio must jump to `maybe_install_pmd` (not `drop_folio`). If step 2 aborts with `nr_mapped_ptes > 0`, `abort:` must call `flush_tlb_mm(mm)`, `folio_ref_sub(folio, nr_mapped_ptes)`, and `add_mm_counter(mm, mm_counter_file(folio), -nr_mapped_ptes)`. [F21, F107]
- Store `min_order_for_split()` in a signed `int` and check `< 0` before passing to `split_huge_page_to_list_to_order()` or `folio_split()`. Check `copy_mc_user_highpage()` / `copy_mc_highpage()` for `> 0` to detect `SCAN_COPY_MC`, and translate `enum scan_result` via `madvise_collapse_errno()` in `madvise_collapse()`. [F45, F49, F51]

## Known Pitfalls

- Verify `!folio_test_anon(folio)` before passing `folio->mapping` to `address_space` helpers (`mapping_large_folio_support()`, `mapping_min_folio_order()`, `shmem_mapping()`), and check `!vma_is_anonymous(vma)` (not `vma->vm_file`) to select `hpage_collapse_scan_file()`. [F53, F106]
- In `__split_huge_pmd_locked()`, `zap_huge_pmd()`, `change_huge_pmd()`, and `madvise_free_huge_pmd()`, check `is_pmd_migration_entry()`, `is_swap_pmd()`, or `pmd_present()` and use `pfn_swap_entry_folio()` rather than `pmd_folio()`. In `copy_huge_pmd()`, check `pmd_present(pmd)` before `pmd_special(pmd)`. [F54, F105]
- In `collapse_file()`, call `xas_set(&xas, index)` before `xas_load(&xas)` after each `xas_unlock_irq(&xas)` drop, and use `xa_load(xas.xa, index)` in post-relock assertions. [F75]
- In `__collapse_huge_page_swapin()`, reset `pte = NULL` after `do_swap_page(&vmf)` unmaps `vmf.pte`. In `__split_huge_zero_page_pmd()`, pass `pte - 1` to `pte_unmap()` after advancing `pte` `HPAGE_PMD_NR` times. [F78, F125]
- In PTE scan/collapse loops, pass the current subpage `addr` (not `start_addr`) to `vm_normal_page()` and `mmu_notifier_test_young()`, and clamp `folio_pte_batch()` to `(end - addr) >> PAGE_SHIFT`. Clear `cc->node_load` and `cc->alloc_nmask` before each scan. [F60, F96, F111]
- In `vmf_insert_pfn_pmd()` / `vmf_insert_pfn_pud()`, pass the `pgprot` updated by `pfnmap_setup_cachemode_pfn()` (not `vma->vm_page_prot`) to `insert_pmd()` / `insert_pud()`. In `__thp_get_unmapped_area()`, return `0` when `!IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_64BIT) || in_compat_syscall()`, convert `IS_ERR_VALUE(ret)` from `mm_get_unmapped_area_vmflags()` to `0`, and cast `pgoff` to `loff_t` before `<< PAGE_SHIFT`. [F47, F61, F118, F158]
