# AMD POWERPLAY AND SWSMU

This unit provides the AMDGPU Dynamic Power Management (DPM) dispatch layer, user-space telemetry and control interfaces (sysfs, hwmon, and debugfs), legacy Kaveri/Kabini SMU7 DPM (`kv_dpm.c`), and the SWSMU common helpers (`smu_cmn.c`) and SMU v14.0 hardware backend (`smu_v14_0.c`, `smu_v14_0_0_ppt.c`, `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`). It manages firmware loading, C2PMSG mailbox transactions, shared DRAM tables, clock/voltage/fan/thermal policies, OverDrive, I2C transfers, and power-gating transitions.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Before casting `adev->powerplay.pp_handle` in `amdgpu_dpm.c`, verify `is_support_sw_smu(adev)` for `struct smu_context *`, or both `!is_support_sw_smu(adev)` and `!amdgpu_dpm_is_legacy_dpm(adev)` for `struct pp_hwmgr *`. [F256]
- On APU SMU v14.0.0 (`smu_v14_0_0_ppt_funcs`), `smu->clock_map`, `pwr_src_map`, `workload_map`, and `smu_dpm.dpm_context` are NULL; do not wire or call `smu_v14_0.c` helpers that dereference `dpm_context` (`smu_v14_0_set_performance_level`) or require `CMN2ASIC_MAPPING_CLK` (`smu_v14_0_set_soft_freq_limited_range`, `smu_v14_0_get_dpm_ultimate_freq`). [F33]
- In `__smu_cmn_send_msg`, write `smu->resp_reg = 0`, then `smu->param_reg = param`, and finally `smu->msg_reg = msg`. In `smu_cmn_send_smc_msg_with_param` and `smu_cmn_send_msg_without_waiting`, skip pre-send polling on `SMU_FW_INIT` to `SMU_FW_RUNTIME` transitions or when `__smu_cmn_ras_filter_msg` sees `resp == 0` for a priority/RAS message (which also rejects non-`SMU_MSG_RAS_PRI` messages with `-EACCES` when `SMU_FW_CAP_RAS_PRI` and `amdgpu_ras_get_fed_status(adev)` are active); when polling runs, abort only on `reg == SMU_RESP_NONE` or `res == -EREMOTEIO`. [F80, F105, F109]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_od_edit_dpm_table` and `smu_v14_0_2_restore_user_od_settings`, save and restore `OverDriveTable.FeatureCtrlMask` across `PP_OD_RESTORE_DEFAULT_TABLE` copies from `boot_overdrive_table` (which falls through to `PP_OD_COMMIT_DPM_TABLE`, unlike `smu_v14_0_od_edit_dpm_table`), skip it via `(u8 *)od_table + offset_of_voltageoffset` when updating `user_overdrive_table`, and zero it after upload. In `smu_v14_0_2_set_default_od_settings`, when `adev->in_suspend` and `user_od` are set, back up `user_od_table` to `user_od_table_bak` and restore editable fields after refreshing from `boot_od_table`. [F53, F54, F77, F98]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_od_edit_dpm_table` and `smu_v14_0_2_od_restore_table_single`, `PP_OD_EDIT_FAN_CURVE` must set `FanMode = FAN_MODE_MANUAL_LINEAR` and `BIT(PP_OD_FEATURE_FAN_CURVE_BIT)` in `FeatureCtrlMask`, whereas acoustic/target/min-PWM edits and fan restores must set `FanMode = FAN_MODE_AUTO`. In `smu_v14_0_2_set_power_limit`, lowering from `smu->current_power_limit > msg_limit` to `limit <= msg_limit` must first reset `od_table->OverDriveTable.Ppt = 0` and upload `od_table`. [F55, F56]
- In `smu_v14_0_set_single_dpm_table` and `smu_v14_0_2_set_default_dpm_table`, keep `dpm_table->min` and `max` equal to `dpm_levels[0].value` and `dpm_levels[count - 1].value`. When `single_dpm_table->is_fine_grained` is true, only `dpm_levels[0..1]` are populated, so `smu_v14_0_2_force_clk_levels` must clamp indices `>= 1` to `1` despite `smu_v14_0_2_print_clk_levels` printing a synthetic level `2`. [F48, F68]
- In `smu_v14_0_0_ppt.c`, allocate `clocks_table` with `max(sizeof(DpmClocks_t), sizeof(DpmClocks_t_v14_0_1))` and branch on `amdgpu_ip_version(adev, MP1_HWIP, 0) == IP_VERSION(14, 0, 1)` before casting; `MemPstateTable` is ordered highest-to-lowest (`0` is max, `NumMemPstatesEnabled - 1` is min, `count - i - 1` when printing ascending `SMU_MCLK`/`SMU_UCLK`). [F66, F67]
- In `amdgpu_dpm_change_power_state_locked`, sync `adev->pm.dpm.state = adev->pm.dpm.user_state` only when both `thermal_active` and `uvd_active` are false; when `thermal_active` is true, save `adev->pm.dpm.forced_level` before calling `pp_funcs->force_performance_level(adev, AMD_DPM_FORCED_LEVEL_LOW)` and restore it afterward. [F154, F188]
- In `amdgpu_dpm_set_powergating_by_smu`, update `adev->pm.pwr_state[block_type]` only after `pp_funcs->set_powergating_by_smu` succeeds, and bypass the `pwr_state[block_type] == pwr_state` early return for `AMD_IP_BLOCK_TYPE_VCN` when `adev->vcn.num_vcn_inst > 1`. [F184]

## Synchronization
- `smu_cmn_send_smc_msg_with_param` must hold `smu->message_lock` continuously across `__smu_cmn_ras_filter_msg`, `smu->smc_fw_state` updates, pre-send status polling, `__smu_cmn_send_msg`, post-send `__smu_cmn_poll_stat`, and `smu_cmn_read_arg`. Conversely, `smu_cmn_send_msg_without_waiting` and `smu_cmn_wait_for_response` must never acquire `smu->message_lock` internally because split-phase callers hold it across both. [F15, F16]
- Lock ordering: `adev->pm.stable_pstate_ctx_lock` -> `adev->pm.mutex` -> `smu->message_lock`. `amdgpu_set_power_dpm_force_performance_level` must hold `stable_pstate_ctx_lock` across `amdgpu_dpm_force_performance_level` and clear `adev->pm.stable_pstate_ctx = NULL` on success; `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_xfer` must acquire `adev->pm.mutex` around `smu_cmn_update_table` and unlock it before checking `r` or jumping to `fail:` to `kfree(req)`. [F11, F19, F153]
- In `smu_cmn_update_table`, call `amdgpu_asic_flush_hdp` after `memcpy` into `table->cpu_addr` before `SMU_MSG_TransferTableDram2Smu` (`drv2smu`), and call `amdgpu_asic_invalidate_hdp` after `SMU_MSG_TransferTableSmu2Dram` before `memcpy` from `table->cpu_addr` (`!drv2smu`). [F18]
- Interrupt callbacks `smu_v14_0_irq_process`, `kv_dpm_process_interrupt`, and `kv_dpm_set_interrupt_state` run in atomic/interrupt context and must not sleep, call SMU mailbox/table functions, or lock `adev->pm.mutex`: `smu_v14_0_irq_process` must use direct MMIO and `schedule_delayed_work(&smu->swctf_delayed_work, ...)`, `kv_dpm_process_interrupt` must schedule `adev->pm.dpm.thermal.work`, and `kv_dpm_set_interrupt_state` must only use `RREG32_SMC`/`WREG32_SMC`. [F20, F264, F272]
- `amdgpu_dpm_compute_clocks` must update display bandwidth and drain active rings via `amdgpu_fence_wait_empty` before acquiring `adev->pm.mutex` to call `pp_funcs->pm_compute_clocks`; callers already holding `adev->pm.mutex` must call `amdgpu_legacy_dpm_compute_clocks` (or `amdgpu_dpm_change_power_state_locked`), never `amdgpu_dpm_compute_clocks`. [F253, F266]
- In `kv_dpm.c`, drain `adev->pm.dpm.thermal.work` via `cancel_work_sync` in `kv_dpm_suspend` and `flush_work` in `kv_dpm_sw_fini` outside `adev->pm.mutex` and before `kv_dpm_fini` frees `ps` and `priv`. [F161]

## Object Lifetimes
- Borrowed pointers that must never be `kfree`d: `smu->smu_table.power_play_table` (aliased to VBIOS tables, `adev->pm.fw->data`, or `combo_pptable`), `smu->od_settings` (aliased to `&powerplay_table->overdrive_table` inside `combo_pptable` in `smu_v14_0_2_check_powerplay_table` after `smu_v14_0_2_store_powerplay_table`), `*table` from `smu_v14_0_0_get_gpu_metrics`/`smu_v14_0_2_get_gpu_metrics`, and outputs of `amdgpu_dpm_get_gpu_metrics`, `amdgpu_dpm_get_pp_table`, `amdgpu_dpm_get_smu_prv_buf_details`, and `amdgpu_dpm_get_vce_clock_state`. [F4, F5, F6, F52, F146]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_init_smc_tables`, run `smu_v14_0_2_tables_init` before `smu_v14_0_init_smc_tables` so table sizes are set before allocation; `smu_v14_0_fini_smc_tables` must `kfree` and null all allocations from `smu_v14_0_2_tables_init`, `smu_v14_0_2_allocate_dpm_context`, and `smu_v14_0_init_smc_tables`. In `smu_v14_0_0_init_smc_tables`, unwind `metrics_table`, `clocks_table`, `watermarks_table`, and `gpu_metrics_table` in reverse order on failure and `kfree`/null all four in `smu_v14_0_0_fini_smc_tables`. [F7, F8, F34]
- `smu_v14_0_fini_power` must reset both `smu_power->power_context = NULL` and `power_context_size = 0` after freeing `power_context`. `smu_cmn_is_audio_func_enabled` must call `pci_dev_put(p)` whenever `pci_get_domain_bus_and_slot` returns non-NULL `p`. `smu_v14_0_init_microcode` must call `amdgpu_ucode_release(&adev->pm.fw)` on error, and `ucode[AMDGPU_UCODE_ID_SMC].fw` borrows `adev->pm.fw` until `smu_v14_0_fini_microcode`. [F2, F3, F14]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_init`, initialize `smu_i2c->adev`, `port`, `mutex`, `control->algo`, `control->quirks`, and `i2c_set_adapdata(control, smu_i2c)` before calling `devm_i2c_add_adapter(adev->dev, control)`; because it is devm-managed, `smu_v14_0_2_i2c_control_fini` must only null `ras_eeprom_i2c_bus` and `fru_eeprom_i2c_bus` without calling `i2c_del_adapter`. [F10, F21]
- In `amdgpu_pm.c`, runtime sysfs/hwmon/debugfs handlers accessing hardware or DPM state must gate access with `amdgpu_pm_get_access` (writes/debugfs) or `amdgpu_pm_get_access_if_active` (telemetry reads) and call `amdgpu_pm_put_access` on all exit paths. [F165]
- In `amdgpu_od_set_init`, initialize `INIT_LIST_HEAD(&container->attribute)` and `container->priv = adev` before `sysfs_create_file`, and call `amdgpu_od_set_fini` if `od_kobj_list` remains singular. In `amdgpu_od_set_fini`, call `sysfs_remove_file` and `kfree` on each `struct od_attribute` before `kobject_put(&container->kobj)` (never `kfree(container)` directly). In `amdgpu_pm_sysfs_fini`, manually remove `int_hwmon_dev`, `pm_attr_list`, and `od_kobj_list`, but not devm-managed `amdgpu_pm_policy_attr_group` or `amdgpu_board_attr_group`. [F147, F166, F255, F258]

## API Contracts
- `smu_cmn_to_asic_specific_index` returns negative errnos on unmapped or restricted IDs (`-ENOTSUPP` for unmapped `CMN2ASIC_MAPPING_WORKLOAD`, which `smu_v14_0_2_get_power_profile_mode` skips via `continue` while aborting on other errors; `-EACCES` on SR-IOV VFs for messages lacking `SMU_MSG_VF_FLAG`, which `smu_cmn_send_smc_msg_with_param` translates to `0` without writing `*read_arg`; `-EINVAL` otherwise). Always check `< 0` before using the returned index in bit shifts or messages. In `smu_cmn_feature_is_enabled`, check `enabled_features == ULLONG_MAX` and return `1` before calling `smu_cmn_to_asic_specific_index`. [F60, F78, F107, F108]
- `smu_cmn_feature_is_supported` and `smu_cmn_feature_is_enabled` return boolean `1` or `0` (never negative errno). They and `smu_cmn_feature_set_enabled` / `smu_cmn_disable_all_features_with_exception` take an `enum smu_feature_mask` bit index, whereas `smu_cmn_feature_update_enable_state` and `smu_cmn_set_pp_feature_mask` take a 64-bit shifted mask (`1ULL << feature_id`). Feature mask variables passed to `smu_cmn_get_enabled_mask` or `test_bit` must be `uint64_t`, and `smu_v14_0_2_get_allowed_feature_mask` requires `num == 2` and a `uint32_t[2]` buffer. [F24, F40, F101, F102]
- `smu_cmn_send_smc_msg_with_param` overwrites `*read_arg` from `smu->param_reg` even when `__smu_cmn_reg2errno` returns an error, but leaves `*read_arg` untouched and returns `0` when `adev->no_hw_access` is set or VF access is denied (`-EACCES`); callers must initialize `*read_arg` and not pass live state variables without restoring them on error. [F26]
- `smu_v14_0_0_get_gpu_metrics` and `smu_v14_0_2_get_gpu_metrics` return `ssize_t` (negative errno on `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table` failure without touching `*table`, or positive byte size on success) and must call `smu_cmn_init_soft_gpu_metrics` on the typed `gpu_metrics_v*` buffer (sized in table init) before populating fields. In `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c`, pass `SmuMetricsExternal_t` and `DpmActivityMonitorCoeffIntExternal_t` (not the inner structs) to `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table` and `smu_cmn_update_table`. [F27, F28, F32, F97]
- `smu_v14_0_0_print_clk_levels` and `smu_v14_0_2_print_clk_levels` must call `smu_cmn_get_sysfs_buf(&buf, &size)` before `sysfs_emit_at(buf, size, ...)`. Callers of `amdgpu_dpm_emit_clock_levels` pass page-aligned `buf` and `&size`, whereas callers of fallback `amdgpu_dpm_print_clock_levels` pass `buf + size` and add its return value to `size`. [F29, F247]
- Unit and parameter contracts: `smu_table.boot_values` clocks and `AMDGPU_PP_SENSOR_GFX_{SCLK,MCLK}` are in 10 kHz (`* 10000` for Hz in hwmon, `/ 100` for MHz in DPM tables and debugfs); `smu->thermal_range.software_shutdown_temp*` are in whole degrees C while other thermal fields are millidegrees C; `smu_v14_0_2_is_od_feature_supported` takes a `PP_OD_FEATURE_*_BIT` bit index whereas `smu_v14_0_2_get_od_setting_limits` takes a `PP_OD_FEATURE_*` selector (`0..11`); and SMU DRAM table address messages must pass GPU `mc_address`, not `cpu_addr`. [F36, F38, F39, F128, F281]
- In `amdgpu_dpm.c`: `amdgpu_dpm_get_uclk_dpm_states` returns `0` or negative errno (not `PP_SMU_RESULT_OK`); `amdgpu_dpm_set_power_profile_mode(adev, input, size)` expects `profile_mode` at `input[size]` without incrementing `size`; every `amdgpu_dpm_enable_*` helper calling `amdgpu_dpm_set_powergating_by_smu` must pass `!enable`; and `kv_dpm_read_sensor` / `amdgpu_dpm_read_sensor` require `*size >= 4` initialized on entry. [F168, F174, F220, F237]

## Error Handling
- In `amdgpu_dpm_force_performance_level`, call `amdgpu_dpm_enter_umd_state` and `amdgpu_dpm_exit_umd_state` outside `adev->pm.mutex`; if `pp_funcs->force_performance_level` fails, unlock `adev->pm.mutex` and call the inverse helper to roll back the UMD state. [F152]

## Known Pitfalls
- Device pointer extraction: in `smu_v14_0_2_ppt.c` I2C callbacks, use `smu_i2c->adev` (never `to_amdgpu_device(smu_i2c)`); in `amdgpu_pm.c`, use `drm_to_adev(dev_get_drvdata(dev))` for `adev->dev` sysfs attributes, `dev_get_drvdata(dev)` directly for `int_hwmon_dev` callbacks, and `container_of(kobj, struct od_kobj, kobj)->priv` (never `kobj_to_dev(kobj)`) for `gpu_od` (`od_feature_ops`) callbacks. [F103, F245, F246]
- In `smu_v14_0_2_print_clk_levels` and `smu_v14_0_2_force_clk_levels`, every `clk_type` handled in the second `switch (clk_type)`'s DPM-level group must also bind `single_dpm_table` in the first `switch (clk_type)`. In both `smu_v14_0_0_force_clk_levels` and `smu_v14_0_2_force_clk_levels`, check `mask != 0` before computing `ffs(mask) - 1` and `fls(mask) - 1`. [F83, F92]
- In `smu_cmn_get_metrics_table`, check cache expiry with `!smu_table->metrics_time || time_after(jiffies, smu_table->metrics_time + msecs_to_jiffies(1))`, update `metrics_time` only after `smu_cmn_update_table` succeeds, and reset `metrics_time = 0` when `metrics_table` is allocated or freed. [F44, F91]
- In `kv_dpm.c`: `kv_patch_voltage_values` converts uvd/vce/samu/acp `.v` fields in place to mV once during init while `vddc_dependency_on_sclk` remains 8-bit VID indices (which are inverted and require `kv_convert_8bit_index_to_voltage` and a `count > 0` check with `sys_info` fallback); and Kaveri SMU7 SRAM (`SMU7_Fusion_DpmTable`) is big-endian (`cpu_to_be16`/`cpu_to_be32` / `be32_to_cpu`), where `amdgpu_kv_read_smc_sram_dword` requires 4-byte alignment (`(smc_address & 3) == 0`). [F181, F182, F191, F235, F273]
