# MARVELL OCTEONTX2 PHYSICAL FUNCTION DRIVER

This component implements the Marvell OcteonTX2, CN10K, and CN20K RVU Physical Function (PF) Ethernet network driver and shared PF/VF hardware configuration helpers. It manages NIX (network interface), NPA (buffer pool/aura), NPC (MCAM flow filtering), and CPT (inline IPsec) hardware blocks via an AF/PF/VF mailbox protocol, while exposing netdev, ethtool, devlink, DCBNL, MACsec, and XFRM offload interfaces.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `otx2_pfvf_mbox_init()` initializes `struct otx2_mbox` only on `pf->mbox_pfvf[0].mbox` and `pf->mbox_pfvf[0].mbox_up`; PF-VF mailbox paths must access VF `vf_idx` via `pf->mbox_pfvf[0].mbox.dev[vf_idx]` or `mbox_up.dev[vf_idx]`, never `pf->mbox_pfvf[vf_idx].mbox`. [F137]
- Per-VF and per-CQ work structs (`flr_work.work`, `mbox_wrk`, `mbox_up_wrk`, `link_event_work`, `pool_refill_work`) must stay embedded in their contiguous arrays on `struct otx2_nic` (`flr_wrk`, `mbox_pfvf`, `vf_configs`, `refill_wrk`) because handlers recover the 0-based index via pointer subtraction. [F139]
- XDP SQs sit between normal TX and QoS SQs in `pf->qset.sq[]`: `otx2_xmit()` must add `pf->hw.xdp_queues` when `qidx >= pf->hw.tx_queues`, `otx2_update_sq_stats()` must subtract `pfvf->hw.non_qos_queues` before testing `qos_sq_bmap`, `otx2_alloc_queue_mem()` must compute `non_qos_queues = tx_queues + xdp_queues`, and `otx2_cq_init()` must classify `qidx` as `CQ_RX` (`< rx_queues`), `CQ_TX` (`< non_xdp_queues`), `CQ_XDP` (`< non_xdp_queues + xdp_queues`), then `CQ_QOS`. [F60, F90, F107]
- In `otx2_get_smq_idx()`, check `qidx < NIX_PF_PFC_PRIO_MAX` before indexing `pfvf->pfc_alloc_status[]` and `pfvf->pfc_schq_list[NIX_TXSCH_LVL_SMQ][]`, and in `otx2_select_queue()`, check `vlan_prio < pf->hw.tx_queues - 1` before returning it as a PFC queue. [F88]
- PFC TX scheduler queues in `otx2_pfc_txschq_alloc_one()` and `otx2_pfc_txschq_stop_one()` are allocated/freed only for levels `0 .. pfvf->hw.txschq_link_cfg_lvl` in `pfvf->pfc_schq_list[lvl][prio]` via `otx2_txschq_free_one()`; higher levels share `pfvf->hw.txschq_list[lvl][0]` and must never be freed by PFC stop. When disabling PFC on a priority in `otx2_pfc_txschq_update()`, flush its SMQ (`otx2_smq_flush()`), stop the PFC scheduler, and call `otx2_pfc_update_sq_smq_mapping()`. [F66, F67]
- `otx2_open()` must reapply RX VLAN, hardware timestamping, `otx2_qos_config_txschq()`, `otx2_dmacflt_reinstall_flows()`, and `otx2_tc_apply_ingress_police_rules()` before `otx2_rxtx_enable(pf, true)`. At the end of `otx2_stop()`, reset `qset` with `memset_startat(qset, 0, sqe_cnt)` (keeping `rqe_cnt` and `sqe_cnt` at the top of `struct otx2_qset`) to preserve ring sizes. [F63, F64]
- `pfvf->flow_cfg` and `pfvf->ptp` can be `NULL`; all consumers (`otx2_tc_flower_rule_cnt()`, `otx2_get_maxflows()`, `otx2_dl_*`, `otx2_sq_init()`, `otx2_get_ts_info()`, `otx2_config_hwtstamp()`) must NULL-check them before dereferencing. [F46, F47]
- In `otx2_mcam_entry_init()`, `flow_cfg->def_ent[]` is partitioned into VF VLAN flows (`vf_vlan_offset = 0`), unicast filters (`unicast_offset`), and RX VLAN offload (`rx_vlan_offset`); every `def_ent[]` access must add its region offset, and user-configured `flow_cfg->ntuple_cnt` and `flow_cfg->ucast_flt_cnt` must persist across `otx2_mcam_flow_del()` and `otx2_mcam_entry_init()`. [F59, F91]
- Ethtool flow locations in `[0, flow_cfg->max_flows - 1]` map to `flow_cfg->flow_ent[location]`, while DMAC filter rules use `flow_cfg->dmacflt_bmap` bit positions (bounded by `u32` `dmacflt_max_flows`, not `OTX2_DMAC_FLTR_BITMAP_SZ`) at `flow_cfg->max_flows + flow->entry`. Bit 0 is reserved for the PF MAC: `otx2_remove_flow()` must reject deleting it with `-EPERM` and auto-remove it when `bitmap_weight == 1`, while `otx2_set_mac_address()` must update `otx2_dmacflt_update_pfmac_flow()` and `otx2_install_rxvlan_offload_flow()`. [F56, F92, F119, F143, F195]
- RQ aura buffer DMA addresses in `otx2_rq_aura_pool_init()` and `cn10k_refill_pool_ptrs()` must be offset by `+ OTX2_HEAD_ROOM` when `!pool->xsk_pool` (un-offset when `pool->xsk_pool` is set), and `otx2_free_aura_ptr()` must subtract `OTX2_HEAD_ROOM` from `AURA_NIX_RQ` IOVAs before `otx2_free_bufs()`. [F187]
- In `cn10k_macsec.c`, every SA callback must reject `ctx->sa.assoc_num >= CN10K_MCS_SA_PER_SC` with `-EOPNOTSUPP`. In `cn10k_mcs_write_keys()`, compute the AES-ECB hash on the original `sak` before byte-reversing `sak`, `hash`, and `salt` into `req->plcy[0]`. In `cn10k_mdo_upd_secy()`, call `cn10k_mcs_sync_stats()` using `txsc->last_validate_frames` and `txsc->last_replay_protect` before updating hardware policy and `last_*`, only call `cn10k_mcs_link_tx_sa2sc()` if `encoding_sa` changed, and pass `sw_tx_sa = NULL` to `cn10k_mcs_secy_tx_cfg()`. [F70, F71, F93, F99, F183]
- Guard CGX/RPM pause and link callbacks with `is_otx2_lbkvf(pfvf->pdev) || is_otx2_sdp_rep(pfvf->pdev)`, and guard CGX/RPM backpressure and PFC (`otx2_nix_config_bp()`, CQ/Aura `bp_ena`, `otx2_config_priority_flow_ctrl()`) with `!is_otx2_lbkvf(pfvf->pdev)`. [F153, F154]

## Synchronization
- Callers of `otx2_mbox_alloc_msg_*`, `otx2_sync_mbox_msg()`, and `otx2_mbox_get_rsp()` on `pfvf->mbox`, as well as `otx2_txschq_config()` and `otx2_pfc_txschq_config()`, must hold `pfvf->mbox.lock`. Conversely, `otx2_smq_flush()`, `otx2_txschq_free_one()`, `otx2_txschq_stop()`, `otx2_pfc_txschq_stop_one()`, `otx2_ctx_disable()`, `otx2_free_sq_res()`, and `otx2_free_cq_res()` acquire `mbox.lock` internally and must NOT be called with it held (unlock `mbox->lock` before `err_free_*` labels in `otx2_init_hw_resources()` and in `otx2_pfc_txschq_update()`). [F6, F9, F19, F21]
- Receive buffer allocation from `cq->rbpool` must run with bottom halves disabled or in NAPI poll: `otx2_pool_refill_task()` must only reschedule `wrk->napi` under `local_bh_disable()`/`local_bh_enable()`, and `otx2_alloc_rbuf()` must wrap `__otx2_alloc_rbuf()` in `local_bh_disable()`/`local_bh_enable()`. [F24]
- NIX completion interrupts (`NIX_LF_CINTX_INT`) are level-triggered: `otx2_cq_intr_handler()` must disable `NIX_LF_CINTX_ENA_W1C` before scheduling NAPI, and `otx2_napi_handler()` must clear `NIX_LF_CINTX_INT` only after draining CQs and before re-enabling `NIX_LF_CINTX_ENA_W1S` (unless `OTX2_FLAG_INTF_DOWN` or `!filled_cnt`). [F174]
- CN10K LMTST flush helpers (`cn10k_sqe_flush()`, `__cn10k_aura_freeptr()`, `cn10k_cpt_inst_flush()`) must issue `dma_wmb()` before copying descriptors to per-CPU `lmt_info->lmt_addr` and calling `cn10k_lmt_flush()`, and process-context callers like `cn10k_aura_freeptr()` must wrap the call in `get_cpu()`/`put_cpu()`. [F160]
- `otx2_pfvf_mbox_init()` must allocate `pf->mbox_pfvf_wq` with `max_active = 0` (never ordered/single-threaded) so `otx2_pfvf_mbox_up_handler` work items can run concurrently to ack `otx2_sync_mbox_up_msg()` waits. Before scheduling `config->link_event_work` or sending an UP message in `otx2_vf_link_event_task()`, check `if (config->intf_down)` and skip down VFs. [F23, F168]
- Asynchronous work handlers (`otx2_reset_task()`, `cn10k_ipsec_sa_wq_handler()`) must acquire `rtnl_lock()` before calling `otx2_stop()`/`otx2_open()` or `netdev_update_features()`, whereas RTNL-locked netdev callbacks (`otx2_change_mtu()`, `otx2_xdp_setup()`) must not re-acquire `rtnl_lock()`. [F171]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `otx2_remove()` and on error unwind in `otx2_probe()`, `unregister_netdev()` must be called before `cn10k_ipsec_clean()` and `cn10k_mcs_free()` because netdev unregistration invokes MACsec notifier callbacks (`cn10k_mdo_del_secy()`) that dereference `pf->macsec_cfg`. [F1]
- On teardown in `otx2_init_rsrc()`, `otx2_probe()`, and `otx2_remove()`, `otx2_detach_resources(&pf->mbox)` and synchronous AF mailbox calls must complete before `otx2_disable_mbox_intr()` and `otx2_pfaf_mbox_destroy()`. In `otx2_realloc_msix_vectors()`, call `otx2_disable_mbox_intr()` before `pci_free_irq_vectors()`. [F22, F145]
- Non-devres allocations in `otx2_probe()`/`otx2_init_rsrc()` (`pf->af_xdp_zc_qidx`, `pf->hw.lmt_info`, `pf->dync_lmt`) must be explicitly freed before `free_netdev()`, whereas devres buffers freed and reallocated while the PCI device stays bound (`pf->refill_wrk`, `pfvf->qset.pool`, `pool->xdp`, `pf->flr_wrk`, `flow_cfg->flow_ent`) must be freed with `devm_kfree()`, never `kfree()`. [F2, F152]
- In `otx2_stop()`, setting `OTX2_FLAG_INTF_DOWN` with `smp_wmb()`, masking `NIX_LF_CINTX_ENA_W1C`, `synchronize_irq()`, and `napi_synchronize()` must complete before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&pf->refill_wrk[wrk].pool_refill_work)`, `otx2_free_hw_resources()`, `otx2_disable_napi()` (which must call `cancel_work_sync(&cq_poll->dim.work)` before `napi_disable()` and `netif_napi_del()`), and `otx2_free_queue_mem()`. [F25, F26]
- `otx2_remove()` must set `OTX2_FLAG_PF_SHUTDOWN` before `unregister_netdev()`, and in `otx2_free_hw_resources()`, NIX queues and the NIX LF must be flushed, disabled, drained (`otx2_cleanup_rx_cqes()`, `otx2_cleanup_tx_cqes()`, then `otx2_free_pending_sqe()` before `otx2_free_sq_res()`), and freed via `NIX_LF_FREE` (setting `NIX_LF_DONT_FREE_TX_VTAG` when `!(pf->flags & OTX2_FLAG_PF_SHUTDOWN)`) before disabling NPA aura/pool contexts, calling `otx2_aura_pool_free()` (which must check `!pfvf->qset.pool` and set `pfvf->qset.pool = NULL`), and sending `NPA_LF_FREE`. [F4, F15, F148, F149]
- In `otx2_forward_vf_mbox_msgs()` when `dir == MBOX_DIR_PFAF`, `dst_mdev->mbase` is temporarily set to `src_mdev->mbase` under `pf->mbox.lock` and must be restored to `pf->mbox.bbuf_base` before unlocking on all paths. [F10]
- In `otx2_add_flow()`, when replacing an existing rule (`otx2_find_flow()` returns non-NULL, so `new` is `false`), update `struct otx2_flow` in place without incrementing `flow_cfg->nr_flows`, and only `kfree(flow)` on error when `new` is `true`. [F16]

## API Contracts
- `otx2_mbox_alloc_msg_*()` returns `NULL` (never `ERR_PTR`) when the mailbox buffer is full; `otx2_get_fwdata()` returns an `ERR_PTR` (check `IS_ERR(rsp)`); `cn10k_mcs_create_txsc()`/`rxsc()` return `ERR_PTR` while `cn10k_mcs_get_txsc()`/`rxsc()` return `NULL`; `otx2_alloc_mcam_entries()` returns `-ENOMEM` or the non-negative count allocated (store in signed `int` and check `<= 0`). [F36, F42, F43, F44]
- Callers sending AF mailbox messages from non-sleepable atomic contexts must use `otx2_sync_mbox_msg_busy_poll()` instead of `otx2_sync_mbox_msg()`. Indexed NIX/NPA operation registers (`NPA_LF_AURA_OP_ALLOCX`, `NIX_LF_RQ/SQ_OP_*`, `NIX_LF_CQ_OP_INT`) must be read via `otx2_atomic64_add()` rather than `otx2_read64()`. [F157, F169]
- Software VF indices (`vf`, `vf_idx`, `pf->vf_configs[vf]`, `pf->mbox_pfvf[vf]`, `set_vf_perm->vf`) are 0-based (`0 .. total_vfs - 1`), whereas `pcifunc & RVU_PFVF_FUNC_MASK`, `npc_install_flow_req->vf`, and `ethtool_get_flow_spec_ring_vf()` use 1-based VF numbers (`vf + 1`, with `0` for PF). [F50]
- On the TX path, `otx2_sqe_add_sg()` and `otx2_sqe_add_sg_ipsec()` reset `sq->sg[sq->head].num_segs = 0` and increment `num_segs` only after each successful fragment DMA mapping so `otx2_sq_append_skb()` can unmap partial fragments via `otx2_dma_unmap_skb_frags()` without `otx2_sqe_add_sg*()` freeing `skb`. When `xfrm_offload(skb)` is set, `otx2_dma_map_skb_frag()` and `otx2_dma_unmap_skb_frags()` must `skb_unshare(skb, GFP_ATOMIC)` and use `DMA_BIDIRECTIONAL`. [F18, F176]

## Error Handling
- In `otx2_open()`, if `otx2_rxtx_enable(pf, true)` fails with `-EIO`, the error path must call `otx2_rxtx_enable(pf, false)` before stopping TX queues and freeing resources. [F3]
- When `otx2_sq_init()` fails after allocating `sq->sg`, it must `kfree(sq->sg)` and set `sq->sg = NULL` before returning because `otx2_init_hw_resources()` unwinds `otx2_config_nix_queues()` via `err_free_txsch` without calling `otx2_free_sq_res()`. [F5]
- In `cn10k_mcs_create_txsc()` and `cn10k_mcs_create_rxsc()`, if any `cn10k_mcs_alloc_rsrc()` fails, free previously allocated MCS hardware resources in reverse order via `cn10k_mcs_free_rsrc()` and `kfree()` `txsc`/`rxsc` before returning `ERR_PTR(ret)`. [F7]
- In `otx2_dcbnl_ieee_setpfc()`, restore `pfvf->pfc_en = old_pfc_en` on failure, and if `otx2_pfc_txschq_update()` fails, also disable backpressure (if `pfc->pfc_en` was set), call `otx2_pfc_txschq_stop()`, and reconfigure `otx2_config_priority_flow_ctrl()`. [F8]
- If a function aborts after `otx2_mbox_alloc_msg_*()` without calling `otx2_sync_mbox_msg()`, it must call `otx2_mbox_reset(&mbox->mbox, 0)`. When batching AQ messages in a loop (`otx2_set_rss_table()`, `otx2_aura_init()`, `otx2_pool_init()`), a `NULL` return from `otx2_mbox_alloc_msg_*()` must flush via `otx2_sync_mbox_msg()` and retry before returning `-ENOMEM`. Callers returning negative errno (like `otx2_add_flow()`) must convert `MBOX_MSG_INVALID` (`0xFFFE`) from `otx2_sync_mbox_msg()` to `-EINVAL`. [F37, F38, F41]

## External Contracts
- Before forwarding VF mailbox messages to the AF (`MBOX_DIR_PFAF`) in `otx2_pfvf_mbox_handler()` and `otx2_forward_vf_mbox_msgs()`, verify `msg->sig == OTX2_MBOX_REQ_SIG`, stamp `msg->pcifunc` with `(vf_idx + 1) & RVU_PFVF_FUNC_MASK`, validate `mbox_hdr->msg_size` is non-zero and `<= dst_size`, and clamp `hdr->msg_size` to `mbox->rx_size - msgs_offset` in `otx2_sync_mbox_bbuf()`. [F80, F194]

## Known Pitfalls
- `otx2_get_fwdata()` returns a pointer into the shared mailbox buffer (`pfvf->mbox.mbox`), so callers that send another mailbox message (such as `otx2_get_phy_fec_stats()`) must call `otx2_get_fwdata()` again before reading `rsp->fwdata`. [F69]
- Cast operands to `u64` before >=32-bit shifts: `qidx` (`<< 32` in `otx2_nix_rq/sq_op_stats()` and `otx2_sqb_flush()`; `<< 44` in `otx2_q_intr_handler()`), `cq_time_wait * 10` (`<< 48`) and `cq_qcount_wait` (`<< 32`) in `otx2_config_irq_coalescing()` (also writing `cq_ecount_wait - 1`), ceiling-divided `gthr_size` (`<< 60` in `cn10k_ipsec_transmit()`), and 32-bit SSCI/RSS key words (`<< 32` in `cn10k_mcs_write_keys()` and `otx2_set_rss_key()`). [F120, F121, F122, F123, F124]
- For 64-VF interrupt registers across two banks, bank 0 uses `INTR_MASK(numvfs)` and bank 1 is accessed only when `numvfs > 64` using `INTR_MASK(numvfs - 64)`; single-VF bit operations in FLR/ME/mailbox handlers must normalize the VF index to `0..63` (`vf - 64` or `i - first`) and use `BIT_ULL()`. Clear W1C interrupt status registers (`RVU_PF_INT`, `NIX_LF_CINTX_INT`, `RVU_PF_VFFLR_INTX`) before setting `*_ENA_W1S`, and in `otx2_q_intr_handler()`, read and clear (`BIT_ULL(44)`) `NIX_LF_SQ_OP_ERR_DBG`, `NIX_LF_MNQ_ERR_DBG`, and `NIX_LF_SEND_ERR_DBG`. [F125, F126, F146, F158]
- In `__cn10k_aura_freeptr()` and `cn10k_refill_pool_ptrs()`, reserve `ptrs[0]` for the NPA header word, place buffer pointers starting at `ptrs[1]`, include the header in `num_ptrs` (`<= NPA_MAX_BURST`), and increment `size = (sizeof(u64) * num_ptrs) / 16` by 1 when `num_ptrs` is odd (`!count_eot`). Never enclose `kcalloc(..., GFP_KERNEL)` or `otx2_alloc_rbuf()` inside `get_cpu()`/`put_cpu()` in `otx2_sq/rq_aura_pool_init()`. [F95, F129, F167]
