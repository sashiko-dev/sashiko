# VIRTIO CORE AND NET DRIVERS

This unit comprises the Virtio network driver (`drivers/net/virtio_net.c`) and the Virtio crypto driver (`drivers/crypto/virtio/`). `virtio_net` implements a multi-queue paravirtualized Ethernet interface supporting small, big, and mergeable RX buffers, NAPI RX/TX polling, XDP and AF_XDP (XSK) zero-copy, RSS/hash reporting, dynamic interrupt moderation (DIM), and hardware statistics. `virtio_crypto` integrates with the kernel crypto engine to offload symmetric (AES-CBC skcipher) and asymmetric (RSA akcipher) operations over data and control virtqueues.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `virtnet_rq_get_buf()`, `virtnet_rq_unmap()`, `virtnet_rq_init_one_sg()`, and `virtnet_rq_alloc()` require `!vi->big_packets || vi->mergeable_rx_bufs` and `!rq->xsk_pool`. `struct virtnet_rq_dma` sits at offset 0 of `alloc_frag->page` while `dma->addr` maps from `head + sizeof(*dma)`, so DMA addresses/sync offsets must be relative to `head + sizeof(*dma)` and `add_recvbuf_mergeable()` must subtract `sizeof(struct virtnet_rq_dma)` from `len` when `!alloc_frag->offset && len + room + sizeof(struct virtnet_rq_dma) > alloc_frag->size`. [F48, F94, F134]
- `mergeable_len_to_ctx()` packs `truesize` (< `1 << 22`) in the low 22 bits and `headroom` at bit `MRG_CTX_HEADER_SHIFT` (22); `mergeable_ctx_to_truesize()` and `mergeable_ctx_to_headroom()` apply only to mergeable-mode `ctx`. Every mergeable RX buffer dequeued in `receive_mergeable()`, `xdp_linearize_page()`, or `virtnet_build_xdp_buff_mrg()` must pass `check_mergeable_len()` against `truesize - SKB_DATA_ALIGN(headroom + tailroom)` before copying or attaching its page. [F78, F129]
- In `add_recvbuf_mergeable()`, when XDP is enabled (`headroom > 0`), the `page_frag` tail `hole` must not be added to `len` so `truesize` (`len + room`) stays `PAGE_SIZE`. In `xdp_linearize_page()`, both the initial copy and every subsequent copy in `while (--*num_buf)` must check `page_off + len + SKB_DATA_ALIGN(sizeof(struct skb_shared_info)) <= PAGE_SIZE`. `virtnet_build_xdp_buff_mrg()` must check `*num_buf <= MAX_SKB_FRAGS + 1`, and `page_to_skb()` must check residual `len <= MAX_SKB_FRAGS * PAGE_SIZE`. [F79, F81, F188]
- `receive_buf()` must verify `len >= vi->hdr_len + ETH_HLEN` before calling `receive_mergeable()`, `receive_big()`, or `receive_small()`. `add_recvbuf_small()` advances `buf` by `VIRTNET_RX_PAD + xdp_headroom` before `virtnet_rq_init_one_sg()` and `virtqueue_add_inbuf_premapped()`, and `receive_small()` must subtract that offset after `virtnet_rq_get_buf()`. [F88, F132]
- In non-mergeable big-packet mode (`!vi->mergeable_rx_bufs`), `add_recvbuf_big()` and `page_to_skb()` place payload on the first page at offset `sizeof(struct padded_vnet_hdr)` rather than `vi->hdr_len`. In `page_to_skb()`, `headroom` must be subtracted from both `buf = p - headroom` and tailroom (`truesize - headroom - hdr_padded_len - len`), `offset` must be reset to `0` on subsequent chained pages, and `get_a_page()` must clear `p->private = 0`. [F95, F108, F111, F183]
- In `receive_buf()` and `virtnet_receive_xsk_buf()`, the virtio header `flags` byte must be saved into a local variable before running XDP and restored into `skb_vnet_common_hdr(skb)->hdr.flags` in `virtnet_receive_done()`. `receive_small_xdp()` and `mergeable_xdp_get_buf()` must drop packets with `hdr->hdr.gso_type != 0` or `VIRTIO_NET_HDR_F_NEEDS_CSUM` before XDP, and `__virtnet_xdp_xmit_one()` must decrement `xdpf->headroom` by `vi->hdr_len` alongside decrementing `xdpf->data` and incrementing `xdpf->len`. [F66, F68, F189]
- `virtnet_xmit_ptr_pack()` packs `enum virtnet_xmit_type` (<= 3) into the low 2 bits (`VIRTNET_XMIT_TYPE_MASK`), requiring 4-byte aligned pointers and XSK lengths shifted by `VIRTIO_XSK_FLAG_OFFSET` (2). Whenever queued TX buffers are freed without `netdev_tx_completed_queue()` (`virtnet_tx_resize()`, `virtnet_sq_bind_xsk_pool()`, `remove_vq_common()`), `netdev_tx_reset_queue()` must be called via `virtnet_sq_free_unused_buf_done()` or after `free_unused_bufs()`, and never in `virtnet_enable_queue_pair()`. [F60, F128]
- Queue pair `q` maps to virtqueue `2*q` (RX), `2*q + 1` (TX), and `vi->max_queue_pairs * 2` (CVQ), which per-VQ coalescing and stats commands must use. `vi->curr_queue_pairs` must be >= 1; TX queues in `[vi->curr_queue_pairs - vi->xdp_queue_pairs, vi->curr_queue_pairs)` are dedicated XDP queues (`is_xdp_raw_buffer_queue()`), and in `virtnet_tx_resize()`, `ring_num` must be `> MAX_SKB_FRAGS + 2`. [F84, F85, F87, F131]
- Device-wide coalescing/DIM updates in `virtnet_send_{tx,rx}_notf_coal_cmds()` must update `sq[i].intr_coal`, `rq[i].intr_coal`, and `rq[i].dim_enabled` (under `rq[i].dim_lock`) across all `vi->max_queue_pairs`, and `virtnet_set_ringparam()` must resend `vi->intr_coal_{tx,rx}` after `virtnet_{tx,rx}_resize()` (ignoring `-EOPNOTSUPP`). `virtnet_stats()` and `virtnet_get_base_stats()` must sum across all `vi->max_queue_pairs`, `VIRTIO_NET_STATS_TYPE_*` bitmasks must be `u64`, and `virtnet_fill_stats_qstat()` must divide `desc[i].qstat_offset` by `sizeof(*ctx->data)`. [F63, F64, F65, F124, F138]
- In `virtnet_probe()`, `vi->rss_key_size` must not exceed `VIRTIO_NET_RSS_MAX_KEY_SIZE`, `rss_hash_key_data` must immediately follow `rss_trailer` in `struct virtnet_info`, and `vi->rss_hdr` is sized to `virtnet_rss_hdr_size(vi)`. Default RSS must be committed via `virtnet_commit_rss_command()` after `virtio_device_ready()` (clearing `NETIF_F_RXHASH`, `has_rss`, and `has_rss_hash_report` on failure), and disabling `NETIF_F_RXHASH` in `virtnet_set_features()` must not overwrite `vi->rss_hash_types_saved`. [F62, F71, F82, F136]
- Multi-byte fields in `virtio_net_rss_config_hdr`, `virtio_net_hdr_v1_hash`, `virtio_net_ctrl_coal_*`, and `virtio_net_stats_*` are little-endian (`cpu_to_le*()`/`le*_to_cpu()`), and `virtnet_xdp_rx_hash()` must convert `hdr_hash->hash_report` with `__le16_to_cpu()` and clamp to `VIRTIO_NET_HASH_REPORT_NONE` if `>= VIRTIO_NET_HASH_REPORT_MAX_TABLE`. In `virtio_crypto`, `virtio_crypto_dataq_akcipher_callback()` must subtract `sizeof(vc_req->status)` from `len`, and `virtio_crypto_{skcipher,akcipher}_algs_{register,unregister}()` must update algorithm tables under `algs_lock` based on `active_devs`. [F76, F83, F92, F96]

## Synchronization
- Before `virtnet_napi_disable()` in `virtnet_rx_pause()`, `virtnet_rx_pause_all()`, `virtnet_close()`, or `virtnet_open()` rollback, delayed refill must be stopped with `disable_delayed_refill(vi)` and `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&vi->refill)`. In `__virtnet_rx_resume()`, `virtnet_napi_enable(rq)` must precede `schedule_delayed_work(&vi->refill, 0)`. Outside `virtnet_open()`, `__virtnet_rx_resume()`, or `refill_work()`, scheduling `vi->refill` requires holding `vi->refill_lock` (`spin_lock_bh()` in process context) and checking `vi->refill_enabled`. [F24, F25, F26, F173]
- In `virtnet_probe()`, `register_netdevice()`, `virtio_config_driver_disable()`, `virtio_device_ready()`, and initial CVQ commands must run under `rtnl_lock()` (using `virtio_config_changed(vi->vdev)` rather than synchronous `virtnet_config_changed_work()`). Every error path after `rtnl_lock()` in `virtnet_probe()` and `virtnet_rx_mode_work()` must unlock before returning or calling `unregister_netdev()`, and `flush_work(&vi->rx_mode_work)` / `flush_work(&vi->config_work)` in `virtnet_remove()` and `virtnet_freeze_down()` must run without `rtnl_lock()` after `virtnet_cpu_notif_remove(vi)` and `disable_rx_mode_work(vi)`. [F13, F23, F35, F145, F177]
- `virtnet_send_command_reply()` must hold `vi->cvq_lock` across populating `vi->ctrl`, adding SGs, kicking/polling `vi->cvq` (checking `!virtqueue_is_broken(vi->cvq)` and calling `cond_resched()`), and reading `vi->ctrl->status` before unlocking. `rq->dim_lock` nests outside `vi->cvq_lock`, must not be nested across multiple queues, and in `virtnet_rx_dim_work()` must guard rechecking `rq->dim_enabled` and setting `dim->state = DIM_START_MEASURE` on all exit paths. [F14, F27, F28, F29, F117]
- `sq->reset` must be updated under `__netif_tx_lock_bh(txq)` in `virtnet_tx_{pause,resume}()` and checked under `__netif_tx_trylock(txq)` in `virtnet_poll_cleantx()`. `virtnet_poll_tx()` must hold `__netif_tx_lock(txq, raw_smp_processor_id())` across `virtqueue_disable_cb()`, TX/XSK work, `virtqueue_enable_cb_prepare()`, and `napi_complete_done()`, returning `budget` without completing NAPI when `xsk_done >= budget`. [F30, F37, F119]
- In `virtnet_xdp_set()`, all RX queues must be paused via `virtnet_rx_pause_all(vi)` and (if running) TX NAPIs disabled via `virtnet_napi_tx_disable()` before updating `rq[i].xdp_prog` or `vi->xdp_queue_pairs` (plus `synchronize_net()` when clearing `prog`). XDP transmit paths must use `virtnet_xdp_get_sq()`/`virtnet_xdp_put_sq()`, and process-context `virtqueue_napi_schedule()` callers must wrap it in `local_bh_disable()`/`local_bh_enable()`. [F32, F170, F174]
- In `virtio_crypto`, `virtcrypto_dataq_callback()` must defer buffer draining and `vc_req->alg_cb` to `virtcrypto_done_task()`, and `virtcrypto_remove()` must call `tasklet_kill(&vcrypto->data_vq[i].done_task)` after `virtcrypto_dev_stop()` before resetting the device or freeing queues. `virtio_crypto_ctrl_vq_request()` must call `init_completion()` before `spin_lock_irqsave(&vcrypto->ctrl_lock, flags)`, and `virtio_crypto_table` accesses require `table_lock`. [F38, F39, F40, F165]

## Object Lifetimes
- Each buffer from `virtnet_rq_alloc()` holds a `virtnet_rq_dma::ref` and a page reference on `alloc_frag->page`, while `rq->last_dma` holds the initial reference. If `virtqueue_add_inbuf_premapped()` fails in `add_recvbuf_small()` or `add_recvbuf_mergeable()`, the error path must call both `virtnet_rq_unmap(rq, buf, 0)` and `put_page(virt_to_head_page(buf))`; before dropping `rq->alloc_frag.page` in `free_receive_page_frags()` or rolling over in `virtnet_rq_alloc()`, `virtnet_rq_unmap(rq, rq->last_dma, 0)` must be called and `rq->last_dma` set to `NULL`. [F1, F3, F59]
- Detached unused RX buffers in `virtnet_rx_resize()`, `virtnet_rq_bind_xsk_pool()`, and `free_unused_bufs()` must be passed to `virtnet_rq_unmap_free_buf()`, never directly to `virtnet_rq_free_buf()`. `virtnet_xdp_set()` must add `vi->max_queue_pairs - 1` references via `bpf_prog_add()` (and `bpf_prog_sub()` on error) and drop `old_prog` across all `vi->max_queue_pairs` via `bpf_prog_put()`. [F2, F11]
- In RX XDP paths, when `virtnet_xdp_handler()` returns `XDP_TX` or `XDP_REDIRECT`, the caller must return `NULL` without freeing the primary page, XDP frags, or `xdp_buff`. Otherwise, if `build_skb_from_xdp_buff()` returns `NULL` or XDP drops the packet in `receive_mergeable_xdp()`, `put_xdp_frags(&xdp)` must be called before `put_page(page)` and `mergeable_buf_free()`. [F5, F105]
- Teardown in `virtnet_remove()` and `virtnet_probe()` must call `unregister_netdev()` and `net_failover_destroy(vi->failover)` (skipping `free_failover` if `vi->failover` is an `ERR_PTR`) before `remove_vq_common()`, which must run `virtio_reset_device()`, `free_unused_bufs()`, `netdev_tx_reset_queue()`, `free_receive_bufs()`, and `free_receive_page_frags()` before `virtnet_del_vqs()`. `virtnet_free_queues()` must call `synchronize_net()` after `__netif_napi_del()` before freeing `vi->rq`/`vi->sq`. [F16, F49, F147, F148, F149]
- In `virtio_crypto`, `virtcrypto_init_vqs()`, `virtcrypto_start_crypto_engines()`, and `virtio_device_ready()` must precede `virtcrypto_dev_start()`, and `virtcrypto_clear_crypto_engines()` (`crypto_engine_exit()`) and `virtio_reset_device()` must precede `virtcrypto_del_vqs()`. `virtcrypto_get_dev_node()` increments `ref_count` via `virtcrypto_dev_get()`; `virtio_crypto_skcipher_setkey()` must not call `virtcrypto_get_dev_node()` again when rekeying a non-NULL `ctx->vcrypto`, and must close sessions, call `virtcrypto_dev_put(ctx->vcrypto)`, and clear `ctx->vcrypto = NULL` on session init failure or exit. [F17, F18, F21, F150, F152]

## API Contracts
- `buf_to_xdp()` expects `len` excluding `vi->hdr_len` on the first buffer (`first_buf = true`, max `xsk_pool_get_rx_frame_size(rq->xsk_pool)`) and raw `len` on continuation buffers, freeing its input via `xsk_buff_free()` on error; `xsk_construct_skb()` unconditionally frees `xdp` via `xsk_buff_free()`, so callers must never free `xdp` after either call. In big-packet mode, `page_to_skb()` recycles unused trailing pages via `give_pages()` on success, but leaves the input chain untouched on `NULL` return for `receive_big()` to free via `give_pages(rq, page)`. [F4, F6, F58, F77]
- `virtnet_send_command()`, `virtnet_send_command_reply()`, and `virtnet_commit_rss_command()` return `bool` (`true` on success) and must pre-set `vi->ctrl->status = ~0`. `virtio_crypto_ctrl_vq_request()` returns `0` without checking device status, so callers must pre-set `status = VIRTIO_CRYPTO_ERR` and verify `status == VIRTIO_CRYPTO_OK` afterward. [F43, F46]
- `try_fill_recv()` returns `err != -ENOMEM` (`false` only on `-ENOMEM`), and `virtnet_xdp_xmit()` returns negative errno on pre-check failure or the count `0 <= nxmit <= n` of queued frames without freeing unqueued frames (requiring `xdp_return_frame_rx_napi(xdpf)` in `virtnet_xdp_handler()` when it returns `0`). [F44, F45]
- In `virtnet_rq_unmap()`, `virtqueue_map_sync_single_range_for_cpu()` must run whenever `dma->need_sync && len` before checking `dma->ref`. Shared TX queue XDP/XSK transmits must call `check_sq_full_and_disable()`, BQL applies only to `VIRTNET_XMIT_TYPE_SKB` when `sq->napi.weight != 0`, and `free_old_xmit()` must receive `!!budget` as `in_napi`. `virtcrypto_algo_is_supported()` and `virtcrypto_get_dev_node()` take unshifted bit indices (subtracting 32 and testing `*_algo_h` when `algo > 31`), and all virtqueue SG buffers must be heap-allocated. [F57, F89, F143, F164, F169, F184, F191]

## Error Handling
- On mid-batch failure at index `i`, `virtnet_add_recvbuf_xsk()` must free `xsk_buffs[i .. num-1]` via `xsk_buff_free()`, and `virtnet_xsk_xmit_batch()` must call `xsk_tx_completed(sq->xsk_pool, nb_pkts - i)`. `virtnet_xsk_pool_enable()` must check `virtqueue_dma_dev(rq->vq) == virtqueue_dma_dev(sq->vq)` and non-NULL, unwind in reverse order on failure, and in `virtnet_{rq,sq}_bind_xsk_pool()`, clear `pool = NULL` if `virtqueue_reset()` fails. [F8, F9, F51, F181]
- `vi->rss_hdr` must use `devm_kzalloc()`/`devm_kfree()`. In `virtnet_set_queues()`, reallocating `vi->rss_hdr` must call `virtnet_rss_update_by_qpairs()` after `*vi->rss_hdr = *old_rss_hdr`, restore `old_rss_hdr` on allocation failure, free the new `vi->rss_hdr` and restore `old_rss_hdr`/`old_rss_trailer` on commit failure, and free `old_rss_hdr` on success. `virtio_crypto` session init/close helpers must free `vc_ctrl_req` and key buffers on all paths. [F12, F19, F137, F151]

## Known Pitfalls
- `virtcrypto_clear_request()` frees `vc_req->req_data` and `vc_req->sgs`, so `virtio_crypto_rsa_do_req()` must set `vc_req->sgs = NULL` when using a stack `sgs` array and clear `vc_req->req_data = NULL` on error, while `virtio_crypto_akcipher_finalize_req()` must free and NULL `src_buf`/`dst_buf`. `virtio_crypto_skcipher_{encrypt,decrypt}()` must verify `req->cryptlen` is a non-zero multiple of `AES_BLOCK_SIZE`, and in `__virtio_crypto_skcipher_do_req()`, `dst_len` must be accumulated in `u64` (checked against `U32_MAX` and `vcrypto->max_size`) and `req->iv` copied to the heap `iv` buffer before decrypt copies the final ciphertext block of `req->src` into `req->iv`. [F20, F74, F90, F127]
- When `receive_small_xdp()` or `mergeable_xdp_get_buf()` allocates a new `xdp_page`, it must `put_page()` the old page and refresh `page`, `buf`, and `buflen`/`frame_sz`. Across mergeable RX paths, `num_buf` must be a signed `int` pre-decremented (`--num_buf`), checked for `!*num_buf` before `virtnet_build_xdp_buff_mrg()` loops, and passed (not `hdr->num_buffers`) to `mergeable_buf_free()` or `xsk_drop_follow_bufs()`. In `xmit_skb()` when `can_push` is true, `virtio_net_hdr_tnl_from_skb()` must precede `__skb_push(skb, hdr_len)`, and `__skb_pull(skb, hdr_len)` must immediately follow `skb_to_sgvec()`. [F67, F69, F80, F110]
