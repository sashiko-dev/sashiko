# W1 DALLAS'S 1-WIRE BUS

This unit implements the Linux 1-Wire (Dallas/Maxim w1) bus core subsystem, netlink connector interface, bus master controller drivers (GPIO, UART/serdev, I2C DS2482, USB DS2490, OMAP HDQ, i.MX), and slave family drivers (thermal sensors, EEPROMs/EPROMs, battery gauges, switches, and I2C bridges).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `struct w1_reg_num` bitfields place `family` in bits 0..7, `id` in bits 8..55, and `crc` in bits 56..63 when cast to `u64` in CPU byte order; code serializing a ROM ID onto the 1-Wire bus or into `w1_calc_crc8()` must convert it with `cpu_to_le64()` or `le64_to_cpu()`. [F71]
- Both `w1_master` and `w1_slave` devices register on `w1_bus_type`; any `w1_bus_type` callback receiving `struct device *dev` (such as `w1_uevent()`) must check `dev->driver == &w1_master_driver` or `dev->driver == &w1_slave_driver` before calling `dev_to_w1_master()` or `dev_to_w1_slave()`. [F42]
- `struct w1_cb_block` must keep `struct cn_msg request_cn` as its final member because `w1_cn_callback()` copies `cn->len` bytes into `block->request_cn.data`, places the `struct w1_cb_node` array at `block->request_cn.data + cn->len`, and sizes `block->first_cn` at `sizeof(struct cn_msg) + reply_size` while setting `block->maxlen = reply_size`. [F113, F114]
- In `w1_send_slave()`, `block->cmd` must be saved in `cache_cmd` before `w1_reply_make_space()`; if `w1_reply_make_space()` flushes and resets `block->cmd` to `NULL`, `cache_cmd->len` must be zeroed and re-queued via `w1_netlink_queue_cmd()` (which must guard `memcpy(block->cmd, cmd, space)` with `if (block->cmd != cmd)`), and appending payload bytes must increment `block->cn->len`, `block->msg->len`, and `block->cmd->len` together. [F54, F55, F56]
- When walking variable-length `struct w1_netlink_msg` items in `w1_cn_callback()` or `struct w1_netlink_cmd` items in `w1_list_count_cmds()` and `w1_process_cb()`, each iteration must verify `entry->len + sizeof(*entry) <= remaining_len` before advancing the pointer or subtracting from the remaining length. [F73]
- In `ds2482.c`, every I2C command helper (`ds2482_select_register()`, `ds2482_send_cmd()`, `ds2482_send_cmd_data()`, `ds2482_set_channel()`) must update `pdev->read_prt` to match the hardware read pointer register selected by that command (`DS2482_PTR_CODE_*`). [F57]
- In `w1_ds2433.c` and `w1_ds28e04.c`, when page CRC caching is enabled, `eeprom_write()` must reject `off` or `count` not aligned to `W1_PAGE_SIZE` and verify `crc16() == CRC16_VALID`; `w1_f23_write()` and `w1_f1C_write()` must clear `addr >> W1_PAGE_BITS` in `validcrc`, and a `w1_reset_select_slave()` failure in `w1_f23_refresh_block()` or `w1_f1C_refresh_block()` must zero the entire `validcrc` mask (`W1_VALIDCRC_MAX >= cfg->page_count`). [F64, F83, F119]

## Synchronization

- Mutexes in the w1 core nest as `w1_mlock` -> `w1_master.mutex` -> `w1_master.list_mutex` and `w1_master.mutex` -> `w1_master.bus_mutex`; `w1_process_command_master()` is entered and must return with `dev->bus_mutex` held, temporarily unlocking `dev->bus_mutex` before calling `w1_get_slaves()` or acquiring `dev->mutex` for `w1_process_command_addremove()` and re-locking `dev->bus_mutex` afterward. [F10, F145]
- Callers iterating `w1_master.slist` under `w1_master.list_mutex` must drop `list_mutex` before calling `w1_slave_detach()`, `w1_attach_slave_device()`, or `w1_unref_slave()`, and copy `sl->reg_num` into a local variable before `w1_slave_detach(sl)` (as in `w1_reconnect_slaves()`) so `sl` is not read after being freed. [F19, F70]
- `w1_attach_slave_device()` is entered with `dev->mutex` held, must unlock `dev->mutex` around `request_module()` so module loading can call `w1_register_family()` and `w1_reconnect_slaves()` without deadlocking on `dev->mutex`, and must re-lock `dev->mutex` before returning. [F11]
- `w1_family_registered()` and the subsequent `__w1_family_get()` must run within the same `w1_flock` spinlock critical section, whereas `w1_reconnect_slaves()` must be called outside `w1_flock`. [F26]
- 1-Wire bus transactions (`w1_reset_bus()`, `w1_reset_select_slave()`, `w1_reset_resume_command()`, `w1_next_pullup()`, and bit/byte/block I/O) must hold `w1_master.bus_mutex` and unlock it on all exits; `w1_search()` and `w1_bus_master.search` (`ds9490r_search()`) must check `test_bit(W1_ABORT_SEARCH, &dev->flags)` and unlock `bus_mutex` before invoking `w1_slave_found_callback`. [F27, F101, F148]
- `w1_read_bit()` disables local IRQs via `local_irq_save()` before calling `w1_bus_master.write_bit` and `w1_bus_master.read_bit` (and `w1_write_bit()` does so when `w1_disable_irqs` is set), so `read_bit` and `write_bit` callbacks must be atomic and never sleep or call `_cansleep` GPIO helpers. [F142]
- `w1_process_callbacks()` requires `dev->list_mutex` on entry and drops it around each `async_cmd->cb(dev, async_cmd)` call; `w1_process()` must hold `dev->list_mutex` across `w1_process_callbacks()` until after `__set_current_state(TASK_INTERRUPTIBLE)`, and any loop exit after setting `TASK_INTERRUPTIBLE` must restore `__set_current_state(TASK_RUNNING)`. [F6, F149]
- In `w1_cn_callback()`, `w1_cb_block` and each `w1_cb_node` (including `block->refcnt`, `dev->refcnt`/`sl->refcnt`, and `node->msg` pointing into `block->request_cn`) must be initialized before `list_add_tail(&node->async.async_entry, &dev->async_list)`, and subsequent errors in `w1_cn_callback()` must use `w1_netlink_send_error()` without touching `block`. [F22, F115]
- In `w1_therm.c`, `convert_t()` and `conv_time_measure()` must keep `dev_master->bus_mutex` locked across `w1_poll_completion()` (and across `msleep_interruptible(t_conv)` when `!strong_pullup` in `convert_t()`) and unlock `dev_master->bus_mutex` before calling `read_scratchpad()`. [F28]
- In `w1-uart.c`, `w1_uart_serdev_tx_rx()` must call `reinit_completion(&w1dev->rx_byte_received)` before `serdev_device_write_buf()`, protect `w1dev->rx_byte` and `w1dev->rx_err` with `w1dev->rx_mutex`, and `w1_uart_serdev_receive_buf()` must unlock `w1dev->rx_mutex` before `complete(&w1dev->rx_byte_received)` because `w1_uart_serdev_tx_rx()` uses `mutex_trylock(&w1dev->rx_mutex)`. [F31, F150]

## Object Lifetimes

- `w1_search_slave()` increments both `sl->refcnt` and `sl->master->refcnt` so callers must release the slave with `w1_unref_slave()`, whereas `w1_slave_search_device()` returns a borrowed `struct w1_slave *` without incrementing refcounts, and `w1_search_master_id()` increments `dev->refcnt`. [F1, F2]
- Detaching a slave from `w1_master.slist` must go through `w1_slave_detach()`, which tests and sets `W1_SLAVE_DETACH` in `sl->flags` under `sl->master->list_mutex` before calling `w1_unref_slave()`, ensuring the attachment reference on `sl` is dropped at most once. [F4]
- `w1_alloc_dev()` allocates `w1_master` and `w1_bus_master` in one `kzalloc` block (`w1_master_release()` only calls `kfree(md)`) and initializes `dev->refcnt` to 1; `w1_process()` increments `dev->refcnt` on entry and decrements on exit, while `__w1_remove_master_device()` unlinks `dev` from `w1_masters` under `w1_mlock`, sets `W1_ABORT_SEARCH` and stops `dev->thread`, detaches slaves, and drains `w1_process_callbacks(dev)` until `dev->refcnt` reaches 0. [F5, F23, F112]
- `w1_unregister_family()` must unlink `fent->family_entry` from `w1_families` under `w1_flock` before calling `w1_reconnect_slaves(fent, 0)` and wait until `atomic_read(&fent->refcnt)` reaches 0 before returning. [F12]
- In `__w1_attach_slave_device()` and `w1_family_notify()`, uevents on `sl->dev` stay suppressed until `fops->add_slave()`, `fops->groups`, and `sl->hwmon` registration complete; on `BUS_NOTIFY_DEL_DEVICE` (which runs before `device_unregister(&sl->dev)`), `hwmon_device_unregister(sl->hwmon)` runs before `fops->remove_slave(sl)`, and `remove_slave` must unregister child devices (`i2c_del_adapter()` in `w1_f19_remove_slave()`, `platform_device_unregister()` in `w1_ds2780`/`w1_ds2781`). [F25, F126, F127]
- Because `fops->remove_slave(sl)` runs before `sysfs_remove_groups()` on `BUS_NOTIFY_DEL_DEVICE`, in `w1_therm.c` operations that drop `bus_mutex` or sleep (`convert_t()`, `conv_time_measure()`) must hold a `THERM_REFCNT(sl->family_data)` reference, `w1_therm_remove_slave()` must wait for `THERM_REFCNT(sl->family_data)` to reach 0 before `kfree(sl->family_data)` and set `sl->family_data = NULL`, and sysfs handlers must check `sl->family_data` before using `SLAVE_SPECIFIC_FUNC(sl)`. [F15, F128]
- Master drivers must initialize locks, completions, clocks, and `w1_bus_master` callbacks before `w1_add_master_device()` (and before `w1_uart_serdev_open()` in `w1_uart_probe()`), and call `w1_remove_master_device()` before disabling clocks/PM or freeing state (`omap_hdq.c` callers of `pm_runtime_get_sync()` must call `pm_runtime_put_noidle()` on negative error and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` on active exit). [F24, F122, F140, F141]

## API Contracts

- Every `w1_async_cmd.cb` callback invoked by `w1_process_callbacks()` is entered with `dev->list_mutex` unlocked and must lock `dev->list_mutex` to `list_del(&async_cmd->async_entry)`, free its backing storage, and unlock `dev->list_mutex` before returning. [F8]
- `w1_add_master_device()` copies `struct w1_bus_master` by value and `w1_remove_master_device()` matches `dev->bus_master->data == bm->data`, so `bm->data` must be unique per master instance; if `w1_bus_master` sets `(write_byte && read_byte && reset_bus)` with `touch_bit == NULL`, it must also set `(read_bit && write_bit)` or `triplet`/`search`. [F153, F154]
- `w1_read_block()` and `w1_bus_master.read_block` return an unsigned `u8` byte count (`0` on error in `ds9490r_read_block()`, never negative errno), and `w1_triplet()` / `w1_bus_master.triplet` return a `u8` bitmask (`id_bit` in `BIT(0)`, `comp_bit` in `BIT(1)`, `dir_taken` in `BIT(2)`) where `(ret & 0x03) == 0x03` indicates bus failure or no responding slaves. [F35, F36]
- `w1_next_pullup()` sets a one-shot `dev->pullup_duration` that `w1_pre_write()` passes to `set_pullup(data, pullup_duration)` and `w1_post_write()` resets to 0 via `set_pullup(data, 0)` at the end of the next `w1_write_8()`, so callers must invoke `w1_next_pullup()` immediately before the final command byte, and `w1_gpio_set_pullup()` must save `pullup_duration = delay` when `delay != 0` and execute the GPIO high pulse and `msleep()` when `delay == 0`. [F51, F155]
- In `ds2490.c`, `ds_send_data()` and `ds_recv_data()` pass `buf` directly to `usb_bulk_msg()`, and `read_block_chunk()` must `memset(buf, 0xFF, len)` and write `buf` to `EP_DATA_OUT` before `COMM_BLOCK_IO`, so `ds9490r_write_block()` and `ds9490r_read_block()` must use `kmemdup()`/`kmalloc()` heap buffers. [F49]
- In `ds2482.c`, `DS2482_CMD_WRITE_CONFIG` requires `ds2482_calculate_config()`, `DS2482_CMD_1WIRE_SINGLE_BIT` and `DS2482_CMD_1WIRE_TRIPLET` take their bit in bit 7 (`bit ? 0xFF : 0`), and every `w1_bus_master` callback must lock `pdev->access_lock`, call `ds2482_wait_1wire_idle(pdev)`, and when `pdev->w1_count > 1` call `ds2482_set_channel()` (writing `ds2482_chan_wr[channel]` and checking `ds2482_chan_rd[channel]`). [F50, F58, F88, F130]
- On a `w1_slave`, sysfs `device_attribute` callbacks obtain `struct w1_slave *` via `dev_to_w1_slave(dev)` and `bin_attribute` callbacks via `kobj_to_w1_slave(kobj)`, whereas `hwmon_ops` callbacks registered via `fops->chip_info` receive the child hwmon `struct device *` and must use `dev_get_drvdata(dev)`. [F157]
- In `w1_ds2430.c`, `w1_ds2431.c`, and `w1_ds2805.c`, unaligned `addr` or `len < W1_F*_SCRATCH_SIZE` writes must read-modify-write a full `W1_F*_SCRATCH_SIZE` block; in `w1_ds2433.c` and `w1_ds28e04.c`, callers of `w1_f23_write()` and `w1_f1C_write()` must bound `len <= W1_PAGE_SIZE - (addr & W1_PAGE_MASK)` so `w1_read_block(sl->master, rdbuf, len + 3)` cannot overflow `rdbuf[W1_PAGE_SIZE + 3]`. [F67, F82]

## Error Handling

- In `w1_attach_slave_device()` and `__w1_attach_slave_device()`, after incrementing `sl->master->refcnt` and calling `__w1_family_get()`, any error before registration must decrement `sl->master->refcnt`, call `w1_family_put(sl->family)`, and `kfree(sl)`, and if `device_register(&sl->dev)` fails after `of_find_matching_node()`, `__w1_attach_slave_device()` must call `of_node_put(sl->dev.of_node)` before `put_device(&sl->dev)`. [F3, F135]
- `w1_unref_slave()` returns the post-decrement `sl->refcnt` (`0` when destroyed, `> 0` when references remain), whereas `w1_slave_detach()` returns `0` when destroyed immediately or `-EBUSY` if references remain or `W1_SLAVE_DETACH` was already set. [F38]
- `w1_reset_bus()` masks `bus_master->reset_bus()` with `& 0x1` (`0` for presence, `1` for error), and `w1_reset_select_slave()` / `w1_reset_resume_command()` return `-1` on failure (which slave drivers must translate to standard negative errnos); in `w1_therm.c`, `bus_mutex_lock()` returns `true` when locked and `false` on failure; and in `w1_ds2405.c`, `w1_ds2405_select()` returns `1` on success and `0` on failure while `w1_ds2405_read_pio()` returns `0`, `1`, or `-ENODEV`. [F39, F40, F168]

## Known Pitfalls

- `w1_reset_select_slave()` sends `W1_SKIP_ROM` instead of `W1_MATCH_ROM` when `sl->master->slave_count == 1`, so `w1_ds2405.c` (where `W1_MATCH_ROM` toggles PIO state) and `w1_therm.c` (`reset_select_slave()`, called while `slave_count == 1` during initial discovery of multi-slave buses) must not use `w1_reset_select_slave()`. [F53]
- In `w1_therm.c`, `w1_DS18B20_set_resolution()` and `alarms_store()` must call `read_scratchpad()` before `write_scratchpad()` to preserve unchanged bytes, and `w1_DS18B20_set_resolution()` must also call `read_scratchpad()` after `w1_DS18B20_write_data()` to verify the resolution bits changed; `w1_DS18B20_convert_temp()` and `w1_DS1825_convert_temp()` must decode `rom[0..1]` via `le16_to_cpup((__le16 *)rom)` into `u16` and mask/shift before casting to `s16`, and `w1_DS18S20_convert_temp()` must check `rom[7] != 0` before dividing. [F66, F91, F132]
- Lookup loops over `w1_masters`, `dev->slist`, or `w1_families` (`w1_remove_master_device()`, `w1_search_master_id()`, `w1_search_slave()`, `w1_family_registered()`) must record matches in a separate variable rather than returning the `list_for_each_entry()` cursor; in `w1_cn_callback()` and `w1_process_cb()`, per-item errors (`err == -ENODEV` in `w1_cn_callback()` and command `err` in `w1_process_cb()`) must be reset to 0 before the next iteration, and non-fatal branches in the second `w1_cn_callback()` loop must `goto out_cont` rather than `continue`. [F98, F99]
