# MARVELL PRESTERA ETHERNET SWITCH DRIVER

This unit implements the Linux Ethernet switch driver for Marvell Prestera DX, AC3X, and AC5X ASICs over PCI, managing firmware loading, mailbox IPC, SDMA RX/TX with Marvell DSA tagging, port netdevices, devlink, phylink/ethtool, TC flower/matchall ACL and SPAN offloads, hardware counter polling, and L3 router/FIB/neighbour offloads.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `prestera_fix_tb_id()` maps `RT_TABLE_LOCAL` and `RT_TABLE_MAIN` to one hardware table: `struct prestera_kern_fib_cache_key` must preserve unmapped `kern_tb_id` to resolve overlaps, `prestera_fib_node` and `prestera_rif_entry` lookups/creations must convert via `prestera_fix_tb_id()`, and `prestera_k_arb_fib_evt()` must demote an overlapped `bfib_cache` (`reachable = false`) before applying a new `RT_TABLE_LOCAL` entry and re-promote it on deletion [F68, F169].
- In `__prestera_pr_k_arb_fc_lpm_info_calc()`, direct `/32` unicast FIB routes must be programmed as `PRESTERA_FIB_TYPE_UC_NH` (not `TRAP`), and `__prestera_k_arb_n_lpm_set()` must not delete that `/32` `prestera_fib_node` while its `prestera_kern_fib_cache` is reachable. In `struct prestera_fib_info`, `nh_grp` is only populated when `type == PRESTERA_FIB_TYPE_UC_NH` (`NULL` for `TRAP`/`DROP`), so callers must check `type` before accessing `nh_grp` [F47, F170].
- In `__prestera_k_arb_nc_apply()`, call `prestera_nh_neigh_set()` only when `nc->nh_neigh_info` differs from `nh_neigh->info` (`memcmp` non-zero), and `prestera_nh_neigh_set()` must propagate the updated `nh_neigh->info` to every `prestera_nexthop_group` in `neigh->nexthop_group_list` [F69].
- A port's `prestera_rif_entry` and paired `dev_hold(port_dev)`/`dev_put(port_dev)` are created on `NETDEV_UP` in `__prestera_inetaddr_valid_cb()` only when adding the first IPv4 address (`!idev->ifa_list`) and destroyed on `NETDEV_DOWN` in `__prestera_inetaddr_cb()` only after the last IPv4 address is removed (`!idev->ifa_list`) [F78].
- ACL `chain_index` must be validated by `prestera_acl_chain_is_supported()` and `prestera_acl_chain_to_client()` (`0` on egress; `< ARRAY_SIZE(ingress_client_map)` and within `PRESTERA_ACL_CHAIN_MASK` `0x3` on ingress), `uid` in `prestera_acl_ruleset_create()` must be bounded to `U8_MAX` before packing `ruleset->pcl_id` via `PRESTERA_ACL_PCL_ID_MAKE`, and `prestera_flower_parse_goto_action()` must require `act->chain_index > chain_index` [F91, F115].
- `prestera_flower_prio_check()` and `prestera_mall_prio_check()` enforce opposite priority orderings: on ingress (`block->ingress`), flower priorities must be strictly greater than `mall.prio_max`; on egress (`!block->ingress`), strictly less than `mall.prio_min`. `prestera_mall_destroy()` must reset `prio_min = UINT_MAX`, `prio_max = 0`, and `bound = false` after unbinding all ports [F59, F102].
- In `prestera_acl_rule_del()`, `list_del(&rule->list)` must precede `prestera_acl_ruleset_prio_refresh(sw->acl, ruleset)`. In `prestera_flower_tmplt_destroy()`, only templates matching `template->chain_index == f->common.chain_index` may be freed from `block->template_list`, whereas `prestera_flower_template_cleanup()` frees all templates on the block [F61, F62].
- In `prestera_sdma_rx_poll()`, once `PRESTERA_SDMA_RX_DESC_IS_RCVD(desc)` is true, trim `buf->skb` to `PRESTERA_SDMA_RX_DESC_PKT_LEN(desc)` before `prestera_sdma_rx_skb_get()`, sample `buf->buf_dma`, `buf->skb`, and `skb->len` before `prestera_sdma_rx_skb_alloc()`, always call `prestera_sdma_rx_desc_init()` to re-arm DMA ownership, and ensure every path reaches `rx_next_buf` to advance `ring->next_rx` via `% PRESTERA_SDMA_RX_DESC_PER_Q` (1000, not bitwise AND) [F74, F89, F99].
- Firmware IPC structs (`struct prestera_msg_*`) in `prestera_hw.c` must avoid C bitfields, include explicit trailing `u8` padding arrays so sizes match `prestera_hw_build_tests()` on 2-byte 32-bit alignment architectures, and mark 12-byte union members containing `__le64` (`prestera_msg_event_port_param.phy`, `prestera_msg_policer_req.sr_tcm`) as `__packed` [F79, F80].

## Synchronization

- `__prestera_cmd_ret()` and all `prestera_hw_*` wrappers lock `fw->cmd_queue[qid].cmd_mtx` and sleep in `prestera_fw_wait_reg32()`, so they must only be called from sleepable process context—never from hardirq, NAPI softirq, `.ndo_start_xmit`, `.ndo_get_stats64`, or under a spinlock, rwlock, or RCU read lock [F145].
- Atomic/RCU router notifiers (`__prestera_router_fib_event()`, `prestera_router_netevent_event()`) must pin `fen_info->fi` (`fib_info_hold()`) or `n` (`neigh_clone()`), allocate work with `GFP_ATOMIC`, and defer to `prestera_owq` workers that hold `rtnl_lock()` across `prestera_k_arb_*` and `prestera_hw_nhgrp_blk_get()` (which uses a function-local `static struct prestera_msg_nh_chunk_resp resp` after releasing `cmd_mtx`) [F37, F71, F141].
- When walking `sw->router->kern_neigh_cache_ht` in `prestera_k_arb_hw_evt()` and `__prestera_k_arb_fib_evt2nc()`, continue on `IS_ERR(n_cache)` and bracket per-entry work with `rhashtable_walk_stop(&iter)` and `rhashtable_walk_start(&iter)` before sleeping. In `__prestera_k_arb_nc_kern_n_fetch()`, hold `read_lock_bh(&n->lock)` while reading `n->nud_state`, `n->dead`, and `n->ha`, and unlock before `neigh_release(n)` or sleeping [F98, F143].
- In `prestera_sdma_xmit()`, hold `spin_lock(&sdma->tx_lock)`, call `dma_sync_single_for_device()`, busy-wait via `udelay(1)` in `prestera_sdma_tx_wait()`, and call `prestera_sdma_tx_desc_xmit(buf->desc)` (which executes `wmb()` before setting `PRESTERA_SDMA_TX_DESC_DMA_OWN` `1U << 31` in `desc->word1`; `prestera_sdma_rx_desc_init()` requires the same `wmb()`) before setting `buf->is_used = true`. `prestera_sdma_tx_recycle_work_fn()` must unmap and free `buf->skb` followed by `wmb()` before clearing `buf->is_used = false` [F32, F110, F136, F151].
- In `prestera_counter.c`, `prestera_counter_block_get()` must initialize `block` fields before `prestera_counter_block_list_add()` (which reallocates `counter->block_list` via `krealloc()` under `counter->mtx`), so lookups (`prestera_counter_block_get_by_idx()`) must pin `block` with `prestera_counter_block_incref()` under `counter->mtx` and `prestera_counter_stats_work()` must track position by index (`counter->curr_idx`) rather than caching a pointer across lock drops [F34, F66].
- Under `prestera_counter_block_lock(block)`, if `prestera_counter_get()` allocates a counter while `block->is_updating` is true, set `block->counter_flag[id - block->offset] = COUNTER_FLAG_INVALID` until `prestera_counter_stats_work()` zeroes `block->stats[i]` and sets `COUNTER_FLAG_READY`; because `prestera_hw_counters_get()` accumulates deltas with `+=`, both `prestera_counter_stats_get()` (after `prestera_counter_is_ready()`) and `prestera_counter_put()` must call `prestera_counter_stats_clear()` under `prestera_counter_block_lock(block)` [F64, F65].
- In `prestera_port_create()`, initialize `port->id`, `port->dev_id`, `port->hw_id`, `port->dev`, `port->dl_port`, `port->state_mac_lock`, and `port->cached_hw_stats.caching_dw` before `prestera_port_list_add(port)`. Protect `sw->port_list` with `sw->port_list_lock` (`write_lock` on add/del, `read_lock` on lookup), and serialize `port->state_mac` with `port->state_mac_lock` [F27, F28, F29].

## Object Lifetimes

- In `prestera_pci_probe()`, `prestera_fw_init()`, `alloc_workqueue()`, `INIT_WORK(&fw->evt_work, ...)`, and `request_irq()` must precede `prestera_device_register()`, and in `prestera_switch_init()`, `prestera_hw_switch_init(sw)` must precede router, LAG, and port init. On probe failure or `prestera_pci_remove()`, run `prestera_device_unregister()`, `free_irq()`, `pci_free_irq_vectors()`, and `destroy_workqueue(fw->wq)` in that order before `prestera_fw_uninit(fw)`, without manually freeing devres-managed `fw` or BARs [F24, F127, F129, F130].
- In `prestera_port_create()`, `prestera_devlink_port_register()` and `SET_NETDEV_DEVLINK_PORT()` must precede `register_netdev(dev)`; on post-`register_netdev()` failure or in `prestera_port_destroy()`, cancel `caching_dw` (on destroy), then call `unregister_netdev(dev)`, `prestera_port_list_del(port)`, and `prestera_devlink_port_unregister(port)` before `free_netdev(dev)` [F1, F30, F162].
- In `prestera_port_sfp_bind()`, `of_node_get(sw->np)` must precede `of_find_node_by_name(sw->np, "ports")`, and every exit from `for_each_child_of_node(ports, node)` must jump to `out:` to call both `of_node_put(node)` and `of_node_put(ports)` [F2].
- In `prestera_router_fini()`, unregister all four notifiers before `prestera_neigh_work_fini()` and `prestera_queue_drain()` drain workqueues (without `rtnl_lock()`), and finish draining before `prestera_k_arb_abort()`. `prestera_k_arb_abort()` must destroy `kern_fib_cache_ht` before `kern_neigh_cache_ht` without touching hardware LPM entries (which `prestera_router_hw_fini()` tears down afterwards) [F20, F39].
- Because `prestera_nh_neigh_put()` destroys `struct prestera_nh_neigh` iff `list_empty(&neigh->nexthop_group_list)`, `__prestera_nexthop_group_create()` rollback and `__prestera_nexthop_group_destroy()` must `list_del(&nh_grp->nh_neigh_head[nh_cnt].head)` before calling `prestera_nh_neigh_put()`. `__prestera_nexthop_group_create()` must also clear `sw->router->nhgrp_hw_state_cache[gid / 8] &= ~BIT(gid % 8)` [F18, F70].
- Because `prestera_acl_rule_create()` increments `ruleset->refcount` on top of `prestera_acl_ruleset_get()`, `prestera_flower_replace()` must call `prestera_acl_ruleset_put(ruleset)` on both success and error paths. In `prestera_acl_vtcam_id_put()`, if `refcount_dec_and_test(&vtcam->refcount)` is true and `prestera_hw_vtcam_destroy()` fails with an error other than `-ENODEV`, restore `refcount_set(&vtcam->refcount, 1)` without unlinking or freeing `vtcam` [F13, F14].
- In `prestera_counter_block_put()`, when `prestera_counter_block_decref(block)` returns true, clear `counter->block_list[i] = NULL` under `prestera_counter_lock(counter)` before destroying `block`, and `prestera_counter_get()` must call `prestera_counter_block_put()` if `prestera_counter_get_vacant()` fails. In `prestera_span_rule_del()`, check `binding->span_id == PRESTERA_SPAN_INVALID_ID` (`-ENOENT`) before unbinding and reset `binding->span_id = PRESTERA_SPAN_INVALID_ID` afterwards [F11, F16, F44].

## API Contracts

- Verify `prestera_netdev_check(dev)` before casting `netdev_priv(dev)` to `struct prestera_port *`. In `prestera_netdev_port_event()`, only cast `ptr` to `netdev_notifier_changeupper_info` on `NETDEV_PRECHANGEUPPER`/`NETDEV_CHANGEUPPER` and `netdev_notifier_changelowerstate_info` on `NETDEV_CHANGELOWERSTATE`. In `__prestera_router_fib_event()` and helpers, only downcast `fib_notifier_info` to `fib_entry_notifier_info` after checking `info->family == AF_INET` and `FIB_EVENT_ENTRY_REPLACE`/`DEL` [F116, F117, F119].
- Zero-initialize stack rhashtable keys (`prestera_acl_ruleset_ht_key`, `prestera_kern_fib_cache_key`, `prestera_kern_neigh_cache_key`, `prestera_nh_neigh_key`, `prestera_nexthop_group_key`, `prestera_fib_key`), `prestera_rif_entry_key` (`prestera_rif_entry_create()` must also normalize `e->key` via `__prestera_rif_entry_key_copy()`), and `struct prestera_acl_rule` before `prestera_flower_parse()` [F50, F51, F52].
- In `struct prestera_counter_block`, index `block->stats` and `block->counter_flag` with `counter_id - block->offset`, but pass raw `counter_id` to `idr_remove()` and `prestera_hw_counter_clear()`. `prestera_hw_port_info_get()` and `prestera_find_port()` take `port->id`, whereas all other port firmware commands, `prestera_dsa_build()`, and `prestera_port_find_by_hwid()` require `(port->dev_id, port->hw_id)`. Inspect IPv4 route nexthops with `fib_info_nhc()`, never `fib_info_nh()` [F53, F54, F155].

## Error Handling

- In `prestera_acl.c`, `prestera_acl_rule_lookup()`, `prestera_acl_rule_entry_find()`, and `prestera_acl_rule_entry_create()` return `NULL`, whereas `prestera_acl_ruleset_lookup()`, `prestera_acl_ruleset_get()`, and `prestera_acl_rule_create()` return `ERR_PTR`. In `prestera_router_hw.c`, `prestera_vr_get()` and `prestera_nexthop_group_get()` return `ERR_PTR`, whereas `prestera_rif_entry_create()`, `prestera_nh_neigh_get()`, `__prestera_nexthop_group_create()`, `prestera_fib_node_create()`, and all `*_find()` helpers return `NULL` [F42, F43].
- In `prestera_flower_parse_goto_action()`, assign `rule->jump_ruleset` only after verifying `!IS_ERR(ruleset)`. In `__prestera_acl_rule_entry_act_construct()`, set `e->police.valid` only after both `prestera_hw_policer_create()` and `prestera_hw_policer_sr_tcm_set()` succeed [F48, F49].
- In `prestera_acl_ruleset_block_bind()` and `prestera_mall_replace()`, unwind partial port binds with `list_for_each_entry_continue_reverse()` (and reset `block->ruleset_zero = NULL`), but if `prestera_span_rule_add()` returns `-EEXIST` in `prestera_mall_replace()`, return `-EEXIST` immediately without jumping to `rollback` [F15, F58].
- In `prestera_fw_cmd_send()`, once `PRESTERA_CMD_F_REQ_SENT` is written, every exit path must jump to `cmd_exit` to write `PRESTERA_CMD_F_REPL_RCVD` before unlocking `cmd_mtx`. In `prestera_counter_stats_work()`, if `counter->is_fetching` is true on error or missing block, or if `prestera_hw_counter_trigger()` fails, jump to `abort` to call `prestera_hw_counter_abort()` [F6, F104].

## External Contracts

- Because `prestera_pci_probe()` sets `DMA_BIT_MASK(30)`, allocate SDMA descriptors with `GFP_DMA | GFP_KERNEL`, RX SKBs in NAPI softirq with `GFP_DMA | GFP_ATOMIC`, and translate all DMA addresses written to descriptors or queue registers via `prestera_sdma_map(sdma, pa)` [F135, F149].
- In `prestera_dsa_parse()` and `prestera_dsa_build()`, the 16-byte DSA tag (`PRESTERA_DSA_HLEN`) splits `port_num` across `W0[23:19]`, `W1[11:10]`, and `W2[20]` on RX (`TO_CPU`) but uses `W3[23:7]` (`PRESTERA_DSA_W3_DST_EPORT`) on TX (`FROM_CPU`). Before moving the 12-byte MAC addresses (`2 * ETH_ALEN`, not `ETH_HLEN`) with `memmove()`, `prestera_rxtx_xmit()` must call `skb_cow_head()` and `prestera_rxtx_process_skb()` must check `pskb_may_pull()`, pass `skb->data - ETH_TLEN` to `prestera_dsa_parse()`, and pull with `skb_pull_rcsum()` [F75, F87, F122].

## Known Pitfalls

- In `prestera_hw_vtcam_rule_add()` and `prestera_hw_flood_domain_ports_set()`, assign the `__counted_by_le` count field (`req->n_act` or `req->ports_num`) via `__cpu_to_le32()` before indexing `req->actions_msg[]` or `req->ports[]` [F82].
- Convert multi-byte values to big-endian (`__be16`/`__be32`) of their exact field width before calling `rule_match_set()`. In `prestera_router.c`, `fib_entry_notifier_info::dst` is host-endian `u32` (`%pI4h`, convert via `cpu_to_be32()`), whereas `prestera_ip_addr::u.ipv4` is `__be32` (`%pI4n`) [F90, F124].
- Use `BIT_ULL()` for 64-bit `dissector->used_keys` masks in `prestera_flower_parse()` and for `port_link_modes` `.pr_mask` if any `PRESTERA_LINK_MODE_*` reaches 31. Index `u8 *nhgrp_hw_state_cache` by `gid / 8` and `BIT(gid % 8)` (never `unsigned long *` bitops), pass bit offset `buf_offset * 8` and clamp `memcpy()` to `buf_size - buf_offset` in `prestera_hw_nhgrp_blk_get()`, and use a separate cursor variable `tmp` when breaking from `list_for_each_entry()` in `prestera_port_find_by_hwid()` and `prestera_find_port()` [F93, F96, F108, F109, F125].
