# CONTROL GROUP - MEMORY RESOURCE CONTROLLER (MEMCG)

The Memory Resource Controller (memcg) implements hierarchical memory, swap, zswap, slab/kernel object, and socket memory accounting and limiting for cgroup v1 and v2. It tracks ownership of user folios (`folio->memcg_data`), kernel/slab allocations via reparentable `obj_cgroup` buckets, and swap entries via `swap_cgroup` maps, while maintaining per-CPU charge stocks (`memcg_stock`, `obj_stock`) and hierarchical statistics flushed via cgroup rstat.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `root_mem_cgroup` does not account charges in `memory` or `memsw` (`try_charge()`, `uncharge_folio()`, `drain_obj_stock()`, and `refill_stock()` exclude root), so `mem_cgroup_usage()` must compute root usage from `global_node_page_state()` and swap counters rather than `memcg->memory` or `memcg->memsw`. [F86]
- In `struct mem_cgroup`, `swap` (v2) and `memsw` (v1) share an anonymous `union`, and `tcpmem_pressure` (v1) is separate from `socket_pressure` (v2); check `cgroup_subsys_on_dfl(memory_cgrp_subsys)` or `do_memsw_account()` before accessing them. Under v1, `mem_cgroup_resize_max()` must enforce `memcg->memory.max <= memcg->memsw.max` under `memcg_max_mutex` to prevent unsigned underflow in `mem_cgroup_get_max()`. [F66, F94]
- When charging swap in `__mem_cgroup_try_charge_swap()` and `memcg1_swapout()`, `swap_cgroup_record()`, `MEMCG_SWAP`, and `id.ref` must target the online ancestor from `mem_cgroup_id_get_online()` (plus `mem_cgroup_id_get_many(memcg, nr_pages - 1)` for multi-page folios to match `mem_cgroup_id_put_many(memcg, nr_pages)` in `__mem_cgroup_uncharge_swap()`), and `memcg1_swapout()` must transfer the `memsw` charge to `swap_memcg` when `memcg != swap_memcg`. [F14, F70]
- `swap_cgroup_swapon()` skips allocating `swap_cgroup_ctrl[type].map` when `mem_cgroup_disabled()`, so `lookup_swap_cgroup_id()`, `swap_cgroup_swapoff()`, `mem_cgroup_try_charge_swap()`, and `mem_cgroup_uncharge_swap()` must guard on `mem_cgroup_disabled()`. `struct swap_cgroup` packs two 16-bit IDs into `atomic_t ids`, so `__swap_cgroup_id_xchg()` must cast `unsigned short new_id` to `unsigned int` before shifting by `(offset % ID_PER_SC) * ID_SHIFT` and update only the target 16-bit half via `atomic_try_cmpxchg()`. [F60, F89, F134]
- In `__account_obj_stock()` and `drain_obj_stock()`, cached slab byte deltas (`nr_slab_reclaimable_b`, `nr_slab_unreclaimable_b`) in `struct obj_stock_pcp` must be flushed via `mod_objcg_mlstate()` to `stock->cached_pgdat` and zeroed before changing `cached_pgdat` or `cached_objcg`. Sub-page leftover bytes flushed into `old->nr_charged_bytes` by `drain_obj_stock()` can accumulate across CPUs into whole pages that `obj_cgroup_release()` must uncharge (`nr_charged_bytes >> PAGE_SHIFT`) before freeing `objcg`. [F73, F74]
- Modifying `min` or `low` on a `struct page_counter` (including zeroing in `mem_cgroup_css_offline()` and `mem_cgroup_css_reset()`) must use `page_counter_set_min()` and `page_counter_set_low()` so `propagate_protected_usage()` updates ancestor usage. Updating `memcg->memory.high` or `memcg->memory.max` in `memory_high_write()`, `memory_max_write()`, or `mem_cgroup_css_reset()` requires calling `memcg_wb_domain_size_changed(memcg)`. [F79, F81]
- `mem_cgroup_calculate_protection()` and `page_counter_calculate_protection()` require top-down walks from `root` and skip `root == counter`, so `mem_cgroup_protection()`, `mem_cgroup_below_low()`, and `mem_cgroup_below_min()` (`mem_cgroup_unprotected()`) must treat `memcg == target` as unprotected. In `effective_protection()`, verify all three inequalities (`parent_effective > siblings_protected`, `parent_usage > siblings_protected`, `usage > protected`) before subtracting across lockless reads. [F71, F108, F129]
- `mem_cgroup_zswap_writeback_enabled()` must walk up `parent_mem_cgroup(memcg)` checking `READ_ONCE(memcg->zswap_writeback)` at every ancestor. Because shared reclaim iterators (`mz->iter.position`) do not hold a CSS ref across `mem_cgroup_iter()` calls, `mem_cgroup_css_released()` must call `invalidate_reclaim_iterators()` to `cmpxchg(&iter->position, dead_memcg, NULL)` across `dead_memcg`, its ancestors, and `root_mem_cgroup`. [F72, F77]

## Synchronization

- Any path clearing `folio->memcg_data` on a non-kmem folio (`uncharge_folio()`, `mem_cgroup_migrate()`, `memcg1_swapout()`) must call `folio_unqueue_deferred_split()` before setting `folio->memcg_data = 0`, because unqueueing locks `memcg->deferred_split_queue.split_queue_lock` resolved via `folio_memcg(folio)`. [F31]
- Calling `folio_memcg()`, `folio_lruvec()`, or `folio_lruvec_lock*()` on a non-kmem folio requires the folio's memcg binding to be stabilized by the folio lock, LRU isolation (`!folio_test_lru(folio)`), or an exclusive/frozen (refcount 0) reference. `mem_cgroup_update_lru_size()` updates `mz->lru_zone_size[zid][lru]` non-atomically and requires holding `lruvec->lru_lock`. [F35, F36]
- `memcg_offline_kmem()` must call `memcg_reparent_list_lrus()` before `memcg_reparent_objcgs()`. All manipulations of `objcg->list` and `memcg->objcg_list` in `memcg_reparent_objcgs()` and `obj_cgroup_release()` must hold `objcg_lock`, and `memcg_reparent_objcgs()` must add `memcg->objcg` to `memcg->objcg_list`, set `iter->memcg = parent` via `WRITE_ONCE()`, and splice `memcg->objcg_list` onto `parent->objcg_list` before `percpu_ref_kill(&objcg->refcnt)`. [F30, F75]
- `objcg_lock`, `mctz->lock`, and `memcg->event_list_lock` are acquired in IRQ-off contexts and require `spin_lock_irq()` or `spin_lock_irqsave()`. Under `mctz->lock`, `memcg1_update_tree()` must remove `mz` via `__mem_cgroup_remove_exceeded()` before mutating `mz->usage_in_excess` and re-inserting, and `__mem_cgroup_largest_soft_limit_node()` must remove `mz`, check `soft_limit_excess()`, and pin `css_tryget(&mz->memcg->css)` before unlocked reclaim in `memcg1_soft_limit_reclaim()`, which must recalculate `soft_limit_excess()` after relocking. [F43, F82, F157]
- Per-CPU stocks `memcg_stock` and `obj_stock` use `local_trylock_t`: charge/uncharge paths must use `local_trylock()` with a fallback, while `local_lock()` is restricted to `in_task()` workqueue drainers (`drain_local_memcg_stock`, `drain_local_obj_stock`). In `refill_obj_stock()`, `local_unlock(&obj_stock.lock)` must precede `obj_cgroup_uncharge_pages()`, which calls `refill_stock()` and takes `memcg_stock.lock`. [F42, F154]
- Under `CONFIG_MEMCG_NMI_SAFETY_REQUIRES_ATOMIC`, when `in_nmi()` is true, `account_slab_nmi_safe()` and `account_kmem_nmi_safe()` must update `pn->slab_reclaimable`, `pn->slab_unreclaimable`, or `memcg->kmem_stat` atomically and call `css_rstat_updated()` instead of `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()` or `mod_memcg_state()`. Conversely, v1 `memcg->events_percpu` updates (`memcg1_charge_statistics()`, `memcg1_check_events()`) have no internal lock and require local IRQs disabled. [F153, F155]
- `active_memcg()` and `current_obj_cgroup()` must test `in_task()` (not `in_interrupt()`) to select between task state (`current->active_memcg`, `current->objcg`) and per-CPU `int_active_memcg`, checking `current->active_memcg` before `current->objcg` in `current_obj_cgroup()`. When `!in_task()`, `try_charge_memcg()` and `mem_cgroup_sk_alloc()` must not attribute `memory.high` penalties or `sk->sk_memcg` to `current`, using `schedule_work(&memcg->high_work)` instead. [F126, F149, F150]
- In `try_charge_memcg()`, when `!gfpflags_allow_spinning(gfp_mask)`, cap `batch` to `nr_pages` and pass `allow_spinning = false` to `__memcg_memory_event()` to skip `cgroup_file_notify()`. Synchronous `memory.high` reclaim (`__mem_cgroup_handle_over_high()`) must only run when `!(current->flags & PF_MEMALLOC)` and `gfpflags_allow_blocking(gfp_mask)`, forwarding `gfp_mask` to `reclaim_high()`. [F148, F151]
- `page_counter_try_charge()` must increment `c->usage` with `atomic_long_add_return()` (full barrier) before reading `c->max`, pairing with `xchg(&counter->max, nr_pages)` in `page_counter_set_max()`. `page_counter_reset_watermark()` must write `local_watermark` before `watermark`, and `page_counter_charge()` / `page_counter_try_charge()` must update `c->local_watermark` via `WRITE_ONCE()` before testing `new > READ_ONCE(c->watermark)`. [F45, F49]

## Object Lifetimes

- To replace a folio that stays on the LRU until freed, use `mem_cgroup_replace_folio(old, new)` (which leaves `old->memcg_data` intact for uncharging on free) rather than `mem_cgroup_migrate(old, new)` (which requires `!folio_test_lru(old)` and zeroes `old->memcg_data`). Both require `@old` and `@new` to be locked, have matching `folio_test_anon()`, and have equal `folio_nr_pages()`. [F5, F177]
- When uncharging a folio (`uncharge_folio()`, `memcg1_swapout()`), kmem page (`__memcg_kmem_uncharge_page()`), or slab object (`__memcg_slab_free_hook()`), clear `folio->memcg_data`, `page->memcg_data`, or `obj_exts[off].objcg` to `0`/`NULL` before or when putting the `css`/`obj_cgroup` ref. In `__memcg_slab_post_alloc_hook()`, call `obj_cgroup_get(objcg)` and assign `slab_obj_exts(slab)[off].objcg = objcg` only after `obj_cgroup_charge_account()` succeeds. [F7, F8]
- `obj_cgroup_memcg()` and callers (`folio_memcg()` on kmem folios, `folio_memcg_check()`, `mem_cgroup_from_slab_obj()`) require `rcu_read_lock()` or `cgroup_mutex`, and pinning a CSS ref from `objcg` must loop with `css_tryget()` under RCU as in `get_mem_cgroup_from_objcg()`. `mem_cgroup_from_id()` also requires `rcu_read_lock()`, can return `NULL` (before `mem_cgroup_css_online()` stores into `mem_cgroup_ids`), and requires `css_tryget_online()` or `css_tryget()` before dropping RCU if `id.ref` is not owned. [F13, F97, F163, F164]
- `task->objcg` tags its pointer with `CURRENT_OBJCG_UPDATE_FLAG` on `mem_cgroup_fork()` and `mem_cgroup_kmem_attach()`, so `current_objcg_update()` and `mem_cgroup_exit()` must mask `~CURRENT_OBJCG_UPDATE_FLAG` before `obj_cgroup_put()`. In `current_objcg_update()`'s `try_cmpxchg(&current->objcg, &old, objcg)` loop, reset `old = NULL` after releasing any `xchg` value and `obj_cgroup_put(objcg)` any ref from a failed iteration before re-fetching. [F84, F116]
- `mem_cgroup_css_free()` must wait on `memcg->cgwb_frn[i].done`, clean up `vmpressure`, `cancel_work_sync(&memcg->high_work)`, call `memcg1_remove_from_trees()`, and `free_shrinker_info()` before `mem_cgroup_free(memcg)`, and must `static_branch_dec()` `memcg_sockets_enabled_key` and `memcg_bpf_enabled_key` under the exact conditions they were incremented in `mem_cgroup_css_alloc()` or `memcg_update_tcp_max()` (`memcg1_tcpmem_active(memcg)` on v1). [F16, F17, F85]
- `mem_cgroup_sk_inherit(sk, newsk)` must return early if `sk->sk_memcg == newsk->sk_memcg`, call `mem_cgroup_sk_free(newsk)` to drop any prior CSS ref, and call `css_get(&memcg->css)` if `sk->sk_memcg` is non-NULL. `refill_stock()` and `refill_obj_stock()` hold refs on `cached[i]` and `cached_objcg` that `drain_stock()` and `drain_obj_stock()` must uncharge, clear, and put (both drained in `memcg_hotplug_cpu_dead()`). [F10, F18]

## API Contracts

- `mem_cgroup_iter()` takes a CSS ref on each non-root descendant and drops `@prev` on the next call; breaking early from `mem_cgroup_iter()`, `for_each_mem_cgroup_tree()`, or `for_each_mem_cgroup()` requires calling `mem_cgroup_iter_break(root, iter)` (including both passes in `mem_cgroup_oom_trylock()`, where rollback must stop at `iter == failed`). [F1, F118]
- `get_mem_cgroup_from_mm()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_current()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_folio()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_objcg()`, and `mem_cgroup_get_oom_group()` return a `struct mem_cgroup *` with an owned CSS ref that callers must release via `css_put()` or `mem_cgroup_put()`. In `get_mem_cgroup_from_mm(mm)`, consult `active_memcg()` and `current->mm` only when `mm == NULL`; otherwise resolve `rcu_dereference(mm->owner)` via `mem_cgroup_from_task()` (falling back to `root_mem_cgroup` if `NULL`) in a `css_tryget()` loop under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F3, F62, F125]
- `current_obj_cgroup()` returns a borrowed pointer valid only in the current task or `set_active_memcg()` scope; persisting it requires `obj_cgroup_get()`. `get_obj_cgroup_from_folio()` must resolve `obj_cgroup` from `folio_memcg_kmem(folio)` or `__folio_memcg(folio)` directly (gated only by `memcg_kmem_online()`) without checking `current` task context, since `kswapd` processes user folios. [F4, F170]
- `folio->memcg_data` multiplexes `struct mem_cgroup *`, `MEMCG_DATA_KMEM`-tagged `struct obj_cgroup *`, or `MEMCG_DATA_OBJEXTS`-tagged `struct slabobj_ext *`; use `folio_memcg_check()` when slab status is unknown. `folio_memcg(folio)` can return `NULL` for uncharged folios even when `!mem_cgroup_disabled()`, so callers (`mem_cgroup_track_foreign_dirty()`, `__lruvec_stat_mod_folio()`, `mod_memcg_page_state()`, `mem_cgroup_migrate()`) must check for `NULL`. [F61, F142]
- `mod_memcg_state()`, `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()`, `memcg_page_state()`, and `lruvec_page_state()` use `memcg_page_state_unit(item)` units (bytes for `*_B`, `SZ_1K` for `NR_KERNEL_STACK_KB`, `PAGE_SIZE` otherwise), whereas `*_output()` helpers multiply by `memcg_page_state_output_unit(item)`. `mod_memcg_state()` and `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()` normalize `val` via `memcg_state_val_in_pages(idx, val)` (`max(val * unit / PAGE_SIZE, 1UL)` for sub-page units) before `memcg_rstat_updated()`. [F64, F65, F132]

## Error Handling

- In `try_charge_memcg()`, when `do_memsw_account()` is enabled, `&memcg->memsw` is charged before `&memcg->memory`; if `page_counter_try_charge(&memcg->memory, batch, &counter)` fails, immediately roll back `page_counter_uncharge(&memcg->memsw, batch)`. In `page_counter_try_charge()`, when ancestor `c` exceeds `c->max`, `c->usage` is reverted via `atomic_long_sub(nr_pages, &c->usage)` and `*fail = c`, so the `failed:` loop must stop at `c != *fail`. [F21, F117]
- Callers of `uncharge_folio()` and `uncharge_batch()` must zero `struct uncharge_gather` via `uncharge_gather_clear(&ug)`; in `uncharge_folio()`, when `ug->memcg != memcg`, flush any non-NULL `ug->memcg` via `uncharge_batch(ug)` and `uncharge_gather_clear(ug)` before setting `ug->memcg = memcg` and calling `css_get(&memcg->css)`; and `__mem_cgroup_uncharge_folios()` must guard `uncharge_batch(&ug)` with `if (ug.memcg)`. [F69, F121]
- Every stat or event passed to `memcg_page_state()`, `lruvec_page_state()`, `mod_memcg_state()`, `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()`, `count_memcg_events()`, or `memcg_events()` must be in `memcg_node_stat_items[]`, `memcg_stat_items[]`, or `memcg_vm_event_stat[]` and checked with `BAD_STAT_IDX(i)`. Never pass items `>= NR_VM_NODE_STAT_ITEMS` (`memcg_stat_items`) to `lruvec_page_state*()` or `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()`, as `struct lruvec_stats` has size `NR_MEMCG_NODE_STAT_ITEMS`. [F68, F99, F100]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `try_charge_memcg()`, when `page_counter_try_charge()` fails at `counter`, downcast via `mem_cgroup_from_counter(counter, member)` using the exact member (`memory` vs `memsw`) that failed; if `batch > nr_pages`, retry with `batch = nr_pages` before reclaim; target `mem_over_limit` for reclaim, stock draining, margin checks, OOM, and `MEMCG_MAX`, but target `memcg` for force-charging and stock refilling; and after `mem_cgroup_oom()`, set `passed_oom`, reset `nr_retries = MAX_RECLAIM_RETRIES`, and `goto nomem` if `passed_oom && task_is_dying()`. [F112, F113, F146]
- Avoid 32-bit overflow: cast `unsigned long` page counts to `u64` before multiplying by `PAGE_SIZE` or `K()` (`seq_puts_memcg_tunable()`, `memory_current_read()`, `mem_cgroup_read_u64()`, `mem_cgroup_print_oom_meminfo()`), and in `calculate_overage()`, clamp `high = max(high, 1UL)` and use `div64_u64((u64)(usage - high) << MEMCG_DELAY_PRECISION_SHIFT, high)`. [F127, F128]
- Avoid unsigned underflow: read `state[i]` / `state_local[i]` into a signed `long` and clamp `< 0` to `0` under `CONFIG_SMP` in `memcg_page_state*()` and `lruvec_page_state*()`; clamp usage against limits before unsigned subtraction in `mem_cgroup_wb_stats()`, `mem_cgroup_margin()`, and `soft_limit_excess()`; and use signed `min_t(long, ...)` for `READ_ONCE(memcg->swap.max) - page_counter_read(&memcg->swap)` in `mem_cgroup_get_nr_swap_pages()`. [F130, F137, F138]
