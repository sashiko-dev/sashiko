# QUALCOMM ATHEROS ATH10K WIRELESS DRIVER

Core infrastructure, hardware/bus abstraction (including AHB platform attachment), bootloader (BMI) messaging, Copy Engine (32-bit and 64-bit) DMA ring management, firmware/board/calibration loading, asynchronous crash recovery and devcoredump generation, and per-device/per-station debugfs interfaces for the Qualcomm Atheros 802.11ac (`ath10k`) wireless driver.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- On 64-bit targets (`ar->hw_params.target_64bit` / `ce_64_ops`), CE rings must use 16-byte `struct ce_desc_64` (`CE_SRC_RING_TO_DESC_64` / `CE_DEST_RING_TO_DESC_64`) instead of 8-byte `struct ce_desc`, and `ath10k_ce_src_ring_base_addr_set()` / `ath10k_ce_dest_ring_base_addr_set()` must null-check `ce_set_src_ring_base_addr_hi` / `ce_set_dest_ring_base_addr_hi` (which are `NULL` in 32-bit `ce_ops`). [F49, F82]
- CE free-slot checks must pass `sw_index - 1` as `toidx` to `CE_RING_DELTA(nentries_mask, write_index, sw_index - 1)`, and `ath10k_ce_rx_update_write_idx()` must decrement `nentries` by 1 if `((cur_write_idx + nentries) & nentries_mask) == dest_ring->sw_index` so `write_index` never catches up to `sw_index`. [F115]
- `ath10k_ce_alloc_src_ring*()` / `ath10k_ce_alloc_dest_ring*()` must round `nentries` via `roundup_pow_of_two()`, set `nentries_mask = nentries - 1`, and allocate `CE_DESC_RING_ALIGN` extra DMA bytes to align `base_addr_owner_space` and `base_addr_ce_space`. `ath10k_ce_init_src_ring()` / `ath10k_ce_init_dest_ring()` must zero `base_addr_owner_space` and sync `sw_index`, `hw_index`, and `write_index` from hardware registers masked with `nentries_mask`. [F60, F83]
- Because AHB devices embed `struct ath10k_ahb` at `&ath10k_pci_priv(ar)->ahb[0]` and reuse `ath10k_pci_*` helpers, `ath10k_ahb_probe()` must pass `sizeof(*ar_pci) + sizeof(*ar_ahb)` to `ath10k_core_create()` and initialize `ar_pci->mem`, `ar_pci->mem_len`, `ar_pci->ar`, `ar_pci->ce.bus_ops`, `ar_pci->targ_cpu_to_ce_addr`, `ar->ce_priv`, and `ar_pci->oper_irq_mode = ATH10K_PCI_IRQ_INTX`. [F62, F128]
- In `ath10k_ahb_halt_chip()`, `ar_ahb->tcsr_mem` accesses must select register offsets via `ATH10K_AHB_WLAN_CORE_ID_REG` (0 or 1) and use read-modify-write on `haltreq_reg` and `glb_cfg_reg`. After halting the AXI bus and asserting all five resets, `AHB_AXI_BUS_HALT_REQ` and `TCSR_WIFIX_GLB_CFG_DISABLE_CORE_CLK` must be cleared before deasserting `core_cold_rst`. [F63, F137]
- `ath10k_core_restart()` must set `ATH10K_FLAG_CRASH_FLUSH` before draining TX and waking waiters (and `ath10k_core_start()` must clear it). Under `ar->conf_mutex`, `ATH10K_STATE_ON` transitions to `ATH10K_STATE_RESTARTING` and calls `ath10k_halt(ar)` before `ieee80211_restart_hw(ar->hw)`, whereas a second crash in `ATH10K_STATE_RESTARTED` must transition to `ATH10K_STATE_WEDGED` without restarting. [F24, F68, F106]
- `ath10k_core_fetch_board_data_api_n()` must scan the entire `board-2.bin` IE list via `ath10k_core_search_bd()` for the variant `boardname` before making fallback passes for `fallback_boardname1` and `fallback_boardname2`. In `ath10k_core_parse_bd_ie_board()`, `name_match_found` requires both `board_ie_len == strlen(boardname)` and `memcmp` match, gating `ATH10K_BD_IE_BOARD_DATA`. [F99, F102]
- In `ath10k_init_hw_params()` and `_ath10k_coredump_get_mem_layout()`, hardware lookups must match the 3-tuple `(target_version/hw_id, bus, dev_id/hw_rev)`, and `ath10k_init_hw_params()` must return `-EINVAL` if `i == ARRAY_SIZE(ath10k_hw_params_list)` before dereferencing `hw_params`. [F110]
- Calibration data in `ath10k_download_cal_dt()` and `ath10k_download_cal_nvmem()` must equal `ar->hw_params.cal_data_len` (<= `ATH10K_DEBUG_CAL_DATA_LEN`, returning `-EMSGSIZE` otherwise). In `ath10k_push_board_ext_data()`, when `hi_board_ext_data` is non-zero, `data_len` must equal `board_size + board_ext_size` before reading `data + board_data_size`. [F89, F90]
- In `coredump.c`, `struct ath10k_mem_section` arrays must have `mem_region->start <= sections[0].start` and strictly ascending ranges, with `IRAM1`/`IRAM2` last in `qca6174_hw30_mem_regions`. `ath10k_coredump_get_ramdump_size()` must add `sizeof(struct ath10k_dump_ram_data_hdr)` per region, and `ath10k_coredump_build()` must add identical TLV + payload sizes to `len` and `sofar`. [F92, F93, F124]
- In `debug.c`, `fops_cal_data` and `simple_open` files store `struct ath10k *` in `file->private_data`, whereas `fops_fw_stats`, `fops_tpc_stats`, and `fops_tpc_stats_final` store a `vmalloc` `char *` buffer. In `debugfs_sta.c`, files store `struct ieee80211_sta *sta` in `file->private_data` (`arsta = sta->drv_priv`), and `ath10k_dbg_sta_dump_tx_stats()` must null-check `arsta->tx_stats` and format `stats->bw[j]` in `enum rate_info_bw` order (20, 5, 10, 40, 80, 160 MHz). [F54, F79, F130, F131]
- Before requesting fw stats in `ath10k_debug_fw_stats_request()`, `ath10k_debug_fw_stats_reset()` must clear `fw_stats_done` and `fw_stats.extended` and free all four `fw_stats` lists under `ar->data_lock`. In `ath10k_sta_update_rx_duration()`, `stats->extended` selects between iterating `stats->peers_extd` (`ath10k_fw_extd_stats_peer`) or `stats->peers` (`ath10k_fw_stats_peer`). [F53, F74]

## Synchronization

- `ce->ce_lock` and `ar->data_lock` must always use `spin_lock_bh()` / `spin_unlock_bh()`. `__ath10k_ce_send_revert()`, `__ath10k_ce_rx_num_free_bufs()`, `__ath10k_ce_rx_post_buf*()`, `ath10k_ce_send_nolock()`, and `ath10k_ce_completed_send_next_nolock()` require the caller to hold `ce->ce_lock`. [F161, F162]
- `ar->data_lock` nests inside `ar->conf_mutex` and protects `ar->debug.fw_stats` lists, `fw_stats_done`, `tpc_stats`, and `tpc_stats_final`. `ath10k_debug_fw_stats_process()` holds `ar->data_lock` across `ath10k_sta_update_rx_duration()` (which must not sleep or re-lock `data_lock`), and debugfs read handlers must allocate `GFP_KERNEL` buffers before `spin_lock_bh(&ar->data_lock)` and call `simple_read_from_buffer()` after unlocking. [F29, F164, F171]
- `ath10k_sta_update_rx_tid_stats_ampdu()` and `ath10k_sta_update_rx_tid_stats()` must hold `rcu_read_lock()` across station/peer lookup and acquire `spin_lock_bh(&ar->data_lock)` inside RCU to update `arsta->tid_stats` (which has `IEEE80211_NUM_TIDS + 1` entries, using `<= IEEE80211_NUM_TIDS` bounds). [F30, F95]
- `cancel_work_sync(&ar->set_coverage_class_work)` in `ath10k_core_restart()` and `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&ar->debug.htt_stats_dwork)` in `ath10k_debug_unregister()` must not be called under `ar->conf_mutex`; `ath10k_debug_stop()` holds `ar->conf_mutex` and must use `cancel_delayed_work()`. [F11]
- `ath10k_core_napi_enable()` and `ath10k_core_napi_sync_disable()` require `ar->conf_mutex` and test/update `ATH10K_FLAG_NAPI_ENABLED`. In `ath10k_ahb_hif_stop()`, interrupts must be masked via `ath10k_ahb_irq_disable()` and drained via `synchronize_irq(ar_ahb->irq)` before calling `ath10k_core_napi_sync_disable()` and `ath10k_pci_flush()`. [F22, F23]
- Access to `ar->coredump.fw_crash_data` across `ath10k_coredump_new()`, `ath10k_ce_dump_registers()`, and `ath10k_coredump_build()` must be serialized by `ar->dump_mutex`, with `ce->ce_lock` nesting inside `ar->dump_mutex`. [F28]
- In `ath10k_core_start()`, `ar->free_vdev_map` and `ar->arvifs` must be initialized before `ath10k_htt_setup()`. In `ath10k_core_reset_rx_filter()`, `ath10k_wmi_barrier()` must be called after deleting the dummy vdev and before `ath10k_htt_rx_ring_refill()`. [F19, F20]
- In `ath10k_ce_per_engine_service()`, `ath10k_ce_engine_int_status_clear()` must be called before `ce_state->recv_cb` or `ce_state->send_cb`, and CE registers must not be accessed after clearing the last CE interrupt without waking the target. [F33]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `ath10k_ahb_probe()` and `ath10k_ahb_remove()`, `ath10k_pci_setup_resource()` pairs with `ath10k_pci_release_resource()`, and `ath10k_core_destroy()` must be called last after `ath10k_pci_release_resource()` and `ath10k_ahb_resource_deinit()`. Because `ath10k_ahb_resource_init()` maps `gcc_mem` and `tcsr_mem` with raw `ioremap()`, error labels and `ath10k_ahb_resource_deinit()` must explicitly `iounmap()` both, `devm_iounmap()` `mem`, and reset all three to `NULL`. [F1, F2]
- In `ath10k_ahb_prepare_device()` and `ath10k_ahb_chip_reset()`, `ath10k_ahb_clock_enable()` and `ath10k_ahb_clock_disable()` must be balanced—with `ath10k_ahb_chip_reset()` halting the chip and disabling clocks before calling `ath10k_ahb_prepare_device()`. [F157]
- `ath10k_core_free_firmware_files()` and `ath10k_core_free_board_files()` must guard every `release_firmware()` call with `!IS_ERR()` on `fw_file.firmware`, `cal_file`, `pre_cal_file` (kept separate from `cal_file`), `board`, and `ext_board`, and reset all pointers and lengths to `NULL`/`0`. [F5, F66]
- In `ath10k_core_start()`, errors after `ath10k_wmi_attach()`, `ath10k_htt_tx_start()`, `ath10k_htt_rx_alloc()`, and `ath10k_hif_start()` must unwind in reverse order under `ar->conf_mutex`. `ath10k_core_unregister()` must call `cancel_work_sync(&ar->register_work)`, return if `ATH10K_FLAG_CORE_REGISTERED` is unset, and call `ath10k_spectral_destroy()` before `ath10k_mac_unregister()`. [F9, F10]
- In `ath10k_debug_fw_stats_process()`, all four temporary lists in `stats` (`pdevs`, `vdevs`, `peers`, `peers_extd`) must be freed at `free:`, `stats.peers_extd` must only be spliced when `!list_empty(&stats.peers)`, and exceeding `ATH10K_MAX_NUM_PEER_IDS` must free both `ar->debug.fw_stats.peers` and `peers_extd`. [F16]
- `ath10k_coredump_submit()` transfers ownership of the `vzalloc` `ath10k_dump_file_data` buffer to `dev_coredumpv()`, whereas `ar->coredump.fw_crash_data` and `crash_data->ramdump_buf` remain owned by `ar` and are freed with `vfree()` in `ath10k_coredump_unregister()` / `ath10k_coredump_destroy()`. [F144]

## API Contracts

- `ar->running_fw` is a borrowed read-only pointer assigned in `ath10k_core_start()` and must never be modified or passed to `release_firmware()`. `ath10k_debug_tpc_stats_process()` and `ath10k_debug_tpc_stats_final_process()` take ownership of `tpc_stats`: under `CONFIG_ATH10K_DEBUGFS` they must `kfree()` the old pointer under `ar->data_lock` before storing the new one, while `!CONFIG_ATH10K_DEBUGFS` stubs must `kfree(tpc_stats)`. [F8, F17]
- Before calling any CE function, `ar->ce_priv` must point to a valid `struct ath10k_ce` with `ce.bus_ops` initialized. Target DMA must be stopped before `ath10k_ce_revoke_recv_next()` or `ath10k_ce_cancel_send_next()`. In `_ath10k_ce_send_nolock*()`, the hardware write index register is only updated when `CE_SEND_FLAG_GATHER` is cleared, and `__ath10k_ce_send_revert()` must only roll back uncommitted gather descriptors. In CE completion/revoke/cancel functions, `per_transfer_contextp` may be `NULL` and must be null-checked. [F25, F46, F129, F153]
- `ath10k_coredump_new()` and `ath10k_coredump_get_mem_layout()` return `NULL` when `ath10k_coredump_mask == 0` or `!CONFIG_DEV_COREDUMP`; dump helpers must null-check `crash_data`, and non-coredump callers (`ath10k_core_copy_target_iram()`) must use `_ath10k_coredump_get_mem_layout()` and treat `NULL` as `return 0`. [F39, F40]
- `ath10k_bmi_lz_data*()` require 4-byte-aligned `length`, so `ath10k_bmi_fast_download()` must send `rounddown(length, 4)` bytes, pad any trailer into a zeroed 4-byte buffer (`length = 4`), and finish with `ath10k_bmi_lz_stream_start(ar, 0x00)`. Once `ath10k_bmi_done()` sets `ar->bmi.done_sent = true`, BMI commands return `-EBUSY` until `ath10k_bmi_start()`. [F50, F64, F156]
- `ath10k_core_init_firmware_features()` must scope `ar->fw_stats_req_mask` and target resource limits to `fw_file->wmi_op_version` rather than enabling WMI stat flags unconditionally. [F147]

## Error Handling

- `ath10k_ahb_clock_enable()`, `ath10k_ahb_release_reset()`, and `ath10k_ahb_halt_chip()` must check all `ar_ahb` clock/reset pointers with `IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`, and `ath10k_ahb_clock_enable()` must unwind `cmd_clk` and `ref_clk` via `clk_disable_unprepare()` on error. [F3, F44]
- Errors after `ath10k_core_fetch_firmware_files()` in `ath10k_core_probe_fw()` must jump to `err_free_firmware_files` (or `err_unlock`), and errors after `ath10k_fetch_fw_file()` in `ath10k_core_fetch_board_data_api_n()` / `ath10k_core_fetch_firmware_api_n()` must jump to `err` to free the files and reset pointers to `NULL`. [F6, F7]
- In `ath10k_ce_alloc_pipe()`, if `ce_alloc_src_ring()` or `ce_alloc_dst_ring()` returns `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)`, `ce_state->src_ring` or `dest_ring` must be reset to `NULL` because `_ath10k_ce_free_pipe*()` only checks for non-`NULL`. In `_ath10k_ce_completed_send_next_nolock*()`, `ath10k_ce_src_ring_read_index_get() == 0xffffffff` must return `-ENODEV` before masking with `nentries_mask`. [F38, F48]
- `-EPROBE_DEFER` from `ath10k_download_cal_nvmem()` must be returned immediately without falling back to other calibration sources, whereas `-EOPNOTSUPP` from `ath10k_core_get_board_id_from_otp()` / `ath10k_core_get_ext_board_id_from_otp()` is non-fatal and absent optional cal/board sources must log via `ath10k_dbg()`, not `ath10k_warn()`/`ath10k_err()`. [F51, F52, F178]

## External Contracts

- In debugfs `.write` handlers (`debug.c`, `debugfs_sta.c`), an unchanged value must return `count` (not `0`) as an early no-op before branching on non-zero, and error jumps to an unlock label must preserve negative `ret` rather than returning `count`. [F41, F97, F179]
- On 64-bit CE targets, high 32-bit DMA address words must be masked with `CE_DESC_ADDR_HI_MASK` (`GENMASK(4, 0)`), and destination addresses in `__ath10k_ce_rx_post_buf_64()` with `CE_DESC_ADDR_MASK` (`GENMASK_ULL(34, 0)`). [F116]
- In `ath10k_debug_get_et_stats()`, the `data[i++]` count must equal `ATH10K_SSTATS_LEN` (`ARRAY_SIZE(ath10k_gstrings_stats)`). `ath10k_core_restart()` must not call `ieee80211_hw_restart_disconnect()` before `ieee80211_restart_hw()`, and `ath10k_fetch_fw_file()` must use `firmware_request_nowarn()`. [F96, F175, F177]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `_ath10k_ce_completed_recv_next_nolock*()`, the descriptor must be snapshotted before checking `nbytes == 0` (returning `-EIO` without advancing `sw_index`), and `dest_ring->per_transfer_context[sw_index]` must be cleared for every pipe except `ce_state->id == 5`. In `_ath10k_ce_completed_send_next_nolock*()`, popping `sw_index` must zero `desc->nbytes` and clear `per_transfer_context[sw_index]`. [F57, F58]
- `ath10k_bmi_lz_data_large()` must `kzalloc()` `sizeof(*cmd) + BMI_MAX_LARGE_DATA_SIZE - BMI_MAX_DATA_SIZE` bytes and `kfree(cmd)` on all paths. `ath10k_bmi_write_memory()` must copy `txlen = min(length, BMI_MAX_DATA_SIZE - hdrlen)` bytes before `roundup(txlen, 4)`, and clamp `txlen = min(txlen, length)` before subtracting from `length`. [F4, F85, F87]
- When walking `struct ath10k_fw_ie` records, advance by `sizeof(*hdr)` then `ALIGN(ie_len, 4)`, and bound non-NUL-terminated string IEs by `ie_len`. `ath10k_core_fetch_firmware_api_n()` must populate its `fw_file` parameter (not `ar->running_fw` or `normal_mode_fw.fw_file`) and unpack `ATH10K_FW_IE_FEATURES` byte-by-byte (`i / 8`, `i % 8`) via `__set_bit()`. [F100, F127, F133, F135]
- Zero-initialize `buf` before `ath10k_core_get_fw_features_str()`, `variant` in `ath10k_core_create_board_name()`, and stack buffers in debugfs `.write` handlers (passing `sizeof(buf) - 1` to `simple_write_to_buffer()`), and NUL-terminate `buf` on all paths in `ath10k_tpc_stats_fill()`. [F42, F43, F132, F134]
- In `ath10k_core_start()`, `ar->free_vdev_map` initialization must guard `ar->max_num_vdevs >= 64` before shifting `1LL << ar->max_num_vdevs`. In `ath10k_init_uart()`, when `!uart_print`, `return 0` immediately after `uart_pin_workaround`. [F98, F113]
