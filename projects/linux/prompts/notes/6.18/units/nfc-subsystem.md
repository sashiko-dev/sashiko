# NFC SUBSYSTEM

This unit implements the Linux NFC core device and netlink layer (`net/nfc/core.c`), `AF_NFC` sockets (`net/nfc/af_nfc.c`), the LLCP, Digital, HCI (with SHDLC/NOP LLC), and NCI (with SPI/UART) protocol stacks, and the `s3fwrn5`, `st-nci`, `st21nfca`, and `st95hf` hardware drivers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `nfc_llcp_put_ssap` and `struct nfc_llcp_local`, SAP bitmaps `local_wks`, `local_sdp` / `local_sdp_cnt[]`, and `local_sap` must be checked in threshold order (`LLCP_WKS_NUM_SAP`=16, `LLCP_LOCAL_NUM_SAP`=32, `LLCP_MAX_SAP`=64) and indexed by range-relative offsets (`0..15`, `ssap - LLCP_WKS_NUM_SAP`, `ssap - LLCP_LOCAL_NUM_SAP`), never raw SAPs. Dynamic SDP SAPs are refcounted in `local_sdp_cnt[]` and only cleared when `atomic_dec_and_test` is true; `llcp_sock_release` must check `reserved_ssap < LLCP_SAP_MAX` before calling `nfc_llcp_put_ssap`. [F241, F253, F291, F318]
- In `struct nfc_digital_dev`, changing RF technology (`digital_target_found`, `digital_in_recv_psl_res`, `digital_tg_set_rf_tech`) must update `curr_rf_tech` and non-NULL `skb_add_crc`/`skb_check_crc` (using `digital_skb_add_crc_none`/`digital_skb_check_crc_none` when `DIGITAL_DRV_CAPS_IN_CRC`/`TG_CRC` holds). Before calling `nfc_targets_found` or `nfc_tm_activated`, `poll_tech_count` must be saved and zeroed, and restored on error. [F72, F126, F263, F266]
- In HCP fragmentation (`nfc_hci_hcp_message_tx`, `nfc_hci_recv_from_llc`, `nci_hci_send_data`, `nci_hci_data_received_cb`), every fragment has a 1-byte packet header (`cb | pipe`), only the first has the 1-byte message header (`hci_len = payload_len + 1`; each fragment copies `data_link_len - 1` data bytes), and only the final fragment sets `~NFC_HCI_FRAGMENT` (`0x80`). Unfragmented packets must mask `packet->header &= NFC_HCI_FRAGMENT` (`0x7f`), and after reassembly `packet` must be refreshed from `hcp_skb->data`. [F250, F276, F290, F313]
- Requeuing unacknowledged frames: `llt_ndlc_requeue_data_pending` must `skb_dequeue_tail` from `ack_pending_q`, set the PCB retransmit bit in `skb->data[0]`, and `skb_queue_head` onto `send_q`; `llc_shdlc_requeue_ack_pending` must `skb_dequeue_tail`, pull the 1-byte SHDLC control header, `skb_queue_head` onto `send_q`, and reset `shdlc->ns = shdlc->dnr`; `nfc_llcp_recv_hdlc` must use `skb_queue_reverse_walk_safe` when prepending `tx_pending_queue` to `local->tx_queue` via `skb_queue_head`. [F76, F247, F297]
- In `struct nfc_hci_dev` and `struct nci_hci_dev`, `pipes[]` is sized to `*_MAX_PIPES` (128) and `gate2pipe[]` to `*_MAX_GATES` (256); never index `pipes[]` with sentinel values (`0x80`/`0x81`). Both arrays (plus per-device `init_data.gates[j].pipe` in `st21nfca_hci_load_session` and `st_nci_hci_load_session`, never mutating static `st21nfca_gates`/`st_nci_gates`) must be updated in lockstep when connecting, creating, loading (checking `*_DM_IS_PIPE_OPEN` `(p & 0x0f) == 0x06` and `j < ARRAY_SIZE`), or resetting pipes. [F73, F87, F100, F249]
- In NCI (`net/nfc/nci/`), `nci_cmd_work` decrements `ndev->cmd_cnt` and arms `cmd_timer`, while `nci_rsp_packet` (after stopping `cmd_timer`) or `nci_cmd_timer` resets `cmd_cnt` to 1 and queues `cmd_work`. `nci_rf_deactivate_rsp_packet` must not call `nci_req_complete` when `status == NCI_STATUS_OK` and `state == NCI_POLL_ACTIVE` (deferred to `nci_rf_deactivate_ntf_packet`, which purges `tx_q`, frees/nulls `rx_data_reassembly`, completes `NCI_DATA_EXCHANGE` with `-EIO`, and calls `nci_clear_target_list` only on `IDLE_MODE` or `DISCOVERY`). [F195, F257, F259, F295]

## Synchronization
- In `net/nfc/core.c`, `dev->shutting_down` is set under `device_lock(&dev->dev)` in `nfc_unregister_device` (which must unlock before `timer_delete_sync(&dev->check_pres_timer)` and `cancel_work_sync(&dev->check_pres_work)`) and must be checked under `device_lock` in all core entry points and before `mod_timer(&dev->check_pres_timer, ...)`. `check_pres_timer` must be stopped before `im_transceive` or `deactivate_target`, and `nfc_check_pres_work` must copy `dev->active_target->idx` and drop `device_lock` before calling `nfc_target_lost`. `dev->rfkill` lifecycle and `nfc_dev_up` access also require `device_lock`. [F2, F23, F25, F69, F70, F151, F152]
- Atomic lock contexts: `nfc_sock_create` holds `read_lock(&proto_tab_lock)` across `nfc_protocol.create` (while `proto_register`/`proto_unregister` must run outside `write_lock(&proto_tab_lock)` after/before updating `proto_tab[]`); `nci_uart_tty_receive` holds `spin_lock(&nu->rx_lock)` across `nci_uart_default_recv_buf` and `nu->ops.recv`; `nfc_llcp_send_to_raw_sock` holds `read_lock(&local->raw_sockets.lock)`; and `nfc_llcp_socket_release` holds `write_lock` on `sockets.lock`/`raw_sockets.lock` (requiring `bh_lock_sock`, not `lock_sock`). None of these sections may sleep or use `GFP_KERNEL`. [F29, F148, F149, F345, F346, F347]
- In LLCP (`net/nfc/llcp_*.c`), lock order is `local->sdp_lock` outer to `local->sockets.lock` inner, and listening parent socket lock (`lock_sock_nested`, `lock_sock`, or `bh_lock_sock` under `write_lock(&local->sockets.lock)`) outer to child `accept_sk` lock. Each `llcp_sock_list` requires `write_lock` for link/unlink and `read_lock` across iteration and `sock_hold`. `local->sdreq_lock` protects `pending_sdreqs` and must be dropped before `nfc_genl_llc_send_sdres`. After `release_sock`/`schedule_timeout`, relock and recheck state/errors; `llcp_sock_connect` and `llcp_sock_getname` must validate `sk_state` and `llcp_sock->dev` under `lock_sock`. [F218, F219, F220, F221, F222, F223, F349]
- In NCI (`net/nfc/nci/`), `nci_close_device` flushes `rx_wq`, `tx_wq`, and `cmd_wq` while holding `ndev->req_lock`, so work items and `rsp`/`ntf` callbacks must never acquire `req_lock` (e.g. via `nci_request`), whereas `init`/`setup`/`post_setup` helpers use `__nci_request` directly. `nci_close_device` must call `nci_req_cancel(ndev, ENODEV)` before locking `req_lock`, purge `rx_q`/`tx_q` and flush `rx_wq`/`tx_wq` before `CORE_RESET` and `ops->close`, and preserve `BIT(NCI_UNREG)` when clearing `ndev->flags` (set `NCI_INIT` and `cmd_cnt = 1` before init/reset commands; `nci_recv_frame` drops frames unless `NCI_UP` or `NCI_INIT` is set). [F206, F208, F213, F330, F331, F332, F353, F354, F355]
- In `net/nfc/digital_core.c`, `ddev->cmd_lock` protects `cmd_queue` and the head `cmd->pending` flag (kept at the head with `pending = 1` until `digital_wq_cmd_complete` or synchronous error unlinks it and schedules `cmd_work`) and must be dropped before calling `ddev->ops` or `cmd->cmd_cb`. `ddev->poll_lock` protects `poll_tech_count` (which `digital_poll_next_tech` must check non-zero before modulo) and must be dropped after zeroing `poll_tech_count` before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&ddev->poll_work)`. [F30, F32, F110, F114]

## Object Lifetimes
- `nfc_allocate_device` initializes `dev->dev`, while `nfc_register_device`/`nfc_unregister_device` only call `device_add`/`device_del`; callers must always call `nfc_free_device` after unregistering or on registration failure. `nci_allocate_device` and `nfc_hci_allocate_device` also require explicit `nci_free_device`/`nfc_hci_free_device` (whereas `nci_spi_allocate_spi` is `devm_kzalloc`-managed, and `ndev->conn_info_list` entries use `devm_kzalloc`/`devm_kfree` on `&ndev->nfc_dev->dev`, clearing `ndev->rf_conn_info = NULL` if equal on close). [F136, F194, F336]
- Because `dev->active_target` is an interior pointer into `dev->targets`, `nfc_deactivate_target`, `nfc_dep_link_down`, and `nfc_target_lost` (when `i < dev->n_targets`) must clear `dev->active_target = NULL` under `device_lock(&dev->dev)`. [F3, F105]
- Reference counting in LLCP: every `kref` on `struct nfc_llcp_local` (`nfc_llcp_register_device`, `nfc_llcp_local_get` under `llcp_devices_lock` in `nfc_llcp_find_local`) is paired with an `nfc_get_device` reference released via `nfc_put_device` after `kref_put` in `nfc_llcp_local_put`. A socket in `LLCP_CONNECTED` holds an `nfc_dev` reference on `llcp_sock->dev` that must be released via `nfc_put_device` on any transition out of `LLCP_CONNECTED` or in `llcp_sock_destruct`. `nfc_llcp_accept_enqueue` pairs `sock_hold(sk)` and `sk_acceptq_added(parent)` with `sk_acceptq_removed(parent)` and `sock_put(sk)` in `nfc_llcp_accept_dequeue`/`unlink`. [F204, F212, F251, F252]
- Stack waiter lifetimes: `nfc_hci_execute_cmd` (`ew_wq`, `hcp_ew` on stack; `nfc_hci_execute_cb` sets `exec_complete = true` last) and `llc_shdlc_connect` (`connect_wq` on stack; `llc_shdlc_connect_complete` only called in `SHDLC_CONNECTING`/`SHDLC_NEGOTIATING`) must use uninterruptible waits (`wait_event`), whereas `__nci_request` uses `wait_for_completion_interruptible_timeout`, so `nci_request` callbacks must copy stack `opt` data into SKBs before returning. [F225, F320, F324]
- In Digital NFC-DEP and polling, `ddev->saved_skb` (which requires `skb_get` before retransmission in `digital_in_send_saved_skb`/`digital_tg_send_saved_skb`) and `ddev->chaining_skb` must be freed and nulled on completion or error, and `struct nfc_target` passed as `cb_context` across async `digital_in_send_cmd` calls (`digital_in_recv_sens_res`, `digital_in_recv_sensb_res`) must be heap-allocated and freed on terminal completion or error. [F201, F202, F265, F328]

## API Contracts
- Always consume caller's `skb` (even on error): `nfc_data_exchange`, `nfc_tm_data_received` (including in `digital_tg_recv_dep_req`, which must set `resp = NULL` if `nfc_tm_data_received` returns non-zero before `exit:`), `nci_send_data` (after `nci_send_frame` calls `skb_orphan`), `nci_spi_send`, `s3fwrn5_nci_send`, and `nu->ops.recv` called from `nci_uart_default_recv_buf` (which sets `nu->rx_skb = NULL`). [F5, F11, F173, F196, F198, F229, F238, F359]
- Never consume caller's `skb` on synchronous error: `digital_send_cmd` (caller frees `skb` and `params` on `-ENOMEM`), `nfc_digital_ops` async callbacks (`in_send_cmd`, `tg_send_cmd`, `tg_listen*`: `digital_wq_cmd` frees `cmd->req` when callback returns non-zero; once returning `0`, driver must eventually invoke `nfc_digital_cmd_complete_t` with `resp` or `ERR_PTR`, and the callback owns/frees non-`ERR_PTR` `resp`), `nfc_llc_xmit_from_hci` / `nfc_hci_ops.xmit`, and `s3fwrn5_fw_send_msg` (caller always frees `msg`, and frees `rsp` on `0`). [F8, F10, F13, F165, F166, F191]
- Never consume `skb` at all: physical transport `.write` callbacks and `nfc_hci_ops.xmit` (must return `0` on full write or negative errno, never positive byte count, and restore any pushed framing/CRC), `hdev->ops->im_transceive` (`hci_transceive` always frees `skb`; returns `<= 0` if handled or `1` for `NFC_HCI_WR_XCHG_DATA` fallback), and `nfc_llcp_rx_skb` handlers (must `skb_get(skb)` when `sock_queue_rcv_skb` succeeds because callers `kfree_skb`). [F49, F231, F296, F357]
- Conditional ownership: in `nfc_hci_event_received` and `st21nfca`/`st-nci` gate event handlers, returning `<= 0` means the handler consumed `skb` (`< 0` triggers `nfc_hci_driver_failure`), whereas returning `1` (including `default:` branches) leaves `skb` unconsumed for the caller to free; in `llcp_sock_recvmsg`, on `skb_copy_datagram_msg` `-EFAULT` or unconsumed bytes without `MSG_PEEK`, re-queue `skb` with `skb_queue_head(&sk->sk_receive_queue, skb)`; `st_nci_se_io` and `st21nfca_hci_se_io` take ownership of `cb_context` and must `kfree(cb_context)` on any synchronous error before arming `bwi_timer`. [F6, F103, F185, F230]
- Return value conventions and sentinels: `nfc_allocate_device`, `nfc_digital_allocate_device`, and `llcp_allocate_pdu` return `NULL` on error, whereas `nfc_llcp_allocate_snl` returns an `ERR_PTR`. `nfc_digital_cmd_complete_t` takes `ERR_PTR(-errno)` on error (never `NULL`), which `digital_in_send_complete` converts to `resp = NULL, rc = PTR_ERR(resp)`. `st_nci_control_se` and `st21nfca_hci_control_se` return positive `se_idx` on success (`< 0` on error); `nfc_hci_sak_to_protocol` returns `0xffffffff` on unknown SAK; `nfc_hci_create_pipe`/`nci_hci_create_pipe` return `0x80` (`*_INVALID_PIPE`); `nfc_llcp_get_sdp_ssap`/`get_local_ssap`/`reserve_sdp_ssap` return `LLCP_SAP_MAX` (`0xff`). `nfc_fw_download_done`, `nci_req_cancel`, and `nfc_llcp_socket_release`/`sk->sk_err` take positive errno values, while `nci_req_complete` takes an NCI status code. [F46, F50, F54, F65, F233, F234, F236, F242, F244, F245]

## Error Handling
- Packet length validation before reading headers or allocating: in `st21nfca_connectivity_event_received` and `st_nci_hci_connectivity_event_received` (`*_EVT_TRANSACTION`), guard checks must use `||` to progressively verify `skb->len >= 2`, `aid_len <= sizeof(transaction->aid)`, `skb->len >= aid_len + 4`, and `skb->len >= aid_len + 4 + params_len` before allocating `sizeof(*transaction) + params_len`. `nci_rx_work` must check `nci_valid_size(skb)`; `s3fwrn82_uart_read` must verify `recv_skb->len >= S3FWRN82_NCI_HEADER` (3) before subtracting; `st_nci_i2c_read`/`spi_read` must check `len <= ST_NCI_*_MAX_SIZE` (250); `digital_skb_check_crc` must reject `skb->len <= 2`; `nfc_llcp_recv_agf` must reject `pdu_len < LLCP_HEADER_SIZE || pdu_len > skb->len` and skip `LLCP_PDU_SYMM`/`AGF`; `nfc_llcp_build_sdreq_tlv` must reject `uri_len > U8_MAX - 4` and allocate `sdreq->tlv_len + 1`. [F86, F88, F93, F94, F99, F279, F302, F307, F314]
- In `net/nfc/digital_dep.c` and `net/nfc/digital_technology.c`, completion callbacks must check `IS_ERR(resp)` and set `resp = NULL` before jumping to cleanup labels that free `resp`. `digital_recv_dep_data_gather` returns `ERR_PTR(rc)` on error (having freed `resp` and `chaining_skb`), `NULL` when MI chaining continues, or the reassembled SKB; `digital_send_dep_data_prep` returns `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)`, a new fragment SKB (storing `skb` in `chaining_skb`), or `skb` itself (so callers only `kfree_skb(tmp_skb)` on send error when `tmp_skb != skb`, and must write `dep_req`/`dep_res` headers before calling it). [F226, F227, F228, F327]

## Known Pitfalls
- Protocol numbers vs bitmasks and sizes: `digital_target_found`, `nfc_activate_target`, `nci_activate_target`, and `ddev->curr_protocol` use unshifted `NFC_PROTO_*` IDs (validate `< NFC_PROTO_MAX` / `1..7` before `1 << protocol`), whereas `nfc_allocate_device`, `nfc_digital_allocate_device`, `nfc_start_poll`, `nfc_tm_activated`, and `target->supported_protocols` use `NFC_PROTO_*_MASK` bitmasks. `nfc_set_vendor_cmds`, `nci_set_vendor_cmds`, `nfc_hci_set_vendor_cmds`, and `init_data.gate_count` take `ARRAY_SIZE(...)`, whereas `memcpy` into `init_data.gates` and `nci_core_conn_create`'s `params_len` (`sizeof(*params) + params->length`) take byte sizes. [F115, F122, F123, F164, F177, F309]
- Sequence number and bitmask pitfalls: in `llt_ndlc_requeue_data_pending` and `llt_ndlc_rcv_queue`, mask `pcb` with `PCB_TYPE_MASK` and `PCB_SYNC_MASK` before comparing; in `llc_shdlc_handle_rcv_queue`, `control & SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_MASK` (`0xe0`) must match both `SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_I` (`0x80`) and `SHDLC_CONTROL_HEAD_I2` (`0xa0`) for I-frames, and SHDLC modulo-8 window checks must use `llc_shdlc_x_lt_y_lteq_z`, `llc_shdlc_x_lteq_y_lt_z`, and `llc_shdlc_w_used`; in NFC-DEP (`net/nfc/digital_dep.c` and `st21nfca_im_recv_dep_res_cb`), arithmetic on `curr_nfc_dep_pni` or `pfb` must be masked with `*_NFC_DEP_PFB_PNI()` (`& 0x03`). [F101, F118, F289, F311, F312]
- `struct nfc_target::idx` is a monotonically increasing ID from `dev->target_next_idx++`, so targets must be looked up via `nfc_find_target` rather than indexing `dev->targets[target_idx]`. `llcp_sock->service_name` (clamped to `NFC_LLCP_MAX_SERVICE_NAME` = 63) and wire SN/SDREQ buffers are not NUL-terminated. `nfc_llcp_connecting_sock_get` must reset `llcp_sock = NULL` after the `sk_for_each` loop, and `nfc_llcp_recv_connect` must not shadow the remote peer's `ssap` with the inner local `u8 ssap` when setting `new_sock->dsap` or calling `nfc_llcp_send_dm`. [F91, F272, F300, F305, F315]
