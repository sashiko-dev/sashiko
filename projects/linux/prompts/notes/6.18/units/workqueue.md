# WORKQUEUE

The workqueue subsystem (`kernel/workqueue.c`, `include/linux/workqueue.h`, `kernel/workqueue_internal.h`) provides the Linux kernel's generic asynchronous execution mechanism in process and bottom-half (`WQ_BH`) contexts. It manages per-CPU, BH, and dynamic unbound `worker_pool` instances, concurrency-managed `worker` kthreads, `rescuer` threads for `WQ_MEM_RECLAIM` forward progress, and `pool_workqueue` (`pwq`) intermediaries linking `workqueue_struct` instances to pools.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `work->data` is discriminated by `WORK_STRUCT_PWQ`: upper bits hold an unshifted `struct pool_workqueue *` (requiring `1 << WORK_STRUCT_PWQ_SHIFT` alignment) when `WORK_STRUCT_PWQ` is set, and off-queue `pool_id`, `disable` depth, and `WORK_OFFQ_*` flags when clear. `clear_pending_if_disabled()`, `__cancel_work()`, and `enable_work()` must unpack with `work_offqd_unpack()` and repack with `set_work_pool_and_clear_pending(..., work_offqd_pack_flags(&offqd))` to preserve `pool_id`, `disable`, and `WORK_OFFQ_BH`. [F46, F77, F80]
- Off-queue `work->data` must be initialized with `WORK_DATA_INIT()` (`WORK_STRUCT_NO_POOL`), as `0` is a valid ID in `worker_pool_idr` (`worker_pool_assign_id()` allocates in `[0, WORK_OFFQ_POOL_NONE)`). Shift operands in `set_work_pool_and_keep_pending()`, `set_work_pool_and_clear_pending()`, and `work_offqd_pack_flags()` must be widened to `unsigned long` (`1ul`), while `shift_and_mask()` must use `1U << bits` with `bits < 32` (`WORK_OFFQ_POOL_BITS <= 31`). [F53, F81, F112, F113]
- In `struct worker`, `entry` (idle list) and `hentry` (`busy_hash`) share a union discriminated by `WORKER_IDLE`: call `hash_del(&worker->hentry)` before `worker_enter_idle()` and `worker_leave_idle()` before `hash_add()`. [F47]
- For attached workers, `WORKER_NOT_RUNNING` flags (`WORKER_PREP`, `WORKER_CPU_INTENSIVE`, `WORKER_UNBOUND`, `WORKER_REBOUND`) must be modified via `worker_set_flags()` and `worker_clr_flags()` under `pool->lock` (except in `unbind_workers()`/`rebind_workers()`), adjusting `pool->nr_running` only when transitioning between zero and non-zero `WORKER_NOT_RUNNING` bits. [F65, F95]
- `process_one_work()` must unconditionally call `worker_clr_flags(worker, WORKER_CPU_INTENSIVE)` after the callback (as `wq_worker_tick()` sets it dynamically), and copy `worker->current_func` to `worker->last_func` under `pool->lock` before clearing `worker->current_func = NULL`. [F67, F68]
- BH pool pseudo-workers (`pool->flags & POOL_BH`) have `worker->task == NULL` and run in softirq context; shared paths (`create_worker()`, `kick_pool()`, `process_one_work()`, including its `in_atomic()` check and `cond_resched()`) must guard on `worker->task != NULL` or `pool->flags & POOL_BH`. [F48, F142]
- When `try_to_grab_pending()` steals a queued item with `WORK_STRUCT_INACTIVE` set, it must call `move_linked_works(work, &pwq->pool->worklist, NULL)` before `list_del_init(&work->entry)` and keep `WORK_STRUCT_INACTIVE` in the `work_data` passed to `pwq_dec_nr_in_flight()`. [F61]
- In `__queue_work()`, when `req_cpu == WORK_CPU_UNBOUND`, call `wq_select_unbound_cpu()` only if `wq->flags & WQ_UNBOUND` (bound queues use `raw_smp_processor_id()`); skip `last_pool` redirection when `wq->flags & __WQ_ORDERED` so ordered items always use the newest `pwq`. For `__WQ_ORDERED` queues, `apply_wqattrs_prepare()` sets `ctx->dfl_pwq->plugged = true` when `!list_empty(&wq->pwqs)`, and `pwq_release_workfn()` calls `unplug_oldest_pwq(wq)`. [F78, F92, F93]
- Concurrency limits must be written to `wq->saved_max_active` and `wq->saved_min_active` under `wq->mutex` (clamping `saved_min_active` to `max_active` in `workqueue_set_max_active()` on `WQ_UNBOUND` queues) followed by `wq_adjust_max_active(wq)`. [F71, F72]
- `wq->node_nr_active[]` is allocated only for `WQ_UNBOUND` queues (sized `nr_node_ids + 1`, where `nr_node_ids` is the `NUMA_NO_NODE` entry accessed via `wq_node_nr_active(wq, node)`). In `wq_update_node_max_active()`, `NUMA_NO_NODE` always gets `max_active`, per-node limits use `DIV_ROUND_UP(max_active * node_cpus, total_cpus)`, and `!total_cpus` after subtracting an offlining CPU (`off_cpu >= 0`) must return early before dividing. [F70, F83, F115, F116, F121]
- For shared unbound pools in `init_worker_pool()` and `get_unbound_pool()`, workqueue-only fields (`affn_scope`, `ordered`, and `cpumask` when `affn_strict` is set) must be cleared via `wqattrs_clear_for_pool()` and ignored by `wqattrs_hash()` and `wqattrs_equal()`. `wqattrs_hash()` must pass `BITS_TO_LONGS(nr_cpumask_bits) * sizeof(long)` to `jhash(cpumask_bits(...), ...)`, and unbound `rescuer->task` must be bound to `unbound_effective_cpumask(wq)`. [F73, F75, F123]

## Synchronization

- Any path setting `WORK_STRUCT_PENDING_BIT` and calling `__queue_work()` / `__queue_delayed_work()`, or clearing `PENDING` in `__cancel_work()` / `enable_work()`, must keep local IRQs disabled while the item is `PENDING` and off-queue (`delayed_work` timers must use `TIMER_IRQSAFE`). In `set_work_pool_and_clear_pending()`, `smp_wmb()` must precede `set_work_data()` and `smp_mb()` must follow it. [F18, F137, F138]
- `get_work_pool()` and `for_each_pool()` require `rcu_read_lock()` or `wq_pool_mutex`; `for_each_pwq()` requires `rcu_read_lock()` or `wq->mutex`. After locking `pool->lock` on `get_work_pool(work)`, callers (`try_to_grab_pending()`, `start_flush_work()`) must re-read `get_work_pwq(work)` and verify `pwq->pool == pool` (or `find_worker_executing_work(pool, work)`). In `__queue_work()`, if `!pwq->refcnt` under `pool->lock` on a `WQ_UNBOUND` queue, unlock `pool->lock` and `goto retry`. [F23, F155, F158]
- `wq_node_nr_active->lock` (`nna->lock`) nests inside `pool->lock`: in `node_activate_pending_pwq()`, `locked_pool` tracks the held pool lock across `retry:` jumps (dropping `nna->lock` before `raw_spin_lock(&locked_pool->lock)` if trylock fails and re-reading `nna->pending_pwqs`), and `out_unlock:` must restore `caller_pool->lock` when `locked_pool != caller_pool`. In `flush_workqueue_prep_pwqs()`, pool switches or exits from `for_each_pwq()` must unlock `current_pool->lock`. [F21, F104, F105]
- In `pwq_tryinc_nr_active()`, when lockless `tryinc_node_nr_active(nna)` fails, queuing `pwq->pending_node` on `nna->pending_pwqs` under `nna->lock` must be followed by `smp_mb()` and a retry of `tryinc_node_nr_active(nna)`. `wq_adjust_max_active()` and `unplug_oldest_pwq()` must pass `fill = true` to `pwq_activate_first_inactive()`. [F20, F110]
- `wq_worker_sleeping()` must check `worker->flags & WORKER_NOT_RUNNING` before reading `worker->pool` (detached rescuers have `worker->pool == NULL`), return early if `READ_ONCE(worker->sleeping)` is non-zero, and recheck `WORKER_NOT_RUNNING` after locking `pool->lock`. `wq_worker_tick()` must skip setting `WORKER_CPU_INTENSIVE` when `READ_ONCE(worker->sleeping)` is non-zero. [F49, F147, F170]
- `POOL_DISASSOCIATED` and `PF_WQ_WORKER` must only be modified under `wq_pool_attach_mutex` (which also serializes `worker_attach_to_pool()`, `worker_detach_from_pool()`, `idle_cull_fn()`, and `wq_worker_comm()`, where `pool->lock` is also taken). Updates to `wq_unbound_cpumask` must hold both `wq_pool_mutex` and `wq_pool_attach_mutex`. [F33, F149, F169]
- `wq_worker_running()` must disable preemption around checking `!(worker->flags & WORKER_NOT_RUNNING)` and incrementing `pool->nr_running`, and `unbind_workers(cpu)` in `workqueue_offline_cpu(cpu)` must run on `cpu` itself. In `rebind_workers()`, worker CPU affinity must be restored before locking `pool->lock` to clear `POOL_DISASSOCIATED`, and `WORKER_UNBOUND` must be replaced with `WORKER_REBOUND` via `WRITE_ONCE(worker->flags, ...)`. `apply_wqattrs_lock()`, `__alloc_workqueue()`, and `apply_workqueue_attrs()` must not acquire `cpus_read_lock()` internally (it nests outside `wq_pool_mutex`). [F32, F146, F153]
- In `bh_worker()`, exiting on restart/jiffies limits must call `kick_pool(pool)` after `worker_enter_idle(worker)`. `kick_bh_pool()` for `pool->cpu != smp_processor_id()` when `!(pool->flags & POOL_BH_DRAINING)` must use `irq_work_queue_on(bh_pool_irq_work(pool), pool->cpu)`. `drain_dead_softirq_workfn()` must set `POOL_BH_DRAINING` (suppressing remote kicks and `pwq->wq->lockdep_map` in `process_one_work()`) and re-test `need_more_worker(pool)`. [F107, F140, F141]
- In `__flush_work()`, reading `*work_data_bits(work)` outside `pool->lock` after `start_flush_work()` is only valid when `from_cancel` is true. For `WORK_OFFQ_BH` items, `__cancel_work_sync()` checks `WARN_ON_ONCE(in_hardirq())` instead of `might_sleep()`, and `__flush_work()` busy-waits with `try_wait_for_completion(&barr.done)` and `workqueue_callback_cancel_wait_running()`. [F25, F143]
- `touch_wq_lockdep_map()` and `touch_work_lockdep_map()` must wrap lockdep calls in `local_bh_disable()`/`local_bh_enable()` for `WQ_BH` queues, and `insert_wq_barrier()` must use separate lockdep keys (`bh_key` vs `thr_key`). In `start_flush_work()`, `touch_work_lockdep_map()` runs unconditionally, while `check_flush_dependency()` and `touch_wq_lockdep_map()` are skipped when `from_cancel` is true. Any `printk` under `pool->lock` must use `printk_deferred*()`, and `pwq->stats[]` updates require `pool->lock`. [F26, F144, F145, F154, F171]

## Object Lifetimes

- Because a callback may free its own `struct work_struct`, `process_one_work()` must copy `work->lockdep_map` to the stack and call `set_work_pool_and_clear_pending()` before `worker->current_func(work)`, never dereferencing `work` afterwards. `try_to_grab_pending()` and `process_one_work()` must snapshot `work_data` before overwriting `work->data` and call `pwq_dec_nr_in_flight(pwq, work_data)` as the final step under `pool->lock`. [F8, F15]
- `put_pwq()` runs under `pwq->pool->lock` and queues `pwq->release_work` onto `pwq_release_worker` when `--pwq->refcnt` reaches zero. In `pwq_release_workfn()`, `pwq->wq->mutex` and `pwq->wq->pwqs` must only be accessed when `!list_empty(&pwq->pwqs_node)`. [F4, F151]
- Queuing `pwq->mayday_node` on `wq->maydays` under `wq_mayday_lock` (inside `pool->lock`) requires `get_pwq(pwq)`. `rescuer_thread()` must sample `kthread_should_stop()` before locking `wq_mayday_lock`, drain all `wq->maydays` entries before exiting, and call `put_pwq_unlocked(pwq)` only after `worker_detach_from_pool(rescuer)`. [F1, F39, F108]
- `worker->pool` must only be cleared to `NULL` in the worker's own thread (`worker_detach_from_pool()` or `worker_thread()` after `set_pf_worker(false)`), never in `detach_worker()`. `set_worker_dying()` must call `get_task_struct(worker->task)` under `pool->lock` for `kthread_stop_put()` in `reap_dying_workers()`, and `worker_thread()` must not touch `worker->entry` after dropping `pool->lock` on `WORKER_DIE`. [F2, F3, F28]
- In `__alloc_workqueue()`, if `alloc_and_link_pwqs(wq)` fails on `WQ_UNBOUND`, `kthread_flush_worker(pwq_release_worker)` must run before freeing `wq`; failures after `alloc_and_link_pwqs(wq)` must call `destroy_workqueue(wq)`. In `destroy_workqueue(wq)`, `workqueue_sysfs_unregister(wq)` and `drain_workqueue(wq)` run first; `wq->rescuer` is cleared under `wq_mayday_lock` before `kthread_stop()` and `kfree(rescuer)`; `wq->list` is removed under `wq_pool_mutex`; and `cpu_pwq`/`dfl_pwq` references are dropped under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F5, F13, F36, F133, F136, F156]
- In `put_unbound_pool()`, when `--pool->refcnt` reaches zero under `wq_pool_mutex`, remove `pool` from `worker_pool_idr` and `unbound_pool_hash`, wait on `manager_wait` for `!(pool->flags & POOL_MANAGER_ACTIVE)` before locking `wq_pool_attach_mutex` and `pool->lock` to claim `POOL_MANAGER_ACTIVE` and cull workers, and synchronously stop `idle_timer`, `idle_cull_work`, and `mayday_timer` before `call_rcu()`. [F37, F152]
- `apply_wqattrs_commit()` swaps `ctx->dfl_pwq` and `ctx->pwq_tbl[]` in-place with displaced old PWQs, so callers of `apply_wqattrs_prepare()` must always call `apply_wqattrs_cleanup(ctx)`. `__cancel_work_sync()` passes `WORK_CANCEL_DISABLE` to `__cancel_work()`, so when `!(cflags & WORK_CANCEL_DISABLE)` it must call `enable_work(work)` after `__flush_work()`. [F6, F11]

## API Contracts

- `try_to_grab_pending()` returns `1` (stole pending), `0` (claimed idle), or `-EAGAIN` (busy); on `>= 0` it leaves local IRQs disabled in `*irq_flags` and `WORK_STRUCT_PENDING_BIT` set, whereas on `-EAGAIN` it restores `*irq_flags` itself. Canceling or disabling a `struct delayed_work` must use `cancel_delayed_work*()` or `disable_delayed_work*()` (`WORK_CANCEL_DELAYED`). [F42, F59]
- Bitops (`test_bit`, `__set_bit`, `__clear_bit`) on `work_data_bits(work)` require bit indices (`WORK_STRUCT_*_BIT`), whereas bitwise mask operations require shifted masks (`WORK_STRUCT_*`). `unbound_pwq*()` and `install_unbound_pwq()` take `@cpu < 0` (`-1`) for `dfl_pwq`, whereas `queue_work_on()`, `queue_delayed_work_on()`, `mod_delayed_work_on()`, and `workqueue_congested()` require `WORK_CPU_UNBOUND`. [F54, F55]
- `kthread_data(task)` / `kthread_probe_data(task)` must only be cast to `struct worker *` after verifying `task->flags & PF_WQ_WORKER` (and `in_task()` in `current_wq_worker()`). Worklist walkers must check `work->func == wq_barrier_func` before `container_of(work, struct wq_barrier, work)`. [F127, F130, F139]

## Error Handling

- `apply_wqattrs_prepare()` returns an `ERR_PTR` (checked with `IS_ERR()`), unlike `alloc_workqueue*()`, `get_unbound_pool()`, and `alloc_unbound_pwq()` which return `NULL`. `init_worker_pool()` must initialize `id = -1`, `cpu = -1`, `refcnt = 1`, locks, lists, timers, `idle_cull_work`, and `worker_ida` before `alloc_workqueue_attrs()` so `put_unbound_pool()` can safely clean up. [F43, F52]
- `workqueue_unbound_exclude_cpumask()`, `workqueue_set_unbound_cpumask()`, and `workqueue_apply_unbound_cpumask()` must only update `wq_isolated_cpumask`, `wq_requested_unbound_cpumask`, and `wq_unbound_cpumask` after `workqueue_apply_unbound_cpumask()` succeeds for all unbound workqueues. [F74]

## Known Pitfalls

- Pass `&n` as `@nextp` when calling `assign_work()` or `move_linked_works()` inside `list_for_each_entry_safe(work, n, &pool->worklist, entry)`, and use `while ((work = list_first_entry_or_null(...)))` in `process_scheduled_works()` because `process_one_work()` drops `pool->lock`. [F63, F102]
- When `worker` is non-NULL in `start_flush_work()` and `insert_wq_barrier()` (`target` is executing), read `pwq`, `work_color`, and the list head from `worker->current_pwq`, `worker->current_color`, and `worker->scheduled.next` rather than `target`. In `__flush_workqueue()`, check `if (list_empty(&wq->flusher_queue)) break;` before dereferencing `next` as `wq->first_flusher`. [F103, F111]
- In `__queue_work()`, save `cpu` in `req_cpu` before `retry:` and check `req_cpu == WORK_CPU_UNBOUND`. In `apply_wqattrs_prepare()`, restore `new_attrs` from `attrs` (masked with `cpu_possible_mask`) after `wqattrs_actualize_cpumask()` and `wq_calc_pod_cpumask()` mutate it before setting `ctx->attrs = new_attrs`. [F76, F106]
- Check `cpu >= 0` before passing a `-1` sentinel (`off_cpu`, `pool->cpu`, `unbound_pwq` `cpu`) to `cpumask_test_cpu()`, `cpu_to_node()`, or `per_cpu_ptr()`, and intersect with `cpu_online_mask` and verify `< nr_cpu_ids` when selecting a CPU via `cpumask_any_and*()` or `cpumask_next_and_wrap()`. [F84, F85]
- Check `!list_empty(&pool->worklist)` before computing stall duration from `pool->watchdog_ts`. `print_worker_info()` runs unsynchronized and must use `kthread_probe_data(task)`, zero-initialize `name`/`desc`, and read via `copy_from_kernel_nofault()` with `sizeof(...) - 1`. [F69, F90, F159]

## Other

- `wq_watchdog_touch()` must be marked `notrace` because it is called from `touch_softlockup_watchdog()` on stopped CPUs during `stop_machine()`. [F160]
