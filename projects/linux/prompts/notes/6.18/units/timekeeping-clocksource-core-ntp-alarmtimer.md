# TIMEKEEPING, CLOCKSOURCE CORE, NTP, ALARMTIMER

This unit implements the Linux kernel's core timekeeping infrastructure, hardware clocksource registration and watchdog monitoring, the NTP/PPS clock discipline state machine, and RTC-backed alarm timers. It maintains system and POSIX auxiliary clocks, provides lockless seqcount- and latch-protected time readout APIs, and handles time-related syscalls, conversions, and suspend/resume time injection.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct tk_data`, `seqcount_raw_spinlock_t seq` must immediately precede `struct timekeeper timekeeper`, and `struct tk_read_base tkr_mono` (56 bytes on 64-bit) must remain the first member of `struct timekeeper` so `seq` and `tkr_mono` share a 64-byte cacheline. [F132]
- In `struct timekeeper`, `{offs_tai, offs_aux}` and `{monotonic_to_boot, monotonic_to_aux}` are discriminated by `tk->id`: auxiliary timekeepers (`tk_is_aux(tk)`) must only access `offs_aux`/`monotonic_to_aux` and reject `ADJ_TAI`, `STA_INS`, `STA_DEL`, `STA_PPSFREQ`, and `STA_PPSTIME` in `timekeeping_validate_timex()`. [F38]
- Coupled `struct timekeeper` offsets must stay synchronized via their helpers: `tk->wall_to_monotonic` via `tk_set_wall_to_mono()` (updates `wall_to_monotonic`, `offs_real`, `offs_tai`), `tk->tai_offset` via `__timekeeping_set_tai_offset()` (updates `offs_tai`), `tk->offs_boot` via `tk_update_sleep_time()` (updates `monotonic_to_boot`), and `tk->offs_aux` via `tk_update_aux_offs()` (updates `monotonic_to_aux`). [F51, F52, F56]
- In `tk_update_ktime_data()`, `tk->tkr_mono.base` and `tk->tkr_raw.base` must be computed from whole-second fields (`tk->xtime_sec`, `tk->wall_to_monotonic`, `tk->raw_sec`) without adding shifted `xtime_nsec`, whereas `tk->ktime_sec` must add `tk->tkr_mono.xtime_nsec >> tk->tkr_mono.shift`. `tk->coarse_nsec` must be updated via `tk_update_coarse_nsecs()` only when setting/adding/forwarding time or when `__timekeeping_advance()` accumulated cycles (`orig_offset != offset`), never after `TK_ADV_FREQ` with `orig_offset == offset`. [F53, F54]
- When `tk_setup_internals()` switches from a non-NULL `old_clock` to `clock`, it must shift `tk->tkr_mono.xtime_nsec` and `tk->tkr_raw.xtime_nsec` by `clock->shift - old_clock->shift` before overwriting `shift`, and clamp `tk->cycle_interval` to at least `1`. [F57, F118]
- When `timekeeping_apply_adjustment()` changes `tk->tkr_mono.mult` by `mult_adj`, it must adjust `tk->xtime_interval` by `tk->cycle_interval * mult_adj` and subtract `offset * mult_adj` from `tk->tkr_mono.xtime_nsec`. If `xtime_nsec` underflows in `timekeeping_adjust()`, it must set `tk->skip_second_overflow = 1`, which `accumulate_nsecs_to_secs()` must clear and `continue` before `second_overflow()`. [F58, F98]
- In `timekeeping_cycles_to_ns()`, when `delta = (cycles - tkr->cycle_last) & mask` exceeds `tkr->clock->max_cycles`, a delta with `delta & ~(mask >> 1)` set must be treated as backward counter motion (returning `tkr->xtime_nsec >> tkr->shift`), while a positive delta above `max_cycles` must use `delta_to_ns_safe()`. [F90]
- During `timekeeping_suspend()`, after setting `timekeeping_suspended = 1` and `timekeeping_update_from_shadow(&tk_core, 0)`, `halt_fast_timekeeper()` must snapshot `cycles_at_suspend` and switch `tk_fast_mono`/`tk_fast_raw` to `&dummy_clock` before `clocksource_suspend()`. `suspend_timing_needed` is set `true` only in `timekeeping_suspend()` and cleared when sleep time is injected in `timekeeping_resume()` or `timekeeping_inject_sleeptime64()`. [F60, F145]
- Whenever `watchdog` changes in `clocksource_select_watchdog()` or `clocksource_watchdog()` hits `WD_READ_SKIP`, `clocksource_reset_watchdog()` must be called. `clocksource_watchdog()` must sample `watchdog_reset_pending` before iterating `watchdog_list`, re-read it after updating `cs_last`/`wd_last` to skip skew checks if a reset occurred, and decrement it only after the loop finishes. [F66, F94]
- In `ntp_adjtimex()`, `txc->modes & ADJ_ADJTIME` (`ADJ_OFFSET_SINGLESHOT` or `ADJ_OFFSET_SS_READ`, overlapping `ADJ_OFFSET` and `ADJ_NANO`) must take an exclusive branch bypassing `process_adjtimex_modes()`, and sample `ntpdata->time_adjust` into `save_adjust` before overwriting `ntpdata->time_adjust = txc->offset`. [F65, F101]
- In `process_adjtimex_modes()`, `ADJ_TIMECONST` must clamp `txc->constant` to `[0, MAXTC]` both before and after adding `4` for `!(ntpdata->time_status & STA_NANO)`; `ADJ_MAXERROR` and `ADJ_ESTERROR` must clamp to `[0, NTP_PHASE_LIMIT]`. In `ntp_update_offset()`, when `!STA_NANO`, `offset` must be clamped to `[-USEC_PER_SEC, USEC_PER_SEC]` before multiplying by `NSEC_PER_USEC`, then clamped to `[-MAXPHASE, MAXPHASE]`. [F69, F70, F72]
- In `alarmtimer.c`, `alarm_bases[ALARM_NUMTYPE]` only contains `ALARM_REALTIME` and `ALARM_BOOTTIME`; `ALARM_REALTIME_FREEZER` and `ALARM_BOOTTIME_FREEZER` in `freezer_alarmtype` are for tracing only and must never index `alarm_bases[]`. Relative timeouts in `alarm_start_relative()`, `alarm_timer_arm()`, and `alarm_timer_nsleep()` must use `ktime_add_safe()`. [F77, F81]

## Synchronization

- In `timekeeping_update_from_shadow()`, `write_seqcount_begin(&tkd->seq)` (under `tkd->lock`) must precede `update_vsyscall()`, `update_pvclock_gtod()`, `update_fast_timekeeper()`, and `vdso_time_update_aux()`, and `write_seqcount_end(&tkd->seq)` must follow `memcpy(&tkd->timekeeper, tk, sizeof(*tk))`. `pvclock_gtod_chain` is serialized by `tk_core.lock`; its callbacks must not sleep, take non-raw spinlocks, or call getters that spin on `tk_core.seq`. [F151, F159]
- Lockless seqcount-protected timekeeping readers must read the clocksource via `tk_clock_read()`, which uses `READ_ONCE(tkr->clock)`. On `!IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_64BIT)`, reading 64-bit fields (`*offset` in `ktime_mono_to_any()`, `tk->xtime_sec` in `ktime_get_real_seconds()`) requires a `read_seqcount_begin(&tk_core.seq)` retry loop; on 64-bit they rely on `WRITE_ONCE()`. `__ktime_get_real_seconds()` must remain `noinstr` and lockless for NMI/MCE use only. [F152, F154, F155]
- `ktime_get_mono_fast_ns()`, `ktime_get_raw_fast_ns()`, `ktime_get_boot_fast_ns()`, and `ktime_get_tai_fast_ns()` must be marked `notrace`, and `__ktime_get_fast_ns()`, `ktime_get_real_fast_ns()`, and `timekeeping_get_ns()` must remain lockless and NMI-safe. [F153]
- Time setters (`do_settimeofday64()`, `timekeeping_inject_offset()`, `timekeeping_inject_sleeptime64()`, `__do_adjtimex()`, `do_adjtimex()`) must call `add_device_randomness()`, `audit_tk_injoffset()`, `clock_was_set()`, and `ntp_notify_cmos_timer()` outside `tkd->lock`, while `update_wall_time()` must call `clock_was_set_delayed()`. [F156]
- The auxiliary clock lock hierarchy is `aux_clock_mutex` -> `tk_core.lock` -> `aux_tkd->lock`. `aux_clock_enable_store()` must initialize `aux_tkd` under `tk_core.lock` nested outside `aux_tkd->lock` before setting its bit in `aux_timekeepers`, `change_clocksource()` and `update_wall_time()` must drop `tk_core.lock` before taking `aux_tkd->lock`, and lockless readers of `aux_timekeepers` must recheck `shadow_timekeeper.clock_valid` under `aux_tkd->lock`. [F25, F157]
- In `timekeeping_resume()`, `clockevents_resume()` and `clocksource_resume()` must run before acquiring `tk_core.lock` to read `tk_clock_read(&tks->tkr_mono)` and call `clocksource_stop_suspend_timing()`, while `tick_resume()` and `timerfd_resume()` must run after releasing `tk_core.lock`. [F144]
- In `kernel/time/clocksource.c`, `clocksource_mutex` nests outside `watchdog_lock`. Manipulations of `clocksource_list` (`clocksource_enqueue()`, `clocksource_change_rating()`, and `list_del_init(&cs->list)` in `clocksource_unbind()`) must hold both locks so `clocksource_mark_unstable()` can test `!list_empty(&cs->list)` under `watchdog_lock` alone. [F13, F161]
- Clocksource reselection (`clocksource_select()` / `timekeeping_notify()`) calls `stop_machine()` and must not run from a workqueue callback or under `watchdog_lock`; `watchdog_work` must spawn `clocksource_watchdog_kthread` to take `clocksource_mutex`. In `clocksource_watchdog()`, under `watchdog_lock`, `watchdog_timer` must only be re-armed if `!timer_pending(&watchdog_timer)`. [F14, F15]
- `clocksource_verify_percpu()` must acquire `cpus_read_lock()`, `migrate_disable()`, and `preempt_disable()` in that order and release them in reverse order on all paths, calling `clocksource_verify_choose_cpus()` under `migrate_disable()` before entering `preempt_disable()`. [F12, F163]
- In `kernel/time/alarmtimer.c`, `base->lock` must be held across `alarmtimer_enqueue()` and `alarmtimer_dequeue()` (synchronizing `alarm->node`, `ALARMTIMER_STATE_ENQUEUED`, and `alarm->timer`), but must be released in `alarmtimer_fired()` before `alarm->function()` and in `alarm_cancel()` before `hrtimer_cancel_wait_running()`. [F27, F68]

## Object Lifetimes

- `clocksource_unbind()` must return `-EBUSY` if fallback selection cannot replace `cs` as `watchdog` or `curr_clocksource`, reselect `suspend_clocksource` if `clocksource_is_suspend(cs)`, and unlink `cs` with `list_del_init(&cs->list)` so `clocksource_unregister()` sees `list_empty(&cs->list)`. [F8]
- In `change_clocksource()`, `try_module_get(new->owner)` and `new->enable(new)` must succeed before `tk_core.lock` is taken (calling `module_put(new->owner)` if `enable()` fails), and `old->disable(old)` and `module_put(old->owner)` must run only after both `tk_core` and `tk_aux_update_clocksource()` switch to `new`. [F135]
- Stack-allocated `struct alarm` instances in `alarm_timer_nsleep()` and `alarm_timer_nsleep_restart()` must be initialized with `alarm_init_on_stack()` and unconditionally cleaned up with `alarm_cancel()` and `destroy_hrtimer_on_stack(&alarm->timer)` in `alarmtimer_do_nsleep()` before returning. [F129]

## API Contracts

- Timekeeping readers (`__ktime_get_fast_ns()`, `ktime_get_real_fast_ns()`, `ktime_get_snapshot()`) must convert raw cycles via `timekeeping_get_ns(tkr)` or `timekeeping_cycles_to_ns(tkr, cycles)` before adding to `tkr->base` or `tkr->base_real`. Callers of `clocksource_delta()` must pass `cs->max_raw_delta` as `max_delta`, and `clocksource_cyc2ns()` may only be used on relative cycle deltas `< cs->max_cycles` (`cycles_to_nsec_safe()` uses `mul_u64_u32_shr()` otherwise). [F47, F107, F109]
- `ktime_get_aux()` and `ktime_get_aux_ts64()` return `false` without modifying `*kt` or `*ts` when the auxiliary clock is invalid or disabled, so `ktime_get_clock_ts64()` must pre-initialize `ts->tv_sec = -1; ts->tv_nsec = 0`. Auxiliary clock code must translate between `clockid_t` (`CLOCK_AUX..CLOCK_AUX_LAST`), `timekeeper_data[]` indices (`clockid_to_tkid()`), and `aux_timekeepers` bit indices (`0..MAX_AUX_CLOCKS - 1`, adding `TIMEKEEPER_AUX_FIRST` for `timekeeper_data[]`). [F34, F48]
- Callers of `clock_was_set()` must pass `CLOCK_SET_*` bitmask constants (`CLOCK_SET_WALL`, `CLOCK_SET_BOOT`), never `CLOCK_*` IDs. Before dereferencing `cs->base` (`convert_base_to_cs()`, `convert_cs_to_base()`, `timekeeping_clocksource_has_base()`), callers must load `READ_ONCE(cs->base)` and verify `base != NULL` and `base->id == target_id` (and reject `CSID_GENERIC` in `get_device_system_crosststamp()`). [F39, F44]
- In `__do_adjtimex()`, `txc->time.tv_usec` must be multiplied by `1000` when `!(txc->modes & ADJ_NANO)`, and `process_adjtimex_modes()` must update `STA_NANO` before processing `ADJ_TIMECONST` or `ADJ_OFFSET`. [F49]
- `alarmtimer_get_rtcdev()` returns a borrowed pointer to `rtcdev` without incrementing refcounts; callers must not call `put_device()` or `module_put()`. Remaining alarm time must be computed with `ktime_sub(expires, now)`. [F2, F46]

## Error Handling

- Any path under `tkd->lock` that calls `timekeeping_forward_now(&tkd->shadow_timekeeper)` and aborts on an error before `timekeeping_update_from_shadow()` (as in `do_settimeofday64()` and `__timekeeping_inject_offset()`) must call `timekeeping_restore_shadow(tkd)` before returning `-EINVAL`. When enabling an auxiliary clock in `aux_clock_enable()`, `aux_tks` must be zeroed with `memset()` and have `aux_tks->id = aux_tkd->timekeeper.id` restored before `tk_setup_internals()`. [F37, F43, F76]
- Callers of `__do_adjtimex()` and `do_adjtimex()` must check failure with `ret < 0` rather than `ret != 0` because `ntp_adjtimex()` returns non-negative NTP states (`0..5`) that still require post-update notifications and copyout, and callers of `__do_adjtimex()` must zero-initialize `struct adjtimex_result result = { };`. [F30, F41]
- Because `change_clocksource()` runs via `stop_machine()` and always returns `0`, `timekeeping_notify()` must check `tk->tkr_mono.clock == clock` after `stop_machine()` to determine whether the switch succeeded. [F35]
- In `get_device_system_crosststamp()`, `struct system_counterval_t system_counterval = {};` must be zero-initialized before `get_time_fn()`. In `scale64_check_overflow()` and `convert_ns_to_cs()`, `fls64()` checks must verify that multiplication by `mult` or left-shift by `tkr->shift` fits in 64 bits, and `treal >= tk->tkr_mono.base_real` must be checked before subtraction. [F40, F91, F114]
- In `alarm_try_to_cancel()`, `alarmtimer_dequeue()` must only be called when `hrtimer_try_to_cancel()` returns `ret >= 0` (`-1` means the callback is currently running). [F31]

## External Contracts

- `do_sys_settimeofday64()` (and `stime`/`stime32`) must check `security_settime64(tv, tz)` before modifying `sys_tz`, warping the clock, or calling `do_settimeofday64()`. In `timekeeping_validate_timex()`, any non-zero `txc->modes` other than read-only singleshot (`ADJ_ADJTIME | ADJ_OFFSET_SINGLESHOT | ADJ_OFFSET_READONLY`) and any `ADJ_SETOFFSET` require `capable(CAP_SYS_TIME)`, as does `aux_clock_enable_store()`. [F178, F179]
- In `process_adj_status()`, `STA_PLL` transitions between `ntpdata->time_status` and `txc->status` must be checked before updating `ntpdata->time_status`, and read-only bits in `STA_RONLY` (including `STA_NANO`) must be preserved via `(ntpdata->time_status & STA_RONLY) | (txc->status & ~STA_RONLY)`. [F73, F102]
- `get_old_timex32()` and `put_old_timex32()` must `memset()` the destination struct before field assignment; `get_timespec64()` must mask `kts.tv_nsec &= 0xFFFFFFFFUL` when `in_compat_syscall()`; and `get_old_timespec32()`/`put_old_timespec32()` must check `COMPAT_USE_64BIT_TIME` to copy `struct timespec64` directly on x32-style ABIs. [F42, F86]
- In `alarm_timer_nsleep()`, `TIMER_ABSTIME` expirations must be converted via `timens_ktime_to_host(which_clock, exp)`, and interrupted sleeps must return `-ERESTARTNOHAND` for `TIMER_ABSTIME` or use `set_restart_fn(restart, alarm_timer_nsleep_restart)` for relative sleeps. `get_boottime_timespec()` (`alarm_bases[ALARM_BOOTTIME].get_timespec`) must apply `timens_add_boottime(tp)`. [F175, F176]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `timekeeping_forward_now()`, `delta` must be consumed in chunks bounded by `tk->tkr_mono.clock->max_cycles` with `tk_normalize_xtime()` called on each iteration before multiplying `incr` by `mult`. [F108]
- Integer promotion and signedness pitfalls: cast `NSEC_PER_SEC` and `ts->tv_nsec` to `u64` before left-shifting by `shift` in `timekeeping.c`; use a 64-bit factor (`u64`/`ULL`) when multiplying seconds by `NSEC_PER_SEC` or `NSEC_PER_USEC` in `ntp.c`; cast `long` phase/frequency values to `s64` before shifting by `NTP_SCALE_SHIFT` in `hardpps_update_freq()`/`hardpps_update_phase()`; use `shift_right()` instead of `>>` on signed NTP values; and use signed division helpers (`div_s64()`, `div_s64_rem()`, `div64_long()`) on signed offsets, skews, or seconds. [F110, F111, F112, F117, F119]
- In `get_device_system_crosststamp()`, `timestamp_in_interval(start, end, ts)` must use inclusive bounds `[start, end]`, and when `system_counterval.cycles` is outside `[interval_start, now]`, `timekeeping_cycles_to_ns()` must receive the clamped `cycles` variable while preserving `system_counterval.cycles` for history delta calculation. [F79, F93]
- `ntp_update_offset()` and `process_adj_status()` compute `ntpdata - tk_ntp_data` to index `timekeeper_data[]`, so `ntpdata` must always point into `tk_ntp_data[]`. [F126]
