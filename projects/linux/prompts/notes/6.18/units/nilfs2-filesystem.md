# NILFS2 FILESYSTEM

This unit implements the core superblock, mount lifecycle, segment usage management (`sufile`), log-structured segment constructor (`segctord`), sysfs hierarchy, and UAPI/on-disk format definitions for NILFS2. It coordinates transaction locking, dirty buffer collection, checkpoint and super-root creation, garbage collection (`nilfs_clean_segments`), and multi-mount supervision across the current tree and read-only snapshots via `struct the_nilfs`.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Superblock buffer heads `ns_sbh[0..1]` and pointers `ns_sbp[0..1]` must be swapped, fallen back, replaced, or released in lockstep (`nilfs_swap_super_block()`, `nilfs_fall_back_super_block()`, `nilfs_release_super_block()`, `nilfs_move_2nd_super()`), and because `ns_sbh[1]` and `ns_sbp[1]` can be `NULL`, every access to them requires a non-NULL check. [F43, F57]
- `nilfs->ns_writer` is `NULL` on read-only mounts and after `nilfs_detach_log_writer()`, and `sci->sc_task` is `NULL` before `kthread_create()` succeeds and after `nilfs_segctor_thread()` exits; callers must check `ns_writer` and `sc_task` before dereferencing them or calling `kthread_stop()`. [F44]
- Because a `super_block` is shared between the current checkpoint (`s->s_root`) and snapshot mounts (`ctx->cno != 0`), `nilfs_get_tree()` must never overwrite `s->s_root` with a snapshot dentry and must only call `nilfs_reconfigure()` on an existing `s->s_root` when `!nilfs_tree_is_busy(s->s_root)`. [F69]
- Every mutation to a segment usage entry's clean/dirty state in `fs/nilfs2/sufile.c` must update both in-memory `NILFS_SUI(sufile)->ncleansegs` and on-disk `sh_ncleansegs` (plus `sh_ndirtysegs` when dirty state changes) under `NILFS_MDT(sufile)->mi_sem`, and mark both buffers and `sufile` dirty. [F53]
- Across iterations of the `do ... while` loop in `nilfs_segctor_do_construct()`, `sci->sc_stage.flags` must only clear `NILFS_CF_HISTORY_MASK` and preserve `NILFS_CF_NODE` so a file interrupted by `-E2BIG` after data collection resumes at node collection; also, when `nilfs->ns_blocksize_bits != PAGE_SHIFT`, `nilfs_segctor_wait(sci)` must run after each `nilfs_segctor_write()` iteration even before `NILFS_ST_DONE`. [F90, F149]
- In `nilfs_segctor_complete_write()`, completing a super-root segment (`update_sr`) must reset `nilfs->ns_flushed_device = 0`, call `nilfs_set_last_segment()` with `nilfs->ns_cno++`, clear `NILFS_SC_HAVE_DELTA` and `NILFS_SC_DIRTY`, set `NILFS_SC_SUPER_ROOT`, and call `nilfs_segctor_clear_metadata_dirty(sci)`. [F70]
- When `nilfs_segctor_notify()` finishes a non-`SC_LSEG_SR` flush (`SC_FLUSH_FILE` or `SC_FLUSH_DAT`) with `NILFS_SEGCTOR_COMMIT` set, `sci->sc_task` alive, and `time_before(jiffies, sci->sc_timer.expires)`, it must re-add `sci->sc_timer`; in `nilfs_segctor_flush_mode()`, `SC_FLUSH_FILE` / `SC_FLUSH_DAT` apply only when `!(sci->sc_flush_request & ~FLUSH_*_BIT)`, falling back to `SC_LSEG_SR` if multiple bits are set. [F68, F95]
- Variable on-disk strides and offsets: address `struct nilfs_super_root` inodes via `NILFS_SR_*_OFFSET(inode_size)` and `NILFS_SR_BYTES(inode_size)`; advance `sufile` entries by `NILFS_MDT(sufile)->mi_entry_size` (validated in `[NILFS_MIN_SEGMENT_USAGE_SIZE, sb->s_blocksize]`) after adding `mi_first_entry_offset` to 0-based `segnum`; and use `nilfs_get_segment_range()` / `nilfs_get_segment_start_blocknr()` because segment 0 starts at `ns_first_data_block`. [F75, F76, F77, F78]
- In `fs/nilfs2/segment.c`, the `binfo_size` reserved by `nilfs_segctor_add_file_block()` must match the size consumed in `nilfs_segctor_update_payload_blocknr()` (`nilfs_sc_file_ops` vs `nilfs_sc_dat_ops`). [F84]
- Superblock validation: `nilfs_valid_sb()` must verify `s_bytes` is in `[offsetof(..., s_sum) + 4, BLOCK_SIZE]`; `nilfs_get_blocksize()` and `nilfs_sb2_bad_offset()` must verify `s_log_block_size <= ilog2(NILFS_MAX_BLOCK_SIZE) - BLOCK_SIZE_BITS`; `nilfs_sb2_bad_offset()` and `nilfs_store_disk_layout()` must verify `blocks_per_segment >= NILFS_SEG_MIN_BLOCKS` and `s_nsegments <= nilfs_max_segment_count(nilfs)`; and `NILFS_SB2_OFFSET_BYTES()` callers must verify byte size `>= 4096`. [F71, F72, F73, F74, F108]
- Arithmetic and wrap-around: cast `unsigned long` segment counts to `u64`/`sector_t` before multiplying by `ns_blocks_per_segment`; use `div64_ul`, `do_div`, or `sector_div` on 64-bit quantities; clamp `end_block` via `max_blocks - start_block < len` in `nilfs_sufile_trim_fs()`; compare `sc_seq_*` via `nilfs_cnt32_ge(a, b)`; and compare `jiffies` via `time_before` / `time_after_eq`. [F83, F102, F110, F112, F113]
- Updates to `nilfs->ns_nsegments` must go through `nilfs_set_nsegments()` so `ns_nrsvsegs` is updated via `nilfs_nrsvsegs()` using `DIV_ROUND_UP`. [F56, F111]

## Synchronization

- Lock ordering: `nilfs->ns_segctor_sem` nests outside `nilfs->ns_sem` (never acquire `ns_segctor_sem` or call `nilfs_construct_segment()` under `ns_sem`); `ns_sem` must never be held while acquiring any MDT `mi_sem` (e.g., in `nilfs_count_free_blocks()`) or while calling `sb_min_blocksize()` / `sb_set_blocksize()` in `init_nilfs()`. [F29, F128, F131]
- Callers of `nilfs_prepare_super()`, `nilfs_commit_super()`, `nilfs_set_log_cursor()`, `nilfs_cleanup_super()`, `nilfs_setup_super()`, and `nilfs_move_2nd_super()` must hold `nilfs->ns_sem` for write, and runtime readers of `nilfs->ns_sbp` (sysfs show handlers and `nilfs_reconfigure()`) must hold `ns_sem` for read. [F28]
- `nilfs_transaction_begin()`, `nilfs_transaction_commit()`, and `nilfs_transaction_abort()` acquire/release `sb_start_intwrite(sb)` and `ns_segctor_sem` only on the outermost call (`ti->ti_count == 0`), and `nilfs_construct_segment(sb)` must never be called before `nilfs_transaction_commit()` releases `ns_segctor_sem` and restores `current->journal_info`. [F10, F130]
- `nilfs_segctor_destroy()` requires its caller to hold `ns_segctor_sem` for write, drops it with `up_write(&nilfs->ns_segctor_sem)` to stop `sci->sc_task`, call `nilfs_segctor_wakeup(sci, 0, true)` (before `flush_work(&sci->sc_iput_work)`), and flush segments, and must re-acquire `down_write(&nilfs->ns_segctor_sem)` before freeing `sci`. [F4, F22]
- `sci->sc_task` and `sci->sc_timer` must be initialized before `wake_up_process(sci->sc_task)`; `sc_timer` must only be armed or deleted after checking `sci->sc_task != NULL` under `sci->sc_state_lock`; `nilfs_segctor_thread()` must clear `sc_task` and call `timer_shutdown_sync(&sci->sc_timer)` under `sc_state_lock`; and `nilfs_construction_timeout()` must never acquire `sc_state_lock`. [F19, F20, F137]
- In `nilfs_segctor_sync()`, incrementing `sci->sc_seq_request` and queuing `wait_req.wq` onto `sci->sc_wait_request` must occur atomically under `sci->sc_state_lock`, and `set_current_state(TASK_INTERRUPTIBLE)` must precede checking `sci->sc_task` and `wait_req.done`. [F21]
- Drop `nilfs->ns_inode_lock` before sleeping calls like `iput()` or `nilfs_ifile_get_inode_block()`; in `nilfs_segctor_collect_dirty_files()`, after dropping `ns_inode_lock` to fetch `ibh`, recheck `ii->i_bh` upon relocking (`brelse(ibh)` if already set) and restart traversal via `goto retry`. [F32, F136]
- `nilfs_put_root()` must use `refcount_dec_and_lock(&root->count, &nilfs->ns_cptree_lock)` before erasing `root->rb_node`, and `nilfs_find_or_create_root()` must re-search `ns_cptree` under `ns_cptree_lock` after allocating `new` outside the lock. [F25, F26]
- In `nilfs_prepare_write_logs()`, call `folio_wait_writeback(bd_folio)` under `folio_lock(bd_folio)` before starting writeback on summary/super-root folios, and call `nilfs_begin_folio_io()` on payload folios (`fs_folio`) before `nilfs_add_checksums_on_logs()`; in `nilfs_lookup_dirty_data_buffers()`, hold `folio_lock(folio)` across the entire `folio_buffers(folio)` loop. [F27, F144, F147]
- `nilfs_sufile_mark_dirty()` must hold `NILFS_MDT(sufile)->mi_sem` while setting `NILFS_SEGMENT_USAGE_DIRTY` on `su`; shrinking in `nilfs_sufile_resize()` must update `sui->allocmax`/`allocmin` under `mi_sem`, whereas expansion in `nilfs_resize_fs()` must delay `nilfs_sufile_set_alloc_range()` until after `nilfs_move_2nd_super()` and `nilfs_commit_super()`. [F54, F55]
- In `nilfs_relax_pressure_in_lock()`, recheck both `sci->sc_flush_request` and `NILFS_SC_PRIOR_FLUSH` after acquiring `down_write(&nilfs->ns_segctor_sem)`; order `nilfs->ns_flushed_device = 1` before device flush with `smp_wmb()`; and guard runtime accesses to `ns_last_pseg`, `ns_last_seq`, `ns_last_cno`, `ns_prot_seq`, and `ns_prev_seq` with `ns_last_segment_lock`. [F33, F36, F37]

## Object Lifetimes

- Call `nilfs_sysfs_create_device_group()` only after `nilfs_load_super_root()` initializes `ns_dat`, `ns_cpfile`, and `ns_sufile`, and call `nilfs_sysfs_delete_device_group()` (which requires both `kobject_del()` and `kobject_put(&nilfs->ns_dev_kobj)` before freeing `ns_dev_subgroups`) before `iput()` on those inodes; any failed `kobject_init_and_add()` in `sysfs.c` must still call `kobject_put()`. [F1, F2]
- Any error in `init_nilfs()` after `nilfs_load_super_block()` and before `set_nilfs_init(nilfs)` must call `nilfs_release_super_block(nilfs)` because `destroy_nilfs()` only releases `ns_sbh[0..1]` when `THE_NILFS_INIT` is set; in `nilfs_destroy_cachep()`, call `rcu_barrier()` before `kmem_cache_destroy(nilfs_inode_cachep)`. [F15, F141]
- In `nilfs_attach_log_writer()`, check `IS_ERR(t)` before assigning `sci->sc_task = t`, and once `nilfs_segctor_new()` sets `nilfs->ns_writer`, clean up any error via `nilfs_detach_log_writer(sb)` rather than `kfree(sci)`; in `nilfs_detach_log_writer()`, wrap `nilfs_dispose_list()` with `set_nilfs_purging()` / `clear_nilfs_purging()` and return early in `__nilfs_mark_inode_dirty()` when `nilfs_purging(nilfs)` is true. [F3, F24, F46]
- In `nilfs_segctor_drop_written_files()` and force-disposal in `nilfs_dispose_list()`, `brelse(ii->i_bh)` and clear `ii->i_bh = NULL`, and defer `iput()` via `sci->sc_iput_queue` and `sci->sc_iput_work` whenever `!ii->vfs_inode.i_nlink` or `!(sci->sc_super->s_flags & SB_ACTIVE)`. [F17]
- Drop the `nilfs_attach_checkpoint()` reference via `nilfs_put_root()` in `nilfs_fill_super()` and `nilfs_attach_snapshot()` after dentry/log-writer setup; in `nilfs_get_root_dentry()`, `d_make_root()` and `d_obtain_root()` consume `inode` on both success and error, whereas `d_find_alias()` hit requires explicit `iput(inode)`; in `nilfs_get_tree()`, if `nilfs_reconfigure(fc)` fails on a temporary `fc->root = dget(s->s_root)`, call `dput(fc->root)` and set `fc->root = NULL`. [F12, F13, F14]
- In `nilfs_clean_segments()`, call `nilfs_mdt_restore_from_shadow_map(nilfs->ns_dat)` if `nilfs_ioctl_prepare_clean_segments()` fails, and clear `sci->sc_freesegs = NULL`, `sci->sc_nfreesegs = 0`, and `nilfs_mdt_clear_shadow_map(nilfs->ns_dat)` before `nilfs_transaction_unlock(sb)`; any function installing a stack `nilfs_transaction_info` into `current->journal_info` must restore `ti->ti_save` via commit, abort, or unlock on every exit path. [F11, F123]
- Pin `bh_org` with `get_bh()` before `nilfs_bmap_assign()` and `brelse(bh_org)` after `nilfs_list_replace_buffer()` in `nilfs_segctor_update_payload_blocknr()`; on error in `nilfs_segctor_extend_segments()`, free the unlinked `segbuf`, call `nilfs_sufile_free()` for all segments on `list`, and call `nilfs_destroy_logs(&list)`. [F9, F16]

## API Contracts

- `nilfs_segctor_apply_buffers()` unlinks and calls `brelse()` on every `buffer_head` in `listp`, so `nilfs_segctor_scan_file()` must drain `node_buffers` via `nilfs_segctor_apply_buffers(sci, inode, &node_buffers, NULL)` if applying `data_buffers` fails. [F8]
- `nilfs_attach_checkpoint()` must key `ns_cptree` with `NILFS_CPTREE_CURRENT_CNO` (`0`) when `curr_mnt` is true while passing the 1-based on-disk `cno` to `nilfs_ifile_read()`; `nilfs_segctor_do_flush(sci, bn)` takes a bit number `bn` whereas `FLUSH_FILE_BIT` and `FLUSH_DAT_BIT` are pre-shifted bitmasks. [F50, F51]
- In `nilfs_sufile_set_segment_usage()`, `modtime == 0` is reserved for `nilfs_cancel_segusage()` (updating `su_nblocks` without overwriting `su_lastmod` or warning on `nilfs_segment_usage_error(su)`); in `nilfs_sufile_get_suinfo()` and `nilfs_sufile_set_suinfo()`, scale element pointers and hole `memset` by runtime `sisz` and `supsz` bytes rather than `sizeof`. [F52, F116]
- Mutate `sci->sc_stage.scnt` only via `nilfs_sc_cstage_inc()` / `nilfs_sc_cstage_set()` and keep `NILFS_ST_*` in sync with `show_collection_stage`; use `struct kobj_attribute *` for `nilfs_feature_attr` callbacks; and never pass a trailing `\n` to `nilfs_msg()` / `nilfs_error()` family helpers. [F67, F120, F125]

## Error Handling

- In `nilfs_segctor_do_construct()`, any error inside the construction loop must jump to `failed` or `failed_to_write` to redirty `sc_dirty_files` (when `mode == SC_LSEG_SR` and stage `>= NILFS_ST_IFILE`) and `sc_gc_inodes` (if `nilfs_doing_gc()`) and call `nilfs_segctor_abort_construction()`. [F5]
- `nilfs_segctor_collect()` must intercept `-E2BIG` from `nilfs_segctor_feed_segment()`, and when retrying at or after `NILFS_ST_CPFILE` in `SC_LSEG_SR` mode, must call `nilfs_clear_logs()`, call `nilfs_sufile_cancel_freev()` if `NILFS_CF_SUFREED` is set, extend segments, and restore `sci->sc_stage = prev_stage`. [F42, F63]
- `nilfs_sufile_updatev()` must set `*ndone` on every return path, and `nilfs_free_segments()` must roll back partial frees on error via `nilfs_sufile_cancel_freev(nilfs->ns_sufile, segnumv, ndone, NULL)`; when `load_nilfs()` falls back from `sbp[0]` to `sbp[1]`, it must copy `sbp[1]` to `sbp[0]`, refresh `ns_crc_seed` and `ns_sbwtime`, verify `blocksize`, call `nilfs_store_log_cursor()`, and clear `NILFS_VALID_FS`. [F7, F49, F66]
- Translate `-ENOENT` from `sufile` reads with `create == 0` to `-EIO`/`-EINVAL` when a block is required (`nilfs_sufile_get_header_block`, `nilfs_sufile_mark_dirty`, `nilfs_free_segments`), but skip it as a hole in range scans; check `nilfs_iget()` with `IS_ERR()` and `nilfs_iget_locked()` / `nilfs_ilookup()` for `NULL`; and reserve `nilfs_error()` for on-disk metadata corruption. [F39, F41, F152]

## External Contracts

- In `include/uapi/linux/nilfs2_ondisk.h`, place any new `struct nilfs_checkpoint` fields after `cp_ifile_inode`, and use `<asm/byteorder.h>` `__cpu_to_le*` / `__le*_to_cpu` bitwise operations (never `set_bit`/`test_bit` or kernel-only `cpu_to_le*`) in `NILFS_CHECKPOINT_FNS` and `NILFS_SEGMENT_USAGE_FNS`. [F124, F127, F146]

## Known Pitfalls

- Loops over `segbuf->sb_payload_buffers` in `nilfs_segctor_update_payload_blocknr()`, `nilfs_prepare_write_logs()`, `nilfs_abort_logs()`, and `nilfs_segctor_complete_write()` must break when `bh == segbuf->sb_super_root`; finish the final `fs_folio` and `bd_folio` after the outer `segbuf` loop; and guard against repeated `nilfs_begin_folio_io()` / `nilfs_end_folio_io()` calls on split B-tree node folios. [F92, F93, F148]
- On `nilfs_segctor_scan_file()` error in `nilfs_segctor_collect_blocks()`, save `list_entry(ii->i_dirty.prev, ...)` into `gc_inode_ptr` or `dirty_file_ptr` so `list_for_each_entry_continue` re-visits `ii`; in `nilfs_cancel_segusage()` and `nilfs_free_incomplete_logs()`, process `NILFS_FIRST_SEGBUF(logs)` separately before `list_for_each_entry_continue`. [F89, F99]
- In `nilfs_sufile_trim_fs()`, `kunmap_local(kaddr)` before `blkdev_issue_discard()` and recompute `offset = nilfs_sufile_segment_usage_offset(sufile, segnum, su_bh)` when remapping afterward, and advance `segnum += n` before `continue` on `-ENOENT`; in `nilfs_sufile_set_suinfo()`, mask off `BIT(NILFS_SEGMENT_USAGE_ACTIVE)` and sample clean/dirty state before overwriting `su->su_flags`; and cast `-1` to `u64` when passing negative deltas to `nilfs_sufile_mod_counter()`. [F59, F60, F98, F106]
- In `nilfs_move_2nd_super()`, identify `sb2i` by checking `b_blocknr > nilfs->ns_first_data_block` on both `ns_sbh[1]` and `ns_sbh[0]` and `memcpy` before `memset`; in sysfs, recover `the_nilfs` from `kobj->parent` in subgroup `show`/`store`, `nilfs_sysfs_dev_subgroups` from `kobj` in subgroup `release`, and `nilfs_root` from `kobj` in `nilfs_snapshot_attr`; and bound `s_volume_name` (`__kernel_nonstring`) reads to `NILFS_MAX_VOLUME_NAME`. [F86, F100, F121, F122, F126]
