# ALPS PS/2 TOUCHPAD DRIVER

The ALPS PS/2 Touchpad Driver (`alps.c` and `alps.h`) is a `psmouse` protocol subdriver in the Linux input subsystem that detects, initializes, and processes packets for ALPS GlidePoint and DualPoint touchpads across protocol versions V1 through V8. It manages primary touchpad reporting (`dev1`), secondary trackstick input devices (`priv->dev2`), and on-demand registration of pass-through or interleaved bare PS/2 mice (`priv->dev3`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` and `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `priv->f` is zeroed before decoding the current packet, and `priv->decode_fields()` must only write fields for that packet type so re-decoding `priv->multi_data` preserves companion fields. In `alps_decode_ss4_v2()`, `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI` must only write `f->mt[2..3]`, and `SS4_PACKET_ID_TWO` with `SS4_IS_MF_CONTINUE(p)` must only write `f->mt[0..1]`, `f->pressure`, `f->first_mp = 1`, and `f->is_mp = 0` without modifying `f->fingers`. [F20, F28]
- In `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` and `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, when `priv->multi_packet` is non-zero and `f->is_mp` is true, `priv->multi_packet` must remain `1` until after `if (!priv->multi_packet && f->first_mp)`; when `f->is_mp` is false, `priv->multi_packet` must be cleared to `0` before that check. [F29]
- `struct alps_data::multi_data` is 6 bytes, whereas `psmouse->packet` is 8 bytes. `alps_process_packet_v4()` must verify `priv->multi_packet <= 2` before writing `packet[6..7]` at offset `2 * priv->multi_packet`, and 6-byte multi-packet handlers must copy `sizeof(priv->multi_data)` rather than `sizeof(psmouse->packet)`. [F42]
- In `alps_report_mt_data()`, `n` bounds the loop over `slot[MAX_TOUCHES]` and `f->mt[MAX_TOUCHES]` (`MAX_TOUCHES` is 4). Callers (`alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v7()`) must cap `n` at `MAX_TOUCHES` via `alps_get_mt_count(f->mt)` or an explicit `<= 4` clamp even when `f->fingers` reports 5 to 8 touches to `input_mt_report_finger_count()`. [F39]
- In `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` under `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI`, `no_data_x` and `no_data_y` must be selected in lockstep with `priv->flags & ALPS_BUTTONPAD` and `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV(priv->dev_id)` (`SS4_PLUS_MFPACKET_NO_AX*`, `SS4_MFPACKET_NO_AX*`, `SS4_MFPACKET_NO_AY*`) to match the bit shifts applied to `f->mt[3]`. [F27]
- In `alps_process_byte()`, bare PS/2 detection on `(psmouse->packet[0] & 0xc8) == 0x08` must be guarded by `priv->proto_version != ALPS_PROTO_V8 && !psmouse->out_of_sync_cnt` because V8's first byte (`0x18`) matches that mask and bare PS/2 packets cannot self-synchronize when out of sync. [F50]
- In `alps_process_byte()`, the `ALPS_PS2_INTERLEAVED` check (`psmouse->pktcnt >= 4 && (psmouse->packet[3] & 0x0f) == 0x0f`) must run before the `priv->proto_version < ALPS_PROTO_V5` bit-7 (`0x80`) check. On 6-byte protocols (`pktsize == 6`), `alps_handle_interleaved_ps2()` returns `PSMOUSE_GOOD_DATA` at `pktcnt == 6` to receive a 7th byte into `psmouse->packet[6]`, and must mask `psmouse->packet[6]` with `0xf7` when shifting it to `psmouse->packet[3]` before resetting `pktcnt` to 1 or 4. [F43, F51]
- Static protocol descriptors (`alps_model_data` and `alps_v*_protocol_data`) are `const`; `alps_set_protocol()` copies `protocol->flags` into `priv->flags` and dynamically clears `ALPS_DUALPOINT` on V3, Rushmore V3, and V7 when `alps_probe_trackstick_v3_v7()` returns `< 0`. `priv->dev2` is only allocated and registered in `alps_init()` when `priv->flags & ALPS_DUALPOINT` is set, so packet handlers and `alps_disconnect()` must check `priv->flags & ALPS_DUALPOINT` or `dev2 != NULL` before touching `priv->dev2`. [F17, F71]
- Once `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()` sets `ALPS_QUIRK_TRACKSTICK_BUTTONS` in `priv->quirks`, `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` must skip reporting `f->ts_left`, `f->ts_right`, and `f->ts_middle` on `priv->dev2` so touchpad packets do not clear trackstick button presses. [F32]
- In `alps_init()`, `priv->hw_init(psmouse)` must execute before `priv->set_abs_params(priv, dev1)` because `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3()` and `alps_hw_init_v7()` populate `priv->x_res` and `priv->y_res` via `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()`, which `alps_set_abs_params_mt_common()` then applies to `dev1`. [F36]
- In `alps_identify()` and `alps_match_table()`, `ec`-qualified branches (including `ec[0] == 0x88` for V7, Rushmore V3, and V3) must precede the `e7`-only check for `alps_v8_protocol_data`. Stack buffers `e7[4]` and `ec[4]` are 4 bytes, whereas `model->signature`, `priv->dev_id`, and `priv->fw_ver` are `u8[3]`, so comparisons and copies must use the 3-byte destination size rather than `sizeof(e7)` or `sizeof(ec)`. [F48, F64]
- In `alps_get_packet_id_v7()`, `V7_PACKET_ID_TWO`, `V7_PACKET_ID_MULTI`, and `V7_PACKET_ID_NEW` must be checked before `V7_PACKET_ID_IDLE` (`byte[1] == 0x00 && byte[4] == 0x00`). `alps_decode_packet_v7()` returns `0` for reportable packets (including `V7_PACKET_ID_IDLE`), `1` for `V7_PACKET_ID_NEW`, and `-1` for `V7_PACKET_ID_UNKNOWN`, so `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v7()` must abort reporting on any non-zero return from `priv->decode_fields()`. [F15, F53]
- In `alps_get_bitmap_points()`, `point` starts at `low` and switches to `high` on the first `1 -> 0` transition (`!bit && prev_bit`) while incrementing `*fingers` on every `0 -> 1` transition, so callers must zero-initialize `*fingers`, `*low`, and `*high` before each call. In `alps_process_bitmap()`, `priv->second_touch` is selected only when `priv->second_touch == -1`, and `alps_report_semi_mt_data()` must reset `priv->second_touch = -1` whenever `fingers < 2`. [F54, F55]
- `alps_dmi_has_separate_stick_buttons` must remain non-`__initconst` and end with an unconditional empty `{ }` sentinel outside `#if defined(CONFIG_DMI) && defined(CONFIG_X86)`, because `alps_set_protocol()` passes it to `dmi_check_system()` during both `alps_detect()` and runtime `alps_reconnect()`. [F76]

## Synchronization

- `alps_process_byte`, `alps_handle_interleaved_ps2`, `alps_flush_packet`, and all `priv->process_packet` and `priv->decode_fields` callbacks execute in atomic context under `serio->lock` (`spin_lock_irq`), so they must not call sleeping functions such as `ps2_command`, `input_allocate_device`, or `input_register_device`. [F82]
- In `alps_init()` and `alps_set_protocol()`, `priv->timer`, `priv->psmouse`, `priv->dev3_register_work`, `priv->process_packet`, and `priv->dev2` (when `ALPS_DUALPOINT` is set) must be initialized before `psmouse->protocol_handler` is set to `alps_process_byte`. [F8]
- `alps_flush_packet()` must acquire `serio_pause_rx(psmouse->ps2dev.serio)` and re-check `psmouse->pktcnt == psmouse->pktsize` under that guard before reading `psmouse->packet`, calling `priv->process_packet(psmouse)`, and resetting `psmouse->pktcnt = 0`. [F10]
- `alps_register_bare_ps2_mouse()` must hold `alps_mutex` across checking `!priv->dev3`, calling `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse(priv)`, and storing `ERR_PTR(error)` into `priv->dev3` on failure. Inside `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()`, `dev3` must be fully configured and registered via `input_register_device(dev3)` before being assigned to `priv->dev3`. [F9, F11]
- `alps_disconnect()` must call `timer_shutdown_sync(&priv->timer)` before unregistering `priv->dev2` or `priv->dev3` and before freeing `priv`, and `alps_flush_packet()` must not acquire `psmouse_mutex` (which `psmouse_disconnect()` holds across `alps_disconnect()`). [F84]

## Object Lifetimes

- Secondary input devices `priv->dev2` (in `alps_init()`) and `priv->dev3` (in `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()`) must have their `phys` strings formatted into persistent `priv->phys2` and `priv->phys3` buffers (never stack buffers), must be freed with `input_free_device()` if `input_register_device()` fails, and once registered must only be released via `input_unregister_device()` in `alps_disconnect()`. [F3, F65]

## API Contracts

- `alps_absolute_mode_v3()`, `alps_absolute_mode_v4()`, and `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()` require the touchpad to already be in command mode and do not exit it themselves; their callers (`alps_hw_init_v3`, `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3`, `alps_hw_init_v4`, `alps_hw_init_v7`) must call `alps_exit_command_mode()` on both normal and error paths. [F6]
- `alps_trackstick_enter_extended_mode_v3_v6()` requires the touchpad to be in passthrough mode, and callers that enable passthrough mode (`alps_poll`, `alps_hw_init_v6`, `alps_setup_trackstick_v3`) or monitor mode (`alps_absolute_mode_v6`) must disable that mode before returning even when the inner PS/2 operation fails. [F7]
- `__alps_command_mode_write_reg()` only transmits the two data nibbles and requires the target register address to have already been latched in the current command-mode session by an immediately preceding `alps_command_mode_read_reg()` or `alps_command_mode_set_addr()`. `alps_command_mode_send_nibble()` requires `nibble <= 0xf`, and whenever `command & 0x0f00` is non-zero it must pass the 4-byte stack `dummy` buffer to `ps2_command()` instead of `&priv->nibble_commands[nibble].data`. [F34, F41]
- In `alps_detect()`, after initial `alps_identify(psmouse, NULL)` succeeds, the driver must check `alps_is_cs19_trackpoint(psmouse)` and return `-EINVAL` if true so CS19 trackpoint-only devices are handed off; otherwise it must call `psmouse_reset(psmouse)` before `alps_identify(psmouse, priv)` so the `TP_READ_ID` probe does not break trackstick detection. `alps_identify()` must guard all writes to `priv` and the call to `alps_set_protocol()` with `if (priv)`. [F18, F75, F93]
- When `alps_detect()` is called with `set_properties == false`, or when `alps_init()` fails after `alps_detect()` succeeded with `set_properties == true`, the driver must `kfree()` the `struct alps_data` in `psmouse->private` and reset `psmouse->private = NULL`. [F85]
- On V8 (`ALPS_PROTO_V8`) devices, `alps_update_device_area_ss4_v2()`, `alps_update_btn_info_ss4_v2()`, `alps_update_dual_info_ss4_v2()`, and `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` must branch on `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV(priv->dev_id)` (`73 03 28`) for SS4+ OTP bit offsets, electrode constants, and coordinate macros. In `alps_update_dual_info_ss4_v2()`, if an `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV` touchpad lacks the OTP dualpoint bit (`(otp[0][0] >> 4) & 0x01`), the driver must fall back to reading command-mode register `0xD7` (matching `0x0C` or `0x1D`) and then call `alps_exit_command_mode()` and `PSMOUSE_CMD_ENABLE`. [F73, F74]
- In `alps_report_buttons(dev1, dev2, left, right, middle)`, each shared button is routed to `dev2` if `dev2` already has that button active in `dev2->key` and otherwise to `dev1`; because `alps_report_buttons()` only calls `input_sync(dev2)`, callers must pass the packet's primary device as `dev1` and call `input_sync(dev1)` afterwards. [F56]
- In `alps_init()`, `psmouse->dev` (`dev1`) must have `EV_REL`, `REL_X`, and `REL_Y` cleared before setting `EV_ABS` capabilities, only re-enabling `EV_REL` and `REL_WHEEL` if `priv->flags & ALPS_WHEEL` is set, and must clear `BTN_RIGHT` when `priv->flags & ALPS_BUTTONPAD` is set. Axis resolutions `priv->x_res` and `priv->y_res` passed to `input_abs_set_res()` in `alps_set_abs_params_mt_common()` must be in units/mm (`priv->x_max * 10 / x_phys` when `x_phys` is in `0.1 mm` units). [F86, F89]

## Error Handling

- `alps_command_mode_read_reg()` and `__alps_command_mode_read_reg()` return a non-negative 8-bit register value (`0x00`–`0xff`) on success and `-1` on failure, so callers must store the result in a signed `int` and test `< 0` or `== -1` rather than boolean truthiness. [F13]
- `alps_setup_trackstick_v3()` returns `-ENODEV` when the trackstick E7 report fails and `-EIO` on fatal bus or command-mode failures; its callers (`alps_hw_init_v3()` and `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3()`) must only abort touchpad initialization when the return value is `-EIO`. [F14]
- `priv->dev3` can hold `NULL` (before registration), an `ERR_PTR` (set by `alps_register_bare_ps2_mouse()` when registration fails), or a valid `struct input_dev *`, so consumers must test `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(priv->dev3)` before dereferencing or unregistering it and `!IS_ERR(priv->dev3)` before queueing `dev3_register_work`. On `ALPS_PROTO_V2` with `ALPS_DUALPOINT`, `alps_report_bare_ps2_packet()` routes bare PS/2 packets to `priv->dev2` instead of `priv->dev3`. [F16, F57]
- On `ALPS_PROTO_V3_RUSHMORE`, when the final byte of a packet (`psmouse->pktcnt == psmouse->pktsize`) has bit 7 set (`0x80`), `alps_process_byte()` must drop the packet and return `PSMOUSE_FULL_PACKET` rather than `PSMOUSE_BAD_DATA`. [F91]

## External Contracts

- All ALPS trackstick packet handlers (`SS4_TS_Y_V2` in `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `alps_process_packet_v1_v2()`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, `alps_process_packet_v6()`, and `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v7()`) must negate the sign-extended hardware Y delta when reporting `REL_Y` on `priv->dev2`. [F22]

## Known Pitfalls

- The `SS4_*_MF_*_V2(_b, _i)` coordinate and Z macros index `_b[0 + (_i) * 3]` through `_b[2 + (_i) * 3]` within a 6-byte packet, so `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` must pass packet-local indices `_i` of `0` and `1` even when filling `f->mt[2]` and `f->mt[3]` for `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI`. [F40]
- In `struct alps_fields`, boolean flags (`first_mp`, `is_mp`, `left`, `right`, `middle`, `ts_left`, `ts_right`, `ts_middle`) are 1-bit `unsigned int` bitfields rather than `bool`, so bit-masked expressions testing non-LSB bits must be normalized with `!!` or shifted to bit 0 before assignment. [F58]
- Sign-extending 8-bit trackstick motion bytes or 4-bit register nibbles (in `SS4_TS_X_V2`, `SS4_TS_Y_V2`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, `alps_process_packet_v6()`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v7()`, and `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()`) requires an explicit `(s8)` cast rather than `(char)`. When assembling the 35-bit `palm_data` bitmap in `alps_decode_dolphin()`, packet bytes shifted to or past bit 31 must be widened to `(u64)` before shifting. [F59, F60]
- When testing whether a hardware report byte falls within a 16-value hex range (such as `param[1]` in `0x20..0x2f` in `alps_is_cs19_trackpoint()` or `id[1]`/`ec[1]` in `0xb0..0xbf` and `0xc0..0xcf` in `alps_check_valid_firmware_id()` and `alps_identify()`), the full upper nibble must be masked with `0xf0` before comparing for equality. In `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, 7-bit trackstick `z`, `x`, and `y` fields in `packet[4]`, `packet[1]`, and `packet[2]` must be masked with `0x7f`. [F46, F47]
