# MELLANOX ETHERNET DRIVER (mlx5e)

The `mlx5e` driver (`drivers/net/ethernet/mellanox/mlx5/core`) implements the ConnectX-4+ Ethernet netdevice, E-Switch representors, RX/TX/XDP/AF_XDP NAPI datapaths, hardware TC offloads, flow steering, QoS/DCBNL, and statistics.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct mlx5_flow_attr` ends with a flexible-array union of `esw_attr` (`MLX5_FLOW_NAMESPACE_FDB`) and `nic_attr`; allocate via `mlx5_alloc_flow_attr(ns)`, copy using `ns_to_attr_sz(ns)`, and check `mlx5e_is_eswitch_flow(flow)` or `ns_type == MLX5_FLOW_NAMESPACE_FDB` before accessing `esw_attr` or `nic_attr`. [F47, F128]
- Code shared across NIC, switchdev, and representor modes must check `ndev->netdev_ops` (`&mlx5e_netdev_ops` for NIC/uplink rep, `&mlx5e_netdev_ops_rep` for VF rep) before casting `netdev_priv(ndev)` to `mlx5e_priv`, check `rpriv = priv->ppriv` before dereferencing `rpriv`, and check `is_mdev_switchdev_mode(priv->mdev)` before accessing `esw->offloads` or uplink state. [F48, F139, F140, F185]
- Guard optional pointers: `priv->channels.ptp` can be `NULL` even when `priv->tx_ptp_opened` or `MLX5E_PFLAG_TX_PORT_TS` is set; `sq->ptpsq` can be `NULL` even when `SKBTX_HW_TSTAMP` is set; `priv->txq2sq[qid]` can be `NULL` in `mlx5e_xmit` for uncreated HTB queues; `priv->rx_res` can be `NULL` in `mlx5e_num_channels_changed` and channel activate/deactivate; and `fs->vlan` is `NULL` on profiles without `MLX5E_PROFILE_FEATURE_FS_VLAN`. [F51, F237, F238, F416, F417]
- `priv->stats_nch`, `priv->max_opened_tc`, `priv->tx_ptp_opened`, and `priv->rx_ptp_opened` are monotonic high-water marks; `priv->channel_stats[i]` entries are lazily allocated up to `priv->stats_nch` and preserved until `mlx5e_priv_cleanup`, so all readers and cleanup must bound iterations by `priv->stats_nch` (and check `queue < priv->channels.num` under `priv->state_lock` when indexing `priv->channels.c[queue]`). [F261, F286, F289]
- In `parse_tc_actions`, splitting a flow prepends each new `mlx5_flow_attr` to `flow->attrs` via `list_add` after calling `mlx5e_tc_act_post_parse`, cloning via `mlx5e_clone_flow_attr_for_post_act` (which allocates a fresh `parse_attr` and resets per-stage action, destination, counter, cookie, CT, and branch fields), setting `i_split = i + 1`, and resetting `parse_state->if_count = 0`; because the original `flow->attr` remains last, post_act loops must stop at `list_is_last(&attr->list, &flow->attrs)`. [F86, F87, F109]
- Whenever `rq->hw_gro_data->skb` is passed to `napi_gro_receive` or dropped in `mlx5e_shampo_flush_skb`, `mlx5e_handle_rx_cqe_mpwrq_shampo`, or `mlx5e_shampo_complete_rx_cqe`, `rq->hw_gro_data->skb` must be reset to `NULL` immediately, and `mlx5e_poll_rx_cq` must flush any remaining `rq->hw_gro_data->skb` (and call `mlx5e_xdp_rx_poll_complete(rq)` if `rq->xdp_prog` is active) when `work_done > 0`. [F45, F67, F177]
- Before posting any non-MPWQE descriptor (`mlx5e_txqsq_get_next_pi` or `mlx5e_post_nop`) on an MPWQE-capable `mlx5e_txqsq`, `mlx5e_tx_mpwqe_ensure_complete(sq)` must be called; in `mlx5e_sq_xmit_mpwqe`, `dma_map_single` must succeed before `mlx5e_tx_mpwqe_session_start(sq, eseg)`, and `mlx5e_tx_mpwqe_session_complete` must clear `sq->mpwqe.wqe = NULL`. [F392, F393, F468]
- Because `mlx5e_poll_tx_cq` and `mlx5e_free_txqsq_descs` do not clear `sq->db.wqe_info[ci]`, every WQE producer (`mlx5e_txwqe_complete`, `mlx5e_tx_mpwqe_session_complete`, `mlx5e_tx_flush`) must overwrite `sq->db.wqe_info[pi]` with a full compound literal, and both consumers must unmap DMA via `mlx5e_tx_wi_dma_unmap` before freeing SKBs and pass accumulated `npkts` and `nbytes` to `netdev_tx_completed_queue`. [F396, F414, F431, F479]

## Synchronization
- Lock order is `rtnl_lock()` -> `netdev_lock(netdev)` -> `mutex_lock(&priv->state_lock)`; unlock `priv->state_lock` before calling `netdev_update_features(netdev)`, hold `netdev_lock(netdev)` outside `priv->state_lock` when `dcbnl_rtnl_ops` or representor enable/disable paths call `mlx5e_safe_switch_params`/`mlx5e_open`/`mlx5e_close`, and hold `rtnl_lock()` across `mlx5e_set_xdp_feature(netdev)` and `mlx5e_psp_register(priv)` when `netdev->reg_state == NETREG_REGISTERED`. [F80, F160, F226, F227, F367]
- Before reconfiguring TX queue counts/TCs or closing channels/queues, `mlx5e_deactivate_priv_channels` must call `netif_tx_disable`, and queue deactivation (`mlx5e_deactivate_rq`, `mlx5e_deactivate_txqsq`, `mlx5e_deactivate_icosq`, `mlx5e_close_xdpsq`, `mlx5e_dim_rx_change`, `mlx5e_dim_tx_change`) must clear `MLX5E_{RQ,SQ}_STATE_ENABLED` or `MLX5E_{RQ,SQ}_STATE_DIM` and call `synchronize_net()` before canceling work (`dim->work`, `recover_work`, `rx_timeout_work`) or destroying queues. [F216, F218, F220, F221]
- Async recovery works (`mlx5e_tx_timeout_work`, `mlx5e_rq_timeout_work`, `mlx5e_tx_err_cqe_work`) must acquire `netdev_lock` via a `netdev_trylock` loop that aborts when `MLX5E_STATE_CHANNELS_ACTIVE` or `MLX5E_SQ_STATE_ENABLED` is cleared; teardown must clear that bit before `cancel_work_sync`, and `mlx5e_cancel_tx_timeout_work` must skip `cancel_work_sync(&priv->tx_timeout_work)` when `current_work() == &priv->tx_timeout_work`. [F219, F359]
- Atomic-context callbacks must not sleep or call firmware commands directly: `mlx5e_sync_netdev_addr` (under `netif_addr_lock_bh`) and `mlx5e_rx_flow_steer` (under `spin_lock_bh(&arfs->arfs_lock)`) must allocate with `GFP_ATOMIC` and defer hardware updates; `mlx5e_set_rx_mode` and `async_event` must queue `priv->set_rx_mode_work` and `priv->update_carrier_work`; and `mlx5e_poll_tx_cq` on `MLX5_CQE_REQ_ERR` must `test_and_set_bit(MLX5E_SQ_STATE_RECOVERING, &sq->state)` and queue `sq->recover_work`. [F352, F353, F354, F388, F477]
- Lookups in `tc_ht` (`mlx5e_configure_flower`, `mlx5e_delete_flower`, `mlx5e_stats_flower`) must run under `rcu_read_lock()`, pinning `flow` with `mlx5e_flow_get` (`IS_ERR(flow)` check) before `rcu_read_unlock()`; in `mlx5e_delete_flower`, lookup, `flow_flag_test_and_set(flow, DELETED)`, and `rhashtable_remove_fast` must all occur inside a single `rcu_read_lock()` section before `mlx5e_flow_put` (`kfree_rcu`). [F29, F42, F165]
- In `mlx5e_hairpin_flow_add`, initialize `hpe` fields (`flows_lock`, `flows`, `dead_peer_wait_list`, `refcnt = 1`, `res_ready`) before inserting into `tc->hairpin_tbl` under `tc->hairpin_tbl_lock`, and call `complete_all(&hpe->res_ready)` even if `mlx5e_hairpin_create` fails (storing `ERR_PTR` in `hpe->hp`); `mlx5e_hairpin_put` must use `refcount_dec_and_mutex_lock(&hpe->refcnt, &tc->hairpin_tbl_lock)` to unlink `hpe`, and both `mlx5e_hairpin_put` and `mlx5e_tc_hairpin_update_dead_peer` must call `wait_for_completion(&hpe->res_ready)` and check `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(hpe->hp)` outside `tc->hairpin_tbl_lock`. [F16, F23, F33, F53, F148]
- `mlx5e_napi_poll` must hold `rcu_read_lock()` until `out:`, read `c->qos_sqs` with `rcu_dereference()` and `c->qos_sqs_size` with `READ_ONCE()` after `smp_rmb()`, and when `budget == 0` only poll TX CQs and jump to `out:`; in `mlx5e_poll_tx_cq`, `sq->cc` and `sq->dma_fifo_cc` must be updated only after `mlx5_cqwq_update_db_record` and `wmb()`. [F464, F473, F475]

## Object Lifetimes
- Each `page_pool` allocation in `mlx5e_page_alloc_fragmented` is biased by `MLX5E_PAGECNT_BIAS_MAX` and unreferenced by `MLX5E_PAGECNT_BIAS_MAX - frag_page->frags` in `mlx5e_page_release_fragmented`, so every SKB fragment attachment and XDP transmit (`__test_and_clear_bit(MLX5E_RQ_FLAG_XDP_XMIT, rq->flags)`) must increment `frag_page->frags`, and in `mlx5e_skb_from_cqe_mpwrq_nonlinear` (`prog != NULL`), `wi->linear_page` must be released via `mlx5e_page_release_fragmented` on every exit path. [F1, F6, F176]
- Because `mlx5e_free_rx_shampo_hd_entry` only releases the backing page when `((header_index + 1) & (MLX5E_SHAMPO_WQ_HEADER_PER_PAGE - 1)) == 0`, `mlx5e_alloc_rx_hd_mpwqe` and `mlx5e_build_shampo_hd_umr` must keep `ksm_entries` aligned to `MLX5E_SHAMPO_WQ_HEADER_PER_PAGE`, and before `mlx5e_shampo_dealloc_hd` frees entries set in `shampo->bitmap`, uncompleted ICOSQ UMR entries between `shampo->ci` and `shampo->pi` must be marked via `mlx5e_shampo_fill_umr`. [F4, F5]
- Because RQ page release is deferred until reallocation, `mlx5e_rq_alloc_mpwqe_info`, `mlx5e_init_frags_partition`, and `mlx5e_init_xsk_buffs` must pre-set skip-release bits; `mlx5e_dealloc_rx_mpwqe` and `mlx5e_alloc_rx_mpwqe` (`err_unmap`) must `bitmap_fill(wi->skip_release_bitmap, ...)`, while `mlx5e_alloc_rx_mpwqe` zeroes `skip_release_bitmap` and `consumed_strides`; and `mlx5e_free_rx_descs` must free `missing` descriptors before popping the WQ (calling `xsk_buff_free` for unskipped XSK buffs). [F69, F180, F270, F305]
- Representor and TC teardown: `mlx5e_rep_neigh_init` must precede `mlx5e_init_uplink_rep_tx` and `mlx5e_tc_ht_init` (and `mlx5e_rep_neigh_cleanup` must follow their cleanup); `mlx5e_tc_esw_init` must call `mlx5_esw_offloads_devcom_init` last and `mlx5e_tc_esw_cleanup` must call `mlx5_esw_offloads_devcom_cleanup` first; `mlx5e_cleanup_ul_rep_rx` must `cancel_work_sync(&rpriv->uplink_priv.mpesw_work)` before `mlx5e_cleanup_rep_rx`; and in `mlx5e_destroy_rep_root_ft`, only destroy `rpriv->root_ft` when `rep->vport == MLX5_VPORT_UPLINK`. [F9, F11, F27, F145]
- Whenever `mlx5e_tc_tun_parse` is called on a `mlx5_flow_spec`, call `mlx5_geneve_tlv_option_del(priv->mdev->geneve)` before freeing `spec` if `mlx5_flow_has_geneve_opt(spec)` is true; in `mlx5e_configure_flower`, `mlx5_esw_hold` is released via `mlx5_esw_release` before returning on all paths, whereas `mlx5e_tc_block_ipsec_offload` and `mlx5_esw_get` are per-flow holds undone on error/duplicate-cookie exits and in `mlx5e_delete_flower`. [F20, F21]

## API Contracts
- Return-value contracts: `mlx5e_tc_table_alloc()` returns `ERR_PTR(-ENOMEM)` on failure when `CONFIG_MLX5_CLS_ACT=y` and `NULL` when disabled (check `IS_ERR()`); `mlx5e_mqprio_rl_create()` returns `NULL` when no TC has `max_rate`, valid pointer, or `ERR_PTR` (check `IS_ERR(rl)` on creation, `if (rl)` on cleanup); `mlx5_esw_offloads_devlink_port()`, `mlx5e_rx_res_create()`, and `mlx5e_create_devlink()` return `ERR_PTR`, whereas `mlx5e_fs_init()` and `mlx5e_create_netdev()` return `NULL` on failure; `mlx5e_tc_match_to_reg_set_and_get_id()` returns action index `>= 0` or `< 0`; and `validate_flow()` returns tuple count `> 0`, `0` (invalid empty `ETHER_FLOW`), or `< 0`. [F41, F43, F44, F233, F234, F235]
- When `mlx5e_tc_update_skb` restores a tunnel or internal-port device on `skb->dev`, it transfers a `net_device` reference in `tc_priv->fwd_dev` (even when `tc_priv->skb_done` is true for `MLX5_MAPPED_OBJ_SAMPLE`) that the caller must release via `dev_put(tc_priv->fwd_dev)`. [F22]
- When `mlx5e_accel_tx_begin` returns `false`, it has already freed `skb`, so `mlx5e_xmit` must return `NETDEV_TX_OK` without freeing `skb`; any offload step that posts auxiliary WQEs to `sq` must run in `mlx5e_accel_tx_begin` before `mlx5e_txqsq_get_next_pi`, never in `mlx5e_accel_tx_finish`; TX error/teardown paths must free SKBs with `dev_kfree_skb_any()`, while `mlx5e_poll_tx_cq` passes `napi_budget` to `napi_consume_skb()`. [F412, F427, F476]

## Error Handling
- Because `mlx5e_tc_nic_init` and `mlx5e_tc_esw_init` store the `ERR_PTR` from `mlx5e_tc_post_act_init` in `post_act`, callers of `get_post_action()` must check `IS_ERR(post_act)`; because `mlx5e_add_offloaded_nic_rule` and `mlx5e_tc_add_nic_flow` store `ERR_PTR` in `tc->t` (under `tc->t_lock`) and `flow->rule[0]`, checks and teardown of `tc->t` and `flow->rule[0]` must use `IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`; for all other `ERR_PTR`-returning constructors assigned to fields whose teardown checks `if (obj->field)`, error paths must reset the field to `NULL`. [F31, F49, F54, F241]
- `mlx5_uplink_netdev_get()` can return `NULL` when the uplink netdev is unbound (leaving `rpriv->netdev == NULL` even when `mlx5e_vport_uplink_rep_load` returns `0`), so callers must check `netdev != NULL` before `netdev_priv(netdev)` and release non-NULL refs with `mlx5_uplink_netdev_put()`. [F50]
- When `mlx5e_sq_xmit_wqe`, `mlx5e_sq_xmit_mpwqe`, or `mlx5i_sq_xmit` drops `skb` on a DMA mapping error, it must free `skb` via `dev_kfree_skb_any(skb)` and call `mlx5e_tx_flush(sq)` so any open MPWQE session, edge NOPs, or prior `xmit_more` WQEs are completed and notified; `mlx5e_txwqe_build_dsegs` returns a signed `int` (`< 0` on error after unwinding via `mlx5e_dma_unmap_wqe_err`, incrementing `num_dma` and `sq->dma_fifo_pc` only after each mapping succeeds) that must be checked before narrowing to `u8`. [F391, F409, F448]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `mlx5e_skb_from_cqe_nonlinear` and `mlx5e_skb_from_cqe_mpwrq_nonlinear` (XDP path), `mlx5e_build_linear_skb` resets `sinfo->nr_frags = 0`, so `xdp_update_skb_frags_info` must recompute `nr_frags` from `wi - head_wi - 1` or `frag_page - head_page`, and fragment loops in `mlx5e_skb_from_cqe_nonlinear` must iterate `[head_wi, wi)`. [F70, F91]
- `rq->hw_gro_data->fk.control.thoff` is computed after `eth_type_trans()` pulls `ETH_HLEN` bytes, so offsets into `mlx5e_shampo_get_packet_hd()` must add `ETH_HLEN` to `thoff`, whereas offsets into `skb->data` must not; in `mlx5e_handle_rx_cqe_mpwrq_shampo`, if `head_size == 0` and `netmem_is_net_iov(frag_page->netmem)` is true, the packet must be dropped before `mlx5e_skb_from_cqe_mpwrq_nonlinear`. [F138, F179]
- Because `mlx5e_mod_hdr_alloc()` may `krealloc` `mod_hdr_acts->actions` and returns the slot at `mod_hdr_acts->num_actions * MLX5_MH_ACT_SZ`, callers must not cache pointers into `mod_hdr_acts->actions` across calls and must increment `mod_hdr_acts->num_actions` after each allocation; in firmware `set_action_in` descriptors, a 5-bit `length` of `0` encodes 32 bits; and `mlx5e_tc_match_to_reg_match` must never be called with `mlx5e_tc_attr_to_reg` types that omit `.soffset`. [F62, F82, F122, F136, F137]
- Flexible-array and header-only layout hazards in `en.h`: access `rq->mpwqe.info` only via `mlx5e_get_mpw_info(rq, i)` (never `rq->mpwqe.info[i]`); never touch `inline_mtts`/`inline_klms`/`inline_ksms` in `mlx5e_build_umr_wqe` because `&rq->mpwqe.umr_wqe` is a header-only `mlx5e_umr_wqe_hdr`; size `union mlx5e_alloc_units` via its member size (`sizeof(*aus->frag_pages)`), not `sizeof(*aus)` (which is 0); and in `mlx5e_tx_mpwqe_add_dseg`, cast `session->wqe` to `struct mlx5_wqe_data_seg *` before adding `session->ds_count`. [F321, F322, F323, F461]
- 16-bit WQEBB counters (`sq->pc`, `sq->cc`, `sqcc`, `cqe->wqe_counter`) advance by `wi->num_wqebbs` (not 1), must be compared with `==`/`!=` or `mlx5e_wqc_has_room_for` rather than `<`/`<=`, and must be converted with `mlx5_wq_cyc_ctr2ix` before indexing `sq->db.wqe_info`; in `mlx5e_txwqe_complete` and `mlx5e_sq_xmit_mpwqe`, `sq->pc` advancement and queue-stop checks (`mlx5e_tx_check_stop`, PTP freelist stop) must precede `__netdev_tx_sent_queue` so stopping `sq->txq` forces a doorbell ring even with `xmit_more`. [F127, F320, F442, F454, F467]
- In `mlx5e_sq_xmit_wqe`, `eseg->inline_hdr.sz` must be updated with `|= cpu_to_be16(ihs)` (not `=`) after `MLX5E_TX_FETCH_WQE` zeroes the WQE, because `eseg->inline_hdr.sz` aliases the upper 16 bits of `eseg->trailer` populated by `mlx5e_txwqe_build_eseg` for IPsec/PSP; because `mlx5e_priv_cleanup` zeroes `*priv` via `memset`, `mlx5e_netdev_change_profile` must snapshot `priv->profile`, `priv->netdev`, `priv->mdev`, and `priv->ppriv` beforehand. [F273, F439]
