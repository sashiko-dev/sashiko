# MULTIMEDIA CARD (MMC), SECURE DIGITAL (SD) AND SDIO SUBSYSTEM

`drivers/mmc/host` contains MMC/SD/SDIO host controller drivers (Alcor AU6601/AU6621, Broadcom BCM2835, TI DaVinci, STM32 SDMMC MMCI, MOXA ART, Freescale i.MX/MPC512x and MXS, Marvell PXA, Realtek PCIe/USB card readers, and Renesas SuperH MMCIF), the MMC Host Software Queue (HSQ) implementation, and OpenFirmware SPI MMC helpers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Device `drvdata` stores `struct mmc_host *` (requiring `mmc_priv()`) in `moxart-mmc.c`, `mxcmmc.c`, `mxs-mmc.c`, and `pxamci.c`, whereas `alcor.c`, `bcm2835.c`, `davinci_mmc.c`, `rtsx_pci_sdmmc.c`, `rtsx_usb_sdmmc.c`, and `sh_mmcif.c` store their private host struct directly; `mmc_hsq_init()` stores `struct mmc_hsq *` in `mmc->cqe_private` (not `mmc_priv(mmc)`). [F130, F131]
- In `mmci_stm32_sdmmc.c`, `sdmmc_idma_validate_data()` sets `idma->use_bounce_buffer` when any non-final scatterlist entry has unaligned `sg->offset` or `sg->length` or the final cursor `sg` (not `data->sg`) has unaligned `sg->offset`; `_sdmmc_idma_prep_data()`, `sdmmc_idma_unprep_data()`, and `sdmmc_idma_error()` must call `dma_map_sg()`/`dma_unmap_sg()` only when `!idma->use_bounce_buffer`. [F53, F97]
- In `mmci_stm32_sdmmc.c`, when `sdmmc_busy_complete()` enables `host->variant->busy_detect_mask` in `MMCIMASK0` due to `MCI_STM32_BUSYD0`, it must record `host->busy_status`, later clear `busy_detect_mask` in `MMCIMASK0` and `host->busy_status = 0` on completion or error, and unconditionally write `busy_detect_mask` to `MMCICLEAR` at `complete:`. [F64, F148]
- In `bcm2835.c`, all writes to `SDHCFG` and `SDCDIV` must be mirrored in `host->hcfg` and `host->cdiv` because `bcm2835_reset_internal()` zeroes both registers, resets `host->clock = 0`, and restores them from `host->hcfg` and `host->cdiv`. [F59]
- In `davinci_mmc.c`, `mmc_davinci_start_dma_transfer()` may use EDMA only when `host->bytes_left` and all mapped `sg_dma_len(data->sg + i)` are multiples of `rw_threshold` (otherwise unmapping and falling back to PIO), and must set `host->do_dma = 1` and `host->bytes_left = 0` so `mmc_davinci_irq()` skips PIO. [F68, F89]
- In `mxcmmc.c`, `host->do_dma` is reset to `1` per request when `host->dma` is non-NULL and cleared to `0` in `mxcmci_setup_data()` if any segment has `sg->offset & 3`, `sg->length & 3`, or `sg->length < 512` (permanently disabling DMA requires `host->dma = NULL` too); `mxcmci_irq()` must mask out `STATUS_SDIO_INT_ACTIVE | STATUS_DATA_TRANS_DONE | STATUS_WRITE_OP_DONE` on its initial `MMC_REG_STATUS` write, clearing `STATUS_WRITE_OP_DONE` only when `mxcmci_use_dma(host)` is true. [F69, F93, F112]
- In `sh_mmcif.c`, `sh_mmcif_request()` and `sh_mmcif_set_ios()` require `host->state == STATE_IDLE`, so `sh_mmcif_irqt()` and `sh_mmcif_timeout_work()` must reset `host->wait_for = MMCIF_WAIT_FOR_REQUEST`, `host->state = STATE_IDLE`, and `host->mrq = NULL`; `sh_mmcif_sync_reset()` must save and restore `0x010f0000 & sh_mmcif_readl(host->addr, MMCIF_CE_CLK_CTRL)`, timeout bits, and `BUF_ACC_ATYP` across `SOFT_RST_ON`/`SOFT_RST_OFF`. [F73, F74]
- In `mmc_hsq.c`, `hsq->slot` and `hsq->tag_slot` have `HSQ_NUM_SLOTS` (64) entries and `HSQ_INVALID_TAG` (`HSQ_NUM_SLOTS`) must be checked before indexing; while `hsq->recovery_halt` is set between `mmc_hsq_recovery_start()` and `mmc_hsq_recovery_finish()`, `mmc_hsq_request()` must return `-EBUSY`, `mmc_hsq_pump_requests()` and `mmc_hsq_post_request()` must not dispatch slots, and `mmc_hsq_queue_is_idle()` must return `true` with `*ret = -EBUSY`. [F84, F175]

## Synchronization

- In `mmc_hsq.c`, `mmc_hsq_pump_requests()`, `mmc_hsq_post_request()`, `mmc_hsq_finalize_request()`, and `mmc_hsq_recovery_start()` must use `spin_lock_irqsave(&hsq->lock, flags)`; `mmc_hsq_finalize_request()` must clear `hsq->slot[hsq->next_tag].mrq` under `hsq->lock` and drop `hsq->lock` before `mmc_cqe_request_done()` while keeping `hsq->mrq` and `hsq->next_tag` until `mmc_hsq_post_request()`; `mmc_hsq_pump_requests()` must drop `hsq->lock` before `request_atomic()`/`request()`, deferring `-EBUSY` to `hsq->retry_work`. [F24, F159, F160]
- In `mmci_stm32_sdmmc.c`, `sdmmc_post_sig_volt_switch()` must hold `host->lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave()` when accessing `host->pwr_reg` or calling `mmci_write_pwrreg()`, and drop `host->lock` around `readl_relaxed_poll_timeout()` on `MCI_STM32_VSWEND`; writes to coherent DMA memory (`idma->bounce_buf` and `struct sdmmc_lli_desc`) must be followed by `dma_wmb()` before starting IDMA via `writel_relaxed()`. [F25, F26]
- In `bcm2835.c`, `host->irq_block`, `host->irq_busy`, and `host->irq_data` are protected by `host->lock` between `bcm2835_irq()` and `bcm2835_threaded_irq()`, which must drop `host->lock` before taking `host->mutex`; `bcm2835_dma_complete()` must defer completion to `host->dma_work`; `bcm2835_finish_request()` and `alcor_request_complete()` callers holding `host->cmd_mutex` must use asynchronous `cancel_delayed_work(&host->timeout_work)`, and `alcor` must check `host->mrq` under `host->cmd_mutex`. [F29, F30, F157, F162]
- In `mxcmmc.c` and `mxs-mmc.c`, callers of `mmc_signal_sdio_irq()` (`mxcmci_irq()`, `mxs_mmc_irq_handler()`, `mxs_mmc_enable_sdio_irq()`) must release `host->lock` first because `mmc_signal_sdio_irq()` synchronously calls `.enable_sdio_irq` which takes `host->lock`; in `mxcmmc.c`, all `MMC_REG_INT_CNTR` writes must hold `host->lock` and preserve `INT_SDIO_IRQ_EN` when `host->use_sdio` is set, and `mxcmci_cmd_done()` must defer PIO data transfer to `host->datawork` (`mxcmci_datawork()`). [F71, F163, F178]
- `sg_miter_start()` must include `SG_MITER_ATOMIC` in `alcor_prepare_sg_miter()`, `bcm2835_prepare_data()`, and `mmc_davinci_prepare_data()` because PIO runs in hard IRQ context, whereas `sh_mmcif_single_read()`, `sh_mmcif_multi_read()`, `sh_mmcif_single_write()`, and `sh_mmcif_multi_write()` must omit `SG_MITER_ATOMIC` because PIO runs in threaded IRQ `sh_mmcif_irqt()`. [F155, F156]
- In `moxart_request()`, `host->lock` must be dropped around `moxart_transfer_dma()` and `wait_for_completion_interruptible_timeout(&host->pio_complete, ...)`, then re-acquired with `host->is_removed` rechecked before polling `REG_STATUS` or sending a stop command; in `rtsx_pci_sdmmc.c` and `rtsx_usb_sdmmc.c`, `sd_normal_rw()` must allocate bounce buffers with `GFP_NOIO`, and `rtsx_usb_led_control()` must defer USB transfers and `ucr->dev_mutex` locking to `host->led_work`. [F32, F168, F169]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `mmci_stm32_sdmmc.c`, when a transfer error occurs while `dma_inprogress(host)` is true, `sdmmc_idma_error()` must disable `MMCI_STM32_IDMACTRLR`, clear `host->dma_in_progress = false` and `data->host_cookie = 0`, and call `dma_unmap_sg()` if `!idma->use_bounce_buffer`; `sdmmc_idma_finalize()` must call `sdmmc_idma_unprep_data()` only when `!data->host_cookie` so pre-mapped requests are unmapped by `.unprep_data`. [F1, F2]
- In `bcm2835.c`, `bcm2835_prepare_dma()` must unmap `data->sg` if `dmaengine_prep_slave_sg()` fails and set `host->dma_chan` only on success, while `bcm2835_dma_complete_work()`, `bcm2835_finish_request()`, and `bcm2835_reset()` must clear `host->dma_chan = NULL` when unmapping or terminating DMA; in `mxs-mmc.c`, when `host->data` is non-NULL, `mxs_mmc_prep_dma()` must call `dma_unmap_sg()` on prep failure, and `mxs_mmc_request_done()` must call `dma_unmap_sg()` and clear `host->data = NULL` before starting `data->stop` or completing `host->mrq`. [F6, F13]
- Dual DMA channels in `moxart-mmc.c` and `sh_mmcif.c` are all-or-nothing: in `moxart_probe()`, if either `dma_chan_tx` or `dma_chan_rx` fails without `-EPROBE_DEFER`, the valid channel must be released and nulled (and `out` / `moxart_remove()` must release any `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL` channels); in `sh_mmcif.c`, if acquiring, configuring, or preparing either `chan_tx` or `chan_rx` fails, both channels must be released and nulled and `BUF_ACC_DMAREN | BUF_ACC_DMAWEN` cleared. [F10, F72]
- In `davinci_mmc.c` and `sh_mmcif.c`, started `host->sg_miter` iterators must always be stopped with `sg_miter_stop(&host->sg_miter)`: in `mmc_davinci_irq()` on both `MMCST0_DATDNE` and error abort (`davinci_abort_data()`), and in every `sh_mmcif` PIO handler and setup helper on `host->sd_error`, `!sg_miter_next()`, or block completion. [F9, F22]
- In `sh_mmcif.c`, `sh_mmcif_probe()` drops its `pm_runtime_get_sync()` and `clk_prepare_enable()` references after `mmc_add_host()`, `sh_mmcif_set_ios()` gates clock, runtime PM, and DMA acquisition on `!host->power` (`MMC_POWER_UP`) and release on `host->power` (`MMC_POWER_OFF`), and `sh_mmcif_remove()` must set `host->dying = true` and re-acquire `host->clk` and runtime PM before `mmc_remove_host()`, writing `MMCIF_CE_INT_MASK`, and cancelling `timeout_work`. [F21, F134]
- In `rtsx_pci_sdmmc.c` and `rtsx_usb_sdmmc.c`: on `mmc_add_host()` probe failure, `rtsx_pci` must call `pm_runtime_dont_use_autosuspend()` and `pm_runtime_disable()`, and `rtsx_usb` must call `led_classdev_unregister(&host->led)` and `pm_runtime_disable()`; `rtsx_pci_sdmmc_drv_remove()` must clear `pcr->slots[RTSX_SD_CARD]`, cancel `host->work`, complete `host->mrq` under `host->host_mutex`, and disable runtime PM; `rtsx_usb_sdmmc_drv_remove()` must set `host->host_removal = true` and `cancel_work_sync(&host->led_work)` before unregistering `host->led`. [F17, F19]
- In `rtsx_usb_sdmmc.c`, `sd_set_power_mode()` must return early when `power_mode == host->power_mode` to keep `pm_runtime_get_noresume()` and `pm_runtime_put_noidle()` balanced, and `rtsx_usb_update_led()` must pair `pm_runtime_get_noresume()` with `pm_runtime_put_sync_suspend()` on all paths. [F18]
- Probe/remove ordering: `spin_lock_init(&host->lock)` must precede `devm_request_irq()`; `.remove` callbacks must not use `__exit`/`__exit_p()`; `alcor` remove/suspend must `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&host->timeout_work)` and complete `host->mrq` before `alcor_hw_uninit()`; `bcm2835` must free `host->irq` on `mmc_add_host()` failure and stop IRQ/`dma_work`/`timeout_work` before releasing `host->clk` and `host->dma_chan_rxtx`; `davinci_mmc`, `mxcmmc`, `mxs-mmc`, and `pxamci` must unwind clocks, DMA, `reg_vmmc`, cpufreq, and `pdata->exit()` in LIFO order. [F5, F7, F8, F12, F15, F27, F132, F136]

## API Contracts

- Across `drivers/mmc/host` (`bcm2835`, `davinci_mmc`, `moxart-mmc`, `pxamci`, `sh_mmcif`), `dma_unmap_sg()` must be passed the original scatterlist entry count `data->sg_len` rather than the coalesced segment count returned by `dma_map_sg()`. [F39]
- Because `sg_miter_next()` initializes `sgm->consumed = sgm->length`, PIO transfer functions (`alcor_trf_block_pio()`, `bcm2835_transfer_block_pio()`, `davinci_fifo_data_trans()`, `sh_mmcif_mread_block()`, `sh_mmcif_mwrite_block()`) must clamp the transferred byte count to `sgm->length` and overwrite `sgm->consumed` (never `+=` without zeroing first); in `sh_mmcif_multi_read()`/`sh_mmcif_multi_write()`, `sg_miter_next()` must be called once before enabling buffer IRQs and then at the end of each block in `sh_mmcif_mread_block()`/`sh_mmcif_mwrite_block()`. [F82, F99, F100]
- When `mmc->supply.vmmc` is acquired via `mmc_regulator_get_supply()` (`pxamci`, `mxcmmc`, `sh_mmcif`), an absent optional supply is `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)` (not `NULL`), so callers must check `!IS_ERR(mmc->supply.vmmc)` and pass the 0-based bit number `vdd` (or `0` on power-off, not `1 << vdd` or `MMC_VDD_*`) to `mmc_regulator_set_ocr()`, while `pxamci_set_power()` shifts `1 << vdd` against `pdata->ocr_mask`. [F47, F180]
- In `of_mmc_spi.c`, callers must set `spi->dev` drvdata to `struct mmc_host *` before `mmc_spi_get_pdata(spi)`, which must fail on `mmc_of_parse_voltage() < 0` (not `0`); because `mmc_spi_get_pdata()` allocates `struct of_mmc_spi` only when `!dev->platform_data && dev_fwnode(dev)`, `mmc_spi_put_pdata()` must return early if `!dev->platform_data || !dev_fwnode(dev)` and clear `dev->platform_data = NULL` after `kfree(oms)`. [F4, F45]

## Error Handling

- Probe IRQ and DMA: `platform_get_irq(pdev, 0)` must be used for mandatory primary IRQs (propagating its exact negative errno, while `moxart_probe()` using `irq_of_parse_and_map()` checks `irq <= 0` and returns `-EINVAL`) and `platform_get_irq_optional(pdev, 1)` for optional secondary IRQs; in `bcm2835`, `davinci_mmc`, `moxart`, and `mxcmmc` probes, `dma_request_chan()` returning `-EPROBE_DEFER` must abort probe, while other errors fall back to PIO; in `pxamci` and `davinci_mmc`, `-ENOENT` from `mmc_gpiod_request_cd/ro()` must not abort probe or set `use_ro_gpio`. [F41, F57, F186, F187]
- In resume callbacks (`davinci_mmcsd_resume()`, `mxcmci_resume()`, `bcm2835_resume()`, `mxs_mmc_resume()`), `clk_enable()` / `clk_prepare_enable()` errors must be checked and propagated before touching MMIO registers (with `mxcmci_resume()` disabling `clk_per` if `clk_ipg` fails); stack-allocated `struct dma_slave_config` instances (`moxart_probe()`, `pxamci_setup_data()`, `sh_mmcif_dma_slave_config()`) must be zero-initialized before populating fields. [F43, F44]
- In `mmci_stm32_sdmmc.c`, both `dlyb` (`host->variant_priv`) and `dlyb->ops->set_input_ck` (NULL on `st,stm32mp25-sdmmc2`) must be checked before calling `dlyb->ops->set_input_ck(dlyb)` in `mmci_sdmmc_set_pwrreg()` and `sdmmc_dlyb_phase_tuning()`; in `sh_mmcif_end_cmd()`, `wait_for_completion_interruptible_timeout()` must be stored in a signed `long time` and checked for `!time` (`-ETIMEDOUT`) and `time < 0`. [F48, F52]
- Hardware CRC status: response CRC status bits must be ignored for `MMC_SEND_OP_COND` in `bcm2835_finish_command()`, commands without `MMC_RSP_CRC` in `mxcmci_read_response()` and `pxamci_cmd_done()`, and 136-bit responses with MSB set on PXA27x in `pxamci_cmd_done()`; write CRC error bits (`MMCST0_CRCWR` in `mmc_davinci_irq()`, `STATUS_CRC_WRITE_ERR` in `mxcmci_finish_data()`) must check hardware CRC status (`DAVINCI_MMCDRSP == 0x9f` or `((stat >> 9) & 0x3) == 2`) to report write timeouts as `-ETIMEDOUT` instead of `-EILSEQ`. [F188, F189]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `bcm2835_prepare_dma()`, multi-block reads (`data->blocks > 1 && dir_data == DMA_FROM_DEVICE`) must trim `min((u32)(FIFO_READ_THRESHOLD - 1) * 4, (u32)data->blocks * data->blksz)` bytes from the final SG entry before `dma_map_sg()`, and `bcm2835_dma_complete_work()` must normalize `host->drain_page += host->drain_offset >> PAGE_SHIFT` and `host->drain_offset &= ~PAGE_MASK` before `kmap_local_page()` to drain the trailing words from `SDDATA`. [F88, F145]
- In `pxamci.c`, on PXA27x `pxamci_setup_data()` must defer `dma_async_issue_pending(host->dma_chan_tx)` for `MMC_DATA_WRITE` until `END_CMD_RES`, and `pxamci_stop_clock()` must stop `MMC_STRPCL` before starting a request, stop command, or disabling `host->clk` (gated by `CLKRT_OFF`); `sdmmc_dlyb_phase_tuning()` must call `set_input_ck()` before final `set_cfg()`; `alcor_trigger_data_transfer()` runs only after `AU6601_INT_CMD_END`/`DATA_END`; `sh_mmcif_intr()` clears `MMCIF_CE_INT` with `~(state & mask)` (ORed with `INT_CCS` if `!host->ccs_enable`). [F141, F144, F147, F151, F152]
