# QLOGIC QLA2XXX FC-SCSI DRIVER

This unit implements userspace management, Fibre Channel transport attributes, Block SG (BSG) passthrough, firmware crash dump collection, and diagnostic logging for the QLogic `qla2xxx` Fibre Channel HBA driver across `qla_attr.c`, `qla_bsg.c`, `qla_bsg.h`, `qla_dbg.c`, and `qla_dbg.h`.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- When `bsg_request->msgcode == FC_BSG_RPT_ELS`, `bsg_job->dev` is an `fc_rport` rather than a `Scsi_Host`; `qla24xx_bsg_request()` and `qla2x00_process_els()` must obtain `rport` via `fc_bsg_to_rport(bsg_job)` and NULL-check it before calling `rport_to_shost(rport)` or reading `rport->dd_data`. [F38]
- Callbacks that query or reset adapter-wide link state, firmware link statistics, or post firmware dump uevents (`qla2x00_get_host_port_state`, `qla2x00_get_fc_host_stats`, `qla2x00_reset_host_stats`, `qla2x00_get_priv_stats`, and `*_fw_dump`) must use `base_vha = pci_get_drvdata(ha->pdev)` rather than a potentially virtual `vha`. [F57]
- In `qla_attr.c`, `ha->isp_ops->write_nvram` and `read_nvram` are NULL on ISP81xx/8044/83xx/27xx/28xx, `mpi_fw_dump` is NULL except on ISP27xx/28xx, and `beacon_on`/`beacon_off` are NULL on ISP2100/2200; callers must check the pointer or chip-type macro before invoking them. [F39]
- When `qla2x00_sysfs_write_nvram` or `qla2x00_sysfs_write_vpd` writes hardware via `ha->isp_ops->write_nvram`, it must reload `ha->nvram` or `ha->vpd` via `ha->isp_ops->read_nvram` under `ha->optrom_mutex`, and `qla2x00_sysfs_write_vpd` must also call `ha->isp_ops->get_flash_version`. [F54]
- `ha->queue_pair_map` is a 0-based sparse array of length `ha->max_qpairs` separate from `ha->base_qpair`; loops over `0 <= i < ha->max_qpairs` (`qla2x00_get_fc_host_stats`, `qla2x00_reset_host_stats`, `qla24xx_bsg_timeout`) must skip NULL entries and handle `ha->base_qpair` explicitly. [F77]
- In `qla24xx_proc_fcp_prio_cfg_cmd`, `QLFC_FCP_PRIO_GET_CONFIG` and `QLFC_FCP_PRIO_SET_CONFIG` must reject `payload_len` when `!len || len > FCP_PRIO_CFG_SIZE`, update `ha->flags.fcp_prio_enabled` and `FCP_PRIO_ATTR_ENABLE` in `ha->fcp_prio_cfg->attributes` in lockstep followed by `qla24xx_update_all_fcp_prio(vha)`, and free and NULL `ha->fcp_prio_cfg` if `qla24xx_fcp_prio_cfg_valid` fails. [F61, F79]
- In `qla2x00_process_loopback` on `IS_QLA81XX`/`8031`/`8044`, `qla81xx_get_port_config` must verify `(config[0] & INTERNAL_LOOPBACK_MASK) == 0`, and if `new_config[0]` was set by `qla81xx_set_loopback_mode`, `qla81xx_reset_loopback_mode` must revert it after `qla2x00_loopback_test`. In both `qla81xx_*_loopback_mode` functions, `ha->notify_dcbx_comp`, `ha->notify_lb_portup_comp`, `ha->idc_extend_tmo`, and `ha->flags.idc_compl_status` must be reset to 0 on every exit path. [F62, F63]
- In `qla27xx_dump_mpi_ram` and `qla24xx_dump_ram`, setting `ha->flags.mbox_int = 0` before polling `reg->host_status` must be paired with restoring `ha->flags.mbox_int = 1` and updating `*nxt = ram + i` before any early return; while polling, non-mailbox interrupts (`stat & 0xff` not in `0x1, 0x2, 0x10, 0x11`) must be cleared by writing `HCCRX_CLR_RISC_INT` to `reg->hccr` with a readback flush, and `MBX_INTERRUPT` in `ha->mbx_cmd_flags` must be cleared with `test_and_clear_bit` after each chunk poll. [F66, F100, F134]
- `ql_mask_match` must verify `level != 0` before `(level & ql2xextended_error_logging) == level`; `ql_dbg*`, `ql_dump_regs`, and `ql_dump_buffer` must check `ql_mask_match(level)` before formatting strings or reading MMIO; `ql_ktrace()` must set `pbuf[0] = 0` before any early `break` because callers test `if (!pbuf[0])` on uninitialized stack `pbuf`. [F91, F124, F161]

## Synchronization

- In `qla2x00_process_els`, `qla2x00_process_ct`, `qla24xx_process_bidir_cmd`, and `qlafx00_mgmt_cmd`, the scatterlist DMA mappings, `fcport` fields, and `sp` fields (`type`, `u.bsg_job`, `free`, `done`) must all be initialized before `qla2x00_start_sp` or `qla2x00_start_bidir` publishes `sp` to the request queue. [F18]
- `req->outstanding_cmds` uses 1-based indexing across `[1, req->num_outstanding_cmds)` (slot 0 is unused). In `qla_bsg_found`, after setting stack completion `sp->comp = &comp` and dropping `qpair->qp_lock_ptr` to abort and wait, the timeout/failure path must re-acquire `qpair->qp_lock_ptr`, reverify `req->outstanding_cmds[cnt]` against `bsg_job` before clearing it to NULL, and reset `sp->comp = NULL` before calling `kref_put(&sp->cmd_kref, qla2x00_sp_release)`. [F65, F78]
- `ha->optrom_mutex` must be held whenever reading or modifying `ha->optrom_state`, `ha->optrom_buffer`, `ha->optrom_region_start`, or `ha->optrom_region_size` across sysfs (`qla2x00_sysfs_read_optrom`, `qla2x00_sysfs_write_optrom`, `qla2x00_sysfs_write_optrom_ctl`) and BSG (`qla2x00_optrom_setup`, `qla2x00_read_optrom`, `qla2x00_update_optrom`). [F26]
- In `qla24xx_process_bidir_cmd`, `ha->selflogin_lock` must be held across checking `vha->self_login_loop_id == 0`, initializing `vha->bidir_fcport`, calling `qla2x00_fabric_login`, and storing `vha->self_login_loop_id`. [F28]
- In `qla2x00_dev_loss_tmo_callbk` and `qla2x00_terminate_rport_io`, `fcport->scan_state != QLA_FCPORT_FOUND` must be checked before marking `fcport` dead or scheduling session deletion, and `rport->port_state != FC_PORTSTATE_ONLINE` must be checked under `host->host_lock` before clearing `fcport->rport` and `*rport->dd_data`. [F31]
- All `isp_ops->fw_dump` callbacks in `qla_dbg.c` and their helpers (`qla24xx_soft_reset`, `qla24xx_dump_ram`, `qla27xx_dump_mpi_ram`) require `ha->hardware_lock` held with IRQs disabled and must use `udelay()` and pre-allocated buffers (`ha->fw_dump`, `ha->gid_list`) without sleeping or allocating. [F141]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `qla2x00_bsg_job_done`, all fields of `sp` (including `sp->u.bsg_job`, `sp->comp`, `sp->vha`, and `sp->handle`) must be read before calling `kref_put(&sp->cmd_kref, qla2x00_sp_release)`, because `qla2x00_sp_release` invokes `sp->free(sp)` and frees `sp`. [F1]
- In `qla2x00_bsg_sp_free()`, `sp->fcport` must be freed asynchronously by queueing `sp->fcport->free_work` (`qla2xxx_free_fcport_work`) onto `ha->wq` (never calling `qla2x00_free_fcport` directly in interrupt context), and only when `sp->type` is `SRB_CT_CMD`, `SRB_FXIOCB_BCMD`, or `SRB_ELS_CMD_HST` (`SRB_ELS_CMD_RPT` borrows the rport's `fcport` and `SRB_BIDI_CMD` uses embedded `vha->bidir_fcport`). [F128, F140]
- In `qla2x00_bsg_sp_free` and `qlafx00_mgmt_cmd`, `bsg_job->request_payload` and `bsg_job->reply_payload` scatterlists for `SRB_FXIOCB_BCMD` are mapped and unmapped only when `piocb_rqst->flags` has `SRB_FXDISC_REQ_DMA_VALID` and `SRB_FXDISC_RESP_DMA_VALID` set, respectively. [F5]
- Under `ha->optrom_mutex`, `ha->optrom_buffer` is allocated only when `ha->optrom_state == QLA_SWAITING` (transitioning to `QLA_SREADING` or `QLA_SWRITING`), and every path that frees `ha->optrom_buffer` (or fails `vzalloc`) must set `ha->optrom_buffer = NULL` and reset `ha->optrom_state = QLA_SWAITING` before unlocking `ha->optrom_mutex`. [F8]
- In `qla2x00_sysfs_read_xgmac_stats` and `qla2x00_sysfs_read_dcbx_tlv`, `vha->hw->optrom_mutex` must be acquired before checking whether `ha->xgmac_data` or `ha->dcbx_tlv` is NULL and held across `dma_alloc_coherent`, `memset`, and the mailbox fetch; these buffers remain owned by `ha` across subsequent reads and must not be freed after each read. [F12, F27]
- In `qla24xx_vport_delete`, teardown must strictly order: set `VPORT_DELETE` and wait for `LOOP_RESYNC_ACTIVE` to clear, `qla24xx_disable_vp`, `qla2x00_wait_for_sess_deletion`, `qlt_remove_target`, `fc_remove_host` before `scsi_remove_host`, `qla24xx_deallocate_vp_id` (and decrement `ha->cur_vport_count` / clear `ha->vp_idx_map` under `ha->vport_lock`), `qla2x00_vp_stop_timer`, `qla2x00_free_fcports`, delete `vha->qpair` only if `vha->qpair->vp_idx == vha->vp_idx`, and last `scsi_host_put(vha->host)` (as `vha` is in `shost_priv`). [F14, F22, F58, F127, F152]
- `bin_file_entries[]` in `qla_attr.c` must end with a `{ NULL }` sentinel, and `qla2x00_alloc_sysfs_attr()` and `qla2x00_free_sysfs_attr()` must apply identical hardware-capability predicates (`IS_FWI2_CAPABLE(ha)` for non-zero `iter->type`, `IS_QLA25XX(ha)` for type 2, `IS_CNA_CAPABLE(ha)` for type 3) when iterating it. [F76, F129]

## API Contracts

- `qla24xx_dump_ram()` and `qla27xx_dump_mpi_ram()` take `ram_dwords` in 32-bit dword units and advance `addr` and `*nxt` by dwords (callers with byte sizes like `cram_size` in `qla24xx_dump_memory()` must divide by 4), whereas `qla2xxx_dump_ram()` takes `ram_words` in 16-bit words; Flash Layout Table `ha->flt_region_*` fields are also in 32-bit dwords and must be multiplied by 4 (`<< 2`) when used as byte offsets. [F49, F50]
- When determining active flash regions, `qla27xx_get_active_image()` must be called to populate `active_regions.global`, and on `IS_QLA28XX(ha)` adapters `qla28xx_get_aux_images()` must be called before reading `active_regions.aux` fields (such as `vpd_nvram` in `qla2x00_sysfs_read_nvram()`, `qla2x00_sysfs_read_vpd()`, and `qla2x00_get_flash_image_status()`). [F130]
- In `qla2x00_process_els` (for non-`ELS_AUTH_ELS` commands), both `bsg_job->request_payload.sg_cnt` and `bsg_job->reply_payload.sg_cnt` must be validated to be at most 1 (returning `-ENOBUFS` otherwise) before mapping scatterlists for DMA. [F80]
- `qla2xxx_transport_vport_functions` must stay synchronized with `qla2xxx_transport_functions` for host, rport, starget, and BSG callbacks and `.show_*` flags (and `qla24xx_vport_create` must copy `supported_classes`/`supported_speeds` from `base_vha->host`), except `.vport_create`, `.vport_disable`, and `.vport_delete` must remain NULL on `qla2xxx_transport_vport_functions`. [F59, F151]

## Error Handling

- Because `fc_bsg_host_dispatch` and `fc_bsg_rport_dispatch` call `bsg_job_done()` and overwrite `bsg_reply->result` whenever `.bsg_request` returns non-zero, `qla24xx_bsg_request` handlers must either call `bsg_job_done()` (or queue async completion) and return `0`, or return non-zero without calling `bsg_job_done()`. [F149]
- `qla2x00_start_sp()` returns `0` or a negative errno, whereas `qla2x00_start_bidir()` returns `EXT_STATUS_OK` (`0`) or a positive `EXT_STATUS_*` code; in `qla24xx_process_bidir_cmd`, `req_data_len` and `rsp_data_len` must be initialized before comparing them, and any error before `qla2x00_start_bidir` succeeds must unmap scatterlists, set `vendor_rsp[0]`, call `bsg_job_done` with `DID_OK << 16`, and return `0`. [F11, F33, F45]
- When `qla2x00_start_sp(sp)` fails in `qla2x00_process_els` or `qla2x00_process_ct`, the caller must release `sp` with `qla2x00_rel_sp(sp)` (not `sp->free(sp)` or `kref_put(&sp->cmd_kref, qla2x00_sp_release)`) before jumping to `done_unmap_sg` or `done_free_fcport`; in `qla2x00_process_els`, `done_free_fcport` must call `qla2x00_free_fcport(fcport)` only when `qla_port_allocated` is set. [F2, F4]
- In `qla24xx_bsg_request()`, all BSG commands except `QL_VND_MANAGE_HOST_PORT` and `QL_VND_GET_HOST_STATS` must be rejected when `vha->hw->flags.port_isolated` is set, `qla2x00_chip_is_down(vha)` is true, or `PFLG_DRIVER_REMOVING` is set in `vha->pci_flags`. [F132]
- In `qla2x00_process_loopback`, `new_config` must be zero-initialized via `memset` before the loopback setup branches, and DMA resources (`request_payload` map, `reply_payload` map, `req_data` alloc, `rsp_data` alloc) must be torn down in reverse order via `done_free_dma_rsp`, `done_free_dma_req`, `done_unmap_sg`, and `done_unmap_req_sg`. [F9, F47]
- In `qla84xx_mgmt_cmd()`, `mn` is allocated from `ha->s_dma_pool` first, and `mgmt_b = NULL` and `dma_direction = DMA_NONE` must be initialized before the command switch so that `done_unmap_sg` safely frees `mgmt_b` (if non-NULL) and unmaps the scatterlist (if `dma_direction != DMA_NONE`) before `exit_mgmt` frees `mn`. [F10, F43]
- `ha->isp_ops->write_optrom()` returns `QLA_SUCCESS` (`0`) or a positive `QLA_*` status code; callers (`qla2x00_sysfs_write_optrom_ctl()` and `qla2x00_update_optrom()`) must check its return value and translate any non-zero return into a negative errno (`-EIO` or `-EINVAL`). [F35]
- Sysfs store handlers in `qla_attr.c` must check `kstrtol()` or `sscanf()` return values before reading output variables and reject negative/below-minimum values (`val < 100` in `qla2x00_zio_timer_store`, `val < 0` in `qla_zio_threshold_store`) with `-ERANGE`. [F44, F73]
- In `qla2x00_terminate_rport_io()` and `qla2x00_dev_loss_tmo_callbk()`, if `pci_channel_offline(fcport->vha->hw->pdev)` is true, call `qla_pci_set_eeh_busy()`, wait via `qla2x00_eh_wait_for_pending_commands()`, and return without session deletion or port logout. In `qla24xx_bsg_timeout()` and `qla_bsg_found()`, if `qla2x00_isp_reg_stat(ha)` is non-zero, call `qla_pci_set_eeh_busy(vha)`, and when `ha->flags.eeh_busy` is set, `qla_bsg_found()` must skip `abort_command(sp)` and wait on `sp->comp`. [F138, F139]

## External Contracts

- Option ROM staging in `qla2x00_sysfs_write_optrom_ctl`, `qla2x00_sysfs_write_optrom`, and `qla2x00_optrom_setup` must validate `start` against `ha->optrom_size`, set `ha->optrom_region_size` to the transfer length (clamped to `ha->optrom_size - start`, not `start + size`), and clamp writes to the remaining region; sysfs staging (`val == 2`) is only allowed on ISP2300 (`start == 0`), ISP24xx, or ISP25xx. [F51, F115, F158]

## Known Pitfalls

- `rport->dd_data` points to an inline `fc_port_t *` slot and must be dereferenced as `*(fc_port_t **)rport->dd_data` and NULL-checked, whereas `fc_vport->dd_data` stores `scsi_qla_host_t *` directly. Loops over `vha->vp_fcports` (e.g., `qla2x00_get_starget_*`) must NULL-check `fcport->rport`, and search loops over `vha->vp_fcports` (`qla24xx_iidma`) or `req->outstanding_cmds` (`qla_bsg_found`) must use a `found` boolean rather than testing `!fcport` or `!sp` after the loop. [F37, F98, F99]
- In `qla25xx_fw_dump`, `qla81xx_fw_dump`, and `qla83xx_fw_dump`, `qla25xx_copy_mq` must run at `(void *)ha->fw_dump + ha->chain_offset` before `qla24xx_soft_reset(ha)` and preserve `nxt_chain`/`last_chain` across `qla24xx_dump_memory`. Also, `ha->fw_memory_size` is inclusive (requiring `ha->fw_memory_size - start_addr + 1`), and `__be32` size fields in `ha->fw_dump` and chain headers (`qla24xx_copy_eft`, `qla25xx_copy_fce`, `qla25xx_copy_exlogin`, `qla81xx_copy_exchoffld`) require `ntohl`/`be32_to_cpu` before `memcpy` or pointer advancement. [F68, F85, F87]
- Chip-type pitfalls: in `qla2x00_sysfs_read_nvram` and `qla2x00_sysfs_read_vpd`, `ha->isp_ops->read_optrom` must be skipped when `!IS_NOCACHE_VPD_TYPE(ha)`; in `qla2x00_sysfs_write_reset` (`case 0x2025d`), only `IS_QLA83XX(ha)` may take the `qla83xx_idc_lock` branch while 81xx/27xx/28xx must call `qla81xx_restart_mpi_firmware`; in `ql_dump_regs`, `IS_P3P_TYPE(ha)` must be tested before `IS_FWI2_CAPABLE(ha)` when selecting `mbx_reg`. [F92, F93, F94]
- Data-handling pitfalls: `ha->link_data_rate` holds sparse `PORT_SPEED_*` constants and must be mapped via `port_speed_str` or `switch`, never array indexing; `vha->dport_data` elements are `__le16` and require `le16_to_cpu()`; `ha->fw_attributes_h` and `ha->fw_attributes_ext[]` must be cast to `uint64_t` before shifting by 16/32/48 bits; `sg_copy_from_buffer()` callers must pass the pointer `data` (not `&data`); and `qla2x00_serial_num_show()` must pass `PAGE_SIZE - 1` to `qla2xxx_get_vpd_field()` before `strcat(buf, "\n")`. [F74, F82, F108, F113, F125]
