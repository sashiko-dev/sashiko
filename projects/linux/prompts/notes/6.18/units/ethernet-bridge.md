# ETHERNET BRIDGE

The Linux Ethernet bridge (`net/bridge`) implements 802.1D/802.1Q virtual bridging, port management, frame forwarding, unicast FDB, IGMP/MLD multicast snooping (MDB) with Explicit Host Tracking (EHT), STP/MST state machines, ARP/ND proxy suppression, MRP, CFM, `br_netfilter` L2/L3 hook integration, RTNetlink/ioctl UAPIs, and switchdev hardware offload.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Stack `struct br_ip`, `struct net_bridge_port_group_sg_key`, and `union net_bridge_eht_addr` keys must be zeroed with `memset()` before setting fields, because `br_mdb_rht_params`, `br_sg_port_rht_params`, and EHT RB-tree comparators hash or `memcmp()` the full struct/union size including padding and tail bytes. [F54, F128]
- `br_multicast_new_port_group()` inserts `p->rhnode` into `br->sg_port_tbl` only when `!br_multicast_is_star_g(group)`, so `br_multicast_del_pg()` and `br_multicast_del_port_group()` must guard `rhashtable_remove_fast(&br->sg_port_tbl, ...)` with `!br_multicast_is_star_g()`. [F5]
- In `br_multicast.c`, `pmctx` is `NULL` for host/bridge-originated packets (check `pmctx != NULL` before `pmctx->port`), and `pmctx->vlan` / `brmctx->vlan` are `NULL` for non-VLAN contexts (check `br_multicast_port_ctx_is_vlan(pmctx)` or `br_multicast_ctx_is_vlan(brmctx)` first, including in `br_multicast_ctx_vlan_global_disabled()`). [F47, F48, F104]
- Master `struct net_bridge_vlan` entries without `BRIDGE_VLAN_INFO_BRENTRY` are placeholders; active VLAN/tunnel walks must check `br_vlan_should_use(v)`. In `br_multicast_toggle_vlan()`, also check `br_vlan_is_brentry(vlan)` to toggle the master entry via `br_multicast_toggle_one_vlan()`. [F46, F66]
- `(*,G)` `host_joined` changes must propagate to installed `(S,G)` entries via `br_multicast_star_g_host_state()`, and new `(S,G)` entries must copy `star_mp->host_joined` via `br_multicast_sg_host_state()`. Filter-mode changes or deletions must sync `MDB_PG_FLAGS_STAR_EXCL` port groups via `br_multicast_star_g_handle_mode()`, `br_multicast_sg_add_exclude_ports()`, and `br_multicast_sg_del_exclude_ports()`. [F73, F74, F241]
- Each MDB entry's `mp->ports` list and router port lists must remain sorted in descending order of `struct net_bridge_port` pointer value so `br_multicast_flood()` can merge-scan `lport` and `rport` in one pass. Whenever `BROPT_MULTICAST_ENABLED` changes after init, call `br_mc_disabled_update()`. [F65, F262]
- `br->fdb_n_learned` tracks `BR_FDB_DYNAMIC_LEARNED` entries: increment it in `fdb_create()` only after `rhashtable_lookup_insert_fast()` succeeds, and guard every `atomic_dec(&br->fdb_n_learned)` with `test_and_clear_bit(BR_FDB_DYNAMIC_LEARNED, &fdb->flags)`. [F176]
- Static FDB addresses are refcounted in non-promiscuous ports' unicast filters (`dev_uc_add()`/`dev_uc_del()`): call `fdb_add_hw_addr()`/`fdb_del_hw_addr()` only when `BR_FDB_STATIC` transitions; `br_port_clear_promisc()` must succeed at `br_fdb_sync_static()` before `dev_set_promiscuity(p->dev, -1)` and clearing `BR_PROMISC`; `br_port_set_promisc()` must call `br_fdb_unsync_static()`; and `br_add_if()` must call `br_fdb_sync_static()` (and undo on `err6`) when `!br_promisc_port(p)` and `IFF_UNICAST_FLT` is set. [F177, F222, F223]
- Bridge forwarding helpers (`br_allowed_ingress`, `br_forward`, `br_flood`, `br_multicast_flood`, `__br_forward`) require `skb->data` at `ETH_HLEN` bytes past `skb_mac_header(skb)` (`br_dev_xmit()` pulls `ETH_HLEN`; `br_dev_queue_push_xmit()` pushes `ETH_HLEN` and updates `skb->network_header` past VLAN headers when `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` and `eth_type_vlan(skb->protocol)`). In `__br_forward()`, `nbp_switchdev_frame_mark_tx_fwd_offload()` must precede `br_handle_vlan()`. [F234, F307, F312]
- Every netlink attribute emitted by `br_fill_ifinfo()`, `br_port_fill_attrs()`, `br_fill_info()`, `br_multicast_dump_querier_state()`, or `fdb_fill_info()` must be accounted for in `br_nlmsg_size()`, `br_port_info_size()`, `br_get_size()`, `br_multicast_querier_state_size()`, or `fdb_nlmsg_size()`. [F163, F255]

## Synchronization
- `br->lock` nests outside both `br->hash_lock` and `br->multicast_lock`; never acquire `br->lock` while holding either. Process/workqueue paths acquiring `br->lock`, `br->multicast_lock`, or `br->hash_lock` must use `spin_lock_bh()`; `br_fdb_update()` uses bare `spin_lock(&br->hash_lock)` and requires process callers (`__br_fdb_add()`) to wrap it in `local_bh_disable()` and `rcu_read_lock()`. [F136, F137, F290, F291]
- Never call synchronous timer/work cancellations (`timer_delete_sync()`, `cancel_delayed_work_sync()`) or sleeping helpers (`br_multicast_join/leave_snoopers()`, `br_stp_call_user()`, `port_cost()`) while holding `br->lock` or `br->multicast_lock`. Allocations under `br->lock`, `br->hash_lock`, `br->multicast_lock`, or `rcu_read_lock()` (`br_info_notify()`, `fdb_notify()`, `__br_mdb_notify()`, `br_rtr_notify()`, `br_mdb_get_reply_alloc()`, `br_multicast_list_adjacent()`) must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F18, F143, F145, F146, F292, F294, F295]
- Callers of `br_ip4/6_multicast_mark_router()` and `br_ip4/6_multicast_add_router()` (including PIM and MRD RX handlers) must hold `br->multicast_lock`. `brmctx->ip4/6_querier.seq` must be initialized with `seqcount_spinlock_init(..., &br->multicast_lock)`. [F20, F26]
- In `br_ip4_multicast_igmp3_report()` and `br_ip6_multicast_mld2_report()`, after `br_ip4/6_multicast_add_group()` drops and re-acquires `br->multicast_lock`, revalidate `br_multicast_ctx_should_use(brmctx, pmctx)`, re-lookup `mdst` and `pg`, and check `!(pg->flags & MDB_PG_FLAGS_PERMANENT)`. [F29]
- In multicast expiration timer callbacks, after acquiring `br->multicast_lock`, verify the entry is still linked (`!hlist_unhashed()` or `!RB_EMPTY_NODE()`) and `!timer_pending()`. Similarly, in `br_fdb_cleanup()`/`br_fdb_flush()`, after taking `spin_lock_bh(&br->hash_lock)` on an RCU-walked entry `f`, verify `!hlist_unhashed(&f->fdb_node)` before `fdb_delete()`. [F30, F197]
- Paths dereferencing bridge/port VLAN groups or `p->backup_port` without `rtnl_lock` (`br_multicast_pg_to_port_ctx()`, `br_multicast_port_vid_to_port_ctx()`, `br_multicast_start_querier()`, `br_fill_ifinfo()`, `br_port_fill_attrs()`) must hold `rcu_read_lock()` and use `nbp_vlan_group_rcu()`, `br_vlan_group_rcu()`, or `rcu_dereference(p->backup_port)`. [F28, F138, F140]
- In `fdb_create()`, initialize all fields before `rhashtable_lookup_insert_fast()` and `hlist_add_head_rcu()`. RCU readers of `fdb->dst` (`fdb_fill_info()`, `__fdb_flush_matches()`, `br_fdb_test_addr()`, `br_fill_forward_path()`) must load `READ_ONCE(fdb->dst)` into a local variable and null-check it. Set `IFF_BRIDGE_PORT` in `br_add_if()` only after `netdev_rx_handler_register()` succeeds, and clear it before `netdev_rx_handler_unregister()` in `del_nbp()` and rollback. [F186, F188, F199, F280]

## Object Lifetimes
- Multicast entries removed under `br->multicast_lock` (`net_bridge_mdb_entry`, `net_bridge_port_group`, `net_bridge_group_src`, `net_bridge_group_eht_set`, `net_bridge_group_eht_set_entry`) must unhash/clear their node (`hlist_del_init*` or `RB_CLEAR_NODE`) and defer `timer_shutdown_sync()` and freeing to `br->mcast_gc_list`. `br_multicast_del_port()` must call `flush_work(&br->mcast_gc_work)` before `br_multicast_port_ctx_deinit()` / `free_percpu(port->mcast_stats)`, and `br_multicast_dev_del()` must drain `mcast_gc_list`, `cancel_work_sync(&br->mcast_gc_work)`, and call `rcu_barrier()`. [F6, F10]
- `br_multicast_port_ctx_deinit()` must synchronously stop `ip4/6_mc_router_timer` and then unlink `pmctx` from both router lists via `br_ip4/6_multicast_rport_del()` under `br->multicast_lock`. [F1, F79]
- In `br_multicast_del_eht_host()`, never dereference `eht_host` after `hlist_for_each_entry_safe(set_h, tmp, &eht_host->set_entries, host_list)` because `__eht_del_set_entry()` frees `eht_host` when `set_entries` becomes empty. In `br_multicast_toin()` and `br_multicast_block()`, after `br_multicast_find_del_pg()` deletes `pg`, set `changed = false` and do not dereference `pg`. [F77, F110]
- In `br_add_if()`, errors before `kobject_init_and_add(&p->kobj, ...)` must `kfree(p)` after `netdev_put()`, whereas errors at or after it must release `p` via `kobject_put(&p->kobj)` and never `kfree(p)`. `del_nbp()` must detach all subsystems under RTNL (`nbp_backup_clear()` to zero other ports' `backup_port` links to `p`; `br_netpoll_disable()` clearing `p->np = NULL` before `__netpoll_free()`) before deferring `netdev_put()` and `kobject_put()` to `call_rcu(&p->rcu, destroy_nbp_rcu)`. [F170, F171, F178, F184]
- `br_dev_delete()` must call `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&br->gc_work)` before unregistering `br->dev`, and `br_deinit()` must call `rcu_barrier()` after `unregister_pernet_subsys(&br_net_ops)` and before `br_fdb_fini()`. In `br_mrp_del_impl()` and `mep_delete_implementation()`, stop all `delayed_work` items with `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` before clearing RCU port pointers, unlinking, and freeing via `kfree_rcu()`. [F182, F192]
- `struct nf_bridge_info` records the ingress port by ifindex (`physinif`) in `setup_pre_routing()`, so resume callbacks (`br_nf_pre_routing_finish*`, `br_nf_forward_finish`) must resolve it via `nf_bridge_get_physindev()` and free `skb` if `NULL` (releasing `neigh_release()` on all exit paths in `br_nf_pre_routing_finish_bridge()`, `br_do_proxy_suppress_arp()`, and `br_do_suppress_nd()`). [F2, F12, F181]
- `ip_sabotage_in()` must set `nf_bridge->sabotage_in_done = 1` rather than calling `nf_bridge_info_free(skb)`, preserving `SKB_EXT_BRIDGE_NF` until `br_nf_dev_queue_xmit()` or `br_nf_push_frag_xmit()` calls `nf_bridge_info_free(skb)`. When attaching `br->fake_rtable`, call `skb_dst_drop()` and `skb_dst_set_noref()`. [F3, F150, F155]
- In `br_nf_local_in()`, packets with `BR_INPUT_SKB_CB(skb)->promisc` or non-`PACKET_HOST` packets with `refcount_read(&nfct->use) != 1` must call `nf_reset_ct(skb)` instead of confirming; after `ct_hook->confirm(skb)`, never dereference cached `ct`/`nfct` pointers, and if `NF_VERDICT_MASK` is `NF_STOLEN`, return `NF_STOLEN` immediately without touching `skb`. [F14, F62, F151, F152]

## API Contracts
- Return conventions: `br_multicast_new_group()` and `new_nbp()` return `ERR_PTR` (never `NULL`); `br_multicast_new_port_group()` and `br_multicast_new_group_src()` return `NULL` (never `ERR_PTR`); `__br_multicast_add_group()` returns `ERR_PTR` on error, `NULL` when disabled or `!pmctx`, or a valid pointer; `maybe_deliver()` returns `NULL`, a valid pointer, or `ERR_PTR(err)` (check `IS_ERR(prev)` in `br_flood()`/`br_multicast_flood()`). [F41, F42, F203, F204]
- SKB ownership: `br_forward()`, `br_flood()`, and `br_multicast_flood()` consume/free `skb` when `local_rcv` is `false`, and never when `local_rcv` is `true`. `br_allowed_ingress()` (on `false`) and `br_handle_vlan()` (on `NULL`) free `skb`, whereas `br_multicast_rcv()` does NOT free `skb` on error. `br_stp_rcv()` must `kfree_skb(skb)` on all paths; `br_frame_type.frame_handler` callbacks must not free `skb` when returning `1`; and `br_handle_local_finish()` must return `1`. [F19, F173, F175, F207, F305]
- Preconditions: `br_port_get_rcu(dev)` does not check if `dev` is a bridge port—use `br_port_get_check_rcu(dev)` or `netif_is_bridge_port(dev)` on unverified devices. Exported functions taking `struct net_device *` (`br_multicast_enabled()`, `br_multicast_router()`, `br_device_event()`, `br_fdb_find_port()`, `br_mst_enabled()`, `br_mst_get_info()`, `br_del_bridge()`, `br_ioctl_stub()`) require `netif_is_bridge_master(dev)` before `netdev_priv(dev)`. `br_switchdev_event()` runs in atomic context and must use `br_port_get_rtnl_rcu(dev)`. [F132, F133, F185, F281, F299]
- Conventions: bitops on `fdb->flags` take unshifted `BR_FDB_*` indices, while `fdb_create()`, `br_fdb_update()`, and `net_bridge_fdb_flush_desc` take `BIT(BR_FDB_*)`; `br_port_flag_is_set()` and `br_port_flags_change()` take shifted `BR_*` masks, while `br_opt_get()`/`br_opt_toggle()` take unshifted `BROPT_*` indices; `br_vlan_add()`/`nbp_vlan_add()` must set `*changed = false` and `br_cfm_*_mep_count()` must set `*count = 0` before any return; `br_nf_forward_ip()` must call `nf_bridge_unshare(skb)` before mutating `nf_bridge`; and `nf_bridge_update_protocol(skb)` must precede `nf_bridge_push_encap_header(skb)`. [F56, F57, F68, F153, F219, F220]

## Error Handling
- In `br_multicast_create_eht_set_entry()`, failure to create `eht_host` or `set_h` must only destroy `eht_host` if `hlist_empty(&eht_host->set_entries)` and only delete `eht_set` if `RB_EMPTY_ROOT(&eht_set->entry_tree)`, because `__eht_lookup_create_set/host()` may return pre-existing populated objects. [F7]
- `br_multicast_port_ngroups_inc()` always increments `port->multicast_ctx.mdb_n_entries` first and, when `group->vid` has an enabled VLAN port context, also increments the VLAN context's `mdb_n_entries`, rolling back `port->multicast_ctx` if the VLAN quota fails; `br_multicast_port_ngroups_dec()` must decrement both. [F8]
- Ignore `-EOPNOTSUPP` from `switchdev_port_attr_set()`. In `br_multicast_ipv4/6_rcv()`, `-ENOMSG` / `-ENODATA` from `ip_mc_check_igmp()` / `ipv6_mc_check_mld()` indicate non-IGMP/MLD packets and must return `0` without calling `br_multicast_err_count()`. [F43, F44, F206]
- Rollback & checks: `br_dev_newlink()` must call `br_dev_delete(dev, NULL)` if `br_changelink()` fails; `br_setport()` must restore `p->flags = old_flags` on failure; `br_mdb_add_group_srcs()` (signed `int i`, rolling back `for (i--; i >= 0; i--)`) and `br_mdb_replace_group_srcs()` must delete newly added sources and clear `BR_SGRP_F_DELETE` on failure; `br_get_link_af_size_filtered()` must check `p && vg` for `br_mst_info_size(vg)`; and `br_nf_dev_queue_xmit()` must check `skb_valid_dst(skb)` before fragmentation. [F17, F49, F50, F82, F180, F250]

## Known Pitfalls
- After any call that may reallocate `skb->head` (`pskb_may_pull()`, `ip_mc_may_pull()`, `ipv6_mc_may_pull()`, `br_allowed_ingress()`), reload all pointers into SKB linear data (`eth_hdr`, `arp_hdr`, `ipv6_hdr`, `iph`, `grec`, `h_addr`, `buf`). In `br_stp_rcv()`, call `pskb_may_pull(skb, 32)` and verify `bpdu.message_age <= bpdu.max_age`; in `br_ip6_multicast_mld2_report()`, bounds-check `nsrcs_offset` against `ipv6_transport_len(skb)` and read `grec_nsrcs` via `skb_header_pointer()`; and in `br_nd_send()`, check `!ns->opt[i + 1]` and add `na_olen` to `sizeof(*na)`. [F85, F87, F130, F232, F269, F288]
- Because `br_setlink()` and `br_dellink()` set `br = netdev_priv(dev)` before checking `p = br_port_get_rtnl(dev)`, `br_setlink()`, `br_dellink()`, `br_afspec()`, `br_vlan_info()`, `br_process_vlan_info()`, `br_process_vlan_tunnel_info()`, and `br_info_notify()` must use `p->br` whenever `p` is non-NULL. When `vlan` is a port VLAN entry, `br_multicast_rcv()` must resolve `vlan->brvlan` via `br_vlan_is_master(vlan)`, and MST helpers must read `v->brvlan->msti` rather than `v->msti`. [F106, F131, F270]
- When iterating `mp->ports` via `struct net_bridge_port_group __rcu **pp` to delete entries (`br_multicast_sg_del_exclude_ports()`), do not advance `pp = &p->next` on iterations that call `br_multicast_del_pg(mp, p, pp)`. Use `time_before_eq()`/`time_after()` for jiffies deadlines (`br_fdb_cleanup()` must guard `min(work_delay, this_timer - now)` with `time_after()`), `jiffies_delta_to_clock_t()` in `br_timer_value()`, and `querier->delay_timer` (`!timer_pending()`) for querier delay windows. [F108, F119, F120, F125, F273, F274]
