# ROSE NETWORK LAYER

Implements the amateur radio ROSE (RATS Open Systems Environment) X.25 Packet Layer Protocol over AX.25 link-layer connections (`AF_ROSE` / `PF_ROSE`). It provides `SOCK_SEQPACKET` sockets, virtual network devices (`rose0`..`roseN`), packet routing and virtual circuit switching across AX.25 neighbours, a loopback queue, per-socket and neighbour timers, procfs inspection tables, and sysctl tunables.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `rose_sock->device` and `rose_sock->neighbour` can be `NULL` (on unbound/unconnected sockets or after `rose_kill_by_device()`, `rose_kill_by_neigh()`, `rose_route_frame()`, or `rose_release()`), and `rose_del_route_by_neigh()` sets `rose_route->neigh1` or `neigh2` to `NULL` while leaving the route in `rose_route_list` awaiting `ROSE_CLEAR_CONFIRMATION`. Callers (`rose_sendmsg()`, `rose_info_show()`, `rose_remove_route()`, `rose_route_frame()`, `rose_route_show()`) must null-check these pointers before use. [F39, F41]
- `rose_loopback_neigh` has `dev == NULL` and `loopback == 1`, so functions handling a `struct rose_neigh *` (`rose_transmit_clear_request()`, `rose_transmit_link()`, `rose_loopback_timer()`, `rose_neigh_show()`) must check `neigh->dev` or `neigh->loopback` before dereferencing `neigh->dev` or calling `rose_send_frame()`/`rose_link_up()`. [F38]
- `rose_sock.timer` is multiplexed by `rose->state` in `rose_timer_expiry()` (T1 in `ROSE_STATE_1`, T2 in `ROSE_STATE_4`, T3 in `ROSE_STATE_2`, HB in `ROSE_STATE_3`), so state transitions must stop or restart `rose->timer` and `rose->idletimer`. When a `ROSE_STATE_3` socket sends an ack carrying `rose->vr` (`rose_kick()`, `rose_enquiry_response()`, `rose_heartbeat_expiry()`), it must set `rose->vl = rose->vr`, clear `ROSE_COND_ACK_PENDING`, and call `rose_stop_timer(sk)`. [F55, F56]
- Packet sequence numbers (`vs`, `vr`, `va`, `vl`, `nr`) wrap modulo `ROSE_MODULUS` (8); range validation and ack purging must step modulo `ROSE_MODULUS` via `rose_validate_nr()` and `rose_frames_acked()` rather than relational operators, and `sysctl_rose_window_size` must remain in `[1, ROSE_MODULUS - 1]` (1..7). [F73, F91]
- LCI 0 is reserved for link-level frames and as the failure sentinel of `rose_new_lci()`, while active LCIs are in `[1, sysctl_rose_maximum_vcs]` (mirrored as `ROSE_DEFAULT_MAXVC + 1 - lci_i` on loopback). LCI uniqueness on a `rose_neigh` spans both local sockets (`rose_find_socket()`) and transit routes (`rose_route_free_lci()`); disconnecting a socket on `rose_list` must reset `rose->lci = 0`. [F58, F74]
- Frames queued on `rose_neigh.queue` by `rose_transmit_link()` while `neigh->restarted == 0` already have the 1-byte `AX25_P_ROSE` PID pushed at `skb->data[0]`, so `rose_link_rx_restart()` must pass dequeued frames directly to `rose_send_frame()` without pushing `AX25_P_ROSE` again. [F60]
- When `rose_route_frame()` rewrites the 12-bit LCI in `skb->data[0..1]` to forward a transit frame, it must mask `skb->data[0]` with `0xF0` before ORing `(lci >> 8) & 0x0F` to preserve `ROSE_GFI`, `ROSE_Q_BIT`, and `ROSE_D_BIT`. [F61]
- `rose_route_frame()` and `rose_loopback_timer()` must verify `skb->len >= ROSE_MIN_LEN` before reading the 3-byte header, and for `ROSE_CALL_REQUEST` frames must verify `skb->len > ROSE_CALL_REQ_FACILITIES_OFF` and `skb->data[ROSE_CALL_REQ_ADDR_LEN_OFF] == ROSE_CALL_REQ_ADDR_LEN_VAL` before reading addresses or facilities. [F66]
- In `rose_parse_facilities()`, each iteration of `while (facilities_len >= 3 && *p == 0x00)` must reject `len < 0` and `len >= facilities_len` before subtracting `len + 1` and advancing `p += len + 1`, and sub-parsers must advance by at least 2 bytes. `FAC_NATIONAL_DIGIS` must check `l % AX25_ADDR_LEN == 0` and bound `dest_ndigis`/`source_ndigis` by `ROSE_MAX_DIGIS`; `0xC0` CCITT facilities in `rose_parse_ccitt()` must verify `10 <= l <= 20` and NUL-terminate `callsign[l - 10]` before `asc2ax()`. [F69, F86, F95]
- `rose_node_list` must stay ordered in descending order of prefix `mask` (`rose_add_loopback_node()` inserting `mask == 10` at the head, `rose_add_node()` inserting before `rose_tmpn->mask <= rose_route->mask`). In `rose_rt_ioctl()` (`SIOCADDRT`), `mask > 10` and `ndigis > AX25_MAX_DIGIS` must be rejected before indexing `rose_addr` or copying `digipeaters` into `rose_neigh->digipeat->calls`. [F70, F71, F79]
- While a ROSE `net_device` has `IFF_UP`, its `dev->dev_addr` must have a loopback entry in `rose_node_list` maintained via `rose_add_loopback_node()` and `rose_del_loopback_node()` across `rose_open()`, `rose_close()`, and `rose_set_mac_address()`. In `rose_set_mac_address()`, `rose_del_loopback_node()` must be called on the old `dev->dev_addr` before `dev_addr_set()`, and only after `rose_add_loopback_node()` succeeds. [F50, F51]
- When global `rose_callsign` is non-null, it is registered via `ax25_listen_register()`, and any mutation in `rose_ioctl()` (`SIOCRSSL2CALL`) or `rose_exit()` must call `ax25_listen_release()` on the old callsign first. All ROSE sockets (`rose_create()`), device lookups, and `rose_device_event()` must be restricted to `&init_net` because socket and routing tables are global. [F52, F111]

## Synchronization

- Spinlocks must nest in order `rose_node_list_lock` -> `rose_neigh_list_lock` -> `rose_route_list_lock` -> `rose_list_lock`. At runtime (outside `__exit rose_rt_free()`), callers of `rose_remove_node()`, `rose_remove_neigh()`, and `rose_remove_route()` must hold the respective list lock, and `rose_ftimer_expiry()`/`rose_t0timer_expiry()` must not acquire `rose_neigh_list_lock` or `rose_node_list_lock`. [F29, F106]
- In process context, `lock_sock(sk)` must be acquired before `rose_list_lock` (`rose_bind()`, `rose_connect()`, `rose_release()`, `rose_kill_by_device()`); `rose_kill_by_device()` must batch sockets with `sock_hold()` and drop `rose_list_lock` before `lock_sock(sk)`. Calling `netdev_put(rose->device, &rose->dev_tracker)` in `rose_release()` and `rose_kill_by_device()` requires holding both locks and immediately setting `rose->device = NULL`. [F7, F105]
- Socket timers (`sk->sk_timer`, `rose->timer`, `rose->idletimer`) must be armed via `sk_reset_timer()` and cancelled via `sk_stop_timer()`, and their callbacks (`rose_heartbeat_expiry()`, `rose_timer_expiry()`, `rose_idletimer_expiry()`) must acquire `bh_lock_sock(sk)`, reschedule via `sk_reset_timer()` if `sock_owned_by_user(sk)` is true, call `bh_unlock_sock(sk)` before `rose_destroy_socket(sk)` or return, and call `sock_put(sk)` on every exit path. [F14, F107]
- In `rose_bind()`, `lock_sock(sk)` must be held across the `SOCK_ZAPPED` check, `rose_sock` field and `device` initialization, `rose_insert_socket(sk)`, and `sock_reset_flag(sk, SOCK_ZAPPED)`. In `rose_connect()` and `rose_accept()`, wait loops dropping `release_sock(sk)` before `schedule()` must re-acquire `lock_sock(sk)` before continuing and call `finish_wait()` before `out_release:`. [F18, F19]
- In `rose_rx_call_request()`, a child socket from `rose_make_new()` must have its `lci`, addresses, callsigns, `neighbour`, `device`, `state`, and sequence fields initialized before `rose_insert_socket()`, `skb_queue_head()`, and `rose_start_heartbeat()`. In `rose_add_node()` and `rose_add_loopback_neigh()`, `use`, `queue`, `ftimer`, `t0timer`, and `digipeat` must be initialized before linking `rose_neigh` into `rose_neigh_list`. [F20, F21]
- Allocations in paths reachable from softirq/timer context or under BH-disabled spinlocks (`rose_make_new()`, `rose_rx_call_request()`, `rose_add_node()`, `rose_add_loopback_node()`, `rose_route_frame()`) must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F108]
- `rose_loopback_queue()` must cap `loopback_queue` at `ROSE_LOOPBACK_LIMIT`, and `rose_loopback_timer()` must dequeue at most `ROSE_LOOPBACK_LIMIT` packets per run before rescheduling via `mod_timer()`. Inspecting `sk->sk_receive_queue` with `skb_peek()` in `rose_ioctl()` (`TIOCINQ`) must hold `sk->sk_receive_queue.lock` across the peek and `skb->len` read. Every registered ROSE `net_device` must call `rose_set_lockdep_key()`. [F30, F78, F101]

## Object Lifetimes

- Each `rose_node->neighbour[i]` (`0 <= i < count`) holds both `rose_neigh->use` and `rose_neigh->count` via `rose_neigh_hold()`. Removing a slot or calling `rose_remove_node()` must drop `rose_neigh->count` and call `rose_neigh_put()`; partial removals in `rose_del_node()` or `rose_rt_device_down()` must shift remaining slots down and remove the node if `count` reaches 0. [F1, F54]
- `rose_remove_neigh()` must stop `ftimer` and `t0timer` synchronously with `timer_delete_sync()`, purge `rose_neigh->queue`, and unlink `rose_neigh` from `rose_neigh_list`, and every caller removing a neighbour from `rose_neigh_list` must also call `rose_neigh_put()` to drop the initial list-membership reference (`use == 1`). [F2, F25]
- When `rose_del_route_by_neigh()` drops a matching `neigh1` or `neigh2` on a surviving `struct rose_route`, or when `rose_kill_by_neigh()` / `rose_route_frame()` (on `ROSE_CALL_REQUEST` collision) drops `rose->neighbour`, the pointer must be cleared to `NULL` after `rose_neigh_put()` to prevent double-put during later teardown. [F8, F10]
- Whenever `rose_neigh->ax25` is updated (`rose_send_frame()`, `rose_link_up()`) or released (`rose_link_failed()`), the previous `ax25_cb` reference must be dropped with `ax25_cb_put()`, and `rose_link_failed()` must set `rose_neigh->ax25 = NULL` so `rose_neigh_put()` does not release it again. [F9]
- When assigning `rose->device` in `rose_bind()` or `rose_connect()`, `rose->dev_tracker` must be registered via `netdev_tracker_alloc()` to balance `netdev_put(rose->device, &rose->dev_tracker)` in `rose_release()` and `rose_kill_by_device()`. [F6]
- In `rose_destroy_socket()`, after calling `rose_remove_socket(sk)`, stopping `sk_timer`, `idletimer`, and `timer`, and purging `sk_write_queue`, `ack_queue`, and `sk_receive_queue`, `sock_put(sk)` is called only if `!sk_has_allocations(sk)`; otherwise the caller's reference transfers to `sk->sk_timer` (`rose_destroy_timer`) via `add_timer()` without incrementing `sk_refcnt`. [F15, F24]
- Pending child sockets on a listener's `sk_receive_queue` (`skb->sk` from `rose_rx_call_request()`) must be detached via `skb->sk = NULL` and `sk_acceptq_removed(sk)` before `kfree_skb(skb)` in `rose_accept()`, or marked `SOCK_DEAD` in `ROSE_STATE_0` with `rose_start_heartbeat(skb->sk)` in `rose_destroy_socket()`. [F13]
- `rose_kick()` must pass a clone (`skb_clone()`) to `rose_send_iframe()` while keeping the original `skb` on `rose->ack_queue`. `rose_route_frame()`, `rose_process_rx_frame()`, and `rose_rx_call_request()` must return non-zero if and only if they consumed `skb`, leaving `skb` unfreed for the caller when returning `0`. [F113, F114]

## API Contracts

- `rose_get_neigh()` returns an owned reference to `struct rose_neigh` (via `rose_neigh_hold()`), whereas `rose_find_socket()`, `rose_find_listener()`, and `rose_route_free_lci()` return borrowed pointers. Passing `route_frame == 0` to `rose_get_neigh()` acquires `rose_node_list_lock` and allows non-restarted neighbours whose `ftimer` is not running; `route_frame != 0` skips the lock and requires `restarted != 0`. [F3, F47]
- `rose_dev_first()`, `rose_dev_get()`, and `rose_dev_exists()` must iterate `for_each_netdev_rcu(&init_net, dev)` under `rcu_read_lock()`. `rose_dev_first()` and `rose_dev_get()` acquire a reference via `dev_hold()` before `rcu_read_unlock()`, whereas `rose_ax25_dev_find()` returns an unreferenced `struct net_device *` from `__dev_get_by_name()`. [F5, F109]
- `rose_transmit_link()` and `rose_loopback_queue()` unconditionally consume `skb`, so callers must never free or access `skb` afterwards. `rose_route_frame()` accepts `ax25 == NULL` for packets from `rose_xmit()`, and must check `if (!ax25)` and divert to `rose_loopback_queue()` before dereferencing `ax25`. [F11, F40]
- `rosecmp()` and `rosecmpm()` return `0` on match and `1` on mismatch (where `mask` is `0..10` BCD digits). `rose_find_listener()` must scan `rose_list` for an exact `source_call` match (`!ax25cmp(&rose->source_call, call) && !rose->source_ndigis`) before falling back to a second scan for `null_ax25_address`. [F37, F80]
- `rose_disconnect(sk, reason, cause, diagnostic)` stores `reason` into `sk->sk_err` as a non-negative errno, and treats `-1` for `cause` or `diagnostic` as a sentinel preserving existing values. `rose_parse_ccitt()` and `rose_parse_national()` decode Call Request facilities into the receiver's perspective (`FAC_CCITT_DEST_NSAP` into `source_*`, `FAC_CCITT_SRC_NSAP` into `dest_*`), so `rose_route_frame()` must pair `src_addr` with `facilities.dest_call` and `dest_addr` with `facilities.source_call`. [F46, F62]

## Error Handling

- After `rose_get_neigh()`, any failure in `rose_connect()` before `ROSE_STATE_1` must call `rose_neigh_put(rose->neighbour)` and `dev_put(dev)` (if `rose_dev_first()` succeeded), and both error and success paths in `rose_route_frame()` must reach `put_neigh:` to call `rose_neigh_put(new_neigh)`. [F4, F42]
- `rose_get_neigh()` only writes `*cause` and `*diagnostic` when returning `NULL`. Callers of `rose_parse_facilities()` must `memset()` `struct rose_facilities_struct` to zero beforehand, and `rose_parse_facilities()` must store the return of `rose_parse_national()`/`rose_parse_ccitt()` in a signed `int` and return `0` when `len < 0`. [F36, F43, F44]
- In `rose_proto_init()`, `dev` is stored in `dev_rose[i]` only after `register_netdev(dev)` succeeds; on failure, `free_netdev(dev)` is called before `fail:` unwinds `dev_rose[--i]` and `proto_unregister(&rose_proto)`. [F17]

## External Contracts

- `rose_getname()`, `rose_recvmsg()`, and `rose_sendmsg()` must `memset()` `struct full_sockaddr_rose` to zero before populating `srose_digis` or copying `struct sockaddr_rose`. In `rose_bind()` and `rose_connect()`, `addr_len` must match either sockaddr size, unsigned `srose_ndigis` must not exceed `1` or `ROSE_MAX_DIGIS` respectively, and `source_ndigis + srose_ndigis` must not exceed `ROSE_MAX_DIGIS`. [F45, F65]
- `rose_setsockopt()` must check `ROSE_T1`/`ROSE_T2`/`ROSE_T3`/`ROSE_HOLDBACK` against `UINT_MAX / HZ` and `ROSE_IDLE` against `UINT_MAX / (60 * HZ)` before converting to jiffies. `rose_ioctl()` requires `capable(CAP_NET_ADMIN)` for `SIOCADDRT`, `SIOCDELRT`, `SIOCRSCLRRT`, and `SIOCRSSL2CALL`, and `rose_bind()`/`rose_connect()` must resolve `ax25_findbyuid(current_euid())`. [F117, F122]

## Known Pitfalls

- Despite its name, `rose_route_free_lci(lci, neigh)` returns the existing `struct rose_route *` when `(lci, neigh)` is in use and `NULL` when the LCI is free. [F34]
- In `rose_rt_device_down()`, `rose_clear_routes()`, and `rose_rt_free()`, the list cursor is advanced to `->next` at the start of each iteration, so all body accesses must use the saved pointer (`t`, `s`, or `u`). In `rose_rt_device_down()`, removing matching entries from `t->neighbour[]` must iterate in reverse from `t->count - 1` down to `0` and decrement `t->count` before `memmove(&t->neighbour[i], &t->neighbour[i + 1], sizeof(t->neighbour[0]) * (t->count - i))`. [F76, F77, F100]
- In `rose_del_route_by_neigh()`, the two-endpoint removal branch calling `rose_remove_route()` (when both `neigh1` and `neigh2` match `rose_neigh` or the other is `NULL`) must precede the single-endpoint nulling checks and advance `rose_route` via saved `s = rose_route->next` before `continue`. [F82]
- In `rose_parse_national()`, `FAC_NATIONAL_DIGIS` sets `fac_national_digis_received = 1`, and `FAC_NATIONAL_DEST_DIGI` / `FAC_NATIONAL_SRC_DIGI` must remain guarded by `!fac_national_digis_received`. In `rose2asc()` and `ax2asc()`, callers must pass a buffer of at least 11 bytes (`rose2asc()` masking `char` bytes with `& 0xFF`) and pass separate scratch buffers (`rsbuf` and `buf`) when formatting both in one `seq_printf()` call. [F83, F93, F94]
