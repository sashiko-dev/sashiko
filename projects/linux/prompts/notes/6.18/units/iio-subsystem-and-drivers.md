# IIO SUBSYSTEM AND DRIVERS

This unit comprises Industrial I/O (IIO) accelerometer drivers (`drivers/iio/accel/` for MMA9553L, MSA311, MXC4005/MXC6255, SCA3000/SCA3300, Samsung SSP, `st_accel`, and STK8312/STK8BA50) and ADC drivers (`drivers/iio/adc/` for Aspeed, AT91 with integrated touchscreen, AXP20x/AXP22x/AXP717/AXP813/AXP288, Broadcom iProc, Berlin2, and CC10001), managing MEMS sensors and SoC/PMIC converters over I2C, SPI, sensorhub, MMIO, and regmap/syscon interfaces.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct sca3000_state` (`rx`, `tx`) and `struct sca3300_data` (`txbuf`, `rxbuf`), SPI DMA transfer buffers must remain at the end of the struct after driver state fields (`spi`, `lock`, `chip`) and be marked `__aligned(IIO_DMA_MINALIGN)`. Broken by: adding fields after the buffers or removing `__aligned(IIO_DMA_MINALIGN)`. [F161, F162]
- Scan structs passed to `iio_push_to_buffers_with_ts()` (`mxc4005_data`, `stk8312_data`, `stk8ba50_data`, `msa311_buffer_thread()`) must declare timestamps as `aligned_s64` (or use `IIO_DECLARE_BUFFER_WITH_TS` as in `sca3300_trigger_handler()`) for 8-byte alignment on 32-bit architectures; dynamic `u16 *` buffers in `cc10001_update_scan_mode()` (`adc_dev->buf`) and `at91_adc_configure_trigger()` (`st->buffer`) must be sized to `indio_dev->scan_bytes`, not channel count times `sizeof(u16)`. [F128, F130]
- In `at91_adc_channel_init()` and `cc10001_adc_channel_init()`, `for_each_set_bit()` over the channel bitmap must use `bit` only for `chan->channel = bit` while advancing a dense counter `idx` (`0..idx-1`) for `chan_array[idx]`, `chan->scan_index`, and the trailing `IIO_TIMESTAMP` slot; therefore, `at91_adc_trigger_handler()`, `at91_adc_configure_trigger()`, and `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` must map each bit in `active_scan_mask` via `channels[bit].channel` before accessing hardware. [F86, F110]
- In `at91_adc.c`, `st->touchscreen_type` separates mutually exclusive IIO trigger/buffer state (`!st->touchscreen_type`, using `st->trig`) from input touchscreen state (`st->touchscreen_type != 0`, using `st->ts_input`); when non-zero, `at91_adc_channel_init()` must mask `CHAN_MASK_TOUCHSCREEN_4WIRE` or `CHAN_MASK_TOUCHSCREEN_5WIRE` out of `st->channels_mask`, `at91_adc_probe()` must skip `at91_adc_buffer_init()` and `at91_adc_trigger_init()`, and rollback and `at91_adc_remove()` must branch on `!st->touchscreen_type`. [F46, F76]
- Bus `drvdata` and `iio_trigger_set_drvdata()` store `struct iio_dev *` (requiring `iio_priv()`), whereas in `at91_adc.c` `input_set_drvdata(input, st)` stores `struct at91_adc_state *` directly on `st->ts_input` for `atmel_ts_open()` and `atmel_ts_close()`. Broken by: omitting `iio_priv()` on bus/trigger drvdata or calling `iio_priv(input_get_drvdata(dev))` in `atmel_ts_open()`/`atmel_ts_close()`. [F135]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_get_op_mode()` and `sca3300_set_op_mode()` map hardware `SCA3300_REG_MODE` values to a `0..chip->num_avail_modes - 1` index via `avail_modes_table`, which must be mapped via `accel_scale_map`, `incli_scale_map` (when `angle_supported`), or `freq_map` (all sized to `num_avail_modes` in `sca3300_chip_tbl[]`) before indexing `accel_scale`, `incli_scale`, or `freq_table`; `sca3300_set_frequency()` must pick a mode matching the frequency while preserving `accel_scale`. [F70, F89]
- In `aspeed_adc.c`, `aspeed_adc_vref_config()` must run before `aspeed_adc_probe()` reads `ASPEED_ADC_BAT_SENSING_DIV` (bit 6 of `ASPEED_ADC_REF_VOLTAGE` in `ASPEED_REG_ENGINE_CONTROL`) to set `data->battery_mode_gain` (setting `data->battery_mode_gain.div` to `1` or `2` whenever `data->battery_sensing` is true, as `aspeed_adc_read_raw()` divides by it unchecked), and later writes must preserve those bits; `aspeed_adc_set_sampling_rate()` must set `data->sample_period_ns` before `aspeed_adc_compensation()`. [F80, F81, F120]
- In `mma9553.c`, any pedometer configuration change via `mma9553_set_config()` must update the shadow field in `data->conf` and pulse `MMA9553_MASK_CONF_CONFIG` in `MMA9553_REG_CONF_CONF_STEPLEN`, polling until hardware clears the bit; in `mxc4005.c`, because `mxc4005_resume()` issues `MXC4005_REG_INT_CLR1_SW_RST`, `mxc4005_set_trigger_state()` must cache `MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK1` in `data->int_mask1` and `mxc4005_suspend()` must cache `MXC4005_REG_CONTROL` in `data->control` for `mxc4005_resume()`. [F60, F63]

## Synchronization

- In `msa311.c`, callers must invoke `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` before acquiring `msa311->lock` and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` after releasing it because `msa311_runtime_resume()` and `msa311_runtime_suspend()` acquire `msa311->lock`; in `msa311_irq_thread()`, `msa311->lock` must be released before `iio_trigger_poll_nested(msa311->new_data_trig)` because it synchronously runs `msa311_buffer_thread()` on the same thread, which acquires `msa311->lock`. [F24, F167]
- In `mma9553.c`, `mma9553_runtime_resume()` must not acquire `data->mutex`, because `mma9551_read_accel_chan()`, `mma9553_write_raw()`, and `mma9553_write_event_config()` call `mma9551_set_power_state(data->client, true)` (synchronously running `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()`) while holding `data->mutex`. [F25]
- In `sca3000.c`, `st->lock` must be held by callers of `sca3000_read_data_short()`, `sca3000_read_data()`, `sca3000_write_reg()`, `sca3000_read_ctrl_reg()`, and `sca3000_write_ctrl_reg()` (sharing `st->tx`/`st->rx` and control-register unlock/select sequences), and callers of `sca3000_read_data_short()` must copy `st->rx[0]` before dropping `st->lock` or reusing `st->rx`; however, callers must not hold `st->lock` across `sca3000_ring_int_process()` or `__sca3000_hw_ring_state_set()`, which lock `st->lock` internally. [F19, F20, F65, F66]
- In `sca3000.c`, `st->mo_det_use_count` must be updated in `sca3000_motion_detect_set_state()` and checked in `sca3000_read_raw()` under `st->lock` (rejecting `IIO_ACCEL` raw reads with `-EBUSY` when non-zero), incrementing only when an axis bit in `SCA3000_REG_CTRL_SEL_MD_CTRL` transitions `0 -> 1` and decrementing only on `1 -> 0` after `sca3000_write_ctrl_reg()` succeeds. [F5, F21]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_read_reg()` and `sca3300_write_reg()` must hold `sca_data->lock` while populating `sca_data->txbuf` and calling `sca3300_transfer()`, and must unlock `sca_data->lock` before calling `sca3300_error_handler()`, which acquires `sca_data->lock` itself. [F23]
- In `at91_adc.c`, `at91_adc_probe()` must write `AT91_ADC_CR` (`AT91_ADC_SWRST`) and `AT91_ADC_IDR` (`0xFFFFFFFF`) before `devm_request_irq()` because `st->wq_data_avail` and `st->ts_input` are initialized later; when `iio_buffer_enabled(idev)` is true, `handle_adc_eoc_trigger()` must call `disable_irq_nosync(irq)` before `iio_trigger_poll(idev->trig)` and `at91_adc_trigger_handler()` must read `AT91_ADC_LCDR` before `enable_irq(st->irq)`. [F29, F30]
- Raw conversion waits must hold their lock across setup, waiting, and unconditional IRQ/channel masking on timeout or signal interruption: `at91_adc_read_raw()` holds `st->lock` across setting `st->chnb`, waiting on `st->wq_data_avail`, disabling `AT91_ADC_CHDR`/`AT91_ADC_IDR`, and resetting `st->done = false`; `iproc_adc_do_read()` holds `adc_priv->mutex` across `adc_priv->chan_id`, `adc_priv->completion`, and masking `IPROC_INTERRUPT_MASK`; `berlin2_adc_read()` and `berlin2_adc_tsen_read()` share `priv->lock`, mask their IRQ bit, and reset `priv->data_available = false`. [F31, F34, F35]

## Object Lifetimes

- In drivers with manual `.remove()` (`sca3000_remove`, `stk8312_remove`, `stk8ba50_remove`, `mma9553_remove`, `at91_adc_remove`, `axp20x_remove`, `iproc_adc_remove`), `iio_device_unregister()` must run first before tearing down triggers, buffers, maps, IRQs (`sca3000_stop_all_interrupts()` before `free_irq()`), runtime PM, clocks, or power. Broken by: using `devm_iio_device_register()` with manual `.remove()`, or tearing down resources before `iio_device_unregister()`. [F4, F6, F9, F15, F144]
- In `at91_adc.c`, `at91_adc_allocate_trigger()` must call `iio_trigger_free(trig)` on `iio_trigger_register()` failure, and `at91_adc_trigger_init()` rollback (starting at `i--` while `i >= 0`) and `at91_adc_trigger_remove()` must call both `iio_trigger_unregister()` and `iio_trigger_free()`; `at91_ts_register()` `err:` must pass local `input` (not `st->ts_input`) to `input_free_device()`, while `at91_ts_unregister()` calls only `input_unregister_device(st->ts_input)`. [F12, F13, F108]
- In `at91_adc_configure_trigger()`, enabling (`state == true`) must `kmalloc` `st->buffer` before enabling trigger, channels, and `AT91_ADC_IER`, and disabling (`state == false`) must disable `AT91_ADC_IDR`, trigger, and `AT91_ADC_CHDR` before `kfree(st->buffer)`. [F14]
- In `cc10001_adc.c`, because `cc10001_update_scan_mode()` frees and reallocates `adc_dev->buf`, `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` must reload `data = adc_dev->buf` on every trigger invocation; when `!adc_dev->shared`, `cc10001_adc_power_up(adc_dev)` in `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` and `cc10001_adc_read_raw_voltage()` must be paired with `cc10001_adc_power_down(adc_dev)` on all exit paths (including `CC10001_INVALID_SAMPLED`), whereas when `adc_dev->shared` is true power is managed only at probe/remove. [F18, F74]
- In `axp288_adc_read_raw()`, when `info->ts_enabled` is true and reading `AXP288_GP_ADC_H`, `axp288_adc_set_ts()` must under `info->lock` switch `AXP288_ADC_TS_PIN_CTRL` to `AXP288_ADC_TS_CURRENT_ON_ONDEMAND` (waiting `usleep_range(6000, 10000)`) before `axp288_adc_read_channel()` and unconditionally restore `AXP288_ADC_TS_CURRENT_ON` afterward regardless of read error. [F16, F156]
- In `apply_acpi_orientation()` (`st_accel_core.c`), once `acpi_evaluate_object(..., "_ONT", NULL, &buffer)` succeeds, every path must `goto out;` to `kfree(buffer.pointer)`, and `adata->mount_matrix.rotation` must store only static string literals; in `aspeed_adc_set_trim_data()` and `berlin2_adc_probe()`, `struct device_node *` pointers from `of_find_node_by_name()` or `of_get_parent()` must be dropped via `of_node_put()` right after `syscon_node_to_regmap()` and on earlier error returns. [F7, F146]

## API Contracts

- Every IIO triggered-buffer poll handler (`msa311_buffer_thread`, `mxc4005_trigger_handler`, `sca3300_trigger_handler`, `stk8312_trigger_handler`, `stk8ba50_trigger_handler`, `at91_adc_trigger_handler`, `cc10001_adc_trigger_h`) must call `iio_trigger_notify_done(indio_dev->trig)` on all exit paths, including errors; in `st_accel_allocate_ring()` and `msa311_buffer_thread()`, the poll handler must not check hardware data-ready status registers (which belongs in the IRQ thread with `.validate_device`) so external triggers work. [F175, F176]
- In `stk8312_read_raw()`, `stk8ba50_read_raw()`, `mxc4005_read_raw()`, and `cc10001_adc_read_raw()`, `IIO_CHAN_INFO_RAW` must check `iio_buffer_enabled(indio_dev)` and return `-EBUSY` before reading or toggling device power/mode. [F180]
- In `st_accel`, `st_accel_common_probe()`, `apply_acpi_orientation()`, and `st_accel_allocate_ring()` must pass `indio_dev->dev.parent` (not `&indio_dev->dev`) to `ACPI_COMPANION()`, `iio_read_mount_matrix()`, and `devm_iio_*()`; `apply_acpi_orientation()` must return non-zero when ACPI is disabled, `_ONT` is absent, or `_ONT` is invalid so `st_accel_common_probe()` falls back to `iio_read_mount_matrix()`; and `st_accel_read_raw()`/`st_accel_write_raw()` must split/recombine `gain` via `/ 1000000` and `% 1000000`. [F40, F95, F116, F174]

## Error Handling

- Roll back prior state on failure: in `mma9553_write_event_config()`, if `mma9553_conf_gpio(data)` fails when `state == true`, reset `event->enabled = false` and call `mma9551_set_power_state(data->client, false)` under `data->mutex`; in `st_accel_buffer_postenable()`, if `st_sensors_set_enable(indio_dev, true)` fails, restore `st_sensors_set_axis_enable(indio_dev, ST_SENSORS_ENABLE_ALL_AXIS)`; in `stk8312_set_interrupts()`, `stk8312_set_sample_rate()`, and `stk8312_set_range()`, after entering `STK8312_MODE_STANDBY`, restore `stk8312_set_mode(data, mode)` on all paths. [F3, F8, F10]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_transfer()` must return `-EINVAL` only when `SCA3300_MASK_RS_STATUS` equals `SCA3300_VALUE_RS_ERROR` (and `-EIO` for SPI/CRC failures) while still populating `*val`; `sca3300_read_reg()` and `sca3300_write_reg()` must route `ret == -EINVAL` to `sca3300_error_handler()`, which treats `-EINVAL` on reading `SCA3300_REG_STATUS` as the expected status-clearing response and returns `0`. [F38, F104]
- Check non-standard return values: `regulator_get_voltage()` in `cc10001_adc_read_raw()` and `devm_regulator_get_enable_read_voltage()` in `aspeed_adc_vref_config()` return positive microvolts on success (check `ret < 0`, excluding `-ENODEV` in `aspeed_adc_vref_config()` to fall back to internal vref); `cc10001_adc_poll_done()` and `cc10001_adc_read_raw_voltage()` return `u16` with sentinel `CC10001_INVALID_SAMPLED` (`0xffff`); `sca3000_read_ctrl_reg()` returns an 8-bit value or `< 0`, and `sca3000_reg_lock_on()` returns `< 0` on error or `1` when locked. [F41, F42, F43, F100, F105]

## Known Pitfalls

- Zero-initialize (`= { }`) stack scan buffers in `sca3300_trigger_handler()` and `msa311_buffer_thread()` before `iio_push_to_buffers_with_ts()` to prevent kernel stack leaks; in `mxc4005_read_xyz()`, `stk8312_trigger_handler()`, and `stk8ba50_trigger_handler()`, bound bulk reads into `data->scan.chans` to the channel array size (`sizeof(data->scan.chans)`, `STK8312_ALL_CHANNEL_SIZE`, `STK8BA50_ALL_CHANNEL_SIZE`) rather than `sizeof(data->scan)` to avoid overwriting padding and timestamps. [F39, F127]
- In `stk8ba50_set_power(data, mode)`, `mode == true` (`STK8BA50_MODE_SUSPEND = 1`) suspends and `false` (`STK8BA50_MODE_NORMAL = 0`) powers on; in `mxc4005_chip_init()` and `mxc4005_set_trigger_state()`, write-only `MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK0`/`MASK1` must be initialized to `0` and disabling DRDY must write `0` (not `~MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK1_BIT_DRDYE`); in `st_accel_sensors_settings`, `.bdu` for `LIS3L02DQ_ACCEL_DEV_NAME` must remain empty; and in `st_accel_common_probe()`, pass local `pdata` (not `parent->platform_data`) to `st_sensors_init_sensor()`. [F56, F115, F139, F152]
- When converting left-justified 16-bit raw accelerometer words in `msa311_read_raw_data()`, `mxc4005_read_raw()`, `sca3000_read_raw()`, and `stk8ba50_read_raw()`, right-shift by `chan->scan_type.shift` before calling `sign_extend32(..., chan->scan_type.realbits - 1)`; in `sca3000_read_event_value()`, `at91_adc_channel_init()`, and `cc10001_adc_channel_init()`, variables whose addresses are passed to `for_each_set_bit()` or `bitmap_weight()` (`ret`, `st->channels_mask`, `channel_map`) must be `long` or `unsigned long`. [F126, F138]
