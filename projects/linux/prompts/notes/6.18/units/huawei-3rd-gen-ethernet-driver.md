# HUAWEI 3RD GEN ETHERNET DRIVER

The `hinic3` driver supports Huawei 3rd Generation Intelligent Network Interface Card PCIe Virtual Functions (VFs). A low-level hardware layer (`hinic3_lld`, `hinic3_hwdev`) manages MMIO BARs, MSI-X vectors, event queues (AEQ/CEQ), mailboxes (MBOX), and command queues (CMDQ), exposing service devices over the Linux auxiliary bus to the upper-level NIC driver (`hinic3_nic_driver`), which implements `net_device` operations, NAPI TX/RX rings, `page_pool` RX buffers, RSS, and hardware offloads.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct cmdq_wqe`, `struct cmdq_header` must remain at offset 0 of every union member because `cmdq_wqe_fill` casts WQEs to `struct cmdq_header *` and copies the body at `dst + 1`; since `wqe_lcmd` and `wqe_scmd` place `status` and `ctrl` at different offsets, functions handling both (`clear_wqe_complete_bit`, `cmdq_prepare_wqe_ctrl`) must branch on `CMDQ_DATA_SGE` (`CMDQ_WQE_HDR_GET(header_info, DATA_FMT)`). [F37, F113]
- In `hinic3_cmdq_ceq_handler`, `cmdq_read_wqe` does not advance `wq->cons_idx`, so every `switch` branch must either call `clear_wqe_complete_bit` or `return`; `HINIC3_CMD_TYPE_FORCE_STOP` must take the `default` branch so `cmdq_clear_cmd_buf` and `clear_wqe_complete_bit` run only after `CMDQ_WQE_COMPLETED(ctrl_info)` is true (with remaining `cmd_info->buf_in` freed via `hinic3_cmdq_reset_all_cmd_buf` in `hinic3_cmdqs_free` / `hinic3_reinit_cmdq_ctxts`). [F3, F86]
- `recv_mbox_handler` must validate segments with `mbox_segment_valid` (`seq_id <= MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL`, `seg_len <= MBOX_SEG_LEN`, and `seg_len <= MBOX_LAST_SEG_MAX_LEN` when `seq_id == MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL`) before copying into `msg_desc->msg`, and reset `msg_desc->seq_id` to `MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL` (not `0`) on error or channel init. [F66]
- Across `hinic3_send_one_skb`, `hinic3_set_wqe_combo`, and `hinic3_tx_poll`, `tx_info->wqebb_cnt` for an SKB with `num_sge` fragments must equal `num_sge + !!offload`; in `hinic3_set_wqe_combo`, `*curr_pi` must retain the first WQEBB index (`ctrl_bd0`) for `hinic3_get_and_update_sq_owner` and `txq->tx_info[pi]`, while task/SGE allocations use `&tmp_pi` and `hinic3_write_db` uses `hinic3_get_sq_local_pi(txq->sq)`. [F48, F93]
- `hinic3_alloc_txqs_res` must allocate `tqres->bds` with `sq_depth * HINIC3_BDS_PER_SQ_WQEBB + HINIC3_MAX_SQ_SGE` entries because `txq->tx_info[pi].dma_info` points to `&tqres->bds[pi * HINIC3_BDS_PER_SQ_WQEBB]` and `hinic3_tx_map_skb` indexes `dma_info[0 .. nr_frags]` linearly without wrapping at `sq_depth`. [F71]
- In `hinic3_rx_poll`, `hinic3_fetch_rx_buffer` and `packaging_skb` only advance `rxq->cons_idx` and `rxq->delta` by `sge_num` and clear `rx_info->page = NULL` (since `rx_alloc_mapped_page` is a no-op when `rx_info->page` is non-NULL) if `napi_alloc_skb` succeeds; `rx_cqe->status` (statically bound at `rxq->cqe_arr[i]` by `rq_associate_cqes`) must be cleared to `0` only after `recv_one_pkt` succeeds, and LRO packets break out of the poll loop once `num_wqe >= nic_dev->lro_replenish_thld`. [F50, F51, F91]
- `hinic3_wq->prod_idx` and `cons_idx` must remain unmasked `u16` counters wrapping only at 65536 because `hinic3_wq_get_used` subtracts them without masking by `wq->idx_mask`; `hinic3_wq_free_wqebbs`, `hinic3_alloc_rx_buffers`, and `hinic3_rx_fill_buffers` must always reserve 1 unused entry (`- 1`) so a full queue never wraps `used` to 0 or equates masked indices. [F97, F98]

## Synchronization

- Lock acquisition must follow outer-to-inner order with BH disabled on the outer lock: `hwdev->channel_lock` or `aeqs->aeq_lock` before `mbox->mbox_lock`, and `hwdev->channel_lock` or `ceqs->ceq_lock` before `cmdq->cmdq_lock`. [F19]
- `hwdev->mbox` (`hinic3_init_mbox`) and `hwdev->cmdqs` (`hinic3_cmdqs_init`) must be initialized before `hinic3_aeq_register_cb` or `hinic3_ceq_register_cb` (which also call `spin_lock_init` unlocked and must only run during single-threaded init); in `hinic3_comm_mbox_free` and `hinic3_comm_cmdqs_free`, `HINIC3_HWDEV_MBOX_INITED` and `HINIC3_HWDEV_CMDQ_INITED` in `hwdev->func_state` must be cleared under `spin_lock_bh(&hwdev->channel_lock)` before unregistering callbacks and freeing `mbox` or `cmdqs`. [F18, F25, F141]
- `aeq_event_handler` and `ceq_event_handler` invoke `aeqs->aeq_cb` and `ceqs->ceq_cb` while holding `spin_lock_bh(&aeqs->aeq_lock)` or `spin_lock_bh(&ceqs->ceq_lock)`, so callbacks must not sleep; conversely, `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt`, `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt_no_ack`, and `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` acquire mutexes or sleep on completions/polls and may only be called from process context. [F136, F137]
- `mbox->mbox_send_lock` must be held continuously across `mbox->send_msg_id` update, DMA queue preparation (`mbox_prepare_dma_msg`), segment transmission (`send_mbox_msg`), response polling (`wait_mbox_msg_completion`), and copying `msg_desc->msg` to `msg_params->buf_out`; before writing segment data in `send_mbox_seg`, `clear_mbox_status` must zero `*send_mbox->wb_vaddr` and execute `wmb()`. [F20, F59]
- In `cmdq_sync_cmd_direct_resp` and `wait_cmdq_sync_cmd_completion`, `curr_prod_idx` indexes `cmdq->cmd_infos` and the WQE `PI` field while `next_prod_idx` is passed to `cmdq_set_db`; timeout handling must dereference `done` and `cmpt_code` via `saved_cmd_info` because `cmdq_sync_cmd_handler` clears those pointers in `cmd_info`. [F94]
- In `cmdq_wqe_fill`, the WQE body (`dst + 1`) must be copied followed by `wmb()` before publishing the header containing `HW_BUSY_BIT` via `WRITE_ONCE(*dst, *src)`; in `clear_wqe_complete_bit`, `ctrl->ctrl_info` and `cmd_type` must be cleared followed by `wmb()` before advancing `cons_idx` via `hinic3_wq_put_wqebbs`. [F27]
- In `aeq_irq_handler`, `hinic3_cmdq_ceq_handler`, `hinic3_tx_poll`, and `hinic3_rx_poll`, a read barrier (`dma_rmb()` or `rmb()`) must separate reading the hardware completion indicator (`WRAPPED`, `HW_BUSY_BIT`, `hinic3_get_sq_hw_ci`, or `RXDONE`) from reading the descriptor payload, and `hinic3_add_rx_frag` must call `page_pool_dma_sync_for_cpu` before reading received packet data. [F26, F147]

## Object Lifetimes

- `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` does not consume the caller's `buf_in` reference: `cmdq_sync_cmd_direct_resp` calls `refcount_inc(&buf_in->ref_cnt)` for `cmd_info->buf_in`, and the caller must still release its initial reference from `hinic3_alloc_cmd_buf` via `hinic3_free_cmd_buf`. [F1]
- In `hinic3_add_one_adev`, once `auxiliary_device_init(&hadev->adev)` succeeds, `hadev` is owned by the device refcount (`hinic3_comm_adev_release`) and must be freed via `auxiliary_device_uninit(&hadev->adev)`, never direct `kfree(hadev)`. [F4]
- `init_eq` must leave each queue's MSI-X vector disabled (`HINIC3_MSIX_DISABLE`) until `hinic3_aeqs_init` or `hinic3_ceqs_init` initializes all queues; vector ownership transfers from `hinic3_comm_aeqs_init`/`hinic3_comm_ceqs_init` to `hinic3_aeqs_free`/`hinic3_ceqs_free` only when `hinic3_aeqs_init`/`hinic3_ceqs_init` returns 0 (on error, the caller frees them with `hinic3_free_irq`). In `remove_eq`, the driver must disable MSI-X, call `free_irq`, cancel `eq->aeq_work` (`disable_work_sync`), zero the hardware queue length, and disarm CI before `hinic3_queue_pages_free`. [F5, F118, F119]
- In `struct hinic3_wq`, `wq_block_vaddr` aliases `qpages.pages[0].align_vaddr` when `hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)` is true, so `wq_free_pages` must verify `!hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)` before calling `dma_free_coherent` on `wq_block_vaddr`. [F38]
- In `hinic3_add_rx_frag` and `packaging_skb`, after `page_pool_dma_sync_for_cpu`, every RX buffer page must either be copied into linear SKB data and released via `page_pool_put_full_page` or attached via `skb_add_rx_frag` with `skb_mark_for_recycle(skb)`, setting `rx_info->page = NULL`. [F156]
- In `hinic3_close`, `hinic3_vport_down` must first call `netif_tx_disable`, disable the vport (`hinic3_set_vport_enable(..., false)`), flush TXQs (`hinic3_flush_txqs`), wait 100ms, and call `hinic3_flush_qps_res`; next, `hinic3_close_channel` must disable NAPI (`qp_del_napi`) and free QP IRQs (`hinic3_qps_irq_uninit`); finally, `hinic3_uninit_qps` must populate `qp_params` from `nic_dev->nic_io` before `hinic3_free_channel_resources` frees `txqs_res`, `rxqs_res`, `irq_cfg`, and QPs. [F23, F24, F61, F122]

## API Contracts

- `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` returns `0` whenever transport and response length checks (`le16_to_cpu(msg_desc->msg_len) == msg_params->expected_out_size`) succeed even if firmware sets a non-zero status in `struct mgmt_msg_head`, so callers expecting a response must check both the return value and `head.status` / `msg_head.status`. [F30, F67]
- Before calling `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp`, callers must zero-initialize all fields (including reserved fields), set `cmd_buf->size` (`cpu_to_le16`), swap `cmd_buf->buf` via `hinic3_cmdq_buf_swab32`, and check `if (err || out_param)` for ucode errors; in `init_sq_ctxts` and `init_rq_ctxts`, each pass batches `<= HINIC3_Q_CTXT_MAX` (31) queues into `CMDQ_BUF_SIZE` and must rewrite `cmdq_hdr` and all contexts before `hinic3_cmdq_buf_swab32`. [F32, F40, F76, F88]
- `hinic3_alloc_irqs` returns `0` whenever `*act_num > 0` even if fewer than `num` IRQs were allocated, so callers must clamp to `*act_num`; in `hinic3_config_num_qps`, if `hinic3_qp_irq_change` returns `alloc_num_irq < q_params->num_qps`, the fallback `hinic3_qp_irq_change` call must pass `max_t(u16, cur_num_irq, q_params->num_qps)` so existing IRQs in `nic_dev->num_qp_irq` are not freed. [F33, F95]
- OS IRQ helpers (`hinic3_free_irq`, `request_irq`, `free_irq`) take the OS IRQ vector (`msix_entry.vector` / `irq_id`), whereas hardware MSI-X helpers (`hinic3_set_msix_state`, `hinic3_set_msix_auto_mask_state`, `hinic3_msix_intr_clear_resend_bit`, `hinic3_set_interrupt_cfg_direct`) take `msix_entry.entry` (`msix_entry_idx`); in `hinic3_set_msix_state`, `HINIC3_MSIX_ENABLE` is `0` and `HINIC3_MSIX_DISABLE` is `1`. [F42, F84]
- Because each 16-byte software RQ WQE (`struct hinic3_rq_wqe`) spans two 8-byte hardware RQ WQEBBs, software RQ indices and depths must be shifted left by `HINIC3_NORMAL_RQ_WQE` (`1`) when passed to `hinic3_write_db`, `hinic3_rq_prepare_ctxt`, and `hinic3_set_root_ctxt`. [F41]
- Before accessing indirect EQ registers (`HINIC3_CSR_AEQ_CTRL_0_ADDR`, `HINIC3_CSR_AEQ_CTRL_1_ADDR`, `EQ_PROD_IDX_REG_ADDR`, `EQ_HI_PHYS_ADDR_REG`, `EQ_LO_PHYS_ADDR_REG`) or `HINIC3_CSR_DMA_ATTR_TBL_ADDR`, callers must write `eq->q_id` to `HINIC3_EQ_INDIR_IDX_ADDR(eq->type)` or the entry index to `HINIC3_CSR_DMA_ATTR_INDIR_IDX_ADDR`. [F43]
- For `struct hinic3_dma_addr_align`, queue accessors and descriptors must use `align_vaddr`, `align_paddr`, and `qpages->page_size`, whereas freeing must pass `real_size`, `ori_vaddr`, and `ori_paddr` via `hinic3_dma_free_coherent_align`. [F108]
- The `size` argument to `hinic3_get_comm_features`, `hinic3_set_comm_features`, `hinic3_comm_features_nego`, and `hinic3_feature_nego` is the count of `u64` elements (`<= COMM_MAX_FEATURE_QWORD`, 4), not a byte size; `hinic3_test_support` requires all bits in `feature_bits` to be set (`(feature_cap & feature_bits) == feature_bits`). [F85, F110]
- In `hinic3_tx_map_skb` and `hinic3_tx_unmap_skb`, `dma_info[0]` maps linear `skb->data` with `dma_map_single`/`dma_unmap_single`, whereas `dma_info[1..nr_frags]` map paged fragments with `skb_frag_dma_map`/`dma_unmap_page`; in `hinic3_tx_csum`, encapsulated `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` packets not destined to `VXLAN_OFFLOAD_PORT_LE` must fall back to `skb_checksum_help(skb)` and return `0` without setting `INNER_L4_EN`. [F146, F153]

## Error Handling

- In `hinic3_alloc_rxqs_res`, `page_pool_create` returns an `ERR_PTR` on failure, so `rqres->page_pool` must be checked with `IS_ERR` and jump to `err_free_cqe` without calling `page_pool_destroy`. [F29]
- Every command struct passed to `mgmt_msg_params_init_default` and `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` must be zero-initialized (`= {}`) with `in_size` aligned to `MBOX_XOR_DATA_ALIGN` (4 bytes), because `mbox_prepare_dma_entry` XOR-hashes all `in_size` bytes and `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` leaves `buf_out` unmodified on transport error while callers inspect `head.status`. [F31, F65]
- In `hinic3_send_one_skb`, `skb_pad` frees `skb` on failure and must jump to `err_out`, whereas later pre-transmit errors must jump to `err_drop_pkt` (`dev_kfree_skb_any`); because `hinic3_set_wqe_combo` advances `txq->sq->wq.prod_idx` and toggles `txq->sq->owner` before DMA mapping, both must be saved beforehand and restored if `hinic3_tx_map_skb` fails, and `netif_subqueue_sent` must only be called after `hinic3_tx_map_skb` succeeds (paired with `netif_subqueue_completed_wake` in `hinic3_tx_poll` and `netdev_tx_reset_subqueue` in `hinic3_flush_txqs`). [F35, F36, F154]

## External Contracts

- Inline mailbox headers/payloads written to `send_mbox->data` must be 32-bit byte-swapped per dword via `mbox_dword_write`, and AEQ payloads in `aeqe_pos->aeqe_data` must be swapped via `swab32_array` in `aeq_event_handler`; when sending to `MBOX_MGMT_FUNC_ID` in `send_mbox_msg`, the driver must use `mbox_prepare_dma_msg` (`MBOX_MSG_DATA_DMA`) whenever `MBOX_COMM_F_MBOX_SEGMENT` is not set in `hwdev->features[0]`. [F64, F126]
- In `hinic3_sq_prepare_ctxt` and `hinic3_rq_prepare_ctxt_get_wq_info`, the first WQE page address is right-shifted by `WQ_PAGE_PFN_SHIFT` (12) whereas `wq->wq_block_paddr` is right-shifted by `WQ_BLOCK_PFN_SHIFT` (9), unlike `cmdq_init_queue_ctxt` which shifts both by 12 (`CMDQ_PFN`) and packs each CMDQ's 1-level CLA table at `CMDQ_WQ_CLA_SIZE` (512B) offsets in `cmdqs->wq_block_vaddr`. [F73, F74]
- In `hinic3_write_db` and `cmdq_set_db`, the 16-bit producer index `pi` is split so its lower 8 bits select the MMIO offset (`(pi & 0xFF) << 3`) within the doorbell page while its upper 8 bits (`pi >> 8`) go into the doorbell payload written via `writeq`; the SQ CI table `nic_io->ci_vaddr_base` allocates `HINIC3_CI_Q_ADDR_SIZE` (64B) per queue, with `HINIC3_CI_PADDR(nic_io->ci_dma_base, q_id)` right-shifted by 2 (`>> 0x2`) in `hinic3_init_qp_ctxts`. [F75, F81]
- `hinic3_send_one_skb` and `hinic3_tx_offload` must pad frames `< MIN_SKB_LEN` (32), drop non-offload 1-SGE frames with `skb->len > HINIC3_COMPACT_WQEE_SKB_MAX_LEN` (16383), encode TSO payload offset in 2-byte words (`offset >> 1`) into `SQ_CTRL_QUEUE_INFO_PLDOFF_MASK` while dropping frames with `PLDOFF > SQ_CTRL_MAX_PLDOFF` (221), clamp extended WQE `MSS` to `>= HINIC3_TX_MSS_MIN` (80) in `hinic3_prepare_sq_ctrl`, and overwrite `offload = HINIC3_TX_OFFLOAD_INVALID` (not `|=`) on offload error. [F78, F83, F158]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `hinic3_tso`, `skb_cow_head(skb, 0)` must be called before sampling network or transport header pointers (`skb_network_header`, `skb_transport_header`, inner headers) into `ip.hdr`/`l4.hdr` and mutating `l4.udp->check` or `l4.tcp->check`. [F56]
- Because `hinic3_queue_pages` DMA pages are not virtually contiguous, multi-fragment SQ WQEs must obtain buffer descriptors via `hinic3_wq_get_multi_wqebbs` and switch from `wqe_combo->bds_head` to `wqe_combo->bds_sec2` when fragment index `i` reaches `wqe_combo->first_bds_num` in `hinic3_tx_map_skb`. [F70]
- Because `hinic3_get_sq_hw_ci` and `hinic3_get_sq_local_ci` return `u16` indices masked to `wq->idx_mask`, `hinic3_tx_poll` must mask their difference with `txq->q_mask` (`((hw_ci - sw_ci) & txq->q_mask) < tx_info->wqebb_cnt`) to handle `hw_ci` wrap-around. [F99]
- 64-bit hardware descriptor masks used with `CMDQ_CTXT_SET` and `MBOX_MSG_HEADER_SET`/`MBOX_MSG_HEADER_GET` must use `GENMASK_ULL` and `BIT_ULL`, and jiffies polling loops (such as `wait_cmdqs_enable`) must use `time_before(jiffies, end)`. [F106, F107]
