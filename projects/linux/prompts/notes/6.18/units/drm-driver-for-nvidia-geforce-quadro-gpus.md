# DRM DRIVER FOR NVIDIA GEFORCE/QUADRO GPUS

This unit implements the Nouveau DRM driver for NVIDIA GPUs across three layers: `nvhw` (hardware register/class definitions and DRF bitfield macros), `nvkm` (core hardware abstraction for devices, subdevices, engines, MMU/VMM, VBIOS, and GSP firmware), and the upper DRM/GEM/TTM/KMS/HMM layer (`nouveau_*`) bridged via the `nvif` client/object/pushbuffer interface.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- The order of `NVKM_LAYOUT_ONCE` and `NVKM_LAYOUT_INST(type, data, ptr, cnt)` entries in `core/layout.h` defines the forward construction/`preinit`/`init` and reverse `fini`/`dtor` order of `nvkm_device::subdev`; `cnt` must exceed the highest bit set in any chip's `nvkm_device_chip.<ptr>.inst` mask, and `NVKM_LAYOUT_ONCE` subdevices must only set `.inst = 1`. [F49, F85]
- `PUSH_WAIT(push, size)` reserves `size` `u32` words and may call `push->wait` to wrap the ring and reset `bgn`, `cur`, and `end`; callers must reserve the exact word count and never cache `push->cur` or `push->bgn` across `PUSH_WAIT`. In `nouveau_channel_wait()` and `nouveau_channel_kick()`, `chan->dma.cur` (`u32` word index) must be updated by adding `push.cur - push.bgn` before `RING_SPACE()` or `FIRE_RING()`, and `nouveau_channel_wait()` must recompute `push.{bgn,cur,end}` after `RING_SPACE()`. [F35, F37, F358, F417]
- In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, `bind_lock_validate()` must validate all backing GEM BOs before `bind_link_gpuvas()` links new `DRM_GPUVA_OP_MAP`/`DRM_GPUVA_OP_REMAP` mappings, and `nouveau_uvmm_bo_validate()` must first reset evicted placement via `nouveau_bo_placement_set(nvbo, nvbo->valid_domains, 0)`. `nouveau_uvmm_bo_unmap_all()` and `nouveau_uvmm_bo_map_all()` must call `drm_gpuva_invalidate(va, true/false)` in lockstep with `nouveau_uvma_unmap()`/`nouveau_uvma_map()`. [F159, F161, F162]
- `uvmm->region_mt` indexes `struct nouveau_uvma_region` using inclusive bounds `[addr, addr + range - 1]` (while `reg->va` stores `addr` and `range`), and any non-sparse `OP_MAP` intersecting a sparse region must be completely enclosed within `[reg->va.addr, reg->va.addr + reg->va.range)` or rejected with `-ENOSPC`. Every `struct nouveau_job` submitted to `cli->sched` (`VM_BIND`) must be a `struct nouveau_uvmm_bind_job` (`bind_validate_map_sparse()` downcasts via `to_uvmm_bind_job()`). [F182, F183, F229]

## Synchronization

- `nvkm_chan_get_chid()` and `nvkm_chan_get_inst()` return non-NULL `struct nvkm_chan *` with `chan->cgrp->lock` held and IRQs disabled in `*irqflags`, and `nvkm_fifo_pause(fifo, &flags)` acquires `spin_lock_irqsave(&fifo->lock, flags)`; callers must not sleep and must release via `nvkm_chan_put(&chan, irqflags)` or `nvkm_fifo_start(fifo, &flags)` on all paths. [F16, F17]
- `nvkm_event_func` (`nvkm_event_ntfy::func`), `struct nvkm_event_func::init`/`fini`, and `nvkm_inth_func` (`nvkm_subdev_func::intr`) run in atomic/hardirq context under `event->list_lock`, `event->refs_lock`, or `device->intr.lock` and must never sleep. `nvkm_event_ntfy::func` must not call `nvkm_event_ntfy_add()`/`del()` (or `nvkm_event_ntfy_allow()`/`block()`/`nvif_event_allow()`/`block()` when `wait == true`), and `nvkm_inth_func` must not call `nvkm_inth_allow()` or `nvkm_intr_allow()`/`block()`/`rearm()`/`unarm()` on the same device. [F18, F92, F93]
- GSP callbacks (`nvkm_gsp_msg_ntfy_func` and `nvkm_gsp_event_func`) run under `gsp->cmdq.mutex` (and `gsp->client_id.mutex` for events), so they must not synchronously issue GSP RPCs (`nvkm_gsp_rpc_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_alloc_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_free`) or mutate `client->events` (`nvkm_gsp_event_dtor()`). [F19]
- On `struct nvkm_vmm`, mutexes nest as `vmm->mutex.vmm` -> `vmm->mutex.ref` / `vmm->mutex.map` -> `vmm->mmu->mutex`. Callers looking up or splitting a `struct nvkm_vma` under `vmm->mutex.vmm` before `nvkm_memory_map()` (`nvkm_vmm_map()`) must set `vma->busy = true` and unlock `vmm->mutex.vmm` first, relocking to clear `vma->busy = false` on error. [F20, F95]
- In `nouveau_ttm_fault()`, when `ttm_bo_vm_fault_reserved()` returns `VM_FAULT_RETRY` and `!(vmf->flags & FAULT_FLAG_RETRY_NOWAIT)`, return directly without calling `dma_resv_unlock(bo->base.resv)`; all other exit paths after `ttm_bo_vm_reserve()` succeeds must unlock `bo->base.resv`. [F108]
- In `nouveau_job_submit()`, `sched->mutex` must be held continuously across `job->ops->submit()`, `list_add(&job->entry, &sched->job.list.head)`, `drm_sched_job_arm()`, `job->ops->armed_submit()`, and `drm_sched_entity_push_job(&job->base)`, and `job->state = NOUVEAU_JOB_SUBMIT_SUCCESS` must be set before `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`. In `armed_submit`, `job->done_fence` must be added via `drm_gpuvm_exec_resv_add_fence()` before `drm_gpuvm_exec_unlock()`. [F123, F124, F131]
- Operations on `uvmm->base` or `uvmm->region_mt` require `uvmm->mutex` (`nouveau_uvmm_lock()`), acquired outside any GEM `dma_resv` lock (`obj->resv`), while `drm_gpuva_link()`, `drm_gpuva_unlink()`, `drm_gpuvm_bo_obtain()`, and `drm_gpuvm_bo_put()` require holding `obj->resv`. `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()` holds `uvmm->mutex` across op preparations, `drm_exec` locking/validation, and `bind_link_gpuvas()`, whereas `nouveau_exec_job_submit()` holds `uvmm->mutex` across `drm_gpuvm_exec_lock(vme)` and drops it before `drm_gpuvm_exec_validate(vme)`. [F126, F127, F259, F325]
- In `nouveau_svm_fault()`, acquire `mmget_not_zero(mm)` before faulting `svmm->notifier.mm`; in `nouveau_range_fault()` and `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, drop `mmap_read_lock(mm)` before acquiring `svmm->mutex` and hold `svmm->mutex` across `mmu_interval_read_retry()` and `nvif_object_ioctl()`. In `nouveau_svmm_invalidate_range_start()` and `nouveau_svm_range_invalidate()`, check `mmu_notifier_range_blockable()` before `mutex_lock(&svmm->mutex)` and ignore `MMU_NOTIFY_MIGRATE`/`MMU_NOTIFY_EXCLUSIVE` events owned by `svmm->vmm->cli->drm->dev`. [F135, F277, F278, F281]
- `nouveau_abi16_get()` returns with `cli->mutex` held on success; every caller receiving a non-NULL `struct nouveau_abi16 *` must release it via `nouveau_abi16_put(abi16, ret)` on all return paths. `drm->client_mutex` must serialize `nvif_client_ctor()`/`dtor()` in `nouveau_cli_init()`/`fini()` and `nvif_mem_ctor_type()`/`nvif_mem_dtor()` in `nouveau_mem_host()`, `nouveau_mem_vram()`, and `nouveau_mem_fini()`. [F138, F303, F330]
- `nouveau_fence.channel` (`__rcu`) is cleared under `fctx->lock` in `nouveau_fence_signal()`; readers outside `fctx->lock` must use `rcu_read_lock()` (or `unrcu_pointer(fence->channel)` in `nv04_fence_emit()`), and `nouveau_fence_context_del()` must cancel `uevent_work`, call `nouveau_fence_context_kill()`, destroy `fctx->event`, set `fctx->dead = 1`, and call `synchronize_rcu()` before `nouveau_channel_del()` frees `struct nouveau_channel`. [F267, F298, F314]
- In `struct nouveau_dmem`, `dmem->mutex` protects `dmem->chunks` while `dmem->lock` spinlock protects `dmem->free_pages` and `chunk->callocated`; `nouveau_dmem_page_alloc_locked()` must drop `dmem->lock` before calling `nouveau_dmem_chunk_alloc()`. In `nouveau_dmem_migrate_to_ram()`, hold `svmm->mutex` across `nouveau_svmm_invalidate()` and `nouveau_dmem_copy_one()`, and call `nouveau_dmem_fence_done(&fence)` before `dma_unmap_page()` in all DMEM migration/eviction paths. [F323, F324, F354, F459]

## Object Lifetimes

- `nvkm_kmap()` and `nvkm_done()` must bracket all uses of `nvkm_ro32()`, `nvkm_wo32()`, `nvkm_mo32()`, `nvkm_wo64()`, `nvkm_robj()`, `nvkm_wobj()`, and `nvkm_fill()` on a `struct nvkm_memory` or `struct nvkm_gpuobj`. [F1]
- When `nvkm_gsp_rpc_push()` or `nvkm_gsp_rpc_rd()` succeeds with `NVKM_GSP_RPC_REPLY_RECV` and `repc > 0`, free the returned reply buffer with `nvkm_gsp_rpc_done()`. When `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_push(obj, &ctrl, repc)` is called with `repc > 0` (passing `&ctrl` as `void *argv`), `r535_gsp_rpc_rm_ctrl_push()` stores the reply in `*argv` even when `rpc->status != 0`, so `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_done(obj, ctrl)` must be called on both success and error paths. [F3, F4, F71]
- In `nouveau_gem_new()` and `nouveau_gem_prime_import_sg_table()`, `drm_gem_object_init()` and `drm_gem_gpuva_init()` must precede `nouveau_bo_init()`. If `drm_gem_object_init()` fails, call `drm_gem_object_release(&nvbo->bo.base)` and `kfree(nvbo)`; once `nouveau_bo_init()` is called, it consumes ownership of `nvbo` even on failure (via `nouveau_bo_del_ttm()`), and `nouveau_gem_object_del()` must only call `ttm_bo_put(&nvbo->bo)`. [F105, F106, F107, F238, F313]
- In `nouveau_uvmm_ioctl_vm_init()`, `drm_gpuvm_init()` takes ownership of `uvmm` and its own reference on `r_obj` (so `drm_gem_object_put(r_obj)` is called immediately, and later failures must call `drm_gpuvm_put(&uvmm->base)` rather than `kfree(uvmm)`). `op_map_prepare()` calls `nouveau_uvma_gem_get(uvma)`, so `op_map_prepare_unwind()`, `nouveau_uvmm_sm_cleanup()`, and `nouveau_uvmm_fini()` must call `nouveau_uvma_gem_put(uvma)` before `nouveau_uvma_free(uvma)`. [F117, F118]
- In `nouveau_svmm_init()`, `__mmu_notifier_register(&svmm->notifier, current->mm)` transfers ownership of `svmm` to `mmu_notifier` (`nouveau_svmm_free_notifier()`), so `nouveau_svmm_fini()` must set `svmm->vmm = NULL` under `svmm->mutex` and call `mmu_notifier_put(&svmm->notifier)` rather than `kfree(svmm)`. In `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, the folio from `make_device_exclusive()` must be released with `folio_unlock(folio)` and `folio_put(folio)` both on `mmu_interval_read_retry()` loop retry and after `nvif_object_ioctl()`. [F111, F112, F133]

## API Contracts

- Implementations of `nvkm_object_func.dtor`, `nvkm_subdev_func.dtor`, `nvkm_engine_func.dtor`, `nvkm_device_func.dtor`, and `nvkm_memory_func.dtor` must return the outer heap allocation pointer for the core destructor to pass to `kfree()` (or `NULL` if embedded) and must not `kfree()` that pointer while returning non-NULL. [F11]
- `nvif_unpack(r, d, s, m, vl, vh, x)` and `nvif_unvers(r, d, s, m)` only inspect the payload when incoming status `r == -ENOSYS` (so callers must initialize `ret = -ENOSYS`), and `nvif_unvers` must be passed the dereferenced member `args->vn` (not pointer `args`). Passing `x = false` to `nvif_unpack` rejects trailing bytes after `sizeof(m)` with `-E2BIG` and zeroes `*d`/`*s`, whereas `x = true` advances `*d` and decrements `*s` by `sizeof(m)`. [F25, F43, F74]
- `nvkm_mask`, `nvif_mask`, and `DRF_MR`/`NVIF_MR32` require pre-shifted mask and value arguments and return the old 32-bit register value (unserialized MMIO read-modify-writes), whereas `DRF_MV`/`NVIF_MV32` take an unshifted field value and return the old unshifted field value; `DRF_WV`/`NVIF_WV32` and `DRF_WD`/`NVIF_WD32` overwrite the entire register, clearing all other bits to zero. [F32, F33, F88]

## Error Handling

- `nvkm_engine_ref()`, `nvkm_firmware_load()` (whose firmware descriptor list `l` must end with `.load == NULL`), and `u_memcpya()` return an `ERR_PTR()` on failure and must be checked with `IS_ERR()`; never pass an `IS_ERR()` pointer to `u_free()`, and always free valid `u_memcpya()` buffers with `u_free()` (not `kfree()`). [F12, F28, F141, F286, F375]
- `nvkm_nsec`/`usec`/`msec`, `nvkm_wait_nsec`/`usec`/`msec`, and `nvif_nsec`/`usec`/`msec` return a signed `s64` (elapsed ns on `break`, or `-ETIMEDOUT` on timeout), and `nvkm_rdi2cr()`/`nv_rd16i2cr()` return the unsigned register value in `int` or `-EIO`; callers must store the result in a signed type, check `< 0` for error, and exit polling loops via `break` on success. [F27, F30, F59]
- NVIF constructors (`nvif_object_ctor()`, `nvif_vmm_ctor()`, `nvif_mmu_ctor()`, `nvif_mem_ctor_type()`) must initialize `object.client = NULL` and owned sub-pointers to `NULL` before any fallible step. When `nvif_object_sclass_get()` returns `>= 0` (including `0`), callers must release `*psclass` via `nvif_object_sclass_put()`. [F10, F121, F150]
- In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, failures inside `list_for_each_op(op, &bind_job->ops)` must jump to `unwind_continue` (stepping `op` back via `list_prev_op(op)`), whereas failures after that loop must set `op = list_last_op(&bind_job->ops)` and jump to `unwind` (reversing ops via `list_for_each_op_from_reverse`, freeing and zeroing `op->ops` and `op->reg`, and unlocking `uvmm` and `vme`). [F116, F194]
- Return value translations: `dma_resv_wait_timeout()` and `dma_fence_wait_timeout()` return a `long` that must map `0` to `-EBUSY` and `> 0` to `0`; `nouveau_job_ops.run` returns `ERR_PTR(0)` (`NULL`) on synchronous completion without a fence, so `nouveau_job_run()` must check `IS_ERR(fence)`; and `pm_runtime_get_sync()` callers must treat `>= 0` and `-EACCES` as success and still call `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` on other negative errors. [F140, F144, F312, F331, F334]

## External Contracts

- `struct nvif_ioctl_v0` and `struct nvif_ioctl_mthd_v0` are copied from userspace in `nouveau_abi16_ioctl()`, where each subhandler must verify and subtract header size from `argc` before casting `data[]`, and `nvif_object_mthd()`/`nvif_object_ctor()` must check `check_add_overflow(sizeof(*args), size, ...)` before `kmalloc()`. In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()`, `validate_init()` overwrites `bo[i].user_priv` with a kernel pointer, so only `bo[i].presumed` (never the whole `bo[i]`) may be copied back to userspace. [F99, F176, F232, F373]

## Known Pitfalls

- DRF bitfield definitions in `nvhw/drf.h` must be unparenthesized `HI:LO` token pairs with `0 <= LO <= HI < 64` for single-word `DRF_*`/`NVVAL*`/`NVDEF*` macros, and `NVVAL_GET`/`NVVAL_TEST`/`NVDEF_TEST` require `o` to be a 64-bit type when `DRF_LO(drf) >= 32`. Multi-word fields used with `NVVAL_MW_GET`/`NVVAL_MW_SET`/`NVDEF_MW_SET` must be wrapped in `MW(hi:lo)`, span at most two adjacent elements of `o` (element width `< 64` bits), and never pass a `v` expression that reads from `o` or has side effects. [F41, F50, F53, F63, F64, F65, F75]
- Safe downcasting and iteration: check `nouveau_local_fence(fence)` and verify `rcu_dereference(f->channel)` under `rcu_read_lock()` before `to_nouveau_fence(fence)`; check `bo->destroy == nouveau_bo_del_ttm` before `nouveau_bo(bo)` in TTM notification callbacks (`nouveau_bo_move_ntfy()`); skip `va == &uvmm->base.kernel_alloc_node` before `uvma_from_va(va)` in `drm_gpuvm_for_each_va*` loops; and use `nouveau_for_each_non_mst_connector_iter` before `nouveau_connector(connector)`. [F230, F337, F338, F391]
