# INTEL WWAN IOSM DRIVER

The Intel WWAN IOSM (IPC over Shared Memory) driver provides PCIe host communication for Intel XMM 7360 and 7560 cellular modems, implementing shared-memory descriptor rings, IP multiplexing (MUX Lite and Aggregation), WWAN network interfaces and control ports, devlink flashing/coredumps, and relayfs/debugfs tracing.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Shared-memory message ring slots in `ipc_protocol_free_msg_get()`, ACB SKBs in `ipc_mux_acb_alloc()`, and recycled UL ADB SKBs in `ipc_mux_ul_skb_alloc()` must be explicitly zeroed (`memset()` on `msg`, `MUX_MAX_UL_ACB_BUF_SIZE` at `skb->data`, or `adbh`/`adgh`/`qlth_skb->data` plus resetting `ul_adb->dg`, `dg_count[]`, and `qlt_updated[]`) because `ipc_pcie_alloc_skb()` and `skb_trim(skb, 0)` leave stale data in untouched fields. [F55, F56, F57]
- `ipc_mux->ul_adb.pp_qlt[]` buffers are only allocated when `ipc_mux->protocol != MUX_LITE`, and union accesses in `union mux_type_cmdh`, `union mux_type_header`, and `struct mux_adb` (`adgh` vs `adbh`) must be discriminated by `ipc_mux->protocol == MUX_LITE`. When `mux_protocol == MUX_AGGREGATION` on `IPC_MEM_IP_CHL_ID_0`, `ipc_imem_wwan_channel_init()` must override `ul_nr_of_entries`, `dl_nr_of_entries`, and `dl_buf_size` in `struct ipc_chnl_cfg` before `ipc_imem_channel_init()`. [F51, F64]
- Downlink MUX decoders (`ipc_mux_dl_fcth_decode()`, `ipc_mux_dl_adgh_decode()`, `mux_dl_adb_decode()`) must verify `if_id < IPC_MEM_MUX_IP_SESSION_ENTRIES`, clamp with `array_index_nospec()`, and check `ipc_mux->session[if_id].wwan != NULL`. In `mux_dl_adb_decode()` and `mux_dl_process_dg()`, verify `le16_to_cpu(adth->table_length) >= sizeof(struct mux_adth)` before subtracting it, and verify each `le32_to_cpu(dg->datagram_index)` lies in `[sizeof(struct mux_adbh), le32_to_cpu(adbh->block_length))`. [F52, F81, F82]
- In `ipc_protocol_ul_td_send()` and `ipc_protocol_pipe_cleanup()`, read the tail index from `pipe->old_tail` rather than `p_ap_shm->tail_array[pipe->pipe_nr]`, and in `ipc_protocol_pipe_cleanup()` sample `head_array[pipe->pipe_nr]` and `pipe->old_tail` to free in-flight SKBs in `pipe->skbr_start` before zeroing `head_array` and `tail_array`. [F69, F100]
- In `ipc_imem_dl_skb_process()`, `IPC_CTYPE_CTRL` packets must check `port_id == IPC_MEM_CTRL_CHL_ID_7` and `ipc_is_trace_channel(ipc_imem, port_id)` before the `else` branch calling `wwan_port_rx(ipc_imem->ipc_port[port_id]->iosm_port, skb)`, because `ipc_port[]` entries are `NULL` for flash and trace channels. In `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()`, skip `WWAN_PORT_XMMRPC` on `INTEL_CP_DEVICE_7560_ID`, skip `WWAN_PORT_MBIM` on `INTEL_CP_DEVICE_7360_ID`, and assign `ipc_imem->mux->wwan = ipc_imem->wwan` after `ipc_imem_wwan_channel_init()`. [F66, F92, F121]
- In `ipc_coredump_get_list()`, bound `cd_table->list.num_entries` to `1..IOSM_NOF_CD_REGION` and cap each `entry[i].size` to `devlink->cd_file_info[i].default_size` because `ipc_coredump_collect()` allocates `default_size` bytes and reads `actual_size` bytes into it. [F78]

## Synchronization

- Callbacks queued to `ipc_task_queue_send_task()` run in softirq tasklet context (`ipc_task_queue_handler()`), so they must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and never sleep, and atomic/tasklet code must never call `ipc_protocol_msg_send()` or `ipc_task_queue_send_task(..., true)` (use `ipc_protocol_tq_msg_send()` or `ipc_imem_msg_send_feature_set(..., true)`). `ipc_imem_handle_irq()` must defer devlink/MUX/WWAN/port/debugfs init and deinit to `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()`, and `ipc_uevent_send()` must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and defer `kobject_uevent_env()` to `ipc_uevent_work()`. [F34, F129, F132, F133]
- `ipc_task_queue_add_task()` must take `ipc_task->q_lock` with `spin_lock_irqsave()` and execute `smp_wmb()` after populating `ipc_task->args[pos]` before updating `ipc_task->q_wpos` for the lockless consumer `ipc_task_queue_handler()`. In `ipc_protocol_tq_msg_send()`, `ipc_protocol_msg_prep()` must populate `p_ap_shm->msg_ring[index]` and `ipc_protocol->rsp_ring[index]` must be stored before `ipc_protocol_msg_hp_update()` advances `msg_head` and rings the doorbell. [F39, F131]
- In `ipc_wwan_init()`, set `ipc_wwan->dev` and `ipc_imem` before `wwan_register_ops()`. `ipc_wwan_newlink()` must publish `priv` to `sub_netlist[if_id]` via `rcu_assign_pointer()` after `register_netdevice()`, and `ipc_wwan_dellink()` must clear it with `RCU_INIT_POINTER()` before `unregister_netdevice_queue()` (with `needs_free_netdev = true`). `ipc_wwan_receive()` and `ipc_wwan_tx_flowctrl()` must hold `rcu_read_lock()` across `sub_netlist[if_id]` and `priv->netdev` accesses, and `ipc_wwan_receive()` must set `skb = NULL` after `netif_rx(skb)`. [F15, F19, F20, F136, F137]
- In `ipc_trace_init()`, initialize channel `IPC_MEM_CTRL_CHL_ID_3`, `ipc_trace` fields, and `mutex_init(&ipc_trace->trc_mutex)` before `debugfs_create_file()`. `ipc_trace_ctrl_file_read()` and `ipc_trace_ctrl_file_write()` must serialize `ipc_trace->mode` and `ipc_trace->channel` under `trc_mutex` while keeping user-buffer copies outside `trc_mutex`. [F22, F138]
- In `ipc_pm_wait_for_device_active()`, set bit `CONSUME_STATE` (0) of `ipc_pm->host_sleep_pend` (with `smp_mb__before_atomic()` / `smp_mb__after_atomic()`) before waiting on `host_sleep_complete` and clear it afterwards. In `ipc_pcie_suspend_s2idle()`, set bit 0 of `ipc_pcie->suspend` after firing `IPC_MEM_DEV_PM_FORCE_SLEEP`, and in `ipc_pcie_resume_s2idle()` clear it only after firing `IPC_MEM_DEV_PM_FORCE_ACTIVE` and calling `ipc_imem_pm_s2idle_sleep(ipc_pcie->imem, false)`. [F38, F128]

## Object Lifetimes

- SKBs from `ipc_pcie_alloc_skb()`, `ipc_pcie_alloc_local_skb()` (sets `IPC_CB(skb)->mapping = 0`), or `ipc_imem_map_skb_to_dma()` store DMA state/length in `IPC_CB(skb)`, and only these may be passed to `ipc_pcie_kfree_skb()`. In `ipc_imem_dl_skb_process()`, unmap `IPC_CTYPE_CTRL` SKBs via `ipc_pcie_addr_unmap()`, while `IPC_CTYPE_WWAN` SKBs stay mapped until `ipc_mux_dl_decode()` calls `ipc_pcie_kfree_skb()` (`ipc_mux_net_receive()` passes a `skb_clone(..., GFP_ATOMIC)` copy adjusted with `skb_pull()` and `skb_trim(dest_skb, pkt_len)`). [F3, F58, F141, F146, F148]
- UL ADB SKBs dequeued from `ipc_mux->ul_adb.free_list` in `ipc_mux_ul_skb_alloc()` are tagged `UL_MUX_OP_ADB` and, on UL pipe completion, must be recycled via `ipc_mux_ul_encoded_process()` (decrementing `ul_data_pend_bytes`, calling `skb_trim(skb, 0)`, and requeuing to `free_list`) rather than freed with `ipc_pcie_kfree_skb()`. [F68]
- `ipc_imem->ipc_devlink` is allocated only in `IPC_MEM_EXEC_STAGE_BOOT` (bit `IOSM_DEVLINK_INIT` in `ipc_imem->flag`), while `mux`, `wwan`, `ipc_port[]`, and `trace` are allocated in `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()` (setting `FULLY_FUNCTIONAL` with atomic barriers only after all succeed). Both `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()` and `ipc_imem_cleanup()` must guard `ipc_devlink_deinit()` with `test_and_clear_bit(IOSM_DEVLINK_INIT, &ipc_imem->flag)` (freeing slot 0 for WWAN) and guard `mux`/`debugfs`/`wwan`/`port` deinit with `test_and_clear_bit(FULLY_FUNCTIONAL, &ipc_imem->flag)`. [F17, F24, F47]
- In `ipc_devlink_init()`, call `devlink_register()` last after initializing params, regions, channel `IPC_MEM_CTRL_CHL_ID_7`, and `devlink_sio`; in `ipc_devlink_deinit()`, call `devlink_unregister()` first before tearing them down. [F119]
- In `ipc_task_deinit()`, `tasklet_kill(ipc_task->ipc_tasklet)` must run before `ipc_task_queue_cleanup()`. `ipc_protocol_msg_send()` stores stack-allocated `&response` (`struct ipc_rsp`) in `ipc_protocol->rsp_ring[index]`, so both `ipc_protocol_tq_msg_send_cb` and timeout cleanup `ipc_protocol_tq_msg_remove` must be queued with `wait = true`. [F28, F111]
- `ipc_protocol_pipe_cleanup()` must set `pipe->skbr_start = NULL` and `pipe->tdr_start = NULL` after freeing them because `ipc_imem_pipe_cleanup()` runs both on channel close and across all channels in `ipc_imem_channel_reset()`. During `ipc_imem_cleanup()`, `ipc_imem_device_ipc_uninit()` must check `ipc_pcie_check_data_link_active()`, fire `IPC_MEM_DEVICE_IPC_UNINIT`, and poll `ipc_mmio_get_ipc_state()` before `ipc_imem_channel_reset()` and `ipc_protocol_deinit()` free DMA rings and `p_ap_shm`. [F8, F118]

## API Contracts

- `ipc_protocol_msg_prep()`, `ipc_protocol_tq_msg_send()`, `ipc_mux_schedule()`, `ipc_mux_open_session()`, `ipc_mux_close_session()`, and `ipc_imem_sys_wwan_open()` return a 0-based index (`>= 0`) on success and negative errno on failure (check `< 0`, not `!= 0`). `ipc_mux_ul_adb_allocate()` returns `false` on success and `true` when `ipc_mux_ul_skb_alloc()` fails. [F40, F42, F43]
- `ipc_imem_channel_alloc()` returns the compacted slot index `i` in `ipc_imem->channels[i]` (passed to `ipc_imem_channel_open()` and `ipc_imem_channel_close()`), while setting `channel->channel_id` to the logical `enum ipc_channel_id` used to index `ipc_imem->ipc_port[]`. After the search loop in `ipc_imem_channel_alloc()`, check `i >= ipc_imem->nr_of_channels` before dereferencing `channel`. [F93, F94]
- In `ipc_wwan_link_transmit()`, when `ipc_imem_sys_wwan_transmit()` returns `0` the SKB is owned by `session->ul_list`, when `-EBUSY` it must not be freed (`NETDEV_TX_BUSY`), and on any other non-zero return call `dev_kfree_skb_any(skb)`. In `ipc_imem_sys_cdev_write()`, if `ipc_imem_call_cdev_write()` fails after queuing `skb` onto `channel->ul_list`, dequeue it with `skb_dequeue_tail(&channel->ul_list)` and return negative errno without freeing `skb`. [F5, F151]
- `ipc_task_queue_send_task()` duplicates `msg` with `kmemdup(msg, size, GFP_ATOMIC)` and frees it after `args->func` returns when `size > 0`, whereas `size == 0` forwards `msg` directly without copying or freeing it; callers passing persistent objects (`&channel->dl_pipe`) or synchronous stack args (`&send_args`) must pass `size = 0`. [F110]
- `ipc_coredump_collect()` must allocate `*data` with `vmalloc()` (`vfree()` on error) because `devlink_region_ops.destructor` is `vfree`. Because `fw->data` is vmalloc-backed, `ipc_flash_boot_psi()` must copy the PSI payload with `kmemdup(..., GFP_KERNEL)` before `ipc_imem_sys_devlink_write()` maps it via `ipc_pcie_addr_map()`. [F16, F142]
- In `ipc_devlink_flash_update()`, verify `params->fw->data != NULL`, `params->fw->size > IOSM_DEVLINK_HDR_SIZE`, and `IOSM_DEVLINK_MAGIC_HEADER` before subtracting or advancing by `IOSM_DEVLINK_HDR_SIZE`. Callers of `ipc_imem_sys_devlink_read()` must pass destination capacity in `bytes_to_read` (rejects `bytes_to_read < skb->len` and leaves `*bytes_read` untouched on error), and fixed-size readers must check both `ret == 0` and `bytes_read == expected_size`. [F45, F79, F156]
- `FULLY_FUNCTIONAL` (0), `IOSM_DEVLINK_INIT` (1), and `CONSUME_STATE` (0) are bit numbers for `set_bit()`/`test_bit()`, whereas `IPC_MEM_WWAN_MUX` (`BIT(0)`), `UL_AGGR` (`BIT(8)`), `DL_AGGR` (`BIT(9)`), and `UL_FLOW_CREDIT` (`BIT(21)`) are pre-shifted masks. `ipc_imem_msg_send_feature_set()` takes an unshifted boolean (`0` or `IPC_MEM_INBAND_CRASH_SIG` = 1) in `reset_enable` because `ipc_protocol_msg_prep_feature_set()` shifts it by `RESET_BIT` (7). [F59, F60]

## Error Handling

- In `ipc_pcie_probe()`, `pci_enable_device()` and `dma_set_mask(ipc_pcie->dev, DMA_BIT_MASK(64))` must succeed before `ipc_imem_init()`. On error or in `ipc_pcie_cleanup()`, run `ipc_imem_cleanup()` before `ipc_pcie_resources_release()` (freeing IRQs and `iounmap()`ing BARs before `pci_release_regions()` and `pci_disable_device()`). In `ipc_acquire_irq()`, on failure at index `i`, set signed `int ipc_pcie->nvec = i` before `ipc_release_irq()` loops `while (--ipc_pcie->nvec >= 0)`. [F11, F85, F116]
- When `ipc_mux_ul_adgh_encode()` or `mux_ul_dg_encode()` inspects a packet via `skb_peek(ul_list)`, leave `src_skb` on `ul_list` and do not call `dev_kfree_skb(src_skb)` if ADB allocation or space checks fail before `src_skb` is copied and dequeued. [F4]
- In `ipc_mux_init()`, if allocating `pp_qlt[i]` fails, free `pp_qlt[0 .. i - 1]` and `ipc_mux`; if `ipc_pcie_alloc_skb()` fails, call `ipc_mux_deinit(ipc_mux)`. In `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()`, if `ipc_imem_wwan_channel_init()` fails after `ipc_mux_init()`, call `ipc_mux_deinit(ipc_imem->mux)` and exit before setting `FULLY_FUNCTIONAL`. [F12, F13]
- In `ipc_imem_channel_open()`, if opening `dl_pipe` or queuing `ipc_imem_tq_pipe_td_alloc` fails, close opened pipes via `ipc_imem_pipe_close()` and call `ipc_imem_channel_free(channel)`. In `ipc_devlink_create_region()`, check `devlink_region_create()` with `IS_ERR()`; on failure at `i`, unwind `i - 1` down to `0` and in `ipc_devlink_init()` jump past `ipc_devlink_destroy_region()`. [F18, F53]

## Known Pitfalls

- `struct mux_adth` ends with flexible array `dg[]` (so `sizeof(struct mux_adth)` excludes `struct mux_adth_dg`), whereas `struct mux_qlth` and `struct ipc_mem_lite_gen_tbl` embed a trailing single-element `ql` or `vfl` member and must be sized using `offsetof(..., ql)` / `offsetof(..., vfl)` rather than `sizeof(...)`. [F108, F109]
- In `ipc_mux_ul_encode_adth()`, `ul_adb->next_table_index` points into `ul_adb->buf` at the preceding header's `next_table_index` field, so `memcpy(p_adb_qlt, ul_adb->pp_qlt[i], qlt_size)` must run before any subsequent table write updates `*ul_adb->next_table_index`. In `mux_ul_dg_encode()`, whenever `mux_ul_dg_update_tbl_index()` allocates a new ADB mid-loop, reset `nr_of_skb = 0`, `offset = le32_to_cpu(adb->adbh->block_length)`, and `dg = adb->dg[session_id] + adb->dg_count[session_id]`. [F97, F113]
- In `ipc_imem_run_state_worker()`'s `while (ctrl_chl_idx < IPC_MEM_MAX_CHANNELS)` loop and `mux_dl_adb_decode()`'s `while (adth_index)` loop, every `continue` branch must advance `ctrl_chl_idx++` or `adth_index = le32_to_cpu(adth->next_table_index)`. [F90, F91]
- `union ipc_pm_cond` bitfields (`irq`, `hs`, `link`) use `IPC_PM_ACTIVE` (`1`) and `IPC_PM_SLEEP` (`0`), inverted relative to `enum ipc_mem_dev_pm_state` and `enum ipc_mem_host_pm_state` (`ACTIVE == 0`, `SLEEP == 1`). In `ipc_pcie_check_data_link_active()`, read `PCI_EXP_LNKSTA` from root port `ipc_pcie->pci->bus->self`, not `ipc_pcie->pci`. [F99, F101]
