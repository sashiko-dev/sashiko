# INPUT (KEYBOARD, MOUSE, JOYSTICK, TOUCHSCREEN) DRIVERS

This unit comprises joystick, gamepad, and keyboard input drivers across USB, Serio (PS/2 and RS232), SPI, I2C, Gameport, Parport, Maple bus, IIO ADC, and platform MMIO interfaces—including `iforce`, `xpad`, `applespi`, `atkbd`, `sidewinder`, and platform keypads—translating bus transfers and interrupts into Linux input events, LEDs, and force-feedback control.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct ps2dev ps2dev` must stay at offset 0 of `struct atkbd` (`ps2_interrupt()` treats `serio_get_drvdata()` as `struct ps2dev *` and `atkbd_reconnect()` checks `!atkbd` on `atkbd_from_serio()`), and `struct iforce iforce` must stay at offset 0 of `struct iforce_serio` (`iforce_serio_write_wakeup()` treats `serio_get_drvdata()` as `struct iforce *`). Embedded DMA buffers (`data_in`/`data_out` in `struct iforce_usb`, `sendbuf`/`response` in `struct psxpad`) must be `____cacheline_aligned`. [F37, F120, F157]
- When replacing `atkbd->dev` in `atkbd_set_extra`, `atkbd_set_scroll`, `atkbd_set_set`, `atkbd_set_softrepeat`, or `atkbd_set_softraw`, an `input_register_device()` failure must restore `atkbd->dev = old_dev`, old config fields (including both `old_softrepeat` and `old_softraw`, since enabling `atkbd->softrepeat` in `atkbd_connect` or `atkbd_set_softrepeat` requires `atkbd->softraw = true`), and re-run `atkbd_set_keycode_table()` (if called) and `atkbd_set_device_attrs()`. [F57, F58]
- `atkbd_set_keycode_table()` must zero `atkbd->keycode` and `atkbd->force_release_mask`, copy defaults from `atkbd_set2_keycode`/`atkbd_set3_keycode` without mutating them, and recompute `ATKBD_RET_HANGEUL`/`ATKBD_RET_HANJA` via `atkbd_compat_scancode()` before `atkbd_platform_fixup()`; `atkbd_set_device_attrs()` must exclude codes `>= ATKBD_SPECIAL` (`0xfff8..0xfffe`), `KEY_RESERVED`, and `ATKBD_KEY_NULL` before setting `input_dev->keybit`. [F61, F83]
- In `atkbd_select_set()`, checks for `atkbd->translated` (returning `2`), `atkbd->id == 0xaca1` (Set `3`), `allow_extra` (`ATKBD_CMD_EX_ENABLE`, returning `2`), and `atkbd_terminal` (`ATKBD_CMD_SETALL_MB`, returning `3`) must precede `if (target_set != 3) return 2;`. In `atkbd_reconnect()`, before `atkbd_enable()`/`atkbd_activate()`, reset `atkbd->xl_bit` and `atkbd->emul` to `0` and, if `atkbd->write` is set, call `atkbd_set_leds()` and (unless `atkbd->softrepeat`) `atkbd_set_repeat_rate()`. [F59, F105]
- Under `xpad->odata_lock`, `xpad_prepare_next_init_packet()` and `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` must set `xpad->irq_out->transfer_buffer_length` to the length copied into `xpad->odata`, and `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` must clear `packet->pending = false`; because `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` pre-increments `xpad->last_out_packet`, callers prioritizing `out_packets[XPAD_OUT_CMD_IDX]` must set `xpad->last_out_packet = -1`. `xpad_resume()` must reset `xpad->delayed_init_done = false`, and `xpad_start_xbox_one()` must reset `xpad->init_seq = 0` under `xpad->odata_lock`. [F63, F66, F87]
- In `xpad.c`, `xpad->mapping` fields (`MAP_PROFILE_BUTTON`, `MAP_SHARE_BUTTON`, `MAP_PADDLES`) must be decoded in the handler matching `xpad->xtype` (`xpadone_process_packet()`, `xpad360_process_packet()`, or `xpad_process_packet()`); in `xpad360_process_packet()` and `xpad_init_input()`, `xpad->mapping & MAP_DPAD_TO_BUTTONS` and `!(xpad->mapping & MAP_DPAD_TO_BUTTONS) || xpad->xtype == XTYPE_XBOX360W` must remain two independent `if` blocks so `XTYPE_XBOX360W` reports both d-pad buttons and `ABS_HAT0X`/`ABS_HAT0Y`. [F94, F130]
- Every `xpad_device[]` entry must explicitly initialize all five positional fields (`idVendor`, `idProduct`, `name`, `mapping`, `xtype`) sorted by `idVendor`/`idProduct`; `iforce_device`, `xpad_device`, and `tmdc_models` must end with a `0`-ID fallback entry with non-NULL pointers; and axis/button code tables in `iforce-main.c`, `xpad.c`, and `interact.c` must terminate with `-1` and loop while `>= 0` (`0` is `ABS_X`). [F98, F99, F128]
- In `applespi.c`, `struct message::length` excludes `MSG_HEADER_SIZE` (8) and the 2-byte CRC16 (`applespi_send_cmd_msg()` sets `cpu_to_le16(msg_len - 2)`; `applespi_got_data()` checks `le16_to_cpu(message->length) == msg_len - MSG_HEADER_SIZE - 2`), and `PACKET_DEV_TPAD` in `applespi_got_data()` must verify `le16_to_cpu(message->length) + 2 == struct_size(tp, fingers, tp->number_of_fingers)` and clamp `tp->number_of_fingers` to `MAX_FINGERS` (11) before `report_tp_state()`. [F76, F77]
- In `applespi_resume()`, under `applespi->cmd_msg_lock`, clear `have_cl_led_on`, `have_bl_level`, `cmd_msg_queued`, `read_active`, `write_active`, `drain`, and `suspended` while preserving `want_bl_level`, and call `applespi_init(applespi, true)` (`want_mt_init_cmd`). In `applespi_handle_keyboard_event()`, translate key releases for `last_keys_pressed[i]` using saved `last_keys_fn_pressed[i]` (not `keyboard_protocol->fn_pressed`) before zeroing `last_keys_fn_pressed[i]` and before updating `last_keys_pressed`. [F67, F69]

## Synchronization
- In `xpad.c`, `xpad->odata_lock` must be held when calling `xpad_prepare_next_init_packet()`, `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()`, or `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()`, or modifying `out_packets`, `last_out_packet`, `init_seq`, `odata_serial`, or `irq_out_active`. In `xpad_suspend()`/`xpad_resume()`, for non-`XTYPE_XBOX360W` controllers, `input->mutex` must be held while checking `input_device_enabled(input)` and calling `xpad_stop_input()`/`xpad_start_input()`. [F17, F20]
- For `XTYPE_XBOX360W`, `xpad_presence_work()` must publish `xpad->x360w_dev` via `rcu_assign_pointer()` only after `xpad_init_input()` succeeds, and on disconnect must clear `xpad->x360w_dev` and call `synchronize_rcu()` before `xpad_deinit_input()`, while `xpad360w_process_packet()` must read `xpad->x360w_dev` under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F65]
- In `atkbd.c`, `atkbd->mutex` serializes `atkbd_reconnect()`, `atkbd_attr_set_helper()`, and `atkbd_event_work()`; `atkbd->enabled` must only be modified by `atkbd_enable()`/`atkbd_disable()` under `serio_pause_rx(atkbd->ps2dev.serio)` (or inside `atkbd_receive_byte()`); and `atkbd_reconnect()` and `atkbd_attr_set_helper()` must call `atkbd_disable(atkbd)` under `atkbd->mutex` before probing or mutating `atkbd->keycode`, `atkbd->force_release_mask`, or `atkbd->dev`, and `atkbd_enable(atkbd)` afterward. [F25, F26]
- In `iforce`, all accesses to `iforce->xmit` in `iforce_send_packet()`, `iforce_serio_xmit()`, and `__iforce_usb_xmit()` must hold `iforce->xmit_lock` via `spinlock_irqsave`, and `iforce_send_packet()` must release `iforce->xmit_lock` before calling `iforce->xport_ops->xmit(iforce)`. In `iforce_serio_irq()`, non-command packets must only call `iforce_process_packet()` when `iforce->type` is non-NULL. [F31, F142]
- In `applespi.c`, `applespi_send_cmd_msg()` must return while `applespi->cmd_msg_queued` is non-zero (unless timed out) and issue at most one command per call, with `applespi_msg_complete()` clearing `cmd_msg_queued` and calling `applespi_send_cmd_msg()` when `is_write_msg` is true; in `applespi_handle_cmd_response()`, `applespi_register_touchpad_device()` must be deferred to `applespi->work`, publishing `touchpad_input_dev` with `smp_store_release()` and reading it with `smp_load_acquire()` in `report_tp_state()`. [F97, F165]
- `tgfx_open()`/`tgfx_close()` and `n64joy_open()`/`n64joy_close()` guard `tgfx->used` and `priv->n64joy_opened` with `tgfx->sem` and `priv->n64joy_mutex`, starting the timer (and claiming `tgfx->pd`) only on 0->1 and calling `timer_delete_sync()` only on 1->0; therefore `tgfx_timer()` and `n64joy_poll()` must never acquire `tgfx->sem` or `priv->n64joy_mutex`. [F12, F156]

## Object Lifetimes
- `iforce_init_device()` frees `iforce->dev` via `input_free_device()` on any internal error, whereas on success transport disconnect must call `input_unregister_device(iforce->dev)` (and `iforce_init_device()` must clamp `ff_effects` from query `'N'` to `IFORCE_EFFECTS_MAX` (32) before `input_ff_create()`). In `iforce_close()`, when `EV_FF` is set, send `FF_CMD_ENABLE` and wait on `iforce->wait` until `IFORCE_XMIT_RUNNING` clears in `iforce->xmit_flags` before `iforce->xport_ops->stop_io(iforce)`. [F1, F3, F81]
- In `iforce-ff.c` and `iforce-main.c`, `allocate_resource(&iforce->device_memory, ...)` requires `iforce->mem_mutex`, `iforce_upload_effect()` must return `-EAGAIN` while `FF_CORE_UPDATE` is set on `core_effect->flags`, `make_*_modifier` helpers must allocate `mod_chunk` only when `old == NULL`, and `iforce_erase_effect()` must release `mod1_chunk`/`mod2_chunk` iff `FF_MOD1_IS_USED`/`FF_MOD2_IS_USED` is set before zeroing `core_effect->flags[0]`. [F30, F71]
- In `xpad_init_input()`, failure before `input_register_device()` must roll back via `xpad_led_disconnect()`, `input_ff_destroy()`, and `input_free_device()` without setting `xpad->input_created = true`, and `xpad_led_probe()` must reset `xpad->led = NULL` on error. Under `xpad->odata_lock`, `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()` and `xpad_irq_out()` must call `usb_unanchor_urb()` and clear `xpad->irq_out_active` whenever `usb_submit_urb(xpad->irq_out, ...)` fails after `usb_anchor_urb()`. [F4, F7]
- `xpad_disconnect()` must call `xpad360w_stop_input()` and `xpad_deinit_input()` before `xpad_stop_output()` and `xpad_deinit_output()`; `xpad_suspend()` for `XTYPE_XBOX360W` must call `xpad360w_stop_input()` and `xpad360w_poweroff_controller()` before `xpad_stop_output()`; and `xpad360w_stop_input()` must call `usb_kill_urb(xpad->irq_in)` before `flush_work(&xpad->work)`. [F5, F18, F134]
- `applespi_remove()` and `applespi_suspend()` must call `applespi_drain_writes()`, then `acpi_disable_gpe(NULL, applespi->gpe)`, and then `applespi_drain_reads()`. `atkbd_disconnect()` must call `atkbd_disable(atkbd)` and `input_unregister_device(atkbd->dev)` before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&atkbd->event_work)` and `serio_close(serio)`. [F8, F9]

## API Contracts
- In `atkbd_event()` and `applespi_event()` (`input_dev->event` runs in atomic context), hardware commands must not block synchronously: `atkbd_event()` must offload `ps2_command()` work via `atkbd_schedule_event_work()`, and `applespi_event()` must queue async SPI messages under `cmd_msg_lock`. [F164]
- In `iforce_send_packet()`, `data` must have at least `LO(cmd)` bytes with `LO(cmd) < IFORCE_MAX_LENGTH` (16); `iforce_usb_get_id()` must pass timeout `1000` (ms, not `HZ`) to `usb_control_msg()`; `get_id` callbacks must return `0` only after setting `response_data` and `*response_len`; and `iforce_init_device()` must check `!iforce_get_id_packet(iforce, id, buf, &len)` and `len >= N` before reading `buf`. [F36, F80, F92]
- In `xpad.c`, `xpad->idata`/`xpad->odata` (`usb_alloc_coherent()` with `URB_NO_TRANSFER_DMA_MAP`) must be freed with `usb_free_coherent()` only after `irq_in`/`irq_out` are stopped and freed. For `XTYPE_XBOXONE`, `xpad_probe()` must reject `bInterfaceNumber != GIP_WIRED_INTF_DATA` (0) with `-ENODEV` and `xpad_start_xbox_one()` must set altsetting 0 on `GIP_WIRED_INTF_AUDIO` (1) if present. [F131, F147]
- For `XTYPE_XBOX360W` in `xpad.c`, `xpad_probe()` sets `udev->quirks |= USB_QUIRK_RESET_RESUME` (`xpad_driver` must omit `.reset_resume`), `xpad_init_input()` must omit `open`/`close`, and `xpad_probe()`/`xpad_resume()` must start `irq_in` via `xpad360w_start_input()`. [F133, F166]
- `applespi_probe()` must evaluate ACPI `"UIST"` first and return `-ENODEV` if `usb_status` is non-zero before `applespi_enable_spi()`, and when `applespi_enable_spi()` enables SPI via `"SIEN"`, it must call `msleep(50)`. Signed 16-bit `struct tp_finger` fields in `applespi.c` must use `le16_to_int()` rather than `le16_to_cpu()`. In `atkbd_probe()`, when `atkbd_skip_getid(atkbd)` is true, set `atkbd->id = 0xab83` and jump to `deactivate_kbd` (skipping `ATKBD_CMD_GETID` and `ATKBD_CMD_SETLEDS`). [F110, F125, F135, F137]

## Error Handling
- When an async SPI read fails in `applespi_async_read_complete()` or fails CRC verification while draining in `applespi_got_data()`, both `read_active` and `write_active` must be cleared and `drain_complete` woken under `cmd_msg_lock`; after CRC verification succeeds in `applespi_got_data()`, every completion or validation-error path (except intermediate fragments with `rem > 0`) must jump to `msg_complete` to reset `applespi->saved_msg_len = 0` and call `applespi_msg_complete()`. [F24, F68]
- In `applespi.c`, ACPI calls (`ACPI_SUCCESS()`/`ACPI_FAILURE()`) and EFI variable calls (guarded by `efi_rt_services_supported()` and checked against `EFI_SUCCESS`) must be translated to `-ENODEV`. In `xpad.c`, when `xpad->xtype == XTYPE_UNKNOWN`, `xpad_init_output()` skips `irq_out_anchor`, `odata`, and `irq_out`, so `xpad_stop_output()`, `xpad_deinit_output()`, and `xpad_init_ff()` must guard against `XTYPE_UNKNOWN`, and `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()` must not `dev_err()` on `-ENODEV`. [F42, F45, F158]
- In `xpad_irq_in()` and `iforce_usb_irq()`, `switch (urb->status)` must return immediately without resubmitting `urb` on `-ECONNRESET`, `-ENOENT`, and `-ESHUTDOWN`, whereas `default:` for other non-zero statuses must `goto exit` to skip decoding and resubmit `urb`. In `atkbd_probe()`, `param[0..1]` must be initialized to `0xa5` before `ps2_command(ps2dev, param, ATKBD_CMD_GETID)`, and `atkbd_set_force_release()` must parse into a temporary `new_mask` via `bitmap_parselist()` before copying to `atkbd->force_release_mask`. [F39, F60, F104]

## External Contracts
- New forced-release key quirks must not be added to `atkbd_dmi_quirk_table[]` in `atkbd.c` (handled via udev); in `xpad_table[]`, vendor-specific USB matches must use `XPAD_XBOX360_VENDOR()` or `XPAD_XBOXONE_VENDOR()` sorted by vendor ID, and `xpad_device[]`/`xpad_table[]` must never include non-gamepad VID/PIDs; and in `applespi_set_bl_level()`, `value == 0` must be handled separately before `((value * KBD_BL_LEVEL_ADJ) / KBD_BL_LEVEL_SCALE + KBD_BL_LEVEL_MIN)` so brightness `0` maps to `0` rather than `KBD_BL_LEVEL_MIN` (`32`). [F113, F127, F129]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `iforce.h`, `XMIT_INC(var, n)` expands to two unbraced statements `(var)+=n; (var)&= XMIT_SIZE -1` and must never be the body of an unbraced `if`/`else`/`for`/`while`. In `xpad.c`, index `xpad->out_packets` with `XPAD_OUT_CMD_IDX`, `XPAD_OUT_FF_IDX`, `XPAD_OUT_LED_IDX`, and `XPAD_NUM_OUT_PACKETS`, and invert 16-bit stick Y-axis values (`ABS_Y`, `ABS_RY`) with bitwise NOT `~(__s16)le16_to_cpup(...)` rather than unary negation `-`. [F64, F79, F111]
- In `sw_get_bits()` (`sidewinder.c`), cast `((buf[i] >> tri++) & 1)` to `__u64` before `<< bit++` (as `bit` reaches 63); in `n64joy_exec_pif()`, pass explicit size `8 * 8` (never `sizeof(in)` on `const u64 in[8]`) to `dma_cache_wback_inv()` and use 32-bit word indices (`0, 1, 4, 6`) for `SI_*_REG` on `u32 __iomem *reg_base`; and in `sensehat_joystick_report()`, copy `unsigned int keys` into an `unsigned long` before passing its address to `bitmap_xor()` or `for_each_set_bit()`. [F89, F106, F117, F122]
