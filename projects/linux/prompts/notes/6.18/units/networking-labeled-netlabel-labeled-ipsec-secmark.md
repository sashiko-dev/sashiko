# NETWORKING [LABELED] (NetLabel, Labeled IPsec, SECMARK)

The Labeled Networking subsystem provides explicit and fallback packet and socket security labeling for Linux Security Modules. It comprises the NetLabel framework (LSM domain-to-protocol mappings, static/fallback unlabeled address mappings, and Generic Netlink interfaces), the CIPSO/IPv4 and CALIPSO/IPv6 protocol engines for encoding, validating, caching, and parsing MLS/secid IP options across packets, sockets, and request sockets, and the Netfilter `xt_SECMARK` and `xt_CONNSECMARK` targets.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `struct cipso_v4_doi`, `doi_def->map.std` is only allocated when `doi_def->type == CIPSO_V4_MAP_TRANS` (for `CIPSO_V4_MAP_PASS` and `CIPSO_V4_MAP_LOCAL`, `map.std` is uninitialized); check `doi_def->type == CIPSO_V4_MAP_TRANS` before dereferencing `map.std`. In `struct cipso_v4_std_map_tbl`, 0 is valid and unmapped slots use `CIPSO_V4_INV_LVL` or `CIPSO_V4_INV_CAT` (`0x80000000`): fill new `lvl`/`cat` arrays in `netlbl_cipsov4_add_std()` with those sentinels, and check `< *_size` and `< 0x80000000` on every lookup. [F43, F44, F71]
- In `cipso_v4_validate()`, check `opt_iter + 1 == opt_len` before reading `tag[1]`, and verify `tag_len <= opt_len - opt_iter` and `tag_len >= CIPSO_V4_TAG_*_BLEN` before reading payload fields. Raw IPv4 option loops (`cipso_v4_get_actual_opt_len()`, `cipso_v4_optptr()`) must break on `IPOPT_END` (`0`), advance by 1 on `IPOPT_NOP`/`IPOPT_NOOP` (`1`), and advance by `opt[iter + 1]` on TLV options. Loops over `doi_def->tags` must stop when `iter == CIPSO_V4_TAG_MAXCNT` (5) or `doi_def->tags[iter] == CIPSO_V4_TAG_INVALID` (`0`). [F70, F73, F76]
- `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` and `cipso_v4_req_setattr()` allocate `sizeof(*opt) + ((buf_len + 3) & ~3)` for `struct ip_options_rcu` and set `opt->opt.cipso = sizeof(struct iphdr)`, so `cipso_v4_delopt()` and `cipso_v4_sock_getattr()` must subtract `sizeof(struct iphdr)` when indexing `opt->opt.__data`. When `cipso_v4_delopt()` removes CIPSO in place while other options (`srr`, `rr`, `ts`, `router_alert`) remain, decrement any of those offsets `> opt->opt.cipso` by `cipso_len`, reset `opt->opt.cipso = 0`, and recompute `opt->opt.optlen` after `memmove`. [F55, F105]
- When `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` or `cipso_v4_sock_delattr()` updates `inet_sk(sk)->inet_opt` on an `inet_test_bit(IS_ICSK, sk)` socket, adjust `inet_csk(sk)->icsk_ext_hdr_len` by the option length delta and call `icsk_sync_mss(sk, sk_conn->icsk_pmtu_cookie)`. [F54]
- In `struct netlbl_dommap_def`, `{ addrsel, cipso, calipso }` is discriminated by `def.type` and must be `NULL` when `def.type == NETLBL_NLTYPE_UNLABELED`. Downcast `netlbl_af4list`/`netlbl_af6list` nodes in `netlbl_domaddr_map` only via `netlbl_domhsh_addr{4,6}_entry()`, and nodes in `netlbl_unlhsh_iface` only via `netlbl_unlhsh_addr{4,6}_entry()`. [F45, F111]
- Default domain mappings (`entry->domain == NULL`) use `netlbl_domhsh_def_ipv{4,6}`; an `AF_UNSPEC` default in `netlbl_domhsh_add()` must allocate an `AF_INET6` clone (`entry_b`) so they do not alias. Before merging `ADDRSELECT` selectors into `entry_old`, check via `netlbl_af{4,6}list_search_exact()` for duplicates. In `netlbl_domhsh_remove_af{4,6}()` and `netlbl_unlhsh_remove()`, remove the parent if both its IPv4 and IPv6 lists become empty. [F59, F62, F66]

## Synchronization
- Every exit path in `netlbl_{conn,sock,req,skbuff}_setattr()` after `rcu_read_lock()` must jump to the unlock label. `cipso_v4_{sock,req}_setattr()`, `calipso_opt_{insert,del}()`, `cipso_v4_cache_add()`, `calipso_cache_add()`, wire category parsers (`cipso_v4_getattr()`, `calipso_opt_getattr()`), and `netlbl_audit_start_common()` run under `rcu_read_lock()`/spinlocks/softirq and must allocate with `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F11, F119, F120, F128]
- Lock `cipso_v4_cache[bkt]` and `calipso_cache[bkt]` with `spin_lock_bh()` / `spin_unlock_bh()`. Read `cipso_v4_cache_enabled`, `cipso_v4_cache_bucketsize`, `cipso_v4_rbm_optfmt`, and `cipso_v4_rbm_strictvalid` with `READ_ONCE()`, and snapshot `cipso_v4_cache_bucketsize` into a local variable in `cipso_v4_cache_add()`. [F19, F118]
- In `cipso_v4_doi_add()` and `calipso_doi_add()`, call `refcount_set(&doi_def->refcount, 1)` before `list_add_tail_rcu()`. `cipso_v4_doi_getdef()` and `calipso_doi_getdef()` must look up the DOI under `rcu_read_lock()` and call `refcount_inc_not_zero(&doi_def->refcount)` before `rcu_read_unlock()`. [F22, F24]
- Hold `rcu_read_lock()` when calling `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach_rcu()`, `netlbl_af{4,6}list_search*()`, or `netlbl_domhsh_remove_entry()`. When removing from `netlbl_domhsh` or `netlbl_unlhsh`, clear `valid = 0` and unlink under the table lock, then free via `call_rcu()`/`kfree_rcu()`. In `netlbl_domhsh_remove_entry()`, return `-ENOENT` if `!entry->valid` under `netlbl_domhsh_lock`, and unlock before `*_doi_putdef()` or `netlbl_audit_start_common()`. [F9, F26, F126, F127]
- In `netlbl_unlhsh_add_iface()`, re-check `netlbl_unlhsh_search_iface(ifindex)` or `netlbl_unlhsh_def` under `netlbl_unlhsh_lock` before inserting. In `netlbl_unlhsh_condremove_iface()`, checking that `addr4_list` and `addr6_list` are empty, clearing `iface->valid = 0`, and unlinking `iface` must occur within one `netlbl_unlhsh_lock` critical section. [F29, F30]
- `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` requires exclusive access to `sk`, must pass `sk_locked` to `rcu_dereference_protected(sk_inet->inet_opt, sk_locked)`, publish `opt` with `rcu_assign_pointer()`, and free old options with `kfree_rcu()`. In `cipso_v4_req_setattr()` and `calipso_req_{setattr,delattr}()`, replace `req_inet->ireq_opt` and `req_inet->ipv6_opt` with `xchg()`, freeing displaced options via `kfree_rcu()` (IPv4) or `atomic_sub(txopts->tot_len, &sk->sk_omem_alloc)` plus `txopt_put()` (IPv6). [F33, F121]
- `net/netlabel/netlabel_calipso.c` must access CALIPSO only via `netlbl_calipso_ops_get()` (`READ_ONCE(calipso_ops)`), checking for non-NULL `ops`; `netlbl_calipso_ops_register()` updates `calipso_ops` with `xchg()`, `calipso_exit()` must unregister `NULL` before freeing `calipso_cache`, and interface changes must update `struct netlbl_calipso_ops`, `ops` in `net/ipv6/calipso.c`, and wrappers in `net/netlabel/netlabel_calipso.c` in lockstep. [F31, F67, F133]

## Object Lifetimes
- `cipso_v4_doi_add()` and `calipso_doi_add()` take ownership of `doi_def` only when returning 0; on error, the caller must call `cipso_v4_doi_free()` or `calipso_doi_free()`. `netlbl_calipso_add()` must check `netlbl_calipso_ops_get() != NULL` before allocating `struct calipso_doi`, because `calipso_doi_free()` is a no-op when `calipso_ops` is NULL. [F3, F4]
- `cipso_v4_doi_getdef()` and `calipso_doi_getdef()` require a matching `*_putdef()` on every exit, error, and retry path (including in `netlbl_cfg_{cipsov4,calipso}_map_add()`, `netlbl_mgmt_add_common()`, and `netlbl_cipsov4_list()` retry), whereas `*_doi_search()` returns unreferenced RCU pointers. When `refcount_dec_and_test(&doi_def->refcount)` hits zero in `*_putdef()`, invalidate the protocol cache and free `doi_def` via `call_rcu()`. [F6, F23, F50, F94]
- `netlbl_domhsh_add()` consumes `entry` on success, but on failure the caller must free the selector map, `addrmap`, `entry->domain`, `entry`, and put any DOI refcount. In `netlbl_domhsh_remove_af{4,6}()`, after unlinking under `netlbl_domhsh_lock` and calling `rcu_read_unlock()`, call `synchronize_rcu()` before dropping the DOI reference and calling `kfree()`. [F8, F27]
- `calipso_opt_{insert,del}()` allocate a `struct ipv6_opt_hdr` that `calipso_opt_update()` and `ipv6_renew_options()` copy without taking ownership, so callers must `kfree()` that buffer. Release any `txopt_get(pinfo)` result with `txopt_put()` on all paths, and when `ipv6_update_options(sk, txopts)` or `xchg(&req_inet->ipv6_opt, txopts)` returns a non-NULL old `txopts`, call `atomic_sub(txopts->tot_len, &sk->sk_omem_alloc)` before `txopt_put(txopts)`. [F12, F13, F34]
- `cipso_v4_cache_add()` and `calipso_cache_add()` must allocate `entry` and `entry->key` before incrementing `secattr->cache->refcount` and assigning `entry->lsm_data`, because `*_cache_entry_free()` calls `netlbl_secattr_cache_free(entry->lsm_data)` whenever `entry->lsm_data != NULL`. [F15]
- `netlbl_secattr_destroy()` does not zero `secattr`, so call `netlbl_secattr_init()` before reuse; test `secattr->flags & NETLBL_SECATTR_FREE_DOMAIN` (not `NETLBL_SECATTR_DOMAIN_CPY`) to decide whether to free `secattr->domain`. Pair every `security_secid_to_secctx(secid, &ctx) >= 0` in `netlbl_unlhsh_*` and `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist_gen()` with `security_release_secctx(&ctx)`. In `xt_SECMARK.c` and `xt_CONNSECMARK.c`, complete all validation before calling `security_secmark_refcount_inc()` or `nf_ct_netns_get()`. [F16, F17, F90, F112]

## API Contracts
- `netlbl_catmap_walk{,rng}()` and `netlbl_bitmap_walk()` search inclusively and return `< 0` on miss: use a signed `int` cursor initialized to `-1` and pass `cursor + 1` next. `netlbl_bitmap_walk()` takes `@bitmap_len` in bits (`byte_len * 8`) and must check `++bit_spot >= bitmap_len` before reading `bitmap[++byte_offset]`; check `bit < net_cat_len * 8` before `netlbl_bitmap_setbit()`. [F36, F91, F117]
- `netlbl_catmap_{getlong,setlong}()` require `BITS_PER_LONG`-aligned `@offset` (casting `bitmap` to `u64` before `<<` in `setlong`), and `getlong` callers must stop on `*offset == (u32)-1` and add `BITS_PER_LONG` after each call. [F92, F101, F116]
- `calipso_opt_find()` returns `-ENOENT` when no CALIPSO TLV is present while still setting `*start` and `*end`, so `calipso_opt_insert()` and `calipso_skbuff_setattr()` must allow `-ENOENT`. `calipso_opt_insert()` returns `ERR_PTR()` on failure (never `NULL`), whereas `calipso_opt_del()` returns 0 and sets `*new = NULL` when no non-padding options remain. Callers of `calipso_validate()` must first check `option[1] >= 8` and `option[6] * 4 + 8 <= option[1]`. [F38, F39, F69]
- Callers of `netlbl_af{4,6}list_add()` must set `entry->valid = 1` and pre-mask `entry->addr` with `entry->mask`; when moving entries in `netlbl_domhsh_add()`, use `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach_safe()` and restore `iter->valid = 1` after `netlbl_af{4,6}list_remove_entry()`. [F87, F88]
- NetLabel state-mutating Genl commands must set `.flags = GENL_ADMIN_PERM` and call `netlbl_netlink_auditinfo()`. `.doit` handlers must `kfree_skb(ans_skb)` on error before `genlmsg_reply()`, whereas `.dumpit` callbacks borrow `skb`, must call `genlmsg_cancel(skb, data)` on error, decrement post-incremented counters in `netlbl_domhsh_walk()`, `cipso_v4_doi_walk()`, and `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist()` before saving `cb->args[]`, and reset both `iter_*` and `skip_*` to 0 when finishing a list in `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist()`. [F18, F84, F85, F144, F145]
- `netlbl_unlhsh_netdev_handler()` must ignore events when `!net_eq(dev_net(dev), &init_net)`, and callers of `netlbl_unlabel_addrinfo_get()` must first XOR-check that both address and mask attributes are present. In `cipso_v4_error()`, return early when `ip_hdr(skb)->protocol == IPPROTO_ICMP` or `error != -EACCES`, call `__ip_options_compile()` under `rcu_read_lock()`, and use `__icmp_send()`. [F89, F123, F141, F142]

## Error Handling
- When parsing wire categories into `secattr->attr.mls.cat` in `calipso_opt_getattr()` and `cipso_v4_parsetag_{rbm,enum,rng}()`, free `secattr->attr.mls.cat` via `netlbl_catmap_free()` on error, and only set `NETLBL_SECATTR_MLS_CAT` when `secattr->attr.mls.cat != NULL`. [F41]
- Check `sk_to_full_sk(req_to_sk(req)) != NULL` in `calipso_req_{setattr,delattr}()` (SYN cookies have `rsk_listener == NULL`), check `sk->sk_family == AF_INET6` in `netlbl_conn_setattr()` and `inet6_sk(sk) != NULL` in `calipso_sock_{getattr,setattr,delattr}()`, and allow `sk == NULL` in `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` and `skb == NULL` in `cipso_v4_validate()`. Pass `__GFP_NOWARN` for user-sized arrays in `netlbl_cipsov4_add_std()`. [F46, F47, F48, F146]

## External Contracts
- In `CIPSO_V4_TAG_RANGE`, a final `cat_low` of 0 is omitted on the wire: check `(iter + 4) <= rngcat_len` before reading `cat_low`, and in `cipso_v4_map_cat_rng_hton()` reject `net_cat_len > 30` (`CIPSO_V4_OPT_LEN_MAX - CIPSO_V4_HDR_LEN - CIPSO_V4_TAG_RNG_BLEN`) upfront so `array_cnt` cannot overflow `array[CIPSO_V4_TAG_RNG_CAT_MAX * 2]`. Reject `CIPSO_V4_TAG_LOCAL` in `cipso_v4_validate()` unless `skb != NULL`, `skb->dev->flags & IFF_LOOPBACK`, and `tag_len == CIPSO_V4_TAG_LOC_BLEN` (with host-order `secid` at `&tag[2]`). [F77, F80, F106]
- CALIPSO options require `4n + 2` alignment, a 4-byte-multiple category bitmap, and `< 8` trailing pad bytes to 8-byte align the Hop-by-Hop header; in `calipso_skbuff_setattr()`, pass `start & 3` to `calipso_genopt()` and copy from `buf + (start & 3)` to `(char *)hop + start`. [F78, F109]
- In `xt_SECMARK` and `xt_CONNSECMARK`, register targets for `NFPROTO_IPV4`/`NFPROTO_IPV6` (not `NFPROTO_UNSPEC`), restrict `.checkentry` to `"mangle"`/`"security"` using `pr_info_ratelimited()`, NUL-terminate `info->secctx[SECMARK_SECCTX_MAX - 1]` before `strlen()`, and set `.usersize = offsetof(struct xt_secmark_target_info_v1, secid)`. In `netlabel_mgmt.c`, verify both presence and exact `nla_len()` (`sizeof(struct in_addr)` / `sizeof(struct in6_addr)`) for `NLBL_MGMT_A_IPV{4,6}{ADDR,MASK}`. [F82, F83, F129, F147]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `cipso_v4_skbuff_{setattr,delattr}()` and `calipso_skbuff_{setattr,delattr}()`, call `skb_cow()` first, re-fetch `ip_hdr(skb)`/`ipv6_hdr(skb)` after `skb_cow()`, shift headers with byte-cast `memmove()`, call `skb_reset_network_header(skb)`, and for IPv4 update `iph->ihl`, `iph_set_totlen()`, `ip_send_check(iph)`, and `IPCB(skb)->opt`. [F108, F135]
- Pass `calipso + 2` and `calipso[1]` to `calipso_cache_check()` (keys omit the 2-byte TLV header), and in `cipso_v4_cache_check()` and `calipso_cache_check()`, guard `entry->activity - prev_entry->activity` with `entry->activity > prev_entry->activity` and `prev_entry->activity -= 1` with `prev_entry->activity > 0`. [F79, F104]
- Never test `iter != NULL` or dereference `iter` after `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach*` finishes (`iter` is left at `container_of(head, ...)`). In `netlbl_unlabel_getattr()`, downgrade `family == PF_INET6` to `PF_INET` when `ip_hdr(skb)->version == 4`. Shift `NETLBL_CATMAP_BIT` (`(u64)0x01`), not `1` or `1UL`, in `netlbl_catmap_{setbit,walkrng}()`. [F86, F98, F100]
