# MAXLINEAR ETHERNET PHY DRIVER

This unit provides `phylib` drivers for MaxLinear MXL86110/MXL86111 1GbE PHYs (`drivers/net/phy/mxl-86110.c`) and GPY115/GPY2xx/GPY24x 1G/2.5GbE PHYs (`drivers/net/phy/mxl-gpy.c`), handling Clause 22 extended and Clause 45 MMD/mailbox register access, auto-negotiation, dynamic SGMII/2500Base-X SERDES switching, RGMII delay configuration, Wake-on-LAN (WoL), hardware LED control, and hwmon temperature sensing.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `gpy_set_wol()`, the `WAKE_MAGIC` block must run before the `WAKE_PHY` block because enabling `WAKE_MAGIC` overwrites the entire `PHY_IMASK` via `phy_write(phydev, PHY_IMASK, PHY_IMASK_WOL)`, whereas `WAKE_PHY` updates `PHY_IMASK_LSTC` in place via `phy_set_bits()`/`phy_clear_bits()`. Mutating WoL must also update `priv->wolopts` (`WAKE_MAGIC` and `WAKE_PHY`) in lockstep with `VPSPEC2_WOL_CTL` (`WOL_EN`) and `PHY_IMASK` after MDIO writes succeed, as `gpy_get_wol()` and `gpy_config_intr()` rely on `priv->wolopts` as authoritative. [F23, F42]
- `gpy_config_init()` must not clear `PHY_IMASK`, and `gpy_config_intr()` must OR `PHY_IMASK_WOL` (when `priv->wolopts & WAKE_MAGIC`) and `PHY_IMASK_LSTC` (when `priv->wolopts & WAKE_PHY`) into `PHY_IMASK` even when `phydev->interrupts != PHY_INTERRUPT_ENABLED`, so WoL interrupt masks survive suspend/resume reinitialization. [F24]
- In `mxl86110_set_wol()`, enabling or disabling `WAKE_MAGIC` must update both `MXL86110_EXT_WOL_CFG_REG` (`MXL86110_WOL_CFG_WOL_MASK`) and the Clause 22 `PHY_IRQ_ENABLE_REG` (`PHY_IRQ_ENABLE_REG_WOL`) under `phy_lock_mdio_bus()`. [F28]
- In `gpy_config_aneg()` and `gpy_update_interface()`, when `phydev->interface` is `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_USXGMII` or `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_INTERNAL`, the interface mode is fixed and the driver must not call `gpy_2500basex_chk()`, switch `phydev->interface` to SGMII/2500Base-X, or modify `VSPEC1_SGMII_CTRL`. [F27]
- When `gpy_update_interface()` or `gpy_2500basex_chk()` switches `phydev->interface` to `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_2500BASEX` for `SPEED_2500`, it must clear `VSPEC1_SGMII_CTRL_ANEN` in `VSPEC1_SGMII_CTRL`; when switching back to `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_SGMII` for 10/100/1000Mbps while SGMII ANEG is disabled (`!gpy_sgmii_aneg_en(phydev)`), it must set `VSPEC1_SGMII_ANEN_ANRS`. [F63]
- On MXL86111, `mxl86111_probe()` sets `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHY_REG` to `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYUTP_SPACE` as the default Clause 22 register page; any access to Fiber/SerDes space (`MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYFIBER_SPACE`) must use phylib paged helpers (such as `phy_modify_paged()`) so the active page is restored. [F29]
- In `mxl-86110.c`, all LEDs share `MXL86110_COM_EXT_LED_GEN_CFG`: `mxl86110_led_hw_control_set()` must clear `MXL86110_COM_EXT_LED_GEN_CFG_LFE(index)` after writing `MXL86110_LED0_CFG_REG + index`, and both it and `mxl86110_led_brightness_set()` must modify only that `index`'s `3 * index` shifted bitfield. Furthermore, because `mxl86110_led_hw_control_set()` overwrites `MXL86110_LED0_CFG_REG + index` and hardware only blinks an LED when ON by default, it must set `MXL86110_LEDX_CFG_BLINK` whenever `TRIGGER_NETDEV_TX` or `TRIGGER_NETDEV_RX` is requested. [F30, F31]
- In `mxl-gpy.c`, all four LEDs share `PHY_LED` while each has its own `VSPEC1_LED(index)` MMD register: `gpy_led_hw_control_set()` must set `PHY_LED_HWCONTROL(index)` in `PHY_LED` after writing `VSPEC1_LED(index)`, and `gpy_led_brightness_set()` with `LED_OFF` must clear `PHY_LED_HWCONTROL(index) | PHY_LED_ON(index)` in `PHY_LED` and clear `VSPEC1_LED(index)`. [F32]
- In `gpy_read_status()`, `phydev->speed`, `phydev->duplex`, `phydev->pause`, and `phydev->asym_pause` must be reset to `SPEED_UNKNOWN`, `DUPLEX_UNKNOWN`, and `0` before reading link partner abilities and `PHY_MIISTAT`, and `phydev->lp_advertising` must be cleared with `linkmode_zero()` when autoneg is disabled. [F19]
- In `gpy_sgmii_need_reaneg()`, `ver_need_sgmii_reaneg[]` must contain at most one entry per firmware `major` version because the loop executes `break` immediately after testing `priv->fw_minor < ver_need_sgmii_reaneg[i].minor` on the first matching `priv->fw_major`. [F48]
- In `gpy_hwmon_read()`, the `VSPEC1_TEMP_STA` reading must be masked to 10 bits via `FIELD_GET(VSPEC1_TEMP_STA_DATA, ret)` before calling `polynomial_calc(&poly_N_to_temp, ...)`, and `poly_N_to_temp` must keep factor-redistributed per-degree dividers (`1000`) and non-zero `divider_leftover` values to prevent 32-bit `long` overflow or division by zero. [F50]
- `gpy_hwmon_register()` must pass `phydev` as `drvdata` to `devm_hwmon_device_register_with_info()`, and `gpy_hwmon_hwmon_ops` callbacks such as `gpy_hwmon_read()` must retrieve `struct phy_device *` via `dev_get_drvdata(dev)`. Broken by: passing `priv` as `drvdata` or using `to_phy_device(dev)`. [F53]
- When adding or modifying a PHY ID or mask in `mxl_phy_drvs[]` (`mxl-86110.c`) or `gpy_drivers[]` (`mxl-gpy.c`), the corresponding `mdio_device_id` entry in `mxl_tbl[]` or `gpy_tbl[]` must be updated in lockstep. [F33]

## Synchronization

- In `mxl-86110.c`, `__mxl86110_write_extended_reg()`, `__mxl86110_read_extended_reg()`, `__mxl86110_modify_extended_reg()`, `mxl86110_synce_clk_cfg()`, `mxl86110_broadcast_cfg()`, `mxl86110_enable_led_activity_blink()`, and `mxl86110_config_rgmii_delay()` require the caller to hold `phy_lock_mdio_bus(phydev)` and must not lock/unlock it or call locking helpers (`mxl86110_*_extended_reg()`, `phy_modify()`). [F1]
- In `mxl-86110.c`, `mxl86110_write_extended_reg()`, `mxl86110_read_extended_reg()`, `mxl86110_modify_extended_reg()`, and paged phylib helpers such as `phy_modify_paged()` acquire `phydev->mdio.bus->mdio_lock` internally and must never be called while holding `phy_lock_mdio_bus(phydev)`. [F8]
- `mxl86111_read_page()` and `mxl86111_write_page()` are called by phylib (`phy_save_page()`/`phy_select_page()`/`phy_restore_page()`) with `phydev->mdio.bus->mdio_lock` already held, so they must use `__mxl86110_read_extended_reg()` and `__mxl86110_modify_extended_reg()` and never call `phy_lock_mdio_bus()` or `mxl86110_*_extended_reg()`. [F71]
- In `mxl86110_set_wol()`, `mxl86110_config_init()`, and `mxl86111_config_init()`, `phy_lock_mdio_bus(phydev)` is acquired at entry; every error path after acquisition must `goto out;` so `phy_unlock_mdio_bus(phydev)` is always executed before returning. [F3]
- In `mxl-gpy.c`, multi-step `MDIO_MMD_VEND1` mailbox transactions in `gpy_mbox_read()` (`VSPEC1_MBOX_ADDRLO`, `VSPEC1_MBOX_CMD`, polling `VSPEC1_MBOX_CMD_READY`, and reading `VSPEC1_MBOX_DATA`) must be serialized by `priv->mbox_lock`, which nests inside `phydev->lock` and outside `mdio_bus->mdio_lock`. Never acquire `priv->mbox_lock` while holding `phy_lock_mdio_bus(phydev)`. [F9]
- `gpy_mbox_read()` and `gpy_handle_interrupt()` sleep (`mutex_lock(&priv->mbox_lock)` and `phy_read_mmd_poll_timeout()`) and must only be called from sleepable process or threaded-IRQ context, never from hardirq or spinlock context. [F74]
- `gpy_hwmon_read()` is called by the hwmon sysfs layer without holding `phydev->lock`, so it must only perform MDIO bus-locked register reads (`phy_read_mmd()`) and must not access or modify mutable `phydev` or `struct gpy_priv` state protected by `phydev->lock`. [F75]

## API Contracts

- `__mxl86110_read_extended_reg()`, `mxl86110_read_extended_reg()`, and `gpy_mbox_read()` return a 16-bit unsigned value (`0..0xffff`) on success or a negative errno on failure; callers must store the return value in a signed `int` and check `< 0` rather than storing in `u16` or testing `if (ret)`. [F12]
- Every LED callback in `mxl-86110.c` and `mxl-gpy.c` must validate the 0-based `index` against `MXL86110_MAX_LEDS` (3) or `GPY_MAX_LEDS` (4) and return `-EINVAL` before computing register offsets (`MXL86110_LED0_CFG_REG + index`, `VSPEC1_LED(index)`) or bit shifts (`MXL86110_COM_EXT_LED_GEN_CFG_LFE(index)`, `PHY_LED_POLARITY(index)`). [F20]
- LED trigger callbacks in both drivers treat `rules` as a `BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_*)` bitmask (not raw enum values), and `gpy_led_polarity_set()` iterates bit positions in `modes` up to `__PHY_LED_MODES_NUM`. In `gpy_led_hw_control_get()`, `BIT(TRIGGER_NETDEV_LINK)` must be set in `*rules` only when `FIELD_GET(VSPEC1_LED_CON, val)` equals the full four-speed mask `(VSPEC1_LED_LINK10 | VSPEC1_LED_LINK100 | VSPEC1_LED_LINK1000 | VSPEC1_LED_LINK2500)`, whereas individual speed triggers test single bits with `&`. [F44, F80]
- `mxl86111_read_page()` and `mxl86111_write_page()` exchange the pre-shifted bitmask value in `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYSPACE_MASK` (`BIT(1)`: `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYFIBER_SPACE` = `0x2` or `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYUTP_SPACE` = `0x0`), so paged access callers such as `mxl86111_config_inband()` must pass the shifted constant rather than an unshifted page number. [F21]
- `mxl86111_probe()` must read the bootstrap mode from `MXL86110_EXT_CHIP_CFG_REG`, reject modes other than `MXL86111_EXT_CHIP_CFG_MODE_UTP_TO_SGMII` and `MXL86111_EXT_CHIP_CFG_MODE_UTP_TO_RGMII` with `-EOPNOTSUPP`, and select the UTP page by writing `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHYUTP_SPACE` to `MXL86111_EXT_SMI_SDS_PHY_REG`. [F67]
- In `mxl86110_config_rgmii_delay()`, `phydev->interface` must be one of `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_RGMII`, `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_RGMII_RXID`, `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_RGMII_TXID`, or `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_RGMII_ID`; any other mode hits `default` and returns `-EINVAL` without touching `MXL86110_EXT_RGMII_CFG1_REG` or `MXL86110_EXT_CHIP_CFG_REG`. [F49]
- Every `struct phy_driver` in `gpy_drivers[]` that uses `gpy_config_aneg`, `gpy_config_intr`, `gpy_handle_interrupt`, `gpy_set_wol`, `gpy_get_wol`, `gpy_loopback`, or `gpy115_loopback` must set `.probe = gpy_probe` so `phydev->priv` points to a valid `struct gpy_priv`. GPY21x entries that dynamically switch between `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_SGMII` and `PHY_INTERFACE_MODE_2500BASEX` must also use `gpy21x_config_init` to populate both modes in `phydev->possible_interfaces`. [F54, F81]
- In `gpy_loopback()`, disabling loopback when `BMCR_LOOPBACK` was previously set (`phy_modify()` returns `> 0`) must record a 3-second cooldown deadline (`get_jiffies_64() + HZ * 3`) in `priv->lb_dis_to`; enabling loopback must sleep until `priv->lb_dis_to` expires before setting `BMCR_LOOPBACK`, and then sleep 100ms. [F62]

## Error Handling

- In `gpy_config_aneg()`, `genphy_c45_an_config_aneg()` and `phy_modify_changed()` return `< 0` on error, `0` when unchanged, and `> 0` when advertisements changed; positive returns must set `changed = true` before calling `genphy_c45_check_and_restart_aneg()`. Broken by: checking `if (ret) return ret;`, which aborts auto-negotiation with a positive return value. [F14]
- In `gpy_config_aneg()`, when `phy_read_poll_timeout()` polls `MII_BMSR` for `BMSR_LSTATUS` while `phydev->state == PHY_UP`, `-ETIMEDOUT` must return `0` without writing `VSPEC1_SGMII_CTRL_ANRS`, whereas any other negative errno must be returned to the caller. [F47]
- In `gpy_handle_interrupt()`, MDIO or mailbox read failures (`phy_read(phydev, PHY_ISTAT) < 0` or `gpy_mbox_read(phydev, REG_GPIO0_OUT) < 0`) must call `phy_error(phydev)` and return `IRQ_NONE`, while handled interrupts must invoke `phy_trigger_machine(phydev)` and return `IRQ_HANDLED`. [F79]
- In `gpy_hwmon_read()`, a return value of `0` from `phy_read_mmd(phydev, MDIO_MMD_VEND1, VSPEC1_TEMP_STA)` indicates no temperature sample is ready and must return `-ENODATA` before calling `polynomial_calc()`. [F13]
- `mxl86110_set_wol()` must verify that `phydev->attached_dev` is non-NULL (returning `-ENODEV` if NULL) before dereferencing `netdev->dev_addr` to program the magic-packet MAC address registers. [F17]
- In `gpy_led_polarity_set()`, both the `default` branch of `switch (mode)` inside `for_each_set_bit()` and the post-loop path when neither `force_active_low` nor `force_active_high` is set (e.g. `modes == 0`) must return `-EINVAL`. [F40]
- In `gpy_config_mdix()`, the `default` branch of `switch (ctrl)` must return `0` without modifying `PHY_CTL1` or calling `genphy_c45_restart_aneg()`, so unconfigured MDI-X values such as `ETH_TP_MDI_INVALID` do not fail `gpy_config_aneg()` or restart auto-negotiation. [F46]

## External Contracts

- To program the 6-byte WoL MAC address, `mxl86110_set_wol()` writes `(dev_addr[2*i] << 8) | dev_addr[2*i + 1]` in ascending register order (`MXL86110_EXT_MAC_ADDR_CFG1`..`CFG3`), whereas `gpy_set_wol()` writes them in descending register order (`VPSPEC2_WOL_AD45` down to `VPSPEC2_WOL_AD01`). [F36]
- `gpy_mbox_read()` must split the 24-bit internal bus address `addr` by writing the low 16 bits to `VSPEC1_MBOX_ADDRLO` (`0x6`) and packing `addr >> 16` into `VSPEC1_MBOX_CMD_ADDRHI` (`GENMASK(7, 0)`) of `VSPEC1_MBOX_CMD` (`0x7`). [F39]
- `gpy_probe()` must set `PHY_F_NO_IRQ` in `phydev->dev_flags` on all GPY PHY variants unless the boolean device property `"maxlinear,use-broken-interrupts"` is present on `&phydev->mdio.dev`. [F58]

## Known Pitfalls

- Because `PHY_ISTAT` is read-to-clear for all pending interrupt bits, when `gpy_set_wol()` reads `PHY_ISTAT` while enabling `WAKE_PHY` (which leaves normal PHY interrupts active), it must check `ret & (PHY_IMASK_MASK & ~PHY_IMASK_LSTC)` and call `phy_trigger_machine(phydev)` so concurrently pending PHY state machine interrupts are not lost. [F25]
- In `gpy_handle_interrupt()`, after reading `PHY_ISTAT`, if `PHY_IMASK_LSTC` or `PHY_IMASK_LSPC` is set, the handler must call `gpy_mbox_read(phydev, REG_GPIO0_OUT)` to stall on the internal mailbox bus until the PHY releases the MDINT interrupt line. [F59]
- In `gpy_config_aneg()`, when `phydev->state == PHY_UP` on firmware versions matched by `gpy_sgmii_need_reaneg()` (`ver_need_sgmii_reaneg`) and the link is neither `USXGMII`/`INTERNAL`, `2500Base-X`, nor SGMII-ANEG-disabled, the driver must poll `MII_BMSR` for `BMSR_LSTATUS` (up to 4s) and set `VSPEC1_SGMII_CTRL_ANRS` to retrigger SGMII AN. [F64]
- When disabling loopback on GPY115B/GPY115C PHYs in `gpy115_loopback()`, if `priv->fw_minor <= 0x76`, the driver must reset the PHY via `genphy_soft_reset(phydev)` instead of calling `gpy_loopback(phydev, 0, 0)`. [F61]
- In `gpy_loopback()`, the 64-bit jiffies deadline `priv->lb_dis_to` must be checked with `time_before64(now, priv->lb_dis_to)` before computing `jiffies64_to_msecs(priv->lb_dis_to - now)` for `msleep()`, preventing unsigned `u64` subtraction underflow when the cooldown has already elapsed. [F52]
- In `gpy_probe()`, the `PHY_FWV` value read via `phy_read()` must be kept in `fw_version` rather than `ret` so that calling `gpy_hwmon_register(phydev)` does not clobber the raw firmware version before `phydev_info()` logs it and checks `PHY_FWV_REL_MASK`. [F41]
- In `mxl86110_config_rgmii_delay()`, after programming digital RGMII delays in `MXL86110_EXT_RGMII_CFG1_REG`, the driver must clear `MXL86110_EXT_CHIP_CFG_RXDLY_ENABLE` in `MXL86110_EXT_CHIP_CFG_REG` to disable the default additional hardware RX_CLK delay. [F68]
- In `gpy_led_hw_control_set()`, when `TRIGGER_NETDEV_TX` or `TRIGGER_NETDEV_RX` is enabled without any link-speed trigger (`!(val & VSPEC1_LED_CON)`), `val` written to `VSPEC1_LED(index)` must include `FIELD_PREP(VSPEC1_LED_PULSE, VSPEC1_LED_NO_CON) | VSPEC1_LED_CON` before setting `PHY_LED_HWCONTROL(index)` in `PHY_LED`. [F69]
