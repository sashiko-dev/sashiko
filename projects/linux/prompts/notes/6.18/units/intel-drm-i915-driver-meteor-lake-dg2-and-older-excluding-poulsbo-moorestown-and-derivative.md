# INTEL DRM I915 DRIVER (Meteor Lake, DG2 and older excluding Poulsbo, Moorestown and derivative)

`drivers/gpu/drm/i915/gt/uc` implements firmware loading, Command Transport Buffer (CTB) communication (`intel_guc_ct`), hardware context scheduling and virtual/parallel submission (`intel_guc_submission`), Single Loop Power Control (`intel_guc_slpc`) and RC6 (`intel_guc_rc`), firmware log relay and error register capture (`intel_guc_log`, `intel_guc_capture`), HWConfig retrieval (`intel_guc_hwconfig`), and HuC authentication (`intel_huc`) for the Intel GuC and HuC microcontrollers.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In GuC request submission and retirement (`__guc_add_request`, `guc_wq_item_append`, `need_tasklet`, `remove_from_context`, `guc_request_alloc`, `guc_bump_inflight_request_prio`, `guc_retire_inflight_request_prio`), context scheduling state and `guc_id` must be accessed on `request_to_scheduling_context(rq)` (the parent context) rather than `rq->context`. [F115]
- The `guc_id` space `[0, guc->submission_state.num_guc_ids)` is split at `NUMBER_MULTI_LRC_GUC_ID(guc)`: parent contexts allocate regions of order `order_base_2(ce->parallel.number_children + 1)` in `[0, NUMBER_MULTI_LRC_GUC_ID(guc))` from `guc_ids_bitmap` (`new_guc_id`/`__release_guc_id`), while single-LRC contexts use `guc_ids` IDA; parent/child contexts must never use `guc_ids` IDA, `guc_id_list`, or `guc_ids_in_use`. [F87, F139]
- When `steal_guc_id()` steals a `guc_id` from victim `cn`, it must clear `SCHED_STATE_REGISTERED` on `cn` and invalidate `cn->guc_id.id` without calling `clr_ctx_id_mapping()`, so `try_context_registration()` sees `ctx_id_mapped(guc, ctx_id)` and deregisters the old context first. [F72]
- `init_sched_state()` must mask `ce->guc_state.sched_state` with `SCHED_STATE_BLOCKED_MASK` rather than assigning `0` to preserve the upper-bit blocked counter; in `__unwind_incomplete_requests()`, `ce->guc_state.requests` must be traversed with `list_for_each_entry_safe_reverse()` because requests are re-inserted via `list_add(&rq->sched.link, pl)`. [F101, F107]
- In `intel_guc_busyness_park()`, `__update_guc_busyness_running_state()` (clearing `engine->stats.guc.running` under `guc->timestamp.lock`) and `guc_cancel_busyness_worker(guc)` must precede the `last_stat_jiffies` throttle return and `__update_guc_busyness_stats()`. In `__reset_guc_busyness_stats()`, if `stats->running` is set under `guc->timestamp.lock`, `guc->timestamp.gt_stamp - stats->start_gt_clk` must be added to `stats->total_gt_clks` before zeroing `prev_total` and `running`; `guc_action_enable_usage_stats()` must reset every engine's `guc_engine_usage_record` via `__set_engine_usage_record(engine, 0, 0xffffffff, 0)` before sending `INTEL_GUC_ACTION_SET_ENG_UTIL_BUFF`. [F62, F63, F65, F159]
- In `intel_guc_slpc_enable()`, `intel_guc_slpc_set_ignore_eff_freq(slpc, slpc->ignore_eff_freq)` must precede `slpc_set_softlimits(slpc)`. In `guc_create_virtual()`, `ve->base.mask` must be seeded with `VIRTUAL_ENGINES` alongside `I915_ENGINE_IS_VIRTUAL` before OR-ing sibling masks, and when enabling a switchout workaround (`Wa_14014475959`, `Wa_16019325821`, `Wa_14019159160`) in `guc_ctl_wa_flags()` / `guc_ads_waklv_init()`, `guc_default_vfuncs()` must set `I915_ENGINE_USES_WA_HOLD_SWITCHOUT` on the same engine classes and IP/platform range. [F66, F67, F146]

## Synchronization
- Spinlock nesting in `intel_guc_submission.c` must follow `sched_engine->lock -> ce->guc_state.lock` and `guc->submission_state.lock -> ce->guc_state.lock`; `__guc_signal_context_fence()` (under `ce->guc_state.lock`) must defer `i915_sw_fence_complete(&rq->submit)` via `irq_work_queue(&rq->submit_work)`. All mutations and traversals of `ce->guc_state.requests` and `ce->guc_state.prio_count` require `ce->guc_state.lock`. [F22, F161]
- When iterating `guc->context_lookup` (`scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h`, `intel_guc_submission_reset`, `intel_guc_submission_cancel_requests`), callers must pin `ce` via `kref_get_unless_zero(&ce->ref)` and `xa_unlock(&guc->context_lookup)` before taking `ce->guc_state.lock`, `sched_engine->lock`, or calling `release_guc_id()`, and every skip/exit path after unlocking must call `intel_context_put(ce)` and re-acquire `xa_lock(&guc->context_lookup)`. In `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()`, `intel_context_put(ce)` may only be called when `do_put` is true because `SCHED_STATE_DESTROYED` contexts have `ce->ref == 0` and are freed via `__guc_context_destroy(ce)`. [F7, F108, F170]
- Paths outside `sched_engine->lock` that arm pending G2H flags in `ce->guc_state.sched_state` (`try_context_registration`, `guc_context_block`, `guc_context_unblock`, `guc_context_revoke`, `guc_lrc_desc_unpin`) must check `submission_disabled(guc)` under `ce->guc_state.lock`. Callers of `__guc_context_sched_disable()` must sample `ce->guc_id.id` via `prep_context_pending_disable(ce)` under `ce->guc_state.lock` and pass that local copy after unlocking. [F24, F73]
- Before `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()` runs in `intel_guc_submission_reset_prepare()`, submission and interrupts must be disabled and `gt->irq_lock`, `guc->ct.receive_tasklet`, `sched_engine->lock`, `destroyed_contexts`, and `guc->ct.requests.worker` must be flushed. In `intel_guc_sched_done_process_msg()`, `intel_context_sched_disable_unpin(ce)` must precede acquiring `ce->guc_state.lock` and calling `__guc_signal_context_fence(ce)`. [F23, F75]
- `guc_cancel_busyness_worker()` must use `cancel_delayed_work(&guc->timestamp.work)` instead of `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` whenever `mutex_is_locked(&gt->reset.mutex)` or `test_bit(I915_RESET_BACKOFF, &gt->reset.flags)` is true, and `intel_guc_submission_fini()` must call `guc_fini_engine_stats()`. `guc_timestamp_ping()` must take `intel_runtime_pm_get_if_active(&gt->i915->runtime_pm)` before `intel_gt_reset_trylock()` and hold it past `guc_enable_busyness_worker()`. [F29, F154]
- Under `guc->timestamp.lock`, `guc_engine_busyness()` must sample `i915_reset_count(gpu_error)` before checking `I915_RESET_BACKOFF` and re-check it after `guc_update_engine_gt_clks()` and `guc_update_pm_timestamp()`, rolling back `stats` and `gt_stamp` if it changed. `__get_engine_usage_record()` must read `last_switch_in_stamp`, `current_context_index`, and `total_runtime` in a bounded retry loop until two consecutive reads match. [F31, F32]
- Atomic/reclaim rules: `intel_guc_send_busy_loop()` must check `!in_atomic() && !irqs_disabled()` on `-EBUSY` and busy-wait with `cpu_relax()` when atomic; `guc_context_destroy()` must defer deregistration to `destroyed_worker`; `intel_guc_engine_failure_process_msg()` must record `engine->mask` under `guc->submission_state.lock` and queue `reset_fail_worker`; `ct_alloc_msg()` must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F35, F162, F163, F164]
- In `guc_send_invalidate_tlb()`, `xa_alloc_cyclic_irq()` must use `GFP_ATOMIC | __GFP_NOWARN` and on failure fall back to `guc->serial_slot` by waiting for `!READ_ONCE(wq->busy)` via `wait_event_lock_irq(..., guc->tlb_lookup.xa_lock)` and setting `wq->busy = true` before unlocking; cleanup must wait via `must_wait_woken()` and only call `xa_erase_irq(&guc->tlb_lookup, seqno)` if `seqno != guc->serial_slot`. In `ct_handle_event()`, `g2h_release_space()` for `SCHED_CONTEXT_MODE_DONE`, `DEREGISTER_CONTEXT_DONE`, and `TLB_INVALIDATION_DONE` must run in IRQ/tasklet context, with `TLB_INVALIDATION_DONE` calling `ct_process_request()` immediately. [F13, F39, F165, F166]

## Object Lifetimes
- `intel_huc_init_early()` must unconditionally call `delayed_huc_load_init(huc)` before returning on `!vcs_supported(gt)`, and `intel_huc_fini_late()` must unconditionally call `delayed_huc_load_fini(huc)`. `huc->delayed_load.timer` and `huc->delayed_load.fence` must be armed together in `huc_delayed_load_start()` (only when `i915_sw_fence_done(&huc->delayed_load.fence)`) and disarmed together via `delayed_huc_load_complete(huc)` or `gsc_init_error()` on all completion, error, sanitize, and teardown paths. [F1, F79]
- When `guc_context_block()` or `guc_context_revoke()` arms `prep_context_pending_disable(ce)` outside the unpin path, it must first execute `atomic_add(2, &ce->pin_count)` to balance the `-2` in `intel_context_sched_disable_unpin(ce)`. Before reading `ce->lrc_reg_state` or calling `lrc_update_runtime(ce)` on a context from `guc->context_lookup` (`guc_log_context`, `guc_context_update_stats`), the caller must pin it with `intel_context_pin_if_active(ce)` and unpin afterward. [F6, F26]
- Context pin/destroy rules: `guc_child_context_pin()` pins `ce->parallel.parent` and `intel_context_unpin(ce->parallel.parent)` must run in `guc_child_context_post_unpin()` after `lrc_post_unpin(ce)`; in `guc_create_parallel()`, once children are bound via `intel_context_bind_parent_child(parent, ce)`, error unwind must only call `intel_context_put(parent)` (not bound children); `__guc_context_destroy()` must free virtual contexts by putting `ve->base.breadcrumbs` and calling `kfree(ve)` rather than `intel_context_free(ce)`. [F10, F11, F12]
- In `__guc_reset_context()`, `intel_context_get_active_request(ce)` returns a refcounted `struct i915_request *` that must be released with `i915_request_put(rq)` before advancing; in `__guc_add_request()`, if `intel_guc_send_nb()` fails when `!enabled`, it must call `clr_context_pending_enable(ce)` and `intel_context_put(ce)` without incrementing `guc->outstanding_submission_g2h`. [F3, F9]
- In `ct_send()`, the stack `struct ct_request` linked into `ct->requests.pending` and the `GUC_CTB_HXG_MSG_MAX_LEN` dwords reserved via `g2h_reserve_space()` must be unlinked under `ct->requests.lock` and released via `g2h_release_space()` on every exit or `resend` path. In `ct_read()`, ownership of `struct ct_incoming_msg` passes to `ct_handle_msg()`: synchronous handlers (`ct_handle_response`, `ct_process_request`) free `msg` on success, while `ct_handle_msg()` and `ct_process_incoming_requests()` must call `ct_free_msg()` when their callee returns non-zero. [F16, F17]
- In `intel_guc_log_create()`, if `i915_gem_object_pin_map_unlocked()` fails, release `log->vma` via `i915_vma_unpin_and_release(&log->vma, 0)` (no `I915_VMA_RELEASE_MAP`), whereas `intel_guc_log_destroy()` must clear `log->buf_addr = NULL` and pass `I915_VMA_RELEASE_MAP`. [F18]

## API Contracts
- `pin_guc_id()` returns `1` for a new `guc_id`, `0` when an existing `guc_id` is pinned, and `< 0` on error; callers (`guc_request_alloc`, `guc_parent_context_pin`, `guc_kernel_context_pin`) must check `ret < 0`. Synchronous `ct_send()` / `intel_guc_ct_send()` return `request.response_len` (if `response_buf` is non-NULL) or `GUC_HXG_RESPONSE_MSG_0_DATA0` (`>= 0`) on success, so callers expecting zero status data must convert `ret > 0` to `-EPROTO`. `ct_read()` and `ct_receive()` return `< 0` on corruption, `0` with `*msg = NULL` when empty, `available - len >= 0` with `*msg` on success, or `available > 0` with `*msg = NULL` when `ct_alloc_msg()` fails (so `ct_try_receive_message()` reschedules `receive_tasklet`). [F44, F45, F48]
- In `struct intel_guc_ct_buffer`, `size`, `resv_space`, `head`, `tail`, and `space` are in dwords while `guc_ct_buffer_init()` and `ct_register_buffer()` take byte sizes; `g2h_len_dw` passed to `intel_guc_send_nb()` and `guc_submission_send_busy_loop()` must be `0` or the G2H payload length in dwords excluding `GUC_CTB_HXG_MSG_MIN_LEN` (added by `G2H_LEN_DW()`), and `guc_submission_send_busy_loop()` requires `loop == true` whenever `g2h_len_dw > 0`. [F58, F85]
- `__guc_reset_context()` and `intel_guc_submission_reset()` take `stalled` as an `intel_engine_mask_t` bitmask of hung engines, and `__guc_reset_context()` must test `stalled & ce->engine->mask` when marking a started request `guilty`. [F57]

## Error Handling
- In `guc_submission_send_busy_loop()`, `guc->outstanding_submission_g2h` is only incremented when `g2h_len_dw` is non-zero, so the failure path when `intel_guc_send_busy_loop()` fails must also check `g2h_len_dw` before decrementing `guc->outstanding_submission_g2h`. [F2]
- Callers of `intel_guc_allocate_vma()` (`intel_huc_init`, `intel_guc_log_create`) must check `IS_ERR(vma)` on a local pointer and set `err = PTR_ERR(vma)` before jumping to cleanup, never storing an `ERR_PTR` into `huc->heci_pkt` or `log->vma`. [F43]
- In `wait_for_ct_request_update()`, `done` is true both on response arrival and when `intel_guc_ct_enabled(ct)` becomes false, so it must set `err = -ENODEV` when `!ct_enabled` and always set `*status = req->status`. In `guc_wait_ucode()`, `guc_load_done()` returns `true` for both `READY` (`*success = true`) and terminal error states (`*success = false`), so `guc_wait_ucode()` must check `if (ret || !success)` and replace `ret == 0` with a negative errno (`-ENXIO`). [F46, F47]
- In `guc_request_alloc()`, when `try_context_registration()` returns `-EPIPE`, it must call `disable_submission(guc)` and jump to `out` (returning `0` with `ce->guc_id.ref` kept pinned for reset cleanup), whereas any other error must decrement `ce->guc_id.ref`, call `unpin_guc_id(guc, ce)`, and return `ret`. In `guc_lrc_desc_unpin()`, if `deregister_context()` fails, rollback must re-acquire `ce->guc_state.lock` and check `context_destroyed(ce)` before restoring `SCHED_STATE_REGISTERED`, clearing `SCHED_STATE_DESTROYED`, and calling `intel_wakeref_put_async(&gt->wakeref)` after unlocking. [F49, F50]

## External Contracts
- G2H notification handlers in `intel_guc_submission.c` must validate payload `len` and look up `ctx_id` via `g2h_context_lookup(guc, ctx_id)` rather than `__get_context()`. GuC submission must branch on `GUC_SUBMIT_VER(guc) >= MAKE_GUC_VER(1, 0, 0)` to maintain both v69 shared-memory descriptors and v70 inline H2G registration; in `__guc_wq_item_append()`, if `wqi_size > wq_space_until_wrap(ce)`, `guc_wq_noop_append(ce)` must pad to `WQ_SIZE` before writing `WQ_TYPE_MULTI_LRC` with `WQ_LEN_MASK` set to `(wqi_size / sizeof(u32)) - 1` and `WQ_RING_TAIL_MASK` in qwords (`tail / sizeof(u64)`). [F86, F88, F91]

## Known Pitfalls
- Integer width and wrap-around: cast `i915_ggtt_offset(ce->state)` to `u64` before adding `__get_parent_scratch_offset(ce)` or `__get_wq_offset(ce)` in `prepare_context_registration_info_v70()`; cast `gt_stamp_hi` to `u64` before `<< 32` in `__extend_last_switch()` and `guc_update_pm_timestamp()`, while keeping `new_start` and `gt_stamp_last` as `u32` in `__extend_last_switch()` and truncating `total - stats->prev_total` to `u32` in `guc_update_engine_gt_clks()`; and keep `available` as signed `s32` in `ct_read()` so `tail - head < 0` detects wrap. [F116, F117, F118, F119, F121]
- Pointer/unit scaling and table indexing: `__get_parent_scratch()` must subtract `LRC_STATE_OFFSET` and divide byte offsets by `sizeof(u32)` when indexing `u32 *ce->lrc_reg_state` (and `get_wq_pointer()` must divide `wqi_tail` by `sizeof(u32)`), whereas GGTT offsets add `__get_parent_scratch_offset(ce)` directly to `i915_ggtt_offset(ce->state)`; convert engine classes via `engine_class_to_guc_class`/`guc_class_to_engine_class` and use `i915_increase_reset_engine_count()`; GuC log buffer layout and `log->stats[]` follow `enum guc_log_buffer_type` (`DEBUG`, `CRASH_DUMP`, `CAPTURE`) while `log->sizes[]` is indexed by `GUC_LOG_SECTIONS_*` (`CRASH`, `DEBUG`, `CAPTURE`) with `sections[i].default_val` in bytes and 0-based `count = (bytes / units) - 1`. [F56, F89, F90, F97, F134]
- Control-flow and HW hazards: in `guc_context_unblock()`, `SCHED_STATE_NO_UNBLOCK` must test `SCHED_STATE_MULTI_BLOCKED_MASK` because `context_cant_unblock(ce)` runs before `decr_context_blocked(ce)`; in `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()`, `if (pending_enable || destroyed || deregister)` and `if (pending_disable)` must remain separate `if` statements; in `__guc_reset_context()`, post-loop calls (`__unwind_incomplete_requests`, `intel_context_put`) must use `parent` rather than `ce`; `COMMON_GEN8BASE_GLOBAL` in `intel_guc_capture.c` must not contain pre-Gen12-only registers like `GEN8_FAULT_TLB_DATA0`/`1` (which belong in `GEN8_GLOBAL`); and `guc_prepare_xfer()` must program `GUC_SHIM_CONTROL` before `intel_uc_fw_upload()` and update `GEN7_MISCCPCTL` via `intel_uncore_rmw()`. [F102, F103, F109, F147, F151]
