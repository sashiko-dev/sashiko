# HISILICON QM DRIVER

The HiSilicon Queue Manager (QM) driver provides common PCIe queue management, hardware mailbox, doorbell, interrupt handling, SR-IOV, error recovery/reset, runtime PM, and hardware scatter-gather list (SGL) pool infrastructure for HiSilicon Kunpeng hardware accelerators (such as SEC, HPRE, and ZIP). It supports both kernel crypto subsystem consumers via queue pairs (`hisi_qp`) and user-space SVA (Shared Virtual Addressing) consumers through the UACCE framework (`uacce_qm_ops`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `qm->err_ini` is only populated on `QM_HW_PF` (`NULL` on VFs); functions reachable on VFs or via `pci_physfn()` (`qm_check_dev_error()`, `hisi_qm_dev_err_init()`, `hisi_qm_dev_err_uninit()`, `qm_clear_device()`) must return early when `fun_type == QM_HW_VF`, and PF callers must null-check optional `hisi_qm_err_ini` hooks (`set_priv_status`). In `hisi_qm_hw_ops`, `get_vft`, `hw_error_uninit`, and `hw_error_handle` are `NULL` on V1, and `set_ifc_begin`/`set_ifc_end`/`get_ifc` are `NULL` on V1/V2; callers must null-check them or gate IFC calls on `QM_SUPPORT_MB_COMMAND`. [F36, F37]
- Because `qm_alloc_uacce()` failure in `hisi_qm_init()` only logs a warning, `qm->uacce` can be `NULL` and `qm->isolate_data` uninitialized even when `qm->mode == UACCE_MODE_SVA`; callers must check `qm->uacce` (`hisi_qm_set_algs()`) or `qm->use_sva` (`qm_remove_uacce()`). Furthermore, `isolate_data` is owned by `pf_qm` (`pci_physfn(qm->pdev)`): VFs must read `is_isolate` and `err_threshold` from `pf_qm->isolate_data`, only PF may write `err_threshold`, and `hisi_qm_isolate_threshold_write()` must flush `qm_hw_errs` via `qm_hw_err_destroy()`. [F35, F59]
- Across PF/VF management, VFTs, QoS, and IFC (`qm->factor[]`, `hisi_qm_set_vft()`, `qm_set_vft_common()`, `qm_wait_vf_prepare_finish()`, `qm_ping_single_vf()`, `qm_ping_all_vfs()`, `qm_cmd_process()`, `qm_get_ifc_pf()`), index `0` is PF while VFs use 1-based indices `1 .. qm->vfs_num` (`BIT(1) .. BIT(qm->vfs_num)`). In `qm_set_vft_common()` for `SHAPER_VFT`, `qm->factor[fun_num]` must be indexed before ORing `base << QM_SHAPER_VFT_OFFSET` into `fun_num`. [F46, F74]
- `qm->factor` is allocated only when `QM_SUPPORT_FUNC_QOS` is set in `qm->caps`, so all `qm->factor` accesses must check `QM_SUPPORT_FUNC_QOS`. `qm->factor[0].func_qos` (PF) is initialized once in `hisi_qm_memory_init()`, whereas VF entries `1 .. num_vfs` are initialized in `hisi_qm_init_vf_qos()` during `hisi_qm_sriov_enable()`; every `func_qos` must stay in `1..QM_QOS_MAX_VAL` so `ir = func_qos * QM_QOS_RATE` is non-zero before division. In `qm_get_qos_value()`, `pci_physfn(pdev)` must equal `qm->pdev` and `fun_index <= pci_sriov_get_totalvfs(qm->pdev)`. [F39, F62, F70, F95]
- When polling multi-VF bitmasks across `GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)` (`qm->vfs_num` must be in `1..QM_MAX_VFS_NUM_V2` (`63`)), `qm_wait_vf_prepare_finish()` must test `(val & GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)) == GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)` in `QM_IFC_INT_SOURCE_P`, whereas `qm_ping_all_vfs()` must test `!(val & GENMASK(vfs_num, 1))` in `QM_IFC_READY_STATUS`. [F83, F96]
- In `qm_vf_q_assign()`, `num_vfs` must be non-zero, `vfs_q_num = qm->ctrl_qp_num - qm->qp_num >= num_vfs`, and VF queues must start at `q_base = qm->qp_num`. If `hisi_qm_set_vft()` fails for VF `i` (or `qm_shaper_init_vft()` fails in `qm_set_sqc_cqc_vft()`), already-configured VFTs must be cleared (`j = num_vfs` down to `i + 1`, or `qm_set_vft_common(qm, i, fun_num, 0, 0)` at `back_sqc_cqc`) without overwriting `ret`. [F16, F40, F60, F99]
- Every SQE sent in `hisi_qp_send()` increments `qp->qp_status.used`, balanced by `qm_poll_req_cb()` or `qp_stop_fail_cb()` (after `flush_workqueue(qm->wq)` in `qm_stop_qp_nolock()` when `qp->is_resetting`), and `qm_get_avail_sqe()` treats the SQ as full at `qp->sq_depth - 1`. Wrapping CQ, EQ, or AEQ head indices from `depth - 1` to `0` must invert `cqc_phase`/`eqc_phase`/`aeqc_phase` and reset the entry pointer. In `qm_aeq_thread()`, `QM_EQ_OVERFLOW` calls `qm_reset_function(qm)` and returns immediately, while `QM_CQ_OVERFLOW` falls through to `QM_CQE_ERROR`. [F10, F56, F71, F85]
- `qm_create_qp_nolock()` and `hisi_qm_release_qp()` must update `qm->qp_in_used` and `qm->qp_idr` in lockstep under `qm->qps_lock` (`qm_frozen()` sets `qm->qp_in_used = qm->qp_num` when `0`), and `qm_create_qp_nolock()` must call `hisi_qm_unset_hw_reset(qp)` and zero `qp->cqe`. `qm_init_qp_status()` in `qm_qp_ctx_cfg()` must reset `used = 0`, `sq_tail = 0`, `cq_head = 0`, and `cqc_phase = true`. [F50, F53, F54]
- Stack descriptors (`qm_mailbox.rsvd = 0` in `qm_mb_pre_init()`, and `qm_sqc`, `qm_cqc`, `qm_eqc`, `qm_aeqc` in `qm_*_ctx_cfg()`) must be zeroed before transfer. Queue depths in contexts and V2+ `SQC_VFT` counts in `qm_vft_data_cfg()` must be encoded as `depth - 1` / `number - 1` (decoded with `+ 1` in `qm_get_vft_v2()`), casting `base` and `number - 1` to `u64` before shifting by `28` and `45`. [F44, F73, F93]
- `qm_cap_query_info[]` and `qm_basic_info[]` are indexed positionally by `enum qm_cap_table_type` and `enum qm_basic_type` (dual 16-bit entries `QM_XEQ_DEPTH_CAP` and `QM_QP_DEPTH_CAP` require mask `GENMASK(31, 0)`). Capabilities in `qm_cap_query_info[]` are cached in `qm->cap_tables.qm_cap_table` during `qm_get_pci_res()`, and IRQ register/unregister helpers must read `qm_cap_table` rather than MMIO. In `qm_get_version()`, when `pdev->revision == QM_HW_V3`, non-zero `QM_SUB_VERSION_ID` (`0x210`) must override `qm->ver` before selecting `qm->ops` or reading caps. [F69, F114, F116, F117]
- In `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()`, `sge_nr` must be in `1..HISI_ACC_SGL_SGE_NR_MAX` (`255`) so `sgl_num_per_block = block_size / sgl_size` is non-zero; total DMA blocks must not exceed `HISI_ACC_MEM_BLOCK_NR`, loop index `i` must be preserved into `if (remain_sgl > 0)` for `block[i]` and `err_free_mem`, and `hisi_acc_free_sgl_pool()` must pass `block[i].size` to `dma_free_coherent()`. In `hisi_acc_sg_buf_unmap()`, `clear_hw_sgl_sge(hw_sgl)` must run before zeroing `entry_sum_in_sgl`, `entry_sum_in_chain`, and `entry_length_in_sgl`. [F57, F78, F87, F91, F101]

## Synchronization
- Lock order is `qm_list->lock` -> `qm->qps_lock` -> `qm->mailbox_lock`. Runtime PM helpers (`qm_pm_get_sync()` / `qm_pm_put_sync()`) must be called outside `qm->qps_lock` because `hisi_qm_suspend()` and `hisi_qm_resume()` acquire `down_write(&qm->qps_lock)` via `hisi_qm_stop()` and `hisi_qm_start()`. [F21, F135]
- `qm_stop_qp_nolock()` holds `down_write(&qm->qps_lock)` while setting `QP_STOP`, calling `qm_drain_qp()`, flushing `qm->wq`, and calling `qp_stop_fail_cb()`; therefore `qm_work_process()`, `qp->req_cb`, and `qp->event_cb` must never acquire `qm->qps_lock` (or call `hisi_qm_start_qp()`, `hisi_qm_stop_qp()`, `hisi_qm_create_qp()`, or `hisi_qm_release_qp()`). [F134, F138]
- Hardirq and atomic callbacks—`qm->ops->hw_error_handle` (`qm_hw_error_handle_v2`), `qm->err_ini->get_err_result` (called from `qm_abnormal_irq()`), and `qm->ops->qm_db` (`qm_db_v1`, `qm_db_v2`, called from `qm_eq_irq()` and BH-disabled spinlocks in `hisi_qp_send()`)—must not sleep or acquire `qm->mailbox_lock` or `qm->qps_lock`. [F131, F132]
- Mailbox operations (`qm_mb_nolock()`), `qm->xqc_buf` accesses (`qm_set_and_get_xqc()`, checking `qm_check_dev_error(qm)` first), and `qm_mb_write()` (128-bit `stp` with earlyclobber `"=&r"`, `"Q"`, `"dmb oshst"` on ARM64) must hold `qm->mailbox_lock` and poll `hisi_qm_wait_mb_ready(qm)` before and after. `qm->ops->set_ifc_begin` (`qm_set_ifc_begin_v3`/`v4`) returns holding `mailbox_lock` (V3) or `ifc_lock` (V4) even on error; callers must always pair it with `set_ifc_end(qm)` and never acquire `mailbox_lock`/`ifc_lock` in between. [F63, F119, F137, F143]
- `hisi_qm_controller_reset()` and `qm_controller_reset()` must clear `QM_RST_SCHED` on every exit path. Reset flows and `qm_algqos_read()`/`write()` must acquire `QM_RESETTING` (`qm_reset_prepare_ready(qm)` targets `pf_qm->misc_ctl` on `< QM_HW_V3` and `qm->misc_ctl` otherwise) and release it via `qm_reset_bit_clear(qm)` / `clear_bit`. During PF/VF resets, `qm_cmd_uninit(qm)` must mask `QM_IFC_INT_MASK` before polling IFC registers and `qm_cmd_init(qm)` must re-enable it afterward (and before `qm_ping_pf()` in `qm_pf_reset_vf_done()`). [F12, F13, F25, F66, F127]
- In `__hisi_qm_start()` / `hisi_qm_start()`, `qm_init_eq_aeq_status()`, zeroing `qm->qdma.va`, and configuring EQC/AEQC/SQC_BT/CQC_BT must precede `qm_enable_eq_aeq_interrupts()` and setting `QM_WORK` under `qm->qps_lock`. In `hisi_qm_stop()`, set `qm->status.stop_reason = r` under `qm->qps_lock` and reset it to `QM_NORMAL` after `qm_invalid_queues()`. Stopping user-space QPs (`qm_disable_qp()`, `hisi_qm_stop()`) must call `qm_set_qp_disable()` with `QM_RESET_STOP_TX_OFFSET` before stopping and `QM_RESET_STOP_RX_OFFSET` after. [F28, F29, F51, F55]
- In `qm_get_complete_eqe_num()`, on phase mismatch (`QM_EQE_PHASE(eqe) != qm->status.eqc_phase`), ring the EQ doorbell and return early, and validate `cqn < qm->qp_num` before indexing `qm->poll_data[cqn]`. In `qm_poll_req_cb()` and `hisi_qm_is_q_updated()`, execute `dma_rmb()` after matching `QM_CQE_PHASE(cqe)` and before reading `cqe->sq_head` or advancing `cq_head`. [F23, F142]

## Object Lifetimes
- `qm_create_qp_nolock()` returns a borrowed `&qm->qp_array[qp_id]` owned by `struct hisi_qm` (freed only in `hisi_qp_memory_uninit()`). `hisi_qm_create_qp()` and `hisi_qm_sriov_enable()` hold runtime PM references (`qm_pm_get_sync(qm)`) until `hisi_qm_release_qp()` or `hisi_qm_sriov_disable()` (rolling back via `qm_pm_put_sync(qm)` on error, plus `qm_clear_vft_config(qm)` if `pci_enable_sriov()` fails). With `QM_SUPPORT_RPM`, `hisi_qm_pm_init()` (`pm_runtime_put_noidle()`) and `hisi_qm_pm_uninit()` (`pm_runtime_get_noresume()`) must be paired. [F2, F3, F7, F8]
- `hisi_qm_get_dfx_access(qm)` must fail with `-EAGAIN` if `pm_runtime_suspended(&qm->pdev->dev)` is true, and callers must pair it with `hisi_qm_put_dfx_access(qm)` on all paths. `hisi_qm_q_num_set()` must call `pci_dev_put(pdev)` on the device from `pci_get_device()`. When `qm_stop_qp_nolock()` sees `qp_status.flags != QP_START`, it must still set `qp->is_resetting = false` so `qm_restart()` does not restart a released QP. [F5, F6, F9, F129]
- `hisi_qm_uninit()` and `hisi_qm_init()` rollback must run in LIFO order: `qm_cmd_uninit()`, `hisi_qm_unint_work()`, `hisi_qm_memory_uninit()`, `qm_remove_uacce()`, `qm_irqs_unregister()`, `hisi_qm_pci_uninit()`. Before freeing `qm->qdma.va` in `hisi_qm_memory_uninit()` or invalidating queues on `QM_DOWN` in `qm_invalid_queues()`, `hisi_qm_cache_wb(qm)` must flush the SQC/CQC cache on `qm->ver != QM_HW_V1`. In `hisi_qm_wait_task_finish()`, `qm_frozen()` must set `QM_DRIVER_REMOVING` before waiting on `QM_RST_SCHED`/`QM_RESETTING` and flushing `qm->cmd_process`. [F14, F26, F124]
- Because `qm_get_pci_res()` aliases `qm->db_io_base = qm->io_base` when `QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION` is unset, `qm_put_pci_res()` and `err_db_ioremap` must check `test_bit(QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION, &qm->caps)` before `iounmap(qm->db_io_base)`. On `QM_HW_VF` with `qm->ver >= QM_HW_V3`, `qm_register_abnormal_irq()` / `qm_unregister_abnormal_irq()` must request and free the abnormal IRQ (`qm_rsvd_irq`, `IRQF_NO_AUTOEN`). [F38, F115]
- On PF, `qm_dev_mem_reset(qm)` must precede `hisi_qm_start(qm)` or `qm_restart(qm)`. `hisi_qm_suspend()` must call `hisi_qm_stop()` before `qm_prepare_for_suspend()`, and `qm_rebuild_for_resume()` must re-enable PCI MSE, MSI, `qm_dev_hw_init()`, and `qm_dev_mem_reset()` before `hisi_qm_start()`. In `qm_controller_reset_prepare()`, `qm_dev_ecc_mbit_handle(qm)` must precede stopping VFs and `hisi_qm_stop()`, and VF PCI state must be bracketed by `pci_save_state()` and `pci_restore_state()` across resets. [F122, F125, F126, F130]

## API Contracts
- `hisi_qm_pre_init()` sets `pci_set_drvdata(pdev, qm)`, and QM callbacks and `pci_physfn()` lookups cast `pci_get_drvdata(pdev)` directly to `struct hisi_qm *`, so client drivers must place `struct hisi_qm` at offset 0 of their drvdata struct. [F108]
- `hisi_qp_send()` and `hisi_acc_sg_buf_map_to_hw_sgl()` / `hisi_acc_sg_buf_unmap()` have no internal locking; callers must serialize concurrent `hisi_qp_send()` calls on the same `hisi_qp` and ensure each SGL pool `index` is used by at most one in-flight request. `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()` and `hisi_acc_sg_buf_map_to_hw_sgl()` return `ERR_PTR()` on failure (never `NULL`; test with `IS_ERR()`), whereas `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()` returns `0` on failure (test with `!qos`). [F30, F31, F33, F133]
- `func_qos`/`alg_qos` use `100 Mbps` units (`1..QM_QOS_MAX_VAL`) while `qm_get_shaper_para()`, `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()`, and `qm->mb_qos` use `1 Mbps` (`qos * QM_QOS_RATE`). In `qm_get_xqc_depth(qm, low_bits, high_bits, type)`, `*low_bits` gets `eq_depth`/`sq_depth` and `*high_bits` gets `aeq_depth`/`cq_depth`. On `QM_HW_V3+` (`is_read == true`), `hisi_qm_get_hw_info()` shifts right by `shift` before masking with `mask` (post-shift mask). In `qm_get_ifc_v3()`/`v4()`, check `if (data)` before writing `*data` and check `ret` before reading `*cmd`/`*data`. [F42, F45, F47, F48]

## Error Handling
- In `hisi_qm_alloc_qps_node()`, if `IS_ERR(qps[i])` on `tmp->qm`, roll back the `i` created QPs via `hisi_qm_free_qps(qps, i)` before advancing to the next device, and call `free_list(&head)` on all exit paths. `hisi_qm_resume()` must return negative `ret` when `qm_rebuild_for_resume()` fails or when `hisi_qm_start()` fails with `!qm_check_dev_error(qm)`. [F4, F32, F34]
- In `qm_clear_device()`, if `qm_master_ooo_check(qm)` or `set_priv_status(qm)` fails before `qm_reset_device(qm)`, write `0x0` to `ACC_MASTER_GLOBAL_CTRL`. In `qm_hw_error_handle_v2()`, when `error_status & qm_err->reset_mask`, mask the triggered bits in `QM_RAS_NFE_ENABLE` and return `ACC_ERR_NEED_RESET` without clearing `QM_ABNORMAL_INT_SOURCE`. In `qm_get_qos_value()` and `qm_algqos_write()`, check `ret` before reading `*val` or `fun_index`. [F41, F118, F123]

## External Contracts
- `qm_alloc_uacce()` and `hisi_qm_uacce_mmap()` must compute `UACCE_QFRT_MMIO` page counts identically across `QM_HW_V1`, `!QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION`, and `QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION`, and `hisi_qp_alloc_memory()` and `qm_alloc_uacce()` must compute `UACCE_QFRT_DUS` size identically as `PAGE_ALIGN(qm->sqe_size * sq_depth + sizeof(struct qm_cqe) * cq_depth) + PAGE_SIZE`. In `hisi_qm_uacce_ioctl()`, reject `qp_ctx.qc_type > QM_MAX_QC_TYPE` with `-EINVAL`, and initialize `qp_info` via `copy_from_user()` before overwriting fields and copying back. [F76, F102, F146]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `hisi_qm_reset_prepare()` and `hisi_qm_reset_done()`, pass `pf_qm` (`pci_get_drvdata(pci_physfn(pdev))`) to `hisi_qm_dev_err_uninit()` / `hisi_qm_dev_err_init()`, and pass `qm` (`pci_get_drvdata(pdev)`) to `hisi_qm_stop()`, `qm_restart()`, `qm_reset_prepare_ready()`, and `qm_reset_bit_clear()`. Loops over `qm_list->list` matching VFs via `pci_physfn(dev) == pdev` must skip `if (dev == pdev) continue;` first. [F84, F89]
- Pointer scaling and casts: `acc_get_sgl()` must cast `block[block_index].sgl` to `void *` before adding `pool->sgl_size * offset`; `qm_set_qp_disable()` and `hisi_qm_unset_hw_reset()` must cast `qp->qdma.va + qp->qdma.size` to `u32 *`/`u64 *` before subtracting `QM_RESET_STOP_TX_OFFSET`/`RX_OFFSET`; and `qp->sqe` (`void *`) indexing must multiply by `qm->sqe_size` while `qp->cqe` (`struct qm_cqe *`) uses unscaled indices. [F100, F103, F104]
- Shifts and DMA mapping: cast operands to `u64` before shifting by `>= 32` (`qm_db_v1()`/`v2()`, `qm_get_vft_v2()`, `qm_get_mb_cmd()`, `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()`, `qm_set_ifc_begin_v3()`/`v4()`) and cap `PAGE_SHIFT + MAX_PAGE_ORDER` at `31` in `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()`; in `hisi_acc_sg_buf_map_to_hw_sgl()`, use `sg_n_mapped` for `pool->sge_nr` and `for_each_sg()` but pass `sg_nents(sgl)` to `dma_unmap_sg()`; and in `hisi_qm_uacce_mmap()` (`UACCE_QFRT_DUS`), save and zero `vma->vm_pgoff` around `dma_mmap_coherent()`. [F90, F92, F94, F139, F140]
