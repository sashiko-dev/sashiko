# SOUND - SOC LAYER / DYNAMIC AUDIO POWER MANAGEMENT (ASoC)

This unit implements core ASoC card, AC'97, ACPI, and SoundWire machine utilities (`sdw_utils`) along with SoC audio front-ends, digital audio interfaces, codecs, and AHUB crossbar/processing drivers for MediaTek MT8365, Freescale MXS, Renesas SuperH, Allwinner sunxi, and NVIDIA Tegra platforms.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In Tegra ADX, AMX, ASRC, MVC, and OPE runtime PM, volatile state not restored by `regcache_sync()` must be manually handled: `tegra210_adx_write_map_ram()` and `tegra210_amx_write_map_ram()` run after `regcache_sync()`; `tegra186_asrc_runtime_resume()` writes `GLOBAL_SCRATCH_ADDR` and `GLOBAL_ENB` before `regcache_sync()` and restores per-lane `int_part`/`frac_part` afterward (and any `RATIO_INT_PART`/`RATIO_FRAC_PART` write for `TEGRA186_ASRC_RATIO_SOURCE_SW` must call `tegra186_asrc_lock_stream()`); `tegra210_mvc` saves `TEGRA210_MVC_CTRL` before cache-only and rewrites it plus `VOLUME_SWITCH_TRIGGER` after sync; and `tegra210_ope` saves/restores PEQ across `regmap`, `peq_regmap`, and `mbdrc_regmap`. [F176, F177, F178, F297, F305, F357]
- In Tegra AHUB modules, `cif_conf.client_bits` must match `cif_conf.audio_bits` in `tegra186_dspk_hw_params()`, `tegra_admaif_hw_params()`, and `tegra210_adx_set_audio_cif()`; 32-channel Tegra264 variants (`max_stream_ch == TEGRA264_ADMAIF_MAX_CHANNEL` or `soc_data->max_ch == 32`) must use `tegra264_set_cif()` instead of `tegra_set_cif()`; `tegra210_i2s` and `tegra210_amx` must add `soc_data` offsets (`tx_offset`, `i2s_ctrl_offset`, `reg_offset`); and `tegra_admaif_set_pack_mode()` must clear the non-selected `PACK8_EN`/`PACK16_EN` bit. [F181, F182, F239, F362]
- DAI array order is hardcoded into indexing in Tegra ASRC, ADX, AMX, and MIXER: `tegra186_asrc_out_hw_params()` uses `id = dai->id - 7` (6 `OUT_DAI` entries after 7 `IN_DAI` entries); `tegra210_adx_out_hw_params()` uses `dai->id - 1` (`IN_DAI` at 0 before `OUT_DAI(1..4)`); `tegra210_amx_dais` places `IN_DAI` at `0..3` and `OUT_DAI` at `TEGRA_AMX_OUT_DAI_ID` (`4`); and `tegra210_mixer_dais` places `TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_MAX` (`10`) `IN_DAI` entries at `0..9` before `OUT_DAI` (`dai->id - TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_MAX`). [F189, F195, F317, F318]
- In `tegra186_asrc`, `tegra210_mixer`, and `tegra210_mbdrc` regmap callbacks, per-instance offsets must be normalized only within bounds: `tegra186_asrc` reduces `reg %= TEGRA186_ASRC_STREAM_STRIDE` only when `reg < TEGRA186_ASRC_STREAM_LIMIT`; `tegra210_mixer` checks `reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_RX_LIMIT` before `else if (reg < TEGRA210_MIXER_TX_LIMIT)` (adding `TEGRA210_MIXER_TX1_ENABLE` for TX); and `tegra210_mbdrc` reduces `reg >= TEGRA210_MBDRC_IIR_CFG` modulo `TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_PARAM_STRIDE * TEGRA210_MBDRC_FILTER_COUNT` before `switch (reg)`. [F210, F327, F328]
- Readable hardware FIFO and auto-incrementing RAM data registers across Tegra drivers must be marked both `.volatile_reg` and `.precious_reg`, and sequential RAM helpers write `*_ADDR_INIT_EN | *_SEQ_ACCESS_EN` to `*_RAM_CTRL` once and loop over the fixed `*_RAM_DATA` register without incrementing its address; in `tegra210_sfc.c`, `tegra210_sfc_rates[]` and `coef_addr_table[][]` must have `TEGRA210_SFC_NUM_RATES` (`13`) entries matching hardware `RX_FREQ`/`TX_FREQ` indices, and `TEGRA210_SFC_COEF_RAM_EN` is only enabled when `srate_in != srate_out`; in `tegra210_mvc.c`, `tegra210_mvc_put_curve_type()` rejects changes when `TEGRA210_MVC_EN` is set and calls `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings()`, while master volume (`!per_ch_enable`) clears `PER_CHAN_CTRL_EN` and copies `volume[0]` to all channels. [F246, F298, F299, F300, F302, F321, F330, F361]
- In `tegra210_i2s`, `tegra210_i2s_sw_reset()` saves and restores `cif_reg`, `stream_reg`, and `TEGRA210_I2S_CTRL` across `I2S_SOFT_RESET_EN`; `tegra210_i2s_set_fmt()` defers `data_offset` for `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_RIGHT_J` to `tegra210_i2s_set_timing_params()`; `client_sample_format` is a signed `int` initialized to `-EINVAL` (`>= 0` check); in `tegra210_ahub.c`, `MUX_VALUE(npart, nbit)` is 1-based (`1 + nbit + npart * 32`, `0` for `"None"`), `tegra*_ahub_mux_values` must match `tegra*_ahub_mux_texts`, `soc_data->reg_count <= TEGRA_XBAR_UPDATE_MAX_REG`, and all AHUB parent/child `dev_pm_ops` must use `SYSTEM_SLEEP_PM_OPS`. [F286, F306, F307, F312, F313, F314, F344]
- In MT8365 AFE, `be->prepared[substream->stream]` guards refcounted clocks/blocks in `.prepare` and is cleared in `.shutdown`; `afe_priv->be_data` is indexed by `dai_id - MT8365_AFE_BACKEND_BASE` while `dai_priv` and `dai_on` use unshifted `dai->id` (with `struct mtk_afe_i2s_priv` populated for all I2S DAIs); optional `mtk_base_memif_data` offsets/shifts and `memif->irq_usage` use `-1` as sentinel (`< 0` / `>= 0`); `mt8365_afe_fe_hw_params()` writes inclusive `phys_buf_addr + buffer_size - 1` (`+ 4` for `TDM_IN`, `+ 8` otherwise); and `mt8365_afe_irq_handler()` masks `AFE_IRQ_MCU_STATUS` with `AFE_IRQ_MCU_EN`. [F10, F42, F61, F62, F97, F108]
- In `sun8i-codec.c`, `clk_set_rate_exclusive()` and `sysclk_refcnt++` run only when `aif->open_streams == 0` (paired in `hw_free` when `open_streams == BIT(stream)`); `sun8i_codec_update_sample_rate()` runs whenever `sample_rate` or `active_streams` changes; AIF2 and AIF3 share `SUN8I_AIF_CLK_CTRL(AIF2)` dividers (requiring matching `lrck_div_order` and `sample_rate` if partner is open); in `sun8i_codec_set_fmt()`, AIF3 only supports master DSP mode without writing `MSTR_MOD`/`DATA_FMT_MASK` to `AIF_CLK_CTRL(AIF3)`, and `lrck_inversion` only applies when `format != 0x3`. [F184, F185, F206, F237, F238]
- In `sun4i-i2s.c`, `sun4i_i2s_set_fmt()` caches `i2s->format = fmt`; in `sun8i`/`sun50i_h6`, `LEFT_J` and `RIGHT_J` group with `I2S` for `lrck_period = slot_width` (vs `slot_width * slots` for DSP) and `lrclk_pol` is initialized per format before XORing on `IB_IF`/`NB_IF`; `sun4i_i2s_set_clk_rate()` validates `oversample_rate` via `sun4i_i2s_oversample_is_valid()` before division; `sun50i_a64_codec_set_bias_level()` restores `MICADCEN` from `"HBIAS"` on `SND_SOC_BIAS_STANDBY`; and `sun8i_codec_jack_irq()` checks `JACK_OUT_IRQ_ST` before `JACK_IN_IRQ_ST` and requires `jack_status == CONNECTED` and matching `jack_last_sample` on `HMIC_DATA_IRQ_ST`. [F187, F188, F200, F203, F219, F235, F243]
- In `asoc_sdw_parse_sdw_endpoints()`, `soc_end->name_prefix` and `include_sidecar` are set in the outer loop while `soc_end++` runs at the end of the inner loop after all `continue` filters; when `check_sdca` is true, `dai_info->quirk` applies only if `(dai_info->quirk & ctx->mc_quirk)` is non-zero, otherwise `is_sdca_endpoint_present()` is used (`< 0` aborts, `!ret` skips); and in `snd_soc_acpi_sdw_link_slaves_found()`, `unique_id == SDW_UNIQUE_ID(adr)` is required only when `reported_part_count > 1`. [F31, F76, F81, F120]

## Synchronization
- Init before IRQ/child/PM registration: `mt8365_afe_pcm_dev_probe()` must init `regmap`, `memif`, and `irqs[].irq_data` before `devm_request_irq()`; `sun8i_codec_enable_jack_detect()` sets `scodec->jack` and `HMIC_CTRL1`/`CTRL2`/`STS` before `devm_request_threaded_irq()`; `tegra30_ahub_probe()` inits the `ahub` singleton and `pm_runtime_enable()` before `of_platform_populate()` (clearing `ahub = NULL` on teardown); `tegra210_ope_probe()` sets `drvdata` and inits all three regmaps before component/PM registration; and `jack_new()` in `soc-card.c` inits `mutex` and lists before `snd_jack_new()`, while `snd_soc_card_get_kcontrol()` checks `!name` and uses `snd_ctl_find_id_mixer()`. [F19, F27, F28, F135, F156, F277, F279]
- Atomic vs sleepable contexts and locks: `mt8365_afe_enable/disable_apll_tuner_cfg()` take `afe_clk_mutex` (sleepable context outside `afe_ctrl_lock`); `mxs_saif_trigger()` is atomic (`clk_enable/disable()` and `udelay()` only); `tegra_admaif_stop()` is atomic (`regmap_read_poll_timeout_atomic()`), whereas process-context Tegra reset/RAM/gain helpers (`tegra210_adx_startup()`, `tegra210_amx_startup()`, `tegra210_i2s_init/sw_reset()`, `tegra210_mixer_*`, `tegra210_mvc_volume_switch_timeout()`, `tegra210_sfc_soft_reset()`) sleep via `regmap_read_poll_timeout()`; `sun4i_spdif_get/set_status()` require `host->lock`; `sun8i_codec_jack_irq/work()` require `scodec->jack_mutex`; and `adda_pr_regmap_cfg` must not set `.disable_locking`. [F126, F127, F158, F242, F244, F250, F252, F356, F364]

## Object Lifetimes
- Reverse-order teardown and pairing: `snd_soc_free_ac97_component()` calls `snd_soc_ac97_free_gpio()`, `device_del()`, `ac97->bus = NULL`, then `put_device()`; `mt8365_afe_disable_main_clk()` and `mt8365_afe_disable_apll_associated_cfg()` tear down in exact reverse order of enable; `mt8365_dai_i2s_shutdown()` gates `MT8365_TOP_CG_I2S_IN` for consumer capture and only disables APLL cfg if `be->prepared[stream]`; `mt8365_afe_fe_hw_free()` checks `fe_data->use_sram` before freeing DRAM/EMI clk; `siu_pcm_free()` calls `cancel_work_sync()` before `siu_free_port()`; and `sun8i_codec_disable_jack_detect()` calls `devm_free_irq()` before `cancel_delayed_work_sync()`, then clears `scodec->jack = NULL`. [F2, F8, F9, F14, F111, F144, F154]
- PM, component, and node cleanup: in `sun4i-i2s`, `sun4i-spdif`, and `sun8i-codec`, error/remove paths check `!pm_runtime_status_suspended()` and call runtime suspend (`sun4i_i2s` keeps `bus_clk` enabled across regcache sync and `CTRL_REG` writes); `tegra20_i2s`, `tegra20_spdif`, and `tegra30_ahub` runtime resume assert reset, enable clocks, wait `usleep_range(10, 100)`, and deassert reset before `regcache_sync()`; `tegra20_ac97`, `tegra20_i2s`, and `tegra30_i2s` unregister the PCM platform before the component/FIFOs; `tegra_admaif` pairs `tegra_isomgr_adma_register/unregister()` and `setbw(..., true/false)`; `sdw_utils` property helpers call `fwnode_handle_put()` after `device_add_software_node()`; `snd_soc_acpi_find_package()` frees `buffer.pointer`; and DT lookups in MXS and Tegra must call `of_node_put()`. [F6, F16, F113, F148, F149, F150, F152, F231, F267, F271, F275, F352]

## API Contracts
- Custom `snd_kcontrol` `.put` callbacks (`tegra186_dspk_put_*`, `tegra186_asrc_put_*`, `tegra210_admaif_*put_*`, `tegra210_adx_put_byte_map`, `sun4i_spdif_set_status`) must return `1` when changed, `0` when unchanged, or negative errno; and `snd_dmaengine_dai_dma_data.addr` across sunxi/Tegra drivers and `*fiforeg` in `tegra30_ahub_allocate_rx/tx_fifo()` must use physical `struct resource` start addresses (`res->start`, `ahub->apbif_addr`), never virtual MMIO pointers. [F161, F254, F363]
- Tegra contracts: callers of `tegra_set_cif()`, `tegra264_set_cif()`, and `tegra30/124_ahub_set_cif()` must zero-init the config struct and pass 1-based channel counts (`>= 1`); in `tegra210_admaif.c`, `CH_REG`, `CH_TX/RX_REG`, and `NV_SOC_ENUM_EXT` take 0-based channel indices while `ADMAIF_REG_DEFAULTS` and `TEGRA_ADMAIF_CIF_CTRL` take 1-based numbers; in `tegra30_ahub`, `enable/disable/free_rx/tx_fifo()` take the APBIF FIFO CIF while `set/unset_rx_cif_source()` take the destination `rxcif` (`playback_i2s_cif` or `capture_fifo_cif`) first; in `tegra210_ope`, PEQ/MBDRC use `peq_regmap`/`mbdrc_regmap`, `tegra210_peq_param_info()` uses `value.integer.value[]` (`num_regs <= 62`), `tegra210_mbdrc_param_info()` uses `value.bytes.data`, and `struct tegra_soc_bytes` keeps `soc` at offset 0; and CPU DAI `tegra210/30_i2s_set_fmt()` sets master on `SND_SOC_DAIFMT_BP_FP` and clears it on `BC_FC`. [F167, F191, F284, F288, F294, F319, F322, F329, F338, F365, F371]
- SoundWire (`sdw_utils`) and ASoC core contracts: `sdw_utils` exports use `EXPORT_SYMBOL_NS(..., "SND_SOC_SDW_UTILS")`; machine drivers store `struct asoc_sdw_mc_private *` in `card->drvdata`, set `codec_info_list_count`, and call `asoc_sdw_mc_dailink_exit_loop()` on error/remove; multi-amp `rtd_init` callbacks iterate `for_each_rtd_codec_dais()`; `asoc_sdw_find_dailink()` requires a zeroed array with a trailing sentinel; `asoc_sdw_find_codec_info_acpi()` requires a zero-padded `ACPI_ID_LEN` (16-byte) buffer; `snd_soc_alloc/new_ac97_component()` return `ERR_PTR` and require `put_device()` on error; `snd_soc_set_ac97_ops_of_reset()` mutates `ops` and requires `snd_soc_set_ac97_ops(NULL)` on remove; jacks adding DAPM pins must use `snd_soc_card_jack_new_pins()`; and `snd_soc_acpi_mach` tables using `comp_ids` must be writable and zero-terminated. [F36, F45, F47, F67, F70, F78, F102, F107, F118, F134, F138, F140]

## Error Handling
- Return-value signedness and PM cleanup: `platform_get_irq()`, `mt8365_afe_fs_timing()`, `sun4i_codec_get_hw_rate()`, and sunxi divider/rate/width helpers (`sun4i_i2s_get_bclk/mclk_div()`, `get_sr/wss()`, `sun8i_codec_get_hw_rate/lrck_div_order/bclk_div()`) return `0` as a valid value and `< 0` on error, requiring signed `int` storage and `< 0` checks (whereas `sun4i_codec_get_mod_freq()` returns `0` on error); in `tegra210_mvc_update_vol/mute()` and `tegra210_mixer_configure_gain()`, all exits after `pm_runtime_get_sync()` must jump to `pm_runtime_put()`; `mxs_saif_trigger()` checks `clk_enable()` and unwinds `clk_disable(master_saif->clk)` on slave failure; `sun4i_codec_quirks.create_card` sets `card->owner = THIS_MODULE` and returns `ERR_PTR`; and `coef_addr_table` in `tegra210_sfc.c` uses `UNSUPP_CONV` (`ERR_PTR(-EOPNOTSUPP)`) for unsupported non-equal rates. [F34, F35, F43, F170, F171, F172, F236, F261, F276, F287]

## External Contracts
- To preserve ALSA UCM compatibility in `sdw_utils`, `card->components` must append `"-sdca"` when `component->name_prefix` matches `need_sdca_suffix` (`"rt711"`, `"rt713"`) in `asoc_sdw_rt_sdca_jack_rtd_init()`, and format `"rt714"` as `"mic:rt715-sdca"` in `asoc_sdw_rt_dmic_rtd_init()`; in `tegra_admaif_probe()`, the DT `"dma-names"` property is indexed with capture (RX) at `i * 2` and playback (TX) at `(i * 2) + 1`. [F73, F168]

## Known Pitfalls
- Bitfield and math traps: sunxi single-bit macros (`SUN4I_CODEC_DAC_FIFOC_*`, `SUN50I/SUN8I_ADDA_*`, `SUN8I_HMIC_CTRL1_*`) are unshifted bit indices requiring `BIT()`; in `tegra210_mbdrc_hw_params()`, zero-valued `MODE_BYPASS` requires `(val & MODE_MASK) == MODE_BYPASS`; `tegra186_asrc` threshold puts preserve `(thresh & ~(0x3))`; `tegra186_dspk_hw_params()` uses `(dspk->ch_sel + 1) << CH_SEL_SHIFT` and clamps `rx_fifo_th` to `(RX_FIFO_DEPTH / audio_ch) - 1`; `sun4i_spdif_set_status()` casts `status[3]` to `u32` before `<< 24`; `sun8i_codec_get_lrck_div_order()` uses `order_base_2()`; and `tegra210_dmic` gain math requires `u64` and `div_u64()`. [F164, F174, F180, F196, F215, F218, F325, F331]
- Signedness and descriptor traps: `mt8365_afe_get_cm_update_cnt()` requires ceiling division for `ch_pair` and descending fallthroughs; `tegra210_mvc` `CURVE_POLY` shifts by 16 after `/ 100` (while `CURVE_LINEAR` and `tegra210_mbdrc_vol_get()` require signed right shifts) and `pm_runtime_enable()` must precede `tegra210_mvc_reset_vol_settings()`; `sun4i_spdif_controls` are DAI controls requiring `snd_kcontrol_chip()`; `tegra186_asrc` kcontrols must cast `private_value` to the matching `soc_mreg_control`/`soc_enum`/`soc_mixer_control`; and `tegra210_admaif` `.num_reg_defaults` equals `CHANNEL_COUNT * 6 + 1`. [F82, F90, F225, F226, F263, F282, F333, F334]
