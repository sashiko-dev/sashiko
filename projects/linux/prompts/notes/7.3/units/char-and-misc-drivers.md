# CHAR and MISC DRIVERS

This unit implements the `misc_register`/`misc_deregister` framework (`drivers/char/misc.c`) and standard memory pseudo-devices (`/dev/mem`, `/dev/null`, `/dev/zero`, `/dev/full`, `/dev/port` in `drivers/char/mem.c`), along with character and miscellaneous hardware drivers including `lp`, `nvram`, `ttyprintk`, SPARC `adi`, PowerPC `bsr` and `ps3flash`, SGI `uv_mmtimer`, NetWinder `nwflash` and `ds1620`, Atari `dsp56k`, ThinkPad `mwave`, Xilinx `hwicap`, and Analog Devices `ad525x_dpot`.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `drivers/char/xilinx_hwicap/`, ICAP transfers only 32-bit words (`u32`): `hwicap_write()` masks lengths with `~3`, caches 1..3 trailing bytes in `drvdata->write_buffer[4]`, and flushes them zero-padded in `hwicap_release()`. When prepending `write_buffer_in_use` bytes to `kbuf`, `len` and `left` must be reduced by `write_buffer_in_use` and `write_buffer_in_use` reset to `0` after `set_configuration()` succeeds, before `written += len`. In `hwicap_read()`, `read_buffer_in_use` is decremented and remaining bytes shifted to index 0 via `memmove()` only after `copy_to_user()` succeeds. [F65, F66, F83]
- In `buffer_icap_set_configuration()`, each full BRAM flush at `buffer_count == XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS - 1` must reset `buffer_count = 0` and `dirty = false` so post-loop `if (dirty)` flushes only trailing words; `buffer_icap_get_configuration()` starts with `buffer_count = XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS`. In `fifo_icap_get_configuration()`, each inner pass must clamp `read_fifo_occupancy` to `words_to_read` before draining into `*data++`, with both loops bounded by `retries > XHI_MAX_RETRIES`. [F100, F101]
- In `ps3flash_fetch()`, when `start_sector != priv->tag`, `priv->tag` must be invalidated to `-1` after `ps3flash_writeback()` and before `ps3flash_read_write_sectors()`, and set to `start_sector` only after the read succeeds. In `ps3flash_write()`, partial writes (`n != dev->bounce_size`) must call `ps3flash_fetch()` and full writes to a new sector (`sector != priv->tag`) must call `ps3flash_writeback()` before modifying `bounce_buf`, updating `priv->tag` and `priv->dirty` only after the copy into `bounce_buf` succeeds. [F58, F59]
- In `ps3flash_probe()`, flash region byte start and size (`start * blk_size`, `size * blk_size`) must be aligned to `FLASH_BLOCK_SIZE` (256 KiB). In `ps3flash_read()` and `ps3flash_write()`, each bounce-buffer transfer is clamped to `dev->bounce_size - offset`, and `offset` (`*pos % dev->bounce_size`) must be reset to `0` at the end of the first loop pass as `sector` advances by `priv->chunk_sectors`. [F88, F105]
- In `nvram_misc_open()` and `nvram_misc_release()`, `nvram_open_cnt` and `nvram_open_mode` are updated together under `nvram_state_lock`, and `nvram_misc_release()` clears `NVRAM_WRITE` only when `file->f_mode & FMODE_WRITE` is set. On `CONFIG_X86`, the PC CMOS checksum covers NVRAM offsets `PC_CKS_RANGE_START` (2) through `PC_CKS_RANGE_END` (31) inclusive and is stored big-endian at `PC_CKS_LOC` (32) and `PC_CKS_LOC + 1` (33). [F64, F87]
- Whenever `lp_open()`, `lp_write()`, or `lp_release()` changes the parport IEEE-1284 mode via `lp_negotiate()` or `parport_negotiate()`, it must update `lp_table[minor].current_mode` in lockstep, including resetting `current_mode` to `IEEE1284_MODE_COMPAT` before calling `lp_wait_ready()` on an incomplete write. In `lp_check_status()`, `if ((status & LP_PERRORP) && !(LP_F(minor) & LP_CAREFUL))` must precede the `LP_POUTPA` and `!(status & LP_PSELECD)` checks. [F68, F107]
- In `drivers/char/nwflash.c`, `flash_write()` must reject `p < 64 * 1024` unless both `gbWriteEnable` and `gbWriteBase64Enable` are set, and clamp `count` to `gbFlashSize - p`; `write_block()` must clamp `count` to `0x10000 - (p & 0xFFFF)` so a write never crosses a 64 KiB erase-block boundary. [F89]
- In `drivers/char/ttyprintk.c`, accesses to `tpk_buffer` and `tpk_curr` in `tpk_printk()` and `tpk_port_shutdown()` must hold `tpkp->spinlock`. `tpk_flush()` NUL-terminates `tpk_buffer[tpk_curr]` and resets `tpk_curr = 0` on line emission or when `tpk_curr >= TPK_STR_SIZE`, and `tpk_buffer` must be at least `TPK_STR_SIZE + 2` bytes (`TPK_STR_SIZE + 4`) for the wrap `'\\'` and `'\0'`. [F73, F91]
- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `devlist[]` is sparsely indexed by minor, so `memory_open()` must check `minor >= ARRAY_SIZE(devlist)`, `!dev->fops`, and `dev->fops->open`, and `chr_dev_init()` must check `devlist[minor].name`. In `read_mem()` and `write_mem()`, assigning `loff_t *ppos` to `phys_addr_t p` must be followed immediately by checking `p != *ppos` for truncation. [F42, F109]
- In `drivers/char/dsp56k.c`, DSP56001 words are 24 bits (3 bytes in `data.b[1..3]`), so `dsp56k_upload()` must reject firmware with `fw->size % 3 != 0`, and `DSP56K_UPLOAD` must validate `0 < len <= DSP56K_MAX_BINARY_LENGTH`. [F90]

## Synchronization
- In `drivers/char/misc.c`, `misc_mtx` protects `misc_list` and `misc_minors_ida` across `misc_register()`, `misc_deregister()`, `misc_open()`, and `misc_seq_ops`. When `misc_open()` drops `misc_mtx` for `request_module()`, it must relock `misc_mtx` and rescan `misc_list` from the head before `fops_get()`. Because `misc_open()` calls `file->f_op->open()` under `misc_mtx`, a miscdevice `.open` callback must never call `misc_register()`/`misc_deregister()` or take any lock that nests outside `misc_mtx`. [F18, F19]
- Every `CMOS_READ` or `CMOS_WRITE` call (including `__nvram_*` helpers and `drivers/char/mwave/smapi.c`) must hold `rtc_lock` with IRQs disabled. `pc_nvram_read()`, `pc_nvram_write()`, and `pc_nvram_initialize()` must hold `rtc_lock` continuously across checksum verification, the byte loop, and checksum update, whereas user copies (`copy_to_user`, `memdup_user`), `GFP_KERNEL` allocations, and `nvram_mutex` acquisition must happen outside `rtc_lock`. [F21, F154, F155]
- In `drivers/char/lp.c`, lock order is `lp_mutex` -> `lp_table[minor].port_mutex` -> `lp_claim_parport_or_block(&lp_table[minor])`. Process-context claims/releases must use `lp_claim_parport_or_block()` and `lp_release_parport()` to keep `LP_PARPORT_CLAIMED` synchronized with `LP_PREEMPT_REQUEST`; when `lp_error()` sleeps on a polled port (`irq == PARPORT_IRQ_NONE`), it releases and must re-claim the parport via these helpers. `lp_console_write()` runs under the console lock on a `PARPORT_MODE_SAFEININT` port and must use non-blocking `parport_claim(dev)` without `port_mutex`. [F14, F25, F159, F160]
- In `drivers/char/xilinx_hwicap/xilinx_hwicap.c`, `hwicap_open()` orders `hwicap_mutex` -> `drvdata->sem`, and `drvdata->sem` must be held across all accesses to `drvdata->is_open`, `read_buffer*`, `write_buffer*`, and `drvdata->config` callbacks. [F32]
- In `drivers/char/ps3flash.c`, `ps3flash_interrupt()` must assign `dev->lv1_status = status` before calling `complete(&dev->done)` and only when `lv1_storage_get_async_status()` returns `0`. [F29]
- All modifications to NetWinder shared GPIO/CPLD registers (`nw_gpio_modify_op`, `nw_gpio_modify_io`, `nw_cpld_modify`) in `ds1620.c` and `nwflash.c` must hold `nw_gpio_lock` via `raw_spin_lock_irqsave()` and release it before sleeping (`msleep(20)` in `ds1620_out()`). [F156]

## Object Lifetimes
- In `read_mem()` and `write_mem()`, every physical page translation from `xlate_dev_mem_ptr()` must be released with `unxlate_dev_mem_ptr()` on the same page iteration before advancing `p` or returning on error, and `read_mem()` must copy through a kernel bounce buffer via `copy_from_kernel_nofault()` before calling `unxlate_dev_mem_ptr()`. [F152]
- In `xilinx_hwicap.c`, `hwicap_setup()` uses `devm_kzalloc()` and `devm_platform_ioremap_resource()`, and must initialize `drvdata` and `mutex_init(&drvdata->sem)` before `cdev_add()`. Once `probed_devices[id] = 1` is set under `icap_sem`, any setup failure must jump to `failed:` to reset `probed_devices[id] = 0` under `icap_sem` without calling `kfree`/`iounmap`, and `hwicap_drv_remove()` must call `device_destroy()` and `cdev_del()` before clearing `probed_devices[]`. In `hwicap_release()`, `drvdata->is_open` must be cleared to `0` under `drvdata->sem` on all exit paths. [F8, F9, F33, F134]
- In `bsr_create_devs()`, `of_find_compatible_node(bn, ...)` consumes the reference on `bn`, so if `bsr_add_node(bn)` fails, `bsr_create_devs()` must call `of_node_put(bn)` and `bsr_init()` must set `np = NULL` before jumping to error cleanup. In `bsr_add_node()`, `cur` must only be appended to `bsr_devs` after both `cdev_add()` and `device_create()` succeed; if `device_create()` fails, `cdev_del(&cur->bsr_cdev)` and `kfree(cur)` must run before `bsr_cleanup_devs()`. [F6, F7]
- In `lp_open()`, once `LP_BUSY_BIT_POS` is set on `LP_F(minor)`, any failure in `LP_ABORTOPEN` checks or `lp_buffer` allocation must clear `LP_BUSY` before unlocking `lp_mutex`, and `lp_release()` must free `lp_buffer`, set it to `NULL`, and clear `LP_BUSY`. [F13]
- In `dsp56k_upload()`, the temporary `platform_device` from `platform_device_register_simple()` must be unregistered immediately after `request_firmware()`, and `release_firmware(fw)` must be called on `fw->size % 3` failure and after copying. [F15]
- In `ttyprintk_init()` and `ttyprintk_exit()`, `ttyprintk_driver` and `tpk_port.port` must both be released via `tty_driver_kref_put()` and `tty_port_destroy()` on `tty_register_driver()` failure and on exit. [F17]
- `ad_dpot_probe()` must copy `*bdata` by value into `struct dpot_data.bdata` because `ad_dpot_i2c_probe()` and `ad_dpot_spi_probe()` pass a stack-allocated `struct ad_dpot_bus_data`. [F126]

## API Contracts
- In `drivers/char/misc.c`, `misc_init()` must use `__register_chrdev(MISC_MAJOR, 0, MINORMASK + 1, ...)` because dynamic minors are allocated in `[MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR + 1, MINORMASK]`. Static (`< MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR`) and dynamic (`MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR = 255`) minors are reserved in `misc_minors_ida` and freed via `misc_minor_free()`, while caller-passed `misc->minor > MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR` is rejected with `-EINVAL` and dynamically allocated minors must be reset to `MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR` on registration failure and in `misc_deregister()`. In `misc_open()`, `file->private_data` is set to the matched `struct miscdevice *` before `replace_fops()` and `file->f_op->open()`, and `request_module()` is only called when `minor < MISC_DYNAMIC_MINOR`. [F1, F38, F78, F165, F167]
- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `open_port()`/`open_mem()` must enforce `capable(CAP_SYS_RAWIO)` and `security_locked_down(LOCKDOWN_DEV_MEM)`, and set `filp->f_mapping = iomem_get_mapping()` for `DEVMEM_MINOR`. `devlist[]` entries supporting `IOCB_NOWAIT` must set `FMODE_NOWAIT` in `struct memdev.fmode`, and `read_iter_zero()` must return `written ? written : -EAGAIN` instead of calling `cond_resched()` when `need_resched()` is true and `IOCB_NOWAIT` is set. [F153, F168, F170]
- `bsr_mmap()` and `uv_mmtimer_mmap()` must set `pgprot_noncached(vma->vm_page_prot)`. When a BSR device has `bsr_len < PAGE_SIZE`, `bsr_add_node()` clamps `bsr_len` to `4096` if `4096 < bsr_len < PAGE_SIZE`, and `bsr_mmap()` maps a single 4K page via `remap_4k_pfn(..., dev->bsr_addr >> 12, ...)` when `size == PAGE_SIZE`. [F85, F164]
- `hwicap_driver_config` callbacks `get_configuration()`/`set_configuration()` and `buffer_icap_device_read()`/`write()` take `size`/`count` and `offset` in 32-bit words (`u32`) and enforce `offset + count <= XHI_MAX_BUFFER_INTS` (512 words), whereas `buffer_icap_set_size()` (`XHI_SIZE_REG_OFFSET`) takes bytes (`count << 2`). [F43, F84]
- `ps3flash_read()` and `ps3flash_write()` are shared by VFS (`kernelbuf == NULL`) and `ps3flash_kernel_ops` (`userbuf == NULL`), so they must guard `copy_to_user()`/`copy_from_user()` with `if (userbuf)` and `memcpy()` with `if (kernelbuf)`. [F49]
- In `drivers/char/nvram.c`, `__nvram_read_byte()`, `pc_nvram_read_byte()`, `__nvram_write_byte()`, and `pc_nvram_write_byte()` take a 0-based NVRAM offset (`0 .. NVRAM_BYTES - 1`) and add `NVRAM_FIRST_BYTE` internally. [F50]
- In `ad525x_dpot.h` and `ad525x_dpot.c`, `DPOT_CONF(features, wipers, max_pos, uid)` packs `uid` (`<= 63`), `max_pos` (`<= 15`, the log2 bit width of the wiper count), and `wipers` (`<= 255`). `ad_dpot_bus_ops` reads and `dpot_read()` return a signed `int`/`s32` that must be checked `< 0` before `swab16()`, shifts, or `rdac_mask`, and `ad_dpot_i2c_probe()` must check `I2C_FUNC_SMBUS_WORD_DATA`. [F54, F55, F92, F146]
- In `drivers/char/ds1620.c`, internal temperatures are in signed `0.5 C` units, so `ds1620_ioctl()` must shift `struct therm` values `<<= 1` before `ds1620_write_state()` and `>>= 1` after `ds1620_read_state()` for whole-degree ioctls. [F56]

## Error Handling
- In `read_mem()`, `err` (preloaded with `-EFAULT`) must not store the return value of `copy_from_kernel_nofault()` (which returns `0` on success and uses `probe`), or a subsequent `copy_to_user()` fault will return `0`. In `write_mem()`, `write_port()`, `read_zero()`, and `read_iter_zero()`, a fault after partial progress must return the positive byte count transferred so far rather than `-EFAULT`. [F36, F41]
- In `hwicap_setup()`, `devm_platform_ioremap_resource()` returns an `ERR_PTR()` checked with `IS_ERR()`. `drvdata->config->get_status()` returns a raw 32-bit status register bitmask rather than an errno, so `hwicap_get_configuration_register()` must test mask bits (`XHI_SR_DALIGN_MASK`) and return `-EIO` on mismatch. [F37, F44]
- In `ps3flash_read_write_sectors()`, `ps3stor_read_write_sectors()` returns a `u64` LV1 status (`0` on success, positive on failure) that must be translated to `-EIO`. In `nwflash.c`, `write_block()` returns `> 0` on success, `0` on verification mismatch, and `< 0` on fault/timeout, so `flash_write()` retries `RetryBlock` only when `!rc`. [F48, F57]

## External Contracts
- In `drivers/char/mem.c`, `memory_lseek()` must reject `(unsigned long long)offset >= -MAX_ERRNO` with `-EOVERFLOW` and call `force_successful_syscall_return()`, while `mem_fops` and `adi_fops` set `FOP_UNSIGNED_OFFSET`. [F171]
- In `lp_set_timeout()`, negative `tv_sec` or `tv_usec` returns `-EINVAL`, whereas `tv_usec > 999999` is clamped to `999999` and `tv_sec >= MAX_SEC_IN_JIFFIES - 1` is clamped to `MAX_JIFFY_OFFSET`. [F82]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `drivers/char/lp.c`, `lp_set_timeout64()` must right-shift `karg[1]` by 32 bits when `IS_ENABLED(CONFIG_SPARC64) && !in_compat_syscall()` because native sparc64 `suseconds_t` is 32-bit big-endian with 32-bit trailing padding. [F81]
- In `drivers/char/nwflash.c`, `get_flash_id()`, `erase_block()`, and `write_block()` must call `kick_open()` before flash commands and write `0xFF` before returning to restore read mode; polling loops must use `time_before()`/`time_after_eq()`, and `flash_write()` must decrement block count `temp` when `((int)(p + count) & 0xFFFF) == 0`. [F117, F118, F143]
- In `drivers/char/xilinx_hwicap/`, `hwicap_write()` must cast `u32 *kbuf` to `char *` before adding `drvdata->write_buffer_in_use`. `buffer_icap_device_read()`/`write()` must write `XHI_SIZE_REG_OFFSET` and `XHI_BRAM_OFFSET_REG_OFFSET` via `out_be32()` before `XHI_RNC_REG_OFFSET`, and `fifo_icap_get_configuration()` must cap readbacks to `XHI_MAX_READ_TRANSACTION_WORDS` (`0xFFF`) and write `XHI_SZ_OFFSET` before `XHI_CR_OFFSET`. [F120, F135, F136]
- In `ps3flash_read()` and `ps3flash_write()`, buffers are byte-addressed (`+ offset`, `+= n`), whereas `sector` and `priv->tag` are in `dev->blk_size` sectors (`(*pos / dev->bounce_size) * priv->chunk_sectors`, advanced by `priv->chunk_sectors`). [F128]
- In `drivers/char/adi.c`, `adi_init()` must check `adi_capable()`, `adi_write()` must execute `membar #Sync` after `set_mcd_tag()`, and `read_mcd_tag()`/`set_mcd_tag()` inline asm with `__ex_table` fixups using `%%g1` must clobber `"g1"` and `"memory"`. In `uv_mmtimer_ioctl()`, `MMTIMER_GETOFFSET` must return `0` when `uv_get_min_hub_revision_id() == 1`. [F148, F149, F150]
