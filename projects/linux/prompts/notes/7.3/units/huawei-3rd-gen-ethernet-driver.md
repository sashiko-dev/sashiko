# HUAWEI 3RD GEN ETHERNET DRIVER

The `hinic3` driver supports Huawei 3rd Generation Intelligent Network Interface Card PCIe Virtual Functions (VFs). A low-level PCIe hardware layer (`hinic3_lld`, `hinic3_hwdev`) manages MMIO BARs, MSI-X vectors, asynchronous/completion event queues (AEQ/CEQ), mailboxes (MBOX), and command queues (CMDQ), exposing service instances over the Linux auxiliary bus to the upper-level NIC driver (`hinic3_nic_driver`) for `net_device` operations, NAPI TX/RX rings, `page_pool` RX buffers, RSS, and hardware offloads.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct cmdq_wqe`, `struct cmdq_header` must remain at offset 0 of every union member so `cmdq_wqe_fill` can copy `dst + 1` before writing `*dst`, while `wqe_lcmd` and `wqe_scmd` place `status` and `ctrl` at different byte offsets, requiring `CMDQ_DATA_SGE` (`CMDQ_WQE_HDR_GET(header_info, DATA_FMT)`) discrimination in `clear_wqe_complete_bit` and `cmdq_prepare_wqe_ctrl`. [F37, F113]
- In `hinic3_cmdq_ceq_handler`, `cmdq_read_wqe` does not advance `wq->cons_idx`, so every `switch` branch must either call `clear_wqe_complete_bit` or `return`; `HINIC3_CMD_TYPE_FORCE_STOP` must take the `default` branch so `CMDQ_WQE_COMPLETED(ctrl_info)` is verified before calling `cmdq_clear_cmd_buf` and `clear_wqe_complete_bit` (while `hinic3_cmdqs_free` and `hinic3_reinit_cmdq_ctxts` reclaim remaining `cmd_info->buf_in` via `hinic3_cmdq_reset_all_cmd_buf`). [F3, F86]
- Across `hinic3_send_one_skb`, `hinic3_set_wqe_combo`, and `hinic3_tx_poll`, `tx_info->wqebb_cnt` for an SKB with `num_sge` fragments must equal `num_sge + !!offload`; in `hinic3_set_wqe_combo`, `*curr_pi` must retain the first WQEBB index (`ctrl_bd0`) for `hinic3_get_and_update_sq_owner` and `txq->tx_info[pi]` while task/SGE allocations pass `&tmp_pi` and `hinic3_write_db` uses `hinic3_get_sq_local_pi(txq->sq)`. [F48, F93]
- `hinic3_alloc_txqs_res` must allocate `tqres->bds` with `sq_depth * HINIC3_BDS_PER_SQ_WQEBB + HINIC3_MAX_SQ_SGE` entries because `txq->tx_info[pi].dma_info` points to `&tqres->bds[pi * HINIC3_BDS_PER_SQ_WQEBB]` and `hinic3_tx_map_skb` indexes `dma_info[0 .. nr_frags]` linearly without modulo-wrapping at `sq_depth`. [F71]
- Because `rq_associate_cqes` statically binds each RQ WQE `i` to `rxq->cqe_arr[i]`, in `hinic3_rx_poll`, `hinic3_fetch_rx_buffer` must advance `rxq->cons_idx` and `rxq->delta` only if `napi_alloc_skb` succeeds, `rx_cqe->status` must be cleared to `0` (`RXDONE`) only after `recv_one_pkt` succeeds, and LRO packets must break the poll loop once `num_wqe >= nic_dev->lro_replenish_thld`. [F51, F91]
- `hinic3_wq_free_wqebbs` (`wq->q_depth - hinic3_wq_get_used(wq) - 1`), `hinic3_alloc_rx_buffers` (`rq_depth - 1`), and `hinic3_rx_fill_buffers` (`rxq->delta - 1`) must always reserve 1 unused entry so a full queue never wraps `u16` used count to 0 or makes masked producer and consumer indices equal. [F98]
- `recv_mbox_handler` must validate incoming segments with `mbox_segment_valid` (`seq_id <= MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL`, `seg_len <= MBOX_SEG_LEN`, and `seg_len <= MBOX_LAST_SEG_MAX_LEN` when `seq_id == MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL`) before copying into `msg_desc->msg`, and reset `msg_desc->seq_id` to `MBOX_SEQ_ID_MAX_VAL` on validation failure; in `send_mbox_seg`, `clear_mbox_status` must zero `*send_mbox->wb_vaddr` and execute `wmb()` before triggering `HINIC3_FUNC_CSR_MAILBOX_CONTROL_OFF`. [F59, F66]

## Synchronization

- Lock acquisition must follow `hwdev->channel_lock` or `aeqs->aeq_lock` before `mbox->mbox_lock`, and `hwdev->channel_lock` or `ceqs->ceq_lock` before `cmdq->cmdq_lock` (with BH disabled on the outer lock). Because `aeq_event_handler` and `ceq_event_handler` hold `spin_lock_bh` on `aeq_lock`/`ceq_lock`, `aeq_cb`/`ceq_cb` callbacks cannot sleep; `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt`, `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt_no_ack`, and `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` sleep and require process context. [F19, F136, F137]
- `hwdev->mbox` (`hinic3_init_mbox`) and `hwdev->cmdqs` (`hinic3_cmdqs_init`) must be initialized before `hinic3_aeq_register_cb` or `hinic3_ceq_register_cb` (which also call `spin_lock_init` unlocked on `aeq_lock`/`ceq_lock`), and on teardown in `hinic3_comm_mbox_free`/`hinic3_comm_cmdqs_free`, `HINIC3_HWDEV_MBOX_INITED`/`HINIC3_HWDEV_CMDQ_INITED` in `hwdev->func_state` must be cleared under `spin_lock_bh(&hwdev->channel_lock)` before unregistering callbacks and freeing `mbox`/`cmdqs`. [F18, F25, F141]
- `mbox->mbox_send_lock` must be held continuously across `mbox->send_msg_id` update, DMA queue preparation (`mbox->sync_msg_queue` or `mbox->async_msg_queue`), MMIO segment transmission (`send_mbox_msg`), response polling (`wait_mbox_msg_completion`), and copying `msg_desc->msg` to `msg_params->buf_out`. [F20]
- In `cmdq_sync_cmd_direct_resp` and `wait_cmdq_sync_cmd_completion`, `curr_prod_idx` indexes `cmdq->cmd_infos` and the WQE `PI` field while `next_prod_idx` is passed to `cmdq_set_db`, and timeout checks must dereference `done` and `cmpt_code` via `saved_cmd_info` because `cmdq_sync_cmd_handler` clears those pointers in `cmd_info`. [F94]
- In `aeq_irq_handler`, `hinic3_cmdq_ceq_handler`, `hinic3_tx_poll`, and `hinic3_rx_poll`, a read barrier (`dma_rmb()` or `rmb()`) must separate reading the hardware completion indicator (`WRAPPED`, `HW_BUSY_BIT`, `hinic3_get_sq_hw_ci`, or `RXDONE`) from reading the entry payload, `tx_info`, or `rx_cqe->vlan_len`/`offload_type`, and `hinic3_add_rx_frag` must call `page_pool_dma_sync_for_cpu` before reading packet data. [F26, F147]
- In `cmdq_wqe_fill`, the WQE body (`dst + 1`) must be copied followed by `wmb()` before publishing the header containing `HW_BUSY_BIT` via `WRITE_ONCE(*dst, *src)`, and in `clear_wqe_complete_bit`, `ctrl->ctrl_info` and `cmd_type` must be cleared followed by `wmb()` before advancing `cons_idx` via `hinic3_wq_put_wqebbs`. [F27]
- During `hinic3_vport_down`, TX must be stopped via `netif_tx_disable(netdev)` and `hinic3_set_vport_enable(..., false)` before calling `hinic3_flush_txqs`; in `hinic3_close`, `hinic3_close_channel` must disable NAPI (`qp_del_napi`), disable MSI-X, and free QP IRQs (`hinic3_qps_irq_uninit`) before `hinic3_free_channel_resources` frees `txqs_res`, `rxqs_res`, `irq_cfg`, and queue pairs. [F23, F24]

## Object Lifetimes

- Calling `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` does not consume the caller's reference on `buf_in`: `cmdq_sync_cmd_direct_resp` increments `buf_in->ref_cnt` for the WQE slot (`cmd_info->buf_in`), while the caller must still release its own initial reference from `hinic3_alloc_cmd_buf` via `hinic3_free_cmd_buf`. [F1]
- `init_eq` keeps each EQ's MSI-X vector disabled until `hinic3_aeqs_init`/`hinic3_ceqs_init` initializes all queues and returns `0` (taking ownership of IRQs so callers must call `hinic3_free_irq` only on failure); in `remove_eq`, the driver must disable MSI-X, call `free_irq`, `disable_work_sync(&eq->aeq_work)`, zero the HW queue length, and disarm CI before `hinic3_queue_pages_free`. [F5, F118, F119]
- In `hinic3_init_comm_ch`, AEQs/MBOX (`init_basic_mgmt_channel`) must initialize before `init_basic_attributes` and CEQs/CMDQs (`init_cmdqs_channel`). On removal (`hinic3_func_uninit`), `hinic3_flush_mgmt_workq` and `hinic3_detach_aux_devices` must finish before `hinic3_free_hwdev` (which flushes RX/TX with CMDQ stopped and doorbells disabled via `hinic3_toggle_doorbell`, then tears down CMDQs/CEQs before MBOX/AEQs) and BAR unmapping. [F28, F120, F135]
- In `struct hinic3_wq`, `wq_block_vaddr` aliases `qpages.pages[0].align_vaddr` when `hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)` is true and is only a separate coherent DMA allocation when `!hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)`, so `wq_free_pages` must check `!hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)` before calling `dma_free_coherent` on `wq_block_vaddr`. [F38]
- Because `rx_alloc_mapped_page` is a no-op when `rx_info->page` is non-NULL, `hinic3_add_rx_frag` and `packaging_skb` (via `hinic3_fetch_rx_buffer`) must either copy data and call `page_pool_put_full_page` or attach via `skb_add_rx_frag` with `skb_mark_for_recycle(skb)`, clear `rx_info->page = NULL`, and advance `rxq->cons_idx` and `rxq->delta` by `sge_num`. [F50, F156]

## API Contracts

- Both `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` and `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` return `0` when transport succeeds even if firmware/ucode sets an error in the response, so callers of `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` expecting a response must check both the return value and the output struct's `head.status` (`msg_head.status`), and callers of `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp` must check `if (err || out_param)`. [F30, F32]
- Before calling `hinic3_cmdq_direct_resp`, callers must initialize all `cmd_buf->buf` fields (including reserved fields, as `dma_pool_alloc` does not zero), set `cmd_buf->size` via `cpu_to_le16`, and swap in-place with `hinic3_cmdq_buf_swab32`; `init_sq_ctxts` and `init_rq_ctxts` must batch at most `HINIC3_Q_CTXT_MAX` (31) queues per 2048B `CMDQ_BUF_SIZE` buffer and rewrite headers and contexts on every loop iteration. [F40, F76, F88]
- `hinic3_alloc_irqs` returns `0` whenever `*act_num > 0` even if `< num`, so callers must clamp queue/EQ counts to `*act_num`; `hinic3_free_irq` and `request_irq`/`free_irq` take the OS vector (`msix_entry.vector` / `irq_id`), whereas hardware MSI-X helpers (`hinic3_set_msix_state`, `hinic3_set_msix_auto_mask_state`, `hinic3_msix_intr_clear_resend_bit`, `hinic3_set_interrupt_cfg_direct`) take `msix_entry.entry` (`msix_entry_idx`). [F33, F42]
- Because each 16-byte software RQ WQE (`struct hinic3_rq_wqe`) spans two 8-byte hardware RQ WQEBBs (`BIT(HINIC3_RQ_WQEBB_SHIFT)`), software RQ indices and depths must be shifted left by `HINIC3_NORMAL_RQ_WQE` (`1`) when passed to hardware in `hinic3_write_db`, `hinic3_rq_prepare_ctxt`, and `hinic3_set_root_ctxt`. [F41]
- Before accessing indirect per-EQ registers (`HINIC3_CSR_AEQ_CTRL_0_ADDR`, `HINIC3_CSR_AEQ_CTRL_1_ADDR`, `EQ_PROD_IDX_REG_ADDR`, `EQ_HI_PHYS_ADDR_REG`, `EQ_LO_PHYS_ADDR_REG`) or `HINIC3_CSR_DMA_ATTR_TBL_ADDR`, callers must write `eq->q_id` to `HINIC3_EQ_INDIR_IDX_ADDR(eq->type)` or the entry index to `HINIC3_CSR_DMA_ATTR_INDIR_IDX_ADDR`. [F43]
- Once `hinic3_init_qps` transfers `qp_params->sqs`, `qp_params->rqs`, and `qp_params->num_qps` into `nic_dev->nic_io`, teardown paths such as `hinic3_close` that pass a fresh `struct hinic3_dyna_qp_params` to `hinic3_free_channel_resources` must first call `hinic3_uninit_qps` to populate `qp_params` from `nic_io`. [F61]
- `hinic3_queue_pages_init` and `get_q_element` require `q_depth`, `page_size`, and `elem_size` to be non-zero powers of 2 with `page_size >= elem_size`; for `struct hinic3_dma_addr_align`, queue accessors and descriptors must use `align_vaddr`, `align_paddr`, and `qpages->page_size`, whereas `hinic3_dma_free_coherent_align` must pass `real_size`, `ori_vaddr`, and `ori_paddr`. [F96, F108]
- The `size` argument to `hinic3_get_comm_features`, `hinic3_set_comm_features`, `hinic3_comm_features_nego`, and `hinic3_feature_nego` is the count of `u64` elements (at most 4, `COMM_MAX_FEATURE_QWORD`) rather than a byte size, because those functions scale `size` by `sizeof(u64)` in `memcpy`. [F110]
- In `hinic3_tx_map_skb` and `hinic3_tx_unmap_skb`, `dma_info[0]` uses `dma_map_single`/`dma_unmap_single` while `dma_info[1..nr_frags]` use `skb_frag_dma_map`/`dma_unmap_page`; `hinic3_send_one_skb` must call `netif_subqueue_sent` only after `hinic3_tx_offload`, `hinic3_set_wqe_combo`, and `hinic3_tx_map_skb` succeed, balanced by `netif_subqueue_completed_wake` in `hinic3_tx_poll` or `netdev_tx_reset_subqueue` in `hinic3_flush_txqs`. [F146, F154]

## Error Handling

- In `hinic3_alloc_rxqs_res`, `page_pool_create` returns `ERR_PTR` rather than `NULL` on failure, so `rqres->page_pool` must be checked with `IS_ERR` and jump to `err_free_cqe`; in `hinic3_send_one_skb`, a failure from `skb_pad` must jump to `err_out` (as `skb_pad` frees `skb`), whereas subsequent pre-TX errors must jump to `err_drop_pkt` (`dev_kfree_skb_any`). [F29, F35]
- Every management command struct passed to `mgmt_msg_params_init_default` and `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` (or `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt_no_ack`) must be zero-initialized (`= {}`) and have `in_size` aligned to `MBOX_XOR_DATA_ALIGN` (4 bytes) because `mbox_prepare_dma_entry` hashes all `in_size` bytes and `buf_out` is untouched on transport error; when `buf_out` is non-NULL, `hinic3_send_mbox_to_mgmt` must verify `le16_to_cpu(msg_desc->msg_len) == msg_params->expected_out_size`. [F31, F65, F67]

## External Contracts

- In `send_mbox_msg`, sending to `MBOX_MGMT_FUNC_ID` must use the DMA queue (`mbox_prepare_dma_msg` with `MBOX_MSG_DATA_DMA`) whenever `MBOX_COMM_F_MBOX_SEGMENT` is not set in `hwdev->features[0]`; inline mailbox headers/segments written to `send_mbox->data` must be 32-bit byte-swapped via `mbox_dword_write`, and AEQ payloads in `aeqe_pos->aeqe_data` must be swapped via `swab32_array` in `aeq_event_handler`. [F64, F126]
- For 1-level CLA (`!hinic3_wq_is_0_level_cla(wq)`), `wq_init_wq_block` caps `num_pages` at `WQ_MAX_NUM_PAGES` (512) and writes `__be64` page addresses while `create_cmdq_wq` caps `num_pages` at 64 at `CMDQ_WQ_CLA_SIZE` (512B) offsets in shared `cmdqs->wq_block_vaddr`/`paddr`; in `hinic3_sq_prepare_ctxt` and `hinic3_rq_prepare_ctxt_get_wq_info`, the first WQE page uses `WQ_PAGE_PFN_SHIFT` (12) whereas `wq_block_paddr` uses `WQ_BLOCK_PFN_SHIFT` (9), unlike `cmdq_init_queue_ctxt` which shifts both by 12 (`CMDQ_PFN`). [F73, F74]
- In `hinic3_write_db` and `cmdq_set_db`, the 16-bit producer index `pi` (`prod_idx`) must be split so its lower 8 bits select the MMIO offset (`(pi & 0xFF) << 3`) while its upper 8 bits (`pi >> 8`) are written in the doorbell payload via `writeq()`; for the SQ CI table `nic_io->ci_vaddr_base` (`HINIC3_CI_Q_ADDR_SIZE` = 64B stride), `hinic3_init_qp_ctxts` must right-shift `HINIC3_CI_PADDR(nic_io->ci_dma_base, q_id)` by 2 (`>> 0x2`) for `hinic3_set_ci_table`. [F75, F81]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `hinic3_tso`, `skb_cow_head(skb, 0)` must be called before sampling network or transport header pointers into `ip.hdr` and `l4.hdr` and mutating `l4.udp->check` or `l4.tcp->check`; in `hinic3_tx_offload`, error paths must overwrite `offload = HINIC3_TX_OFFLOAD_INVALID` (not `|=`) because `hinic3_send_one_skb` checks `offload == HINIC3_TX_OFFLOAD_INVALID` by exact equality. [F56, F83]
- Because `hinic3_queue_pages` DMA pages are allocated separately and are not virtually contiguous, multi-fragment SQ WQEs must obtain buffer descriptors via `hinic3_wq_get_multi_wqebbs` and switch from `wqe_combo->bds_head` to `wqe_combo->bds_sec2` when fragment index `i` reaches `wqe_combo->first_bds_num` in `hinic3_tx_map_skb`. [F70]
- In `hinic3_set_msix_state`, `HINIC3_MSIX_ENABLE` is `0` and `HINIC3_MSIX_DISABLE` is `1` (`if (flag)` disables MSI-X); in `hinic3_tx_poll`, the difference of masked `u16` indices from `hinic3_get_sq_hw_ci` and `hinic3_get_sq_local_ci` must be masked with `txq->q_mask` (`((hw_ci - sw_ci) & txq->q_mask) < tx_info->wqebb_cnt`); and 64-bit masks in `CMDQ_CTXT_SET` and `MBOX_MSG_HEADER_SET`/`GET` must use `GENMASK_ULL`/`BIT_ULL`. [F84, F99, F106]
