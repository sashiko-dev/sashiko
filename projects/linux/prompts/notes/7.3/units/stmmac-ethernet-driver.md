# STMMAC ETHERNET DRIVER

This component implements the core `net_device` lifecycle, TX/RX datapaths (NAPI, `page_pool`, XDP, and AF_XDP zero-copy), phylink/PCS/MDIO bus management, PTP 1588 hardware clock and timestamping, TC hardware offloads (u32, flower, CBS, ETF, taprio/EST, mqprio), 802.1Q/802.1AD VLAN filtering, ethtool selftests, and platform/PCI bus glue for Synopsys DesignWare Ethernet (DWMAC/DWXGMAC) controllers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct stmmac_tx_queue`, `tx_skbuff` and `xdpf` share an anonymous union discriminated per slot by `tx_q->tx_skbuff_dma[entry].buf_type` (`STMMAC_TXBUF_T_SKB` for `tx_skbuff[entry]`, `STMMAC_TXBUF_T_XDP_TX` or `STMMAC_TXBUF_T_XDP_NDO` for `xdpf[entry]`, and neither for `STMMAC_TXBUF_T_XSK_TX`). Never read or free either union member without checking `buf_type` first. [F34]
- `struct stmmac_xdp_buff` must keep `struct xdp_buff xdp` at offset 0 and satisfy `XSK_CHECK_PRIV_TYPE(struct stmmac_xdp_buff)` (`<= offsetofend(struct xdp_buff_xsk, cb)`), because `xsk_buff_to_stmmac_ctx()` overlays it onto `xdp_buff_xsk` and `stmmac_xdp_rx_timestamp()` casts `const struct xdp_md *` to `const struct stmmac_xdp_buff *`. Broken by: placing a field before `xdp`, growing the struct past `cb`, or passing a bare `struct xdp_buff` to `stmmac_xdp_run_prog()`. [F124]
- In `stmmac_setup_dma_desc()`, `STMMAC_TBS_AVAIL` must be set on `dma_conf->tx_queue[chan].tbs` before `alloc_dma_desc_resources()` runs and never mutated before `__free_dma_tx_desc_resources()`, because TX ring allocation and teardown size the coherent ring (`sizeof(struct dma_edesc)` vs `sizeof(struct dma_desc)`) and select `dma_entx` vs `dma_tx` from `tx_q->tbs`. Every full-device ring init/reinit path (`__stmmac_open()`, `stmmac_xdp_open()`, `stmmac_resume()`) must call `stmmac_reset_queues_param(priv)`. [F49, F117]
- In `stmmac_xdp_enable_pool()` and `stmmac_xdp_disable_pool()`, update the queue bit in `priv->af_xdp_zc_qps` only after stopping old NAPI/DMA queues and before `stmmac_enable_rx_queue()`/`stmmac_enable_tx_queue()`, because ring setup inspects `priv->af_xdp_zc_qps` to select `ch->rxtx_napi` vs `ch->rx_napi` and XSK pool binding (`stmmac_get_xsk_pool()`). In `stmmac_enable_rx_queue()` and `stmmac_xdp_open()`, check `rx_q->xsk_pool && rx_q->buf_alloc_num > 0` before programming `xsk_pool_get_rx_frame_size()` via `stmmac_set_dma_bfsize()`. [F46, F55]
- When `stmmac_rx()` or `stmmac_rx_zc()` exits its NAPI loop before the last segment of a multi-descriptor frame (`status & rx_not_ls`, or non-NULL `skb` in `stmmac_rx()`), it must save `error`, `len`, and `skb` in `rx_q->state` and set `rx_q->state_saved = true`. In `stmmac_rx()`, `read_again:` must precede `if (count >= limit) break;` so paths incrementing `count` before `goto read_again` cannot bypass the NAPI budget check. [F62, F86]
- `stmmac_msi_intr_rx()` and `stmmac_msi_intr_tx()` recover `priv` via runtime-indexed `container_of(rx_q, struct stmmac_dma_conf, rx_queue[rx_q->queue_index])` and `container_of(tx_q, struct stmmac_dma_conf, tx_queue[tx_q->queue_index])`, so `queue_index` must equal the array index `i` in `priv->dma_conf` passed as `dev_id` to `request_irq()`. Keep `priv->dma_cap.host_dma_width` (for `dma_set_mask_and_coherent()` and `GFP_DMA32` checks) distinct from device descriptor width `priv->dma_cap.addr64`. [F123, F153]
- Because `tc_taprio_configure()` zeroes `priv->est` in place with `memset()`, `priv->est_lock` must live in `struct stmmac_priv` rather than `struct stmmac_est`. When `stmmac_adjust_time()` adjusts the PTP clock while `priv->est` is enabled, it must disable EST under `priv->est_lock`, adjust systime under `priv->ptp_lock`, and recompute `priv->est->btr` from `priv->est->btr_reserve` and current PTP time before re-enabling EST. [F50, F53]
- `struct stmmac_tc_entry::val` must remain a 16-byte `__packed` struct matching the 4-word hardware RX Parser layout written as `u32` words by `dwmac5_rxp_update_single_entry()`, and `priv->tc_entries[count - 1]` (`in_use = true`, `is_last = true` via `tc_fill_all_pass_entry()`) must never be handed out by `tc_find_entry()`. In `tc_flow_parsers[]`, `tc_add_basic_flow` must precede `tc_add_ports_flow`, and `tc_add_flow()` must run all parsers without aborting on individual errors, setting `entry->in_use = true` if any returns `0`. [F77, F78, F98]
- In `stmmac_hw_init()`, clamp platform queue counts and FIFO sizes in `priv->plat` to non-zero hardware limits in `priv->dma_cap`; in `stmmac_dvr_probe()`, clear `priv->plat->dma_cfg->dche` after `stmmac_hw_init()` when `priv->synopsys_id < DWMAC_CORE_5_20`. In `stmmac_mac_get_caps()`, call `stmmac_mac_update_caps(priv)`, copy `priv->hw->link.caps` to `config->mac_capabilities`, and call `phylink_limit_mac_speed()` when `priv->plat->max_speed` is non-zero. [F52, F129, F140]
- In `stmmac_select_queue()`, any SKB with `SKB_GSO_TCPV4`, `SKB_GSO_TCPV6`, or `SKB_GSO_UDP_L4` in `skb_shinfo(skb)->gso_type` must be assigned to TX queue 0, and in `stmmac_xmit()`, `stmmac_xdp_xmit_zc()`, and `stmmac_xdp_xmit_xdpf()`, `stmmac_prepare_tx_desc()` must be passed `csum_flag = false` whenever `priv->plat->tx_queues_cfg[queue].coe_unsupported` is set. [F128, F161]

## Synchronization

- Because TX descriptor rings are shared across the networking stack, XDP TX/redirect, and NAPI completion/AF_XDP zero-copy transmit, `stmmac_tx_clean()` must hold `__netif_tx_lock_bh()` and `stmmac_xdp_xmit_back()` / `stmmac_xdp_xmit()` must hold `__netif_tx_lock()` across all accesses to `tx_q->cur_tx`, `tx_q->dirty_tx`, and TX descriptors. [F16]
- Every call to `stmmac_enable_dma_irq()` or `stmmac_disable_dma_irq()` on an active channel must be serialized by `spin_lock_irqsave(&ch->lock, flags)` and `spin_unlock_irqrestore(&ch->lock, flags)`. In `stmmac_tx_clean()`, `dma_rmb()` must execute after `stmmac_tx_status()` observes `tx_dma_own` cleared and before reading TX timestamp/status fields, while `stmmac_rx_refill()` and `stmmac_rx_refill_zc()` must execute `dma_wmb()` before `stmmac_set_rx_owner()`. [F19, F145]
- `stmmac_dvr_probe()` must not enable `NETDEV_XDP_ACT_NDO_XMIT` in `ndev->xdp_features`; `stmmac_xdp_set_prog()` must call `xdp_features_clear_redirect_target(dev)` when `prog` is NULL and `xdp_features_set_redirect_target(dev, false)` only after `xchg(&priv->xdp_prog, prog)` and `stmmac_xdp_open(dev)` complete. When disabling an XSK pool in `stmmac_xdp_disable_pool()` or disabling queues with any active `rx_q->xsk_pool` in `stmmac_disable_all_queues()`, call `synchronize_rcu()` after stopping NAPI/queues and before unmapping or teardown. [F14, F152]

## Object Lifetimes

- `free_dma_desc_resources()` must call `free_dma_tx_desc_resources()` before `free_dma_rx_desc_resources()` so pending `XDP_TX` frames in `tx_q->xdpf[]` are returned to their RX page pool before `__free_dma_rx_desc_resources()` unregisters `xdp_rxq` and destroys `rx_q->page_pool`. In `stmmac_tx_clean()` and `stmmac_free_tx_buffer()`, descriptors with `buf_type == STMMAC_TXBUF_T_XDP_TX` must never be passed to `dma_unmap_single()` or `dma_unmap_page()`. [F4, F5]
- During teardown in `__stmmac_release()` and `stmmac_xdp_release()`, `stmmac_disable_all_queues()` and `hrtimer_cancel(&priv->dma_conf.tx_queue[chan].txtimer)` must complete before `stmmac_free_irq()`, `stmmac_stop_all_dma()`, and `free_dma_desc_resources()`. In `stmmac_probe_config_dt()`, error paths after acquiring `phy_node`, `mdio_node`, `stmmac_clk`, or `pclk` must unwind in reverse via `error_hw_init`, `error_pclk_get`, `error_put_mdio`, and `error_put_phy` (matching `devm_stmmac_remove_config_dt()`). [F1, F23]
- `stmmac_mdio_unregister()`, `stmmac_pcs_clean()`, and `stmmac_ptp_unregister()` must clear `priv->mii`, `priv->hw->xpcs`, and `priv->ptp_clock` to `NULL`, and `stmmac_ptp_register()` must reset `priv->ptp_clock = NULL` and destroy `priv->aux_ts_lock` when `ptp_clock_register()` fails. In `stmmac_selftests.c`, `dev_direct_xmit()` consumes `skb` on both success and failure: call `kfree_skb()` only on error paths before `dev_direct_xmit()`. [F9, F12]
- Callbacks accessing MAC or MDIO registers while the interface may be closed (`stmmac_mdio_access()`, `stmmac_xgmac2_mdio_read()`, `stmmac_xgmac2_mdio_write()`, `stmmac_set_mac_address()`, `stmmac_vlan_rx_add_vid()`, and `stmmac_vlan_rx_kill_vid()`) must bracket MMIO access with `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` and `pm_runtime_put()`. [F136]

## API Contracts

- `__stmmac_open()` shallow-copies `dma_conf` into `priv->dma_conf` without freeing DMA descriptor resources on failure, so callers of `stmmac_setup_dma_desc()` (`stmmac_open()` and `stmmac_change_mtu()`) must call only `kfree(dma_conf)` on success, but must call `free_dma_desc_resources(priv, dma_conf)` before `kfree(dma_conf)` on any error path. [F2]
- `stmmac_xsk_tx_metadata_ops` splits its `void *_priv` context by phase: `xsk_tx_metadata_request()` in `stmmac_xdp_xmit_zc()` must pass `struct stmmac_metadata_request *`, whereas `xsk_tx_metadata_complete()` in `stmmac_tx_clean()` must pass `struct stmmac_xsk_tx_complete *`. `stmmac_set_rx_mode()`, `stmmac_vlan_rx_add_vid()`, and `stmmac_vlan_rx_kill_vid()` may run with BHs disabled and must not call `phylink_rx_clk_stop_block()`/`unblock()`. [F125, F144]
- `stmmac_ptp_register()` divides `(2 * NSEC_PER_SEC)` by `priv->plat->clk_ptp_rate` without checking for zero and mutates per-instance capabilities on `priv->ptp_clock_ops` (recovered via `container_of()`), so callers (`stmmac_setup_ptp()`) must invoke it only after `stmmac_init_timestamping()` succeeds and never register `&stmmac_ptp_clock_ops` directly. [F67, F105]
- Callers of `stmmac_dvr_probe()` (`stmmac_get_platform_resources()`, `stmmac_pci_probe()`) must zero-initialize `struct stmmac_resources`. XDP/AF_XDP require single-buffer frames: `stmmac_xdp_set_prog()` and `stmmac_change_mtu()` must disallow `dev->mtu > ETH_DATA_LEN` while XDP is enabled, and `stmmac_xdp_enable_pool()` must reject XSK pools with `xsk_pool_get_rx_frame_size(pool) < ETH_FRAME_LEN + VLAN_HLEN * 2`. [F40, F83]
- In `vlan_add_hw_rx_fltr()`, when `hw->num_vlan == 1`, reject VID 0 with `-EPERM` and reject a second VID if `hw->vlan_filter[0] & VLAN_TAG_VID` is set. In `stmmac_init_phy()`, verify `priv->plat->phy_addr >= 0` before `mdiobus_get_phy()` when `!phy_fwnode || IS_ERR(phy_fwnode)`; pass `ffs(pcs_mask) - 1` to `xpcs_create_mdiodev()`; and in `stmmac_mdio_read_c45()`/`write_c45()`, pass `devad` as `gr` and `phyreg << MII_GMAC4_REG_ADDR_SHIFT` in `data`. [F25, F28, F45, F84]

## Error Handling

- `stmmac_xdp_run_prog()` encodes `STMMAC_XDP_PASS` (`0`) as `NULL` and non-pass verdicts as `ERR_PTR(-res)`, so `stmmac_rx()` must reset `skb = NULL` whenever `IS_ERR(skb)` is true before `goto read_again` or continuing. In `stmmac_tx_clean()`, call `xsk_tx_metadata_complete()` on non-SKB frames only when both `tx_q->xsk_pool` and `xp_tx_metadata_enabled(tx_q->xsk_pool)` hold. [F26, F29]
- Because `stmmac_do_callback()` returns `-EINVAL` when an optional `hw` callback is unimplemented, callers (`stmmac_safety_feat_interrupt()`, `stmmac_xmit()` for `stmmac_jumbo_frm()`) must ignore `-EINVAL`. In `stmmac_get_tx_hwtstamp()` and `stmmac_xsk_fill_timestamp()`, `stmmac_get_tx_timestamp_status()` returns `1` on success (requiring `stmmac_get_timestamp()`), whereas fallback `stmmac_get_mac_tx_timestamp()` returns `0` on success and populates `ns` directly. [F30, F31]
- Reset `ERR_PTR` fields to `NULL` on failure: `rx_q->page_pool` when `page_pool_create()` fails in `__alloc_dma_rx_desc_resources()`, and optional `plat->stmmac_clk` or `plat->clk_ptp_ref` when `devm_clk_get()` fails in `stmmac_probe_config_dt()`. In `stmmac_mdio_register()`, convert `-ENODEV` from `of_mdiobus_register()` to `err = 0` before freeing `new_bus` without setting `priv->mii`. [F27, F35, F37]
- Avoid division by zero: check `!priv->tx_coal_frames[queue]` before `tx_q->tx_count_frames % priv->tx_coal_frames[queue]` in `stmmac_xdp_xmit_zc()`, `stmmac_tso_xmit()`, and `stmmac_xmit()`; validate `qopt->cycle_time != 0` before `stmmac_calc_tas_basetime()` in `tc_taprio_configure()`; and in `tc_setup_cbs()`, when `!qopt->enable`, reset the queue to `MTL_QUEUE_DCB` and return `0` before checking or dividing by `port_transmit_rate_kbps`. [F85, F110, F113]

## External Contracts

- Hardware checksum offload requires `stmmac_has_ip_ethertype(skb)`: in `stmmac_xmit()`, when `skb->ip_summed == CHECKSUM_PARTIAL`, call `skb_checksum_help(skb)` and clear `csum_insertion` if `coe_unsupported` or `!stmmac_has_ip_ethertype(skb)`; in `stmmac_rx()` and `stmmac_dispatch_skb_zc()`, set `CHECKSUM_UNNECESSARY` only when `priv->hw->rx_csum`, `stmmac_has_ip_ethertype(skb)` after `eth_type_trans()`, and `!(status & csum_none)` hold. In `tc_taprio_map_maxsdu_txq()`, add `ETH_HLEN - ETH_TLEN` to non-zero `qopt->max_sdu[i]` and map via `mqprio` offset/count to per-TXQ `priv->est->max_sdu[j]`. [F158, F159, F168]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `stmmac_setup_dma_desc()` and all helpers it calls (`alloc_dma_desc_resources()`, `init_dma_desc_rings()`, `free_dma_desc_resources()`, and their per-queue callees), queue rings and buffer sizes must be read and written on the passed `struct stmmac_dma_conf *dma_conf` argument rather than `priv->dma_conf`. [F91]
- In `stmmac_rx()` and `stmmac_rx_zc()`, `if (unlikely(error && (status & rx_not_ls))) goto read_again;` must precede `if (unlikely(error))` so non-last descriptors of an erred multi-descriptor frame are drained first. In `stmmac_rx_buf1_len()`, the `priv->sph` checks must precede `if (status & rx_not_ls)`, and `len += buf1_len` in `stmmac_rx()` must execute before `stmmac_rx_buf2_len(priv, p, status, len)`. [F88, F89]
- In `stmmac_xdp_xmit_zc()`, `budget` is tested after the loop in `return !!budget && work_done;` and must be decremented in the `for`-loop step (`for (; budget > 0; budget--)`), never post-decremented (`budget-- > 0`). In `struct stmmac_txq_stats`, TX submission paths must update `txq_stats->q` under `q_syncp`, whereas NAPI paths (`stmmac_tx_clean()`, `stmmac_xdp_xmit_zc()`, `stmmac_napi_poll_tx()`, `stmmac_napi_poll_rxtx()`) must update `txq_stats->napi` under `napi_syncp`. [F71, F92]
- In `stmmac_tc.c`, callers of `tc_find_flow()` and `tc_find_rfs()` must search with `get_free = false` before `get_free = true`; in `tc_fill_actions()`, set `val.af`/`val.rf` on `frag` when `frag != NULL`; and in `tc_fill_entry()`, evaluate `GENMASK(31, rem * 8)` and `GENMASK(rem * 8 - 1, 0)` only when `rem != 0`. Multiply CBS slopes/credits by `1024ll`, cast seconds to `u64` before `* NSEC_PER_SEC` in `stmmac_adjust_time()`/`stmmac_enable()`, and use `memmove()` in `stmmac_rx_vlan()`. [F96, F97, F107, F108, F112, F122]
