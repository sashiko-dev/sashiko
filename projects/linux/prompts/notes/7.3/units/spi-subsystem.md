# SPI SUBSYSTEM

This unit contains SPI subsystem helper headers, the generic bitbanging SPI library (`spi-bitbang.c`, `spi-bitbang-txrx.h`), and SPI, QSPI, and OSPI host controller and peripheral drivers bridging Linux SPI, `spi-mem`, and `dirmap` APIs to MMIO, PIO, interrupt, and DMA hardware.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `platform_set_drvdata()` in `spi-cadence-quadspi.c`, `spi-amlogic-spifc-a1.c`, `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, `spi-ti-qspi.c`, and `spi-sn-f-ospi.c` stores the driver private struct (`cqspi_st`, `amlogic_spifc_a1`, `stm32_ospi`, `stm32_qspi`, `ti_qspi`, `f_ospi`) rather than `struct spi_controller *`; PM and remove callbacks must cast `dev_get_drvdata()` / `platform_get_drvdata()` to that private struct. [F121, F122, F289]
- `spi-bitbang.c` callbacks cast `spi_controller_get_devdata()` directly to `struct spi_bitbang *`, so drivers allocating private state via `spi_alloc_host()` (`struct butterfly`, `struct ath79_spi`, `struct au1550_spi`) must keep `struct spi_bitbang` as their first struct member. [F123]
- In `spi-bitbang-txrx.h` and `spi-bitbang.c`, `bitbang_txrx_le_cpha0()`/`cpha1()` must capture `rxbit = bits - 1` before the `for (; likely(bits); bits--)` loop, and `bitbang_txrx_8()`/`16()`/`32()` must decrement byte `count` by 1, 2, or 4 while guarding with `count > 0`, `count > 1`, or `count > 3`. [F93, F128]
- In `spi-bcm2835.c`, `bcm2835_spi_interrupt()` receives `struct bcm2835_spi *bs` as `dev_id`, whereas DMA callbacks `bcm2835_spi_dma_rx_done()` and `bcm2835_spi_dma_tx_done()` receive `struct spi_controller *ctlr` as `data`. In `struct bcm2835_spidev`, `clear_rx_cs` must stay `____cacheline_aligned` (allocated with `dma_get_cache_alignment()` in `bcm2835_spi_setup()`), and updating `target->prepare_cs` must update `target->clear_rx_cs` (`cs | BCM2835_SPI_CS_TA | BCM2835_SPI_CS_DMAEN | BCM2835_SPI_CS_CLEAR_RX`) and call `dma_sync_single_for_device()` on `target->clear_rx_addr` when `ctlr->dma_rx` is present. [F58, F132, F154]
- In `spi-bcm2835.c`, `bcm2835_spi_can_dma()` must reject transfers `< BCM2835_SPI_DMA_MIN_LENGTH` (96) so `bcm2835_spi_transfer_prologue()` can write up to 6 prologue bytes without scatterlist underflow, and `bcm2835_spi_max_transfer_size()` must cap DMA transfers at `65532` (32-bit aligned in 16-bit `BCM2835_SPI_DLEN`). `bs->tx_prologue` is the sole active-prologue guard and `bcm2835_spi_undo_prologue()` must reset `bs->tx_prologue = 0` on all paths including `!bs->tx_buf`. [F83, F84, F99]
- In `atmel-quadspi.c`, updates to `QSPI_SCR` or `QSPI_MR` must mask only the target bitfield in `aq->scr` or `aq->mr` and pass `aq->mr` for `QSPI_MR` and `aq->scr` for `QSPI_SCR` to `atmel_qspi_write()`. DLL control (`QSPI_CR_DLLON`/`DLLOFF`, `QSPI_DLLCFG`, `QSPI_SR2_DLOCK`) requires `aq->caps->has_dllon`, and `atmel_qspi_set_pad_calibration()` requires `aq->caps->octal && aq->caps->has_padcalib`. `atmel_qspi_exec_op()` must reject `op->addr.val + op->data.nbytes > aq->mmap_size` or `op->addr.nbytes > 4`. [F53, F77, F95, F144]
- In `spi-cadence-quadspi.c`, whenever `cqspi_controller_init()` runs (`cqspi_probe()`, `cqspi_runtime_resume()`), `cqspi->current_cs = -1` and `cqspi->sclk = 0` must be reset so `cqspi_configure()` reprograms registers. `cqspi_command_read()`/`write()` must reject `op->data.nbytes > CQSPI_STIG_DATA_LEN_MAX` (8). In `cqspi_versal_indirect_read_dma()`, `CQSPI_REG_CONFIG_DMA_MASK` and `PM_OSPI_MUX_SEL_DMA` must be reverted to `PM_OSPI_MUX_SEL_LINEAR` on both completion and `failrd` paths before `cqspi_indirect_read_execute()`, and 64-bit `dma_addr` must use `lower_32_bits()` and `upper_32_bits()`. [F61, F63, F78, F120]
- In `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, when `!tspi->is_packed`, each SPI word occupies one 4-byte `u32` in `tx_dma_buf`/`rx_dma_buf` (`curr_dma_words` capped at `max_buf_size / 4`, DMA length `curr_dma_words * 4`); in `spi-tegra114.c`, unpacked helpers must clamp byte counts to `t->len - tspi->cur_pos` before copying, and `tspi->cur_pos` must only be updated from `cur_tx_pos`/`cur_rx_pos` after `tegra_spi_read_rx_fifo_to_client_rxbuf()` or `tegra_spi_copy_spi_rxbuf_to_client_rxbuf()`. When `tspi->is_packed`, FIFO and DMA byte counts must round up to 32-bit words via `DIV_ROUND_UP(..., 4)`. [F252, F268, F282, F290]
- In `spi-tegra114.c` and `spi-tegra20-slink.c`, per-CS updates in `tegra_spi_setup()`, `tegra_spi_set_hw_cs_timing()`, and `tegra_slink_setup()` must preserve sibling CS bits in `def_command1_reg`, `spi_cs_timing1`, `spi_cs_timing2`, and `def_command_reg`; `tegra*_runtime_suspend()` must do a dummy register read before `clk_disable_unprepare()`; and in `spi-tegra114.c`, resetting `SPI_COMMAND2` to `def_command2_reg` in `tegra_spi_resume()` or on timeout reset must set `tspi->last_used_cs = host->num_chipselect + 1`, and accessing `SPI_INTR_MASK` in `tegra_spi_setup()` requires `tspi->soc_data->has_intr_mask_reg`. [F227, F228, F303, F312]
- In `spi-stm32-ospi.c` and `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, `setup()` must cache `OSPI_CR`/`QSPI_CR` and `OSPI_DCR1`/`QSPI_DCR` in `cr_reg` and `dcr_reg` for `resume()`. In `dirmap_read()`, `CR_FMODE_MM` / `CCR_FMODE_MM` must only be selected when `op.addr.val + op.data.nbytes + 1 < mm_size && op.addr.buswidth`, and in `stm32_ospi_send()` / `stm32_qspi_send()`, memory-mapped reads must branch to `abort` after `stm32_ospi_xfer()` / `stm32_qspi_tx()` even when `err == 0` (skipping `wait_cmd`). In `stm32_qspi_setup()`, `mode = spi->mode & (SPI_TX_OCTAL | SPI_RX_OCTAL)` must be grouped with `gpiod_count(qspi->dev, "cs") == -ENOENT`, and `stm32_qspi_get_mode()` must clamp `buswidth >= 4` to `CCR_BUSWIDTH_4`. [F229, F247, F258, F262, F308]
- Polling loops with `jiffies` deadlines in `bcm2835_spi_transfer_one_poll()`, `bcm2835aux_spi_transfer_one_poll()`, `cqspi_wait_idle()`, `tegra_spi_flush_fifos()`, `qspi_is_busy()`, and `ti_qspi_poll_wc()` must use `time_after()` rather than `<` or `>`, and in `bcm2835_spi_transfer_one()` and `bcm2835aux_spi_transfer_one()`, divisions by `polling_limit_us` and `hz_per_byte` must check for non-zero divisors. [F113, F114, F287]

## Synchronization
- In `bcm2835_spi_transfer_one_dma()`, `bs->tx_dma_active = true` with `smp_wmb()` must precede `dma_async_issue_pending(ctlr->dma_tx)`, and `bs->rx_dma_active = true` with `smp_mb()` must follow `dma_async_issue_pending(ctlr->dma_rx)`, pairing with `smp_wmb()` and `cmpxchg(&bs->rx_dma_active, true, false)` in `bcm2835_spi_dma_tx_done()`. In `bcm2835_spi_prepare_sg()`, `bs->target = target` must be set before `dmaengine_submit(desc)` for TX-only DMA (`is_tx && !tfr->rx_buf`). Because `bcm2835_spi_dma_rx_done()` and `bcm2835_spi_dma_tx_done()` run in atomic DMA callback context, they must use `dmaengine_terminate_async()` rather than `dmaengine_terminate_sync()`. [F15, F16, F152]
- Because `bcm2835_spi_interrupt()` and `bcm2835aux_spi_interrupt()` use `IRQF_SHARED` without a lock serializing FIFO state, `bcm2835_spi_transfer_one_irq()` must write `BCM2835_SPI_CS_TA` and pre-fill the TX FIFO before enabling `BCM2835_SPI_CS_INTR | BCM2835_SPI_CS_INTD`, `bcm2835aux_spi_transfer_one_irq()` must program `CNTL1`/`CNTL0` and pre-fill the TX FIFO before `__bcm2835aux_spi_transfer_one_irq()` enables `TXEMPTY | IDLE`, and both IRQ handlers must return `IRQ_NONE` if hardware interrupt-enable bits are clear. [F19, F20, F150]
- In `atmel_qspi_wait_for_completion()`, after reading `QSPI_SR`, `reinit_completion(&aq->cmd_completion)`, `aq->pending = sr & irq_mask`, and `aq->irq_mask = irq_mask` must be set before writing `irq_mask` to `QSPI_IER`, and both `atmel_qspi_wait_for_completion()` and `atmel_qspi_interrupt()` must test `((... & irq_mask) == irq_mask)`. In `atmel_qspi_transfer()`, a dummy `atmel_qspi_read(aq, QSPI_IFR)` must precede AHB accesses on `aq->mem + offset`, and `rmb()`/`wmb()` must precede writing `QSPI_CR_LASTXFER` to `QSPI_CR`. [F21, F22, F96]
- In `spi-stm32-ospi.c` and `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, entry points (`exec_op`, `transfer_one_message`, `setup`, `dirmap_read`, `poll_status`) must call `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` before locking `ospi->lock` / `qspi->lock` and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` after unlocking. The mutex must be held continuously across setting `fmode` (and poll timeout/mask registers) and calling `stm32_ospi_send()` / `stm32_qspi_send()`, and in `transfer_one_message()`, any loop error must `goto end_of_transfer` to deassert CS, unlock `lock`, finalize the message, and put runtime PM. [F173, F185, F186, F320]
- In `spi-ti-qspi.c`, `qspi->list_lock` must be held across checking/updating `qspi->mmap_enabled` and `qspi->current_cs`, configuring clocks and mmap registers, and running transfers in `ti_qspi_exec_mem_op()` and `ti_qspi_start_transfer_one()`. In `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, `ospi->mlock` must be held across the entire indirect read/write sequence in `f_ospi_indir_read()` and `f_ospi_indir_write()`. In `spi-sprd-adi.c`, `sprd_adi_read()` and `sprd_adi_write()` must hold `sadi->hwlock` via `hwspin_lock_timeout_irqsave()` across all register validation and accesses. [F190, F192, F319]

## Object Lifetimes
- When requesting optional DMA channels in `bcm2835_dma_init()`, `atmel_qspi_dma_init()`, and `cqspi_request_mmap_dma()`, `-EPROBE_DEFER` must be propagated, whereas any other error must reset `ctlr->dma_tx`, `ctlr->dma_rx`, `aq->rx_chan`, `aq->tx_chan`, or `cqspi->rx_chan` to `NULL` (releasing `aq->rx_chan` if `aq->tx_chan` fails, and resetting `bs->fill_tx_addr` / `target->clear_rx_addr` to `0` on `dma_mapping_error()` in `bcm2835`), and `bcm2835_dma_release()` must clear `ctlr->dma_tx` and `ctlr->dma_rx` to `NULL`. [F7, F40, F45, F135]
- In `atmel_qspi_dma_xfer()`, `cqspi_direct_read_execute()`, `ti_qspi_dma_xfer()`, `stm32_ospi_tx_dma()`, `stm32_qspi_tx_dma()`, and `sun6i_spi_transfer_one()` (which must wait for `sspi->dma_rx_done` after `sspi->done` when `rx_len > 0`), `reinit_completion()` must precede issuing DMA, and on timeout `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` (or `dmaengine_terminate_all()` in `stm32_ospi`/`qspi_tx_dma`) must be called before unmapping DMA buffers/scatterlists. In `au1550_spi_dma_txrxb()`, `au1xxx_dbdma_stop()` must be called on `dma_tx_ch` and `dma_rx_ch` before `dma_sync_single_for_cpu()`/`dma_unmap_single()`. [F23, F24, F29, F172, F191, F327]
- In `bcm2835_spi_transfer_one_dma()`, once `bcm2835_spi_transfer_prologue()` modifies `tfr->tx_sg` or `tfr->rx_sg`, every completion, error, and abort path must call `bcm2835_spi_undo_prologue(bs)` before unmapping, and reusable cyclic descriptors (`bs->fill_tx_desc`, `target->clear_rx_desc`) must be terminated on transfer completion/abort before resubmission or freeing. In `bcm2835_spi_remove()`, `spi_unregister_controller(ctlr)` must precede `bcm2835_dma_release(ctlr, bs)`, which must call `dmaengine_terminate_sync()` on `dma_tx` and `dma_rx` before freeing `fill_tx_desc` and unmapping `fill_tx_addr`. [F8, F18, F140]
- Driver removal and PM teardown ordering: `cqspi_probe()` must initialize `cqspi->refcount` and `cqspi->inflight_ops` to 1 before `spi_register_controller()`, and `cqspi_remove()` must set `cqspi->refcount = 0` and call `cqspi_wait_idle()` if `!refcount_dec_and_test(&cqspi->inflight_ops)` before `spi_unregister_controller()` and disabling hardware [F26]; `stm32_ospi_remove()` and `stm32_qspi_remove()` must call `spi_unregister_controller()` while runtime-resumed before disabling `OSPI_CR`/`QSPI_CR`, releasing DMA channels, and calling `mutex_destroy()` [F189]; `stm32_ospi` must obtain `ospi->rstc` via `devm_reset_control_array_get_exclusive_released()` and bracket it with `reset_control_acquire()` in probe/resume and `reset_control_release()` in remove/suspend [F300]; and `atmel_qspi_exec_op()` owns the runtime PM reference across `aq->ops->set_cfg` and `aq->ops->transfer`, so those callbacks must not call `pm_runtime_put*()` [F137].

## API Contracts
- `struct spi_bitbang` contracts: custom `bitbang->txrx_bufs` callbacks must return `transfer->len` on success or a negative errno on failure because `spi_bitbang_transfer_one()` turns any other non-negative return into `-EREMOTEIO` [F36]; before `spi_bitbang_init()`/`start()`, `bitbang->ctlr` must be set with `ctlr->transfer` and `ctlr->transfer_one_message` `NULL`, `bitbang->chipselect` must be set if `!ctlr->use_gpio_descriptors` or `SPI_CONTROLLER_GPIO_SS` is set, and in `spi-xilinx.c`, `xspi->bitbang.setup_transfer = xilinx_spi_setup_transfer` must be set alongside `xilinx_spi_txrx_bufs` [F38, F335]; when using default `!bitbang->txrx_bufs`, `spi->controller_state` is owned by `spi_bitbang` as `struct spi_bitbang_cs *`, so glue drivers must use `spi->controller_data` [F124]; and in `spi-bitbang-txrx.h`, `getmiso()` must return only `0` or `1` and `bits` must be in `1..32` [F108, F109].
- In `atmel-quadspi.c`, switching to Serial Memory Mode must use `atmel_qspi_set_serial_memory_mode()` (which updates `aq->mr` and calls `atmel_qspi_update_config(aq)` when `aq->caps->has_gclk`), and when `!aq->caps->octal`, `atmel_qspi_supports_op()` must reject `addr.nbytes == 2 && dummy.nbytes == 0 && cmd.buswidth != addr.buswidth`. In `spi-cadence-quadspi.c`, `cqspi_supports_mem_op()` must require `dtr == true && buswidth == 8` across all non-empty (`nbytes > 0`) phases of any DTR op before calling `spi_mem_default_supports_op()`, and in `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, `f_ospi_supports_op()` must finish by returning `spi_mem_default_supports_op(mem, op)`. [F54, F166, F167, F328]
- In `spi-ti-qspi.c`, `ti_qspi_adjust_op_size()` must clamp `op->data.nbytes` at `qspi->mmap_size - op->addr.val` (and `QSPI_FRAME` above `mmap_size`), `ti_qspi_exec_mem_op()` must return `-EOPNOTSUPP` for non-read ops, invalid address widths, or `from + op->data.nbytes > qspi->mmap_size`, and when `qspi->rx_chan` is used, `spi_controller_dma_map_mem_op_data()` must only be used if `virt_addr_valid(op->data.buf.in)` (otherwise falling back to `ti_qspi_dma_bounce_buffer()`). In `spi-stm32-ospi.c` and `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, `stm32_ospi_xfer()` and `stm32_qspi_tx()` must fall back to polling when `tx_dma` fails, and `transfer_one_message()` must abort with `-EINVAL` if a `transfer->dummy_data` entry is last in `msg->transfers`. [F249, F329, F331, F332, F334]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `spi-cadence-quadspi.c`, writes to `CQSPI_REG_SIZE` (`cqspi_read_setup()`/`cqspi_write_setup()`) and `CQSPI_REG_INDIRECTRD`/`WR` (`cqspi_indirect_read_execute()`/`write_execute()`) must be flushed with `readl()` before accessing `cqspi->ahb_base`; `cqspi_configure()` must disable the controller via `cqspi_controller_enable(cqspi, 0)` before changing chip select, baudrate, delays, or read capture and re-enable it afterward; `ioread32_rep()`/`iowrite32_rep()` take 32-bit word counts (`bytes / 4`) while `rxbuf`/`txbuf` advance in bytes; and `cqspi_direct_read_execute()` and `cqspi_read()` must check `virt_addr_valid(buf)` (and `(dma_align & CQSPI_DMA_UNALIGN) == 0` for `indirect_read_dma`) before DMA mapping. [F131, F134, F146, F155]
- In `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, `spi-stm32-qspi.c`, and `spi-sn-f-ospi.c`, `op->dummy.nbytes * 8 / op->dummy.buswidth` must only be evaluated after checking that `op->dummy.nbytes` (and `op->dummy.buswidth`) is non-zero [F214]. In `stm32_ospi_send()` and `stm32_qspi_send()`, `OSPI_DLR`/`QSPI_DLR` and `CCR_ADSIZE_MASK` encode `nbytes - 1`, so `op->data.nbytes` and `op->addr.nbytes` must be checked for non-zero before subtracting 1 [F248], and in `spi-stm32-ospi.c`, `spi->chip_select[]` and `spi->cs_gpiod[]` must be indexed by `ffs(spi->cs_index_mask) - 1` (with `cs < STM32_OSPI_MAX_NORCHIP`) [F246].
