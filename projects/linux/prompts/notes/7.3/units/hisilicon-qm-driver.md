# HISILICON QM DRIVER

The HiSilicon Queue Manager (QM) driver provides common PCIe queue management, hardware mailbox, doorbell, interrupt handling, SR-IOV, error recovery/reset, runtime PM, and hardware scatter-gather list (SGL) pool infrastructure for HiSilicon Kunpeng hardware accelerators (such as SEC, HPRE, and ZIP). It supports both kernel crypto subsystem consumers via queue pairs (`hisi_qp`) and user-space SVA (Shared Virtual Addressing) consumers through the UACCE framework (`uacce_qm_ops`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `qm->err_ini` is only populated on `QM_HW_PF` and is `NULL` on VFs; functions reachable on VFs or via `pci_physfn()` in a VM (`qm_check_dev_error()`, `hisi_qm_dev_err_init()`, `hisi_qm_dev_err_uninit()`, `qm_clear_device()`) must return early when `fun_type == QM_HW_VF`, and PF callers must null-check optional `hisi_qm_err_ini` hooks (e.g. `set_priv_status`). [F36]
- In `struct hisi_qm_hw_ops`, `get_vft`, `hw_error_uninit`, and `hw_error_handle` are `NULL` in `qm_hw_ops_v1`, and `set_ifc_begin`, `set_ifc_end`, and `get_ifc` are `NULL` in `qm_hw_ops_v1` and `qm_hw_ops_v2`; callers must null-check those hooks or gate IFC calls on `QM_SUPPORT_MB_COMMAND`. [F37]
- `qm->factor` is allocated in `hisi_qm_memory_init()` only when `test_bit(QM_SUPPORT_FUNC_QOS, &qm->caps)` is true (`factor[0].func_qos` is initialized once for PF; `factor[1..num_vfs].func_qos` are initialized in `hisi_qm_init_vf_qos()`); all `qm->factor` accesses must be gated on `QM_SUPPORT_FUNC_QOS`, and `func_qos` must stay in `1..QM_QOS_MAX_VAL` so `ir = func_qos * QM_QOS_RATE` is non-zero before `/ ir` in `qm_shaper_init_vft()` or `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()`. [F39, F62, F95]
- Across PF/VF management, `qm->factor[]`, `hisi_qm_set_vft()`, `qm_set_vft_common()`, and IFC helpers (`qm_wait_vf_prepare_finish()`, `qm_ping_single_vf()`, `qm_ping_all_vfs()`, `qm_cmd_process()`, `qm_get_ifc_pf()`), `0` identifies PF and `1..qm->vfs_num` (`BIT(1)..BIT(qm->vfs_num)`) identify VFs; in `qm_set_vft_common()` (`SHAPER_VFT`), `qm->factor[fun_num]` must be indexed before `base << QM_SHAPER_VFT_OFFSET` is ORed into `fun_num`. [F46, F74]
- In `qm_get_qos_value()`, `pci_physfn(pdev)` must equal `qm->pdev` before extracting `pdev->devfn` as `fun_index`, and `qm_func_shaper_enable()` must verify `fun_index <= pci_sriov_get_totalvfs(qm->pdev)` before indexing `qm->factor[fun_index]`. [F70]
- In `qm_vf_q_assign()`, verify `num_vfs != 0` and `vfs_q_num = qm->ctrl_qp_num - qm->qp_num >= num_vfs` before `vfs_q_num / num_vfs`, start VF queues at `q_base = qm->qp_num`, and if `hisi_qm_set_vft()` fails at VF `i`, clear VFTs for `j = num_vfs` down to `i + 1` (just as `qm_set_sqc_cqc_vft()`'s `back_sqc_cqc` must clear both `SQC_VFT` and `CQC_VFT` if `qm_shaper_init_vft()` fails). [F16, F60, F99]
- `qm->vfs_num` must be non-zero and `<= QM_MAX_VFS_NUM_V2` (`63`) before evaluating `GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)`; `qm_wait_vf_prepare_finish()` must test `(val & GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)) == GENMASK(vfs_num, 1)` in `QM_IFC_INT_SOURCE_P`, whereas `qm_ping_all_vfs()` must test `!(val & GENMASK(vfs_num, 1))` in `QM_IFC_READY_STATUS`. [F83, F96]
- Stack descriptors (`qm_sqc`, `qm_cqc`, `qm_eqc`, `qm_aeqc`, plus `mailbox->rsvd = 0` in `qm_mb_pre_init()`) must zero unwritten fields and encode depths as `depth - 1`; `qm_vft_data_cfg()` (`SQC_VFT`) must encode V2+ queue count as `number - 1` and cast `base` and `number - 1` to `u64` before shifting by `28`/`45`, while `qm_get_vft_v2()` must add `1` after masking `sqc_vft >> QM_SQC_VFT_NUM_SHIFT_V2`. [F44, F73, F93]
- Every SQE submission in `hisi_qp_send()` increments `qp->qp_status.used` (with `qm_get_avail_sqe()` treating the SQ as full at `qp->sq_depth - 1`), which must be balanced by `qm_poll_req_cb()` on completion or by `qp_stop_fail_cb()` (after `flush_workqueue(qm->wq)` in `qm_stop_qp_nolock()` when `qp->is_resetting` is true) invoking `qp->req_cb` and decrementing `used`. [F10, F71]
- `qm_init_qp_status()` must reset `used = 0`, `sq_tail = 0`, `cq_head = 0`, and `cqc_phase = true`; `qm_init_eq_aeq_status()` must reset `eq_head = 0`, `aeq_head = 0`, `eqc_phase = true`, and `aeqc_phase = true` with `qm->qdma.va` zeroed before `qm_eq_aeq_ctx_cfg()`; and wrapping CQ/EQ/AEQ head indices from `depth - 1` to `0` must invert the phase bit and recompute the entry pointer from the ring base. [F50, F55, F56]
- `qm_create_qp_nolock()` and `hisi_qm_release_qp()` must update `qm->qp_in_used` and `qm->qp_idr` in lockstep under `qm->qps_lock`, while `qm_frozen()` reserves the QM by setting `qm->qp_in_used = qm->qp_num` when `qm->qp_in_used == 0`. [F53]
- In `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()`, validate `0 < sge_nr <= HISI_ACC_SGL_SGE_NR_MAX` (`255`) before dividing by `sgl_num_per_block`, preserve loop counter `i` into `if (remain_sgl > 0)` (for `block[i]` and `err_free_mem`), and pass `block[i].size` to `dma_free_coherent()` in `hisi_acc_free_sgl_pool()`; in `hisi_acc_sg_buf_unmap()`, call `clear_hw_sgl_sge(hw_sgl)` before zeroing `entry_sum_in_sgl`, `entry_sum_in_chain`, and `entry_length_in_sgl`. [F57, F87, F91, F101]
- `qm_cap_query_info[]` and `qm_basic_info[]` are indexed positionally by `enum qm_cap_table_type` and `enum qm_basic_type` (with `QM_XEQ_DEPTH_CAP` and `QM_QP_DEPTH_CAP` requiring `GENMASK(31, 0)`), `qm_cap_query_info[]` must be cached in `qm_cap_table` during `qm_get_pci_res()` for IRQ setup/teardown when PCI MSE may be disabled, and `qm_cap_bits` / `qm_misc_ctl_bits` must be `< BITS_PER_LONG`. [F69, F111, F114, F117]
- In `qm_get_version()`, when `pdev->revision == QM_HW_V3`, read `QM_SUB_VERSION_ID` (`0x210`) and override `qm->ver` if non-zero before selecting `qm->ops`; during controller reset on `qm->ver < QM_HW_V3` with `is_qm_ecc_mbit` or `is_dev_ecc_mbit` set, `qm_restart_prepare()` must mask `msi_wr_port` in `ACC_AM_CFG_PORT_WR_EN` before clearing ECC errors, and `qm_restart_done()` must restore `msi_wr_port` and clear both flags. [F67, F116]

## Synchronization

- Lock acquisition must follow `qm_list->lock` -> `qm->qps_lock` -> `qm->mailbox_lock`, and runtime PM helpers (`qm_pm_get_sync()` / `qm_pm_put_sync()`) must be called outside `qm->qps_lock` because `hisi_qm_suspend()` and `hisi_qm_resume()` acquire `down_write(&qm->qps_lock)` via `hisi_qm_stop()` and `hisi_qm_start()`. [F21, F135]
- `qm_stop_qp_nolock()` sets `QP_STOP`, calls `qm_drain_qp()` and `flush_workqueue(qm->wq)`, and runs `qp_stop_fail_cb()` while holding `qm->qps_lock` (`down_write`); thus `qm_work_process()`, `qp->req_cb`, and `qp->event_cb` must never acquire `qm->qps_lock`, and `qp_stop_fail_cb()` must never run before `QP_STOP` is set and `qm->wq` is flushed. [F134, F138]
- `qm->ops->hw_error_handle` (`qm_hw_error_handle_v2()`) and `qm->err_ini->get_err_result` run in hardirq `qm_abnormal_irq()`, and `qm->ops->qm_db` (`qm_db_v1()`, `qm_db_v2()`) runs in hardirq `qm_eq_irq()` and BH-disabled spinlock contexts in `hisi_qp_send()`; none may sleep or take sleeping locks (`mailbox_lock`, `qps_lock`). [F131, F132]
- Reset flows and QoS mailbox/VFT register accesses (`qm_algqos_read()`, `qm_algqos_write()`) must acquire `QM_RESETTING` in `misc_ctl` (`qm_reset_prepare_ready(qm)` sets it on `pf_qm->misc_ctl` when `qm->ver < QM_HW_V3` and on `qm->misc_ctl` otherwise) and release it on all paths via `qm_reset_bit_clear(qm)` (or `clear_bit()`). [F13, F25]
- In `__hisi_qm_start()` / `hisi_qm_start()`, EQ/AEQ status, zeroed `qm->qdma.va`, and EQC/AEQC/SQC_BT/CQC_BT contexts must be configured before `qm_enable_eq_aeq_interrupts()` and before setting `QM_WORK` under `qm->qps_lock`; in `hisi_qm_stop()`, `qm->status.stop_reason` must be set to `r` under `qm->qps_lock` and reset to `QM_NORMAL` after `qm_invalid_queues()`. [F28, F51]
- When stopping user-space QPs on error/reset in `qm_disable_qp()` and `hisi_qm_stop()`, `qm_set_qp_disable()` (which includes `smp_wmb()`) must be called with `QM_RESET_STOP_TX_OFFSET` before stopping and `QM_RESET_STOP_RX_OFFSET` after stopping; when polling CQEs in `qm_poll_req_cb()` or `hisi_qm_is_q_updated()`, execute `dma_rmb()` after checking `QM_CQE_PHASE(cqe)` and before reading `cqe->sq_head` or advancing `cq_head`. [F29, F142]
- During PF/VF reset flows (`qm_controller_reset_prepare()`, `hisi_qm_reset_prepare()`, `qm_pf_reset_vf_process()`), `qm_cmd_uninit(qm)` must mask `QM_IFC_INT_MASK` before polling IFC registers and `qm_cmd_init(qm)` must re-enable it upon completion. [F66]

## Object Lifetimes

- `hisi_qm_uninit()` and `hisi_qm_init()` error labels must tear down in LIFO order: `qm_cmd_uninit()`, `hisi_qm_unint_work()`, `hisi_qm_memory_uninit()` (calling `hisi_qm_cache_wb()` on `ver != QM_HW_V1` before freeing `qdma.va`, as in `qm_invalid_queues()` on `QM_DOWN`), `qm_remove_uacce()`, `qm_irqs_unregister()` (freeing VF abnormal IRQ on `ver >= QM_HW_V3`), and `hisi_qm_pci_uninit()` (checking `QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION` before `iounmap(qm->db_io_base)`). [F14, F38, F115, F124]
- In `hisi_qm_wait_task_finish()`, `qm_frozen()` (setting `QM_DRIVER_REMOVING` under `qm->qps_lock`) must succeed before waiting for `QM_RST_SCHED`/`QM_RESETTING` and flushing `qm->cmd_process`; in `qm_stop_qp_nolock()`, if `qp->qp_status.flags != QP_START`, clear `qp->is_resetting = false` before returning so `qm_restart()` does not restart a released QP. [F9, F26]
- On PF, `qm_dev_mem_reset(qm)` must precede `hisi_qm_start()` / `qm_restart()` VFT setup during init, reset, and resume; in `qm_controller_reset_prepare()`, `qm_dev_ecc_mbit_handle(qm)` must precede stopping VFs and `hisi_qm_stop()`; across PF reset/FLR, child VFs must run `pci_save_state()` before reset and `pci_restore_state()` before restart; and in `hisi_qm_suspend()` / `hisi_qm_resume()`, `hisi_qm_stop()` must precede `qm_prepare_for_suspend()` and `qm_rebuild_for_resume()` must precede `hisi_qm_start()`. [F122, F125, F126, F130]
- Balance references on all paths: `pci_dev_put(pdev)` after `pci_get_device()` in `hisi_qm_q_num_set()`; `hisi_qm_put_dfx_access(qm)` after `hisi_qm_get_dfx_access(qm)`; `qm_pm_get_sync(qm)` held across `hisi_qm_sriov_enable()` until `hisi_qm_sriov_disable()` succeeds (or rolled back via `qm_clear_vft_config()` and `qm_pm_put_sync()`); and when `QM_SUPPORT_RPM` is set, balance `hisi_qm_pm_init()`'s `pm_runtime_put_noidle()` with `pm_runtime_get_noresume()` in `hisi_qm_pm_uninit()`. [F5, F6, F7, F8]

## API Contracts

- `hisi_qp_send()` and `hisi_acc_sg_buf_map_to_hw_sgl()` / `hisi_acc_sg_buf_unmap()` have no internal locking (with SGL pools requiring `index < pool->count`, `sg_n_mapped <= pool->sge_nr`, and total DMA blocks `<= HISI_ACC_MEM_BLOCK_NR`), so callers must externally serialize concurrent `hisi_qp_send()` calls on the same `hisi_qp` and never share an SGL `index` across concurrent requests. [F30, F78, F133]
- `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()` and `hisi_acc_sg_buf_map_to_hw_sgl()` return `ERR_PTR()` on failure and never `NULL` (callers must check `IS_ERR()`), whereas `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()` returns `u32` `0` on failure (callers like `qm_vf_get_qos()` must test `!qos`); `func_qos`/`alg_qos` use `100 Mbps` units while `qm_get_shaper_para()`, `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()`, and `qm->mb_qos` use `1 Mbps` (`* QM_QOS_RATE`). [F31, F33, F47]
- In `qm_get_xqc_depth(qm, low_bits, high_bits, type)`, `*low_bits` gets `eq_depth`/`sq_depth` and `*high_bits` gets `aeq_depth`/`cq_depth`; on `QM_HW_V3`+ (`is_read` true), `hisi_qm_get_hw_info()` shifts right by `shift` before masking with `mask` (requiring a post-shift mask); and in `qm->ops->get_ifc`, `data` is optional (`NULL`), so implementations must check `if (data)` and callers must check `ret` before reading `*cmd`/`*data`. [F42, F45, F48]
- Client drivers must keep `struct hisi_qm` at offset 0 of `pci_get_drvdata(pdev)`; `qm_mb_write()` requires a 16-byte `struct qm_mailbox`; `hisi_qm_get_dfx_access()` must fail with `-EAGAIN` if `pm_runtime_suspended()`; `hisi_qm_set_algs()` requires `ARRAY_SIZE(dev_algs)`, `'\\n'`-terminated `alg` strings, and total length `< QM_DEV_ALG_MAX_LEN`; and `hisi_qm_alg_register()` / `unregister()` must both skip crypto callbacks when `qm->ver <= QM_HW_V2 && qm->use_sva` or `qm->qp_num < guard`. [F107, F108, F112, F129, F148]

## Error Handling

- `hisi_qm_resume()` must return `ret` when `qm_rebuild_for_resume()` fails or when `hisi_qm_start()` fails without a hardware error, returning `0` on `hisi_qm_start()` failure only when `qm_check_dev_error(qm)` is true; because `qm_alloc_uacce()` failure in `hisi_qm_init()` is non-fatal, callers must check `qm->uacce` (in `hisi_qm_set_algs()`) or `qm->use_sva` (in `qm_remove_uacce()`). [F34, F35]
- VFT error rollback in `qm_set_sqc_cqc_vft()` and `qm_vf_q_assign()` must not overwrite `ret`; `qm_get_qos_value()` and `qm_algqos_write()` must check `ret` before reading `*val`/`fun_index`; `qm_clear_device()` must write `0x0` to `ACC_MASTER_GLOBAL_CTRL` if `qm_master_ooo_check()` or `set_priv_status()` fails; and `qm_hw_error_handle_v2()` on `reset_mask` match must mask active bits in `QM_RAS_NFE_ENABLE` and return `ACC_ERR_NEED_RESET` without clearing `QM_ABNORMAL_INT_SOURCE`. [F40, F41, F118, F123]

## External Contracts

- `hisi_qp_alloc_memory()` and `qm_alloc_uacce()` must compute `UACCE_QFRT_DUS` size identically as `PAGE_ALIGN(qm->sqe_size * sq_depth + sizeof(struct qm_cqe) * cq_depth) + PAGE_SIZE`, and `qm_alloc_uacce()` and `hisi_qm_uacce_mmap()` must use the same three-way branch on `QM_HW_V1` and `QM_SUPPORT_DB_ISOLATION` for `UACCE_QFRT_MMIO`; in `hisi_qm_uacce_ioctl()`, `UACCE_CMD_QM_SET_QP_CTX` must reject `qc_type > QM_MAX_QC_TYPE` and `UACCE_CMD_QM_SET_QP_INFO` must `copy_from_user()` before updating `qp_info` and copying back. [F76, F102, F146]

## Known Pitfalls

- Loops over `qm_list->list` matching `pci_physfn(dev) == pdev` (`qm_try_frozen_vfs()`, `qm_vf_reset_prepare()`, `qm_vf_reset_done()`) must skip `dev == pdev` first; in `hisi_qm_reset_prepare()` and `hisi_qm_reset_done()`, pass `pf_qm` to `hisi_qm_dev_err_uninit()` / `hisi_qm_dev_err_init()` and `qm` to `hisi_qm_stop()`, `qm_restart()`, `qm_reset_prepare_ready()`, and `qm_reset_bit_clear()`. [F84, F89]
- Cast/scale untyped pointers accurately: cast `block[block_index].sgl` to `void *` before adding `pool->sgl_size * offset` in `acc_get_sgl()`; cast `qp->qdma.va + qp->qdma.size` to `u32 *` / `u64 *` before subtracting `QM_RESET_STOP_TX_OFFSET` / `RX_OFFSET` in `qm_set_qp_disable()` and `hisi_qm_unset_hw_reset()`; and multiply `qp->sqe` indices by `qm->sqe_size` while keeping `struct qm_cqe *qp->cqe` indices unscaled. [F100, F103, F104]
- Avoid 32-bit shift/underflow bugs: cap `PAGE_SHIFT + MAX_PAGE_ORDER` at `31` in `hisi_acc_create_sgl_pool()`; cast doorbell operands (`index`, `priority`, `qn`) to `u64` in `qm_db_v1()`/`v2()`; cast upper 32-bit register values (`QM_MB_CMD_DATA_ADDR_H`, `QM_VFT_CFG_DATA_H`) and `u32 data` in `qm_set_ifc_begin_v3()`/`v4()` to `u64` before `<< 32`; and compute `cur_head` as `(cur_tail + sq_depth - qp_used) % sq_depth` in `qp_stop_fail_cb()`. [F90, F92, F94, F98]
- In `hisi_qm_uacce_mmap()` (`UACCE_QFRT_DUS`), save and zero `vma->vm_pgoff` around `dma_mmap_coherent()`; in `qm_algqos_write()`, ensure `buf` is NUL-terminated with `count < QM_DBG_READ_LEN` before calling `qm_get_qos_value()`; and format `size_t` with `%zx` and `dma_addr_t` with `%pad` when logging DMA buffers. [F109, F110, F140]

## Other

- When reading or writing VFT registers (`qm_set_vft_common()` and `qm_get_shaper_vft_qos()`), poll `QM_VFT_CFG_RDY` for `BIT(0)` before programming `QM_VFT_CFG_*`, clear `QM_VFT_CFG_RDY` to `0x0`, write `0x1` to `QM_VFT_CFG_OP_ENABLE`, and poll `QM_VFT_CFG_RDY` for `BIT(0)` again. [F121]
