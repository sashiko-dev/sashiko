# ALPS PS/2 TOUCHPAD DRIVER

The ALPS PS/2 Touchpad Driver (`alps.c` and `alps.h`) is a `psmouse` protocol subdriver in the Linux input subsystem that detects, initializes, and processes input packets for ALPS GlidePoint and DualPoint touchpads across protocol versions V1 through V8. It manages primary touchpad input reporting (single-touch, semi-multitouch, and full multitouch), secondary trackstick input devices (`dev2`), and on-demand registration of pass-through or interleaved bare PS/2 mice (`dev3`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` and `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `priv->f` must be zeroed with `memset()` before decoding the current packet, and `priv->decode_fields()` must only write fields belonging to the packet's type so re-decoding `priv->multi_data` preserves the companion packet's fields. In `alps_decode_ss4_v2()`, `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI` must only write `f->mt[2..3]`, and `SS4_PACKET_ID_TWO` with `SS4_IS_MF_CONTINUE(p)` set must only write `f->mt[0..1]`, `f->pressure`, `f->first_mp = 1`, and `f->is_mp = 0` without modifying `f->fingers`. [F20, F28]
- In `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` and `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, when `priv->multi_packet` is non-zero and `f->is_mp` is true, `priv->multi_packet` must remain 1 until after `if (!priv->multi_packet && f->first_mp)`, whereas when `f->is_mp` is false, `priv->multi_packet` must be cleared to 0 before that check. [F29]
- `struct alps_data::multi_data` is 6 bytes, so `alps_process_packet_v4()` must verify `priv->multi_packet <= 2` before writing `packet[6]` and `packet[7]` at offset `2 * priv->multi_packet`, and 6-byte multi-packet handlers must copy `sizeof(priv->multi_data)` rather than `sizeof(psmouse->packet)` (8 bytes). [F42]
- In `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` under `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI`, `no_data_x` and `no_data_y` must be selected in lockstep with `priv->flags & ALPS_BUTTONPAD` and `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV(priv->dev_id)` (`SS4_PLUS_MFPACKET_NO_AX*`, `SS4_MFPACKET_NO_AX*`, `SS4_MFPACKET_NO_AY*`) to match the bit shifts applied to `f->mt[3]`. [F27]
- In `alps_report_mt_data()`, `n` bounds the loop over `slot[MAX_TOUCHES]` and `f->mt[MAX_TOUCHES]` (where `MAX_TOUCHES` is 4), so callers (`alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v7()`) must cap `n` at `MAX_TOUCHES` via `alps_get_mt_count(f->mt)` or an explicit `<= 4` clamp even when `f->fingers` reports 5 to 8 touches to `input_mt_report_finger_count()`. [F39]
- In `alps_process_byte()`, bare PS/2 packet detection on `(psmouse->packet[0] & 0xc8) == 0x08` must be guarded by `priv->proto_version != ALPS_PROTO_V8 && !psmouse->out_of_sync_cnt` because V8's first byte (`0x18`) matches `(byte & 0xc8) == 0x08` and bare PS/2 packets cannot self-synchronize when out of sync. [F50]
- In `alps_process_byte()`, the `ALPS_PS2_INTERLEAVED` check (`psmouse->pktcnt >= 4 && (psmouse->packet[3] & 0x0f) == 0x0f`, only valid on 6-byte protocols where `pktsize == 6`) must run before the `priv->proto_version < ALPS_PROTO_V5` high-bit (`0x80`) check. `alps_handle_interleaved_ps2()` must return `PSMOUSE_GOOD_DATA` at `pktcnt == 6` to receive a 7th byte into `psmouse->packet[6]`, and must mask `psmouse->packet[6]` with `0xf7` when shifting it to `psmouse->packet[3]` before resetting `psmouse->pktcnt` to 1 or 4. [F43, F51]
- In `alps_get_packet_id_v7()`, `V7_PACKET_ID_TWO`, `V7_PACKET_ID_MULTI`, and `V7_PACKET_ID_NEW` must be checked before `V7_PACKET_ID_IDLE` (`byte[1] == 0x00 && byte[4] == 0x00`), and `alps_decode_packet_v7()` must return `0` for `V7_PACKET_ID_IDLE` and non-zero for `V7_PACKET_ID_NEW` and `V7_PACKET_ID_UNKNOWN`. [F53]
- Once `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()` sets `ALPS_QUIRK_TRACKSTICK_BUTTONS` in `priv->quirks`, `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v3_v5()` must check `!(priv->quirks & ALPS_QUIRK_TRACKSTICK_BUTTONS)` and skip reporting `f->ts_left`, `f->ts_right`, and `f->ts_middle` on `priv->dev2` so touchpad packets do not clear trackstick button presses. [F32]
- `priv->dev2` is only allocated and registered in `alps_init()` when `priv->flags & ALPS_DUALPOINT` is set, so packet handlers must check `priv->flags & ALPS_DUALPOINT` (or `dev2 != NULL` in `alps_report_buttons()` and `alps_disconnect()`) before reporting events on or unregistering `priv->dev2`. [F17]
- Static protocol descriptors (`alps_model_data` and `alps_v*_protocol_data`) are `const`; `alps_set_protocol()` copies `protocol->flags` into `priv->flags` and dynamically clears `ALPS_DUALPOINT` on V3, Rushmore V3, and V7 when `alps_probe_trackstick_v3_v7()` returns `< 0`. [F71]
- In `alps_init()`, `priv->hw_init(psmouse)` must execute before `priv->set_abs_params(priv, dev1)` because `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3()` and `alps_hw_init_v7()` populate `priv->x_res` and `priv->y_res` via `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()`, which `alps_set_abs_params_mt_common()` then applies to `dev1`. [F36]
- In `alps_identify()`, the `ec`-qualified branches (including the `ec[0] == 0x88` checks for `alps_v7_protocol_data`, `alps_v3_rushmore_data`, and `alps_v3_protocol_data`) must be evaluated before the `e7`-only check for `alps_v8_protocol_data` (`e7[0] == 0x73 && e7[1] == 0x03 && (e7[2] == 0x14 || e7[2] == 0x28)`). [F48]
- In `alps_identify()` and `alps_match_table()`, the E7 and EC report buffers are 4-byte stack arrays (`unsigned char e7[4], ec[4]`), whereas `struct alps_model_info::signature`, `priv->dev_id`, and `priv->fw_ver` are 3-byte arrays (`u8[3]`), so comparisons and copies must use the 3-byte destination size rather than `sizeof(e7)` or `sizeof(ec)`. [F64]
- In `alps_get_bitmap_points()`, the target `struct alps_bitmap_point *point` starts at `low` and switches to `high` on the first `1 -> 0` transition (`!bit && prev_bit`) while incrementing `*fingers` on every `0 -> 1` transition, so callers must zero-initialize `*fingers`, `*low`, and `*high` before each call. [F54]
- In `alps_process_bitmap()`, `priv->second_touch` is selected only when `priv->second_touch == -1`, and `alps_report_semi_mt_data()` must reset `priv->second_touch = -1` whenever `fingers < 2` so that the opposite bounding-box corner for `fields->mt[1]` is locked for the duration of a two-finger touch sequence. [F55]
- In `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()` and `alps_update_device_area_ss4_v2()`, fixed base offsets on 4-bit pitch and electrode fields keep `x_pitch`, `y_pitch`, `x_electrode - 1`, and `y_electrode - 1` strictly positive across all register values, guaranteeing non-zero `x_phys` and `y_phys` divisors. Broken by: reducing base offsets or widening signed nibble masks without checking `x_phys > 0` and `y_phys > 0` before division. [F61]
- `alps_dmi_has_separate_stick_buttons` must remain non-`__initconst` and end with an unconditional empty `{ }` sentinel outside `#if defined(CONFIG_DMI) && defined(CONFIG_X86)`, because `alps_set_protocol()` passes it to `dmi_check_system()` during both `alps_detect()` and runtime `alps_reconnect()`. [F76]

## Synchronization

- In `alps_init()` and `alps_set_protocol()`, `priv->timer`, `priv->psmouse`, `priv->dev3_register_work`, `priv->process_packet`, and `priv->dev2` (when `ALPS_DUALPOINT` is set) must be initialized before `psmouse->protocol_handler` is set to `alps_process_byte`. [F8]
- `alps_flush_packet()` must acquire `serio_pause_rx(psmouse->ps2dev.serio)` and re-check that `psmouse->pktcnt == psmouse->pktsize` under that guard before reading `psmouse->packet`, calling `priv->process_packet(psmouse)`, and resetting `psmouse->pktcnt = 0`. [F10]
- `alps_disconnect()` must call `timer_shutdown_sync(&priv->timer)` before unregistering `priv->dev2` or `priv->dev3` and before freeing `priv`, and `alps_flush_packet()` must not acquire `psmouse_mutex` (which is held by `psmouse_disconnect` across `alps_disconnect`). [F84]
- In `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()`, `dev3` must be fully configured and registered via `input_register_device(dev3)` before being assigned to `priv->dev3`. [F9]

## Object Lifetimes

- Secondary input devices `priv->dev2` (in `alps_init()`) and `priv->dev3` (in `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()`) must be freed with `input_free_device()` if `input_register_device()` fails, and once registered must only be released via `input_unregister_device()` in `alps_disconnect()`. [F3]
- Secondary input devices `dev2` and `dev3` must have their `phys` strings formatted via `scnprintf()` into the persistent `priv->phys2` and `priv->phys3` buffers in `struct alps_data` before assigning `dev2->phys` and `dev3->phys`, never into stack-allocated buffers in `alps_init()` or `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()`. [F65]

## API Contracts

- `alps_absolute_mode_v3()`, `alps_absolute_mode_v4()`, and `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()` require the touchpad to already be in command mode and do not exit command mode themselves; their callers (`alps_hw_init_v3`, `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3`, `alps_hw_init_v4`, `alps_hw_init_v7`) must call `alps_exit_command_mode()` on both normal and error paths. [F6]
- `alps_trackstick_enter_extended_mode_v3_v6()` requires the touchpad to be in passthrough mode, and callers that enable passthrough mode (`alps_poll`, `alps_hw_init_v6`, `alps_setup_trackstick_v3`) or monitor mode (`alps_absolute_mode_v6`) must disable that mode (`alps_passthrough_mode_v2/v3(..., false)` or `alps_monitor_mode(psmouse, false)`) before returning even when the inner PS/2 operation fails. [F7]
- `__alps_command_mode_write_reg()` only transmits the two data nibbles and requires the target register address to have already been latched in the current command-mode session by an immediately preceding `alps_command_mode_read_reg()` or `alps_command_mode_set_addr()`; otherwise `alps_command_mode_write_reg()` must be used. [F34]
- `alps_command_mode_send_nibble()` requires `nibble <= 0xf` to index the 16-entry `priv->nibble_commands` table, and whenever `command & 0x0f00` is non-zero it must pass the 4-byte stack `dummy` buffer to `ps2_command()` instead of `&priv->nibble_commands[nibble].data`. [F41]
- On V8 (`ALPS_PROTO_V8`) devices, `alps_update_device_area_ss4_v2()`, `alps_update_btn_info_ss4_v2()`, `alps_update_dual_info_ss4_v2()`, and `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` must branch on `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV(priv->dev_id)` (`73 03 28`) to select SS4+ OTP bit offsets, electrode constants, and coordinate macros. [F73]
- In `alps_update_dual_info_ss4_v2()`, when an `IS_SS4PLUS_DEV(priv->dev_id)` touchpad does not have the OTP dualpoint bit (`(otp[0][0] >> 4) & 0x01`) set, the driver must fall back to reading command-mode register `0xD7` (matching `0x0C` or `0x1D`) and then call `alps_exit_command_mode()` and `PSMOUSE_CMD_ENABLE`. [F74]
- When `alps_detect()` is called with `set_properties == false`, or when `alps_init()` fails after `alps_detect()` succeeded with `set_properties == true`, the driver must `kfree()` the `struct alps_data` stored in `psmouse->private` and reset `psmouse->private = NULL`. [F85]
- `alps_identify()` accepts a nullable `priv` pointer (passed as `NULL` during the pre-reset probe in `alps_detect()`), so `alps_identify()` must guard all writes to `priv` and the call to `alps_set_protocol()` with `if (priv)`. [F18]
- In `alps_detect()`, after `alps_identify(psmouse, NULL)` succeeds, the driver must check `alps_is_cs19_trackpoint(psmouse)` and return `-EINVAL` if true so that ALPS CS19 trackpoint-only devices are handed off to the trackpoint or bare PS/2 protocol driver. [F93]
- In `alps_report_buttons(dev1, dev2, left, right, middle)`, each shared button is routed to `dev2` if `dev2` already has that button active in `dev2->key` and otherwise to `dev1`, and because `alps_report_buttons()` only calls `input_sync(dev2)`, callers must pass the packet's primary device as `dev1` and call `input_sync(dev1)` afterwards. [F56]
- In `alps_init()`, the primary touchpad device `psmouse->dev` (`dev1`) must have `EV_REL`, `REL_X`, and `REL_Y` cleared before setting `EV_ABS` capabilities, only re-enabling `EV_REL` and `REL_WHEEL` if `priv->flags & ALPS_WHEEL` is set, and must clear `BTN_RIGHT` when `priv->flags & ALPS_BUTTONPAD` is set. [F86]
- Touchpad axis resolutions `priv->x_res` and `priv->y_res` passed to `input_abs_set_res()` in `alps_set_abs_params_mt_common()` must be converted to units per millimeter (`priv->x_max * 10 / x_phys` and `priv->y_max * 10 / y_phys` when `x_phys` and `y_phys` are computed in `0.1 mm` units). [F89]

## Error Handling

- `alps_command_mode_read_reg()` and `__alps_command_mode_read_reg()` return a non-negative 8-bit register value (`0x00`–`0xff`) on success and `-1` on failure, so callers must store the result in a signed `int` and test `< 0` or `== -1` rather than boolean truthiness or storing into `u8`. [F13]
- `priv->dev3` can hold `NULL` (before registration), an `ERR_PTR` (set by `alps_register_bare_ps2_mouse()` when `alps_do_register_bare_ps2_mouse()` fails), or a valid `struct input_dev *`, so all consumers must test `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(priv->dev3)` before dereferencing or unregistering it and `!IS_ERR(priv->dev3)` before queueing `dev3_register_work`. [F16]
- `alps_decode_packet_v7()` returns `0` for reportable packets, `1` for `V7_PACKET_ID_NEW` packets that must be ignored, and `-1` for `V7_PACKET_ID_UNKNOWN`, so `alps_process_touchpad_packet_v7()` must abort reporting on any non-zero return from `priv->decode_fields()` rather than testing `< 0`. [F15]
- `alps_setup_trackstick_v3()` returns `-ENODEV` when the trackstick E7 report fails and `-EIO` on fatal bus or command-mode failures, and its callers (`alps_hw_init_v3()` and `alps_hw_init_rushmore_v3()`) must only abort touchpad initialization when the return value is `-EIO`. [F14]
- On `ALPS_PROTO_V3_RUSHMORE`, when the final byte of a packet (`psmouse->pktcnt == psmouse->pktsize`) has bit 7 set (`0x80`), `alps_process_byte()` must drop the packet and return `PSMOUSE_FULL_PACKET` rather than `PSMOUSE_BAD_DATA`. [F91]
- In the packet receive path, `WARN_ON_ONCE()` is reserved for internal state invariant violations (`priv->multi_packet > 2` in `alps_process_packet_v4()`), whereas malformed wire packets must be logged with `psmouse_dbg()` (or `psmouse_warn()` when rejecting trackstick packets on `!(priv->flags & ALPS_DUALPOINT)` before touching `priv->dev2`). [F92]

## External Contracts

- All ALPS trackstick packet handlers (`SS4_TS_Y_V2` in `alps_process_packet_ss4_v2()`, `alps_process_packet_v1_v2()`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, `alps_process_packet_v6()`, and `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v7()`) must negate the sign-extended hardware Y delta when reporting `REL_Y` on `priv->dev2`. [F22]

## Known Pitfalls

- The `SS4_*_MF_*_V2(_b, _i)` coordinate and Z macros index `_b[0 + (_i) * 3]` through `_b[2 + (_i) * 3]` within a 6-byte packet, so `alps_decode_ss4_v2()` must pass packet-local indices `_i` of 0 and 1 even when filling `f->mt[2]` and `f->mt[3]` for `SS4_PACKET_ID_MULTI`. [F40]
- In `struct alps_fields`, the boolean flags (`first_mp`, `is_mp`, `left`, `right`, `middle`, `ts_left`, `ts_right`, `ts_middle`) are 1-bit `unsigned int` bitfields rather than `bool`, so bit-masked expressions testing non-LSB bits must be normalized with `!!` or shifted to bit 0 before assignment. [F58]
- Sign-extending 8-bit trackstick motion bytes or 4-bit register nibbles (in `SS4_TS_X_V2`, `SS4_TS_Y_V2`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, `alps_process_packet_v6()`, `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v7()`, and `alps_get_v3_v7_resolution()`) requires an explicit `(s8)` cast rather than `(char)`. [F59]
- When assembling the 35-bit `palm_data` bitmap in `alps_decode_dolphin()`, packet bytes whose shifted bits reach or exceed bit 31 (`p[3] & 0x70` shifted by 27 and `p[0] & 0x01` shifted by 34) must be widened to `u64` before shifting. [F60]
- When testing whether a hardware report byte falls within a 16-value hex range (such as `param[1]` in `0x20..0x2f` in `alps_is_cs19_trackpoint()` or `id[1]`/`ec[1]` in `0xb0..0xbf` and `0xc0..0xcf` in `alps_check_valid_firmware_id()` and `alps_identify()`), the full upper nibble must be masked with `0xf0` before comparing for equality. [F46]
- In `alps_process_trackstick_packet_v3()`, extracting the 7-bit trackstick pressure `z` from `packet[4]` (and the low 7 bits of `x` and `y` from `packet[1]` and `packet[2]`) must mask all 7 lower bits with `0x7f`. [F47]
