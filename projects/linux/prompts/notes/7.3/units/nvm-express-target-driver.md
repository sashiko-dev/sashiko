# NVM EXPRESS TARGET DRIVER

The Linux NVMe Target (`nvmet`) subsystem implements target-side NVMe subsystems, controllers, queues, namespaces, Asymmetric Namespace Access (ANA), discovery, persistent reservations (PR), and Zoned Namespaces (ZNS) configured via configfs and debugfs, executing Admin, Fabrics, and I/O commands over block devices, files, or passthrough NVMe controllers across loopback (`nvme-loop`), PCI Endpoint (`nvmet-pci-epf`), RDMA (`nvmet-rdma`), and TCP/TLS (`nvmet-tcp`) transports.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- Before Fabrics Connect installs a queue, `req->sq->ctrl` (`queue->nvme_sq.ctrl`) is `NULL` and `req->sq->size` is `0`: code in `nvmet_req_init()`, `nvmet_req_transfer_len()`, `nvmet_set_error()`, `nvmet_rdma_error_comp()`, `nvmet_tcp_fatal_error()`, and `trace.h` helpers (`nvmet_req_to_ctrl_id()`, `__assign_req_name()`) must check `req->sq`, `req->sq->ctrl`, and `req->ns` for `NULL`, and `nvmet_update_sq_head()` must check `if (req->sq->size)` before modulo division. [F50, F127, F242, F347]
- In `nvmet_parse_admin_cmd()` and `nvmet_admin_cmd_data_len()`, `nvme_is_fabrics(cmd)` and `nvmet_is_disc_subsys()` must precede `nvmet_check_ctrl_status()` and `nvmet_is_passthru_req()`; in `nvmet_parse_io_cmd()`, `nvme_is_fabrics(cmd)` must precede `nvmet_check_auth_status()`, and `nvmet_is_passthru_req()` must precede `nvmet_req_find_ns()`; in `trace.h`, `parse_nvme_cmd()` must test `opcode == nvme_fabrics_command` before branching on `qid`. [F101, F102, F281]
- In `struct nvmet_ns`, `ns->bdev` is `NULL` when file-backed or `ns->buffered_io` is set (`ns->file != NULL`), so shared helpers must check `ns->bdev` before dereferencing it or `nvmet_ns_dev(ns)`. File-backed I/O handlers (`nvmet_file_execute_io()`, `nvmet_file_execute_discard()`, `nvmet_file_write_zeroes_work()`) must reject `pos + len > req->ns->size` with `-ENOSPC`, and every non-PR I/O opcode must be in `nvmet_is_req_write/read_cmd_group()` in `pr.c`. [F48, F80, F87]
- In `rdma.c`, `cm_id->context` always points to `struct nvmet_rdma_port`, while `struct nvmet_rdma_queue *` is in `cm_id->qp->qp_context`; in `nvmet_rdma_cm_handler()`, `RDMA_CM_EVENT_ADDR_CHANGE` must check `if (!queue)` to schedule `port->repair_work` and `break` before falling through to `nvmet_rdma_queue_disconnect(queue)`. When `nvmet_rdma_read/write_data_done()` refunds `rsp->n_rdma` and calls `nvmet_rdma_rw_ctx_destroy(rsp)`, it must reset `rsp->n_rdma = 0` before `nvmet_rdma_release_rsp(rsp)`. [F202, F288, F313]
- In `tcp.c`, `nvmet_tcp_handle_h2c_data_pdu()` must subtract `sizeof(*data)`, `nvmet_tcp_hdgst_len(queue)`, and `nvmet_tcp_ddgst_len(queue)` from `le32_to_cpu(data->hdr.plen)` and verify `cmd->pdu_len` is non-zero, equals `exp_data_len`, is `<= NVMET_TCP_MAXH2CDATA`, and `le32_to_cpu(data->data_offset) == cmd->rbytes_done` before `nvmet_tcp_build_pdu_iovec()`. `nvmet_tcp_try_send_one()` and `nvmet_tcp_try_recv_one()` must keep sequential `if` statements (not `else if` or `switch`). [F262, F263, F295]

## Synchronization
- Lock hierarchy is `nvmet_config_sem` -> `subsys->lock` -> `ctrl->lock` (and `ns->pr.pr_sem` -> `subsys->lock` -> `ctrl->pr_log_mgr.lock` / `ctrl->lock`); never acquire `subsys->lock` under `ctrl->lock` or `nvmet_config_sem` under `subsys->lock`. `nvmet_ns_changed()` requires holding `subsys->lock`. In `nvmet_async_events_process()` and `nvmet_async_events_failall()`, `ctrl->lock` must be dropped before `nvmet_req_complete()` and re-acquired before the next loop iteration. [F35, F36, F345]
- `nvmet_config_sem` protects `nvmet_transports`, `nvmet_genctr`, `nvmet_disc_subsys->subsysnqn`, `port->subsystems`, `port->referrals`, `subsys->hosts`, and `subsys->allow_any_host`; writers must hold `down_write(&nvmet_config_sem)` and call `nvmet_port/subsys_disc_changed()`. In `nvmet_enable_port()`, `trtype == NVMF_TRTYPE_MAX` must be rejected before indexing `nvmet_transports[]`, and if the slot is `NULL`, `nvmet_config_sem` must be dropped before `request_module()` and re-checked under write lock. [F29, F38, F70, F81]
- `nvmet_ns_disable()` must drop `subsys->lock` before `percpu_ref_kill(&ns->ref)`, `synchronize_rcu()`, and `wait_for_completion(&ns->disable_done)`, and configfs callers of `nvmet_ns_enable()` / `nvmet_ns_disable()` must hold `down_write(&nvmet_config_sem)`. `nvmet_ana_sem` protects `nvmet_ana_group_enabled[]`, `nvmet_ana_chgcnt`, `port->ana_state[]`, and `ns->anagrpid` (updated under `down_write(&nvmet_ana_sem)` with `nvmet_ana_chgcnt++` and ANA AEN). [F31, F69, F171]
- `nvmet_req_init()` (including `nvmet_parse_io_cmd()`, `nvmet_req_find_ns()`, `nvmet_pr_check_cmd_access()`, `nvmet_pr_get_ns_pc_ref()`) runs under `rcu_read_lock()` from `nvme_loop_queue_rq()`, and `nvmet_req_complete()` / `__nvmet_req_complete()` / `queue_response` run in IRQ/softirq or RCU context and must not sleep. `ctrl->error_lock` (protecting `ctrl->err_counter` and `ctrl->slots`) and `cq->lock` in `pci-epf.c` must always use `spin_lock_irqsave()`. [F162, F163, F167, F168]
- In `pr.c`, `pr->holder` and `pr->registrant_list` are read under `rcu_read_lock()`, so mutations under `ns->pr.pr_sem` must use `rcu_assign_pointer()`, `list_add_tail_rcu()`, `list_del_rcu()`, `list_replace_rcu()`, and `kfree_rcu()`. For `NVME_PR_ACQUIRE_ACT_PREEMPT_AND_ABORT`, `nvmet_execute_pr_acquire()` must keep `pr_sem` held across queueing `req->r.abort_work` until `nvmet_pr_do_abort()` drains `pc_ref->free_done`, reinitializes `confirm_done` and `free_done`, resurrects `pc_ref->ref`, and releases `pr_sem`. [F33, F73, F173]
- Controller works (`ctrl->ka_work`, `ctrl->async_event_work`, `ctrl->fatal_err_work`) and passthrough commands with `req->p.use_workqueue` or effects beyond `CSUPP | LBCC` must run on `nvmet_wq` (`WQ_MEM_RECLAIM`), and ZNS zone management recv/send commands must run on `zbd_wq` (`WQ_MEM_RECLAIM`, using `GFP_NOIO` for `d.zbitmap`). [F166, F191, F344]
- In `pci-epf.c`, `NVMET_PCI_EPF_Q_LIVE` must only be tested on entry and set via `set_bit()` after all queue fields, PCI mappings, `nvmet_cq_create()`/`nvmet_sq_create()`, and `sq->iod_wq` are initialized, and `nvmet_pci_epf_queue_response()` must only signal `complete(&iod->done)` for successful `DMA_TO_DEVICE` transfers so `nvmet_pci_epf_exec_iod_work()` runs `nvmet_pci_epf_transfer_iod_data()` in process context. [F19, F164]
- In `rdma.c`, `nvmet_rdma_recv_not_live()` and `nvmet_rdma_queue_established()` must acquire `spin_lock_irqsave(&queue->state_lock, flags)` and drop it before `nvmet_rdma_handle_command()`; `queue->rsp_wr_wait_list` must be protected by `queue->rsp_wr_wait_lock` (dropped before `nvmet_rdma_execute_command()`); and `nvmet_rdma_queue_list` removals must hold `nvmet_rdma_queue_mutex`, iterate with `list_for_each_entry_safe()`, and use `list_del_init()`. [F223, F231, F286, F330]
- In `tcp.c`, socket callbacks must hold `read_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock)` to read `sk->sk_user_data`, and updates to `sk_user_data` or `sk_*` callbacks must hold `write_lock_bh(&sk->sk_callback_lock)` (with `nvmet_tcp_set_queue_sock()` verifying `sk_state == TCP_ESTABLISHED` under the lock). `queue->state_lock` must use `spin_lock_bh()` nested inside `sk_callback_lock`, and `nvmet_tcp_release_queue()` runs under `state_lock` in BH context so it must defer sleeping teardown to `queue->release_work`. [F219, F220, F337, F338, F339]
- In `tcp.c`, `nvmet_prepare_receive_pdu()` must clear `WRITE_ONCE(queue->cmd, NULL)` before `smp_store_release(&queue->rcv_state, NVMET_TCP_RECV_PDU)`, pairing with `smp_load_acquire(&queue->rcv_state)` before `READ_ONCE(queue->cmd)` in `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()`. Cross-CPU completions in `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()` must use `llist_add()` on `queue->resp_list`, while `queue->resp_send_list` and `queue->free_list` are only modified from `queue->io_work`. [F341, F342]

## Object Lifetimes
- `nvmet_req_complete()` must save `req->sq` into a local variable before calling `__nvmet_req_complete(req, status)` and pass the saved pointer to `percpu_ref_put(&sq->ref)`, because `req->ops->queue_response(req)` may free `req`. In `nvmet_sq_destroy()`, if `ctrl->sqs[0] == sq`, `nvmet_async_events_failall(ctrl)` must run before killing `sq->ref` and waiting on `sq->confirm_done` and `sq->free_done`. [F3, F23]
- `nvmet_req_bio_put()` must call `bio_uninit(bio)` when `bio == &req->b.inline_bio` and `bio_put(bio)` otherwise, and `inline_bio` must remain the first member of union branches `req->b`, `req->p`, and `req->z`. In `io-cmd-file.c`, `req->f.bvec != req->inline_bvec` must be freed via `mempool_free()` when `req->f.mpool_alloc` is true (chunked at `NVMET_MAX_MPOOL_BVEC` in `nvmet_file_execute_io()`) and `kfree()` otherwise; `nvmet_file_ns_disable()` must `flush_workqueue(buffered_io_wq)` before destroying `ns->bvec_pool` and `fput(ns->file)`. [F7, F13, F25, F135, F141]
- `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()` does not take ownership of its buffer, so callers allocating temporary response buffers must `kfree()` them before `nvmet_req_complete()`. Dynamically allocated strings on `struct nvmet_subsys` (`subsysnqn`, `model_number`, `firmware_rev`) and `nvmet_disc_subsys->subsysnqn` must be updated by allocating a new string, swapping under lock, and `kfree()`ing the old one; `nvmet_host_release()` must `kfree()` both `host->dhchap_secret` and `host->dhchap_ctrl_secret`. [F16, F17, F133]
- In `pci-epf.c`, `nvmet_pci_epf_disable_ctrl()` must clear `ctrl->enabled` and `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&ctrl->poll_sqs)` before deleting I/O SQs, then I/O CQs, then admin SQ 0, then admin CQ 0; and `nvmet_pci_epf_delete_cq()` must cancel `cq->work`, drain `cq`, unmap `cq->pci_map`, and use `test_and_clear_bit(NVMET_PCI_EPF_Q_IRQ_ENABLED, &cq->flags)` before `nvmet_pci_epf_remove_irq_vector()`. [F66, F149, F150]
- In `rdma.c`, `nvmet_rdma_cm_handler()` callbacks must never call `rdma_destroy_id(queue->cm_id)` directly (deferring `nvmet_rdma_free_queue()` to `queue->release_work` on `nvmet_wq`, or setting `queue->cm_id = NULL` on accept failure); `nvmet_rdma_free_queue()` must call `ib_drain_qp(queue->qp)` before destroying `cm_id`/QP/CQ, freeing `cmds`/`rsps`, and calling `kref_put(&dev->ref, nvmet_rdma_free_dev)`; and `nvmet_rdma_remove_one()` must `flush_workqueue(nvmet_wq)`. [F201, F205, F227, F331]
- In `tcp.c`, when `nvmet_req_init()` fails on an inline-write command, `nvmet_tcp_queue_response()` and `nvmet_tcp_handle_req_failure()` must check `req->cmd->common.dptr.sgl.length` directly (not `nvmet_tcp_has_inline_data()`), map a receive buffer, and set `NVMET_TCP_F_INIT_FAILED` so `cmd` is queued only after draining inline data. Before `nvmet_tcp_install_queue()` succeeds, `queue->nr_cmds` must be `0` and `nvmet_tcp_put_cmd()` must not return `queue->connect` to `queue->free_list`. [F207, F212, F238, F239]
- In `tcp.c`, `nvmet_tcp_alloc_queue()` must initialize `tls_handshake_tmo_work`, `release_work`, and `io_work` unconditionally, and `nvmet_tcp_release_queue_work()` must run `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)`, then `nvmet_tcp_uninit_data_in_cmds()`, then `nvmet_sq_destroy()`, then a second `cancel_work_sync(&queue->io_work)` before freeing buffers/socket/cmds (with `nvmet_tcp_free_cmd_buffers()` nulling out `cmd->iov` and `cmd->req.sg`). [F208, F209, F217]

## API Contracts
- When `nvmet_req_init()` returns `false`, it has already called `__nvmet_req_complete()` without taking `sq->ref`, so callers must not call `nvmet_req_complete()` or `nvmet_req_uninit()`; failures after it returns `true` must call one of them. `nvmet_check_transfer_len()` and `nvmet_check_data_len_lte()` also call `nvmet_req_complete()` before returning `false`, so callers must invoke them before allocating per-command resources and return immediately on `false`. [F4, F42]
- Before `nvmet_req_alloc_sgls(req)`, `req->transfer_len` must include `req->metadata_len`. `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()` and `nvmet_copy_from_sgl()` require a kernel virtual address (`buf`) and must never be passed a `struct page *` like `ZERO_PAGE(0)` (use `nvmet_zero_sgl()` instead). `nvmet_bdev_ns_enable()` must return `-ENOTBLK` when `ns->buffered_io` is set or `bdev_file_open_by_path()` rejects a regular file so `nvmet_ns_enable()` falls back to `nvmet_file_ns_enable()`. [F46, F176, F184]
- Internal `u16 status` values are unshifted, so writing `cqe->status` or error log slots must shift left by 1 (`status << 1`) and reading `cqe->status` (`nvmet_pci_epf_queue_response()`) must shift right by 1. In `rdma.c`, `nvmet_rdma_rw_ctx_init()` returns a negative errno or positive WR count (stored in a signed `int`, checking `ret < 0`), and `cmd->sge[0]` and `rsp->send_sge` require `ib_dma_sync_single_for_cpu/device()`. [F57, F244, F334]

## Error Handling
- In `nvmet_req_init()`, `req->cqe->result.u64`, `req->cqe->status`, `req->cqe->sq_head`, `req->ns`, `req->pc_ref`, `req->error_loc` (`NVMET_NO_ERROR_LOC`), and `req->error_slba` must be initialized before any `goto fail`. In `nvmet_req_find_ns()`, if `ns->enabled` is `false`, `req->ns` must be reset to `NULL` before returning `NVME_SC_INTERNAL_PATH_ERROR`. When `bdev_file_open_by_path()`, `filp_open()`, or `key_lookup()` returns `IS_ERR()`, `ns->bdev_file`, `ns->file`, or `port->keyring` must be reset to `NULL`. [F51, F52, F54]
- In `nvmet_set_feat_write_protect()`, if `nvmet_write_protect_flush_sync()` fails after setting `req->ns->readonly = true`, `req->ns->readonly` must be restored to `false` without calling `nvmet_ns_changed()`. `errno_to_nvme_status()` and `blk_to_nvme_status()` must map `-EOPNOTSUPP` / `BLK_STS_NOTSUPP` to `NVME_SC_INVALID_OPCODE | NVME_STATUS_DNR` without per-opcode overrides, while `nvmet_bdev_discard_range()` and `nvmet_file_execute_discard()` must ignore `-EOPNOTSUPP` and continue. [F79, F107, F108]

## External Contracts
- All admin/discovery response structures copied to host SGLs must be zero-initialized before `nvmet_copy_to_sgl()`, and `sn`, `mn`, `fr` in `nvme_id_ctrl` and `subsys->serial` (20-byte non-NUL-terminated array, formatted with `%.*s` and `NVMET_SN_MAX_SIZE`) must be space-padded with `memcpy_and_pad()`. `nvmet_execute_identify_ns()` must clear `status = 0` and return a zeroed `nvme_id_ns` when `nvmet_req_find_ns()` fails on a non-broadcast NSID, and `nvmet_execute_identify_nslist()` must reject `min_nsid` of `0xFFFFFFFE` or `NVME_NSID_ALL`. [F55, F56, F90, F145, F194]

## Known Pitfalls
- Wire fields (`nvme_command`, `nvme_completion`, `nvmf_connect_data`, `nvmet_alloc_ctrl_args.result`, TCP PDU headers) require `le*_to_cpu()` / `cpu_to_le*()` conversions, and unaligned/24-bit fields (`id->ieee`, 128-bit SMART/endurance counters, `data->regctl` in `pr.c`, `sgl->length` and `sgl->key` in `nvme_keyed_sgl_desc`, and `u8 *` `cdw10`/`spc` byte offsets `(N - 10) * 4` in `trace.c`) must use `get_unaligned_le*()` / `put_unaligned_le*()`. `nvmet_execute_admin/io_connect()` must NUL-terminate `d->subsysnqn` and `d->hostnqn` at `NVMF_NQN_FIELD_LEN - 1`. [F85, F94, F95, F267, F277, F305]
- Configfs `allow_link` callbacks must verify `target->ci_type` (`&nvmet_subsys_type` or `&nvmet_host_type`) before downcasting, and configfs callbacks must use the matching parent depth (`to_subsys(item)` vs `namespaces_to_subsys()` vs `to_subsys(item->ci_parent)`, and `to_nvmet_port(item)` for the referral vs `to_nvmet_port(item->ci_parent->ci_parent)` for the parent port in `nvmet_referral_enable_store()` / `nvmet_referral_notify()`). In `nvmet_ctrl_find_get()`, `ctrl` must be reset to `NULL` if `list_for_each_entry()` finishes without `goto found`. [F110, F120, F138, F139]
- In `pci-epf.c`, `iod->data_segs` and `iod->data_sgt.sgl` alias inline `iod->data_seg` and `iod->data_sgl` for 1-segment IODs (so `nvmet_pci_epf_free_iod()` must check `iod->data_segs != &iod->data_seg` and `iod->data_sgt.nents > 1`). In `tcp.c`, `icreq` and `icresp` alias `queue->pdu` in `nvmet_tcp_handle_icreq()`; `nvmet_tcp_verify_hdgst()` must sample `recv_digest` before `nvmet_tcp_hdgst()` overwrites it; partial PDU/digest pointer math must cast to `void *`/`u8 *` before adding `offset`; and reverse unwind loop counters `i` in `tcp.c`/`rdma.c` must remain signed `int`. [F49, F241, F251, F280, F310]
