# IIO SUBSYSTEM AND DRIVERS

This unit covers IIO accelerometer drivers (`drivers/iio/accel/`: MMA9553L, MSA311, MXC4005/MXC6255, SCA3000/SCA3300, Samsung SSP, `st_accel`, STK8312/STK8BA50) and ADC drivers (`drivers/iio/adc/`: Aspeed, AT91, AXP20x/AXP288, Broadcom iProc, Marvell Berlin2, CC10001).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Scan structs passed to `iio_push_to_buffers_with_ts()` (`mxc4005_data`, `stk8312_data`, `stk8ba50_data`, `msa311_buffer_thread()`) must use `aligned_s64` for timestamps (or `IIO_DECLARE_BUFFER_WITH_TS` as in `sca3300_trigger_handler()`) for 8-byte alignment on 32-bit architectures; bulk reads into `data->scan.chans` (`mxc4005_read_xyz()`, `stk8312_trigger_handler()`, `stk8ba50_trigger_handler()`) must bound length to the channel array (`sizeof(data->scan.chans)`, `STK8312_ALL_CHANNEL_SIZE`, `STK8BA50_ALL_CHANNEL_SIZE`), never `sizeof(data->scan)`. [F127, F128]
- In `sca3000_state` (`rx`, `tx`) and `sca3300_data` (`txbuf`, `rxbuf`), SPI DMA buffers must remain at the end of the struct after driver state (`spi`, `lock`, `chip`) and be marked `__aligned(IIO_DMA_MINALIGN)` so non-coherent DMA does not corrupt adjacent fields. [F161, F162]
- Because `at91_adc_channel_init()` and `cc10001_adc_channel_init()` filter out reserved channels and assign dense `scan_index` values (`0..idx-1`), `at91_adc_trigger_handler()`, `at91_adc_configure_trigger()`, and `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` must map each `active_scan_mask` bit via `channels[bit].channel` before accessing hardware. [F86]
- In `at91_adc.c`, `st->touchscreen_type` makes IIO triggers (`!st->touchscreen_type`, `st->trig`) and input touchscreen (`st->touchscreen_type != 0`, `st->ts_input`) mutually exclusive: when active, `at91_adc_channel_init()` must mask `CHAN_MASK_TOUCHSCREEN_4WIRE`/`5WIRE` out of `st->channels_mask`, `at91_adc_probe()` must skip `at91_adc_buffer_init()`/`at91_adc_trigger_init()`, and `at91_adc_remove()` must branch on `!st->touchscreen_type`. Also, bus `drvdata` and `iio_trigger_set_drvdata()` store `struct iio_dev *` (requiring `iio_priv()`), whereas `input_set_drvdata(input, st)` stores `struct at91_adc_state *` directly on `st->ts_input`. [F46, F76, F135]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_get_op_mode()` and `sca3300_set_op_mode()` map hardware `SCA3300_REG_MODE` values to `0..chip->num_avail_modes - 1` via `avail_modes_table`, which must be mapped via `accel_scale_map`, `incli_scale_map`, or `freq_map` (all sized to `num_avail_modes` in `sca3300_chip_tbl[]`) before indexing `accel_scale`, `incli_scale`, or `freq_table`; `sca3300_set_frequency()` must preserve the current `accel_scale`. [F70, F89]
- In `msa311.c`, `msa311_odr_table` has 11 entries (`0..MSA311_ODR_1000_HZ`) while the 4-bit `F_ODR` field can be `10..15` for 1000 Hz, so callers indexing `msa311_odr_table[odr]` must use `msa311_get_odr()` to clamp `*odr`. Every `enum msa311_fields` entry before `F_MAX_FIELDS` must have a designated initializer in `msa311_reg_fields[]`, and `msa311_set_pwr_mode()` must sample `prev_mode` before writing `mode` to call `msa311_wait_for_next_data()` when waking from `MSA311_PWR_MODE_SUSPEND`. [F68, F69, F91]
- In `mxc4005.c`, `stk8312.c`, and `stk8ba50.c`, `data->dready_trig` is only allocated when `client->irq > 0`, so `mxc4005_trigger_reen()`, probe rollback, and `stk8312_remove()`/`stk8ba50_remove()` must guard `if (data->dready_trig)`. In `mxc4005.c`, because `mxc4005_resume()` issues a software reset, `mxc4005_set_trigger_state()` must cache `MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK1` in `data->int_mask1` and `mxc4005_suspend()` must cache `MXC4005_REG_CONTROL` in `data->control`. [F45, F63]
- In `axp20x_adc.c`, `axp20x_probe()` and `axp20x_remove()` must guard `info->data->adc_rate` (NULL on `axp717_data`) and `info->data->adc_en2_mask` (0 on `axp22x_data`, `axp717_data`, `axp813_data`, where writing `adc_en2` writes reg `0x00`). In `axp717_adc_raw()`, every `axp717_adc_channels` entry at `AXP717_ADC_DATA_H` must have a `switch (chan->channel)` case writing `AXP717_ADC_DATA_SEL` before `regmap_bulk_read()`. [F47, F103]

## Synchronization

- In `sca3000.c`, callers of `sca3000_read_data_short()`, `sca3000_read_data()`, `sca3000_write_reg()`, `sca3000_read_ctrl_reg()`, and `sca3000_write_ctrl_reg()` must hold `st->lock` (protecting `st->tx`/`st->rx` and multiplexed `SCA3000_REG_CTRL_SEL_ADDR`/`SCA3000_REG_CTRL_DATA_ADDR` accesses) and copy `st->rx[0]` before unlocking or re-reading; callers of `sca3000_ring_int_process()` and `__sca3000_hw_ring_state_set()` must not hold `st->lock` because those helpers acquire it internally. [F19, F20, F65, F66]
- In `sca3000.c`, `st->mo_det_use_count` must be checked and updated under `st->lock`: `sca3000_read_raw()` must reject `IIO_ACCEL` raw reads with `-EBUSY` when `st->mo_det_use_count` is non-zero, and `sca3000_motion_detect_set_state()` must increment/decrement it only when an axis bit in `SCA3000_REG_CTRL_SEL_MD_CTRL` transitions (`0->1` or `1->0`) and after `sca3000_write_ctrl_reg()` succeeds. [F5, F21]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_read_reg()` and `sca3300_write_reg()` must hold `sca_data->lock` while populating `sca_data->txbuf` and calling `sca3300_transfer()`, and must release `sca_data->lock` before calling `sca3300_error_handler()`, which acquires `sca_data->lock` itself. [F23]
- In `msa311.c`, callers must call `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` before acquiring `msa311->lock` and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` after releasing it because `msa311_runtime_resume()`/`suspend()` acquire `msa311->lock`; `msa311_irq_thread()` must also unlock `msa311->lock` before `iio_trigger_poll_nested(msa311->new_data_trig)` because `msa311_buffer_thread()` runs synchronously on the same thread and acquires `msa311->lock`. [F24, F167]
- In `mma9553.c`, `mma9553_runtime_resume()` must not acquire `data->mutex` because `mma9551_read_accel_chan()`, `mma9553_write_raw()`, and `mma9553_write_event_config()` call `mma9551_set_power_state(data->client, true)` (running `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()`) while holding `data->mutex`. [F25]
- In `at91_adc_read_raw()`, `iproc_adc_do_read()`, and `berlin2_adc_read()`/`berlin2_adc_tsen_read()`, the driver lock (`st->lock`, `adc_priv->mutex`, `priv->lock`) must be held across setting channel/completion state, enabling the IRQ, waiting, and unconditionally masking/clearing the interrupt registers (`AT91_ADC_CHDR`/`AT91_ADC_IDR`, `IPROC_INTERRUPT_MASK`/`IPROC_INTERRUPT_STATUS`, Berlin2 IRQ bits) and resetting completion flags (`st->done = false`, `priv->data_available = false`) even on timeout or signal. [F31, F34, F35]
- In `at91_adc.c`, `at91_adc_probe()` must write `AT91_ADC_CR` (`AT91_ADC_SWRST`) and `AT91_ADC_IDR` (`0xFFFFFFFF`) before `devm_request_irq()` because `st->wq_data_avail` and `st->ts_input` are initialized later; when `iio_buffer_enabled(idev)` is true, `handle_adc_eoc_trigger()` must call `disable_irq_nosync(irq)` before `iio_trigger_poll(idev->trig)`, and `at91_adc_trigger_handler()` must read `AT91_ADC_LCDR` before `enable_irq(st->irq)`. [F29, F30]
- In `ssp_accel_probe()`, `spd->process_data`, `spd->type`, `devm_iio_kfifo_buffer_setup()`, and `devm_iio_device_register()` must all complete before `ssp_register_consumer()` publishes `indio_dev` to the parent sensorhub. [F27]

## Object Lifetimes

- In drivers with manual `.remove()` callbacks (`mma9553`, `stk8312`, `stk8ba50`, `at91_adc`, `axp20x_adc`, `iproc_adc`), `iio_device_register()` is non-devm and `iio_device_unregister()` must run first in `.remove()` (and probe rollback) before tearing down triggered buffers (`iio_triggered_buffer_cleanup`), triggers (`iio_trigger_unregister`), map arrays (`iio_map_array_unregister`), runtime PM, clocks, or hardware power/enable state (`mma9551_set_device_state(..., false)`, `adc_en1`/`adc_en2`, standby). [F4, F9, F15, F144]
- In `at91_adc_allocate_trigger()`, if `iio_trigger_register(trig)` fails after `iio_trigger_alloc()`, it must call `iio_trigger_free(trig)`; once registered, `at91_adc_trigger_init()` rollback (starting at `i--` while `i >= 0`) and `at91_adc_trigger_remove()` must call both `iio_trigger_unregister()` and `iio_trigger_free()`. In `at91_ts_register()`, the `err:` label must call `input_free_device(input)` on local `input` (not `st->ts_input`), whereas `at91_ts_unregister()` calls only `input_unregister_device(st->ts_input)`. In `mxc4005_probe()`, `iio_trigger_get(data->dready_trig)` must only run after `devm_iio_trigger_register()` succeeds. [F12, F13, F108, F140]
- In `at91_adc_configure_trigger()`, enabling (`state == true`) must allocate `st->buffer = kmalloc(idev->scan_bytes, GFP_KERNEL)` before enabling hardware trigger, channels, and `AT91_ADC_IER`, and disabling (`state == false`) must disable `AT91_ADC_IDR`, trigger, and `AT91_ADC_CHDR` before `kfree(st->buffer)`. In `cc10001_adc.c`, `cc10001_update_scan_mode()` reallocates `adc_dev->buf` (also sized to `indio_dev->scan_bytes`), so `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` must reload `data = adc_dev->buf` on each call. [F14, F74, F130]
- In `cc10001_adc_trigger_h()` and `cc10001_adc_read_raw_voltage()`, when `!adc_dev->shared`, `cc10001_adc_power_up(adc_dev)` must be paired with `cc10001_adc_power_down(adc_dev)` on all exit paths (including `CC10001_INVALID_SAMPLED`), whereas when `adc_dev->shared` is true power is managed at probe/remove. In `axp288_adc_read_raw()`, when `info->ts_enabled` is true for `AXP288_GP_ADC_H`, `axp288_adc_set_ts()` must under `info->lock` switch to `AXP288_ADC_TS_CURRENT_ON_ONDEMAND` before `axp288_adc_read_channel()` and unconditionally restore `AXP288_ADC_TS_CURRENT_ON` afterward. [F16, F18, F156]
- In `apply_acpi_orientation()` (`st_accel_core.c`), once `acpi_evaluate_object(..., "_ONT", NULL, &buffer)` succeeds, every path must `goto out` to `kfree(buffer.pointer)` and store only static string literals in `adata->mount_matrix.rotation`. Device Tree nodes from `of_find_node_by_name()` (`aspeed_adc_set_trim_data()`) or `of_get_parent()` (`berlin2_adc_probe()`) must be released with `of_node_put()` right after `syscon_node_to_regmap()` and on earlier error returns. [F7, F146]

## API Contracts

- Every IIO triggered-buffer poll handler (`msa311_buffer_thread`, `mxc4005_trigger_handler`, `sca3300_trigger_handler`, `stk8312_trigger_handler`, `stk8ba50_trigger_handler`, `at91_adc_trigger_handler`, `cc10001_adc_trigger_h`) must call `iio_trigger_notify_done(indio_dev->trig)` on all exit paths; `st_accel_allocate_ring` and `msa311_buffer_thread` must not check hardware data-ready status bits inside the poll handler so external triggers work. [F175, F176]
- In `stk8312_read_raw()`, `stk8ba50_read_raw()`, `mxc4005_read_raw()`, and `cc10001_adc_read_raw()`, `IIO_CHAN_INFO_RAW` must check `iio_buffer_enabled(indio_dev)` and return `-EBUSY` before reading or toggling device power/mode. [F180]
- In `st_accel_core.c`, `st_accel_common_probe()`, `apply_acpi_orientation()`, and `st_accel_allocate_ring()` must pass `indio_dev->dev.parent` (never `&indio_dev->dev`) to `ACPI_COMPANION()`, `iio_read_mount_matrix()`, `devm_iio_triggered_buffer_setup()`, and `devm_iio_device_register()`; `apply_acpi_orientation()` must return non-zero whenever ACPI is disabled, `_ONT` is absent, or `_ONT` is invalid so `st_accel_common_probe()` falls back to `iio_read_mount_matrix()`. In `st_accel_read_raw()`/`write_raw()`, `IIO_CHAN_INFO_SCALE` must split `gain` into `gain / 1000000` and `gain % 1000000` and recombine `val * 1000000 + val2`. [F40, F116, F174]
- In `mma9553.c`, `mma9551_read/write_config_words()` and `mma9551_read_status_words()` take lengths in 16-bit words (`u16`), not bytes; `mma9553_get/set_bits()` and `mma9553_set_config()` require a non-zero 16-bit `mask` (`ffs(mask) - 1`) and unshifted `val`, whereas `mma9551_gpio_config()` takes `MMA9553_STATUS_TO_BITNUM(bit)`. In `sca3300_read_avail()` and `msa311_read_avail()`, `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_MICRO` lists require `*length = ARRAY_SIZE(...) * 2`. In `stk8312.c`, `data->range` is 1-based (`1` or `2`) while `stk8312_scale_table[]` is 0-indexed (`data->range - 1`). [F52, F53, F55, F57, F121]

## Error Handling

- In `mma9553_write_event_config()`, if `mma9553_conf_gpio(data)` fails after `mma9551_set_power_state(data->client, state)` and `event->enabled = state` when enabling (`state == true`), the error path must reset `event->enabled = false` and call `mma9551_set_power_state(data->client, false)` under `data->mutex`. In `mma9553_event_handler()`, `mma9553_get_event()` returns `NULL` when `ACTIVITY_UNKNOWN` maps to `IIO_NO_MOD`, so both `ev_prev_activity` and `ev_activity` must be NULL-checked. [F3, F44]
- In `st_accel_buffer_postenable()`, if `st_sensors_set_enable(indio_dev, true)` fails after `st_sensors_set_axis_enable(indio_dev, indio_dev->active_scan_mask[0])`, it must restore `st_sensors_set_axis_enable(indio_dev, ST_SENSORS_ENABLE_ALL_AXIS)`. In `stk8312_set_interrupts()`, `stk8312_set_sample_rate()`, and `stk8312_set_range()`, after entering `STK8312_MODE_STANDBY`, both normal and I2C error paths must restore `stk8312_set_mode(data, mode)`. [F8, F10]
- In `sca3300.c`, `sca3300_transfer()` must populate `*val` and return `-EINVAL` only when `SCA3300_MASK_RS_STATUS == SCA3300_VALUE_RS_ERROR` (and `-EIO` for SPI/CRC errors); `sca3300_read_reg()` and `sca3300_write_reg()` must route `ret == -EINVAL` to `sca3300_error_handler()`, which treats `-EINVAL` on reading `SCA3300_REG_STATUS` as the expected status-clearing response and returns `0`. [F38, F104]
- Check return conventions: `regulator_get_voltage()` (`cc10001_adc_read_raw()`) and `devm_regulator_get_enable_read_voltage()` (`aspeed_adc_vref_config()`) return positive microvolts on success (check `ret < 0`, and in `aspeed_adc_vref_config()` exclude `-ENODEV` to fall back to internal vref); `sca3000_read_ctrl_reg()` returns a non-negative byte or `< 0` errno; `sca3000_reg_lock_on()` returns `< 0` on error and `1` when locked; `cc10001_adc_poll_done()`/`cc10001_adc_read_raw_voltage()` return `u16` with `CC10001_INVALID_SAMPLED` (`0xffff`) on timeout; and `msa311_write_scale()`/`write_samp_freq()` must reset `err = -EINVAL` after `pm_runtime_resume_and_get()` before the lookup loop. [F41, F42, F43, F51, F100, F105]

## External Contracts

- Every `struct iio_map` array passed to `iio_map_array_register()` or `devm_iio_map_array_register()` (`axp20x_maps`, `axp22x_maps`, `axp717_maps`, `axp288_adc_default_maps`) must end with an empty sentinel `{ }`, and `.debugfs_reg_access` callbacks (`aspeed_adc_reg_access`, `msa311_debugfs_reg_access`, `sca3300_debugfs_reg_access`) must validate `reg` bounds (plus `reg % 4 == 0` and non-NULL `readval` in `aspeed_adc_reg_access`) before hardware access. [F83, F184]

## Known Pitfalls

- Stack scan buffers passed to `iio_push_to_buffers_with_ts()` (`sca3300_trigger_handler()`, `msa311_buffer_thread()`) must be zero-initialized (`= { }`) to avoid leaking stack memory. When converting left-justified 16-bit raw words (`msa311`, `mxc4005`, `sca3000`, `stk8ba50`), right-shift by `chan->scan_type.shift` before `sign_extend32(..., chan->scan_type.realbits - 1)`. Variables passed by address to `for_each_set_bit()` or `bitmap_weight()` (`ret` in `sca3000_read_event_value()`, `st->channels_mask`, `channel_map`) must be `long` or `unsigned long`. [F39, F126, F138]
- In `stk8ba50_set_power(data, mode)`, `STK8BA50_MODE_NORMAL` (`false`/`0`) powers on and `STK8BA50_MODE_SUSPEND` (`true`/`1`) suspends. In `mxc4005_chip_init()` and `mxc4005_set_trigger_state()`, write-only `MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK0`/`MASK1` must be initialized to `0` after reset, and disabling DRDY must write `0` (not `~MXC4005_REG_INT_MASK1_BIT_DRDYE`). In `st_accel_sensors_settings`, `LIS3L02DQ_ACCEL_DEV_NAME` must leave `.bdu` empty, and in `st_accel_common_probe()`, `st_sensors_init_sensor()` must receive the local `pdata` (defaulted to `&default_accel_pdata`). [F56, F115, F139, F152]
