# ANALOG DEVICES INC IIO DRIVERS

This unit contains Linux Industrial I/O (IIO) accelerometer, ADC, and DAC drivers for Analog Devices and Linear Technology hardware. These drivers bridge SPI, I2C, and parallel/IIO-backend buses to the IIO subsystem, managing raw and scaled channels, hardware FIFOs, triggered and DMA/SPI-offload buffered streaming, threshold/gesture events, and GPIO controllers.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `ad7124` and `ad7173`, hardware setup fields of `ad7124_channel_config` and `ad7173_channel_config` must be inside `struct_group(config_props, ...)` and matched in `ad7124_find_similar_live_cfg` and `ad7173_is_setup_equal`. Runtime changes must update `cfg` and set `cfg->live = false` rather than writing `cfg_slot` registers directly; evicting a slot in `ad7124_pop_config` or `ad7173_free_config_slot_lru` must clear `cfg.live = false` on all channels sharing that `cfg_slot`. [F44, F45, F106]
- In `ad7173.c`, `ad_sigma_delta_info.num_slots` must equal `info->num_channels` (4, 8, or 16), while setup slot allocation in `ad7173_load_config` and `ad7173_free_config_slot_lru` is bounded by `info->num_configs` (4 or 8). When `st->config_usage_counter == U64_MAX`, `ad7173_config_channel` must call `ad7173_reset_usage_cnts` before incrementing. [F98, F123]
- In `ad7173_write_raw` and `ad7173_fw_parse_channel_config`, setting sampling rate must update both `cfg->sinc5_odr_index` and `cfg->sinc3_odr_div` in lockstep (as `ad7173_set_filter_type` switches `filter_type` without recalculating ODR), and `ad7173_sinc3_odr_div_from_odr` must clamp the divider to `[1U, AD7173_FILTER_SINC3_MAP_DIV]`. [F49, F97]
- In `ad7124_parse_channel_config`, `num_channels` must not exceed `AD7124_MAX_CHANNELS` (16), each child `reg` must be `< num_channels`, and `diff-channels` pins must be validated by `ad7124_valid_input_select` to reject `[info->num_inputs, 16)` and `> FIELD_MAX(AD7124_CHANNEL_AINM)`. In `ad7173_fw_parse_channel_config`, pins must be validated by `ad4111_validate_current_ain` or `ad7173_validate_voltage_ain_inputs`. [F64, F65, F66]
- In `__ad7124_calibrate_all` and `ad7173_calibrate_all`, skip non-`IIO_VOLTAGE` channels, and in `__ad7124_calibrate_all` run `AD7124_ADC_CONTROL_MODE_INT_GAIN_CALIB` (when `pga_bits > 0`) and read back `AD7124_GAIN` before running `AD7124_ADC_CONTROL_MODE_INT_OFFSET_CALIB`. [F87]
- In `ad7606.c`, scale lookups/updates index `st->chan_info[0]` when `!st->sw_mode_en` and `st->chan_info[chan->scan_index]` when `st->sw_mode_en`; `st->write_scale` takes `i + ci->reg_offset` while `ci->range` stores `i`. In `ad7616_write_scale_sw`, interleaved channel `ch` must be split into group `ch & 0x1` and `ch_index = ch >> 1` before `AD7616_RANGE_CH_ADDR/MSK/MODE`. [F57, F71]
- In `ad7606_set_sampling_freq`, sample `ad7606_pwm_is_swinging(st)` and `duty_cycle == period` before overwriting `cnvst_pwm_state.period`, updating `duty_cycle` to `period`, `period / 2`, or `0`. In `ad7606_get_chan_config`, 1-based DT `reg` (`1..num_adc_channels`) must be converted to `reg - 1` when indexing `st->chan_info[]`. [F56, F69]
- In `adxl345_core.c`, runtime FIFO, event, or measurement updates (`adxl345_set_fifo`, `adxl345_set_act_inact_en`, `adxl345_write_raw`, `adxl345_write_event_value`, `adxl345_fifo_reset`) must bracket register writes with `adxl345_set_measure_en(st, false)` and `true`. `adxl345_set_range` must rescale `ADXL345_REG_THRESH_ACT` and `st->inact_threshold` (clamped to `[1, U8_MAX]`), and `adxl345_set_odr` must call `adxl345_set_inact_time(st, 0, 0)`. [F26, F54, F55, F104]
- In `adxl345_state`, `ad7266_state`, `ad7280_state`, `ad7298_state`, `ad7606_state`, `ad7766`, and `ad7779_state`, `__aligned(IIO_DMA_MINALIGN)` transfer/scan buffers must stay at the end of the struct after all non-DMA fields and hold all channel samples plus an `aligned_s64` timestamp. [F136, F156]
- In `ad3552r-hs.c`, `st->config_d` must stay synced with `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_INTERFACE_CONFIG_D`; while `AD3552R_MASK_SPI_CONFIG_DDR` is set, use `st->data->bus_reg_write` (never `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits` or `ad3552r_hs_reg_read`). Secondary region (`>= AD3552R_SECONDARY_REGION_START`) registers stream descending, so 2-channel streaming starts at `AD3552R_REG_ADDR_CH_DAC_16B(1)` (`0x2C`), not channel 0. [F200, F215]
- In `ad5592r_alloc_channels`, check child `reg < ARRAY_SIZE(st->channel_modes)` before indexing and allocate `1 + 2 * num_channels` `iio_chan_spec` entries. `ad5592r_gpio_read` must OR `st->gpio_in` with `AD5592R_GPIO_READBACK_EN`, and `ad5592r_write_raw` (`SCALE`) must update `st->cached_gp_ctrl` under `st->lock` while preserving other `AD5592R_REG_CTRL` bits. [F205, F206, F217]
- In `ad5064`, `ad5446`, and `ad5686`, `pwr_down_mode` stores 1-based hardware codes (`1..3`, initialized to `1` at probe) while IIO enum callbacks use 0-based `mode - 1`. Check `!st->use_internal_vref` before accessing `st->vref_reg` in `ad5064.c`, and check `st->chip_info->int_vref == 0` before accessing `st->vref_reg` in `ad5764.c`. [F192, F193, F195]
- In `ad5360`, `ad5380`, and `ad5755`, copy `chip_info->channel_template` into per-device memory before mutating fields. In `ad5755_set_channel_pwr_down`, enabling a channel under `st->lock` must set `AD5755_DAC_INT_EN | AD5755_DAC_DC_DC_EN`, wait `udelay(200)`, and then set `AD5755_DAC_OUT_EN`. In `ad5758_init`, call `ad5758_crc_disable` again after `ad5758_reset`. [F208, F213, F278]

## Synchronization

- In `ad7124.c`, acquire `iio_device_claim_direct` before `st->cfgs_lock` (never while holding `st->cfgs_lock`, since `ad7124_update_scan_mode` takes `st->cfgs_lock` under IIO's `mlock`). Conversely, in `ad7606.c`, `ad7606_write_raw` takes `st->lock` before `iio_device_claim_direct`, so callbacks under `mlock` (`ad7606_update_scan_mode`, `ad7606_buffer_ops`, `ad7606_backend_buffer_ops`) must never acquire `st->lock`. [F16, F17]
- Wrap direct-mode sysfs hardware accesses in `iio_device_claim_direct` and `iio_device_release_direct`: `ad7606_read_raw` (`ad7606_scan_direct`, `ad7606_get_calib_offset/phase`), `ad7779_read_raw`/`write_raw`, `ad7266_read_raw`, `ad7298_read_raw`, `ad7173_write_raw`/`ad7173_set_filter_type`/`ad7173_write_syscalib`, and `ad3552r_hs_write_raw`. [F18, F20, F142, F143, F172]
- In `ad7606.c`, `ad7606_interrupt` calls `iio_trigger_poll_nested(st->trig)`, so `ad7606_trigger_handler` must obtain timestamps via `iio_get_time_ns(indio_dev)` rather than `pf->timestamp`. In `ad5421.c`, `ad5421_fault_handler` must only be registered as `thread_fn` with `IRQF_ONESHOT` because it takes `st->lock`, performs SPI transfers, and calls `msleep(1000)`. [F138, F287]
- Functions in `ad5592r-base.c` and `ad3552r-hs.c` using `guard(mutex)` or `scoped_guard(mutex, ...)` must never call `mutex_unlock` explicitly. In `ad5592r-base.c`, `gpio_chip` callbacks must hold `st->gpio_lock` across `st->gpio_out/in/val` and GPIO register accesses, while `ad5592r_read_raw`/`write_raw` must hold `st->lock` across `st->ops` and `st->cached_dac`/`st->cached_gp_ctrl` accesses. [F159, F176, F177]
- Hold driver state locks across check-update-transfer sequences: `chip->state_lock` in `ad7291_read_raw` and `ad7291_write_event_config`; `st->lock` in `ad3552r-hs.c` (initialized before `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init` and held in `data_source` debugfs callbacks), `ad5064.c`, `ad5758.c`, `ad5766_trigger_handler`, and post-probe callers of `ad5360_write_unlocked`, `ad5421_write_unlocked`, and `ad5755_write*_unlocked`. [F24, F173, F178, F184, F288, F290]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `ad7606_probe`, when `!st->gpio_convst || st->offload_en`, acquire `st->cnvst_pwm`, call `ad7606_pwm_set_low`, and register `ad7606_pwm_disable` via `devm_add_action_or_reset` before `st->bops->iio_backend_config`; stop runtime conversions with `ad7606_pwm_set_low(st)` (not `pwm_disable`). `ad7606_scan_direct` must reset `gpio_convst`/`cnvst_pwm` low on all exit paths, and `ad7606_spi_offload_buffer_postenable` must unwind `spi_optimize_message`, `spi_offload_trigger_enable`, and `ad7606_pwm_set_swing` in reverse order. [F1, F9, F10, F127]
- Probe ordering: call `devm_ad_sd_setup_buffer_and_trigger` before `ad7124_calibrate_all` and `ad7173_setup`; clear `ADXL345_REG_INT_ENABLE` and finish `devm_iio_kfifo_buffer_setup` before `devm_request_threaded_irq` in `adxl345_core_probe`; call `ad3552r_hs_debugfs_init` after `devm_iio_device_register`; call `ad5592r_set_channel_modes` before `ad5592r_gpio_init`. [F14, F25, F174, F297]
- In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable`, configure backend transfer/format/stream only after DAC register writes and bus mode transitions, resetting `TRANSFER_REGISTER` to `AD3552R_SPI` before `bus_set_io_mode` on unwind. In `ad3552r_hs_buffer_predisable`, switch host bus to `AD3552R_IO_MODE_SPI` and disable DDR on target (`st->config_d` write) and backend (`iio_backend_ddr_disable`) before `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits`. [F162, F232]
- In `ad5360`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r`, and `ad5764`, call `iio_device_unregister` before disabling regulators, freeing channels, cleaning up `gpiochip`, or resetting channel modes; in `ad5064_probe` and `ad7303_probe`, register regulator disable `devm_add_action_or_reset` callbacks before `devm_iio_device_register`. In `ad4111_openwire_event`, reset `openwire_comp_chan = -1` and clear `AD4111_GPIO_GP_OW_EN` on all exit paths. [F4, F34, F165, F279, F280]

## API Contracts

- Every IIO triggered buffer poll handler (`ad7606_trigger_handler`, `ad7779_trigger_handler`, `ad7266_trigger_handler`, `ad7298_trigger_handler`, `ad7766_trigger_handler`, `ad5766_trigger_handler`) must call `iio_trigger_notify_done` on all exit paths, including read/write errors (and `ad7606_trigger_handler` must re-assert `st->gpio_convst` high). [F155, F167]
- In `ad7173.c` and `ad7124.c`, pass the 0-based channel slot index (`chan->address` or loop index `i`)—never `chan->channel` or `ch->ain`—to `ad_sd_calibrate` and `ad_sigma_delta_info` callbacks. In `ad7124_calibrate_all`, lower `AD7124_ADC_CONTROL_POWER_MODE` to `AD7124_MID_POWER` when `>= AD7124_FULL_POWER` before `__ad7124_calibrate_all`. In `ad7173_update_scan_mode`, clear all `cfg.live` flags before returning `-EINVAL` on `cfg_slot` collision. [F29, F118, F157]
- When `ad7779` or `ad7173` uses an IIO backend or SPI offload, copy static `iio_chan_spec` templates into per-device memory and set `scan_type.endianness = IIO_CPU`. In `ad7606_probe`, omit `IIO_CHAN_SOFT_TIMESTAMP` when `!slow_bus` (`iio_backend_config` or `offload_en`), and omit `INDIO_DIRECT_MODE` and `BIT(IIO_CHAN_INFO_RAW)` when `iio_backend_config` is set. [F149, F151]
- In `.read_avail` callbacks (`adxl345_read_avail`, `ad7124_read_avail`, `ad7606_read_avail`) returning `IIO_AVAIL_LIST` for a `[][2]` table, set `*length` to `2 * num_pairs`. In `iio_chan_spec_ext_info.write` callbacks, return `len` (`ret ?: len`) on success. In `.debugfs_reg_access` callbacks (`ad7606_reg_access`, `ad3552r_hs_reg_access`, `ad5758_reg_access`, `ad5770r_reg_access`), branch on `readval != NULL` to store into `*readval` and return `0`. [F111, F152, F153, F302]
- In `ad3552r`, only fall back from `ad3552r_get_output_range` to `ad3552r_get_custom_gain` on `-ENOENT` (setting `ch_data->range_override = 1` with `p`, `n`, `rfb`, `gain_offset`). Pre-shift `val` (`FIELD_PREP`) before calling `ad3552r_hs_update_reg_bits`. In `ad5592r-base.c`, `ad5592r_read_raw` (`SCALE`) and `ad5592r_write_raw_get_fmt` must both use `IIO_VAL_INT_PLUS_NANO`. [F190, F196, F201, F219, F292]
- I2C write callbacks (`ad5064_i2c_write`, `ad5622_write`, `ad5686_i2c_write`) must normalize positive byte counts from `i2c_master_send` to `0` (`-EIO` on short writes). In `ad5593r_read_word`, use `i2c_smbus_write_byte` followed by `i2c_master_recv` (not `i2c_smbus_read_word_swapped`); in `ad5592r_read_adc`, follow `AD5592R_REG_ADC_SEQ` with two `ad5592r_spi_wnop_r16` calls. [F189, F271, F283]
- In multi-bus drivers (`ad7606`, `ad5064`, `ad5446`, `ad5592r`/`ad5593r`, `ad5686`/`ad5696`), shared core code must never downcast `st->dev` via `to_spi_device` or `to_i2c_client`. In `ad5360`, `ad5421`, `ad5449`, `ad5592r-base`, `ad5755`, `ad5758`, `ad5764`, and `ad5770r`, bus drvdata stores `struct iio_dev *` (requiring `iio_priv()`), while `ad5360` `device_attribute` callbacks must use `dev_to_iio_dev(dev)`. [F113, F261, F262]

## Error Handling

- When a hardware helper (`ad7266_read_single`, `ad7606_scan_direct`, `ad7298_scan_direct`, `iio_backend_oversampling_ratio_set`) runs under `iio_device_claim_direct`, release direct mode and check `ret < 0` before using `*val` or updating state. Do not overwrite `ret` in error unwind labels of `ad3552r_hs_buffer_postenable` or `ad5592r_probe`. [F30, F194]
- Store results of value-or-errno read helpers (`ad5360_read`, `ad5421_read`, `ad5504_spi_read`, `ad5686_spi/i2c_read`, `ad5755_read`, `ad5758_spi_reg_read`), `ad5360/ad5421_write_unlocked`, and `adxl345_is_act_inact_ac/en` in signed `int` and check `ret < 0` before bitmask/boolean tests; compare `adxl345_get_int_line` against `ADXL345_INT_NONE` (`0xff`), not `< 0` or `0`. [F36, F37, F187, F188, F242]
- Reset `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)` from `devm_regulator_get_optional` to `NULL` in `ad7291_probe`, `ad7298_probe`, `ad5592r_probe`, and `ad7303_probe`, but leave `st->vref[i]` as `ERR_PTR(-ENODEV)` in `ad7124_probe` (where `ad7124_init_config_vref` tests `IS_ERR`). In `ad7606.c`, NULL-check optional `st->bops` callbacks, check `st->offload_en` (not `st->bus_data` or `bus_data->offload`) for SPI offload, and only use `st->cnvst_pwm` when `!st->gpio_convst || st->offload_en`. [F28, F31, F32, F33, F41, F191]

## Known Pitfalls

- Widen to 64-bit before multiplying/shifting in `ad7173_read_raw` (`IIO_TEMP` offset), `ad7779_set_calibscale`, `ad3552r_calc_gain_and_offset`, `ad3552r_get_custom_range`, `ad5592r_init_scales`/`read_raw`, `ad5791_read_raw` (`OFFSET`), and `ad5758_slew_rate_config` (and verify `st->slew_time != 0` before calling it). Sign-extend register values in `adxl345_read_raw` (`CALIBBIAS`), `ad5764_read_raw` (`CALIBBIAS`/`CALIBSCALE`), and `ad7606_scan_direct` (after masking with `GENMASK(realbits - 1, 0)`). [F73, F96, F99, F103, F126, F248, F249, F250, F251, F252, F253, F255]
- In `ad5064`, `ad5360`, `ad5504`, `ad5686`, and `ad5770r`, pass `chan->address` to hardware register helpers but index state arrays/bitmasks with 0-based `chan->channel`. In `ad7173_fw_parse_channel_config`, reset `is_current_chan = false` and `*chan = ad7173_channel_template` each loop iteration, account for `IIO_TEMP` at `chan_arr[0]` when `has_temp` is true, and start ODR lookup at `st->info->odr_start_value`. Clear shadow bitfields in `ad7173`/`ad7124` with `& ~mask`, not `& !mask`. [F81, F82, F83, F122, F197]
- In `ad7606_spi_offload_buffer_postenable`, compute `xfer->len` from `scan_type->realbits` (not `storagebits`); in `ad7606_par8_read_block`, pass byte count `num * 2` to `insb` while advancing `u16 *_buf` by 1. In `adxl345_fifo_transfer`/`push`, index `st->fifo_buf` by `i * ADXL345_DIRS` and pass `sizeof(st->fifo_buf[0]) * ADXL345_DIRS` to `regmap_bulk_read`. In `ad3552r_get_custom_gain`, read gain properties from `gain_child` (`"custom-output-range-config"`), not `child`. [F72, F109, F110, F233]
