# BCACHE (BLOCK LAYER CACHE)

`drivers/md/bcache` implements the Linux block-layer cache, enabling fast SSDs to act as writethrough, writeback, or writearound caches for backing block devices and providing flash-only volumes. It manages superblocks, bucket allocation and generation tracking, a log-structured B+ tree (`btree` and `bset`) with auxiliary floating-point search trees, metadata journaling, moving garbage collection, PI-controlled background writeback, and sysfs administration.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `KEY(inode, offset, size)` stores the ending sector in `KEY_OFFSET(k)` (`KEY_START(k) = KEY_OFFSET(k) - KEY_SIZE(k)`); `size` must be capped at `USHRT_MAX` (`KEY_SIZE` sits next to `KEY_DIRTY`), while `__bch_extent_invalid()` must reject `KEY_SIZE(k) > KEY_OFFSET(k)`. `MAKE_PTR(gen, offset, dev)` takes arguments in that order, and `__ptr_invalid()` requires `PTR_BUCKET_NR` in `[ca->sb.first_bucket, ca->sb.nbuckets)` without crossing a bucket boundary. [F64, F91, F92, F99, F133, F146]
- In `bch_extent_insert_fixup()`, sample `old_offset = KEY_START(k)` and `old_size = KEY_SIZE(k)` before cutting or splitting `k` so `bch_subtract_dirty()` subtracts the exact overwritten dirty sectors; call `bch_bset_fix_invalidated_key(b, k)` whenever `bch_cut_back()`/`__bch_cut_back()` mutates `KEY_OFFSET(k)` in place; when splitting a written key `k`, insert the top half into the unwritten bset and cut `k` in place for the bottom half; and do not `break` early on `bkey_cmp(&START_KEY(k), insert) >= 0` when `!KEY_SIZE(k)`. [F67, F68, F70, F119]
- In `struct btree_keys`, `b->nsets` is the 0-based index of the last active `bset_tree` in `b->set[MAX_BSETS]` (iterate `i <= b->nsets`; compact via `bch_btree_sort()` before `bch_bset_init_next()` if `b->nsets + 1 == MAX_BSETS`). Written `bset_tree`s (`t->size >= 2`) use 1-based `t->tree`/`t->prev` in `[1, t->size)`, whereas unwritten `bset_tree`s use a 0-based `t->prev` table updated by `bch_bset_fix_lookup_table()`. When `__btree_sort()` swaps `b->set->data` with `out`, it must copy `magic`, `seq`, and `version` and call `bch_bset_build_written_tree(b)`. [F71, F72, F96, F97]
- `bch_btree_insert_node()` and `bch_btree_insert_keys()` must check space via `insert_u64s_remaining(b)` so extent leaf nodes reserve `KEY_MAX_U64S` u64s (clamped to `>= 0L`) for `bch_extent_insert_fixup()` splits. In `bch_btree_map_nodes_recurse()` and `bch_btree_map_keys_recurse()`, set `from = NULL` immediately after the first child recursion (`bcache_btree(...)`). In `bch_btree_insert()`, reset `op.op.lock = 0` at the start of every `while` iteration. [F104, F121, F123]
- `mca_bucket_alloc()` must `kzalloc()` `struct btree` because `bch_btree_keys_init()` assumes `b->keys.set[]` is zeroed. When recycling `struct btree` nodes across `c->btree_cache_freeable` and `c->btree_cache_freed`, `mca_bucket_free()` must zero `b->key.ptr[0]` and call `hlist_del_init_rcu(&b->hash)`, `bch_btree_keys_free()` must reset `t->prev`, `t->tree`, and `t->data` to `NULL`, `mca_data_alloc()`/`mca_data_free()` must update `c->btree_cache_used`, and `mca_alloc()` must reset `b->flags`, `b->written`, and `b->keys`. [F12, F62, F76, F77]
- `c->root` must be unlinked from `c->btree_cache` via `list_del_init(&b->list)` when installed in `run_cache_set()` and `bch_btree_set_root()`, and re-added in `cache_set_flush()` before `bch_btree_cache_free()`. Every `c->prio_blocked` increment in `make_btree_freeing_key()` must either transfer to `btree_current_write(b)->prio_blocked` in `bch_btree_ptr_insert_fixup()` or be decremented via `atomic_dec(&b->c->prio_blocked)` on rollback. [F13, F16, F18]
- `bch_journal_next()` must push a new entry onto `j->pin` (refcount 1) and reset `j->cur->dirty`, `need_write`, and `data->keys = 0`. In `btree_flush_write()`, only select dirty nodes whose `btree_current_write(b)->journal` matches `fifo_front_p` (`&fifo_front(&c->journal.pin)`) and stop if `&fifo_front(&c->journal.pin)` changes, `nr >= ref_nr` (`ref_nr` as signed `int`), or `nr == BTREE_FLUSH_NR`. [F59, F85, F116]
- In `struct keylist`, `l->top_p` is an interior pointer into `l->keys_p`; `__bch_keylist_realloc()` must recompute `l->top_p = new_keys + oldsize`, and callers must call `bch_keylist_realloc()` before reading `l->top`. In `bset`, `jset`, and `keylist`, key counts and offsets are in `uint64_t` words (`bkey_u64s(k)`), and on-disk block counts use `DIV_ROUND_UP` via `set_blocks()`. [F81, F145, F149]
- `c->devices[i]` (or `s->d` in `cache_lookup()`) can be a standalone flash `bcache_device` or `dc->disk` in `struct cached_dev`; verify `d && !UUID_FLASH_ONLY(&c->uuids[i])` before `container_of(d, struct cached_dev, disk)`. Scans of `c->uuids` for unattached or flash devices (`flash_devs_run()`, `uuid_find()`) must bound by `c->nr_uuids` rather than `c->devices_max_used`. [F50, F98]
- Every non-bypass `op->bio` passed to `bch_data_insert()` (and `cache_lookup_fn()`) must be embedded in `struct bbio` (or have `front_pad = offsetof(struct bbio, bio)`). In `cached_dev_submit_bio()`, capture `orig_bdev = bio->bi_bdev` and `bio_start_io_acct(bio)` before `bio_set_dev(bio, dc->bdev)`, and pass `orig_bdev` to `bio_end_io_acct_remapped()`. In `cached_dev_read_done()`, reset `s->iop.bio` via `bio_reset()`, restore `bi_sector`/`bi_size`/blkg, and call `bch_bio_map(s->iop.bio, NULL)`. [F75, F89, F187]
- `bch_refill_keybuf()` must update `buf->start` and `buf->end` under `buf->lock`; `refill_keybuf_fn()` must always jump to `out:` to update `buf->last_scanned = *k`; and `bch_keybuf_check_overlapping()` must advance `w = RB_NEXT(w, node)` before `__bch_keybuf_del(buf, p)`. `bcache_dev_sectors_dirty_add()` must update `d->stripe_sectors_dirty[stripe]`, `d->full_dirty_stripes` (`BITS_TO_LONGS(d->nr_stripes) * sizeof(unsigned long)` bytes), and `c->flash_dev_dirty_sectors` in lockstep. [F84, F124, F125, F163, F244]
- `dc->has_dirty` and `BDEV_STATE(&dc->sb)` must transition together via `bch_write_bdev_super()`, and `bch_writeback_thread()` may only clear `dc->has_dirty` when both `refill_dirty(dc)` returns `true` and `RB_EMPTY_ROOT(&dc->writeback_keys.keys)` is true. `dc->writeback_rate.rate`, `writeback_rate_p_term_inverse`, `writeback_rate_i_term_inverse`, and `bdev_share` in `__calc_target_rate()` must be clamped to `>= 1`. [F239, F245, F277, F290]

## Synchronization
- Acquire nested `struct btree` `b->lock` in descending `b->level` order and ascending `bkey_cmp()` order for siblings. After locking `c->root` (`bcache_btree_root()`, `bch_root_usage()`, `bch_sectors_dirty_init()`), recheck `b == c->root` and retry on mismatch. After `mca_find()` under RCU, `bch_btree_node_get()` must lock `b` and recheck `PTR_HASH(c, &b->key) == PTR_HASH(c, k)`. When `bch_btree_insert_check_key()` upgrades `b->lock` (`op->lock == -1`), verify `b->key.ptr[0] == btree_ptr && b->seq == seq + 1` and `downgrade_write(&b->lock)` before returning. [F38, F43, F44, F45, F218]
- Callers of `btree_node_free(b)` must hold `b->lock` for write and call `btree_node_free(b)` before `rw_unlock(true, b)`. In `btree_gc_coalesce()`, error paths after locking `new_nodes[i]->write_lock` must jump to `out_unlock_nocoalesce` before `btree_node_free()`. `btree_flush_write()` must set `BTREE_NODE_journal_flush` under both `c->bucket_lock` and `b->write_lock`, and `mca_reap()`/`btree_node_free()` must wait under `b->write_lock` for it to clear. Toggling `BTREE_NODE_write_idx` in `__bch_btree_node_write()` requires both `b->io_mutex` and `b->write_lock`. [F8, F9, F33, F86]
- Hold `c->bucket_lock` when mutating `c->btree_cache*` lists, `c->bucket_hash`, `c->gc_mark_valid`, or bucket freelists/heaps, and in sysfs handlers inspecting them. In `bch_mca_scan()`, only use blocking `mutex_lock(&c->bucket_lock)` when `sc->gfp_mask & __GFP_IO` is set (else `mutex_trylock()`) and return `SHRINK_STOP` if `c->btree_cache_alloc_lock` is held. In `bch_alloc_sectors()`, drop `c->data_bucket_lock` before `bch_bucket_alloc_set()` and call `bkey_put(c, &alloc.key)` if unused. In `bch_allocator_thread()`, exiting `allocator_wait()` must unlock `bucket_lock` and set `TASK_RUNNING`. [F22, F35, F41, F176, F231]
- Never hold `bch_register_lock` while waiting for `bcache_device_stop()` or `bch_cache_set_stop()`. Under `bch_register_lock`, attaching/detaching a `cached_dev` must call `calc_cached_dev_sectors(c)`, and `bcache_device_unlink()` must gate `bd_unlink_disk_holder()` with `!test_and_set_bit(BCACHE_DEV_UNLINK_DONE, &d->flags)`. Sysfs show/store handlers must hold `bch_register_lock` and reject stores with `-EBUSY` when `bcache_is_reboot` is true. [F40, F74, F164, F230]
- In `bch_cached_dev_attach()`, set `dc->disk.c` and `smp_wmb()` before `refcount_set(&dc->count, 1)`, and hold `dc->writeback_lock` for write across `bch_cached_dev_writeback_start()`, `bch_sectors_dirty_init()`, and `bch_cached_dev_run()` (stopping `writeback_thread` and `writeback_rate_update` directly on failure when `ret != -EBUSY`). Before `bcache_device_stop(&dc->disk)` on error or dirty cache-set failure, set `dc->io_disable = true` and `smp_mb()`. [F25, F30, F166]
- Hold `dc->writeback_lock` shared via `down_read_non_owner()` in `cached_dev_write()` until `cached_dev_write_complete()`, for write in `bch_writeback_thread()` across `refill_dirty(dc)` and clearing `dc->has_dirty`, and for read in `update_writeback_rate()` (using `down_read_trylock()` up to `BCH_WBRATE_UPDATE_MAX_SKIPS` times, breaking rather than returning on skip so `writeback_rate_update` is rescheduled and `BCACHE_DEV_RATE_DW_RUNNING` is cleared). `cancel_writeback_rate_update_dwork()` must wait for `BCACHE_DEV_RATE_DW_RUNNING` to clear. [F36, F167, F222, F223, F276, F323, F325]
- Bio `bi_end_io` callbacks run in atomic context: `dirty_endio()`/`read_dirty_endio()` must only record errors, clear `KEY_DIRTY(&w->key)`, and call `closure_put(&io->cl)`, and `bch_time_stats_update()` must never be called from `bi_end_io`/irq context. In `bch_cache_read_endio()` and `read_moving_endio()`, check `!KEY_DIRTY(&b->key) && ptr_stale(c, &b->key, 0)` and set `s->iop.status = BLK_STS_IOERR` while passing `bio->bi_status` (0) to `bch_bbio_endio()`. [F46, F199, F329, F330]

## Object Lifetimes
- `bch_alloc_sectors()` returns `true` with a bucket pin on each pointer in `k`, and `write_dirty_finish()` must increment `PTR_BUCKET(c, &w->key, i)->pin` for each pointer before `bch_btree_insert()`; `bch_btree_insert()` drops one pin per key via `bkey_put()` on both success and error, while leaving the `keylist` buffer for `bch_keylist_free()`. [F2, F3, F251]
- In `bch_btree_leaf_dirty()`, when replacing `w->journal` with an older `journal_ref` (`journal_pin_cmp()`), decrement the displaced `w->journal` via `atomic_dec_bug()` before assigning and incrementing `journal_ref`. `btree_complete_write()` must reset `w->prio_blocked = 0` and `w->journal = NULL`. Any stack `struct btree_op` traversal that may cannibalize or check reserve must call `bch_cannibalize_unlock(c)` and `finish_wait(&c->btree_cache_wait, &op->wait)`. [F14, F17, F21, F147]
- In `register_bcache()`, reopen the block device with `BLK_OPEN_READ | BLK_OPEN_WRITE` passing `holder` and `fput()` the initial read-only `bdev_file`. In `register_cache()`, call `fput(bdev_file)` only if `cache_alloc()` fails; once `ca->kobj` is initialized, `kobject_put(&ca->kobj)` owns cleanup via `bch_cache_release()`, and `ca`/`bdev_file` must not be accessed after `fput()` or `kobject_put()`. Both `cache_set_free()` and `bch_cache_release()` must clear `ca->set` and `c->cache` to `NULL`, and `bch_cache_set_internal_release()` must be a no-op. [F7, F10, F15, F170, F318]
- In `bcache_device_free()`, read `disk->disk_name` and `disk->first_minor` before `put_disk(disk)`, and in `cached_dev_free()`, call `bd_unlink_disk_holder()` and `del_gendisk()` when `atomic_read(&dc->running)` is non-zero before `bcache_device_free()`. In `bch_cached_dev_detach()`, take `closure_get(&dc->disk.cl)` before `cached_dev_put(dc)` and release it in `cached_dev_detach_finish()` after saving `c = dc->disk.c` and calling `bcache_device_detach()`. [F6, F20, F73, F165]
- `cache_set_flush()` must stop `c->gc_thread` and `ca->alloc_thread` and cancel `c->journal.work` before `cache_set_free()` calls `shrinker_free(c->shrink)` and `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&b->work)`. In `bch_cache_accounting_destroy()`, call `closure_return(&acc->cl)` if and only if `timer_delete_sync(&acc->timer)` returns non-zero. Kthreads exiting on `CACHE_SET_IO_DISABLE` or `dc->io_disable` must call `wait_for_kthread_stop()`, and `bch_writeback_thread()` owns destroying `dc->writeback_write_wq` and calling `cached_dev_put(dc)` once `kthread_create()` succeeds. [F19, F23, F32, F206, F219]
- In `read_dirty()`, every `dirty_io` dispatched after `down(&dc->in_flight)` must reach `write_dirty_finish()` and `dirty_io_destructor()` (even on read error) to free bio pages, delete `w` from `dc->writeback_keys`, and `up(&dc->in_flight)`, and `read_dirty()` must call `closure_sync(&cl)` before returning. `bch_sectors_dirty_init()` must wait for `atomic_read(&state.started) == 0` before `rw_unlock(0, b)`. [F207, F212, F224, F268]

## API Contracts
- `bch_journal()` returns a non-NULL `atomic_t *` pin with refcount incremented by 1 when active; callers must release it via `atomic_dec_bug()` after `bch_btree_insert()`, and must use `bch_keylist_realloc()` (capping to `block_bytes(c->cache) - sizeof(struct jset)`). [F1, F113]
- `bch_btree_node_get()` and `__bch_btree_node_alloc()` never return `NULL`; they return an `ERR_PTR` or a locked `struct btree *` (caller must `rw_unlock()`), and `__bch_btree_node_alloc()` must reset `b = ERR_PTR(-EAGAIN)` right after `retry:`. Neither they nor `bch_btree_insert()` / `bch_journal_meta()` may run when `current->bio_list` is non-NULL (return `-EAGAIN` to defer to `bcache_wq`; `-EINTR` restarts from `c->root`, as required after `bch_btree_insert_check_key()` in `cached_dev_cache_miss()` when `miss != bio`). Map callbacks return `MAP_CONTINUE` (1), `MAP_DONE` (0), or `-errno`. [F4, F42, F47, F54, F56, F80, F114]
- Any standalone `struct bkey` holding pointers or passed as destination to `bkey_copy()` must use `BKEY_PADDED()`, and packed `bkey`s must be stepped with `bkey_next()`/`bkey_idx()`. Callers of `preceding_key(k, &preceding_key_p)` must initialize `preceding_key_p` to point to caller-owned `struct bkey` storage. `bch_bio_map()` requires `bi_size > 0`, `bi_vcnt == 0`, and `DIV_ROUND_UP(sectors, PAGE_SECTORS)` `bio_vec`s (without overwriting `bv_page` when `base == NULL`), while `bch_bio_alloc_pages()` frees its own pages on `-ENOMEM`. [F63, F102, F108, F144, F152, F209, F334, F336]

## Error Handling & Known Pitfalls
- Check `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL()` on `c->root`, `new_nodes[i]` in `btree_gc_coalesce()`, `r[i].b` in `btree_gc_recurse()`, and `w->private` in `bch_keybuf_next()` consumers. Validate version-5+ features in `read_super()`, `set_bytes(j)` bounds in `journal_read_bucket()`, `set_blocks(i, ...)` bounds in `bch_btree_node_read_done()`, and `DIV_ROUND_UP_ULL` stripe count bounds in `bcache_device_init()` / `do_div()` in `offset_to_stripe()`. In `write_dirty()`, a failed read (`!KEY_DIRTY(&w->key)`) must still advance `dc->writeback_sequence_next` (truncated to `uint16_t`) and wake `dc->writeback_ordering_wait`, and `write_dirty_finish()` must only insert the clean key if `KEY_DIRTY(&w->key)` is set. [F48, F49, F65, F90, F93, F94, F95, F143, F250, F265, F292, F294]
- Copy unwritten `k` to a `BKEY_PADDED` temporary before `bch_bset_insert(b, k, &temp.key)` in `bch_extent_insert_fixup()`; saturate `KEY_SIZE` at `USHRT_MAX` in `bch_extent_merge()` and `GC_SECTORS_USED` at `MAX_GC_SECTORS_USED` in `__bch_btree_mark_key()`; cast to 64-bit before shifting/multiplying in `bucket_to_sector()`, `bch_count_io_errors()`, `__update_writeback_rate()`, and `sysfs_hprint()`; do not truncate `int64_t` `bkey_cmp()`; pass `orig_bio->bi_bdev` to `bio_init_clone()` in `do_bio_hook()`; use `page_address(bv->bv_page)` in `do_btree_node_write()`; and call `vfree(p)` (not `vfree(cached)`) in `__bch_cache_show()`. [F69, F115, F134, F135, F136, F137, F139, F151, F216, F240, F282, F283]
