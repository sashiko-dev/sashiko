# QUALCOMM ATH12K WIRELESS DRIVER

Implements the mac80211 interface layer (`ath12k_ops`) for the Qualcomm ath12k 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) driver, managing hardware group registration (`ath12k_hw_group`, `ath12k_hw`, `ath12k`), VDEV and MLO link lifecycles (`ath12k_vif`, `ath12k_link_vif`), station/peer state (`ath12k_sta`, `ath12k_link_sta`), channel contexts, scanning, crypto keys, regulatory/TPC power, and TX routing.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Unstarted or deleted link VIFs have `arvif->is_created == false` and `arvif->ar == NULL`; VIF/link iterators (`ath12k_get_arvif_iter`, `ath12k_mac_handle_beacon_iter`, `ath12k_mac_handle_beacon_miss`, `ath12k_mac_change_chanctx_*_iter`) must verify `arvif->is_created` before reading `arvif->ar` or `arvif->vdev_id`. [F36]
- While `ahvif->link` has `ATH12K_NUM_MAX_LINKS` entries to hold scan links (`>= ATH12K_FIRST_SCAN_LINK`), `ahvif->cache` and `vif->link_conf` have only `IEEE80211_MLD_MAX_NUM_LINKS` entries and require checking `link_id < IEEE80211_MLD_MAX_NUM_LINKS` before indexing. When `ath12k_mac_find_link_id_by_ar()` picks a fallback scan link from `~ahvif->links_map`, it must mask with `ATH12K_SCAN_LINKS_MASK` before `__ffs()`, and callers like `ath12k_mac_initiate_hw_scan()` must reject `link_id >= ATH12K_NUM_MAX_LINKS`. [F62, F63]
- When a scan or remain-on-channel reuses an active started `ath12k_link_vif` (`arvif->is_started`), `ath12k_scan_vdev_clean_work()` and `ath12k_mac_op_cancel_hw_scan()` must skip deleting/unassigning `arvif`, and `ath12k_mac_initiate_hw_scan()` and `ath12k_mac_op_remain_on_channel()` must reject moving it to a different `ar`. [F85]
- Creating or deleting a WMI VDEV in `ath12k_mac_vdev_create()`, `ath12k_mac_vdev_delete()`, and `ath12k_mac_monitor_vdev_delete()` must update `ab->free_vdev_map`, `ar->allocated_vdev_map`, and `ar->num_created_vdevs` in lockstep, and non-monitor VDEVs must add/remove `arvif->list` in `ar->arvifs` under `ar->data_lock`. [F49]
- In `ath12k_mac_get_vdev_stats_id()`, `vdev_stats_id` allocated from `ab->free_vdev_stats_id_map` must be checked against `>= ATH12K_MAX_VDEV_STATS_ID` (0x30) on increment, falling back to `ATH12K_INVAL_VDEV_STATS_ID` (0xFF). Because `total_vdev` and `ATH12K_MAX_VDEV_STATS_ID` can exceed 31, bitmask operations on `ab->free_vdev_map`, `ar->allocated_vdev_map`, and `ab->free_vdev_stats_id_map` must use `1LL << ...` and `__ffs64()`. [F65, F91]
- In `ath12k_mac_hw_register()`, per-wiphy capabilities (`wiphy->max_ap_assoc_sta`, `wiphy->mbssid_max_interfaces`, `wiphy->available_antennas_rx/tx`, `ht_cap`) and per-radio init (`ath12k_regd_update()`, `ath12k_fw_stats_init()`, `ath12k_debugfs_register()`) must run across all radios via `for_each_ar(ah, ar, i)`. Because wiphy antenna masks advertise the maximum across radios, `__ath12k_set_antenna()` must clamp `tx_ant` and `rx_ant` to `ar->pdev->cap.tx_chain_mask` and `rx_chain_mask`. [F58, F106, F111]
- In `ath12k_mac_setup_channels_rates()`, each radio `ar` must `kmemdup()` the shared `ath12k_2ghz_channels`, `ath12k_5ghz_channels`, and `ath12k_6ghz_channels` tables before modifying flags, and `ath12k_mac_update_freq_range()` must expand non-zero `ar->freq_range` bounds with `min()`/`max()` stored in kHz via `MHZ_TO_KHZ()`. Comparisons against MHz channel frequencies (`ath12k_mac_get_ar_by_chan()`, `ath12k_mac_select_scan_device()`) must convert `ar->freq_range` bounds with `KHZ_TO_MHZ()`. [F57, F96]
- Because `ath12k_a_rates` is `ath12k_legacy_rates + 4` (`ATH12K_MAC_FIRST_OFDM_RATE_IDX`), any legacy rate index derived from 5 GHz or 6 GHz `basic_rates`, `mcast_rate`, or `mask->control[band].legacy` must add `ATH12K_MAC_FIRST_OFDM_RATE_IDX` before indexing `ath12k_legacy_rates`. [F67]
- When copying HE/EHT capability arrays between `struct ieee80211_he_cap_elem` / `struct ieee80211_eht_cap_elem_fixed` (`u8[]`) and `struct ath12k_wmi_peer_assoc_arg` or `struct ath12k_band_cap` (`u32[]`) in `ath12k_peer_assoc_h_he()`, `ath12k_peer_assoc_h_eht()`, `ath12k_mac_copy_he_cap()`, and `ath12k_mac_copy_eht_cap()`, `memcpy()` must be bounded by `sizeof()` the smaller `u8[]` field. [F99, F100]
- In `ath12k_mac_parse_tx_pwr_env()`, `tpc_info->num_pwr_levels` must be clamped with `min3()` against both `ATH12K_NUM_PWR_LEVELS` and the source TPE array bound (`IEEE80211_TPE_PSD_ENTRIES_320MHZ` or `IEEE80211_TPE_EIRP_ENTRIES_320MHZ`) before iterating. [F64]
- In `ath12k_peer_assoc_h_mlo()` and `ath12k_mac_mlo_get_vdev_args()`, loops populating `partner_info` must skip the self link (`arsta_p == arsta` or `arvif == arvif_p`) and unstarted/uncreated links, advancing the destination cursor and `num_partner_links` only for valid partner links. [F89]

## Synchronization

- Spinlocks shared with softirq/tasklet WMI, HTT, and data-path handlers (`ar->data_lock`, `ab->base_lock`, `ar->txmgmt_idr_lock`, `dp->tx_desc_lock`) must always use `spin_lock_bh()` / `spin_unlock_bh()`. [F122]
- `ath12k_mac_op_link_sta_rc_update()` runs in atomic context without `wiphy_lock`; it must hold `rcu_read_lock()` while dereferencing `ahvif->link`, `ahsta->link`, and `sta->link`, update rate-control staging fields (`arsta->changed`, `bw`, `bw_prev`, `nss`, `smps`) under `spin_lock_bh(&ar->data_lock)`, and defer blocking WMI work to `arsta->update_wk` (`ath12k_sta_rc_update_wk()`), which snapshots those fields under `ar->data_lock` and releases it before sending WMI commands or waiting on `ar->peer_assoc_done`. [F25, F119]
- In `ath12k_scan_abort()`, `ar->data_lock` must be held while checking `ar->scan.state` and transitioning `ATH12K_SCAN_RUNNING` to `ATH12K_SCAN_ABORTING`, but must be released before calling `ath12k_scan_stop(ar)`. [F22]
- In `ath12k_mac_start()`, `ab->pdevs_active[ar->pdev_idx]` must be published via `rcu_assign_pointer()` only after WMI pdev config, channel list update, VDEV/peer counter resets, and monitor status ring setup succeed. `ath12k_mac_stop()` must cancel `ar->scan.timeout`, `ar->scan.vdev_clean_wk`, `ar->regd_channel_update_work`, `ar->regd_update_work`, `ar->ab->rfkill_work`, and `ar->ab->update_11d_work`, drain `ar->ppdu_stats_info` and `ar->regd_channel_update_queue` under `ar->data_lock`, and clear `ab->pdevs_active[ar->pdev_idx]` followed by `synchronize_rcu()`. [F29, F125]
- Signaling `ar->regd_update_completed` (such as before `ieee80211_register_hw()` in `ath12k_mac_hw_register()`) must use `complete_all()` rather than `complete()` so subsequent `ath12k_regd_update()` calls without new firmware events do not time out. [F17]

## Object Lifetimes

- In `ath12k_mac_unassign_link_vif()` and `ath12k_mac_free_unassign_link_sta()`, `ahvif->link[link_id]` or `ahsta->link[link_id]` (and `links_map`) must be cleared with `rcu_assign_pointer(..., NULL)` followed by `synchronize_rcu()` before freeing or zeroing `arvif`/`arsta`. When `arsta == &ahsta->deflink`, `ath12k_mac_free_unassign_link_sta()` must reset `arsta->link_id = ATH12K_INVALID_LINK_ID`, `arsta->ahsta = NULL`, and `arsta->arvif = NULL` instead of calling `kfree(arsta)`. [F3, F19]
- When moving an existing `arvif` to a different radio `ar` in `ath12k_mac_initiate_hw_scan()`, `ath12k_mac_assign_vif_to_vdev()`, or `ath12k_mac_op_remain_on_channel()`, `ath12k_mac_unassign_link_vif(arvif)` frees or zeroes `arvif`, so `arvif` must be refreshed via `ath12k_mac_assign_link_vif()` before any further dereference. [F55]
- Before tearing down a station or link VIF, pending work items must be synchronously cancelled: `arsta->update_wk` via `wiphy_work_cancel()` in `ath12k_mac_station_remove()`, `arvif->connection_loss_work` via `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` in `ath12k_mac_remove_link_interface()`, and `ar->scan.vdev_clean_wk` via `wiphy_work_cancel()` when removing `ar->scan.arvif`. Whenever a scan or scan VDEV is cleaned up or aborted (`ath12k_scan_vdev_clean_work()`, `ath12k_mac_op_hw_scan()`, `ath12k_mac_op_remove_interface()`), `ar->scan.arvif = NULL`, `ar->scan.state = ATH12K_SCAN_IDLE`, `ar->scan_channel = NULL`, and `ar->scan.roc_freq = 0` must be reset together under `ar->data_lock`. [F23, F60, F129]
- Because `ath12k_mac_hw_allocate()` embeds each `struct ath12k` inside `ah->radio[]` and sets `pdev->ar = ar`, `ath12k_mac_destroy()` must clear `pdev->ar = NULL` across all `ag->ab[i]->pdevs[j]` and clear `ag->ah[i]` via `ath12k_ag_set_ah(ag, i, NULL)` when freeing `ah` with `ath12k_mac_hw_destroy(ah)`. [F14]
- In `ath12k_mac_hw_unregister()`, per-radio regulatory work (`regd_channel_update_work`, `regd_update_work`), debugfs, and `ath12k_fw_stats_reset(ar)` must run before `ieee80211_unregister_hw(hw)`, whereas `txmgmt_idr` destruction, channel array deallocation, and `ath12k_mac_cleanup_iface_combinations()` must happen after. `ath12k_mac_get_fw_stats()` must also call `ath12k_fw_stats_reset(ar)` under `ah->hw_mutex` before sending `ath12k_wmi_send_stats_request_cmd()`. [F12, F108]

## API Contracts

- Before suspending or flushing a radio via `ath12k_mac_wait_tx_complete(ar)`, `ath12k_mac_drain_tx(ar)` must call `synchronize_net()`, cancel `ar->wmi_mgmt_tx_work`, and purge `ar->wmi_mgmt_tx_queue` via `ath12k_mgmt_over_wmi_tx_purge()` before `ath12k_mac_flush(ar)` waits on `ar->dp.tx_empty_waitq` and `ar->txmgmt_empty_waitq`. [F27, F117]
- `ath12k_mac_init_arvif()` takes a signed `link_id`: `link_id < 0` (used by `ath12k_mac_op_add_interface()` for `ahvif->deflink`) sets `arvif->link_id = 0` without populating `ahvif->link[]` or `ahvif->links_map`, whereas `link_id >= 0` publishes `arvif` into `ahvif->link[link_id]` and sets `BIT(link_id)` in `ahvif->links_map`. [F46]
- Firmware expects `wmi_vdev_start_req_arg.punct_bitmap` to have `0` bits for punctured subchannels and `1` bits for unpunctured subchannels, so `ath12k_mac_vdev_start_restart()` must pass `~arvif->punct_bitmap` and `ath12k_mac_monitor_vdev_start()` must pass `0xFFFFFFFF`. [F47]
- Before calling `ath12k_reg_handle_chan_list()` to apply interface-type or AP-power-type regulatory rules in `ath12k_mac_op_add_interface()` or `ath12k_mac_handle_link_sta_state()`, the caller must reset `ah->regd_updated = false`. [F54]
- In `ath12k_mac_update_vif_chan()`, `ath12k_mac_vdev_restart()` must only be called when `arvif->is_up` is true; when `!arvif->is_up`, the loop must call `ath12k_mac_vdev_stop()`, `ath12k_mac_vdev_start()`, and `continue` without sending `ath12k_wmi_vdev_up()`. [F83]

## Error Handling

- Although `ath12k_mac_get_rate_hw_value()` has return type `u32`, it returns `-EINVAL` when a bitrate is missing from `ath12k_legacy_rates`; callers (such as `ath12k_recalculate_mgmt_rate()`) must store its result in a signed `int` and check `< 0`. [F32]
- In `ath12k_scan_stop()`, the residual jiffies returned by `wait_for_completion_timeout(&ar->scan.completed, 3 * HZ)` into `ret` must be normalized (`ret == 0` to `-ETIMEDOUT`, `ret > 0` to `0`) before jumping to `out:`, which treats any non-zero `ret` as an error requiring `__ath12k_mac_scan_finish()`. [F33]
- In `ath12k_mac_vdev_setup_sync()`, `__ath12k_mac_mlo_setup()`, and `ath12k_mac_set_key()`, callers waiting on WMI completions (`vdev_setup_done`, `mlo_setup_done`, `install_key_done`) must check both `wait_for_completion_timeout()` for `0` and the status field on `ar` (`last_wmi_vdev_start_status`, `mlo_setup_status`, `install_key_status`) for non-zero failure. [F34]
- When `ath12k_mac_assign_vif_to_vdev()` fails, it returns `NULL` with `arvif->ar` still `NULL`; callers like `ath12k_mac_op_assign_vif_chanctx()` must check `ar` for `NULL` and log via `ath12k_hw_warn(ah, ...)` rather than dereferencing `arvif->ar`. [F35]
- In `ath12k_setup_peer_smps()` and `ath12k_peer_assoc_h_smps()`, if a peer has neither HT support (`!ht_cap->ht_supported`) nor 6 GHz HE capability (`!he_6ghz_capa` or `!he_6ghz_capa->capa`), the function must return early as a no-op (`0` or `void`) before calling `ath12k_get_smps_from_capa()`. [F77]

## External Contracts

- In `ath12k_sta_rc_update_wk()`, when `IEEE80211_RC_BW_CHANGED` is set, bandwidth upgrades (`bw > bw_prev`) must send `WMI_PEER_PHYMODE` before `WMI_PEER_CHWIDTH`, whereas downgrades (`bw <= bw_prev`) must send `WMI_PEER_CHWIDTH` before `WMI_PEER_PHYMODE`. [F52]
- In `ath12k_mac_setup_iface_combinations()`, single-radio devices with `single_pdev_only` set must advertise two `ieee80211_iface_combination` entries: one from `ath12k_mac_setup_radio_iface_comb()` with `num_different_channels = 1` and `radar_detect_widths` set, and a second with `num_different_channels = 2` and `radar_detect_widths = 0`. [F131]
- In `ath12k_mac_op_set_key()` and `ath12k_mac_set_key()`, when a key must fall back to mac80211 software crypto (such as IGTK with `key->keyidx` 4 or 5, or when `ATH12K_FLAG_HW_CRYPTO_DISABLED` is set), the function must return positive `1` rather than `0` or a negative error. [F137]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `ath12k_mac_set_arvif_ies()`, before reading fixed offsets in `WLAN_EID_EXT_CAPABILITY` (`data[10]`) or iterating subelements of `WLAN_EID_MULTIPLE_BSSID` and `WLAN_EID_EXT_NON_INHERITANCE`, `datalen` must be validated (`>= 11`, `>= 12`, and `>= 2`). In `ath12k_mac_remove_vendor_ie()`, `skb->len < ie_offset` and `next > end` (`ie + ie[1] + 2 > skb->data + skb->len`) must be checked before compacting the trailing payload with `memmove(ie, next, end - next)` (never `memcpy()`) and `skb_trim()`. [F68, F70, F103]
- In `ath12k_mac_op_unassign_vif_chanctx()`, the branch calling `ath12k_bss_disassoc()` and `ath12k_mac_vdev_stop(arvif)` must exclude both `WMI_VDEV_TYPE_STA` and `WMI_VDEV_TYPE_MONITOR`, because monitor VDEVs are already stopped by `ath12k_mac_monitor_stop(ar)`. [F82]
- In `ath12k_mac_op_vif_cfg_changed()`, when handling `BSS_CHANGED_ASSOC` with `vif->cfg.assoc` set, `ath12k_bss_assoc()` must first be called for `ahvif->link[ahsta->assoc_link_id]` and `BIT(ahsta->assoc_link_id)` cleared from the local `links` bitmap before iterating `links` for partner links. [F86]
- Because `ahvif->links_map` (`u32`), `ahsta->links_map` (`u16`), and `vif->valid_links` / `sta->valid_links` (`u16`) are narrower than `unsigned long` on 64-bit architectures, callers of `for_each_set_bit()` must copy the bitmap into a local `unsigned long` variable rather than casting its address. [F104]
