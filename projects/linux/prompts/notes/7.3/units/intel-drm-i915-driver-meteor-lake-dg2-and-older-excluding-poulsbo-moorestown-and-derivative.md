# INTEL DRM I915 DRIVER (Meteor Lake, DG2 and older excluding Poulsbo, Moorestown and derivative)

`drivers/gpu/drm/i915/gt/uc` implements firmware loading, Command Transport Buffer (CTB) communication (`intel_guc_ct`), hardware context scheduling and virtual/parallel submission (`intel_guc_submission`), Single Loop Power Control (`intel_guc_slpc`) and RC6 (`intel_guc_rc`), firmware log relay and error register capture (`intel_guc_log`, `intel_guc_capture`), hardware configuration retrieval (`intel_guc_hwconfig`), and HuC authentication via GuC or GSC (`intel_huc`).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `guc_id` space `[0, num_guc_ids)` splits at `NUMBER_MULTI_LRC_GUC_ID(guc)`: parent contexts allocate regions of order `order_base_2(ce->parallel.number_children + 1)` in `guc_ids_bitmap` (never `guc_ids` IDA, `guc_id_list`, or `guc_ids_in_use`), while single-LRC contexts use `guc_ids` IDA. `steal_guc_id()` must clear `SCHED_STATE_REGISTERED` and invalidate `cn->guc_id.id` without erasing `guc->context_lookup`, so `try_context_registration()` deregisters the old owner. [F72, F87, F139]
- In GuC request submission/retirement paths (`__guc_add_request`, `guc_wq_item_append`, `need_tasklet`, `remove_from_context`, `guc_request_alloc`, `guc_bump_inflight_request_prio`, `guc_retire_inflight_request_prio`), access `guc_state` and `guc_id` on `request_to_scheduling_context(rq)` (parent) rather than `rq->context`. [F115]
- `init_sched_state()` must mask `ce->guc_state.sched_state` with `SCHED_STATE_BLOCKED_MASK` (not `= 0`) to preserve the blocked counter. Because `guc_context_unblock()` checks `context_cant_unblock(ce)` before `decr_context_blocked(ce)`, `SCHED_STATE_NO_UNBLOCK` must test `SCHED_STATE_MULTI_BLOCKED_MASK`. [F101, F102]
- `__unwind_incomplete_requests()` must traverse `ce->guc_state.requests` with `list_for_each_entry_safe_reverse()` because requests are re-added at the head via `list_add(&rq->sched.link, pl)`. [F107]
- In `intel_guc_busyness_park()`, `__update_guc_busyness_running_state()` (clearing `stats.guc.running` under `guc->timestamp.lock`) and `guc_cancel_busyness_worker(guc)` must run before the `last_stat_jiffies` throttle check and `__update_guc_busyness_stats()`. In `__reset_guc_busyness_stats()`, if `stats->running` is set, accumulate `guc->timestamp.gt_stamp - stats->start_gt_clk` into `stats->total_gt_clks` before zeroing `prev_total` and `running`. [F62, F63, F159]
- In `intel_guc_slpc_enable()`, call `intel_guc_slpc_set_ignore_eff_freq()` before `slpc_set_softlimits()` because enabling `ignore_eff_freq` resets min unslice freq to `slpc->min_freq`. Waitboost overrides via `slpc_force_min_freq()` must not alter `slpc->min_freq_softlimit`, and `intel_guc_slpc_dec_waiters()` must restore `slpc->min_freq_softlimit` when `num_waiters` hits zero. [F67, F68]
- In `guc_create_virtual()`, seed `ve->base.mask = VIRTUAL_ENGINES` before OR-ing sibling masks. When enabling switchout workarounds (`Wa_14014475959`, `Wa_16019325821`, `Wa_14019159160`) in `guc_ctl_wa_flags()` / `guc_ads_waklv_init()`, `guc_default_vfuncs()` must set `I915_ENGINE_USES_WA_HOLD_SWITCHOUT` on the same IP range. [F66, F146]

## Synchronization
- Spinlock order in `intel_guc_submission.c` is `sched_engine->lock -> ce->guc_state.lock` and `guc->submission_state.lock -> ce->guc_state.lock`; `__guc_signal_context_fence()` (under `ce->guc_state.lock`) must defer `i915_sw_fence_complete(&rq->submit)` via `irq_work_queue(&rq->submit_work)`. Accessing `ce->guc_state.requests` or `prio_count` requires `ce->guc_state.lock`. [F22, F161]
- In `xa_for_each(&guc->context_lookup, ...)` loops, pin `ce` via `kref_get_unless_zero(&ce->ref)` and `xa_unlock(&guc->context_lookup)` before taking `ce->guc_state.lock`, `sched_engine->lock`, or `release_guc_id()`, and on every path after unlocking call `intel_context_put(ce)` and re-lock `guc->context_lookup`. In `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()`, only call `intel_context_put(ce)` when `do_put` is true (`SCHED_STATE_DESTROYED` contexts have `ce->ref == 0`). [F7, F108, F170]
- In `intel_guc_context_reset_process_msg()`, `g2h_context_lookup()` and `intel_context_get(ce)` must run under `xa_lock_irqsave(&guc->context_lookup, flags)`. In `guc_context_init()`, read `ce->gem_context` via `rcu_dereference()` under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F171, F172]
- Paths outside `sched_engine->lock` setting pending G2H flags in `ce->guc_state.sched_state` (`try_context_registration`, `guc_context_block`, `guc_context_unblock`, `guc_context_revoke`, `guc_lrc_desc_unpin`) must check `submission_disabled(guc)` under `ce->guc_state.lock`. Before `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()` in `intel_guc_submission_reset_prepare()`, disable submission/IRQs and flush `gt->irq_lock`, `guc->ct.receive_tasklet`, `sched_engine->lock`, `destroyed_contexts`, and `guc->ct.requests.worker`. [F23, F24]
- Callers of `__guc_context_sched_disable()` must sample `ce->guc_id.id` via `prep_context_pending_disable(ce)` under `ce->guc_state.lock` before unlocking. In `intel_guc_sched_done_process_msg()`, when `context_pending_disable(ce)` holds, call `intel_context_sched_disable_unpin(ce)` before locking `ce->guc_state.lock` and calling `__guc_signal_context_fence(ce)`. [F73, F75]
- `guc_cancel_busyness_worker()` must use `cancel_delayed_work(&guc->timestamp.work)` (not `_sync`) when `mutex_is_locked(&gt->reset.mutex)` or `I915_RESET_BACKOFF` is set. `guc_timestamp_ping()` must use `intel_runtime_pm_get_if_active(&gt->i915->runtime_pm)` before `intel_gt_reset_trylock()` and hold it past `guc_enable_busyness_worker()`. [F29, F154]
- Under `guc->timestamp.lock`, `guc_engine_busyness()` must sample `i915_reset_count(gpu_error)` before `I915_RESET_BACKOFF` and re-check it after `guc_update_engine_gt_clks()` and `guc_update_pm_timestamp()`, rolling back `stats` and `gt_stamp` if changed. `__get_engine_usage_record()` must loop until two reads of `last_switch_in_stamp`, `current_context_index`, and `total_runtime` match. [F31, F32]
- `guc_send_invalidate_tlb()` runs in reclaim: allocate `tlb_lookup` entries with `GFP_ATOMIC | __GFP_NOWARN`, on fallback to `guc->serial_slot` wait for `!READ_ONCE(wq->busy)` via `wait_event_lock_irq(..., guc->tlb_lookup.xa_lock)` and set `wq->busy = true` before unlocking, wait via `must_wait_woken()`, and only call `xa_erase_irq(&guc->tlb_lookup, seqno)` if `seqno != guc->serial_slot`. [F13, F39, F165]
- `intel_guc_send_busy_loop()` must check `!in_atomic() && !irqs_disabled()` and use `cpu_relax()` instead of `msleep_interruptible()` when atomic. `ct_alloc_msg()` requires `GFP_ATOMIC`, and `ct_track_lost_and_found()` / `ct_check_lost_and_found()` require `GFP_NOWAIT`. [F162, F164]
- Call `intel_guc_write_barrier()` before updating `desc->tail` in `ct_write()` or `*ce->parallel.guc.wq_tail` in `write_wqi()`. Call `drm_clflush_virt_range()` before reading `slpc->vaddr` in `slpc_get_state()` / `slpc_query_task_state()` and after resetting `engine->status_page.addr` in `guc_sanitize()`. In `intel_guc_slpc.c`, always lock `slpc->lock` before acquiring `i915->runtime_pm`. [F158, F173, F174]
- In `__guc_capture_process_output()` and `_guc_log_copy_debuglogs_for_relay()`, copy `struct guc_log_buffer_state` once from WC memory to the stack, and write `read_ptr = write_offset` and `flush_to_file = 0` before sending `INTEL_GUC_ACTION_LOG_BUFFER_FILE_FLUSH_COMPLETE`. Finish relay copies with `guc_move_to_next_buf()` (`smp_wmb()` before `relay_reserve()` / `relay_flush()`). [F78, F183]

## Object Lifetimes
- `intel_huc_init_early()` must call `delayed_huc_load_init(huc)` before the `!vcs_supported(gt)` return, and `intel_huc_fini_late()` must unconditionally call `delayed_huc_load_fini(huc)`. Arm `huc->delayed_load.timer` and `fence` together in `huc_delayed_load_start()` (only if `i915_sw_fence_done()`) and disarm both via `delayed_huc_load_complete()` / `gsc_init_error()` on all completion/error/teardown paths (`intel_huc_register_gsc_notifier()` failure and `intel_huc_unregister_gsc_notifier()` must also clear `notifier_call = NULL`). [F1, F19, F79]
- When `guc_context_block()` or `guc_context_revoke()` calls `prep_context_pending_disable(ce)` outside unpin, it must first call `atomic_add(2, &ce->pin_count)` to balance `intel_context_sched_disable_unpin(ce)`. Pin contexts from `guc->context_lookup` with `intel_context_pin_if_active(ce)` before reading `ce->lrc_reg_state` or calling `lrc_update_runtime(ce)` (`guc_log_context`, `guc_context_update_stats`). [F6, F26]
- `guc_child_context_pin()` pins `ce->parallel.parent`, which must be unpinned in `guc_child_context_post_unpin()` after `lrc_post_unpin(ce)` (not in `guc_child_context_unpin()`). In `guc_create_parallel()`, once children are bound via `intel_context_bind_parent_child()`, error unwind must only call `intel_context_put(parent)`. [F10, F12]
- `__guc_context_destroy()` must check `intel_engine_is_virtual(ce->engine)`: virtual contexts put `ve->base.breadcrumbs` and `kfree(ve)`, while others call `intel_context_free(ce)`. `guc_context_pin()` / `unpin()` must skip `intel_engine_pm_get()` / `put_async()` when `intel_context_is_barrier(ce)` is true, while `guc_virtual_context_pin()` / `unpin()` must take/release PM refs on all siblings in `ce->engine->mask`. [F11, F157]
- In `__guc_reset_context()`, release `intel_context_get_active_request(ce)` with `i915_request_put(rq)`, and pass saved `parent` (not `ce`, which is mutated across child iterations) to post-loop `__unwind_incomplete_requests()` and `intel_context_put()`. [F3, F109]
- In `__guc_add_request()`, if `intel_guc_send_nb()` fails when `!enabled`, call `clr_context_pending_enable(ce)` and `intel_context_put(ce)` without incrementing `guc->outstanding_submission_g2h`. [F9]
- `ct_send()` must call `g2h_release_space()` after `wait_for_ct_request_update()` and unlink stack `request.link` under `ct->requests.lock` on every exit or `resend` path (`GUC_HXG_TYPE_NO_RESPONSE_RETRY` must jump to `unlink:` then `resend:`, whereas pre-link backoff jumps to `retry:`). In `ct_read()` -> `ct_handle_msg()`, `ct_handle_response()` and `ct_process_request()` free `msg` on success while callers must call `ct_free_msg()` on error. [F16, F17, F110]
- `intel_guc_log_relay_close()` must call `guc_log_relay_stop()` (synchronizing IRQs and flushing `flush_work`) before locking `log->relay.lock` to unmap/destroy the relay. Do not `mutex_destroy()` `drmm_mutex_init()`-managed `log->relay.lock` or `log->guc_lock`; release `log->vma` with `I915_VMA_RELEASE_MAP`. [F143, F145]

## API Contracts
- `pin_guc_id()` returns `1` (new ID), `0` (existing ID), or `< 0` (error); callers must check `ret < 0`. Synchronous `ct_send()` / `intel_guc_ct_send()` return `request.response_len` or `DATA0` (`>= 0`) on success, so callers expecting no data must map `ret > 0` to `-EPROTO`. `ct_read()` / `ct_receive()` return `available > 0` with `*msg = NULL` on `ct_alloc_msg()` failure (callers must check `if (msg)`). [F44, F45, F48]
- `intel_guc_ct_buffer` tracks `size`, `resv_space`, `head`, `tail`, and `space` in dwords while `guc_ct_buffer_init()` and `ct_register_buffer()` take bytes. `g2h_len_dw` passed to `intel_guc_send_nb()` and `guc_submission_send_busy_loop()` excludes `GUC_CTB_HXG_MSG_MIN_LEN`, and `guc_submission_send_busy_loop()` requires `loop == true` and only decrements `outstanding_submission_g2h` on failure when `g2h_len_dw > 0`. [F2, F58, F85]
- `__guc_reset_context()` and `intel_guc_submission_reset()` take `stalled` as `intel_engine_mask_t` and must test `stalled & ce->engine->mask` for `guilty`. `intel_context_ops` using `guc_context_update_stats` must set `COPS_RUNTIME_CYCLES`; `virtual_child_context_ops` must omit `.close`, `.revoke`, and `.sched_disable` and use `guc_child_context_destroy()`. [F57, F185, F186]

## Error Handling
- Check `IS_ERR(vma)` from `intel_guc_allocate_vma()` on a local pointer before assigning `huc->heci_pkt` or `log->vma`. In `guc_hwconfig_init()`, any failure after `guc_hwconfig_discover_size()` must zero `hwconfig->size` and free `hwconfig->ptr` (`intel_gt_fini_hwconfig()`). [F43, F53]
- `wait_for_ct_request_update()` must set `err = -ENODEV` when `!ct_enabled` and always set `*status = req->status`. `guc_wait_ucode()` must check `if (ret || !success)` after `guc_load_done()` and map `ret == 0` to `-ENXIO`. [F46, F47]
- In `guc_request_alloc()`, `-EPIPE` from `try_context_registration()` must call `disable_submission(guc)` and return `0` keeping `ce->guc_id.ref` pinned; other errors must decrement `ce->guc_id.ref`, call `unpin_guc_id()`, and return `ret`. In `guc_lrc_desc_unpin()`, if `deregister_context()` fails, re-lock `ce->guc_state.lock` and check `context_destroyed(ce)` before restoring state and putting `gt->wakeref`; in `deregister_destroyed_contexts()`, `break` immediately if `guc_lrc_desc_unpin()` fails. [F49, F50, F112]

## External Contracts
- In `ct_read()`, verify `desc->tail < ctb->size`, keep `available = tail - head` as `s32`, and check `len <= (u32)available` before `ct_alloc_msg(len)`. `ct_handle_hxg()` must check `msg->size >= GUC_CTB_HXG_MSG_MIN_LEN`, and `ct_handle_response()` must clamp `datalen` to `req->response_len`. `ct->requests.last_fence` must remain `u16`. [F83, F84, F121, F122]
- G2H handlers in `intel_guc_submission.c` must check `len` and use `g2h_context_lookup(guc, ctx_id)` (not `__get_context()`). In `__guc_wq_item_append()`, pad wrap-around via `guc_wq_noop_append(ce)`, set `WQ_LEN_MASK` to `(wqi_size / sizeof(u32)) - 1`, and write `WQ_RING_TAIL_MASK` in qwords. [F86, F88]

## Known Pitfalls
- `__get_parent_scratch()` must subtract `LRC_STATE_OFFSET` and divide by `sizeof(u32)` when indexing `u32 *ce->lrc_reg_state` (and `get_wq_pointer()` must divide `wqi_tail` by `sizeof(u32)`), whereas GGTT offsets in `prepare_context_registration_info_v70()` must cast `i915_ggtt_offset(ce->state)` to `u64` and add `__get_parent_scratch_offset(ce)` or `__get_wq_offset(ce)` directly. [F89, F116, F134]
- In `__extend_last_switch()` and `guc_update_pm_timestamp()`, cast `gt_stamp_hi` to `u64` before `<< 32`, keep `new_start` and `gt_stamp_last` as `u32`, and in `guc_update_engine_gt_clks()` truncate `total - stats->prev_total` to `u32` before adding to `stats->total_gt_clks`. [F117, F118, F119]
- GuC log buffer layout (`intel_guc_get_log_buffer_size` / `offset`, `log->stats[]`) is ordered by `enum guc_log_buffer_type` (`DEBUG`, `CRASH_DUMP`, `CAPTURE`), whereas `log->sizes[]` is indexed by `GUC_LOG_SECTIONS_*` (`CRASH`, `DEBUG`, `CAPTURE`) with 0-based `count = (bytes / units) - 1`; add 16 to `sampled_overflow` when 4-bit `full_cnt < prev_full_cnt`. [F56, F97, F124]
- Convert engine classes via `engine_class_to_guc_class()` / `guc_class_to_engine_class()` and increment reset counts via `i915_increase_reset_engine_count()`. In `scrub_guc_desc_for_outstanding_g2h()`, keep `if (pending_enable || destroyed || deregister)` and `if (pending_disable)` as separate `if` statements. In `pin_guc_id()`, keep `PIN_GUC_ID_TRIES - tries > 1` guarding `<< (PIN_GUC_ID_TRIES - tries - 2)`. [F90, F103, F123]
