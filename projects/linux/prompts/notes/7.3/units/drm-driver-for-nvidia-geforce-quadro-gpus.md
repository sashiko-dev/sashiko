# DRM DRIVER FOR NVIDIA GEFORCE/QUADRO GPUS

This unit implements the Nouveau DRM driver for NVIDIA GPUs across three layers: `nvhw` (register/class definitions and DRF bitfield macros), `nvif` (the intermediate client/object/pushbuffer/ioctl interface), and `nvkm` (the core hardware abstraction for subdevices, engines, VMM/MMU, and GSP firmware), alongside the DRM/GEM/TTM memory manager, GPUVM (`nouveau_uvmm`), DRM scheduler (`nouveau_sched`), HMM/SVM (`nouveau_svm`), KMS/DisplayPort, and userspace ioctls.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `PUSH_WAIT(push, size)` reserves `size` `u32` words and may invoke `push->wait` to wrap the ring and reset `push->bgn`/`cur`/`end`. Never cache `push->cur` or `push->bgn` across `PUSH_WAIT`, and wrap pre-reserved end-of-segment words in `PUSH_RSVD(p, d)`. In `nvif_chan_dma_wait()`, reserve `chan->func->gpfifo.post_size` words (`nr += post_size`, `push->end = push->bgn + free - post_size`), and check `chan->func->gpfifo.post != NULL` before calling it on pre-Fermi (`nvif_chan506f`). [F35, F36, F37, F145, F175]
- In `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, `bind_lock_validate()` must validate all backing GEM BOs before `bind_link_gpuvas()` links `DRM_GPUVA_OP_MAP`/`REMAP` to `drm_gpuvm_bo`, and `nouveau_uvmm_bo_validate()` must call `nouveau_bo_placement_set(nvbo, nvbo->valid_domains, 0)` before `nouveau_bo_validate()`. `nouveau_uvmm_bo_unmap_all()` and `nouveau_uvmm_bo_map_all()` must toggle `drm_gpuva_invalidate(va, true/false)` in lockstep with `nouveau_uvma_unmap`/`map`. [F159, F161, F162]
- `uvmm->region_mt` indexes `struct nouveau_uvma_region` with inclusive bounds `[addr, addr + range - 1]`. Any non-sparse `OP_MAP` intersecting a sparse region must be fully enclosed within `[reg->va.addr, reg->va.addr + reg->va.range)` or fail with `-ENOSPC`. Only `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job` instances may be submitted to `cli->sched`. [F182, F183, F229]
- In `nouveau_gem.c`, `validate_init()` stores a `struct nouveau_vma *` in `b->user_priv` on `NVIF_CLASS_VMM_NV50`+ and a `struct nouveau_bo *` otherwise; `nouveau_gem_pushbuf_reloc_apply()` must only run on pre-`NV50` channels, and `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()` must only copy `bo[i].presumed` back to userspace (`&upbbo[i].presumed`), never `bo[i]`. Any `ttm_bo_kmap()` during pushbuf validation must set `nvbo->validate_mapped = true`. [F167, F231, F232]
- The declaration order of `NVKM_LAYOUT_ONCE` (`.inst` must only be `1`) and `NVKM_LAYOUT_INST(type, data, ptr, cnt)` (`cnt` must exceed the highest bit in any chip's `<ptr>.inst` mask) in `nvkm/core/layout.h` defines the exact forward `ctor`/`preinit`/`init` order and reverse `fini`/`dtor` order of `device->subdev`. [F49, F85]

## Synchronization

- `nvkm_chan_get_chid()` and `nvkm_chan_get_inst()` return non-NULL with spinlock `chan->cgrp->lock` held and IRQs disabled in `*irqflags` (callers must not sleep and must call `nvkm_chan_put(&chan, irqflags)` on all paths). `nvkm_fifo_pause(fifo, &flags)` acquires `spin_lock_irqsave(&fifo->lock, flags)` and must be paired with `nvkm_fifo_start(fifo, &flags)` without sleeping. [F16, F17]
- `nvkm_inth_func` and `nvkm_subdev_func::intr` callbacks run in hardirq context under `device->intr.lock` and must not sleep or call `nvkm_inth_allow()`, `nvkm_intr_allow()`, `nvkm_intr_block()`, `nvkm_intr_rearm()`, or `nvkm_intr_unarm()` on the same device. `nvkm_event_func` (`nvkm_event_ntfy::func`) and `nvkm_event_func::init`/`fini` run in atomic context under `event->list_lock` / `event->refs_lock`; when registered with `wait == true`, `nvkm_event_ntfy_allow()`/`block()`/`del()` take `write_lock_irq(&event->list_lock)` and must never be called inside the callback. [F18, F92, F93]
- GSP notification callbacks (`nvkm_gsp_msg_ntfy_func` and `nvkm_gsp_event_func`) run under `gsp->cmdq.mutex` (and `gsp->client_id.mutex` for events) and must never synchronously issue GSP RPCs (`nvkm_gsp_rpc_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_alloc_*`, `nvkm_gsp_rm_free`) or mutate `client->events`. [F19]
- On `struct nvkm_vmm` and `struct nvkm_mmu`, lock ordering is `vmm->mutex.vmm` -> `vmm->mutex.ref` / `vmm->mutex.map` -> `vmm->mmu->mutex`. Callers that look up or split a non-raw `struct nvkm_vma` under `vmm->mutex.vmm` before `nvkm_memory_map()` (`nvkm_vmm_map()`) must set `vma->busy = true` and unlock `vmm->mutex.vmm` first, relocking to clear `vma->busy = false` on error. [F20, F95]
- In `nouveau_job_submit()`, `sched->mutex` must be held continuously across `job->ops->submit()`, `list_add()`, `drm_sched_job_arm()`, `job->ops->armed_submit()`, and `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`, and `job->state = NOUVEAU_JOB_SUBMIT_SUCCESS` must be set before `drm_sched_entity_push_job()`. Once `submit()` succeeds, submission cannot fail; if `submit()` fails, `nouveau_job_fence_attach_cleanup(job)` must clean up prepared `out_sync` state. [F115, F123, F124]
- All accesses to `uvmm->base` or `uvmm->region_mt` require `uvmm->mutex` (`nouveau_uvmm_lock()`), which nests outside GEM `dma_resv` locks (`obj->resv`). Every `drm_gpuva_link()`, `drm_gpuva_unlink()`, `drm_gpuvm_bo_obtain()`, and `drm_gpuvm_bo_put()` requires holding `obj->resv`. Hold `uvmm->mutex` continuously across op prep, validation, and `bind_link_gpuvas()` in `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()`, and across `drm_gpuvm_exec_lock(vme)` (dropping before `validate()`) in `nouveau_exec_job_submit()`. Both `armed_submit` callbacks must call `drm_gpuvm_exec_resv_add_fence()` before `drm_gpuvm_exec_unlock(vme)`. [F126, F127, F131, F259, F325]
- In `nouveau_range_fault()` and `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, release `mmap_read_lock(mm)` before acquiring `svmm->mutex`, and hold `svmm->mutex` continuously across `mmu_interval_read_retry()` and `nvif_object_ioctl()`. In `nouveau_svmm_invalidate_range_start()` and `nouveau_svm_range_invalidate()`, check `mmu_notifier_range_blockable()` before `mutex_lock(&svmm->mutex)`. In `nouveau_svm_fault()`, acquire `mmget_not_zero(mm)` before faulting `svmm->notifier.mm`. [F135, F277, F281]
- `nouveau_fence_chan.lock` protects `fctx->pending`, `fctx->killed`, and `fctx->notify_ref`; in `nouveau_fence_emit()`, check `fctx->killed` under `fctx->lock` before linking `fence->head`, and iterate `fctx->pending` with `list_for_each_entry_safe` whenever calling `nouveau_fence_signal()`. `fence->channel` is cleared via `rcu_assign_pointer(fence->channel, NULL)` under `fctx->lock`; readers outside `fctx->lock` must use `rcu_read_lock()`, and `nouveau_fence_context_del()` must call `synchronize_rcu()` after killing fences. [F298, F314, F316, F393]
- `nouveau_abi16_get()` returns with `cli->mutex` held on success, requiring `nouveau_abi16_put(abi16, ret)` on all return paths. In `nouveau_gem_prime_import_sg_table()`, hold `dma_resv_lock(robj, NULL)` across `nouveau_bo_alloc()`, `drm_gem_object_init()`, and `nouveau_bo_init()`, and `nouveau_bo_init()` must only unreserve when `robj == NULL`. In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()`, drop BO locks via `validate_fini()` before `u_memcpya(req->relocs, ...)` and jump to `revalidate:`. [F136, F137, F294, F303]

## Object Lifetimes

- `nvkm_kmap()` and `nvkm_done()` must bracket all uses of `nvkm_ro32()`, `nvkm_wo32()`, `nvkm_mo32()`, `nvkm_wo64()`, `nvkm_robj()`, `nvkm_wobj()`, and `nvkm_fill()` on a `struct nvkm_memory` or `struct nvkm_gpuobj`. [F1]
- Every `struct nvkm_gsp_mem` initialized by `nvkm_gsp_mem_ctor()` must be destroyed with `nvkm_gsp_mem_dtor()`. When `nvkm_gsp_rpc_push()` or `nvkm_gsp_rpc_rd()` succeeds with `NVKM_GSP_RPC_REPLY_RECV` and `repc > 0`, free the reply via `nvkm_gsp_rpc_done()`. When `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_push()` is called with `repc > 0`, `r535_gsp_rpc_rm_ctrl_push()` stores the reply in `*argv` even when `rpc->status != 0`, so callers must call `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_done()` on both success and error paths. [F2, F3, F4]
- In `nouveau_gem_new()` and `nouveau_gem_prime_import_sg_table()`, call `drm_gem_object_init(&nvbo->bo.base)` after `nouveau_bo_alloc()` and before `nouveau_bo_init()`. If `drm_gem_object_init()` fails, call `drm_gem_object_release(&nvbo->bo.base)` and `kfree(nvbo)`; once `nouveau_bo_init()` is called, it consumes `nvbo` even on failure (via `nouveau_bo_del_ttm()`), so callers must not free or release `nvbo` when `nouveau_bo_init()` fails. [F105, F106, F238]
- `op_map_prepare()` takes a GEM ref via `nouveau_uvma_gem_get(uvma)`, so `op_map_prepare_unwind()`, `nouveau_uvmm_sm_cleanup()`, and `nouveau_uvmm_fini()` must call `nouveau_uvma_gem_put(uvma)` before `nouveau_uvma_free(uvma)`. When `nouveau_uvmm_bind_job_submit()` fails, unwind in reverse order (`unwind_continue` from inside `list_for_each_op`, or `op = list_last_op(&bind_job->ops)` and `goto unwind` after the loop), free and NULL `op->ops` and `op->reg`, and unlock `uvmm` and `vme`. [F116, F118, F194]
- In `nouveau_svmm_init()`, `__mmu_notifier_register()` transfers ownership of `svmm` to `mmu_notifier` (`nouveau_svmm_free_notifier()`), so `nouveau_svmm_fini()` must set `svmm->vmm = NULL` under `svmm->mutex` before calling `mmu_notifier_put(&svmm->notifier)`. In `nouveau_atomic_range_fault()`, release the `folio` from `make_device_exclusive()` via `folio_unlock()` and `folio_put()` both on `mmu_interval_read_retry()` retries and after `nvif_object_ioctl()`, and call `mmu_interval_notifier_remove()` before returning. [F111, F112, F133]

## API Contracts

- Implementations of `nvkm_{object,subdev,engine,device,memory}_func.dtor` must return the outer heap allocation pointer for the core destructor to pass to `kfree()` (or `NULL` if embedded) and must never `kfree()` a pointer they also return. Callers of `nvkm_object_search(client, handle, func)` that downcast via `container_of` must pass the target subtype's `func` pointer (not `NULL`). [F11, F77]
- `DRF_MR` / `NVIF_MR32` / `nvkm_mask` / `nvif_mask` take pre-shifted mask and value arguments and return the old 32-bit register value (unserialized MMIO RMW), whereas `DRF_MV` / `NVIF_MV32` take unshifted field values and return the old unshifted field value. `DRF_WV`/`WD` and `NVIF_WV32`/`WD32` overwrite the entire register (zeroing all other bits), unlike `DRF_MV`/`MD`. [F32, F33, F88]
- Unit conventions: `nvkm_memory_page()`, `nvkm_vmm_get()`, `nvif_vmm_get()`, `nvif_vmm_raw_{get,put,map,unmap}()`, and `nvif_mem_ctor_type()` take page sizes and alignments as log2 bit shifts (`PAGE_SHIFT`), while `struct nvkm_vma::{page,refd}` store 3-bit indices into `vmm->func->page[]`. TTM `ttm_resource.start` and `ttm_place.{fpfn,lpfn}` are in pages (`>> PAGE_SHIFT` / `PFN_UP(size)`), while `nouveau_bo_{alloc,init,new}()` take `align` in bytes. `nouveau_bo_{wr16,rd32,wr32}()` take typed element indices (u16/u32), not byte offsets. [F100, F156, F275, F350, F352]
- `nvif_object_sclass_get()` allocates `*psclass` even when returning `0`, requiring `nvif_object_sclass_put(&sclass)` whenever `ret >= 0`. `nvif_object_map_handle()` returns `< 0` on error, `1` for `NVIF_IOCTL_MAP_V0_IO` (if `client->driver->map()` fails in `nvif_object_map()`, call `nvif_object_unmap_handle()`, and in `nouveau_ttm_io_mem_reserve()`, require `ret == 1` and reset `ret = 0`), and `0` for a kernel VA. [F10, F122, F143, F332]

## Error Handling

- `nvkm_{nsec,usec,msec}`, `nvkm_wait_{nsec,usec,msec}`, and `nvif_{nsec,usec,msec}` return a signed `s64` (elapsed ns on `break`, or `-ETIMEDOUT` if the loop finishes without `break`); callers must exit via `break` on condition match and check `< 0` for errors. `nvkm_engine_ref()`, `nvkm_firmware_load()`, and `u_memcpya()` return `ERR_PTR()` on failure (check `IS_ERR()`, and never pass `IS_ERR()` to `u_free()`). `nouveau_job_ops.run` returns `ERR_PTR(0)` (`NULL`) on synchronous completion without a fence or `ERR_PTR(ret)` on error, so `nouveau_job_run()` must check `IS_ERR(fence)`. [F12, F27, F28, F59, F141, F144]
- Callers of `pm_runtime_get_sync()` must treat `>= 0` and `-EACCES` as success, and on `ret < 0 && ret != -EACCES` must still call `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend()` before returning. `dma_resv_wait_timeout()` and `dma_fence_wait_timeout()` return `long` (`0` -> `-EBUSY`, `> 0` -> `0`, `< 0` -> errno). [F140, F331, F334]

## External Contracts

- Emitting `NVC36F_MEM_OP_D` (`MMU_TLB_INVALIDATE` or `MEMBAR`) into a channel pushbuffer must always be preceded by `NVC36F_MEM_OP_A`, `NVC36F_MEM_OP_B`, and `NVC36F_MEM_OP_C`. In `nouveau_gem_ioctl_pushbuf()`, bound `nr_push`/`nr_buffers`/`nr_relocs` against `NOUVEAU_GEM_MAX_*` before `u_memcpya()`, and reject any `bo_index` or `reloc_bo_index >= req->nr_buffers`. [F21, F177, F179]

## Known Pitfalls

- DRF macros (`nvhw/drf.h`): field definitions must be unparenthesized `HI:LO` token pairs with `0 <= LO <= HI < 64` for single-word `DRF_*`/`NVVAL*`/`NVDEF*`. `NVVAL_GET`/`NVVAL_TEST`/`NVDEF_TEST` shift `(o) >> DRF_LO(drf)` before masking, so `o` must be a 64-bit type when `DRF_LO(drf) >= 32`. Multi-word `NVVAL_MW_GET`/`SET` and `NVDEF_MW_SET` require `MW(hi:lo)` spanning at most two adjacent elements of `o` (where `sizeof((o)[0]) < 8`, e.g. `u32[]`), and `v` in `NVVAL_MW_SET` must not have side effects or read from `(o)[DRF_LW_IDX]`. [F41, F50, F53, F63, F64, F65, F75]
- `nvkm_list_find(p, h, m, c)` and `nvkm_list_find_next()` leave `p` pointing to `container_of(h, ...)` on no match, so callers must assign `p = nvkm_list_find(...)`; `nvkm_list_foreach()` dereferences `p->m.next` in the step expression, so loop bodies must not unlink or free `p` without breaking. [F55, F56]
- `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_push(obj, &ctrl, repc)` takes the address of the parameter pointer (`&ctrl`) and updates it in-place to the reply buffer, whereas `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_wr(obj, ctrl)` and `nvkm_gsp_rm_ctrl_done(obj, ctrl)` take `ctrl` by value. `nouveau_display_fini(dev, suspend, runtime)` takes `(dev, suspend, runtime)`, whereas `disp->fini(dev, runtime, suspend)` takes `runtime` second and `suspend` third. [F71, F349]
- Safe downcasts: before `to_nouveau_fence(fence)`, verify `nouveau_local_fence(fence)` and check `rcu_dereference(f->channel)` under `rcu_read_lock()`; in `nouveau_bo_move_ntfy()`, verify `bo->destroy == nouveau_bo_del_ttm` before accessing `nouveau_bo(bo)`; use `nouveau_for_each_non_mst_connector_iter` before `nouveau_connector(connector)`; when iterating `&uvmm->base` with `drm_gpuvm_for_each_va*`, skip `va == &uvmm->base.kernel_alloc_node` before `uvma_from_va(va)`. [F230, F337, F338, F391]
