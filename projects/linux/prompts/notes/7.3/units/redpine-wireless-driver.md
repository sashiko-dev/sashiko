# REDPINE WIRELESS DRIVER

The Redpine Signals wireless and Bluetooth coexistence driver (`rsi_91x`, `rsi_sdio`, `rsi_usb`) supports RS9113 and RS9116 802.11a/b/g/n devices. It integrates with `mac80211` for Wi-Fi station, AP, and P2P modes, manages firmware loading and device initialization via an internal state machine, schedules WMM/EDCA and coexistence transmit queues, and provides SDIO and USB host bus transport abstractions.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct skb_info` is cast onto `IEEE80211_SKB_CB(skb)->driver_data`, which unions with `control`: it must keep `vif` at `control.vif`'s offset, end before `control.flags`, and copy overlapping fields (like `control.hw_key` into `have_key`) before writing `tx_params` in `rsi_core_xmit()`. On RX in `rsi_fill_rx_status()`, all `rx_params` fields must be copied to locals before `memset(info, 0, ...)` or writing `rxs` (`IEEE80211_SKB_RXCB(skb)`). [F99, F100]
- Untyped context pointers map to distinct types: `ieee80211_hw->priv` and `rsi_91x_usbdev->priv` point to `struct rsi_hw`; `rsi_proto_ops` `priv` callbacks and debugfs `inode->i_private` / `seq->private` point to `struct rsi_common`; and `rx_usb_ctrl_block->data` (`u8 *`) points to `struct rsi_91x_usbdev`, not a packet buffer. [F105]
- USB endpoints (`WLAN_EP = 1`, `BT_EP = 2`, `rx_cb->ep_num`) are 1-based while `bulkout_endpoint_addr`, `bulkin_endpoint_addr`, and `rx_cb` in `struct rsi_91x_usbdev` are 0-based; `rsi_usb_card_write()`, `rsi_rx_urb_submit()`, and `rsi_rx_urb_kill()` must index them with `endpoint - 1` or `ep_num - 1`. [F69]
- `usb_host_intf_ops` leaves `read_pkt`, `master_access_msword`, `reinit_device`, and `ta_reset` as `NULL`; shared code must check `adapter->rsi_host_intf` against `RSI_HOST_INTF_USB` before calling them. [F34]
- SDIO master-mode accesses (`addr | RSI_SD_REQUEST_MASTER`) pass only the low 16 address bits in CMD52/CMD53 and rely on `rsi_sdio_master_access_msword()` for the high 16 bits, which must be updated across 64 KB boundaries and restored to `MISC_CFG_BASE_ADDR` (`0x4105`) for `rsi_sdio_ack_intr()`. [F76]
- `common->coex_cb` and `dev->rx_cb[1].rx_urb` are allocated only when `common->coex_mode > 1`, and `common->bt_adapter` is set only after `BT_CARD_READY_IND`: callers must check both before invoking `rsi_bt_ops`, and every `rsi_bt_ops.detach(common->bt_adapter)` call must immediately set `common->bt_adapter = NULL`. In `rsi_coex_send_pkt()`, `RSI_COEX_Q_COMMON` and `RSI_COEX_Q_WLAN` packets must be sent directly because `rsi_coex_sched_tx_pkts()` only dequeues `RSI_COEX_Q_BT`. [F4, F35, F81]
- RX packet validation: in `rsi_read_pkt()`, `extended_desc` may only be read via `rsi_get_extended_desc()` when `queueno` is `RSI_WIFI_DATA_Q` or `RSI_WIFI_MGMT_Q`, and on USB RX (`rcv_pkt_len == 0`) `offset` must be checked against `RSI_MAX_RX_USB_PKT_SIZE - FRAME_DESC_SZ` before accessing `frame_desc + offset`. `rsi_mgmt_pkt_to_core()` must subtract `pad_bytes` (`msg[4]`) from signed `s32 msg_len` and reject `msg_len <= 0` before copying from `msg + FRAME_DESC_SZ + pad_bytes`. [F66, F67]
- USB buffer bounds: `rsi_usb_reg_read()` and `rsi_usb_reg_write()` must reject `len > RSI_USB_CTRL_BUF_SIZE` before accessing their control buffer, `rsi_usb_read_register_multiple()` and `rsi_usb_write_register_multiple()` must clamp chunks to `RSI_USB_BUF_SIZE`, and `rsi_usb_load_data_master_write()` must keep `block_size <= 256` to fit stack buffer `u8 temp_buf[256]`. [F64, F107]
- TX descriptor layout: `rsi_prepare_mgmt_desc()` and `rsi_prepare_data_desc()` must push `FRAME_DESC_SZ + sizeof(struct rsi_xtended_desc)` plus 64-byte alignment padding (`(unsigned long)skb->data & 0x3f`), storing `header_size - FRAME_DESC_SZ` in `xtend_desc_size` and `header_size` in `tx_params->internal_hdr_size`. `RSI_RATE_*` and `ENABLE_SHORTGI_RATE` (set only when `fixed_rate & 0x100`) belong in `rate_info` (with `vap_id` ORed after all `=` writes), while channel-width flags belong in `bbp_info`. [F68, F78, F79]
- `rsi_rates[12]` holds 2.4 GHz CCK rates at `0..3` and OFDM rates at `4..11`: 5 GHz handling in `rsi_register_rates_channels()` and `rsi_send_auto_rate_request()` must offset by 4 and cap iteration at `ARRAY_SIZE(rsi_rates) - 4`. `configured_mask` packs legacy rates in bits `0..11` and HT MCS0..7 shifted by `ARRAY_SIZE(rsi_rates)`; `rsi_mac80211_set_rate_mask()` must update all bands in `common->rate_config[]` and only use `ffs(bm) - 1` with `fixed_enabled = true` when `hweight32(bm) == 1`. [F51, F73, F97]
- `adapter->ps_state` must transition through `PS_ENABLE_REQ_SENT` (`rsi_enable_ps()`) and `PS_DISABLE_REQ_SENT` (`rsi_disable_ps()`), reaching `PS_ENABLED` or `PS_NONE` only in `rsi_handle_ps_confirm()`. `rsi_mac80211_config()` must not call `rsi_enable_ps()` or `rsi_disable_ps()` when `common->mac_ops_resumed` is true or any VIF in `adapter->vifs[]` is `NL80211_IFTYPE_AP` or `NL80211_IFTYPE_P2P_GO`. [F61, F122]
- `rsi_mac80211_sta_add()` must scan all `common->stations[0..common->max_stations-1]` for an existing `sta->addr` before using `free_index`, and increment `common->num_stations` only when `!sta_exist`. `rsi_mac80211_sta_remove()` and `rsi_mac80211_remove_interface()` must clear `rsta->sta = NULL` and `adapter->vifs[i] = NULL` when decrementing `num_stations` and `sc_nvifs`. [F14, F88]
- Table invariants: `fsm_state[]` in `rsi_stats_read()` must match `enum RSI_FSM_STATES`; `metadata_flash_content[]` and `metadata[]` are indexed by `coex_mode` (`1`, `2`, `4`) and need placeholders at `0` and `3`; `"sdio_stats"` (`rsi_sdio_stats_read()`) must remain last in `dev_debugfs_files[]` so USB (`MAX_DEBUGFS_ENTRIES - 1`) skips it; and `rsi_register_rates_channels()` must `kmemdup()` `rsi_2ghz_channels`/`rsi_5ghz_channels` because `rsi_reg_notify()` mutates `channels[i].flags` in place. [F52, F56, F71, F72]

## Synchronization
- Because `rsi_mac80211_start()` holds `common->mutex` while waiting on `common->wlan_init_completion` during hibernate resume, RX and TX paths that drive firmware init and call `complete(&common->wlan_init_completion)` (`rsi_rx_handler()`, `rsi_read_pkt()`, `rsi_mgmt_pkt_recv()`, `rsi_handle_ta_confirm_type()`, `rsi_core_qos_processor()`) must never acquire `common->mutex`. [F26]
- Every `mac80211_ops` callback in `rsi_91x_mac80211.c` that acquires `common->mutex` must release it on all early return paths. `rsi_core_qos_processor()` must unlock `common->tx_lock` before every `break`, and `rsi_rx_handler()` must unlock `common->rx_lock` before every early `return`. When `coex_mode > 1`, `rsi_send_pkt_to_bus()` takes `common->tx_bus_mutex`, which must be acquired after `common->tx_lock`. [F11, F12, F126]
- Atomic-context restrictions: `rsi_rx_done_handler()` and `rsi_rx_urb_submit()` run in USB URB completion context and must use `GFP_ATOMIC` and defer parsing to `rsi_usb_rx_thread()`; `rsi_enable_ps()`, `rsi_disable_ps()`, and `rsi_send_ps_request()` run under `spin_lock_irqsave(&adapter->ps_lock, flags)` and must only queue frames via `rsi_send_internal_mgmt_frame()`; `rsi_mac80211_tx()` and `rsi_core_xmit()` must only prepare descriptors and enqueue to `common->tx_queue` without acquiring mutexes or doing bus I/O. [F123, F124, F125]
- In `rsi_91x_sdio.c`, SDIO bus configuration and raw command helpers (`sdio_enable_func()`, `sdio_disable_func()`, `sdio_claim_irq()`, `sdio_release_irq()`, `rsi_setupcard()`, `rsi_reset_card()`, `rsi_cmd52readbyte()`, `rsi_cmd52writebyte()`) must be called under `sdio_claim_host()`, balanced by `sdio_release_host()` on all exit paths. [F22]
- Init and PM ordering: in `rsi_91x_init()`, `common->init_done` must stay `false` until `rsi_coex_attach()` completes because `rsi_tx_scheduler_thread()` is already running; in SDIO `rsi_probe()`, `sdev->rx_thread` must be started before `sdio_claim_irq()` and `common->fsm_state` must stay `FSM_FW_NOT_LOADED` until `rsi_hal_device_init()` finishes; and in `rsi_pm_ops`, `rsi_resume()` must be wired to `.resume_noirq` so `rsi_sdio_enable_interrupts()` runs before normal resume callbacks contend for the SDIO bus. [F27, F28, F120]

## Object Lifetimes
- `rsi_91x_deinit()` frees `adapter->rsi_dev` alongside `common` and `adapter`: bus-layer teardown (`rsi_deinit_usb_interface()`, SDIO/USB `rsi_disconnect()`, `rsi_probe()` error paths, and `rsi_coex_detach()`) must first stop receive URBs via `rsi_rx_urb_kill()` (`WLAN_EP` and, when `coex_mode > 1`, `BT_EP`), unwind SDIO IRQ/function/host-claim state, and stop consumer kthreads via `rsi_kill_thread()` before purging `sk_buff_head` queues or freeing structures, and must never `kfree(adapter->rsi_dev)` directly. [F1, F3, F13, F18, F21]
- Internal frame builders (`rsi_send_auto_rate_request()`, `rsi_send_bgscan_probe_req()`, `rsi_send_beacon()`) transfer `skb` ownership to `common->tx_queue` only at `rsi_send_internal_mgmt_frame()` or `skb_queue_tail()`, so they must `dev_kfree_skb(skb)` on earlier errors and always free temporary buffers (`selected_rates`, `probereq_skb`, `mac_bcn`). Driver-generated SKBs queued on `MGMT_SOFT_Q` must set `INTERNAL_MGMT_PKT` in `tx_params->flags` so `rsi_send_mgmt_pkt()` frees them with `dev_kfree_skb()` instead of `rsi_indicate_tx_status()`. [F7, F135]
- In `rsi_rx_urb_submit()`, if `usb_submit_urb()` fails, the newly allocated `skb` in `rx_cb->rx_skb` is not freed by `rsi_rx_done_handler()` and must be freed with `dev_kfree_skb(skb)`. Every function starting `adapter->bl_cmd_timer` via `bl_start_cmd_timer()` must call `bl_stop_cmd_timer(adapter)` on all exit paths. `rsi_mac80211_detach()` must set `adapter->hw = NULL`, free `adapter->sbands[band].channels`, and call `kfree(adapter->dfsentry)` after `rsi_remove_dbgfs()`. [F8, F15, F16]

## API Contracts
- TX SKB ownership: `rsi_core_xmit()` owns the `skb` from `rsi_mac80211_tx()` and frees it via `ieee80211_free_txskb()` at `xmit_fail:`, so `rsi_prepare_mgmt_desc()` and `rsi_prepare_data_desc()` must not free `skb` on error. Once `rsi_core_qos_processor()` dequeues `skb` from `common->tx_queue[q_num]`, `rsi_send_data_pkt()`, `rsi_send_mgmt_pkt()`, and `rsi_send_bt_pkt()` must consume `skb` on both success and failure (via `rsi_indicate_tx_status()` or `dev_kfree_skb()`). [F5, F6]
- All buffers passed to `rsi_sdio_read_register_multiple()`, `rsi_sdio_write_register_multiple()`, `usb_control_msg()`, or `usb_bulk_msg()` must reside in `kmalloc`-backed DMA-capable memory, never on the stack or in `vmalloc` memory (`fw_entry->data`). `rsi_usb_master_reg_read()` must read into a local `u16` and assign to `u32 *value`, never casting `u32 *` to `u16 *`. [F65, F130]
- `rsi_read_pkt()` requires `rcv_pkt_len` (signed `s32`) to be `0` from USB (`rsi_usb_rx_thread()`) so `rcv_pkt_len -= actual_length` goes negative after one frame, and a positive byte count from SDIO. `rsi_set_len_qno()` requires `len` to exclude `FRAME_DESC_SZ` (`skb->len - FRAME_DESC_SZ`). `usb_control_msg()`/`usb_bulk_msg()` take timeouts in ms, whereas `rsi_wait_event()` takes jiffies. [F44, F46, F47]
- Callers of `rsi_set_channel()` must call `rsi_band_check()` first; `rsi_config_wowlan()` requires non-NULL `wowlan` (`WARN_ON(!wowlan)`), so `rsi_shutdown()` must check `hw && hw->wiphy && hw->wiphy->wowlan_config`; and `rsi_find_bulk_in_and_out_endpoints()` must inspect `interface->cur_altsetting` (not `altsetting[0]`) and verify WLAN/BT bulk endpoints exist. [F36, F58, F147]
- `mac80211` contracts: TX EAPOL must be detected via `IEEE80211_TX_CTRL_PORT_CTRL_PROTO` in `control.flags` (and `common->eapol4_confirm` reset to `false` on assoc/reassoc); `rsi_mac80211_set_key()` must set `IEEE80211_KEY_FLAG_GENERATE_IV` on `SET_KEY` and zero `*key` before `rsi_hal_key_config()` on `DISABLE_KEY`; and data queues `q_num < MGMT_SOFT_Q` must use `WME_AC(q_num)` for `ieee80211_stop_queue()` / `ieee80211_wake_queue()`. [F60, F133, F142, F145]

## Error Handling
- Callers of `rsi_usb_reg_read()`, `rsi_usb_reg_write()`, `rsi_usb_master_reg_write()`, and `adapter->host_intf_ops->master_reg_write()` must check `< 0` for errors rather than `!= 0` (USB returns positive byte counts). `adapter->check_hw_queue_status()` returns `QUEUE_NOT_FULL` (`1`), `QUEUE_FULL` (`0`), or `-1` on error, so callers must store it in a signed `int` and check `<= 0`. [F30, F31]
- In multi-step setup/reset functions sharing `status`/`ret` (`rsi_probe()`, `rsi_load_9116_firmware()`, `rsi_reset_card()`), any failure branch after an earlier call set `status`/`ret` to `0` must assign a negative errno before jumping to cleanup; in `rsi_hal_key_config()`, each `rsi_hal_load_key()` call must check `status` rather than returning directly so the trailing WEP `rsi_send_block_unblock_frame()` remains reachable. [F39, F80]

## External Contracts
- `rsi_mac80211_hw_scan_start()` must return `1` when `!vif->cfg.assoc` for software scan fallback, and `rsi_mac80211_resume()` must return `1` when `common->hibernate_resume` is set so `rsi_mac80211_start()` sets `common->reinit_hw = true`, calls `reinit_device()`, and waits on `common->wlan_init_completion` (completed in `rsi_handle_ta_confirm_type()` instead of calling `rsi_mac80211_attach()`). SDIO `rsi_suspend()` and `rsi_freeze()` must call `rsi_set_sdio_pm_caps()` (`MMC_PM_KEEP_POWER`), whereas `rsi_shutdown()` must not. [F32, F119, F121]
- On `BT_CARD_READY_IND` in `rsi_read_pkt()`, `rsi_attach_bt()` may be called immediately only if `common->fsm_state == FSM_MAC_INIT_DONE`; otherwise `common->bt_defer_attach` must be set so `rsi_handle_ta_confirm_type()` attaches BT after Wi-Fi BB/RF init. [F111]
- In `rsi_sdio_host_intf_write_pkt()`, the CMD53 transfer length and lower 12 bits of `address` (`num_blocks * block_size`) must be rounded up to a `dev->tx_blk_size` multiple (`if (len % block_size) num_blocks++;`) before ORing `queueno << 12`. In `load_data_master_write()`, both SDIO and USB require non-zero `adapter->block_size` and must separately write any `instructions_sz % block_size` remainder. [F92, F93]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `rsi_fill_rx_status()`, when `ieee80211_has_protected(hdr->frame_control)` is true, hardware decryption leaves the IV in place: the driver must `memmove` the 802.11 header forward and `skb_pull` 4 bytes (WEP) or 8 bytes (non-WEP, setting `RX_FLAG_MMIC_STRIPPED`), set `RX_FLAG_DECRYPTED | RX_FLAG_IV_STRIPPED`, and re-read `hdr = (struct ieee80211_hdr *)skb->data` before inspecting `hdr` again. [F102, F137]
- Because `rsi_send_internal_mgmt_frame()` sets `DESC_IMMEDIATE_WAKEUP` (`BIT(15)`) in `desc_dword0.len_qno` (bit 7 of `pkt[1]`), functions extracting the 3-bit HAL queue number (`rsi_get_queueno()`, `rsi_usb_host_intf_write_pkt()`) must mask `pkt[1] >> 4` with `0x7`, not `0xf`. [F75]
- For 64-byte alignment of `skb->data`, `dword_align_bytes = (unsigned long)skb->data & 0x3f` is passed directly to `skb_push()`, whereas `rsi_send_beacon()` uses `skb_pull()` and must pass `64 - dword_align_bytes`. In `rsi_get_num_pkts_dequeue()`, `txop` must remain signed `s16` and walking `common->tx_queue[q_num]` via `skb = skb->next` must stop when `skb_queue_len() - pkt_cnt` reaches zero (`sk_buff_head` is circular). [F84, F91, F95]
- When scanning `adapter->vifs[0..RSI_MAX_VIFS-1]`, callers must record matches in a separate variable (`assoc`, `bss`, `sta_vif`) rather than checking the loop cursor `vif` after the loop; and `struct rsi_hw::country` (`char country[2]`) is non-NUL-terminated and must never be passed to `%s` (without `%.2s`) or string functions. [F82, F108]
