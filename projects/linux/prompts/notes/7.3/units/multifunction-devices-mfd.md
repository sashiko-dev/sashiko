# MULTIFUNCTION DEVICES (MFD)

Shared C headers in `include/linux/mfd/` (`sun4i-gpadc.h` through `wm8400.h`, plus `syscon/` and `wcd934x/`) define interfaces between MFD core drivers and child subsystem drivers, including register offsets, bitmasks, regulator/IRQ/module IDs, shared context and platform-data structures, USB wire layouts, and inline helpers/stubs.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- Regulator ID enums/constants must stay in exact sequential lockstep with their driver arrays: `enum tps65218_regulator_id` with `regulators[]` (`tps->strobes`), `enum tps65217_regulator_id` with `regulators[]` and `TPS65217_NUM_REGULATOR` (`pdata->tps65217_init_data[i]` and `tps->strobes`), `enum lm363x_regulator_id` with `lm363x_regulator_desc[]` (`pdev->id`), `TPS65090_REGULATOR_*` (`0..TPS65090_REGULATOR_MAX`) with `tps65090_regulator_desc[]` and `tps65090_matches[]`, and `WM8400_LDO1`..`WM8400_DCDC2` (`0..5`) with `struct wm8400::regulators[6]` and `regulators[]`. [F38, F39, F40, F41, F44]
- `enum twl_module_ids`, `enum twl4030_module_ids`, and `enum twl6030_module_ids` must match `twl4030_map[]` and `twl6030_map[]` in `drivers/mfd/twl-core.c` (`twl_priv->twl_map[mod_no]`). Because both chip-specific enums start at `TWL_MODULE_LAST`, shared TWL4030/TWL6030 code must check `twl_class_is_4030()` or `twl_class_is_6030()` before passing module IDs `>= TWL_MODULE_LAST` to `twl_i2c_read*()`/`twl_i2c_write*()`. [F42, F69]
- Each `struct ucb1x00_dev` is linked simultaneously into `ucb->devs` via `dev_node` and `drv->devs` via `drv_node`; traverse each list with its matching node member, and use `list_for_each_safe` when calling `ucb1x00_remove_dev()`. [F71]
- In `include/linux/mfd/viperboard.h`, `struct vprbrd_i2c_write_hdr` and `struct vprbrd_i2c_write_msg` must remain `__packed` with `sizeof(struct vprbrd_i2c_write_hdr) == 9` and `sizeof(vb->buf) >= VPRBRD_I2C_MSG_LEN` (512), because `vprbrd_i2c_write()` hardcodes 503-byte payload chunks and `vprbrd_i2c_read()` receives up to 512 bytes into `vb->buf`. [F83]
- `IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` in `include/linux/mfd/ti_am335x_tscadc.h` must be computed from maximum hardware step-delay and averaging settings (`STEPCONFIG_MAX_OPENDLY`, `STEPCONFIG_MAX_SAMPLE`, 16x averaging) rather than default delays. [F94]

## Synchronization

- In `struct ti_tscadc_dev`, `reg_lock` protects `reg_se_cache`, `adc_waiting`, `adc_in_use`, and `REG_SE` writes across `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()`, `am335x_tsc_se_set_once()`, `am335x_tsc_se_adc_done()`, and `am335x_tsc_se_clr()`; child drivers must update `REG_SE` through these helpers, and all `reg_lock` acquisitions must disable local IRQs (`spin_lock_irq`/`spin_lock_irqsave`) because hardirq handler `titsc_irq()` calls `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()`. [F11, F113]
- `am335x_tsc_se_set_once()` must only be called from sleepable process context with interrupts enabled (it uses `spin_lock_irq(&tscadc->reg_lock)` and sleeps via `am335x_tscadc_need_adc()` on `reg_se_wait`) and must be paired on every exit path with `am335x_tsc_se_adc_done()` because it sets `adc_in_use = true`, suppressing `REG_SE` writes in `am335x_tsc_se_set_cache()` until `am335x_tsc_se_adc_done()` clears `adc_in_use`. [F12, F112]
- In `struct twl6040`, `power_count`, `pll`, `sysclk_rate`, `mclk_rate`, and regcache state must be updated in lockstep under `twl6040->mutex` (`twl6040_power()` syncs regcache and sets `LPPLL` on `0 -> 1`, and marks regcache dirty/cache-only while zeroing `sysclk_rate` and `HPPLL` `mclk_rate` on `1 -> 0`). [F51]
- Because `twl6040->mutex` nests outside `regmap`'s lock and is held while `twl6040_power_up_automatic()` waits on `twl6040->ready`, `twl6040->ready` and `twl6040->irq_ready` (`twl6040_readyint_handler`) must be initialized and registered before child MFD devices or thermal IRQs can invoke `twl6040_power(twl6040, 1)`, and `twl6040_readyint_handler()` must never acquire `twl6040->mutex`. [F18, F119]
- `tc3589x->lock` serializes read-modify-write updates only inside `tc3589x_set_bits()` (not `tc3589x_reg_read()`/`tc3589x_reg_write()`), and `tps->tps_lock` serializes password-protected writes (`TPS65218_REG_PASSWORD`) only inside `tps65218_set_bits()`/`tps65218_clear_bits()` (not `tps65218_reg_write()`). Child drivers must use `tc3589x_set_bits()` for shared registers (like `TC3589x_RSTCTRL`) and hold `tps->tps_lock` or use `tps65218_set_bits()`/`tps65218_clear_bits()` for concurrent `TPS65218_PROTECT_L1` writes. [F19, F20]
- In `struct tps65217`, `irq_lock` is the `irq_chip` bus lock protecting `tps->irq_mask` updates in `tps65217_irq_enable()`/`tps65217_irq_disable()` and syncing `tps->irq_mask` to `TPS65217_REG_INT` on unlock; do not modify `tps->irq_mask` or `TPS65217_REG_INT` mask bits outside `tps->irq_lock`. [F21]
- In `struct ucb1x00`, `io_lock` (protecting `io_dir`, `io_out`, `UCB_IO_DIR`, `UCB_IO_DATA`), `irq_lock` (`raw_spinlock_t` protecting `irq_mask`, `irq_ris_enbl`, `irq_fal_enbl`, `irq_wake`, `UCB_IE_RIS`, `UCB_IE_FAL`), and `adc_mutex` are private to `ucb1x00-core.c` (`io_lock`/`irq_lock` nest outside `ucb->mcp->lock`). Sub-drivers must use `ucb1x00_io_set_dir()`, `ucb1x00_io_write()`, or `ucb->gpio` callbacks instead of raw `UCB_IO_*` writes, and bracket `ucb1x00_adc_read()` with `ucb1x00_adc_enable()`/`ucb1x00_adc_disable()` in process context. [F4, F15, F48, F116]
- All syscon lookup/registration functions in `include/linux/mfd/syscon.h`, inline register helpers in `include/linux/mfd/twl.h` (`twl_i2c_write_u8`, `twl_i2c_read_u8`, `twl_i2c_write_u16`, `twl_i2c_read_u16`) and `include/linux/mfd/tps65090.h` (`tps65090_write`, `tps65090_read`, `tps65090_set_bits`, `tps65090_clr_bits`), and all `tps65010_*`/`tps65013_*` functions in `include/linux/mfd/tps65010.h` except `tps65010_set_vbus_draw()` sleep on mutexes, I2C/SMBus transfers, or `GFP_KERNEL` allocations and must only be called from sleepable process context. [F118, F120, F121]

## Object Lifetimes

- `twl6040_power()` reference-counts chip power via `twl6040->power_count` under `twl6040->mutex` (returning `-EPERM` if called with `on == 0` when `power_count == 0`), and `twl4030_audio_enable_resource()`/`twl4030_audio_disable_resource()` reference-count `id < TWL4030_AUDIO_RES_MAX` under `audio->mutex` (returning `-EPERM` if disabled when `request_count == 0`); callers must pair every successful enable with a disable. [F6, F109]
- `wm8400_register_regulator()` writes `initdata->driver_data = wm8400` and stores `initdata` directly as `wm8400->regulators[reg].dev.platform_data` without copying; callers must pass a writable, non-stack `struct regulator_init_data *` and `reg` in `0..5` (`WM8400_LDO1`..`WM8400_DCDC2`). [F10]
- `struct ucb1x00` embeds a reference-counted `struct device dev`; once `device_initialize(&ucb->dev)` is called, `ucb` must be freed only via `put_device()`/`device_unregister()` invoking `ucb1x00_release()`, never by direct `kfree(ucb)` or `devm_kzalloc()`. [F97]

## API Contracts

- Sub-device drivers calling `tps65090_write()`, `tps65090_read()`, `tps65090_set_bits()`, or `tps65090_clr_bits()`, or any `tps6586x_*()` helper, must pass the MFD parent device (`pdev->dev.parent`), never the child `&pdev->dev`, because those helpers cast `dev_get_drvdata(dev)` (and `to_i2c_client(dev)` for TPS6586x) to the parent MFD struct. [F85, F86]
- `ti_tscadc_dev_get()` dereferences `p->dev.platform_data` as `struct ti_tscadc_dev **` because `mfd_add_devices()` `kmemdup`s `cell->platform_data`; MFD cells for `struct ti_tscadc_dev` must set `cell->platform_data = &tscadc` and `cell->pdata_size = sizeof(tscadc)`. [F50]
- `classdev_to_ucb1x00(cd)` uses `container_of(cd, struct ucb1x00, dev)` and may only be called on the `ucb1x00_class` device `&ucb->dev`, never on `mcp->attached_device` or a child `platform_device`'s `&pdev->dev`. `struct ucb1x00_driver` passed to `ucb1x00_register_driver()` must be mutable (not `const`) with non-NULL `add` and `remove` callbacks. [F84, F128]
- Callers of `ucb1x00_reg_read()`, `ucb1x00_reg_write()`, `ucb1x00_io_read()`, `ucb1x00_io_write()`, and `ucb1x00_io_set_dir()` must enable the SIB clock via `ucb1x00_enable()` (or `ucb1x00_adc_enable()`) beforehand and pair each `ucb1x00_enable()` with `ucb1x00_disable()`. `ucb1x00_io_set_dir(ucb, in, out)` and `ucb1x00_io_write(ucb, set, clear)` take `UCB_IO_0`..`UCB_IO_9` bitmasks (not pin indices), with `in` overriding `out` and `clear` overriding `set`. [F13, F35]
- In `include/linux/mfd/syscon.h`, every `#ifdef CONFIG_MFD_SYSCON` declaration must have a matching `#else` stub (`syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_optional()` returns `NULL`, other `struct regmap *` lookup stubs return `ERR_PTR(-ENOTSUPP)`, and `of_syscon_register_regmap()` returns `-EOPNOTSUPP`). Use `syscon_node_to_regmap()` instead of `device_node_to_regmap()` when a DT node has a custom regmap driver or managed clocks/resets (`device_node_to_regmap()` skips clock/reset handling). [F45, F88]
- `atmel_hsmc_get_reg_layout()` returns `NULL` for legacy SMC, a `const struct atmel_hsmc_reg_layout *` for HSMC, or `ERR_PTR(-EINVAL)` on an unmatched node; check `IS_ERR()` for errors and never pass a `NULL` layout to `atmel_hsmc_cs_conf_apply()`/`atmel_hsmc_cs_conf_get()`. Post-SAMA5 HSMC controllers must use layout-parameterized `ATMEL_HSMC_*` macros and `atmel_hsmc_cs_conf_*()` (stride `0x14`, which read/write `conf->timings`), whereas legacy SMC uses `atmel_smc_cs_conf_*()` (stride `0x10`, ignoring `conf->timings`). [F24, F52, F90]
- `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_timing()`, `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_setup()`, `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_pulse()`, and `atmel_smc_cs_conf_set_cycle()` update only individual bitfields of `struct atmel_smc_cs_conf` (which must first be initialized with `atmel_smc_cs_conf_init()` or `atmel_*smc_cs_conf_get()`) and require `shift` to be one of the `ATMEL_HSMC_TIMINGS_*_SHIFT` or `ATMEL_SMC_*_SHIFT` constants (`set_cycle` only accepts `ATMEL_SMC_NWE_SHIFT` or `ATMEL_SMC_NRD_SHIFT`). [F25, F77]
- `tc3589x_set_bits(tc3589x, reg, mask, val)` computes `ret &= ~mask; ret |= val;` without masking `val` by `mask` (unlike `tps65217_set_bits()`/`tps65218_set_bits()`), so callers must ensure `val` has no set bits outside `mask`. [F32]
- `tps65217_reg_write()`, `tps65217_set_bits()`, and `tps65217_clear_bits()` accept `level` in `{TPS65217_PROTECT_NONE, TPS65217_PROTECT_L1, TPS65217_PROTECT_L2}`, whereas `tps65218_reg_write()`, `tps65218_set_bits()`, and `tps65218_clear_bits()` accept only `{TPS65218_PROTECT_NONE, TPS65218_PROTECT_L1}` and return `-EINVAL` otherwise. [F33]
- `twl_i2c_write_u16()` and `twl_i2c_read_u16()` convert between CPU endianness and `__le16` internally, so callers must pass host-endian `u16` values (while multi-byte `twl_i2c_read()`/`twl_i2c_write()` callers must handle endianness themselves). [F55]
- In `include/linux/mfd/twl.h`, `struct twl4030_resconfig` arrays in `struct twl4030_power_data` use 1-based resource IDs `RES_VAUX1` (1) through `RES_MAIN_REF` (`TOTAL_RESOURCES`, 28), must end with a `{ 0 }` sentinel (`resource == 0`), and must set `devgroup`, `type`, `type2`, `remap_off`, or `remap_sleep` to `TWL4030_RESCONFIG_UNDEF` (`(u8)-1`) to preserve hardware defaults (`0` overwrites the field with `0`). [F57, F58]
- `tps65010_set_gpio_out_value()` and `tps65010_set_led()` require 1-based indices (`GPIO1..GPIO4` = `1..4`, `LED1..LED2` = `1..2`), whereas `struct tps65010_board.outmask` and `gpio_chip` callbacks index `GPIO1..GPIO4` at 0-based offsets `0..3` and `LED1..LED2` at `4..5`. `struct tps65010_board` must not specify a static GPIO base, and its `setup`/`teardown` callbacks must take `(struct i2c_client *client, struct gpio_chip *gc)`. [F61, F125]

## Error Handling

- `device_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_node_to_regmap()`, `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_compatible()`, `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle()`, and `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_args()` return `ERR_PTR()` on failure and never `NULL` (check `IS_ERR()`), whereas `syscon_regmap_lookup_by_phandle_optional()` returns `NULL` when the property is absent or `!CONFIG_MFD_SYSCON` and `ERR_PTR()` on other errors (callers must check both `IS_ERR()` and `NULL`). [F22, F73]
- `twl_i2c_read_u16()` unconditionally overwrites `*val = le16_to_cpu(value)` with uninitialized stack memory when `twl_i2c_read()` fails; callers must check the return code before using `*val` and cannot rely on a pre-initialized default in `*val`. [F26]
- `twl6040_reg_read()`, `twl6040_get_vibralr_status()`, `tc3589x_reg_read()`, `twl4030_audio_enable_resource()`, `twl4030_audio_disable_resource()`, `twl_get_hfclk_rate()`, and `twl6040_get_pll()` (which returns `0` for `TWL6040_SYSCLK_SEL_LPPLL`, `1` for `HPPLL`, or `-ENODEV`) return non-negative values on success or negative error codes on failure; store results in a signed `int` and test `< 0` before narrowing to unsigned or using in arithmetic. [F27, F28]

## External Contracts

- In `include/linux/mfd/viperboard.h`, the `u16` fields in `__packed` USB wire structs `struct vprbrd_i2c_write_hdr` (`addr`, `spi`), `struct vprbrd_i2c_read_hdr` (`addr`, `tf1`, `tf2`), and `struct vprbrd_i2c_addr_msg` (`len`) lie at odd byte offsets and must be encoded with `cpu_to_le16()` before USB transmission. [F54]

## Known Pitfalls

- `tps65090_set_bits()` and `tps65090_clr_bits()` take a 0-based bit number (`uint8_t bit_num`) and compute `BIT(bit_num)` internally, unlike `tps6586x_set_bits()`, `twl6040_set_bits()`, `tps65217_set_bits()`, `tps65218_set_bits()`, and `tc3589x_set_bits()` which take pre-shifted bitmasks. `tps6586x_update(dev, reg, val, mask)` takes `val` before `mask`—opposite to `regmap_update_bits()`, `tc3589x_set_bits()`, `tps65217_set_bits()`, and `tps65218_set_bits()`. [F30, F31]
- In `include/linux/mfd/ti_am335x_tscadc.h`, `REG_STEPCONFIG(n)` and `REG_STEPDELAY(n)` use 0-based step indices `0 <= n < TOTAL_STEPS`, whereas `REG_SE` (`reg_se_cache`) reserves bit 0 for `CHARGE_STEP` and addresses step `n` at `BIT(n + 1)`. [F60]
- `ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF(x)` (`include/linux/mfd/syscon/atmel-smc.h`) computes `(((x) - 1) << 16)` without masking; clamp `x` to `[ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF_MIN, ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDF_MAX]` (`1..16`) to avoid underflow on `0` or spilling into `ATMEL_SMC_MODE_TDFMODE_OPTIMIZED` (`BIT(20)`). [F59]
- Both `SUN4I_GPADC_CTRL1_ADC_CHAN_SELECT(x)` (`GENMASK(2, 0) & (x)`) and `SUN6I_GPADC_CTRL1_ADC_CHAN_SELECT(x)` (`GENMASK(3, 0) & BIT(x)`) take a 0-based channel number `x` (never `BIT(x)`), and must be matched to the target SoC variant. [F66]
- `MCLK_DIR(x)` in `include/linux/mfd/syscon/imx6q-iomuxc-gpr.h` expects a 1-based SAI index (`1` for SAI1, `2` for SAI2), maps any other value (including `0`) to `IMX6UL_GPR1_SAI3_MCLK_DIR`, and evaluates `x` without parentheses. Parameterized `WCD934X_*` register-offset macros in `include/linux/mfd/wcd934x/registers.h` also multiply their argument without parentheses; do not pass unparenthesized expressions. [F68, F81]
- In `include/linux/mfd/tps6594.h`, register addresses encode `(page << 8) | offset` (`TPS6594_REG_TO_PAGE(reg)` = `(reg) >> 8`), and all `TPS6594_REG_BUCKX_*`, `TPS6594_REG_LDOX_*`, `TPS6594_REG_GPIOX_*`, `TPS6594_BIT_BUCKX_*`, `TPS6594_BIT_LDOX_*`, and `TPS6594_BIT_GPIOX_*` macros require 0-based instance indices (`buck_inst` `0..4`, `ldo_inst` `0..3`, `gpio_inst` `0..10`) despite 1-based hardware rail/pin names. [F78, F106]
- In `include/linux/mfd/tps68470.h`, `TPS68470_CLK_SRC_SHIFT` (`GENMASK(2, 0)` = 7) and `TPS68470_OSC_EXT_CAP_SHIFT` (`BIT(2)` = 4) are used as shift counts (`<<`) when writing `TPS68470_REG_PLLCTL`, not as bitfield masks. [F80]
- On TWL6040 silicon revision `<= TWL6040_REV_ES1_0` (`twl6040_get_revid(twl6040) <= TWL6040_REV_ES1_0`), automatic power-up via the `audpwron` GPIO is broken by hardware errata and must not be enabled; fall back to manual register power-up. [F101]
- In `include/linux/mfd/tps65219.h`, `TPS65214`, `TPS65215`, and `TPS65219` reuse the same register offsets and bit positions for different LDOs (e.g., `0x05` is LDO3 on TPS65219, LDO2 on TPS65215, and LDO1 on TPS65214); sub-drivers must branch on `enum pmic_id` before indexing LDO registers or IRQs. [F110]
