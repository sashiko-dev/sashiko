# CONTROL GROUP - MEMORY RESOURCE CONTROLLER (MEMCG)

The Memory Resource Controller (memcg) implements hierarchical memory, swap, zswap, slab/kernel object, and socket memory accounting and limiting for cgroup v1 and v2. It tracks ownership of user folios (`folio->memcg_data`), kernel/slab allocations via reparentable `obj_cgroup` buckets, and swap entries via `swap_cgroup` maps, while maintaining per-CPU charge stocks (`memcg_stock`, `obj_stock`) and hierarchical statistics flushed via cgroup rstat. It integrates with the page allocator, reclaim (`lruvec`, `shrinker_info`), OOM killer, and cgroup writeback to enforce min/low protection and high/max limits.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `root_mem_cgroup` does not account charges in `memory` or `memsw` (`try_charge()`, `uncharge_folio()`, `drain_obj_stock()`, and `refill_stock()` exclude root), so `mem_cgroup_usage()` must compute root usage from `global_node_page_state()` and swap counters rather than `memcg->memory` or `memcg->memsw`. Broken by: calling `refill_stock()` or `memcg_uncharge()` on `root_mem_cgroup`, or reading `root_mem_cgroup->memory`/`memsw`. [F86]
- In `struct mem_cgroup`, `swap` (v2 only) and `memsw` (v1 only) share an anonymous `union`, and `tcpmem_pressure` (v1) is separate from `socket_pressure` (v2); check `cgroup_subsys_on_dfl(memory_cgrp_subsys)` (or `do_memsw_account()`) before selecting v1 vs v2 fields, and under v1 `mem_cgroup_resize_max()` must enforce `memcg->memory.max <= memcg->memsw.max` under `memcg_max_mutex` to prevent underflow in `mem_cgroup_get_max()`. [F66, F94]
- Modifying `min` or `low` on a `struct page_counter` (including zeroing in `mem_cgroup_css_offline()` and `mem_cgroup_css_reset()`) must use `page_counter_set_min()`/`page_counter_set_low()` so `propagate_protected_usage()` updates ancestor usage; updating `memcg->memory.high` or `max` in `memory_high_write()`, `memory_max_write()`, or `mem_cgroup_css_reset()` must call `memcg_wb_domain_size_changed(memcg)`. [F79, F81]
- `mem_cgroup_calculate_protection()` and `page_counter_calculate_protection()` must only run during a top-down walk from `root` (since child `emin`/`elow` depend on `parent->emin`/`elow`) and skip updating `root == counter`, so `mem_cgroup_protection()`, `mem_cgroup_below_low()`, and `mem_cgroup_below_min()` (via `mem_cgroup_unprotected()`) must treat `memcg == target` as unprotected; in `effective_protection()`, verify all three inequalities (`parent_effective > siblings_protected`, `parent_usage > siblings_protected`, and `usage > protected`) before subtracting and dividing lockless reads. [F71, F108, F129]
- `mem_cgroup_zswap_writeback_enabled()` must walk up `parent_mem_cgroup(memcg)` and check `READ_ONCE(memcg->zswap_writeback)` at every ancestor rather than only reading the leaf `memcg->zswap_writeback` or relying on inheritance at `mem_cgroup_css_alloc()` time. [F72]
- `task->objcg` tags its pointer with `CURRENT_OBJCG_UPDATE_FLAG` on `mem_cgroup_fork()` and `mem_cgroup_kmem_attach()`, so `current_objcg_update()` and `mem_cgroup_exit()` must mask off `~CURRENT_OBJCG_UPDATE_FLAG` before `obj_cgroup_put()`, and `current_objcg_update()` must install the new pointer via `try_cmpxchg()`. [F84]
- Slab object memory accounting in `__memcg_slab_post_alloc_hook()` and `__memcg_slab_free_hook()` must charge and uncharge `obj_full_size(s)` (`s->size + sizeof(struct obj_cgroup *)`), rather than raw `s->size` or `sizeof(struct obj_cgroup)`. [F141]

## Synchronization

- `active_memcg()` and `current_obj_cgroup()` must test `in_task()` (not `in_interrupt()`) to choose between task-scoped state (`current->active_memcg`, `current->objcg`) and per-CPU `int_active_memcg`; in `try_charge_memcg()` and `mem_cgroup_sk_alloc()`, `!in_task()` charges/allocations must not attribute `current->memcg_nr_pages_over_high` or `sk->sk_memcg` to interrupted `current`, using `schedule_work(&memcg->high_work)` instead. [F149, F150]
- In `try_charge_memcg()`, synchronous `__mem_cgroup_handle_over_high()` must only run when `!(current->flags & PF_MEMALLOC)` and `gfpflags_allow_blocking(gfp_mask)` (forwarding `gfp_mask` to `reclaim_high()`); when `!gfpflags_allow_spinning(gfp_mask)`, cap `batch` to `nr_pages` to avoid `refill_stock()` and pass `allow_spinning = false` to `__memcg_memory_event()` to skip `cgroup_file_notify()`. [F148, F151]
- Per-CPU stocks `memcg_stock` and `obj_stock` use `local_trylock_t`: charge/uncharge paths (`consume_stock`, `refill_stock`, `consume_obj_stock`, `refill_obj_stock`) must use `local_trylock()` with a fallback, while `local_lock()` is restricted to `in_task()` workqueue drainers (`drain_local_memcg_stock`, `drain_local_obj_stock`). [F154]
- When `CONFIG_MEMCG_NMI_SAFETY_REQUIRES_ATOMIC` is enabled and `in_nmi()` is true, `account_slab_nmi_safe()` and `account_kmem_nmi_safe()` must not call `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()` or `mod_memcg_state()`, and must instead update `pn->slab_reclaimable`, `pn->slab_unreclaimable`, or `memcg->kmem_stat` atomically and call `css_rstat_updated()`. [F153]
- `objcg_lock`, `mctz->lock` (soft-limit RB-tree lock), and `memcg->event_list_lock` are acquired from contexts with interrupts disabled, so process-context callers must use `spin_lock_irq()`/`spin_lock_irqsave()`; cgroup v1 `memcg->events_percpu` updates (`memcg1_charge_statistics()`, `memcg1_check_events()`) have no lock and require local IRQs disabled. [F155, F157]
- `page_counter_try_charge()` must increment `c->usage` via `atomic_long_add_return()` (full barrier) before reading `c->max`, pairing with `xchg(&counter->max, nr_pages)` flanked by `page_counter_read()` in `page_counter_set_max()`; `page_counter_reset_watermark()` must write `local_watermark` before `watermark`, and charge paths must `WRITE_ONCE(c->local_watermark)` before testing `new > READ_ONCE(c->watermark)`. [F45, F49]
- Shared reclaim iterators (`mz->iter.position`) do not hold a CSS reference across `mem_cgroup_iter()` calls: `mem_cgroup_iter()` must load `iter->position` under `rcu_read_lock()`, pin the next CSS with `css_tryget()`, and update via `cmpxchg(&iter->position, pos, next) == pos` before releasing `prev`, while `mem_cgroup_css_released()` must call `invalidate_reclaim_iterators()` to `cmpxchg(&iter->position, dead_memcg, NULL)` up to `root_mem_cgroup`. [F47, F77]
- Under `mctz->lock`, `memcg1_update_tree()` must remove `mz` (`__mem_cgroup_remove_exceeded()`) before mutating `mz->usage_in_excess` and re-inserting (`__mem_cgroup_insert_exceeded()`); `__mem_cgroup_largest_soft_limit_node()` must remove `mz`, verify `soft_limit_excess()`, and pin `css_tryget(&mz->memcg->css)` under `mctz->lock` before unlocked reclaim in `memcg1_soft_limit_reclaim()`, which must recalculate `soft_limit_excess()` after relocking. [F43, F82]
- Under `memcg->thresholds_lock` in `__mem_cgroup_usage_register_event()` and `__mem_cgroup_usage_unregister_event()`, call `__mem_cgroup_threshold()` first, set `new->size` before accessing `new->entries` (`__counted_by(size)`), sort `new->entries` ascending, and set signed `new->current_threshold` before `rcu_assign_pointer(thresholds->primary, new)` and `synchronize_rcu()`, preserving `thresholds->spare` (`>= primary->size - 1`). [F28, F83, F101, F139]
- `memcg->socket_pressure` (`u64` jiffies) must be accessed via `mem_cgroup_set_socket_pressure()` and `mem_cgroup_get_socket_pressure()` (`socket_pressure_seqlock` on 32-bit); `mem_cgroup_print_oom_meminfo()` requires `oom_lock` held (`lockdep_assert_held(&oom_lock)`) and must format into its `static char buf[SEQ_BUF_SIZE]` via `memory_stat_format()` without dynamic allocation. [F131, F152]
- `cond_resched()` must be called unconditionally after every `fn(task, arg)` in `mem_cgroup_scan_tasks()`'s `css_task_iter_next()` loop, and after `mem_cgroup_out_of_memory()` on each OOM retry iteration in `memory_max_write()`. [F109, F110]

## Object Lifetimes

- `obj_cgroup_memcg()` and helpers that delegate to it (`folio_memcg()` on a kmem folio, `folio_memcg_check()`, `mem_cgroup_from_slab_obj()`) require `rcu_read_lock()` or `cgroup_mutex`, and acquiring a CSS reference from `objcg` must loop with `css_tryget()` under RCU as in `get_mem_cgroup_from_objcg()`. [F163]
- When uncharging a folio (`uncharge_folio()`, `memcg1_swapout()`), kmem page (`__memcg_kmem_uncharge_page()`), or slab object (`__memcg_slab_free_hook()`), clear `folio->memcg_data`, `page->memcg_data`, or `obj_exts[off].objcg` to `0`/`NULL` before or when dropping the reference; `drain_stock()` and `drain_obj_stock()` must uncharge, clear `cached[i]`/`cached_objcg`, and put the held reference, and `memcg_hotplug_cpu_dead()` must drain both stocks. [F7, F18]
- `mem_cgroup_sk_inherit(sk, newsk)` must return early on self-assignment (`sk->sk_memcg == newsk->sk_memcg`), call `mem_cgroup_sk_free(newsk)` to drop any prior CSS reference on `newsk->sk_memcg`, and call `css_get(&memcg->css)` if `sk->sk_memcg` is non-NULL before assigning `newsk->sk_memcg = sk->sk_memcg`. [F10]
- In `mem_cgroup_css_alloc()`, wrap `mem_cgroup_alloc(parent)` in `old_memcg = set_active_memcg(parent)` and restore `set_active_memcg(old_memcg)` immediately; in `mem_cgroup_css_free()`, wait on `wb_wait_for_completion(&memcg->cgwb_frn[i].done)`, clean up `vmpressure`, `cancel_work_sync(&memcg->high_work)`, call `memcg1_remove_from_trees()` and `free_shrinker_info()`, and decrement `memcg_sockets_enabled_key`/`memcg_bpf_enabled_key` under the exact conditions they were incremented (`memcg1_tcpmem_active(memcg)` on v1) before `mem_cgroup_free(memcg)`. [F15, F16, F17, F85]

## API Contracts

- `get_mem_cgroup_from_mm()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_current()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_folio()`, `get_mem_cgroup_from_objcg()`, and `mem_cgroup_get_oom_group()` return an owned CSS reference (or `root_mem_cgroup`/`NULL`) that callers must release via `css_put()`/`mem_cgroup_put()`; in `get_mem_cgroup_from_mm(mm)`, only consult `active_memcg()` and `current->mm` when `mm` is `NULL`, and otherwise loop on `css_tryget(&memcg->css)` reading `mm->owner` under `rcu_read_lock()`. [F3, F125]
- `current_obj_cgroup()` returns a borrowed `struct obj_cgroup *` valid only within the current task or `set_active_memcg()` scope; storing the pointer beyond that scope (such as in `slab_obj_exts`, `page->memcg_data`, or returning it from `get_obj_cgroup_from_current()`) requires acquiring a reference with `obj_cgroup_get()`. [F4]
- Return conventions and units: `page_counter_try_charge()` returns `bool` (`true` on success, `false` on failure while setting `*fail`), and `mem_cgroup_sk_charge()`/`memcg1_charge_skmem()` take `unsigned int nr_pages` and return `bool`; whereas `try_charge_memcg()`, `try_charge()`, `page_counter_set_max()`, `obj_cgroup_charge()` (takes byte `size_t`), and `mem_cgroup_charge()` (takes `struct folio *`) return `0` on success or negative errno on failure. [F54, F55]
- `memcg_page_state()` and `lruvec_page_state()` return raw counters in `memcg_page_state_unit(item)` units (bytes for `*_B` items, `SZ_1K` for `NR_KERNEL_STACK_KB`, pages otherwise), whereas `memcg_page_state_output()` and `lruvec_page_state_output()` multiply by `memcg_page_state_output_unit(item)`. Broken by: multiplying a `*_B` item from `memcg_page_state()` by `PAGE_SIZE`, or treating `NR_KERNEL_STACK_KB` as pages. [F65]
- For swapin folios, callers must use `mem_cgroup_swapin_charge_folio()` (which checks `lookup_swap_cgroup_id(entry)` before `get_mem_cgroup_from_mm(mm)`) rather than `mem_cgroup_charge()`; `mem_cgroup_migrate(old, new)` and `mem_cgroup_replace_folio(old, new)` require both folios locked, matching `folio_test_anon()`, and equal `folio_nr_pages()`, with `!folio_test_lru(old)` for `mem_cgroup_migrate()`. [F177, F178]
- Because `mem_cgroup_lruvec()` returns `&pgdat->__lruvec` when `mem_cgroup_disabled()` is true, any function downcasting `struct lruvec *` via `container_of(lruvec, struct mem_cgroup_per_node, lruvec)` must first verify `!mem_cgroup_disabled()`; resolving `struct mem_cgroup_per_node *` to `struct mem_cgroup *` must read `pn->memcg` (never `container_of`, as `nodeinfo[nid]` holds dynamically allocated pointers for `0 <= nid < nr_node_ids`). [F105, F144, F145]

## Error Handling

- In `try_charge_memcg()`, when `do_memsw_account()` is enabled, `&memcg->memsw` is charged before `&memcg->memory`; if `page_counter_try_charge(&memcg->memory, batch, &counter)` fails, `page_counter_uncharge(&memcg->memsw, batch)` must roll back `memsw` immediately; inside `page_counter_try_charge()`, the failing ancestor `c` already runs `atomic_long_sub(nr_pages, &c->usage)`, so `failed:` must only roll back `for (c = counter; c != *fail; c = c->parent)`. [F21, F117]
- Every stat or VM event item passed to `memcg_page_state()`, `lruvec_page_state()`, `mod_memcg_state()`, `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()`, `count_memcg_events()`, or `memcg_events()` must be registered in `memcg_node_stat_items[]`, `memcg_stat_items[]`, or `memcg_vm_event_stat[]`, and those helpers must check `BAD_STAT_IDX(i)` (`U8_MAX` sentinel in `mem_cgroup_stats_index[]` and `mem_cgroup_events_index[]`) before indexing `state[]` or `events[]`. [F68, F100]

## Known Pitfalls

- Because `mem_cgroup_stats_index[]` maps `memcg_stat_items` to indices `>= NR_MEMCG_NODE_STAT_ITEMS` while `struct lruvec_stats` has size `NR_MEMCG_NODE_STAT_ITEMS`, `lruvec_page_state()`, `lruvec_page_state_local()`, and `mod_memcg_lruvec_state()` must never be passed items `>= NR_VM_NODE_STAT_ITEMS`; all rstat readers (`memcg_page_state*`, `lruvec_page_state*`) must read into a signed `long` and clamp `< 0` to `0` under `CONFIG_SMP`. [F99, F130]
- In `try_charge_memcg()`, when `page_counter_try_charge()` fails at ancestor `counter`, downcasting `counter` with `mem_cgroup_from_counter(counter, member)` must specify the exact charged member (`memory` vs `memsw`), and reclaim, stock draining, margin checks, OOM, and `MEMCG_MAX` events must target `mem_over_limit`, whereas force-charging and stock refilling must target `memcg`. [F112, F146]
- In `__mem_cgroup_handle_over_high()`, `retry_reclaim` must immediately jump to `out` (releasing `memcg` via `css_put`) when `task_is_dying()` is true, and must switch `reclaim_high()`'s target from `nr_pages` on the first pass to `SWAP_CLUSTER_MAX` when `in_retry` is true. [F111]
- Guard against unsigned underflow/overflow: clamp or compare usage before subtracting from limits in `mem_cgroup_wb_stats()`, `mem_cgroup_margin()`, and `soft_limit_excess()`, and use `min_t(long, ...)` in `mem_cgroup_get_nr_swap_pages()`; cast `unsigned long` page counts to `u64` before `* PAGE_SIZE` or `K()`, and in `calculate_overage()` clamp `high = max(high, 1UL)` and compute `(u64)(usage - high) << MEMCG_DELAY_PRECISION_SHIFT` via `div64_u64()`. [F127, F128, F137, F138]
