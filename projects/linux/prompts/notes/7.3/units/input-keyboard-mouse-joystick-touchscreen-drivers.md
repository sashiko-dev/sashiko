# INPUT (KEYBOARD, MOUSE, JOYSTICK, TOUCHSCREEN) DRIVERS

This component comprises joystick, gamepad, and keyboard input drivers spanning USB, Serio (PS/2 and RS232), SPI, I2C, Gameport, Parport, Maple bus, IIO ADC, and platform MMIO interfaces. Key drivers include `iforce`, `xpad`, `applespi`, `atkbd`, and platform keypad/joystick drivers translating hardware interrupts, DMA/URB transfers, and polled bus frames into Linux input events while managing LEDs, force-feedback memory/motors, and power states.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `struct ps2dev ps2dev` must remain at offset 0 of `struct atkbd` (since `ps2_interrupt()` treats `serio_get_drvdata()` as `struct ps2dev *` and `atkbd_from_serio()` relies on it), and `struct iforce iforce` must remain at offset 0 of `struct iforce_serio` (since `iforce_serio_write_wakeup()` casts `serio_get_drvdata()` to `struct iforce *`). [F37, F120]
- Embedded DMA transfer buffers in heap-allocated structs (`data_in`/`data_out` in `struct iforce_usb` and `sendbuf`/`response` in `struct psxpad`) must be `____cacheline_aligned`. [F157]
- Every `xpad_device[]` entry must initialize all five positional fields (`idVendor`, `idProduct`, `name`, `mapping`, `xtype`) sorted by VID/PID before `{ 0x0000, 0x0000 }`. `iforce_device`, `xpad_device`, and `tmdc_models` must end with a `0`-ID fallback entry having non-NULL pointer fields (`name`, `btn`/`abs`/`ff`, `axes`/`buttons`), as post-loop cursors are dereferenced. Input code arrays in `iforce-main.c`, `xpad.c`, and `interact.c` must terminate with `-1` and iterate with `>= 0` (`ABS_X` is `0`). [F98, F99, F128]
- Under `xpad->odata_lock`, `xpad_prepare_next_init_packet()` and `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` must set `xpad->irq_out->transfer_buffer_length` to the packet length copied into `xpad->odata`, and `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` must clear `packet->pending = false`. Because `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()` pre-increments `xpad->last_out_packet`, callers prioritizing `out_packets[XPAD_OUT_CMD_IDX]` must set `xpad->last_out_packet = -1` (not `0`). [F63, F87]
- In `xpad.c`, report fields gated by `xpad->mapping` (`MAP_PROFILE_BUTTON`, `MAP_SHARE_BUTTON`, `MAP_PADDLES`) must be decoded in the handler matching `xpad->xtype` (`xpadone_process_packet()`, `xpad360_process_packet()`, `xpad_process_packet()`). In `xpad360_process_packet()` and `xpad_init_input()`, `xpad->mapping & MAP_DPAD_TO_BUTTONS` and `!(xpad->mapping & MAP_DPAD_TO_BUTTONS) || xpad->xtype == XTYPE_XBOX360W` must remain two independent `if` blocks. `xpad_resume()` must reset `xpad->delayed_init_done = false`, and `xpad_start_xbox_one()` must reset `xpad->init_seq = 0` under `odata_lock`. [F66, F94, F130]
- In `applespi.c`, `struct message::length` excludes `MSG_HEADER_SIZE` (8) and the 2-byte CRC16: `applespi_send_cmd_msg()` sets `cpu_to_le16(msg_len - 2)` and `applespi_got_data()` requires `le16_to_cpu(message->length) == msg_len - MSG_HEADER_SIZE - 2`. For `PACKET_DEV_TPAD`, `applespi_got_data()` must verify `le16_to_cpu(message->length) + 2 == struct_size(tp, fingers, tp->number_of_fingers)` and clamp `tp->number_of_fingers` to `MAX_FINGERS` (11). [F76, F77]
- In `applespi_resume()`, under `cmd_msg_lock`, `have_cl_led_on`, `have_bl_level`, `cmd_msg_queued`, `read_active`, `write_active`, `drain`, and `suspended` must be cleared while preserving `want_bl_level`, and `applespi_init(applespi, true)` must set `want_mt_init_cmd` (not `want_tp_info_cmd`). In `applespi_handle_keyboard_event()`, released keys in `last_keys_pressed[i]` must be translated using saved `last_keys_fn_pressed[i]` before zeroing it, and `last_keys_pressed` updated only after reporting all releases and presses. [F67, F69]
- When replacing `atkbd->dev` in `atkbd_set_*()`, if `input_register_device()` fails, the error path must restore `atkbd->dev = old_dev` and old config fields and re-run `atkbd_set_keycode_table()` and `atkbd_set_device_attrs()`. Enabling `atkbd->softrepeat` must also set `atkbd->softraw = true` (saving/restoring both on error). `atkbd_set_keycode_table()` must zero `keycode` and `force_release_mask` and recompute `ATKBD_RET_HANGEUL`/`ATKBD_RET_HANJA` before `atkbd_platform_fixup()`, and `atkbd_set_device_attrs()` must exclude codes `>= ATKBD_SPECIAL`, `KEY_RESERVED`, and `ATKBD_KEY_NULL`. [F57, F58, F61, F83]
- In `atkbd_reconnect()`, before `atkbd_enable()` and `atkbd_activate()`, reset `atkbd->xl_bit` and `atkbd->emul` to `0` and, if `atkbd->write` is set, restore LEDs (`atkbd_set_leds()`) and repeat rate (`atkbd_set_repeat_rate()`, unless `atkbd->softrepeat`). [F59]
- In `iforce_init_device()`, clamp `ff_effects` from query `'N'` (`buf[1]`) to `IFORCE_EFFECTS_MAX` (32) before `input_ff_create()`. In `iforce_serio_irq()`, reset `pkt`, `id`, `len`, `idx`, and `csum` to `0` when `idx == len`, and reset `pkt` and `id` to `0` when `len > IFORCE_MAX_LENGTH`. [F72, F81]

## Synchronization

- In `xpad.c`, `xpad->odata_lock` must be held across `xpad_prepare_next_init_packet()`, `xpad_prepare_next_out_packet()`, `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()`, and any mutation of `out_packets`, `last_out_packet`, `init_seq`, `odata_serial`, or `irq_out_active`. [F17]
- For `XTYPE_XBOX360W`, `xpad_presence_work()` must assign `xpad->x360w_dev` via `rcu_assign_pointer()` only after `xpad_init_input()` succeeds, and on disconnect must clear `xpad->x360w_dev` and call `synchronize_rcu()` before `xpad_deinit_input()`; `xpad360w_process_packet()` must read `xpad->x360w_dev` under `rcu_read_lock()`. For non-`XTYPE_XBOX360W`, `xpad_suspend()` and `xpad_resume()` must hold `input->mutex` while checking `input_device_enabled(input)` and calling `xpad_stop_input()`/`xpad_start_input()`. [F20, F65]
- In `atkbd.c`, `atkbd->mutex` serializes `atkbd_reconnect()`, `atkbd_attr_set_helper()`, and `atkbd_event_work()`. `atkbd->enabled` must only be modified by `atkbd_enable()`/`atkbd_disable()` under `serio_pause_rx()`. `atkbd_reconnect()` and `atkbd_attr_set_helper()` must call `atkbd_disable(atkbd)` under `atkbd->mutex` before probing, mutating `keycode`/`force_release_mask`, or replacing `atkbd->dev`, and `atkbd_enable(atkbd)` afterward. [F25, F26]
- In `iforce`, all `iforce->xmit` accesses in `iforce_send_packet()`, `iforce_serio_xmit()`, and `__iforce_usb_xmit()` must hold `iforce->xmit_lock` (`spin_lock_irqsave`), and `iforce_send_packet()` must drop `xmit_lock` before calling `iforce->xport_ops->xmit(iforce)`. `allocate_resource(&iforce->device_memory, ...)` must hold `iforce->mem_mutex`, and `iforce_upload_effect()` must return `-EAGAIN` while `FF_CORE_UPDATE` is set on `core_effect->flags`. [F30, F142]
- In `applespi.c`, `applespi_send_cmd_msg()` must return if `cmd_msg_queued` is non-zero (unless timed out) and issue at most one command per call; `applespi_msg_complete()` clears `cmd_msg_queued` and calls `applespi_send_cmd_msg()` on write completion. `applespi_register_touchpad_device()` must be deferred from `applespi_handle_cmd_response()` to `applespi->work`, publishing `touchpad_input_dev` with `smp_store_release()` and reading it in `report_tp_state()` with `smp_load_acquire()`. [F97, F165]
- `n64joy_poll()` and `tgfx_timer()` must never acquire `priv->n64joy_mutex` or `tgfx->sem`, because `n64joy_close()` and `tgfx_close()` call `timer_delete_sync()` while holding those locks. [F156]

## Object Lifetimes

- `iforce_init_device()` frees `iforce->dev` with `input_free_device()` on internal error and registers it on success; transport probe error paths must not free/unregister `iforce->dev`, while disconnect handlers call `input_unregister_device(iforce->dev)`. In `iforce_close()`, when `EV_FF` is set, send `FF_CMD_ENABLE` and wait on `iforce->wait` for `IFORCE_XMIT_RUNNING` to clear before `iforce->xport_ops->stop_io(iforce)`. [F1, F3]
- In `iforce-ff.c`, `make_*_modifier()` must allocate `mod_chunk` from `iforce->device_memory` only when `old == NULL`, and `iforce_erase_effect()` must release `mod1_chunk`/`mod2_chunk` iff `FF_MOD1_IS_USED`/`FF_MOD2_IS_USED` is set before zeroing `core_effect->flags[0]`. [F71]
- In `xpad_init_input()`, failure before `input_register_device()` must roll back via `xpad_led_disconnect()`, `input_ff_destroy()`, and `input_free_device()` without setting `xpad->input_created`, and `xpad_led_probe()` must reset `xpad->led = NULL` on error. `xpad_disconnect()` must call `xpad360w_stop_input()` (which calls `usb_kill_urb(xpad->irq_in)` before `flush_work(&xpad->work)`) and `xpad_deinit_input()` before `xpad_stop_output()` and `xpad_deinit_output()`. [F4, F5, F18]
- Under `xpad->odata_lock`, `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()` and `xpad_irq_out()` must call `usb_unanchor_urb()` and clear `xpad->irq_out_active` whenever `usb_submit_urb(xpad->irq_out, ...)` fails after `usb_anchor_urb()`. [F7]
- `applespi_remove()` and `applespi_suspend()` must call `applespi_drain_writes()`, then `acpi_disable_gpe(NULL, applespi->gpe)`, and then `applespi_drain_reads()`, as in-flight writes need the GPE read response to complete. [F8]
- `atkbd_disconnect()` must call `atkbd_disable(atkbd)` and `input_unregister_device(atkbd->dev)` before `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&atkbd->event_work)` and `serio_close(serio)`. [F9]
- `tgfx_open()`/`tgfx_close()` and `n64joy_open()`/`n64joy_close()` guard `tgfx->used` and `priv->n64joy_opened` under `tgfx->sem` and `priv->n64joy_mutex`, starting the timer (and claiming `tgfx->pd`) only on `0 -> 1` and calling `timer_delete_sync()` only on `1 -> 0`. In `walkera0701_close()`, `parport_disable_irq()` must precede `hrtimer_cancel(&w->timer)` and `parport_release()`, and `walkera0701_irq_handler()` must set `w->counter = NO_SYNC` and abort if `hrtimer_try_to_cancel(&w->timer)` is non-zero. [F12, F33]

## API Contracts

- `atkbd_event()` and `applespi_event()` (`input_dev->event`) run in atomic context and must not block: `atkbd_event()` schedules `atkbd->event_work` for `ps2_command()`, and `applespi_event()` queues async SPI messages under `cmd_msg_lock`. [F164]
- In `xpad.c`, `idata` and `odata` are allocated with `usb_alloc_coherent()` (`URB_NO_TRANSFER_DMA_MAP` on `irq_in`/`irq_out`) and must be freed with `usb_free_coherent()` only after stopping and freeing the URBs. For `XTYPE_XBOX360W`, do not set `input_dev->open`/`close` in `xpad_init_input()`, start `irq_in` in `xpad_probe()`/`xpad_resume()` via `xpad360w_start_input()`, set `USB_QUIRK_RESET_RESUME` on `udev->quirks` without defining `.reset_resume` on `xpad_driver`, and in `xpad_suspend()` run `xpad360w_stop_input()` and `xpad360w_poweroff_controller()` before `xpad_stop_output()`. For `XTYPE_XBOXONE`, `xpad_probe()` must reject `bInterfaceNumber != GIP_WIRED_INTF_DATA` (0) with `-ENODEV`, and `xpad_start_xbox_one()` must set altsetting 0 on `GIP_WIRED_INTF_AUDIO` (1) if present. [F131, F133, F134, F147, F166]
- In `iforce_send_packet()`, `data` must have at least `LO(cmd)` bytes with `LO(cmd) < IFORCE_MAX_LENGTH` (16). `iforce_usb_get_id()` must pass its `usb_control_msg()` timeout in ms (`1000`), `get_id` callbacks must return `0` only after setting `response_data` and `*response_len`, and `iforce_init_device()` must check both `!iforce_get_id_packet(...)` and `len >= N` (`3` for 16-bit fields at `buf + 1`, `2` for `buf[1]`). [F36, F80, F92]
- `applespi_probe()` must evaluate ACPI `"UIST"` first and return `-ENODEV` if `usb_status` is non-zero before `applespi_enable_spi()`, and `applespi_enable_spi()` must call `msleep(50)` after enabling SPI via `"SIEN"`. Signed 16-bit `struct tp_finger` fields must be converted with `le16_to_int()`, not `le16_to_cpu()`. [F110, F135, F137]
- In `bcm_kp_probe()`, call `bcm_kp_stop(kp)` after configuring `kp->clk` and before `devm_request_threaded_irq()` and `input_register_device()`. In `bcm_kp_get_keycode()`, pass `get_count_order(kp->n_cols)` (not `kp->n_cols`) as the third argument to `MATRIX_SCAN_CODE()`. [F48, F138]
- In `n64joy_exec_pif()`, DMA buffers must be `____cacheline_aligned` and flushed/invalidated via `dma_cache_wback_inv()` (using `8 * 8`, not `sizeof(in)` on parameter `const u64 in[8]`) and `dma_cache_inv()` before polling `SI_STATUS_REG` and writing `SI_DRAM_REG` with IRQs disabled. In `psxpad_command(pad, sendcmdlen)`, `sendcmdlen` must not exceed `sizeof(pad->response)` (21 bytes). [F88, F117, F140]

## Error Handling

- When an async SPI read fails in `applespi_async_read_complete()` or fails CRC verification while draining in `applespi_got_data()`, clear both `read_active` and `write_active` and wake `drain_complete` under `cmd_msg_lock`. Translate `acpi_status` and `efi_status_t` (guarded by `efi_rt_services_supported()`) to `-ENODEV` before returning from `int` functions. [F24, F42]
- In `xpad.c`, when `xpad->xtype == XTYPE_UNKNOWN`, `xpad_init_output()` skips initializing `irq_out_anchor`, `odata`, and `irq_out`, so `xpad_stop_output()`, `xpad_deinit_output()`, and `xpad_init_ff()` must guard against `XTYPE_UNKNOWN`. Do not call `dev_err()` on `-ENODEV` from `usb_submit_urb()` in `xpad_try_sending_next_out_packet()`. In `xpad_irq_in()` and `iforce_usb_irq()`, `urb->status` of `-ECONNRESET`, `-ENOENT`, or `-ESHUTDOWN` must return without resubmitting `urb`, while other non-zero statuses jump to `exit:` to resubmit. [F45, F104, F158]
- In `atkbd_probe()`, initialize `param[0]` and `param[1]` to `0xa5` before `ps2_command(ps2dev, param, ATKBD_CMD_GETID)`. `atkbd_set_force_release()` must parse into a temporary `new_mask` via `bitmap_parselist()` before copying to `atkbd->force_release_mask`. [F39, F60]
- Check `devm_platform_ioremap_resource()` with `IS_ERR()` in `n64joy_probe()` and `bcm_kp_probe()`. In `bcm_kp_probe()`, pass `PTR_ERR(kp->clk)` on `devm_clk_get_optional()` error, and when `kp->clk` is non-NULL, treat `clk_round_rate() <= 0` as `-EINVAL`. In `tgfx_attach()` and `sw_connect()`, failed `input_register_device()` at `i` must call `input_free_device(dev[i])` before unregistering `dev[--i]`. Zero-initialize stack `struct pardev_cb` in `tgfx_attach()` and `walkera0701_attach()`, check `spi_setup(spi)` in `psxpad_spi_probe()`, and check `err == sizeof(...)` after `i2c_smbus_read_i2c_block_data()` in `qwiic-joystick.c`. [F47, F49, F51, F52, F53, F101]

## External Contracts

- Do not add forced-release quirks to `atkbd_dmi_quirk_table[]` in `atkbd.c` (handled in udev). Vendor-specific entries in `xpad_table[]` must use `XPAD_XBOX360_VENDOR()` or `XPAD_XBOXONE_VENDOR()` (sorted by VID) and never match non-gamepad VID/PIDs. In `applespi_set_bl_level()`, handle `value == 0` before applying `KBD_BL_LEVEL_MIN` so brightness `0` maps to `0`. [F113, F127, F129]

## Known Pitfalls

- In `iforce.h`, `XMIT_INC(var, n)` expands to two unbraced statements; never use it in an unbraced `if`/`else`/`for`/`while`. In `make_condition_modifier()` (`iforce-ff.c`), multiply 16-bit parameters (`__s16` `rk`, `lk`, `center`; `u16` `db`, `rsat`, `lsat`) by 32-bit signed `int` scale factors (`100`, `500`, `1000`) before shifting right by `15`/`16`. [F79, F112]
- In `xpad.c`, index `out_packets` with `XPAD_OUT_CMD_IDX`/`XPAD_OUT_FF_IDX`/`XPAD_OUT_LED_IDX` (`XPAD_NUM_OUT_PACKETS`), and invert stick Y axes (`ABS_Y`, `ABS_RY`) with `~(__s16)le16_to_cpup(...)`, not `-(__s16)...`. [F64, F111]
- In `sw_get_bits()`, cast `((buf[i] >> tri++) & 1)` to `__u64` before `<< bit++`. In `walkera0701_irq_handler()`, bound `u64 pulse_time` and cast to `u32` before dividing by `ANALOG_DELTA`. In `n64joy.c`, `priv->reg_base` is `u32 __iomem *`, so `SI_*_REG` constants are 32-bit word indices (`0`, `1`, `4`, `6`), not byte offsets. In `sensehat_joystick_report()`, copy `unsigned int keys` into `unsigned long curr_states` before `bitmap_xor()`/`for_each_set_bit()`. [F89, F106, F108, F122]
- In `atkbd.c`, compare `jiffies` deadlines with `time_before()`/`time_after()`. In `tmdc_setup_port()`, `model->name` is passed as the format string to `snprintf(..., int, int, int)`. [F114, F124]
