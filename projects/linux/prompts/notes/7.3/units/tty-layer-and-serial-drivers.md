# TTY LAYER AND SERIAL DRIVERS

This unit implements the Linux TTY core (`tty_io.c`, `tty_buffer.c`, `tty_baudrate.c`, `tty_audit.c`), Magic SysRq (`sysrq.c`), the Serial Core and `serial-base` bus (`serial_core.c`, `serial_base_bus.c`, `serial_port.c`, `serial_mctrl_gpio.c`), and hardware UART drivers (OMAP, OWL, PCH, PIC32, PMAC Zilog, PXA, RSCI, SA1100, SB1250, SC16IS7xx, SCCNXP, SH-SCI, SiFive, Spreadtrum, STM32, SyncLink GT, Tegra TCU/UTC, Timbuart, TXx9, and Xilinx UARTPS).

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `struct uart_port port` must remain at offset 0 of `struct uart_pmac_port`, `struct uart_pxa_port`, `struct sci_port`, and `struct sprd_uart_port` because callbacks and drvdata cast pointers directly to/from `struct uart_port *`. [F114, F115, F294, F298]
- `serial_ctrl_device` and `serial_port_device` share `serial_base_bus_type`; bus callbacks must check `dev->type` (`&serial_ctrl_type` vs `&serial_port_type`) before calling `to_serial_base_ctrl_device()`/`to_serial_base_port_device()`, and drivers registered via `serial_base_driver_register()` must have names starting with `"ctrl"` or `"port"`. [F121, F162]
- Under `tty_lock`, `tty->count` must equal open entries in `tty->tty_files` plus 1 for an open PTY master (`tty->link->count`) plus 1 if `tty_port_kopened(tty->port)`; keep `tty_add_file()`/`tty_del_file()` in lockstep with `tty->count`. In `__tty_hangup()`, if any file has `write_iter == redirected_tty_write` (`cons_filp`), do not set `hung_up_tty_fops` on `cons_filp` and call `tty->ops->close()` `closecount` times instead of `tty->ops->hangup()`. [F420, F421]
- `tty_buffer_alloc()` allocates `struct_size(p, data, 2 * size)` bytes (`flags == false` uses all `2 * size` bytes for chars, so check `head->flags` before `flag_buf_ptr()` and allocate a new buffer in `__tty_buffer_request_room()` when `!b->flags && flags`), stores `size` in `p->size`, and adds `size` to `port->buf.mem_used` even when popping from `buf->free`; `tty_buffer_free()` subtracts `b->size` before pushing to `buf->free`. [F397, F417, F484]
- In `tty_buffer_flush()` and `flush_to_ldisc()`, `buf->head` is only freed and advanced when `smp_load_acquire(&buf->head->next)` is non-NULL; `tty_buffer_flush()` must set both `read` and `lookahead` to `commit` on the tail buffer, and `tty_buffer_free()`/`tty_buffer_free_all()` must check `b->size > 0` so `&port->buf.sentinel` (`size == 0`) is never freed. [F418, F462, F487]
- After `uport->ops->set_termios()`, `uart_change_line_settings()` must hold `uport->lock` to sync `UPSTAT_CTS_ENABLE`/`UPSTAT_DCD_ENABLE` with `c_cflag`, recompute `hw_stopped` via `uart_softcts_mode()` and `get_mctrl()`, and call `stop_tx()` or `__uart_start()`. In `uart_set_termios()`, reload `cflag` from `tty->termios.c_cflag` after `uart_change_line_settings()` before testing `CBAUD == B0`. [F226, F227]
- While `SER_RS485_ENABLED` is set, `uart_update_mctrl()`, `uart_suspend_port()`, `uart_resume_port()`, and `uart_configure_port()` must not call `ops->set_mctrl()`; when `uart_set_rs485_config()` clears `SER_RS485_ENABLED`, it must call `port->ops->set_mctrl(port, port->mctrl)` under `port->lock`. Both `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485()` (which masks `rs485->flags` with `rs485_supported.flags` before resolving RTS polarity) and `uart_get_rs485_mode()` must sanitize delays via `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485_delays()`. [F228, F230, F249, F265]
- Both `uart_shutdown()` and `uart_tty_port_shutdown()` must call `tty_port_set_suspended(port, false)`, and `uart_suspend_port()` must save and restore `uport->mctrl` around zeroing it for `ops->shutdown()`. In `serial_port_runtime_resume()`/`suspend()`, call `port->ops->start_tx(port)` only if `!(port->flags & UPF_DEAD)` and `port_dev->tx_enabled` is true under `uart_port_lock_irqsave()`; `serial_port_runtime_suspend()` must return `0` immediately when `!pm_runtime_enabled(dev)`. [F232, F233, F306, F307]
- In `uart_insert_char()`, test `(status & port->ignore_status_mask & ~overrun) == 0` for `@ch` and `status & ~port->ignore_status_mask & overrun` for `TTY_OVERRUN`, then push with `tty_flip_buffer_push()`. In `rsci_receive_chars()` and `sci_receive_chars()`, if `copied == 0` (`tty_buffer_request_room()` returned `0`), still read `RDR`/`SCxRDR` and `CSR`/`SCxSR` and clear `DRxC`/`SCxSR_RDxF_CLEAR` to prevent IRQ storms. [F99, F266, F275, F345]

## Synchronization
- In `serial_core.c`, dereferencing `state->uart_port` without `state->port.mutex` requires `uart_port_ref()`/`uart_port_ref_lock()` and `uart_port_deref()`/`uart_port_unlock_deref()` (`uart_port_check()` requires `state->port.mutex`). `uart_port_unlock_deref()` must unlock `uport->lock` before `uart_port_deref(uport)`, and `uart_alloc_xmit_buf()`/`uart_free_xmit_buf()` must never call `free_page()` under `uport->lock` (detach `port->xmit_buf = NULL` and reset `xmit_fifo` under the lock first). [F173, F174, F193]
- Acquire `uart_port::lock` via `uart_port_lock*()`/`uart_port_unlock*()` rather than raw `spin_lock*()`, except in `CON_NBCON` `device_lock`/`device_unlock` callbacks and `uart_port_set_cons()` which must use `__uart_port_lock_irqsave()`. Callers of `uart_handle_dcd_change()`, `uart_handle_cts_change()`, `uart_write_wakeup()`, and `uart_update_timeout()` must hold `port->lock` (lockless `uart_fifo_timeout()` reads `port->frame_time` via `READ_ONCE()`). [F200, F201, F207, F343]
- Never call sleeping functions or `tty_flip_buffer_push()`/`uart_handle_sysrq_char()` under `port->lock`: use `copy_from_user`/`copy_to_user` and `gpiod_set_value_cansleep()` (`rs485_term_gpio`, `rs485_rx_during_tx_gpio`) outside `uart_port_lock_irqsave`; use non-sleeping GPIO APIs in `mctrl_gpio_set`/`get`/`get_outputs` and `mctrl_gpio_disable_ms_no_sync` in atomic/locked contexts; use `GFP_ATOMIC` in `dma_handle_tx()` (`pch_uart.c`); and drop `port->lock` (or use `uart_prepare_sysrq_char()` + `uart_unlock_and_check_sysrq*()`) before `tty_flip_buffer_push()` or `uart_handle_sysrq_char()`. [F154, F325, F327, F328, F329, F338, F386]
- Cancel timers, work, IRQs, and synchronous DMA outside `port->lock`: `timer_delete_sync(&sport->timer)` in `sa1100`; `free_irq()` in `pmz_shutdown()`; `cancel_work_sync(&s->work_tx)`, `dmaengine_terminate_sync()`, and `dma_free_coherent()` in `sci_dma_tx_release()`/`sci_dma_rx_release()`; `dmaengine_synchronize()` in `stm32_usart_shutdown()` (`stm32_usart_rx/tx_dma_terminate()` under `port->lock` must use `dmaengine_terminate_async()`); and `hrtimer_cancel(&cdns_uart->tx_timer)` in `cdns_uart_shutdown()`. [F24, F27, F335, F339, F531]
- Legacy console `.write` callbacks (`pch`, `omap`, `owl`, `pxa`, `sh-sci`, `sprd`, `stm32`, `cdns_uart`) must use `uart_port_trylock_irqsave()` when `oops_in_progress` is set (and skip locking when `port->sysrq` is non-zero in `sh-sci.c` and `sprd_serial.c`), unlocking only if `locked`. `CON_NBCON` callbacks in `tegra-utc.c` must bracket all TX MMIO accesses with `nbcon_enter_unsafe()`/`nbcon_exit_unsafe()`. [F160, F341, F528, F532]
- In `__tty_buffer_request_room()`, `smp_store_release(&b->commit, b->used)` must precede `smp_store_release(&b->next, n)`, and consumers (`flush_to_ldisc()`, `lookahead_bufs()`, `tty_buffer_flush()`) must `smp_load_acquire(&head->next)` before `smp_load_acquire(&head->commit)`. `tty_buffer_lock_exclusive()`/`tty_buffer_flush()` must increment `port->buf.priority` before locking `port->buf.lock`, and `tty_buffer_unlock_exclusive()` must requeue `buf->work` if `head->commit != head->read`. [F372, F374]
- PTY `legacy_mutex` locks master-then-slave (`TTY_LOCK_NORMAL` before `tty_lock_slave()` `TTY_LOCK_SLAVE`), whereas nested `port->buf.lock` locks slave-then-master (`tty_buffer_set_lock_subclass()`). `tty->write_buf`/`write_cnt` require `tty->atomic_write_lock`; `tty_encode_baud_rate()`/`tty_get_baud_rate()` require `tty->termios_rwsem`; `tty_audit_buf` fields and `tty_audit_buf_push()` require `buf->mutex`; and `show_cons_active()` must lock `console_list_lock()` before `console_lock()`. [F380, F381, F383, F519, F525]
- `__handle_sysrq()` runs `op_p->handler` under `rcu_read_lock()` in atomic context: handlers must not sleep, `__sysrq_swap_key_ops()` must call `synchronize_rcu()`, and `sysrq_handle_crash()` must call `rcu_read_unlock()` before `panic()`. Similarly, `do_SAK()` and `tty_hangup()` must schedule `SAK_work`/`hangup_work`, and `tty_kref_put()` must defer `release_one_tty()` via `queue_release_one_tty()`. [F384, F516, F522, F523]

## Object Lifetimes
- Once `serial_base_device_init()` is called in `serial_base_ctrl_add()` or `serial_base_port_add()`, error paths must use `put_device()` (not `kfree()`), and `serial_base_port_add()`/`serial_base_port_device_remove()` must free `port->port_id` from `ctrl_dev->port_ida` via `ida_free()` while `port_dev->port` and `ctrl_dev` remain valid (before `serial_base_ctrl_device_remove()`). [F1, F2, F163]
- In `serial_core_register_port()`, set `UPF_DEAD` in `port->flags` before `serial_core_port_device_add()` and keep it set until `serial_core_add_one_port()` finishes. In `__uart_start()`, `pm_runtime_get(&port_dev->dev)` must be balanced with `pm_runtime_put_noidle(&port_dev->dev)` on error (`err < 0 && err != -EINPROGRESS`) and `pm_runtime_put_autosuspend(&port_dev->dev)` otherwise, whereas `pm_runtime_enabled()` checks `port->dev`. [F175, F192, F279]
- In `tty_init_dev()`, once `tty_driver_install_tty()` succeeds, error paths must unlock `tty` and call `release_tty(tty, idx)` (never `free_tty_struct(tty)`). `tty_release_struct()` and `tty_kclose()` must call `tty_ldisc_release(tty)` and `tty_flush_works(tty)` before `release_tty()` because `queue_release_one_tty()` reuses `tty->hangup_work`. In `__tty_hangup()`, drop the kref count returned by `tty_signal_session_leader()` via `tty_kref_put()` and set `tty->ctrl.session` and `tty->ctrl.pgrp` to `NULL` after `put_pid()`. [F354, F358, F359]
- `tty_buffer_free_all()` runs unlocked and checks `atomic_xchg(&buf->mem_used, 0)`, so it must only be called after `port->buf.work` is canceled and all producers/consumers have stopped. In `tty_audit_exit()`, replace `tty_audit_buf` with `ERR_PTR(-ESRCH)` and call `tty_audit_buf_push(buf)` before `tty_audit_buf_free(buf)`, while `tty_audit_buf_get()` installs new buffers via `cmpxchg()` against `NULL`. [F363, F375]

## API Contracts
- `serial_base_ctrl_device_remove()` and `serial_base_port_device_remove()` must accept `NULL` as a no-op. `uart_port_startup()` and `uart_startup()` return `1` when `uport->type == PORT_UNKNOWN` or `ops->startup()` fails for `CAP_SYS_ADMIN`; callers must only mark the port initialized on `0` and translate `> 0` to `0` for userspace. [F35, F210]
- `mctrl_gpio_init()` and `mctrl_gpio_init_noauto()` return `ERR_PTR` on error when `CONFIG_GPIOLIB` is enabled and `NULL` when disabled; callers must check `IS_ERR()` (never `!gpios` or `IS_ERR_OR_NULL()`), reset `gpios = NULL` on non-deferral errors if continuing (`sa1100`), and compact output descriptors in `mctrl_gpio_set()` into dense `0..count-1` indices with `__assign_bit()` on the uninitialized `values` bitmap. [F38, F211, F257, F293]
- `__uart_read_properties()` must always set `port->iotype` (defaulting to `UPIO_PORT` if `port->iobase` is non-zero else `UPIO_MEM`, or `UPIO_UNKNOWN` on invalid `reg-io-width`) before returning `-EINVAL`; when `use_defaults` is `false` (`uart_read_and_validate_port_properties()`), validate `port->mapsize < value` before applying `reg-offset`, whereas `uart_read_port_properties()` (`use_defaults == true`) requires an IRQ. [F214, F236, F352]
- Callers of `uart_update_timeout()`, `uart_get_divisor()`, `cdns_uart_calc_baud_divs()`, and `sc16is7xx_set_baud()` must pass a validated non-zero `baud` rate. `uart_xchar_out()` requires `uport->serial_out` to be populated. `tty_buffer_set_limit()` must be called before any flip-buffer allocation and rejects `limit < MIN_TTYB_SIZE` (256); `*chars` from `tty_prepare_flip_string()` must never be used directly for DMA. [F107, F281, F344, F410, F478, F515]
- `tty_init_dev()` and `tty_kopen_exclusive()` return `tty` with `tty_lock(tty)` held, whereas `tty_kopen_shared()` returns `tty` with an incremented kref and unlocked. `tty_driver_lookup_tty()` returns `ERR_PTR`, `NULL`, or a kref-incremented `tty_struct *` (requiring `IS_ERR()` check and `tty_kref_put()`), and `tty_open_current_tty()` returns `NULL` only when `device != MKDEV(TTYAUX_MAJOR, 0)` (propagating any `ERR_PTR` in `tty_open()`). [F355, F391, F392]
- `tty_termios_encode_baud_rate(termios, ibaud, obaud)` takes `ibaud` before `obaud`, forces `ibaud = 0` when `obaud == 0`, samples `ibinput` and `BOTHER` before clearing `CBAUD` and `CBAUD << IBSHIFT`, and matches `obaud` (`ofound = i`) before `ibaud` in the table loop so `IBSHIFT` bits stay `B0` when `ibaud == obaud` and `!ibinput`. [F407, F415, F467]

## Error Handling
- In `tty_open()`, use `tty_free_file()` only before `tty_add_file()`; once `tty_add_file()` is called, clean up via `tty_release()` and reset `filp->f_op = &tty_fops` if `tty_hung_up_p(filp)` before retrying. In `tty_init_dev()`, abort via `err_release_lock` if `tty->port` is `NULL`, and guard `tty->port` and `tty->link` in `release_tty()`. [F398, F401]
- In `iterate_tty_read()`, if `copy_to_iter()` copies fewer than `size` bytes while `cookie` is non-NULL, continue the loop with `count = 0` and `retval = -EFAULT` without letting a subsequent negative `ld->ops->read()` overwrite `retval`; if `ld->ops->write()` in `iterate_tty_write()` returns `0 < ret < size`, call `iov_iter_revert(from, size - ret)`. To prevent leaking terminal input, `receive_buf()` must `memset(p, 0, n)` and `iterate_tty_read()` must `memzero_explicit(kernel_buf, sizeof(kernel_buf))`. [F402, F403, F469]

## External Contracts
- Zero `*retinfo` at entry in `uart_get_info()` (sysfs `.show` ignores return values) and zero `icount`/`v`/`v32` in `tty_get_icount()` and `compat_tty_tiocgserial()`/`tiocsserial()` (setting `v.iomap_base = 0` and stopping `memcpy` at `offsetof(struct serial_struct32, iomem_base)`). Carve new `struct serial_rs485` fields from `padding0`/`padding1` and zero unused padding in `uart_sanitize_serial_rs485()`, and reject non-zero `iso7816.reserved` words in `uart_set_iso7816_config()`. [F251, F253, F350, F404, F442]
- Return `-ENOIOCTLCMD` when unhandled in `tty->ops->ioctl()` (translated to `-ENOTTY` by `tty_ioctl()`), break (not return) after `tty_buffer_flush()` in `TCFLSH`, skip `tty_wait_until_sent()` for `TIOCCBRK`, and pass `real_tty` to `TIOCGWINSZ`/`TIOCSWINSZ`/`TIOCGDEV` while rejecting `TIOCCONS` when `real_tty != tty`. Enforce `tty_legacy_tiocsti`, controlling-tty, `CAP_SYS_ADMIN`, and `tty_audit_tiocsti()` under `tty_buffer_lock_exclusive()` in `tiocsti()`, and run `__tty_hangup()` synchronously in `tty_vhangup()`/`tty_vhangup_session()`. [F394, F457, F458, F472, F541, F542]

## Known Pitfalls
- In `tty_termios_baud_rate()` and `tty_termios_input_baud_rate()`, check `cbaud == BOTHER` before `cbaud & CBAUDEX` (`0x1000` is shared); in `tty_init_termios()`, overwrite restored `tty->termios.c_line` with `init_termios.c_line` and recompute `c_ispeed`/`c_ospeed`; in `tty_buffer_space_avail()`, clamp signed `int space = mem_limit - atomic_read(&mem_used)` with `max(space, 0)`; in `__tty_insert_flip_string_flags()`/`tty_prepare_flip_string()`, read `port->buf.tail` only after `__tty_buffer_request_room()`; in `file_tty_write()`, use `file` (not `iocb->ki_filp`); and in `this_tty()`, verify `file->f_op->read_iter == tty_read` before `file_tty(file)` and return `fd + 1`. [F395, F406, F454, F473, F482, F486, F488]
- Widen `tty_get_frame_size()` to `u64` with `DIV64_U64_ROUND_UP()` in `uart_update_timeout()` and cast `delay_rts_after_send` to `u64` with `DIV_ROUND_UP(frame_time, 7)` in `cdns_calc_after_tx_delay()`; use `time_after()`/`time_before()` for `jiffies` deadlines and `!=` for `uart_icount` in `uart_wait_modem_status()`. [F280, F283, F476, F477, F483]
