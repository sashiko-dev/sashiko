# COMMON INTERNET FILE SYSTEM CLIENT (CIFS and SMB3)

This unit implements the Linux CIFS and SMB2/3 client filesystem (`fs/smb/client`), providing VFS/netfs integration, TCP/SMBDirect connection and session lifecycle management, socket demultiplexing (`cifsd`), authentication, multichannel interface management, DFS referral resolution and caching, directory caching, and SMB1/2/3 wire protocol operations.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `struct cifs_ses`, `ses->chans[0]` is the primary channel and secondary channels are at `1..ses->chan_count - 1`, while `smb_ses_list` lives only on the primary server (`pserver = SERVER_IS_CHAN(server) ? server->primary_server : server`). Secondary-channel loops in `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()` and `__cifs_put_smb_ses()` must start at index 1, and `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()`, `smb2_check_message()`, `smb2_is_valid_lease_break()`, `smb2_is_valid_oplock_break()`, and `cifs_is_network_name_deleted()` must iterate `&pserver->smb_ses_list` while passing `server` to per-channel helpers. [F104, F130, F456]
- In `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()`, `ses->ses_status = SES_NEED_RECON` and marking `tcon_list`/`tcon_ipc` entries `need_reconnect = true` and `status = TID_NEED_RECON` must happen only when `mark_smb_session` is true or `CIFS_ALL_CHANS_NEED_RECONNECT(ses)` becomes true. When `__cifs_reconnect()` is called with `mark_smb_session` true, it must call `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect(server, mark_smb_session)` before `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()`. [F69, F70]
- In `struct tcon_link`, a multiuser `tl_tcon` holds an `ERR_PTR` while `TCON_LINK_PENDING` is set or after `cifs_construct_tcon()` fails; `cifs_put_tlink()` and `cifs_sb_tlink()` must check `!IS_ERR(tlink_tcon(tlink))` before dereferencing or passing `tl_tcon` to `cifs_put_tcon()`. [F53]
- Under `cinode->open_file_lock`, `cifs_new_fileinfo()` must insert `FMODE_READ` `cifsFileInfo` entries at the head of `cinode->openFileList` (`list_add`) and write-only entries at the tail (`list_add_tail`), because `find_readable_file()` stops at the first non-`FMODE_READ` entry. Whenever `cifs_new_fileinfo()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, or `cifs_oplock_break()` sets a read-only oplock while `cifs_has_mand_locks(cinode)` is true, the oplock level must be reset to 0. [F205, F254]
- In `smb2_compound_op()`, `SMB2_OP_OPEN_QUERY` (`cmds[0]`) and `SMB2_OP_MKDIR` do not emit a separate request PDU in `rqst[]` (`num_rqst` is not incremented and no `*_free(&rqst[num_rqst++])` is called). After adding 2 to `num_cmds` to process `Open`/`Close` responses, `num_cmds` must be decremented by 2 before jumping to `replay_again`. [F408, F446]

## Synchronization

- Spinlocks nest as `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` -> `server->srv_lock` -> `ses->ses_lock` -> `ses->chan_lock` -> `tcon->tc_lock`, with `match_server()` requiring `srv_lock` (and `net_eq(cifs_net_ns(server), current->nsproxy->net_ns)`), `match_session()` requiring `ses_lock` and `chan_lock` (and `uid_eq(ctx->cred_uid, ses->cred_uid)` for `Kerberos`/`IAKerb`), and `match_tcon()` requiring `tc_lock`. `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` must be held whenever modifying `srv_count`, `ses_count`, `tc_count`, or `cifs_tcp_ses_list`/`smb_ses_list`/`tcon_list`. [F32, F33, F178, F182]
- `ses->session_mutex` ranks outside `srv_lock`, `ses_lock`, and `chan_lock`, and `ses->iface_lock` ranks outside `chan_lock`. All `cifs_chan_*` helpers and `cifs_ses_get_chan_index()` require `ses->chan_lock`; `cifs_chan_update_iface()` must drop `chan_lock` before taking `iface_lock`, re-verify `cifs_ses_get_chan_index(ses, server) != CIFS_INVAL_CHAN_INDEX` after relocking `chan_lock`, and update `server->dstaddr` under `srv_lock`. [F163, F364, F365, F368, F401]
- `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()` must never acquire `server->srv_lock` while holding `ses->ses_lock` or `ses->chan_lock`; it must pin secondary channel `TCP_Server_Info` instances (`nserver->srv_count++`) onto a local list under `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` and `chan_lock` before updating `tcpStatus` under `srv_lock` outside the session locks. [F22]
- In `cifs_negotiate_protocol()`, `cifs_setup_session()`, and `cifs_tree_connect()`, post-RPC state updates must re-acquire `srv_lock`, `ses_lock`, or `tc_lock` and only transition to `CifsGood`, `SES_GOOD`, or `TID_GOOD` if still `CifsInNegotiate`, `SES_IN_SETUP`, or `TID_IN_TCON`. In `cifs_reconnect_tcon()`, after acquiring `ses->session_mutex`, `server->tcpStatus` (under `srv_lock`) and `cifs_chan_needs_reconnect()` / `ses->ses_status` (under `ses_lock` and `chan_lock`) must be revalidated before `cifs_negotiate_protocol()`. [F36, F37]
- `cifs_put_tcp_session()` and `cifs_demultiplex_thread()` coordinate termination via `xchg(&server->tsk, NULL)` (if `cifs_demultiplex_thread()` observes `NULL`, it must sleep until `signal_pending(current)` before exiting). `cifs_put_tcp_session()` must drop `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` before cancelling delayed work and use `cancel_delayed_work(&server->reconnect)` when `from_reconnect != 0` (e.g., from `smb2_reconnect_server()`). [F28, F170]
- In `dfs_cache.c`, `htable_rw_lock` must be dropped before `get_dfs_referral()` and, after `down_write(&htable_rw_lock)`, `lookup_cache_entry(path)` and `cache_entry_expired(ce)` must be re-evaluated. `cache_refresh_path()` returns a valid `ce` with `htable_rw_lock` held for reading and releases it on all `IS_ERR(ce)` paths, so callers must call `up_read(&htable_rw_lock)` iff `!IS_ERR(ce)`. [F15, F34]
- `_cifsFileInfo_put()` and `cifsFileInfo_put()` must never be called under `tcon->open_file_lock`, `cinode->open_file_lock`, or `cifs_file->file_info_lock` (and must pass `wait_oplock_handler = false` from `cifs_oplock_break()` or `cifs_close_deferred_file()`). Callers closing or reopening entries from `openFileList` (`cifs_close_deferred_file*()`, `cifs_reopen_persistent_handles()`, `cifs_get_writable_file()`) must pin entries with `cifsFileInfo_get()` or move them onto a local list under `open_file_lock` before dropping the lock. [F189, F222, F283]
- In `smb2_is_valid_lease_break()` and `smb2_is_valid_oplock_break()`, locks nest as `cifs_tcp_ses_lock` -> `tcon->open_file_lock` -> `cfile->file_info_lock`: drop `file_info_lock` before `cifs_queue_oplock_break(cfile)` (under `open_file_lock`), and drop all spinlocks before `smb2_queue_pending_open_break()`. [F377]
- In `cifs_reopen_file()`, checking/clearing `cfile->invalidHandle` and reopening the handle must be serialized under `cfile->fh_mutex`, which must be dropped before `filemap_write_and_wait()` or `cifs_relock_file()`. Write-locking `cinode->lock_sem` must use `cifs_down_write(&cinode->lock_sem)`, `cifs_relock_file()` must use `down_read_nested(&cinode->lock_sem, SINGLE_DEPTH_NESTING)`, and in `cifs_writev()`/`cifs_strict_readv()` netfs I/O locks (`netfs_start_io_*`) must be acquired before `cinode->lock_sem`. [F217, F318, F319]
- `cifs_d_revalidate()` must return `-ECHILD` immediately when `flags & LOOKUP_RCU` is set, and `cifs_lookup()`, `cifs_reopen_file()`, and `__cifs_unlink()` must never acquire `sb->s_vfs_rename_mutex`. In `__cifs_unlink()` on SMB2+, checking `d_count(dentry) > 1` for silly-rename (`-EBUSY`) must happen under `dentry->d_lock` after `cifs_close_deferred_file_under_dentry()`. [F213, F313, F314]

## Object Lifetimes

- `cifs_get_smb_ses()` and `cifs_get_tcon()` require the caller to hold an active reference on `server->srv_count` and `ses->ses_count` respectively: each drops the reference when returning an existing object, transfers it into `ses->server` or `tcon->ses` when creating a new object, and leaves it with the caller on `ERR_PTR` return. [F1, F2]
- Every path calling `sock_release(server->ssocket)` (`cifs_abort_connection()`, `clean_demultiplex_info()`, `generic_ip_connect()`) must immediately set `server->ssocket = NULL`. In `cifs_get_tcp_session()`, `get_net()` and any `tcp_ses->primary_server` reference must be released on failure before `cifs_demultiplex_thread` starts; once started, `cifsd`-accessed fields (`server->hostname`, `server->leaf_fullpath`) and `server` itself must only be freed in `clean_demultiplex_info()`. [F4, F5, F23]
- `cifs_put_tcon()` must return immediately when `tcon->ipc` is true (freed in `__cifs_put_smb_ses()`), and otherwise must call `cancel_delayed_work_sync()` on both `tcon->query_interfaces` and `tcon->dfs_cache_work` before draining `tcon->dfs_ses_list` or freeing `tcon`. Conversely, `__dfs_mount_share()` and `cifs_construct_tcon()` must populate `tcon->origin_fullpath` and `tcon->dfs_ses_list` (via `ref_walk_set_tcon()`, which nulls each `ref->ses`) under `tcon->tc_lock` before queuing `tcon->dfs_cache_work`. [F7, F13, F24, F25]
- Once a server open succeeds in `cifs_do_create()`, `cifs_nt_open()`, `cifs_query_reparse_point()`, or `cifs_create_reparse_inode()`, any subsequent failure must close the server handle, and in `cifs_create_reparse_inode()` also call `CIFSSMBDelFile()` while preserving the original error `rc`. Every stack `struct cifs_pending_open` added via `cifs_add_pending_open*()` must be removed by `cifs_new_fileinfo()` or `cifs_del_pending_open()` before returning. [F18, F19, F58, F198, F307]
- Ownership transfers that must null the source pointer or set `CIFS_NO_BUFFER` to avoid double-free include: `alloc_cache_entry()` stealing `refs[0].path_name` (so `cache_entry_hash()` must run first); `data->symlink_target` -> `fattr->cf_symlink_target` -> `cifs_i->symlink_target`; `smb_target` -> `linux_target` in `smb2_parse_native_symlink()`; and reparse response iovs in `smb2_compound_op()` (`SMB2_OP_GET_REPARSE`) and `smb2_query_reparse_point()`. [F14, F195, F350, F361]

## API Contracts

- `cifs_reconnect()` and `cifs_mark_tcp_ses_conns_for_reconnect()` must only be called from `cifs_demultiplex_thread`; other threads must call `cifs_signal_cifsd_for_reconnect()`. `CIFSSMBQFSUnixInfo()` and `CIFSSMBSetFSUnixInfo()` must use `smb_init_no_reconnect()`, and `CIFSTCon()` must clear `tcon->need_reconnect = false` before calling `reset_cifs_unix_caps()`. [F29, F43, F78]
- `smb2_compound_op()` unconditionally consumes the caller's `cfile` reference via `cifsFileInfo_put(cfile)`, so any caller retrying or falling back to another `smb2_compound_op()` must re-fetch `cfile` first. Non-NULL `out_iov` and `out_buftype` arrays passed to `smb2_compound_op()` must have `>= num_cmds + 2` elements and be freed with `free_rsp_buf()`/`free_rsp_iov()`. [F349, F409]
- Non-standard return conventions: `cifs_convert_address()` returns `1` on success and `0` on failure; `check_smb2_hdr()` and `smb2_check_message()` return `0` on valid PDU and `1` on failure; `target_share_matches_server()` and `match_target_ip()` return `0` on evaluation success and write the boolean result to `*target_match`/`*result`; `cifs_get_dfs_tcon_super()` returns `ERR_PTR` (never `NULL`); and `cifs_filldir()` returns `1` when the dir buffer is full (which `cifs_readdir()` must reset to `0`). [F48, F51, F227, F382, F395]
- `dns_resolve_name()`, `dns_resolve_unc()`, and `cifs_convert_address()` require a `struct sockaddr *` backed by at least `sizeof(struct sockaddr_in6)` (e.g., `struct sockaddr_storage`), and because hostname inputs (including `extract_unc_hostname()`'s `dfs_host`) may be non-NUL-terminated slices, all string operations on them must be bounded by `namelen`/`dfs_host_len` (`memchr`, `strncasecmp`, `%.*s`). [F154, F309, F310]
- Callers of `build_path_from_dentry*()` and `automount_fullpath()` must pass the original pointer from `alloc_dentry_path()` to `free_dentry_path()`, never the returned interior pointer. DFS cache/referral helpers (`dfs_cache_find()`, `dfs_cache_noreq_find()`, `dfs_cache_get_tgt_share()`, `dfs_parse_target_referral()`, etc.) require a single-slash-prefixed path, so callers with `\\`-prefixed UNC strings must pass `path + 1`. [F64, F298]
- `cifs_reopen_file(cfile, can_flush)` must be called with `can_flush = false` from writeback/write-prep paths (`cifs_prepare_write()`, `cifs_get_writable_file()`, `cifs_reopen_persistent_handles()`), and must mask `O_CREAT | O_EXCL | O_TRUNC` off `cfile->f_flags` before `cifs_posix_open()`. [F237, F277]

## Error Handling

- `cifs_reconnect_tcon()` returns `-EAGAIN` after reconnecting for handle-based SMB1 commands (`SMB_COM_READ_ANDX`, `SMB_COM_WRITE_ANDX`, `SMB_COM_CLOSE`, `SMB_COM_FIND_CLOSE2`, `SMB_COM_LOCKING_ANDX`), so handle-based callers in `cifssmb.c` must never loop on `-EAGAIN`, and `CIFSSMBClose()`, `CIFSFindClose()`, `CIFSSMBTDis()`, and `CIFSSMBLogoff()` must map `-EAGAIN` to `0`. [F45]
- Pointers passed to unconditional cleanup (`kfree()` or `ref_walk_free()`) must be reset to `NULL` on `IS_ERR()` or uninitialized early error: `*rw` in `dfs_referral_walk()` when `ref_walk_alloc()` fails; `ctx->prepath`/`cifs_sb->prepath` after `cifs_sanitize_prepath()`; `ctx->source` after `smb3_fs_context_fullpath()`; and `*target_nodes = NULL` before calling `parse_dfs_referrals()`. [F57, F187, F225, F233]

## Known Pitfalls

- UTF-16 length and NUL conventions: `cifs_strtoUTF16()` returns 2-byte code units and writes a 2-byte NUL (callers must multiply by `sizeof(__le16)`); `utf8s_to_utf16s()` in `parse_reparse_wsl_symlink()` returns a signed `wchar_t` count that must be checked for `< 0` and multiplied by 2 before `cifs_strndup_from_utf16()`; `UniStrnlen()` takes a 2-byte code unit count (`symlink_len_utf16 / 2` in `cifs_sfu_type()`) whereas `cifs_strndup_from_utf16()` takes a byte length; `cifs_strndup_to_utf16()` includes the 2-byte NUL in `utf16_len` (`mknod_nfs()` and `SMB2_OP_RENAME`/`HARDLINK` callers must subtract 2); and `cifs_from_utf16()` in `cifs_filldir()` includes the NUL in its return value (must subtract `nls_nullsize(nlt)` from `name.len`). [F275, F304, F391, F471, F477, F480, F485]
- Subtle pitfalls: `cifs_enable_signing()` must test `((global_secflags & CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN) == CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN)` because `CIFSSEC_MUST_SIGN` includes `CIFSSEC_MAY_SIGN`; `cifs_get_disposition()` and `cifs_do_create()` must test `(O_CREAT | O_EXCL)` and `(O_CREAT | O_TRUNC)` with full-mask equality before single-bit checks; `free_xid()` evaluates `(int)rc`, so callers must initialize `int rc = 0` on all paths reaching it; `ctx->echo_interval` (seconds) must be multiplied by `HZ` when stored/compared against `server->echo_interval` (jiffies); and `smb2_unlock_range()`/`smb2_push_mandatory_locks()` must snapshot `server->maxBuf` locally and verify `>= sizeof(struct smb2_lock_element)` before dividing. [F62, F67, F109, F278, F373]
