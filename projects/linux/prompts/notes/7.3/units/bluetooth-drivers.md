# BLUETOOTH DRIVERS

Implements Bluetooth host controller transport drivers for USB (`btusb`), SDIO (`btsdio`, `btmtksdio`), PCMCIA (`dtl1_cs`), RSI (`btrsi`), Virtio (`virtio_bt`), virtual HCI (`hci_vhci`), and UART/Serdev protocols (`hci_ldisc`, `hci_serdev`, H4, BCSP, H5, `hci_bcm`, `hci_intel`, `hci_ll`, `hci_mrvl`, `hci_nokia`, `hci_ag6xx`, `hci_ath`), plus vendor libraries for MediaTek (`btmtk`), Qualcomm (`btqca`), and Realtek (`btrtl`).

## Architecture & Data Flow

- `btusb_recv_intr()`, `btusb_recv_bulk()`, `btusb_recv_isoc()`, and `btmtk_recv_isopkt()` must check `skb_tailroom(skb) < hci_skb_expect(skb)` once the HCI header is complete and free `skb` with `-EILSEQ` if exceeded; `btusb_recv_isoc()` must also validate the handle via `btusb_validate_sco_handle()` unless `HCI_USER_CHANNEL` is set. [F94, F180]
- In `btusb.c`, `btusb_switch_alt_setting()` must kill `data->isoc_anchor`, free/NULL `data->sco_skb` under `data->rxlock`, and call `__set_isoc_interface()`; when `data->sco_num == 0` in `btusb_work()`, it must kill `isoc_anchor`, clear `BTUSB_ISOC_RUNNING`, reset altsetting to 0, and clear `BTUSB_DID_ISO_RESUME` while calling `usb_autopm_put_interface()`. Optional `data->isoc` and `data->diag` can be `NULL` and must be checked first. [F50, F55, F56]
- Firmware coredump RX (`case 0xfc6f:` in `btmtk_usb_recv_acl()` / `btmtksdio_recv_acl()`, and `seqno == 0` in `handle_dump_pkt_qca()`) must disable runtime autosuspend (`usb_disable_autosuspend()` / `pm_runtime_forbid()`). In `btmtk_usb_recv_acl()`, `case 0xfc6f:` must pass `skb_clone(skb)` to `btmtk_process_coredump()` before falling through to `hci_recv_diag()`, while `btmtk_process_coredump()` must check `skb->len > MTK_COREDUMP_END_LEN` before indexing the trailer and fall through from `HCI_DEVCOREDUMP_IDLE` to `HCI_DEVCOREDUMP_ACTIVE`. [F85, F107, F108, F156]
- `handle_dump_pkt_qca()` requires prior validation via `acl_pkt_is_dump_qca()` or `evt_pkt_is_dump_qca()`, pulls `offsetof(struct qca_dump_hdr, data0)` on `seqno == 0` vs `offsetof(..., data)` on `seqno > 0`, sets `BTUSB_HW_SSR_ACTIVE` on `seqno == 0`, and at `out:` (completion or error) re-enables autosuspend, zeroes `ram_dump_size`/`ram_dump_seqno`, and clears `BTUSB_HW_SSR_ACTIVE`. [F61, F95]
- In `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtksdio_close()`, `btmtksdio_remove()`, and PM callbacks must check `test_bit(BTMTKSDIO_FUNC_ENABLED, &bdev->tx_state)` before accessing the SDIO function; `btmtksdio_shutdown()` must skip WMT commands when `BTMTKSDIO_HW_RESET_ACTIVE` is set, and `btmtksdio_setup()` must clear `BT_DL_STATE` in `MT7921_DLSTATUS` and `BTMTKSDIO_HW_RESET_ACTIVE`. [F64, F65]
- In `btqca.c`, `qca_download_firmware()` must copy `fw->data` into a writable `vmalloc()` buffer before `qca_tlv_check_data()` mutates `TLV_TYPE_NVM` tags in place, and `qca_tlv_check_data()` must initialize both `config->dnld_mode` and `config->dnld_type` because `dnld_mode` is overwritten on the final segment while `dnld_type` gates `qca_inject_cmd_complete_event()`. [F69, F70]
- `btrtl_initialize()` returns a valid `struct btrtl_device_info *` with `ic_info == NULL` when unmatched in `ic_id_table`, so `btrtl_download_firmware()`, `btrtl_set_quirks()`, and `btrtl_setup_realtek()` must null-check `btrtl_dev->ic_info`. In `btrtl_parse_section()` and `rtlbt_parse_firmware()`, the firmware's 1-based chip ID must match `btrtl_dev->rom_version + 1`. [F42, F90]
- In `hci_bcsp.c` and `hci_h5.c`, `bcsp_enqueue()`/`h5_enqueue()` must reject `skb->len > 0xfff`, and `bcsp_recv()`/`h5_rx_pkt_start()` must allocate RX SKBs `>= 0x1005` bytes (`H5_MAX_LEN`). Under `unack.lock`, dequeue increments `msgq_txseq`/`tx_seq` mod 8, queues to `unack`, and arms the timer; timeout moves SKBs from `unack` tail to `rel` head while decrementing `(seq - 1) & 0x07`; and `bcsp_pkt_cull()`/`h5_pkt_cull()` walk backward `(seq - 1) & 0x07` at most `skb_queue_len(&unack)` times and stop the timer when `unack` empties. [F230, F231, F250, F252, F253, F267, F276]

## Synchronization

- In `btusb.c`, `data->rxlock` (`spin_lock_irqsave`) protects `data->evt_skb`, `data->acl_skb`, and `data->sco_skb`, which must be freed with `dev_kfree_skb_irq()` (not `kfree_skb()`) in `btusb_free_frags()` and `btusb_switch_alt_setting()` and set to `NULL` before unlocking. `data->txlock` (`spin_lock_irqsave`/`spin_lock_irq`) protects `BTUSB_SUSPENDING` and `data->tx_in_flight`, and `btusb_oob_wake_handler()` (`disable_irq_nosync()`) and `btusb_resume()` must guard disabling `oob_wake_irq` with `test_and_clear_bit(BTUSB_OOB_WAKE_ENABLED, &data->flags)`. [F8, F31, F58, F157, F159, F162]
- `btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()` must wait on `BTMTK_TX_WAIT_VND_EVT` with `TASK_UNINTERRUPTIBLE`; when waking a bit-waiter (`btmtk_usb_wmt_recv`, `btmtksdio_recv_event`, `mrvl_recv_fw_req`, `mrvl_recv_chip_ver`), the receiver must store `evt_skb` or `mrvl->tx_len`/`id`/`rev` before clearing the wait bit and call `smp_mb__after_atomic()` before `wake_up_bit()`. [F22, F166, F207, F312]
- In `btmtksdio.c`, `btmtksdio_fw_pmctrl()` and `btmtksdio_drv_pmctrl()` must claim the SDIO host and poll `MTK_REG_CHLPCR` (and `MTK_REG_PD2HRM0R` when applicable); `btmtksdio_open()` must clear `C_INT_EN_CLR` and mask `MTK_REG_CHIER` before `sdio_claim_irq()`; and `btmtksdio_close()` must clear `C_INT_EN_CLR`, call `sdio_release_irq()`, and `cancel_work_sync(&bdev->txrx_work)` before `btmtksdio_fw_pmctrl()` and `sdio_disable_func()`. [F29, F133, F150]
- In `hci_ldisc.c`, `hci_uart_tx_wakeup()` uses `percpu_down_read_trylock(&hu->proto_lock)`, so `percpu_down_write(&hu->proto_lock)` must only be acquired in `hci_uart_tty_close()` and released before `cancel_work_sync(&hu->write_work)` or `hci_unregister_dev()`. `hci_uart_tx_wakeup()` must set `HCI_UART_TX_WAKEUP` before `test_and_set_bit(HCI_UART_SENDING, ...)`, and `hci_uart_write_work()` must clear `HCI_UART_TX_WAKEUP` before dequeuing and recheck it after clearing `HCI_UART_SENDING`. [F199, F202, F303]
- In `hci_bcsp.c` and `hci_h5.c`, `unack.lock` must be acquired before `rel.lock` via `spin_lock_irqsave_nested(..., SINGLE_DEPTH_NESTING)`, and `bcsp_prepare_pkt()`, `h5_prepare_pkt()`, `h4_recv_buf()`, `bcsp_recv()`, `h5_recv()`, and their `recv` callbacks must allocate SKBs with `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F203, F307, F311]
- `bcm_device_lock` protects `bcm_device_list`, `bcm_device_exists()`, and platform `bcm->dev` / `bdev->hu` in `hci_bcm.c`. In `hci_intel.c`, `idev->hu_lock` nests inside `intel_device_list_lock` (`intel_suspend_device()`/`intel_resume_device()` must only take `idev->hu_lock`), and `intel_irq()` must be a threaded IRQ (`IRQF_ONESHOT`). In `hci_vhci.c`, `data->open_mutex` serializes `__vhci_create_device()`, must never be taken in `hdev` callbacks (`vhci_send_frame()`), and must never be held across `cancel_delayed_work_sync(&data->open_timeout)`. [F38, F209, F242, F246, F309, F310, F313]

## Object Lifetimes

- `hci_devcd_append()` consumes and may immediately free `skb` even on error, so callers (`btmtk_process_coredump()`, `handle_dump_pkt_qca()`) must read `skb->data` and `skb->len` before calling it and never call `kfree_skb(skb)` after; `btmtk_process_coredump()` unconditionally takes ownership of `skb` and must free it on any early return. [F1, F2]
- `hdev->send` callbacks (`btusb_send_frame`, `btsdio_send_frame`, `btmtksdio_send_frame`, `dtl1_hci_send_frame`, `rsi_hci_send_pkt`) must not free `skb` on error because the caller frees it; conversely, `hci_uart_send_frame()` ignores `hu->proto->enqueue(hu, skb)`'s return value, so every `enqueue` callback must queue or free `skb` on all paths. [F174, F322]
- In `btsdio_work()`, `btmtksdio_txrx_work()`, and `vhci_read()`, failed packets are requeued to `txq`/`readq` head via `skb_queue_head()`: `btsdio_tx_packet()` and `btmtksdio_tx_packet()` must `skb_pull()` their pushed header and not free `skb` on error, and `vhci_read()` must requeue `skb` on `vhci_put_user()` failure. In `bcsp_dequeue()` and `h5_dequeue()`, reliable SKBs move to `unack` on `prepare_pkt` success or requeue to `rel` head on failure, while unreliable SKBs are freed on success or requeued on failure. [F11, F188, F196]
- `btrtl_initialize()` returns an owned `struct btrtl_device_info *` that callers must release via `btrtl_free()` (never `kfree()`). Because `patch_subsecs` entries store borrowed pointers into `btrtl_dev->fw_data`, `fw_data` must outlive `rtlbt_parse_firmware_v2()` and `btrtl_free()` must only free each `entry` via `list_for_each_entry_safe()`. In `btmtk.c` and `btmtksdio.c`, whenever `evt_skb` is freed via `kfree_skb()`, it must immediately be set to `NULL`. [F5, F6, F7, F109]
- In `hci_ldisc.c` and `hci_serdev.c`, `hci_uart_unregister_device()` and `hci_uart_tty_close()` must call `cancel_work_sync(&hu->init_ready)` before checking `HCI_UART_REGISTERED`, and `cancel_work_sync(&hu->write_work)` before `hu->proto->close(hu)`. `bcsp_close()` and `h5_close()` must synchronously stop their timers (`timer_shutdown_sync`/`timer_delete_sync`) before purging queues, and `serdev_device_close(hu->serdev)` must only run when clearing `HCI_UART_PROTO_READY`. [F190, F192, F289, F314]
- `vhci_release()` must synchronously cancel `data->open_timeout` and `data->suspend_work` and call `vhci_debugfs_remove(hdev)` (when `!IS_ERR_OR_NULL(hdev->debugfs)`) before unregistering `hdev` and freeing `data`. `virtbt_probe()` must register `vbt->hdev` before `virtio_device_ready()`, `virtbt_remove()` must call `virtio_reset_device()` and `virtbt_close_vdev()` (`cancel_work_sync(&vbt->rx)`) before detaching buffers or freeing `hdev`, and `virtbt_rx_done()` must defer `virtbt_add_inbuf()` (`GFP_KERNEL`) to `virtbt_rx_work()`. [F211, F212, F228, F315]

## API Contracts

- Any `struct hci_dev` passed to `btrtl_*` / `btrealtek_*` or `btmtk.c` helpers must be allocated with `sizeof(struct btrealtek_data)` or `sizeof(struct btmtk_data)` via `hci_alloc_dev_priv()` (or `hci_uart_register_device_priv()` in `h5_vnd` / serdev drivers), because they cast `hci_get_priv(hdev)` directly; `btmtksdio` uses `hci_alloc_dev()` and must not call `btmtk.c` helpers. [F126, F127, F282]
- Serdev drivers using `hci_serdev.c` (`struct bcm_device`, `struct h5`, etc.) must keep `struct hci_uart` as the first struct member because `serdev_device_get_drvdata()` is cast directly to `struct hci_uart *`. Because `hci_uart_register_device_priv()` calls `p->open(hu)` before `hci_alloc_dev_priv()`, serdev-capable `open` callbacks must not dereference `hu->hdev`, and `hci_uart_proto` drivers must store private state in `hu->priv` rather than overwriting `hci_set_drvdata(hdev, hu)`. [F73, F281, F286, F287]
- `h4_recv_buf()` frees any partial `skb` before returning an `ERR_PTR` and ignores `pkts[i].recv()`'s return value, so callers storing `rx_skb` must reset `rx_skb = NULL` on `IS_ERR(rx_skb)` without calling `kfree_skb()`, every `h4_recv_pkt.recv` callback must consume or free `skb`, and each `struct h4_recv_pkt` must have `lsize` in `{0, 1, 2}`, `loff + lsize <= hlen` (when `lsize > 0`), and `maxlen >= hlen`. [F213, F248, F323]
- TX URB allocation helpers (`alloc_ctrl_urb`, `alloc_bulk_urb`, `alloc_isoc_urb`, `alloc_diag_urb`, `alloc_mtk_intr_urb`) return `ERR_PTR(-errno)` rather than `NULL` (check `IS_ERR(urb)`), and must set `urb->context = skb` and `skb->dev = (void *)hdev` (and `kfree(urb->setup_packet)` on control URB failure/completion). In `btusb_resume()` and `btmtk_usb_resume()`, RX URB submissions must use `GFP_NOIO`. [F10, F49, F128, F160]
- Vendor coredump triggers (`btusb_coredump_qca`, `btmtk_coredump`, `btrtl_coredump`) must use `__hci_cmd_send()` rather than `__hci_cmd_sync()`, and commands that suppress `HCI_EV_CMD_COMPLETE` (`qca_download_firmware()`, `BTINTEL_HCI_OP_RESET` in `btusb_send_frame_intel()`, or `intel_dequeue()` while `STATE_BOOTLOADER` is set) must inject a synthetic `HCI_EV_CMD_COMPLETE` via `hci_recv_frame()` (so `intel_setup()` must clear `STATE_BOOTLOADER` before returning success). [F172, F173, F243]
- `hdev->quirk_flags` and `realtek->flags` are `DECLARE_BITMAP` arrays and must be accessed via `hci_set_quirk()`/`btrealtek_set_flag()` helpers. In `btusb_hci_drv_read_info()`, `rp_size` must scale `num_supported_commands` by `sizeof(__le16)`, `rp->num_supported_commands` must be set before indexing `rp->supported_commands[]` (`__counted_by_le`), and `rp->driver_name` must use `strscpy_pad()`. In `btmtk.h`, `btqca.h`, and `btrtl.h`, every declaration must have a matching `#else` stub. [F78, F124, F125, F132]

## Error Handling

- In `btqca.c`, helpers that initialize `err = 0` and jump to `out` (`qca_read_soc_version()`, `qca_read_fw_build_info()`, `qca_read_fw_board_id()`, `qca_tlv_send_segment()`) must assign a negative errno to `err` before every `goto out`, and in `qca_download_firmware()`, every error path after `data = vmalloc(...)` must jump to `goto out` to call `vfree(data)`. [F4, F43]
- In `btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()` and `mtk_hci_wmt_sync()`, `wait_on_bit_timeout()` returns `1` on timeout, so any non-zero non-`-EINTR` return must clear the wait bit and set `err = -ETIMEDOUT`; when polling `btmtk_usb_func_query()` or `btmtksdio_func_query()` via `readx_poll_timeout()`, callers must check both `err < 0` and signed `status < 0`. Coredump registration (`hci_devcd_register()`, `btmtk_register_coredump()`) returns `-EOPNOTSUPP` when `CONFIG_DEV_COREDUMP` is disabled and must not abort setup. [F45, F47, F48]

## External Contracts

- In `btqca.c`, QCA EDL commands returning Command Complete (`qca_read_fw_build_info()`, `qca_send_patch_config_cmd()`, `qca_read_fw_board_id()`, and `soc_type >= QCA_WCN3991` in `qca_read_soc_version()` / `qca_tlv_send_segment()`) must pass `0` as `event` to `__hci_cmd_sync_ev()` rather than `HCI_EV_VENDOR`, and on `soc_type >= QCA_WCN3991` `qca_read_soc_version()` reads `qca_btsoc_version` from `edl->data + 1`. `qca_set_bdaddr()` and `hci_ll.c`'s `hci_ti_probe()` / `ll_set_bdaddr()` must byte-swap `bdaddr_t` via `baswap()`. [F44, F83, F84, F264]
- In MediaTek WMT commands (`btmtk_usb_hci_wmt_sync()`, `mtk_hci_wmt_sync()`), `hlen` must be `<= 255`, `hdr->dlen` is `cpu_to_le16(wmt_params->dlen + 1)`, and `btmtk_hci_wmt_evt_funcc::status` is big-endian (`be16_to_cpu()`). In `virtio_bt.c`, `virtbt_probe()` must check `VIRTIO_BT_F_CONFIG_V2` before reading config fields beyond `type`. [F86, F87, F260]

## Known Pitfalls

- `btusb_reset()` and vendor `hdev->reset` callbacks (`btusb_intel_reset`, `btusb_rtl_reset`, `btusb_qca_reset`) must reset the device directly via `usb_queue_reset_device(data->intf)` or GPIO and never call `hdev->reset(hdev)`. [F105]
- In `alloc_mtk_intr_urb()`, outgoing MediaTek ISO packets must have `skb->len <= MTK_ISO_THRESHOLD` and call `btmtk_isopkt_pad()` before `usb_fill_int_urb()` captures `skb->data` and `skb->len`. In `rsi_hci_send_pkt()`, `bt_cb(new_skb)->pkt_type` must be copied on `skb_realloc_headroom()`, and DMA alignment must use `memmove()` (not `memcpy()`). [F72, F123, F129, F181]
- In `btqca.c`, `qca_read_fw_build_info()` must copy the non-NUL-terminated build label at `&edl->data[1]` with `kstrndup()` after checking `skb->len`; in `qca_tlv_check_data()` (`TLV_TYPE_NVM`), after `tlv++`, the bounds check must use `length + (tlv->data - fw_data)` rather than `length + sizeof(struct tlv_type_hdr)`. [F130, F131]
