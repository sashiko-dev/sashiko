# VIRTIO AND VHOST VSOCK DRIVER

Implements the virtio and vhost transport layers for AF_VSOCK stream and seqpacket sockets between host and guest VMs: `net/vmw_vsock/virtio_transport_common.c` provides shared packet framing, flow-control credit accounting, zero-copy SKB construction, and socket state machine logic, while `net/vmw_vsock/virtio_transport.c` implements the guest-to-host virtio driver and `drivers/vhost/vsock.c` implements the host-to-guest `/dev/vhost-vsock` backend driver.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- `virtio_vsock_hdr(skb)` accesses `struct virtio_vsock_hdr` at `skb->head` in the `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM` bytes before `skb->data`, while `skb->data` points to payload and `skb->len` counts only payload bytes. For nonlinear SKBs (`skb_is_nonlinear(skb)`), the linear buffer must contain only the header (`skb_headroom(skb) == VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM`) with all payload in `skb_shinfo(skb)->frags` (`<= MAX_SKB_FRAGS`, matching `virtio_vsock::out_sgs[MAX_SKB_FRAGS + 1]`), and `virtio_vsock_skb_put()` must set `skb->len = len` directly rather than calling `skb_put()`. [F78, F84, F118]
- Because `__virtio_vsock_alloc_skb_with_frags()` does not zero SKB headroom, `virtio_transport_init_hdr()` must explicitly initialize every field of `struct virtio_vsock_hdr` in `skb->head` (including zeroing `hdr->buf_alloc` and `hdr->fwd_cnt`), and `virtio_vsock_rx_fill()` must zero `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM` bytes at `skb->head`. [F56]
- Before passing a packet to `virtio_transport_recv_pkt()`, both `vhost_vsock_alloc_skb()` and `virtio_transport_rx_work()` must validate that `len >= sizeof(struct virtio_vsock_hdr)` and `le32_to_cpu(hdr->len) <= len - sizeof(*hdr)`. In `vhost_vsock_alloc_skb()`, `len = iov_length(vq->iov, out)` must also be bounded within `[VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM, VIRTIO_VSOCK_MAX_PKT_BUF_SIZE + VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM]` before `virtio_vsock_alloc_skb()`. [F80, F163]
- Every SKB with `virtio_vsock_skb_reply(skb)` queued to `send_pkt_queue` must increment `queued_replies` (never on `virtio_transport_send_skb_fast_path()`), and when fully sent or purged by `virtio_transport_purge_skbs()`, `queued_replies` must be decremented and the stopped RX/TX handler (`tx_vq->poll` or `rx_work`) restarted if the count drops below the vring size. When `virtio_transport_more_replies()` or `vhost_vsock_more_replies()` is false, `virtio_transport_rx_work()` and `vhost_vsock_handle_tx_kick()` must jump out (`goto out` / `goto no_more_replies`), bypassing `virtqueue_enable_cb()` / `vhost_enable_notify()`. [F71, F104]
- When `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()` splits an SKB across guest RX buffers (`payload_len > iov_len - sizeof(*hdr)`), it must clear `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SEQ_EOM` and `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SEQ_EOR` from `hdr->flags` before copying the non-final header, then restore those flags and call `virtio_vsock_skb_clear_tap_delivered(skb)` before requeueing. In both `virtio_transport_send_skb()` and `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()`, `virtio_transport_deliver_tap_pkt(skb)` must only be called after the packet/fragment is queued or copied to guest descriptors. [F70, F160]
- In `virtio_transport_stream_do_dequeue()` and `virtio_transport_notify_set_rcvlowat()`, wrapping `u32` counters `vvs->fwd_cnt` and `vvs->last_fwd_cnt` must be compared only via `!=` or 32-bit unsigned subtraction (`u32 fwd_cnt_delta = vvs->fwd_cnt - vvs->last_fwd_cnt`), never `<` or `>`. When `vvs->fwd_cnt != vvs->last_fwd_cnt`, `virtio_transport_send_credit_update()` must be called if `vvs->rx_bytes` is below the receive low-water mark (`sock_rcvlowat()` or new `SO_RCVLOWAT`) or `free_space < VIRTIO_VSOCK_MAX_PKT_BUF_SIZE`. [F66, F114]
- Whenever an SKB with `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SEQ_EOM` in `le32_to_cpu(hdr->flags)` is enqueued or dequeued on `vvs->rx_queue` (in `virtio_transport_recv_enqueue()`, `virtio_transport_seqpacket_do_dequeue()`, or `virtio_transport_read_skb()`), `vvs->msg_count` must be incremented or decremented in lockstep under `vvs->rx_lock`. [F64]
- In `virtio_transport_do_socket_init()` and `virtio_transport_notify_buffer_size()`, `u64` buffer sizes (`vsk->buffer_size` or `*val`) must be clamped to `VIRTIO_VSOCK_MAX_BUF_SIZE` (`0xFFFFFFFFUL`) before assigning to `u32 vvs->buf_alloc`. [F112]
- Full-shutdown checks on `vsk->peer_shutdown` and `sk->sk_shutdown` in `virtio_transport_close()`, `virtio_transport_recv_connected()`, and `virtio_transport_recv_listen()` must test equality against `SHUTDOWN_MASK` (`RCV_SHUTDOWN | SEND_SHUTDOWN`), not a non-zero bitwise AND. [F95]
- `info->vsk` in `virtio_transport_alloc_skb()` and `skb->sk` in `virtio_transport_consume_skb_sent()` can be `NULL` (for socketless `VIRTIO_VSOCK_OP_RST` packets), so `skb_set_owner_w()` and `vsock_sk(skb->sk)` must be guarded by a non-NULL check and `zcopy` must be `false` when `info->vsk` is `NULL`. [F54]
- `virtio_vsock_vqs_init()` must reset `vsock->rx_buf_nr` and `vsock->rx_buf_max_nr` (under `vsock->rx_lock`) and `vsock->queued_replies` to 0, and call `virtio_vsock_update_guest_cid(vsock)`. On `VIRTIO_VSOCK_EVENT_TRANSPORT_RESET` (handled in `virtio_transport_event_work()` only when `len == sizeof(*event)`), `virtio_vsock_event_handle()` must refresh `vsock->guest_cid` and reset connected sockets via `virtio_vsock_reset_sock`, while `virtio_transport_recv_pkt()` must update `vsk->local_addr.svm_cid` to `dst.svm_cid` when `vsk->local_addr.svm_cid != VMADDR_CID_ANY`. [F72, F73, F92]

## Synchronization
- Spinlocks `vvs->tx_lock`, `vvs->rx_lock`, and `send_pkt_queue.lock` must always use `spin_lock_bh()` / `spin_unlock_bh()`. After `virtio_transport_do_socket_init()`, all accesses to `tx_cnt`, `peer_fwd_cnt`, `peer_buf_alloc`, and `bytes_unsent` must hold `vvs->tx_lock`, and on active sockets (before `SOCK_DONE` in `virtio_transport_remove_sock()`), all accesses to `fwd_cnt`, `last_fwd_cnt`, `rx_bytes`, `buf_used`, `rx_queue`, and `msg_count` must hold `vvs->rx_lock`. [F36, F37, F141]
- `virtio_transport_stream_do_peek()`, `virtio_transport_stream_do_dequeue()`, `virtio_transport_seqpacket_do_peek()`, and `virtio_transport_seqpacket_do_dequeue()` must drop `vvs->rx_lock` via `spin_unlock_bh()` before calling `skb_copy_datagram_iter()` and keep `vvs->rx_lock` balanced across all exits. [F23]
- In `virtio_transport_send_pkt()`, `virtio_transport_send_skb_fast_path()` may only be called when `skb_queue_empty_lockless(&vsock->send_pkt_queue)` is true. Because `virtio_transport_send_skb_fast_path()` and `virtio_transport_build_skb()` (via `vsock_deliver_tap()`) run under `rcu_read_lock()`, they must never sleep: `virtio_transport_send_skb_fast_path()` must use `mutex_trylock(&vsock->tx_lock)` and pass `GFP_ATOMIC` to `virtio_transport_send_skb()`, and `virtio_transport_build_skb()` must allocate with `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F45, F137, F138]
- Callbacks passed to `vsock_for_each_connected_socket()` (`vhost_vsock_reset_orphans()` and `virtio_vsock_reset_sock()`) run under `spin_lock_bh(&vsock_table_lock)` and must never acquire `lock_sock(sk)` or sleep. Virtqueue callbacks (`virtio_vsock_rx_done()`, `virtio_vsock_tx_done()`, `virtio_vsock_event_done()`) run in interrupt context and must only check `vq->vdev->priv` and queue work on `virtio_vsock_workqueue`. [F140, F143]
- In `drivers/vhost/vsock.c`, `vsock->dev.mutex` must be acquired outer to `vq->mutex` (in `vhost_vsock_start()`, `vhost_vsock_stop()`, and `vhost_vsock_set_features()`), and `vhost_vsock_flush()` must be called without holding `vq->mutex`. [F144]

## Object Lifetimes
- Every socket-bound `sk_buff` must set `skb->sk` via `skb_set_owner_w()` in `virtio_transport_alloc_skb()` (before `virtio_transport_fill_skb()`) or `skb_set_owner_sk_safe()` in `virtio_transport_recv_pkt()` so `virtio_transport_purge_skbs()` and `virtio_transport_consume_skb_sent()` can match `skb->sk`. [F5]
- Closing a socket in `virtio_transport_release()` or `virtio_transport_cancel_close_work()` must call `virtio_transport_remove_sock(vsk)` so `vvs->rx_queue` is purged via `__skb_queue_purge(&vvs->rx_queue)` before `vsock_remove_sock(vsk)`. `virtio_transport_destruct()` must call `virtio_transport_cancel_close_work(vsk, true)`, `kfree(vvs)`, and set `vsk->trans = NULL`. [F14, F19]
- In `virtio_transport_close()`, `sock_hold(sk)` and `vsk->close_work_scheduled = true` must be set under `lock_sock(sk)` before `schedule_delayed_work()`, and `virtio_transport_close_timeout()` must pin an extra `sock_hold(sk)` around `lock_sock(sk)` and recheck `!sock_flag(sk, SOCK_DONE)`. `virtio_transport_cancel_close_work()` must only clear `vsk->close_work_scheduled`, call `virtio_transport_remove_sock(vsk)`, and drop `sock_put(sk)` when `vsk->close_work_scheduled` is true and either `!cancel_timeout` or `cancel_delayed_work(&vsk->close_work)` succeeds. [F43, F77]
- Only one `virtio_vsock` instance is supported (`-EBUSY` if `the_virtio_vsock` is non-NULL under `the_virtio_vsock_mutex`). In `virtio_vsock_probe()` and `virtio_vsock_restore()`, `vdev->priv`, `vsock->seqpacket_allow`, and `vsock->out_sgs[i] = &vsock->out_bufs[i]` must be initialized before `virtio_vsock_vqs_init()` calls `virtio_device_ready(vdev)` (after `virtio_find_vqs()`), and `rcu_assign_pointer(the_virtio_vsock, vsock)` must precede `virtio_vsock_vqs_start(vsock)`. [F25, F26, F27, F124, F131]
- In `virtio_vsock_remove()` and `virtio_vsock_freeze()`, `rcu_assign_pointer(the_virtio_vsock, NULL)` and `synchronize_rcu()` must execute under `the_virtio_vsock_mutex` before `virtio_vsock_vqs_del(vsock)` (while `freeze` preserves `vdev->priv` for `restore`). Inside `virtio_vsock_vqs_del()`, `rx_run`, `tx_run`, and `event_run` must be cleared under their mutexes before `virtio_reset_device(vdev)`, which must precede `virtqueue_detach_unused_buf()`, `virtio_vsock_skb_queue_purge()`, and `vdev->config->del_vqs(vdev)`. [F126, F132, F147]

## API Contracts
- Any transport using `virtio_transport_common.c` callbacks must embed `struct vsock_transport` as `struct virtio_transport::transport` for `virtio_transport_get_ops()`. `virtio_transport_can_zcopy()` must check `t_ops->can_msgzerocopy` for non-NULL before calling it with `pages_to_send + 1` (accounting for the header scatterlist entry). [F61, F152]
- `virtio_transport_purge_skbs()` must iterate with `skb_queue_walk_safe()` under `queue->lock` and return only the count of purged SKBs with `virtio_vsock_skb_reply(skb)` set, because `vhost_transport_cancel_pkt()` and `virtio_transport_cancel_pkt()` subtract its return value from `vsock->queued_replies`. [F49, F101]
- `virtio_transport_dec_rx_pkt(vvs, bytes_read, bytes_dequeued)` subtracts `bytes_read` from `vvs->rx_bytes` on every partial read, whereas `bytes_dequeued` (`le32_to_cpu(virtio_vsock_hdr(skb)->len)`) is subtracted from `vvs->buf_used` and added to `vvs->fwd_cnt` only when an entire SKB is unlinked. [F60]
- Callers of `virtio_vsock_alloc_skb()` and `virtio_vsock_alloc_linear_skb()` must include `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SKB_HEADROOM` in `size`. In `virtio_transport_seqpacket_enqueue()`, `size_t len` must be checked against `u32 vvs->peer_buf_alloc` before `virtio_transport_stream_enqueue()` narrows `len` to `u32 info.pkt_len`. [F113, F115]
- `VIRTIO_VSOCK_F_SEQPACKET` is a bit index (`1`): virtio APIs (`virtio_has_feature()`) take it directly, whereas vhost bitmasks (`VHOST_VSOCK_FEATURES`, `vhost_vsock_set_features()`) must shift it as `1ULL << VIRTIO_VSOCK_F_SEQPACKET`. `vhost_vsock_set_features()` must reject bits outside `VHOST_VSOCK_FEATURES` with `-EOPNOTSUPP`, validate `VHOST_F_LOG_ALL` and `VIRTIO_F_ACCESS_PLATFORM` under `vsock->dev.mutex`, and update `vq->acked_features` under each `vq->mutex`. [F58, F133]
- `vhost_vsock_dev_open()` must pass `UIO_MAXIOV` to `vhost_dev_init()`, and `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()` / `vhost_vsock_handle_tx_kick()` must pass `ARRAY_SIZE(vq->iov)` to `vhost_get_vq_desc()` while rejecting nonzero `out` on RX and nonzero `in` on TX. `vhost_vsock_start()` and `vhost_vsock_stop(vsock, true)` must check `vhost_dev_check_owner(&vsock->dev)` under `vsock->dev.mutex`, while `vhost_vsock_dev_release()` passes `check_owner = false`. [F82, F167]

## Error Handling
- `vhost_vsock_init()` and `virtio_vsock_init()` error paths and module exit functions must unwind registrations and `virtio_vsock_workqueue` in reverse order. If `vhost_vsock_start()` fails on any virtqueue, it must clear `vhost_vq_set_backend(vq, NULL)` under `vq->mutex` on the failing and all previously initialized virtqueues before releasing `vsock->dev.mutex`. [F7, F8, F12]
- `virtio_transport_stream_do_dequeue()` must inspect the head packet with `skb_peek(&vvs->rx_queue)` and only advance `offset`, decrement `vvs->rx_bytes`, and unlink/free `skb` after `skb_copy_datagram_iter()` succeeds; if `skb_copy_datagram_iter()` fails in `stream_do_peek()` or `stream_do_dequeue()` after copying bytes (`total > 0`), they must return `total`. In `virtio_transport_seqpacket_do_dequeue()`, on copy failure `dequeued_len` is set to the negative error while the loop continues dequeuing and freeing fragments until `VIRTIO_VSOCK_SEQ_EOM`. [F22, F48, F62]
- `virtio_transport_fill_skb()` must use `skb_copy_datagram_from_iter_full()` rather than `skb_copy_datagram_from_iter()` so `msg->msg_iter` is reverted on fault. [F68]
- Callers of `vhost_get_vq_desc()` must store the result in a signed `int head` and check both `head < 0` and `head == vq->num`. On descriptor exhaustion in `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()` or `virtio_transport_send_pkt_work()`, the dequeued `skb` must be requeued at the head of `vsock->send_pkt_queue` via `virtio_vsock_skb_queue_head()`. [F24, F50]
- In `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()`, guest RX buffer length `iov_len` must be checked `>= sizeof(*hdr)` before subtracting `sizeof(*hdr)`. Invalid guest descriptors or copy faults in `drivers/vhost/vsock.c` must report via `vq_err()` and free `skb` rather than calling `WARN*()`. [F81, F168]

## External Contracts
- In `vhost_add_used(vq, head, len)`, `len` must be `0` on `VSOCK_VQ_TX` in `vhost_vsock_handle_tx_kick()` (device-readable `out` buffers) and `sizeof(*hdr) + payload_len` on `VSOCK_VQ_RX` in `vhost_transport_do_send_pkt()`. Virtqueue loops in both functions must terminate when `vhost_exceeds_weight(vq, ++pkts, total_len)` is true. [F57, F136]
- Wire and config CID fields (`virtio_vsock_hdr::src_cid`, `dst_cid`, `virtio_vsock_config::guest_cid`) are `__le64` and must use `le64_to_cpu()` / `cpu_to_le64()`, even though in-memory CIDs are `u32`. [F87]

## Known Pitfalls
- Listener sockets (`TCP_LISTEN`) have `vsk->transport == NULL` and `vsk->trans == NULL`, so `virtio_transport_send_pkt_info()` and `virtio_transport_reset()` must never be called on them, and `virtio_transport_recv_listen()` error paths must call `virtio_transport_reset_no_sock(t, skb)`. Both `virtio_transport_get_ops(vsk)` and `t` in `virtio_transport_reset_no_sock(t, skb)` can be `NULL` and must be checked before dereferencing or allocating a reply SKB. [F51, F52]
- `vhost_vsock_dev_open()` allocates `struct vhost_vsock` with `kvmalloc()` (not `kvzalloc()`), so every field must be explicitly initialized there, and `vhost_vsock_set_features()` must unconditionally assign `vsock->seqpacket_allow`. `struct vhost_vsock::vqs` has only 2 elements (`VSOCK_VQ_RX`, `VSOCK_VQ_TX`), not `VSOCK_VQ_MAX` (3), so loops in `drivers/vhost/vsock.c` must use `ARRAY_SIZE(vsock->vqs)`. [F55, F83]
- Every insertion and lookup on `vhost_vsock_hash` (`vhost_vsock_set_cid()`, `vhost_vsock_get()`) must pass a 32-bit `u32` key rather than a `u64` expression, because `hash_min()` dispatches on `sizeof(key)`. [F110]
