# INTEL MANAGEMENT ENGINE (mei)

The Intel Management Engine Interface (MEI) subsystem provides the core character device interface (`/dev/mei*`), client bus abstraction, hardware transport bindings (PCI ME, PCI TXE, and SPI/platform Visual Sensing Controller), and client drivers (iAMT watchdog, i915/Xe PXP, and late-binding firmware upload). It manages host-to-firmware communication sessions, virtual tags (vtags), flow control credits, power gating transitions, and firmware loading.

## Architecture & Data Flow

- In `mei_read()`, `*offset` and the completed read callback `cb` must be updated in lockstep: `*offset` is reset to `0` when no completed `cb` is queued before `mei_cl_read_start()` and whenever `mei_cl_del_rd_completed(cl, cb)` frees `cb`, while `cb` is kept in `cl->rd_completed` when `*offset < cb->buf_idx` [F58].
- After initial device setup, `dev->dev_state` changes must use `mei_set_devstate()` rather than direct assignment so `dev->wait_dev_state` waiters and sysfs pollers (`sysfs_notify()`) are woken; in `mei_write()`, user `length` must be checked against `mei_cl_mtu(cl)` (`-EFBIG` if larger) and checked for zero before `mei_cl_alloc_cb()` [F59, F72].
- In `vsc_tp_xfer_helper()`, `pkt` (`tp->tx_buf`) must be zeroed with `memset(pkt, 0, VSC_TP_MAX_XFER_SIZE)` right after the first `vsc_tp_dev_xfer()` so subsequent SPI reads clock out zeroes on TX, and payload copying into `ibuf` must be bounded by `min(ilen, le16_to_cpu(ack.len))` while still consuming and accumulating CRC over any remaining bytes up to `sizeof(ack) + le16_to_cpu(ack.len)` [F51, F80].
- In `vsc-tp.c`, `struct vsc_tp_packet.buf` must stay a fixed-size array (`VSC_TP_MAX_XFER_SIZE - sizeof(struct vsc_tp_packet_hdr)`) rather than `__counted_by(hdr.len)` because `vsc_tp_xfer()` writes the CRC at `pkt->buf + olen`; in `platform-vsc.c`, `mei_vsc_read()` must check `mei_vsc_read_helper() >= sizeof(u32)` before `get_unaligned_le32(&hw->rx_hdr)`, and `mei_vsc_read_slots()` must check `len == hdr->length` and `hdr->length + sizeof(*hdr) == hw->rx_len` [F81, F103].
- In `vsc_identify_csi_image()` and `vsc_identify_ace_image()`, the first `struct vsc_fw_sign` sits at `img->image_location + le32_to_cpu(img->image_count)` (not `img + 1`), each `sign` must be bounds-checked via `(u8 *)sign + sizeof(*sign) <= image->data + image->size`, and `vsc_identify_csi_image()` must run first because a zero `img->image_location[i]` in ACE derives `frag->location` as `ALIGN(last_frag->location + last_frag->size, SZ_4K)` from `fw_loader->frags[frag_index - 1]` [F76, F98, F105].
- In `vsc_identify_cfg_image()`, `image->size` must be `> sizeof(u32)`, `<= VSC_SKU_MAX_SIZE`, and equal to `le32_to_cpu(*((__le32 *)image->data)) + sizeof(u32)`; in `vsc_download_firmware()`, the `VSC_CMD_DL_SET` loop must iterate `fw_loader->frags[]` from `VSC_IMG_CSI_SEM_FRAG` through `VSC_IMG_CSI_EM7D_FRAG` inclusive (matching `fw_loader->count`), whereas `VSC_CMD_DL_START` iterates up to `VSC_IMG_FRAG_MAX` exclusive [F77, F79].
- In `mei_me_pci_tbl` and `mei_me_probe()`, PCH PCI IDs that can run SPS or IGN firmware without MEI support must use an `*_SPS_*_CFG` config and check `mei_me_quirk_probe(pdev, cfg)` before `pcim_enable_device(pdev)`; across `mei_me_probe()` and `mei_me_remove()`, `pm_runtime_put_noidle(&pdev->dev)` and `pm_runtime_get_noresume(&pdev->dev)` must both be gated on `if (mei_pg_is_enabled(dev))` [F127, F129].
- In `drivers/watchdog/mei_wdt.c`, `mei_wdt_register()` must cap `wdt->wdd.max_timeout` at `MEI_WDT_MAX_TIMEOUT` (`65535`) to fit 16-bit `wdt->timeout`, `mei_wdt_ops_set_timeout()` must update both `wdt->timeout` and `wdd->timeout`, `mei_wdt_ops_start()` must set `wdd->timeout = wdt->timeout`, outgoing requests must set `req.hdr.bytecount = sizeof(req) - offsetof(struct mei_mc_hdr, subcommand)`, and `mei_wdt_rx()` must ignore 0-byte responses and reject `< sizeof(struct mei_mc_hdr)` [F62, F83, F101].

## Synchronization

- In `mei_read()`, `mei_write()`, `mei_fsync()`, `mei_ioctl_connect_client()`, and `mei_ioctl_connect_vtag()`, `dev->device_lock` must be dropped before `wait_event*()` on `cl->rx_wait`, `cl->tx_wait`, `cl->wait`, or `dev->wait_dev_state` and relocked to re-verify state; in `mei_read()`, after waiting on `cl->rx_wait`, `mei_cl_is_connected(cl)` must be rechecked and `mei_cl_read_cb(cl, file)` refetched, returning `0` (not looping) if `cb` is `NULL` [F21, F145].
- `cl->rd_completed` must be accessed under `spinlock_t rd_completed_lock` because `mei_cl_read_cb()` inspects it inside `wait_event_interruptible()` in `mei_read()` while `dev->device_lock` is dropped; in `main.c`, `mei_minor_get()` and `mei_minor_free()` on `mei_idr` must be serialized under `mei_minor_lock` [F24, F144].
- In `mei_me_pm_runtime_suspend()`, `mei_txe_pm_runtime_suspend()`, and `mei_vsc_suspend()`, `dev->device_lock` must be held continuously across `mei_write_is_idle()` and any synchronous power-gating or aliveness transition; ME and TXE runtime suspend must guard `schedule_work(&dev->reset_work)` with `if (ret && ret != -EAGAIN)`, and `mei_vsc_suspend()` / `mei_vsc_resume()` must not call `mei_stop()` or `mei_restart()` [F25, F95, F126].
- In `vsc_tp_probe()`, `vsc_tp_isr` must be a threaded IRQ (`IRQF_TRIGGER_FALLING | IRQF_ONESHOT`) deferring `tp->event_notify` to `vsc_tp_event_work()`; `tp->event_notify_mutex` must protect all reads, calls, and updates of `tp->event_notify` and `tp->event_notify_context`, and lock order across `vsc-tp.c` and `platform-vsc.c` is `tp->event_notify_mutex` -> `mei_dev->device_lock` -> `tp->mutex` [F16, F18, F117].
- In `vsc_tp_xfer()` and `vsc_tp_rom_xfer()`, `tp->mutex` must serialize `tp->tx_buf`, `tp->rx_buf`, `tp->seq`, wakeup handshaking, and `vsc_tp_dev_xfer()`; `vsc_tp_xfer_helper()` validates `le32_to_cpu(ack.seq) == tp->seq` (and `~recv_crc == crc`), every `vsc_tp_wakeup_request(tp)` in `vsc_tp_xfer()` must be paired with `vsc_tp_wakeup_release(tp)`, and `vsc_tp_wakeup_request()` must poll `gpiod_get_value_cansleep(tp->wakeuphost)` via `read_poll_timeout()` outside `wait_event_timeout()` [F19, F102, F125, F138].
- In `drivers/watchdog/mei_wdt.c`, `wdt->reg_lock` must protect `__mei_wdt_is_registered()`, `watchdog_register_device()`, and `watchdog_unregister_device()`, and drvdata on `wdt->wdd` must be reset to `NULL` on failed registration or unregistration (zeroing `wdt->wdd`); `mei_wdt_ops_ping()` must call `init_completion(&wdt->response)` before `mei_wdt_ping(wdt)`, and in `mei_wdt_rx()`, `MEI_WDT_WDSTATE_NOT_REQUIRED` during `MEI_WDT_RUNNING` must defer unregistration via `schedule_work(&wdt->unregister)` and `goto out` to complete `wdt->response` [F12, F28, F29, F91].

## Object Lifetimes

- Every `struct mei_device` and its trailing `dev->hw[]` must be a single `kfree`-compatible allocation stored unchanged as `&dev->dev`'s drvdata for `mei_device_release()`; `mei_register()` and `mei_deregister()` must cache `dev->minor` in a local variable before `cdev_del()` or `device_destroy()` can free `dev`, and `mei_deregister()` must be the final call in `mei_me_remove()`, `mei_txe_remove()`, and `mei_vsc_remove()` after `mei_stop()`, PM/callback teardown, `mei_disable_interrupts()`, and `free_irq()` [F1, F2, F107].
- `mei_me_set_pm_domain()` and `mei_txe_set_pm_domain()` must copy `*pdev->dev.bus->pm` into `dev->pg_domain.ops` and clear `dev_pm_domain_set(dev->parent, NULL)` on remove/shutdown; on `mei_vsc_probe()` error and in `mei_vsc_remove()`, `vsc_tp_register_event_cb(tp, NULL, NULL)` must clear the event callback so `vsc_tp_event_work()` cannot pass a dangling `mei_dev` to `mei_vsc_event_cb()` [F3, F60].
- Because `mei_ioctl_connect_vtag()` shares an existing `struct mei_cl` across tagged opens, `mei_release()` must remove the file from `cl->vtag_map`, pass `file` (not `NULL`) to `mei_cl_flush_queues(cl, file)` when other entries remain, and only disconnect, unlink, and `kfree(cl)` if `cl->vtag_map` is empty both before and after `mei_cl_disconnect()` [F5, F53].
- `mei_me_cl_by_uuid()` returns a refcounted `struct mei_me_client` via `mei_me_cl_get()`, so callers (`mei_ioctl_connect_client()`, `mei_vt_support_check()`) must call `mei_me_cl_put()` on every exit path and before `goto retry`; in `vsc_tp_init()`, `release_firmware()` must not be called on `csi`, `ace`, or `cfg` until `vsc_download_bootloader()` and `vsc_download_firmware()` finish using `fw_loader->frags[]` [F6, F9].
- In `mei_pxp_probe()` and `mei_pxp_remove()`, `mei_cldev_set_drvdata(cldev, comp_master)` must precede `component_master_add_with_match()`, and `component_master_del()` must complete before freeing `comp_master`, clearing drvdata, and disabling `cldev`; in `mei_wdt_remove()`, `wdt->response` must be completed and `cancel_work_sync(&wdt->unregister)` called before `mei_wdt_unregister()`, `mei_cldev_disable()`, `dbgfs_unregister()`, and `kfree(wdt)` [F11, F134].

## API Contracts

- `mei_cl_write()` unconditionally takes ownership of `struct mei_cl_cb` (queuing it or freeing it via `mei_io_cb_free()` on error), so `mei_write()` must only call `mei_io_cb_free(cb)` when failing before `mei_cl_write()`; callers of `mei_fw_status_str()` and `mei_fw_status2str()` must pass a buffer of at least `MEI_FW_STATUS_STR_SZ` (54) bytes, and `mei_data2slots()` / `mei_hbm2slots()` must round up with `DIV_ROUND_UP(..., MEI_SLOT_SIZE)` [F7, F47, F96].
- In `mei_vsc_hw_reset()`, when `intr_enable` is `false` (from `mei_stop()`), it must return `0` after `vsc_tp_reset(hw->tp)` without calling `vsc_tp_init()`; in `mei_vsc_hw_start()`, after `vsc_tp_intr_enable(hw->tp)`, it must poll `mei_vsc_read_helper()` via `read_poll_timeout()` with `sleep_before_read = true`, `MEI_VSC_POLL_DELAY_US` (100 ms), and `MEI_VSC_POLL_TIMEOUT_US` (400 ms) before setting `hw->fw_ready = true` [F115, F123].
- `mei_pxp_ops` and `mei_lb_ops` callbacks must only be called from sleepable process context; in `mei_lb_push_payload()`, `req_size` (`struct_size(req, payload, payload_size)`) must be checked against `mei_cldev_mtu(cldev)` before `kmalloc()`, `req->reserved[]` must be zeroed, and `req->payload_size` must be initialized before copying into `req->payload[]` (`__counted_by(payload_size)`) [F74, F148].

## Error Handling

- `mei_cldev_send()`, `mei_cldev_send_timeout()`, and `vsc_tp_xfer()` return the byte count (`>= 0`) on success or `-errno` on failure, so 0-on-success callers (`mei_pxp_send_message()`, `mei_wdt_ping()`, `mei_wdt_stop()`, and `mei_vsc_write()` via `mei_vsc_write_helper()`) must normalize non-negative returns to `0` (`ret < 0 ? ret : 0`) [F32, F34].
- `vsc_tp_xfer()` rejects `obuf == NULL`, `ibuf == NULL`, or `olen > VSC_TP_MAX_MSG_SIZE` with `-EINVAL`, so `mei_vsc_write_helper()` must pass a non-NULL receive buffer; conversely, `vsc_tp_rom_xfer()` returns `0` on SPI transfer success regardless of payload, so `vsc_identify_silicon()` and `vsc_download_bootloader()` must also check `ack->token` against `VSC_TOKEN_ERROR` and expected tokens [F35, F37].
- `mei_cl_fp_by_vtag()`, `mei_cl_vtag_alloc()`, and `mei_cl_alloc_linked()` return `ERR_PTR(-errno)` on failure (and `mei_cl_fp_by_vtag()` returns `NULL` as a valid bus client `fp`), so callers must check `IS_ERR()`; `dev->ops->trc_status` is optional and `NULL` on TXE and VSC, so callers must use `mei_trc_status(dev, &trc)` [F38, F44].
- A `struct mei_cl` on `dev->file_list` has `cl->me_cl == NULL` until connected, so `mei_ioctl_connect_vtag()` must check `pos->me_cl != NULL` before `mei_cl_uuid(pos)` or `pos->me_cl->props`, and when merging an `MEI_FILE_INITIALIZING` file with `pos`, it must unlink and `kfree(cl)` and update both `file->private_data` and local `cl` to `pos` before adding `cl_vtag` to `cl->vtag_map` [F46, F56].
- In `mei_pxp_receive_message()`, `-ENOMEM` retries `mei_cldev_recv_timeout()` once (`!retry`, setting `retry = true` before `goto retry`) before falling through to `mei_pxp_reenable()`; `mei_pxp_reenable()` must still call `mei_cldev_enable()` if `mei_cldev_disable()` fails, and `mei_pxp_send_message()` / `mei_pxp_receive_message()` must preserve the negative `byte` error when `mei_pxp_reenable()` returns `0` [F68, F89].
- `mei_me_pci_suspend()` must stop the device, disable interrupts, free `pdev->irq`, and call `pci_disable_msi(pdev)`, and `mei_me_pci_resume()` must re-enable MSI, re-request the IRQ, and call `free_irq(pdev->irq, dev)` if `mei_restart(dev)` fails; in `mei_wdt_probe()`, `wd_info` must stay non-`const` and `mei_cldev_register_notif_cb()` must allow `-EOPNOTSUPP` [F130, F135].

## External Contracts

- In `mei_ioctl()`, `IOCTL_MEI_CONNECT_CLIENT` must connect vtag-capable clients (`mei_vt_support_check() == 0`) via `mei_ioctl_connect_vtag()` with `vtag = 0`, while `IOCTL_MEI_CONNECT_CLIENT_VTAG` must reject `vtag == 0` with `-EINVAL`; in `mei_poll()`, both `req_events` checks and the returned `mask` must use `__poll_t` `EPOLL*` constants rather than `POLL*` macros [F57, F150].
- In `vsc_tp_probe()`, the host wakeup GPIO must be requested via `devm_gpiod_get(dev, "wakeuphostint", GPIOD_IN)` (not `"wakeuphost"`), and the child `"intel_vsc"` platform device must be registered after `tp` is allocated with `pinfo.fwnode` set to the child ACPI device's `fwnode_handle` and `pinfo.data = &tp` (`size_data = sizeof(tp)`), so `mei_vsc_probe()` must dereference `*(struct vsc_tp **)dev_get_platdata(dev)` [F106, F118, F119].
- In `mei_pxp_probe()` and `mei_lb_probe()`, `master_match` must be initialized to `NULL` before `component_match_add_typed()` and checked with `IS_ERR_OR_NULL(master_match)`; in `mei_pxp_component_match()` and `mei_lb_component_match()`, `dev` must be checked with `dev_is_pci(dev)` before `to_pci_dev(dev)`, must accept both `PCI_CLASS_DISPLAY_VGA << 8` and `PCI_CLASS_DISPLAY_OTHER << 8`, and in `mei_pxp_component_match()`, `dev == base` must be checked before `dev = dev->parent` [F48, F90, F110, F131].

## Known Pitfalls

- In `struct mei_connect_client_data` and `struct mei_connect_client_data_vtag`, the input (`in_client_uuid` / `connect`) and output (`out_client_properties`) share a union, so `mei_ioctl()` must copy `in_client_uuid` (and `vtag`) into locals before passing `&conn.out_client_properties` (avoiding UUID clobbering on retry), and must write the output into the `copy_from_user`-initialized union (or a zeroed struct) to avoid leaking `reserved[3]` and padding bytes [F39, F151].
- In `vsc_get_sensor_name()`, `union acpi_object obj` must initialize both `.integer.type` and `.integer.value` within `.integer` (mixing `.type` and `.integer.value` clobbers `.integer.type`); after `acpi_evaluate_object(handle, "SID", &arg_list, &buffer)` succeeds, `buffer.pointer` (`ret_obj`) must be checked for non-NULL, `ACPI_TYPE_STRING`, and `string.length < sizeof(fw_loader->sensor_name)` before copying, and `ACPI_FREE(buffer.pointer)` must run on all exit paths [F42, F43, F120].
- In `struct vsc_fw_cmd`, `data.dl_start.option` and `data.dl_set.option` sit at unaligned offset 5 and must use unaligned helpers (`put_unaligned_le16()`); in `vsc_download_firmware()`, because `cmd` aliases `fw_loader->tx_buf` and the fragment loop overwrites `tx_buf`, `cmd->magic` and `cmd->cmd_id` must be re-initialized on every outer loop iteration and before `VSC_CMD_CAM_BOOT`, and any final partial chunk (`len < VSC_FW_PKG_SIZE`) must zero the trailing `VSC_FW_PKG_SIZE - len` bytes of `tx_buf` [F50, F78, F94].
