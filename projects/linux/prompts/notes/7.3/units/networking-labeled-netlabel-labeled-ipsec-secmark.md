# NETWORKING [LABELED] (NetLabel, Labeled IPsec, SECMARK)

The Labeled Networking subsystem provides packet and socket security labeling for Linux Security Modules. It comprises the NetLabel framework (LSM domain-to-protocol mappings, static/fallback unlabeled mappings, and Generic Netlink interfaces), the CIPSO/IPv4 and CALIPSO/IPv6 protocol engines, and the Netfilter `xt_SECMARK` and `xt_CONNSECMARK` targets.

## Architecture & Data Flow
- In `struct cipso_v4_doi`, `doi_def->map.std` is only allocated when `doi_def->type == CIPSO_V4_MAP_TRANS` (for `CIPSO_V4_MAP_PASS` and `CIPSO_V4_MAP_LOCAL`, `map.std` is uninitialized); check `type == CIPSO_V4_MAP_TRANS` before dereferencing `map.std`. In `struct cipso_v4_std_map_tbl`, `0` is a valid level/category and unmapped slots use `CIPSO_V4_INV_LVL` or `CIPSO_V4_INV_CAT` (`0x80000000`), so `netlbl_cipsov4_add_std()` must fill arrays with those sentinels, and lookups in `lvl.{cipso,local}` or `cat.{cipso,local}` must verify `< *_size` and `< CIPSO_V4_INV_*`. [F43, F44, F71]
- In `struct cipso_v4_doi`, `tags` has `CIPSO_V4_TAG_MAXCNT` (5) bytes with unused trailing slots set to `CIPSO_V4_TAG_INVALID` (`0`); loops over `doi_def->tags` must stop when either `iter == CIPSO_V4_TAG_MAXCNT` or `doi_def->tags[iter] == CIPSO_V4_TAG_INVALID`. [F76]
- In `cipso_v4_validate()`, the tag loop must check `opt_iter + 1 == opt_len` before reading `tag[1]`, and verify `tag_len <= opt_len - opt_iter` and `tag_len >= CIPSO_V4_TAG_*_BLEN` before reading tag payload fields. Loops walking raw IPv4 option buffers (`cipso_v4_get_actual_opt_len()`, `cipso_v4_optptr()`) must terminate immediately on `IPOPT_END` (`0`), advance by 1 on `IPOPT_NOP`/`IPOPT_NOOP` (`1`), and advance by `opt[iter + 1]` on multi-byte TLV options. [F70, F73]
- `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` and `cipso_v4_req_setattr()` allocate `sizeof(*opt) + ((buf_len + 3) & ~3)` for `struct ip_options_rcu` and set `opt->opt.cipso = sizeof(struct iphdr)`, so `cipso_v4_delopt()` and `cipso_v4_sock_getattr()` must subtract `sizeof(struct iphdr)` when indexing `opt->opt.__data`. When `cipso_v4_delopt()` removes CIPSO in place while `srr`, `rr`, `ts`, or `router_alert` remain, any of those offsets `> opt->opt.cipso` must be decremented by `cipso_len`, `opt->opt.cipso` reset to `0`, and `opt->opt.optlen` recomputed after `memmove`. [F55, F105]
- When `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` or `cipso_v4_sock_delattr()` updates `inet_sk(sk)->inet_opt` on an `inet_test_bit(IS_ICSK, sk)` socket, it must adjust `inet_csk(sk)->icsk_ext_hdr_len` by the option length delta and call `icsk_sync_mss(sk, sk_conn->icsk_pmtu_cookie)`. [F54]
- `netlbl_af4list` and `netlbl_af6list` nodes in `netlbl_domaddr_map` must only be downcast via `netlbl_domhsh_addr{4,6}_entry()`, whereas nodes in `netlbl_unlhsh_iface` must only be downcast via `netlbl_unlhsh_addr{4,6}_entry()`. In `struct netlbl_dommap_def`, the `{ addrsel, cipso, calipso }` union is discriminated by `def.type` and must be `NULL` when `def.type == NETLBL_NLTYPE_UNLABELED`. [F45, F111]
- When `netlbl_domhsh_add()` merges `NETLBL_NLTYPE_ADDRSELECT` selectors into an existing `entry_old`, it must verify via `netlbl_af{4,6}list_search_exact()` that none of the new selectors already exist in `entry_old` before moving any selector. When removing an address in `netlbl_domhsh_remove_af{4,6}()` or `netlbl_unlhsh_remove()`, the parent `netlbl_dom_map` or `netlbl_unlhsh_iface` must be removed if both its IPv4 and IPv6 lists are now empty. [F59, F66]

## Synchronization
- Every exit path in `netlbl_conn_setattr()`, `netlbl_sock_setattr()`, `netlbl_req_setattr()`, and `netlbl_skbuff_setattr()` taken after `rcu_read_lock()` must jump to the return label that calls `rcu_read_unlock()`. [F11]
- In `cipso_v4_doi_add()` and `calipso_doi_add()`, `doi_def->refcount` must be initialized to 1 via `refcount_set()` before `list_add_tail_rcu()`. `cipso_v4_doi_getdef()` and `calipso_doi_getdef()` must look up the DOI under `rcu_read_lock()` and call `refcount_inc_not_zero(&doi_def->refcount)` before `rcu_read_unlock()`, returning `NULL` if it reached zero. [F22, F24]
- Callers of `netlbl_domhsh_remove_entry()` must hold `rcu_read_lock()`. Under `netlbl_domhsh_lock`, `netlbl_domhsh_remove_entry()` must check and clear `entry->valid` (returning `-ENOENT` immediately if already `0`), then release `netlbl_domhsh_lock` before calling `cipso_v4_doi_putdef()`, `calipso_doi_putdef()`, or `netlbl_audit_start_common()`, and free `entry` via `call_rcu(&entry->rcu, netlbl_domhsh_free_entry)`. [F9, F26]
- When removing entries from `netlbl_domhsh` or `netlbl_unlhsh`, writers must clear `valid = 0` and unlink the entry under `netlbl_domhsh_lock` or `netlbl_unlhsh_lock`, and defer reclamation via `call_rcu()` or `kfree_rcu()`. In `netlbl_domhsh_remove_af{4,6}()`, after unlinking under `netlbl_domhsh_lock` and calling `rcu_read_unlock()`, `synchronize_rcu()` must be called before dropping the DOI refcount and calling `kfree(entry)`. [F27, F127]
- Callers of `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach_rcu()` and `netlbl_af{4,6}list_search*()` must hold `rcu_read_lock()` even when holding `netlbl_domhsh_lock` or `netlbl_unlhsh_lock`. In `netlbl_unlhsh_add_iface()`, re-check `netlbl_unlhsh_search_iface(ifindex)` or `netlbl_unlhsh_def` under `netlbl_unlhsh_lock` before inserting; in `netlbl_unlhsh_condremove_iface()`, checking that `addr4_list` and `addr6_list` are empty, setting `iface->valid = 0`, and unlinking must happen in one `netlbl_unlhsh_lock` critical section. [F29, F30, F126]
- All accesses to `cipso_v4_cache[bkt]` and `calipso_cache[bkt]` must use `spin_lock_bh()` / `spin_unlock_bh()`. Cache entry/key allocations (`cipso_v4_cache_add()`, `calipso_cache_add()`), wire category bitmap allocations (`cipso_v4_getattr()`, `calipso_opt_getattr()`), socket/req option allocations (`cipso_v4_sock_setattr()`, `cipso_v4_req_setattr()`, `calipso_opt_insert()`, `calipso_opt_del()`), and audit buffers in `netlbl_audit_start_common()` must use `GFP_ATOMIC`. [F118, F119, F120, F128]
- In `net/ipv4/cipso_ipv4.c`, sysctl globals `cipso_v4_cache_enabled`, `cipso_v4_cache_bucketsize`, `cipso_v4_rbm_optfmt`, and `cipso_v4_rbm_strictvalid` must be read with `READ_ONCE()`, and `cipso_v4_cache_add()` must snapshot `cipso_v4_cache_bucketsize` into a local variable. [F19]
- `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` requires exclusive access to `sk`, must pass `sk_locked` to `rcu_dereference_protected(sk_inet->inet_opt, sk_locked)`, publish `opt` via `rcu_assign_pointer()`, and free old options via `kfree_rcu()`. In `cipso_v4_req_setattr()`, `calipso_req_setattr()`, and `calipso_req_delattr()`, `req_inet->ireq_opt` and `req_inet->ipv6_opt` must be swapped via `xchg()`, freeing displaced options via `kfree_rcu()` (IPv4) or `atomic_sub()` on `sk->sk_omem_alloc` and `txopt_put()` (IPv6). [F33, F121]

## Object Lifetimes
- `cipso_v4_doi_add()` and `calipso_doi_add()` take ownership of `doi_def` only when returning `0`; on error, the caller must free it via `cipso_v4_doi_free()` or `calipso_doi_free()`. `netlbl_calipso_add()` must check `netlbl_calipso_ops_get() != NULL` before allocating `struct calipso_doi` because `calipso_doi_free()` is a no-op when `calipso_ops` is unregistered. [F3, F4]
- `cipso_v4_doi_getdef()` and `calipso_doi_getdef()` increment `doi_def->refcount` and require a matching `*_doi_putdef()` on every exit, error, and retry path (such as before `netlbl_domhsh_add()` succeeds in `netlbl_cfg_{cipsov4,calipso}_map_add()` and `netlbl_mgmt_add_common()`, or on retry in `netlbl_cipsov4_list()`), whereas `*_doi_search()` returns unreferenced pointers valid only under `rcu_read_lock()`. When `refcount_dec_and_test()` hits zero in `*_doi_putdef()`, invalidate the label cache and free `doi_def` via `call_rcu()`. [F6, F23, F50, F94]
- `netlbl_domhsh_add()` consumes `entry` on success, but on failure the caller must free the selector map, `addrmap`, `entry->domain`, `entry`, and put any DOI reference. [F8]
- `calipso_opt_insert()` and `calipso_opt_del()` allocate a `struct ipv6_opt_hdr` buffer that `calipso_opt_update()` and `ipv6_renew_options()` copy without taking ownership, so callers (`calipso_{sock,req}_{setattr,delattr}()`) must `kfree()` that buffer. Any `ipv6_txoptions` obtained via `txopt_get(pinfo)` must be released with `txopt_put()` on every exit path, and whenever `ipv6_update_options()` or `xchg(&req_inet->ipv6_opt, txopts)` returns a non-NULL old `txopts`, subtract `txopts->tot_len` from `sk->sk_omem_alloc` before `txopt_put(txopts)`. [F12, F13, F34]
- `cipso_v4_cache_add()` and `calipso_cache_add()` must allocate both `entry` and `entry->key` before calling `refcount_inc(&secattr->cache->refcount)` and setting `entry->lsm_data`, because `*_cache_entry_free()` calls `netlbl_secattr_cache_free(entry->lsm_data)` whenever `entry->lsm_data` is non-NULL. [F15]
- `netlbl_secattr_destroy()` frees `secattr->domain` (testing `secattr->flags & NETLBL_SECATTR_FREE_DOMAIN`, not the two-bit mask `NETLBL_SECATTR_DOMAIN_CPY`), `secattr->cache`, and `secattr->attr.mls.cat` without zeroing `secattr`; callers must call `netlbl_secattr_init()` before reusing `secattr` and only set `NETLBL_SECATTR_FREE_DOMAIN` when `secattr->domain` is `kfree()`-able. [F90, F112]
- Every `security_secid_to_secctx(secid, &ctx)` returning `>= 0` in `netlbl_unlhsh_add()`, `netlbl_unlhsh_remove_addr{4,6}()`, and `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist_gen()` must be paired with `security_release_secctx(&ctx)` on all paths, including when `nla_put()` fails. [F17]
- In `xt_SECMARK.c` and `xt_CONNSECMARK.c`, `checkentry_lsm()` and `connsecmark_tg_check()` must finish all table (`"mangle"` or `"security"` only), mode, and LSM checks before calling `security_secmark_refcount_inc()` or `nf_ct_netns_get(par->net, par->family)`, matching the unconditional release in `*_tg_destroy()`. [F16, F129]

## API Contracts
- `net/netlabel/netlabel_calipso.c` must access CALIPSO exclusively via `netlbl_calipso_ops_get()` (`READ_ONCE(calipso_ops)`) after checking for non-NULL `ops`. `calipso_ops` is updated with `xchg()` in `netlbl_calipso_ops_register()`, and `calipso_exit()` must unregister it with `NULL` before freeing `calipso_cache`. Any CALIPSO interface change must update `struct netlbl_calipso_ops`, `ops` in `net/ipv6/calipso.c`, and `net/netlabel/netlabel_calipso.c` in lockstep. [F31, F67, F133]
- `netlbl_catmap_walk()`, `netlbl_catmap_walkrng()`, and `netlbl_bitmap_walk()` search inclusively from `offset` (`netlbl_catmap_walkrng()` returns the last set bit in the range) and return `< 0` (`-ENOENT` or `-1`) when no bit is found; callers must store the return value in a signed `int`, initialize the cursor to `-1`, and pass `cursor + 1` on the next call. [F36, F91]
- `netlbl_catmap_getlong()` and `netlbl_catmap_setlong()` require `offset` aligned to `BITS_PER_LONG` and shift between `unsigned long` and `u64 bitmap[]` by `offset % NETLBL_CATMAP_MAPSIZE` (casting `bitmap` to `u64` before `<<` in `netlbl_catmap_setlong()`). Callers of `netlbl_catmap_getlong()` must check `*offset == (u32)-1` to stop and add `BITS_PER_LONG` to `*offset` after each call. [F92, F101, F116]
- `netlbl_bitmap_walk()` takes `@bitmap_len` in bits (`byte_len * 8`) and must check `++bit_spot >= bitmap_len` before reading `bitmap[++byte_offset]`; callers of `netlbl_bitmap_setbit()` must verify `bit < net_cat_len * 8` beforehand. [F117]
- `calipso_opt_find()` returns `-ENOENT` when no CALIPSO TLV is present while still setting `*start` and `*end` to trailing padding bounds, so `calipso_opt_insert()` and `calipso_skbuff_setattr()` must allow `-ENOENT`. `calipso_opt_insert()` returns an `ERR_PTR()` on failure and never `NULL`, whereas `calipso_opt_del()` returns `0` and sets `*new = NULL` when no non-padding options remain. [F38, F39]
- Callers of `calipso_validate()` must first verify `option[1] >= 8` and `option[6] * 4 + 8 <= option[1]` because `calipso_validate()` unconditionally reads `option[0..9]` and `option + 10` for `len - 10` bytes. [F69]
- Callers of `netlbl_af{4,6}list_add()` must set `entry->valid = 1` and pre-mask `entry->addr` with `entry->mask`. When moving entries in `netlbl_domhsh_add()`, use `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach_safe()` and restore `iter->valid = 1` after `netlbl_af{4,6}list_remove_entry()` before re-adding. Never dereference `iter` after `netlbl_af{4,6}list_foreach*` completes normally because it ends at `container_of(head, ...)`, not `NULL`. [F86, F87, F88]
- NetLabel `.dumpit` callbacks borrow `skb` and must call `genlmsg_cancel(skb, data)` on error (whereas `.doit` handlers call `kfree_skb(ans_skb)` on error before `genlmsg_reply()`). In `netlbl_domhsh_walk()`, `cipso_v4_doi_walk()`, and `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist()`, entry counters post-incremented before the callback must be decremented on negative callback return before saving `cb->args[]`; in `netlbl_unlabel_staticlist()`, finishing an address or bucket list must reset both `iter_*` and `skip_*` to `0`. [F18, F84, F85, F144]

## Error Handling
- When parsing wire categories into `secattr->attr.mls.cat` in `calipso_opt_getattr()` and `cipso_v4_parsetag_{rbm,enum,rng}()`, any error must call `netlbl_catmap_free(secattr->attr.mls.cat)` before returning, and `NETLBL_SECATTR_MLS_CAT` must only be set if `secattr->attr.mls.cat` is non-NULL. [F41]
- In `calipso_req_{setattr,delattr}()`, `sk_to_full_sk(req_to_sk(req))` must be checked for `NULL` (SYN Cookie paths). `netlbl_conn_setattr()` must verify `sk->sk_family == AF_INET6` before handling an `AF_INET6` address, and `calipso_sock_{getattr,setattr,delattr}()` must check `inet6_sk(sk) != NULL` before `txopt_get()`. `cipso_v4_sock_setattr()` must allow `sk == NULL`, and `cipso_v4_validate()` must allow `skb == NULL` (rejecting `CIPSO_V4_TAG_LOCAL` unless `skb != NULL`, `skb->dev->flags & IFF_LOOPBACK`, and `tag_len == CIPSO_V4_TAG_LOC_BLEN`). [F46, F47, F48, F80]

## External Contracts
- In `CIPSO_V4_TAG_RANGE`, ranges are descending big-endian `u16` `[cat_high, cat_low]` pairs where a final `cat_low == 0` is omitted on the wire; parsers must check `(iter + 4) <= rngcat_len` before reading `cat_low` and allow up to `CIPSO_V4_TAG_RNG_CAT_MAX` (8) ranges. `cipso_v4_map_cat_rng_hton()` must reject `net_cat_len > (CIPSO_V4_OPT_LEN_MAX - CIPSO_V4_HDR_LEN - CIPSO_V4_TAG_RNG_BLEN)` (30 bytes) upfront so `array_cnt` cannot overflow `array[CIPSO_V4_TAG_RNG_CAT_MAX * 2]`. [F77, F106]
- A CALIPSO TLV option requires `4n + 2` alignment from the Hop-by-Hop header start, a 4-byte-multiple category bitmap (`calipso[6]` 32-bit words), and `< 8` bytes of trailing pad to align the header to 8 bytes. In `calipso_skbuff_setattr()`, `calipso_genopt()` must be called with offset `start & 3` into `buf[CALIPSO_MAX_BUFFER]`, copying `buf + (start & 3)` to `(char *)hop + start` for `new_end - start` bytes. [F78, F109]
- Because `netlbl_mgmt_genl_policy` omits `NLBL_MGMT_A_IPV{4,6}{ADDR,MASK}`, handlers in `netlabel_mgmt.c` must verify both address and mask attributes exist and `nla_len()` equals `sizeof(struct in_addr)` or `sizeof(struct in6_addr)` before `nla_data()`. Callers of `netlbl_unlabel_addrinfo_get()` must first validate with an XOR check that both `NLBL_UNLABEL_A_IPV4{ADDR,MASK}` or both `NLBL_UNLABEL_A_IPV6{ADDR,MASK}` are present. [F83, F89]

## Known Pitfalls
- In the CALIPSO label cache (`calipso_cache_add()`, `calipso_cache_check()`, `calipso_opt_getattr()`), `entry->key` stores `calipso_ptr[1]` bytes starting at `calipso_ptr + 2` (omitting the 2-byte TLV header), so `calipso_cache_check()` must be passed `calipso + 2` and `calipso[1]`. [F79]
- In `cipso_v4_cache_check()` and `calipso_cache_check()`, the `u32` subtraction `entry->activity - prev_entry->activity` must be guarded by `entry->activity > prev_entry->activity` (and `prev_entry->activity -= 1` by `prev_entry->activity > 0`) to prevent unsigned underflow. [F104]
- Single-bit left shifts into `struct netlbl_lsm_catmap.bitmap[]` in `netlbl_catmap_setbit()` and `netlbl_catmap_walkrng()` must shift `NETLBL_CATMAP_BIT` (`(u64)0x01`) rather than `1` or `1UL`. In `netlbl_unlabel_getattr()`, `family == PF_INET6` with `ip_hdr(skb)->version == 4` must downgrade `family` to `PF_INET` before extracting the source address. [F98, F100]
