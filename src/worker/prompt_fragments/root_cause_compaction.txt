Root-cause compaction rule:
- Prefer one canonical finding per root cause when retained concerns share the same changed file/function or call path, resource/object, trigger path, and consequence class.
- Mark duplicate or overlapping concerns as drop_reason=subsumed_by with subsumed_by_finding_id pointing at the canonical finding.
- The canonical finding must preserve the strongest concrete evidence from the group.
- For proof-required, argument-order, seqcount/IRQ, retry-resource, resource-cleanup, and lifecycle-ordering concerns, compaction is allowed only if the surviving finding still satisfies that concern's preservation rule.
